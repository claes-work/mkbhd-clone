# 2024 Tesla Model 3 Refresh: The Perfect Starter EV!

## Summary

Marques is **"very, very impressed"** by the refreshed **Tesla Model 3** ("Highland"). His frame:
Tesla's most *important* car now **"feels like a mini Model S"** — a worthy flagship built from a
pile of subtle upgrades (better seals/quieter cabin, softer suspension, better materials, a rear
screen, a faster computer). The one controversial change: **no more stalks.** His bottom line — **the
EV he'd recommend most easily to anyone looking for a first EV.** Fully Marques-narrated; corroborates
Plaid ownership.

## Key claims (biography marked ⬥)

- **[2024-02-01]** ⭐ The framing: "This is the car people know and see most often as a Tesla — arguably
  their most important car — **and it kind of feels now like a mini Model S.**" Tells: the new angular
  headlights, Ultra Red paint, one-piece rear taillights, no logo (just "Tesla" text). (quote)
- **[2024-02-01]** ⬥ BIOGRAPHY: **"the fact that it's getting the same range as what my Plaid was
  getting is pretty impressive"** — ~330 mi rated (real ~285–290), drag coefficient 0.219. (Corroborates
  Model S Plaid ownership.) (quote)
- **[2024-02-01]** The upgrades that add up: a **better door seal → much quieter cabin**; **softer,
  noticeably more comfortable suspension** ("the old coil suspension was a little harsh — owners would
  agree"); a **tighter steering radius** (unadvertised); better Alcantara/materials; wrap-around LED
  lighting; a **faster, more responsive computer** ("Tesla's had the best in-car software that isn't
  CarPlay/Android Auto for years"); High-Fidelity Park Assist; **a rear touchscreen** ("a way better
  back-seat experience — huge for the Uber Model 3s"). (paraphrase)
- **[2024-02-01]** ⚠️ The one gripe: **no more stalks** — you swipe the screen to change gear (or use the
  on-screen PRNDL), and blinkers/wipers moved to steering-wheel buttons. "Some things people will like,
  some they'll have to get used to." (quote)
- **[2024-02-01]** On a performance version: it would only add track mode + a firmer suspension; "for
  the non-performance version, it's complete, it's great, it's a flagship — a mini Model S." (paraphrase)
- **[2024-02-01]** ⭐ VERDICT: **"This is the car I would recommend so easily to anyone just looking for a
  first EV."** "Might be the easiest EV to calibrate to — one-pedal driving, charging network, just in
  general." (quote)
