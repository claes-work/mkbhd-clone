# The In-Glass Fingerprint Reader: Explained! (@mkbhd, 2018-02-21)

## Summary
A teach-the-mechanism first-look at the **first shipping in-display fingerprint reader** (Synaptics Clear ID in
the Vivo X20 Plus UD). Explains exactly how the optical sensor works, where it lands versus Touch ID / Face ID,
and why it matters for the bezel-less future — a direct setup for the State of Bezels essay a few weeks later.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-02-21] **The mechanism.** "It's an **optical CMOS sensor** — the same type as in a camera — under the OLED
  glass. It needs an OLED (so nearby pixels can shine light), and it **lights up your fingerprint** so the
  reflection reads on the sensor. Screen off → raise the phone → indicator lights → touch → a pulse of light
  reads the print, all in **under 1.5 seconds.**" Clear, correct optics explanation.
- [2018-02-21] **Where it lands — between Touch ID and Face ID.** "It's **faster than Face ID but slower than Touch
  ID** — think of it as a **first-gen new Touch ID.** The speed is the thing to improve." Calibrated, not hyped.
- [2018-02-21] **The vision — touch anywhere to unlock.** "This first gen is a little rectangle, but the sensor
  isn't limited to that — it could be the bottom third, the bottom half, or **the entire display.** Imagine
  taking the phone out and just touching it anywhere to unlock — **that's what Face ID wanted to be.**" His
  characteristic extrapolate-the-trajectory move.
- [2018-02-21] **Ties to the bezel-less goal.** Framed explicitly as a piece of the full-screen puzzle: relocating
  the fingerprint reader off the front is one of the sacrifices the "bezel-less revolution / infinity display"
  demands — "this gets us closer to a fully bezel-less phone." Foreshadows [[2018-03-14-yt-xIvaQ1Hv2Ug]].
- [2018-02-21] **Realistic on adoption.** "You won't see this in the Galaxy S9 or the next LG/Note — it's still a
  bit slow, so the big manufacturers won't ship this first-gen product. But a more refined version could show up
  as early as next year." Measured on the diffusion timeline.

## Notable verbatim quotes
