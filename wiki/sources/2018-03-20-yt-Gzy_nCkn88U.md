# Samsung Galaxy S9 Review: The Perfect... Samsung! (@mkbhd, 2018-03-20)

## Summary
The full Galaxy S9/S9+ review, built on one thesis: it's **"a nearly perfect Samsung phone."** Great at almost
everything (display, build, camera, feature-completeness), weak on battery, and it won't convert anyone who
already dislikes Samsung software. Confirms the variable-aperture camera works but still ranks it 3rd.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-03-20] **The thesis — "a nearly perfect Samsung phone."** "If you've liked Samsung phones, this is **the
  best, most complete version of that they've ever made.** But if slow Android updates or the skin aren't your
  thing, **this won't change your mind.**" A framing that respects the buyer's existing preferences rather than
  declaring a universal verdict.
- [2018-03-20] **The fingerprint move is bigger than people credit.** Relocating the reader to **under the camera**
  (centered, slightly raised) is "**perfect now** — even more than people are giving it credit for," plus the
  slick **roll-your-finger-down-once** enrollment. A small-detail win he calls out.
- [2018-03-20] **Battery = the biggest weakness.** "Among all the great things about these phones, **battery life
  is definitely not great** — the S9+ (3500 mAh) would be dead by the end of a heavy day." Fast + wireless
  charging offsets it "for me," but he flags it as the real compromise. Consistent with his battery-matters stance.
- [2018-03-20] **Variable aperture — verdict.** It works: low-light shots get a real bump (f/1.5 lets in more
  light → less grain → less smoothing). But "it's **still not the best camera in any smartphone — that's still
  the Pixel 2, hands down**, and it falls a little behind the iPhone X. Nobody will be disappointed with a great
  **3rd-place** camera." His camera ranking, restated. Also notes Samsung's tendency to **overexpose** and the
  Rec.2020-with-HDR-on quirk.
- [2018-03-20] **Bixby — "gave it another chance, still not great."** "I could've disabled the Bixby button day
  one, but I left it on to give it another chance. Every day I open it, scroll the cards hoping for something
  useful, and usually don't. **I'm going to disable it.**" His willing-to-retry-then-honestly-judge method.
- [2018-03-20] **S9 vs S9+ — turns out not a big deal.** The extra RAM/screen/battery/camera differences are minor
  in daily use; but the S9+'s existence "means there's **way less reason to buy the Galaxy Note 8**" (the Note's
  only real edge is the stylus). Practical buying guidance.
- [2018-03-20] **The close — "master of one, good at everything."** "You could call it a jack of all trades, master
  of none — but it's actually the **master of one (the display), good at so many things, and bad at so few** that
  it's easy to recommend. **I recommend the S9.** Though I'll personally switch back to my Pixel 2." Nuanced
  endorsement with an honest personal caveat.
