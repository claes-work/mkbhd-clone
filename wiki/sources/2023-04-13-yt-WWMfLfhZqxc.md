## Summary
David explains the Leica M11 Monochrom ($9,195) — why a black-and-white-only sensor
(no color filter array) gives 3× the light, more detail, less color noise, higher usable
ISO, and film-like grain — plus his street/architecture shots. Team-authored; no subject data.

## Key claims (David-attributed — CONTEXT ONLY)
- [2023-04-13] The technical core (context): color sensors use a Bayer color-filter array +
  demosaicing (which can cause purple fringing on fine detail); removing the CFA for a
  monochrome sensor absorbs ~3× the light and directly represents photon intensity → more
  detail/contrast, cleaner high-ISO (up to 200,000). Verdict: only for someone already
  buying an M11 who shoots exclusively black-and-white.
