# Charging a non-Tesla at a Tesla Supercharger!

## Summary

Marques drives his **Rivian R1T** to try the first-ever **"charge your non-Tesla"** Superchargers
(the retrofitted **Magic Dock** CCS adapters) — a companion to his charging-infrastructure thesis.
It works ("as close to plug-and-play as it gets" — 30→85% in ~30 min), and he calls it a game-changer
for whether he'd recommend a non-Tesla EV. But it exposes the **charge-port-placement / parking-grid
problem**: non-Teslas (port on the wrong side) block two stalls, which he live-demonstrates with a
gaggle of non-Teslas awkwardly stretching cables. Fully Marques-narrated.

## Key claims (biography marked ⬥)

- **[2023-03-01]** ⬥ Context: **"Up until yesterday there were basically two types of EVs — Teslas and
  non-Teslas"** (re: charging). He drives the **Rivian R1T** to a Brewster, NY 250 kW Supercharger with
  a Magic Dock. The flow: Tesla app → "charge your non-Tesla" → pick a stall → unlock the built-in CCS
  adapter. **"It's already working — 100 miles/hour of charging. That was really quick."** (quote)
- **[2023-03-01]** ⭐ The verdict: **"This changes a lot — how likely I'd be to recommend or buy a
  non-Tesla EV. You now choose between risking flaky public chargers or the reliability/uptime of a
  (slightly pricier) Tesla Supercharger — I'd pick the Supercharger every time. This just made my Rivian
  a better truck."** (quote)
- **[2023-03-01]** ⚠️ The catch (live-demonstrated): the R1T's port is **front-left**, but Teslas'
  are **rear-left**, and the parking grid + short cables are optimized for Teslas — **so a non-Tesla
  blocks two stalls.** He ends up in an impromptu group (a Model 3, an F-150 Lightning, a Lucid) all
  awkwardly stretching cables. **"There's going to be an etiquette question — 'maybe you move over so I
  can charge.'"** No battery preconditioning, and the 400V setup won't max out a fast-charging EV. (quote)
- **[2023-03-01]** ⬥ CONTEXT: bumps into a fellow charger — **the "State of Charge" YouTuber** (an
  EV-charging channel) — and an F-150 Lightning owner; they help each other spot the cars into position.
  (Confirms he's a recognizable figure at chargers — "you're Marcus Brownlee.") (paraphrase)
- **[2023-03-01]** ⭐ Tesla's strategy read: **"Roll it out to a few and see how it goes — the charging-
  spot problem is the biggest question mark. If a huge, busy California Supercharger suddenly had messily-
  parked non-Teslas taking two spots each, that'd be a problem. If I were a huge Tesla person I'd worry
  my own experience gets worse."** (quote)
