# WWDC 2024 Recap: Is Apple Intelligence Legit? (@mkbhd, 2024-06-11)

## Summary
A live-tweeted recap of "possibly one of the biggest Apple WWDC keynotes of all time" — notable because, in a year when
everyone expects **AI at the front**, Apple put **AI at the back half**: **they didn't say "AI" once for the entire
first hour** (callback to his "words Apple won't say" video), then spent the second hour dumping it all and **branding
it "Apple Intelligence."** Highlights across the OSes: **VisionOS 2** ("feels more like a 1.2"); **iOS 18** home-screen
freedom (place icons anywhere, a theme/tint engine) — "yes, Android's had this for years; have your fun" — plus the
observation that letting people go crazy means **"you can also make some really ugly home screens"**; and the runaway
star, the iPad's **one genuinely killer feature: Math Notes** (handwritten equations solved in your own handwriting,
with variables and live graphs — "clearly AI, before they even said the word"). On **Apple Intelligence** itself: a
bucket of **Apple-built** generative models sprinkled everywhere (Writing Tools, a Clean Up magic-eraser, Genmoji,
Image Playground, a smarter context-aware Siri) — but **only on iPhone 15 Pro and M1-or-later** devices. The privacy
architecture: **almost everything runs on-device**, spillover goes to **Private Cloud Compute** (Apple-silicon servers,
nothing stored), and **ChatGPT is "about as unintegrated as it gets"** — it asks permission every single time, obscures
your IP, stores nothing. His overall take: **"the Humane Pin and Rabbit R1 were so doomed from the start"** — no
standalone box can match this personalization on the phone you already own — and **"it feels like we've entered a new
age with Apple,"** where the AI story overshadows every non-AI feature.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2024-06-11] **AI at the back half; they branded it Apple Intelligence.** "'In a year when everyone expects AI at the
  front of what you do, Apple put it at the back half — they didn't say "AI" once in the entire first hour, then spat
  it all out in the second and, of course, branded it: it's now Apple Intelligence.'"
- [2024-06-11] **iOS 18 home-screen freedom — Android had it, but have your fun.** "'You can finally put icons anywhere
  on the grid (it got an applause moment as if they invented it) plus a theme engine to tint every icon to match your
  wallpaper. Android's had this with launchers for years — but now people can go crazy and kind of ruin their home
  screen, and they clearly want to; I've seen some rough ones. Apple's at least tints all the icons at once, unlike
  Material You.'"
- [2024-06-11] **The iPad's one killer feature is Math Notes.** "'I was ready to dunk on "the calculator finally comes
  to iPad" — then they pulled the Pencil and showed Math Notes: write equations by hand and it solves them in your own
  handwriting, supports variables, and gives live-adjusting graphs. That's clearly AI — handwriting recognition +
  semantic understanding — before they even said the word. It's the one genuinely impressive new iPad feature.'"
- [2024-06-11] **On-device first; Private Cloud Compute; ChatGPT barely integrated.** "'Almost everything runs on-device
  intentionally, on Apple-built models. Spillover goes to Private Cloud Compute — Apple-silicon servers where nothing
  is stored or sent to Apple. And when something is genuinely ChatGPT's job, it asks permission every single time,
  stores nothing, and obscures your IP — about as unintegrated as it gets. Only iPhone 15 Pro and M1-or-later get any
  of it.'"
- [2024-06-11] **Humane and Rabbit were doomed; a new age with Apple.** "'My overall take: the Humane Pin and Rabbit R1
  were so doomed from the start — no standalone box can be as good as this stuff on the phone that already knows
  everything about you. And it feels like we've entered a new age with Apple, where the AI story overshadows whatever
  your favorite non-AI feature is.'"
