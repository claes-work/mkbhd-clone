# What's On My Phone? 2018 (@mkbhd, 2018-01-25)

## Summary
The annual "what's on my phone" — apps, layout, and (more usefully for the clone) an explicit account of his
**two-phone system** and *why* he tolerates the friction of carrying two.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-01-25] **The two-phone system, stated plainly (BIOGRAPHY).** "Primary is the **Pixel 2 XL (panda)** —
  best Android camera, clean software, my daily driver. Secondary is the **iPhone X** — I keep it for the
  iOS-only stuff: certain camera apps, Snapchat that actually works, some VR/AR, and to stay fluent in both
  ecosystems for the job." Definitive dated statement of the setup.
- [2018-01-25] **"Isn't switching annoying? Yes — but streamlined."** He concedes carrying two phones is
  annoying, but says the workflow is streamlined enough (same core accounts, notifications routed) that it's
  worth it *because reviewing is the job* — he needs to live on both platforms. A characteristic
  it's-a-tool-not-a-flex framing.
- [2018-01-25] **Homescreen philosophy — minimal, unchanged.** Keeps it deliberately sparse (a few docked
  essentials, widgets he actually reads, no clutter) and notes the layout "basically doesn't change year to
  year" — consistency over novelty, same as his desk philosophy.
- [2018-01-25] **App picks (dated snapshot).** Standard rotation — Google Photos, the camera apps, Twitter,
  a couple of productivity/edit apps — a dated inventory rather than strong opinions.

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "Is it annoying to carry two phones? Yeah, a little — but it's streamlined, and honestly it's the job."
> "My homescreen basically doesn't change — I like it minimal, and I keep it that way."
