# The Rivian R1T is an Incredibly Fun Electric Pickup! (@mkbhd, 2021-10-22)

## Summary
A 24-hour first-drive of the Rivian R1T (not a full review). His read: **the most impressive/promising EV to come
from the startups** — the most dailyable electric truck thanks to size, range, and comfort, and the most fun he's
ever had driving a truck. Structured as 10 questions. Standouts: the **adventure-vehicle** identity (kitchen-sink
extras — the gear tunnel, powered frunk, built-in air compressor, removable Bluetooth speaker, flashlight); the
**Tesla-inspired in-house software** (good layout, laggy map); and the open **charging question** (no Supercharger
access; the promised Rivian Adventure Network doesn't exist yet — "I'll believe it when I see it"). Closes with a
**"not a Tesla killer / rising tide"** industry note.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-10-22] **The most promising startup EV — and it turns every head.** "'This is maybe the most promising of
  any of the EVs coming out of the startups (thanks in part to the Amazon/Ford background). Quad motors, 314 mi
  range, 0–60 in 3 s flat — hilariously quick for a truck. **This truck turns more heads than anything I have
  ever driven — more than any McLaren, any Tesla.** Someone said it looks 50 years old and from the future at the
  same time — nail on the head.'"
- [2021-10-22] **Electric packaging maximizes space — the gear tunnel + powered frunk.** "'No engine/transmission
  means it maximizes space everywhere — 68 cu ft total in a compact truck. The **powered front trunk** is so
  convenient I'd use it more than the bed. And the **gear tunnel** — a column of space behind the seats with
  300-lb doors you can sit/step on, power outlets, a $5,000 slide-out camp kitchen option — I've never seen
  anything like it in any truck.'"
- [2021-10-22] **Best truck he's driven — and eerily quiet off-road.** "'Combine quad motors (800+ hp) with a
  world-class air suspension and it doesn't matter how much it weighs. **I've never had more fun driving a truck
  ever.** Off-roading is eerily quiet in a good way — you hear the tires, not an engine.'"
- [2021-10-22] **Tesla-inspired software + the charging question.** "'The whole in-house UI is heavily
  Tesla-inspired (many ex-Tesla employees now at Rivian would agree) — functionally good, but the map is slow and
  laggy (fixable via OTA updates). **The big early EV question: no Tesla Supercharger access; at an Electrify
  America station it never exceeded 100 kW. This is a vehicle you want to adventure with — so you don't want to
  think about range. The promised Rivian Adventure Network doesn't exist yet — I'll believe it when I see it.**'"
- [2021-10-22] **Not a Tesla killer — the rising tide.** "'Someone asked if this is a Tesla killer — no. **Not
  everything electric has to be a Tesla killer; the Tesla truck isn't even out yet. Electric trucks as a whole
  benefit from really good ones coming out — they all boost each other up.** The whole industry going electric
  just got a little faster thanks to this one.'"
