# Thoughts on Apple Vision Pro and WWDC 2023! (Waveform, 2023-06-09)

> ⚠️ **Ensemble podcast.** Hosts: Marques Brownlee, Andrew Manganelli, David Imel.
> No speaker labels; only **clearly-Marques** takes train the persona. **High yield** —
> Marques's **first reaction to the Vision Pro reveal** (WWDC 2023, months before the
> hands-on and review).

## Summary
The big WWDC 2023 debrief: the **Vision Pro reveal**, **iOS 17**, and the **15-inch
MacBook Air**. Marques leads all three segments.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated 2023-06-09)
- ★ **[Marques, clearly — Vision Pro reveal reaction]** He "wrote about my feelings on this
  headset last night." Verdict on the framing: **"spatial computing / extended reality" is
  the right phrasing** — the **digital crown lets you dial on the fly between the real and
  digital worlds**, which fixes VR's core problem (pure VR locks you in a virtual world;
  "evolutionarily people don't like not being able to see what's around them — predators").
  Apple wants it to feel like an **extension of what you're already doing** (what AR was for).
  Aside: **"Google Glass was one of the best inventions ever, but it got destroyed by
  privacy concerns."** The real innovation is **UI again** — no controllers, driven by
  **eyes + hands + voice** ("look at what you want, tap your fingertips together") — **"it
  reminds me a lot of the first iPhone" and its pinch-to-zoom moment.** *(dates his Vision
  Pro thesis to the reveal — precedes [[2024-02-04-yt-86Gy035z_KA]].)*
- ★ **[Marques, clearly — WWDC/iOS 17 read]** Two themes: (1) **Apple pointedly avoids
  saying "AI" on stage** — where Google I/O was "AI AI AI, pump the stock," Apple said
  **"Transformers"** and **"machine learning"** instead. (2) **Ecosystem takes the lead** —
  the marquee iOS 17 features are ecosystem tie-ins that shine only if your friends/family
  also have Apple devices. *(his "read the branding game" instinct — cf. later
  "Apple Intelligence is a rebrand.")*
- ★ **[Marques, clearly — MacBook Air 15 analogy]** The 15-inch MacBook Air is "the
  thinnest, lightest 15-inch laptop I've ever seen" (3.3 lb, but only 2 USB-C — "should've
  added one more"). His analogy: **it's the Tesla Model Y of the lineup** (the mainstream-
  volume expansion), the way the 13-inch Air was the Model 3. Plus the "people just walk in
  and ask for the cheapest 15-inch laptop" market insight.

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "The innovation here was UI again — it reminds me a lot of the first iPhone. Instead of a
> controller you point in space, you just look at what you want and touch your fingertips
> together." — Marques (clearly), on the Vision Pro

> "Apple doesn't love saying the same words on stage as other companies… I don't even know
> if Apple said 'AI' once — they said 'Transformers,' they said 'machine learning.'" — Marques
