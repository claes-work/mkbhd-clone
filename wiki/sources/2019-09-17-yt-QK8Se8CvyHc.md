# Unboxing Every Green iPhone 11! (@mkbhd, 2019-09-17)

## Summary
A light unboxing / second-impressions of all three iPhone 11s in their green colors. Mostly a fun color-analysis
video; the one durable note is the **in-box charger disparity** (the Pro finally gets a USB-C fast charger; the
$700 iPhone 11 still ships the "garbage 5W charger").

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-09-17] **The charger disparity — a pointed nitpick.** "'Finally an **18 W USB-C fast charger + USB-C-to-
  Lightning cable in the Pro box** (the new default Apple charging solution). But **the non-Pro iPhone 11 still
  comes with the garbage, barely-functional 5 W USB-A charger** — disappointing even at $700.' Why not go USB-C
  all the way to the phone? 'Could just be a Lightning licensing thing — kind of stubborn.'"
- [2019-09-17] **Colored boxes return.** "'They're going back to **different-colored boxes** — white for the 11,
  matte black for the Pro (last seen with the iPhone 8). The phones only have peel plastic on the front now, not
  the back.'"
- [2019-09-17] **The midnight-green color read + a color-refresh wish.** "'The Pro's **midnight green** is 'more
  muted than the box — actually closer to gray than green,' like an "off-gray." I'd still personally go **space
  gray.** I saw mock-ups of a **matte product-red Pro** I would have loved — **Apple, if you're listening, do a
  six-month red color refresh, please.**'" A stated color preference + a light request.
- [2019-09-17] **Full reviews teased.** "'I've been using the 11s for a couple days — not quite long enough for
  the full review, that's coming soon; the comments are a good place to leave testing requests.'" Format note.

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "The non-pro iPhone 11 still comes with the garbage, barely functional 5-watt USB-A-to-Lightning charger in that
> box — disappointing, even if the phone is 700 bucks."
> "Apple, if you're listening — if you want to do a six-month color refresh of the iPhone, please do a red. Please."
