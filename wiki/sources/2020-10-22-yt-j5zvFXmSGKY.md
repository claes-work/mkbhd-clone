# Huawei Mate 40 Pro Impressions: Technically Awesome! (@mkbhd, 2020-10-22)

## Summary
Impressions of the Huawei Mate 40 Pro — the **"ultimate expression"** of a recurring tragedy: **no matter how
technically impressive the hardware, it's impossible to recommend** because of the Google-services ban. Durable
threads: the **"technically impressive but can't recommend"** refrain, his **don't-buy-on-the-promise-of-future-
software** rule applied to Huawei's app-gallery build-out, his **curved-display criticism**, and his
**pro-competition** stance ("don't think the other companies aren't noticing").

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-10-22] **THE REFRAIN — "technically awesome, impossible to recommend."** "'Every time Huawei drops a
  phone the conversation is the same: **great hardware, real advances — but if you live outside China you
  probably shouldn't get it, and even if you do, the experience is so gutted it's not worth it.** This is the
  ultimate expression of that: **no matter how good the phone is, it's impossible to recommend, which sucks.**'"
- [2020-10-22] **The genuinely-impressive hardware (cataloged).** "'A 50 MP ~1/1.28" primary (near RX100 1"),
  5x periscope, "space ring" camera layout (centered, so it rocks less); **66 W wired + 50 W wireless charging;**
  a **5 nm Kirin 9000 (with the A14, the first 5 nm chips shipping this year);** loud, good stereo speakers; a
  smart **wide-angle selfie camera** (last seen on the Pixel 3 XL — "that one's not technically impressive, it's
  just smart").'"
- [2020-10-22] **The curved-display criticism (restated).** "'They curve the Mate display right over the edges
  because it looks dramatic, but **curved displays create unnecessary accidental touches, cost more, are more
  fragile,** and here they had to move the buttons back and the keyboard "melts" off the side. Inconvenient, but
  technically impressive.'" A consistent MKBHD position.
- [2020-10-22] **DON'T BUY ON THE PROMISE OF FUTURE SOFTWARE — applied to the app gallery.** "'The story is: if
  the phone's this good, what are they building to replace the missing Google services? The **App Gallery is
  growing, first-party apps improving — but Instagram, YouTube, key apps are missing (some listed as web apps in
  a browser).** My rule: **don't buy tech on the promise of future software updates — buy it because it's great
  now.** These services aren't done yet, so I'll keep waiting for the replacement level.'" (Same rule as the RED
  Komodo, [[2020-08-21-yt-9Hk_rvjtxKc]].)
- [2020-10-22] **The "take the logo off" thought experiment + pro-competition.** "'**Take the Huawei logo off
  this phone and imagine it from Samsung or Apple — it'd be right up against their bleeding edge.** I'm not
  recommending it, but **I'm so happy to see the effort — because don't think the other companies aren't
  noticing this stuff.**'" His competition-lifts-everyone value.

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "This is the ultimate expression of, no matter how good their new phone is, it's impossible to recommend —
> which sucks."
> "Don't buy a piece of tech based on the promise of future software updates — buy it because it's good now and
> you'd be great with it now if it never got updated."
> "Take the logo off of this phone... imagine this phone from someone else, the Samsungs, the Apples of the
> world — this would be right up against the bleeding edge of the stuff that they make."
