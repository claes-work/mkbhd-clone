# iPhone 11 Impressions: A Familiar Sequel! (@mkbhd, 2019-09-11)

## Summary
First look at the cheaper iPhone 11 (successor to the XR) — "a subtle, incremental update" whose story is the
camera: a dual system with the long-awaited ultrawide, a new night mode, and — unusually — a **price drop to $699.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-09-11] **"Incremental is the word we like to use" — and that's fine for Apple.** "'Almost the exact same
  design as the XR — same curves, notch, LCD, ~720p display.' But **the XR was by far Apple's best-seller last
  year** (more than the XS/XS Max), so **lowering the price to $699** (Apple 'not a company that drops prices very
  often') likely makes the 11 their most popular phone again." A shrewd product-strategy read.
- [2019-09-11] **"Finally on the ultrawide train."** "**Dual cameras** (12 MP standard + 12 MP ultrawide), both 4K
  60, intense cross-lens color matching. 'I'm just **pumped to have an ultrawide on the iPhone now** — I'm glad
  they finally got on this.'" His consistent pro-ultrawide enthusiasm.
- [2019-09-11] **Night mode arrives (after Night Sight's jabs).** "'Ever since **Pixel's Night Sight took direct
  jabs at the iPhone on stage** and Huawei's night mode got so good, we've been curious what Apple would do.'
  Here it is — but it **only activates automatically when it's dark enough** (no manual toggle). 'Very curious to
  test it in the full review.'"
- [2019-09-11] **The U1 chip / ultra-wideband — a genuine first.** "'A new **U1 chip** for spatial positioning —
  point one iPhone 11 at another and **beam AirDrops directly at it** (shows first in the share sheet). Uses
  ultra-wideband — the first time I've seen it used this way in a phone.'"
- [2019-09-11] **The one disappointment — the display resolution again.** "'My only real disappointment is the
  **display** — I was hoping they'd bump the resolution (the XR's low res was everyone's disappointment). But
  dropping to $699, I can't really be mad.'" A dated, honest reservation.

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "It's a subtle update, no way around that — 'incremental' is the word we like to use — but honestly, for Apple,
> this is probably not a bad thing."
> "I'm just glad they finally got on this ultra wide train."
