# Google Pixel 4 Review: Inside the Hype Machine! (@mkbhd, 2019-10-22)

## Summary
The full Pixel 4 XL review — one of his most-anticipated 2019 phones. A landmark review: it introduces his
**"Pixel vs iPhone camera graph"** framework, reaffirms 90 Hz as **"my favorite trend in smartphones of the past
year,"** and ends declining to recommend the Pixel 4 to most people (battery). "A victim of the hype machine."

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-10-22] **THE CAMERA-GRAPH FRAMEWORK — Pixel vs iPhone.** "'My **Pixel-vs-iPhone camera graph** is a pair of
  lines: the **middle is where most photos are taken most of the time — and there I prefer the Pixel** (contrasty,
  punchy, detailed). But on the **edges of photography — low light and ultrawide — the iPhone does better** (its
  night mode + Deep Fusion stay **truer to life**; the Pixel gets more dramatic and noisier). **If I could only
  pick one, iPhone 11 Pro — it's more versatile (ultrawide) and crushes video.**'" A reusable way to say "best
  camera depends on the scenario."
- [2019-10-22] **Face-unlock-only — misses the fingerprint; a security quirk.** "'No fingerprint anywhere — **I
  still like fingerprint sensors and find myself missing one.** Face unlock is **incredibly fast (possibly too
  fast)** — but it **works with your eyes closed** (Google even warns you in settings), a security quirk. My fix:
  turn off skip-lock-screen.'" A dated reservation about removing all other biometrics.
- [2019-10-22] **90 Hz — his favorite trend — but a bad dynamic-switching implementation.** "'**High refresh is my
  favorite trend in smartphones of the past year.** But the Pixel's **Smooth Display keeps dropping to 60 Hz** for
  no clear reason — it felt choppier than the always-90 OnePlus 7 Pro. I dug into **developer options and found
  "Force 90 Hz,"** which fixed it — but it **obliterates battery** (C+ → D).'" Praise for 90 Hz, criticism of
  Google's execution.
- [2019-10-22] **"The most useless second camera they could've put in."** "'If you asked what the most useless
  second camera for the Pixel would be — behind a depth or time-of-flight sensor — I'd have said a **2x telephoto.
  And that's what we got.** An ultrawide, or a 3-5x tele, would've been way more fun. Portrait mode even **zooms to
  ~1.7x but still uses the main camera for the photo** — so why zoom? No answer.'" Reinforces the ultrawide gap.
- [2019-10-22] **THE VERDICT — a victim of the hype machine; not recommending it to most.** "'The camera's still
  punchy/excellent for stills, and I love the 90 Hz + finally-smooth Pixel. But it **still needs a bigger battery
  and more storage** and is **overpriced for the storage.** I recommend it for the **tiny group like me** (high-
  refresh + camera + latest Android + willing to overpay) — but **for most people I'm not recommending the Pixel
  4, mainly the battery**; there are better deals, including the cheaper iPhone 11. **Possibly a victim of the
  hype machine of being the most-leaked phone of all time.**'"
