# Oppo Find X: Motorized Madness! (@mkbhd, 2018-06-22)

## Summary
First look at the Oppo Find X — "the hottest phone on the planet right now" — whose **whole top motorizes up** to
reveal both cameras and a Face-ID-style sensor array. A celebration of creative industrial design "just when
phones were getting boring," tempered by his standard moving-parts caution.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-06-22] **Design is the whole story — 93.8% screen-to-body, no fingerprint reader anywhere.** "The specs
  (SD845, 8 GB, 256 GB) are the boring part. To hit 93.8% screen-to-body, they made sacrifices: **no fingerprint
  reader at all**, and the **entire top of the phone motorizes up** to reveal the rear camera, front camera, and a
  **Face-ID-clone** dot-projector/IR array." A striking bezel-less solution.
- [2018-06-22] **"An integrated part of a thoughtful design," not a hack.** "Different from the Vivo NEX — the NEX's
  pop-up feels like a hack to fit a selfie cam; **this feels like an integrated part of a thoughtful design.**" A
  design-taste distinction between two same-week pop-up phones.
- [2018-06-22] **The face unlock genuinely impresses.** "Register your face in ~3 seconds; it pops up, looks, and
  retracts in about half a second — **possibly even faster than Face ID.** I doubt it's as secure, but if it's not
  easily fooled by photos, it's impressive." Calibrated praise.
- [2018-06-22] **The moving-parts caution (consistent, hands-on).** "You'll trigger this motor **every time you
  unlock, hundreds of times a day.** I tried things: push it down and the motor retracts; resist it and it pauses
  and retries (failsafes) — but **I could literally pull the top open. Forget waterproofing, and one grain of sand
  could be bad news.** How long is the motor rated for? I'd love to know." Empirical stress-poking.
- [2018-06-22] **The verdict — creativity worth celebrating.** "**I love this** — a genuinely interesting, creative
  design just when everyone was doing the same notch-and-dual-camera dance. A new player from left field. Throw a
  Nova Launcher on it and it might actually stay in my pocket." Design-appreciation over spec-checking. Callback:
  Oppo's history (**the Find 5 — the first 1080p phone** — and Find 7).

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "The Vivo NEX feels like a hack to get a front camera in somehow; this feels like an integrated part of a
> thoughtful design."
> "I could literally pull the top of the phone open — so it's not the most durable mechanism in the world."
> "I love what we're getting here — a genuinely interesting, creative design just when phones were getting boring."
