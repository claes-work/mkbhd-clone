# Living with the Tesla Cybertruck So You Don't Have To!

## Summary

Marques's **living-with review of the Tesla Cybertruck** (~1,000 miles over a couple weeks) — a
**canonical biography source** (Ridge provided the loaner "until I got mine"; he's Ridge's chief
creative partner). The truck is defined by **big highs and lows**: it's an unmatched attention-magnet
with genuinely great tech (steer-by-wire nimbleness, best truck sound system he's heard, the
lightest EV pickup), undercut by **the worst panel gap he's ever seen** and a pile of **paid-for
features that don't ship yet** (Autopilot, the range extender, the light bar). Fully Marques-narrated.

## Key claims (biography marked ⬥)

- **[2024-03-19]** ⬥ BIOGRAPHY: **he owns / is getting a Cybertruck** — "shout out to Ridge for
  providing this Cybertruck I've been testing until I got mine… I've joined Ridge's board, I'm chief
  creative partner." He's driven the dual-motor "for almost 1,000 miles" and "recently living with the
  triple-motor." (quote)
- **[2024-03-19]** The looks are the whole verdict: **"iconic — you either love it or hate it… in
  person it looks even more like a low-poly render than video shows."** **"The most attention-getting
  vehicle by a long way I've ever driven"** — people accelerate alongside to film it ("actually kind of
  dangerous"); Sentry Mode has "hundreds of clips of people walking up to it." (quote)
- **[2024-03-19]** ⚠️ **"This panel gap is the worst I've ever seen in a production vehicle"** (he's
  "the guy who doesn't care about panel gaps" and still flags it); rust at the uncovered wheel nuts;
  "low-VIN production-quality stuff." (quote)
- **[2024-03-19]** ⚠️ **"You paid for Autopilot, you don't get it"** — only traffic-aware cruise ships;
  the range extender and roof light bar "exist on the website" but not on his truck. (A vivid instance
  of his **don't-buy-on-the-promise-of-future-updates** rule.) ~320 mi indicated (drive ~290); "not the
  ideal long-term road-tripper." (quote)
- **[2024-03-19]** The highs: **steer-by-wire + variable ratio + rear-wheel steer = "the most nimble-
  feeling truck ever made"** ("full lock is one turn — I drove an F-150 Lightning after and felt like I
  was doing all this extra movement"); **"best sound system I've ever heard in a truck, top-five in any
  vehicle"**; an underrated air suspension with ~a foot of travel and little body roll; the lightest EV
  pickup (800V architecture); the best in-car computer he's used (though "no CarPlay, no Android Auto").
  (quote)
- **[2024-03-19]** ⭐ The reframe: **"90-something percent of people almost never use truck stuff with
  their trucks"** — so as a large SUV the Cybertruck is "actually amazing"; "if you made me road-trip an
  electric truck, I'd want to be in this one (the Supercharger advantage is real) or the F-150
  Lightning." Verdict rests entirely on the aesthetics. (quote)
