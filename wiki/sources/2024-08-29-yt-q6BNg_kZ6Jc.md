# The Most In Disguise EV... (Mercedes eSprinter)

## Summary

Marques reviews the **Mercedes eSprinter** electric cargo van and uses it to make two points: it's a
prime example of **a vehicle that's ideal to go electric** (local delivery — quieter, smoother,
cheaper to run, fewer moving parts) and it's **the EV that's hardest to tell is electric** (it looks
and drives exactly like the gas Sprinter). He caps it with his recurring **folding-phone analogy for
EV adoption**: EVs win when the tech is good enough that "electric vs. gas" is just a no-tradeoff
preference. Fully Marques-narrated.

## Key claims

- **[2024-08-29]** Two informal AutoFocus "awards" he'd give: **"the EV that was best to become
  electric from the gas version"** and **"the EV that is hardest to tell is electric"** — the eSprinter
  wins the latter (only tells: one "electric" badge + the "eSprinter" e; prior holder was the F-150
  Lightning). (paraphrase)
- **[2024-08-29]** Why it's ideal to go electric: for **local deliveries** it's "smoother, quieter,
  more comfortable, cheaper to operate, less maintenance, and even a little friendlier (quieter for
  neighborhoods)." ~275–300mi rated (payload-adjusted down to ~245–255 with a 450lb load); only ~$7–8k
  more than the gas Sprinter ($60k → $68k). (quote + paraphrase)
- **[2024-08-29]** Comic runner: "here are all the features in the cabin of the eSprinter: cargo
  space… I'm just kidding, but not really" — cup holders everywhere, no glovebox, a **camera-mirror**
  (no rear glass), a very-elevated backup camera. (paraphrase)
- **[2024-08-29]** ⭐ The **folding-phone analogy for EVs**: **"Electric cars have started to remind me
  of folding phones. Folding phones will hit the tipping point only when the tech has caught up so
  there are no trade-offs — you just choose the folding one or not. EVs are advancing the same way:
  someday you'll just buy the electric or the gas version, whichever is better for you. But the tech's
  not there yet — so there are still trade-offs: weight, price, and range/road-trip anxiety."** (quote)
- **[2024-08-29]** The eSprinter dodges most of those trade-offs "because it's a Sprinter — big and
  heavy anyway, and you're not road-tripping it, just doing local delivery." **"One of the better types
  of vehicles to go electric."** (quote)
