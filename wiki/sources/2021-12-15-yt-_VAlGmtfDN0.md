# Oppo Find N Impressions: The Best Folding Phone?! (@mkbhd, 2021-12-15)

## Summary
Impressions (not a full review; a China-only phone) of the Oppo Find N — "maybe the best folding phone on Earth
right now, at least in form factor." The durable idea: **the #1 thing people wanted changed about the Galaxy Z
Fold was "make it smaller / less tall" — and the Find N did exactly that.** Shrinking the fold to a normal 2:1
outside screen (iPhone 13 mini-sized) makes it far more usable and pocketable, opening to a near-square 7.1"
"notebook-feel" inside display. Impressively, the smaller phone fits a **bigger battery than the Fold 3** — and
its **closed crease shows no gap at all** ("as good as it gets right now"). His read: it doesn't push tech forward
so much as **prove the category is getting better** — more options, more variations. He personally prefers the
notebook feel to the bigger fold.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-12-15] **The fix everyone wanted was "make it smaller."** "'The Fold 3 is the gold standard. Ask fold users
  the #1 thing they'd change and the most common answer is **"make it not quite as tall — smaller."** Enter the
  Find N: shrink the whole thing to a normal 2:1 outside screen (about iPhone 13 mini size) and it's way more
  usable corner-to-corner and way more pocketable. Low-key a contender for best small phone of the year.'"
- [2021-12-15] **The notebook feel.** "'It opens like the Fold to a 7.1" near-square (8.4:9) "serene" display —
  only slightly smaller than the Fold 3's 7.4" on paper but meaningfully smaller in feel, **like a little
  notebook.** Less intimidating; you're more likely to flip it between portrait and landscape. Good open/closed
  software continuity (swipe up to resume what you had on the cover screen).'"
- [2021-12-15] **Smaller phone, bigger battery — and no gap.** "'Wild: they did so much of what Samsung did (ultra-
  thin glass, a firm smooth hinge, triple cameras, an 888) in a smaller phone — **and somehow fit a bigger battery
  than the Fold 3 (4,500 vs 4,400 mAh, split one cell per side).** The one con is the 60 Hz outside screen. And
  the closed crease: **a piece of paper, no gap at all while closed — as good as it gets right now.**'"
- [2021-12-15] **The category is getting better.** "'This is China-only (~$1,500), so it's more my evaluation of
  the hardware and the idea of a smaller fold than a buy recommendation. It doesn't necessarily move things
  forward technologically — but **the whole folding category has gotten better: more options, more variations,
  more ideas.** Team big-fold or little-fold? I like the notebook feel better than the bigger fold.'"

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "The most common answer that I've heard is: if it was just not quite as tall — if we could just make it smaller."
> "They've somehow managed to fit a larger battery in this phone than the Fold."
> "A piece of paper — no gap at all while closed. That's as good as it gets right now."
