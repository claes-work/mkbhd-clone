# Living with Xiaomi's Electric Car - It's Insane.

## Summary

Marques lives with the **Xiaomi SU7 Max** (a Chinese EV, ~$40–45k there but "feels like $70–75k…
actually more") and lands two theses: (1) **if these Chinese EVs came to the US, "we'd be
cooked"** — they'd take over; and (2) it's **"the closest thing to an Apple car I've been able to
review"** (a phone/appliance company making a car, with wild phone-to-car integration). The
software, the phone screen-mirroring, and the modular accessory ecosystem are things he's "never
seen before." Fully Marques-narrated (a "living with" companion to the main-channel review).

## Key claims

- **[2025-12-08]** The thesis game: badge-off, "there's no way you'd guess the price — and the more
  you know about cars, the less likely you'd guess right." The **SU7 Max** retails ~$40–45k in China
  "but feels a lot more like $70–75k" (later: "starting to feel like a six-digit car" vs a Taycan/
  Model S/Lucid). (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-12-08]** **Theory #1: "if these Chinese EVs came to the US, we'd be cooked"** — "extremely
  competitive, they'd take over"; coming to Europe soon, already doing super well in China. (quote)
- **[2025-12-08]** **Theory #2: "the closest thing to an Apple car I've been able to review"** — made
  by a company that makes smartphones/appliances (**he's shooting the video on a Xiaomi 17 Pro Max**);
  "the way this phone integrates into the car and software is crazy." (quote)
- **[2025-12-08]** The tech: a Porsche-Taycan-ish silhouette (McLaren-ish headlights, LiDAR for FSD);
  a **16" center display** with the best non-CarPlay software (still supports **full-screen wireless
  CarPlay**); a **50W cooled wireless charger**; and **Xiaomi phone screen-mirroring** — hit the
  interconnectivity button and "you're using your phone, this is now the interface. This is the exact
  type of thing I'd expect Apple to do if they made an Apple car." (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-12-08]** **A modular accessory ecosystem** "I've never seen before" — magnetic modules that
  talk to the car's software (a smart dashboard, a walkie-talkie, underglow, a karaoke mic, music-
  reactive light strips) you can add/subtract to make it "minimalist or maximalist." (paraphrase)
- **[2025-12-08]** "Wolf in sheep's clothing" — looks tame/chill but has ~700hp (0–60 ~2.5–2.8s, a
  20-second boost button). Comfort mode air suspension "feels as good as any Lucid or Model S." His
  **favorite feature**: it's very quiet (ANC via 25 speakers) and it **routes the nav voice to just
  the speaker behind the driver's head** so the music never ducks — "amazing, works with any phone."
  (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-12-08]** ⬥ BIOGRAPHY: checks the back seat "behind a 6'3 driving position" (Model-3-like
  legroom, more than a Taycan). (paraphrase)
- **[2025-12-08]** Verdict: "extremely well-built, really good fundamentals/specs/build/software —
  it kind of doesn't have any downsides." **"If this came to the US, would it be really good? I don't
  think there's any question — yeah, it would cook."** But tariffs/import fees mean "not in the US
  anytime soon." (quote)
