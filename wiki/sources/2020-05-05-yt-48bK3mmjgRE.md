# Google Pixel Buds Review: WAY Better! (@mkbhd, 2020-05-05)

## Summary
Review of the 2020 Pixel Buds — "miles better" than the disappointing originals, now in his **top three wireless
earbuds.** The lesson: they're **not spec-impressive** ($179, 5 hr, no ANC) but **nail all the fundamentals**, so
they're the ones he'll actually carry.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-05-05] **THE THESIS — nail the fundamentals over specs.** "'On paper they're **not impressive** — $179,
  average battery, **no noise cancellation.** What makes them so good is they **nail all the fundamentals really
  well and have been rock-solid**: a small **pocketable case** (AirPods-Pro-sized) with USB-C + wireless charging,
  a low-profile secure fit, and great sound.'" A restatement of the fundamentals/everyday-use-beats-specs belief
  (cf. [[2019-11-05-yt-cG8PXdTlDag]]).
- [2020-05-05] **Top three — above the better-sounding Sennheisers (because portability).** "'Top three wireless
  earbuds, with AirPods Pro and Galaxy Buds+ — **above the Sennheisers I love, even though the Sennheisers sound
  better, because these are so much smaller/more portable and more likely for me to actually use.**'" The
  most-likely-to-use tiebreaker.
- [2020-05-05] **The honest downsides.** "'The **bass lacks punch** (fine for video/podcasts, less for music) and
  **there's no EQ in the app** to add it back; **passive noise isolation, not ANC** (I wouldn't fly with them). If
  they'd added ANC, battery would've suffered — maybe a future Pixel Buds Pro.'"
- [2020-05-05] **"The assistant in your ear feels like the future."** "'Having Google Assistant in your ear — put
  your finger to your ear like an FBI agent and ask a question, get an answer — **feels like the future** (even if
  I just keep asking the weather).'"
- [2020-05-05] **BIOGRAPHY — quarantine turned him into a headphones-everywhere person.** "'These are enough to
  turn me into the wear-earbuds-everywhere guy — I've never been that person. Something about **quarantine life**
  (walk around the house, phone upstairs, still call the assistant / listen to a podcast). **This is my review
  unit — I'll be buying a pair and making them my everyday headphones.**'" A datable habit change + the buy-what-I-
  review practice.
