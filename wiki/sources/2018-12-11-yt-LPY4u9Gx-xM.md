# Top 5 McLaren 720S Features! [Auto Focus Ep. 4] (@mkbhd, 2018-12-11)

## Summary
**Auto Focus Episode 4** — a Top-5 on the McLaren 720S (the review pretext: the OnePlus 6T McLaren Edition tie-in).
Notable for his appreciation of McLaren's **conservative spec estimates**, the aero teach-the-mechanism, and the
"Tesla Roadster training wheels" framing tied to his own EV taste.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-12-11] **#1 Performance — and the under-promise appreciation.** "700+ hp, ~3,000 lb, 0–60 rated at **2.7 s**
  — but **McLaren is very conservative: YouTube videos show 2.6/2.5/2.4, owners dyno bigger numbers than claimed.**
  The theory: they don't want to anger their million-dollar P1/Senna customers, because **this $300k car is as fast
  as those.** Coming from someone who drives a P100D, the **60–100 speed is really fast.**" His appreciation for
  a maker that under-promises and over-delivers.
- [2018-12-11] **#2 Aero — teach-the-mechanism.** "Big specs are easy; the aero is the craft. The **headlights are
  just the slits — the rest is a carbon-fiber intake to cool the brakes**; air passes through **slots in the
  doors** and out over the rear wing. **The whole car is a teardrop around the mid-engine — you're in a cockpit
  cutting through the air.**" The wing doubles as an air brake. His engineering-eye read.
- [2018-12-11] **#3 Modes — dual dials.** "Next-level modular adjustment: **separate Handling (H) and Performance
  (P) dials**, each Comfort/Sport/Track, set independently; Track mode flips the dashboard down to a narrow
  gear/RPM/speed strip. There's even a **variable drift-control** angle dial." Appreciates the granularity.
- [2018-12-11] **#4 Design + the attention (biography).** "It turns so many heads — the raised exhaust, the doors
  with a window in the top, and **my favorite steering wheel of any car I've driven — which matters, like having a
  favorite phone display, it's how you interact with it.**" Biography tell: "**I already get asked if I play
  basketball a lot — but I've never been asked more if I play in the NBA than getting out of this car**" (his 6'3"
  height). People wait at the car in parking lots.
- [2018-12-11] **#5 The details + "Roadster training wheels."** The engine/turbo sound, a frunk "bigger than my
  Tesla," the carbon fiber everywhere. "**Driving this feels like Tesla Roadster training wheels**" — but "you
  can't get over that it's **rear-wheel drive; an all-wheel-drive car always feels more planted and under
  control**" (his stated preference). "It has too much power for its own good."
- [2018-12-11] **The OnePlus 6T McLaren Edition tie-in (disclosed sponsor).** "**The reason I had the car:**
  OnePlus partnered with McLaren for a **6T McLaren Edition** — carbon-fiber hints, McLaren orange, **Warp Charge**
  (even faster charging), **10 GB RAM**. Thanks to OnePlus for sponsoring." Transparent about the arrangement.
