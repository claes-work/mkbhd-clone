# The 2019 iPod Touch: Why Does It Exist? (@mkbhd, 2019-05-31)

## Summary
A "why does this product exist" piece on the $199 2019 iPod Touch (product RED, A10 refresh in the years-old
design). Notable as a **biography** source — his first-ever tech purchase was an iPod Touch — and for the
**specs-don't-tell-the-whole-story** thesis. (Format credit: he asked Austin Evans's permission to use the
"why does it exist" title.)

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-05-31] **BIOGRAPHY — his first-ever tech purchase.** "The **first piece of tech I ever bought with my own
  money** — high-school allowance, before I had a channel or made any videos — was an **iPod Touch** (30-pin).
  'Near and dear to my heart' — then it got **stolen from my school locker** a week later. After that I got a
  **Zune HD**, which I actually really liked." A concrete, dated origin-story data point.
- [2019-05-31] **The iPod's historical weight.** "The iPod was 'Apple's first really super massive hit' — in high
  school **everyone had one or wanted one.** Then the iPhone ('an iPod, a phone, and an internet communicator')
  absorbed it. In 2019, **iPhone is ~60% of Apple's revenue** and the iPod is a 'retired category' you have to
  dig to find."
- [2019-05-31] **THE THESIS — specs on paper don't tell the whole story.** "'Despite the **abysmal specs on
  paper**, this is a prime example where the **numbers really don't tell the whole story.** It still opens apps
  quickly, scrolls smoothly, and is more than enough to listen to music — an excellent iPod.'" A recurring
  MKBHD spec-skepticism principle.
- [2019-05-31] **Who it's actually for (the reasonable case for the $200 one).** "**Kids too young for a phone**
  (iMessage/FaceTime on Wi-Fi, no calls/texts); anyone who wants a **tiny, headphone-jack music player** for
  running; **developers** wanting a throwaway latest-iOS test device. **The $300/$400 variants are 'straight-up
  robbery'** — buy a used iPhone 7 instead. Only the **$200** one makes sense." His characteristic
  buy-this-not-that specificity.
- [2019-05-31] **The size point.** "The **entire 2019 iPod Touch fits inside just the screen of the iPhone XS
  Max** — one of the last devices whose 4" 16:9 screen lets you 'reach all four corners without struggling.'"
