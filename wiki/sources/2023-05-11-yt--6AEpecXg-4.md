# Google Pixel Tablet Impressions: A New Bargain? (@mkbhd, 2023-05-11)

## Summary
A first-look at the **$499 Pixel Tablet**, whose value proposition is **one killer feature**: it magnetically docks
onto an **included speaker dock and becomes a Google Nest Hub** (charging at 15 W, upgraded audio). The dock is the
"two-devices-in-one" pitch — Google told him **"80%+ of tablet use happens in the home,"** so for the other 20% when
it's just sitting around, popping it onto a dock to become a smart-home/photo-frame hub makes sense (and the dock
sells separately for $130 — so it's "~$370 for the tablet and $130 for the dock"). His honest frame: **"I've come
away knowing I don't need one, but really liking it anyway."** The rest of the tablet is **"pretty mid"** — an 11"
60 Hz LCD, Tensor G2, a basic 8 MP camera — deliberately not a high-end workstation iPad. And the recurring
**Android-tablet software question** ("what are you actually going to do on it?"): Google is **smart to not start
with an $800 premium tablet** (which would invite that question immediately) and instead cover the basics
(split-screen, 50+ optimized apps that share the Fold's work). It "settles nicely into the mid-range."

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2023-05-11] **I don't need one, but I like it — the dock is the killer feature.** "'I got about an hour with it
  and came away knowing I don't need one but really liking it anyway. The killer feature: with the power of magnets,
  it slaps onto an included dock and basically becomes a Nest Hub Max — charging at 15 W and connecting to the
  dock's bigger speaker. It's as awesome as it looks.'"
- [2023-05-11] **80% of tablet use is at home — so double up the use.** "'They told me something like 80%+ of tablet
  use happens in the home, so for the other 20% when it's just sitting around, it makes sense to pop it on a dock and
  now it's a polished Nest Hub display. I'm glad Google fleshed out this idea and doubled up its use.'"
- [2023-05-11] **The value math — two devices in one.** "'The dock's included, and it sells separately for $130. So
  $500 for the tablet and dock means about $370 for the tablet and $130 for the dock — the Nest Hub Max is $230 on
  its own, so getting that device out of it too is pretty good value.'"
- [2023-05-11] **The rest is "pretty mid" — deliberately.** "'The rest is pretty mid: an 11" 60 Hz LCD, Tensor G2, a
  basic 8 MP camera. It's not trying to compete at the high end like a workstation iPad — and Google's being smart
  about intentionally not starting with a crazy $800 premium tablet, because that's what makes people immediately
  ask "what am I going to do on this?" With a mid-tier media tablet they won't ask that as much.'"
- [2023-05-11] **The Android-tablet software question remains.** "'It's still an Android tablet, and it's been a
  minute since a real hit one (for Google, since the Nexus 7). It covers the basics — split-screen, 50+ optimized
  apps that aren't just stretched phone apps — and benefits from all the work that went into the $1,800 Fold.'"
