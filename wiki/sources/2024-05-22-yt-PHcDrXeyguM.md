# 5 Weird iPad Pro (M4) Decisions (@mkbhd, 2024-05-22)

## Summary
Two weeks with the **M4 iPad Pro** doing "a lot of the same stuff I did with my M1 from three years ago" — and
**"almost exactly the same experience"** — pivoting into **five interesting/weird Apple decisions**. (1) **Thinner
instead of battery life**: nobody asked, and with the M4's efficiency Apple could have kept last year's thickness and
shipped **enormous battery life** — instead they chose strikingly thin with the same rock-solid battery. He'd have
loved option one. (2) **Things quietly removed — physics**: the **ultra-wide camera is gone**, and thinner means
smaller **quad speakers** (magnet paper reveals new circular drivers) that are "a little less bassy." (3) **Still ships
a slow white 20W brick**: they stopped bricking the iPhone but still box a brick with a $1,500–$2,500 iPad — at least
make it the **fastest charging the iPad supports**, and match the cable color. (4) **Computer-like stratification**:
buying a pricier iPad Pro now changes **RAM (8→16 GB), CPU cores (9→10), storage, *and* the Nano-texture option** —
"very computer-like." But (5) it's still hobbled: **hardware outpaces software** (a new OLED begs for an **always-on
display** the iPad doesn't have — maybe at WWDC), because **"Apple has Macs to protect,"** so the Pro stays an
overpowered iPad. The **Pencil Pro** (haptics, squeeze, rotation, a *fake computer-generated shadow*) is great — but
**"you'll need to buy a whole new iPad to use it, and that was on purpose"** (they rearranged the internal magnets when
moving the camera to the landscape side, rather than offset the Pencil). His bottom line: **"I'm personally not buying
one — I already have a relatively new M1"** — but if you don't have a recent iPad it's better than ever; everyone's
**waiting on WWDC / iPadOS** to see if the software finally does anything new.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2024-05-22] **Two weeks in — almost exactly the same as the M1.** "'I've been doing the same stuff I did on my
  three-year-old M1 iPad Pro — YouTube, browsing, email, music — and spoiler, it's almost exactly the same experience.
  The M1 was already overpowered, smooth, great battery; this is even more so, of all the same stuff.'"
- [2024-05-22] **Thinner instead of an enormous battery.** "'With the M4's efficiency, if they'd kept last year's
  thickness they could have made an iPad with insane battery life. Instead they chose strikingly thin with the same
  rock-solid battery. Both are valid — I just would've loved to have seen option one.'"
- [2024-05-22] **Physics — removed ultra-wide, smaller/less-bassy speakers.** "'They quietly dropped the ultra-wide
  camera, and thinner means less room inside — magnet paper shows new circular speaker drivers, and back to back this
  one is a little less bassy. Make something thinner and the speakers have to suffer. Physics.'"
- [2024-05-22] **Still ships a slow white 20W brick.** "'They stopped shipping a brick with the iPhone, but still box
  one with a $1,500–$2,500 iPad — and it's a slow white 20W brick. If you're going to include it, at least make it the
  fastest charging the iPad supports, and maybe match the cable color. Nitpicking, hopefully not.'"
- [2024-05-22] **Computer-like stratification — but Apple has Macs to protect.** "'Buying a pricier iPad Pro now
  changes RAM (8→16 GB), CPU cores (9→10 on the 1–2 TB), storage, and the Nano-texture option — very computer-like. But
  they won't let it become a full computer because Apple has Macs to protect. So it's stuck as an overpowered iPad — a
  perfect example of hardware outpacing software is a new OLED with no always-on display (maybe iPadOS adds it). We're
  all waiting on WWDC.'"
- [2024-05-22] **The Pencil Pro forces a whole new iPad — on purpose.** "'The new Pencil Pro is great — haptics,
  squeeze sensor, rotation, even a fake computer-generated shadow that matches your tool. But to use it you need a
  whole new iPad, and that was on purpose: moving the camera to the landscape side collided with the Pencil's charging
  magnets, so they rearranged the internal magnet array instead of just offsetting the Pencil. Convenient. I'm not
  buying one — I already have a relatively new M1.'"
