# Sony WH-1000XM4 Review: The Final Form! (@mkbhd, 2020-08-13)

## Summary
Review of the $349 Sony WH-1000XM4 — his **ANC king** (succeeding the XM3). Sony took an **"if it ain't broke,
don't fix it"** approach and **knocked down all the small nitpicks** from the last generation, chiefly adding
multi-device support.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-08-13] **"They lined up all my nitpicks and knocked them down."** "'They look identical to the XM3 — fine,
  no one had major complaints — and **smartly just addressed all the small requests** people like me had. "It's
  like they lined up all their little nitpicks and knocked them all down."' A stated appreciation for iterate-on-
  feedback over reinvention.
- [2020-08-13] **#1 — multipoint (finally).** "'My **biggest request: multi-device support** — took two years, but
  it's here. Paired to your laptop and phone at once: music on one, a call comes in on the other, it **seamlessly
  auto-switches**, then switches back. "They probably could have stopped there and I'd have been happy." (It also
  improved over software updates.)'"
- [2020-08-13] **Still the ANC king (now better).** "'The **XM3 was my ANC king** — I picked them over better-
  looking/feeling headphones (the Surface Headphones) for the ANC. The **XM4 is better** — now cancels more higher
  frequencies too (dishwashers, cars, voices, my **mechanical-keyboard clacking**). **You're not going to find a
  better pair for pure isolation + sound quality — if there's a better one, I haven't found it.** "Another
  dimension."'"
- [2020-08-13] **The features — wear detection good, speak-to-chat gimmicky.** "'**Wear detection** (pause on
  removal) and **hand-over-the-cup pass-through** (fast — that matters) are useful; **speak-to-chat** (auto-pause
  when you talk) is the most gimmicky — "if someone walks up and I go 'what,' the music pauses" — I turned it off.'"
  His use-it-vs-gimmick sorting.
- [2020-08-13] **"Basically my ideal headphones" + the XM3 discount tip.** "'Nothing fundamentally wrong — **basically
  my ideal headphones** (I'd like cooler design/colors — a matte red — but the understated matte black is fine).
  A bonus: **the XM3 is now ~$250** (no multipoint) — a nice discount if you don't need two paired devices.'"
