# The Polestar 2 has Flaws but I Like It Anyway!

## Summary

Marques reviews the limited **Polestar 2 BST 230** and captures a recurring type of verdict: **"a
great EV that no one should ever buy — and I really mean that."** He loves it (design, handling, the
enthusiast engineering) while telling you not to buy it. The BST's cleverness is that it did **the
opposite of Tesla** — not more straight-line speed, but handling and precision (better tires,
stiffer springs, carbon-ceramic front brakes, twist-to-adjust Öhlins dampers in the frunk). At $80k
it makes no financial sense. Fully Marques-narrated.

## Key claims

- **[2023-10-26]** ⭐ The verdict type: **"There's an EV I really like despite knowing it has a lot of
  flaws, and I'd never buy it or even recommend it — a great EV that no one should ever buy. I really
  mean that about this one."** (quote)
- **[2023-10-26]** ⭐ The BST's contrarian move: **"They did the exact opposite of what Tesla did. Tesla
  knows everyone likes straight-line speed — so 'here's Plaid, an extra motor, it goes faster, same
  brakes/handling.' The BST is barely faster (~4s 0–60, +50–60 hp) — everything they did was handling
  and precision: better tires, stiffer springs, better dampers, carbon-ceramic front brakes. A quirky
  thing to do."** (quote)
- **[2023-10-26]** ⭐ Best-EVs-at-disguising-weight list (reconfirmed): **"My top three EVs I've ever
  driven that are best at disguising their weight are the Porsche Taycan, the $2M Rimac Nevera, and this
  thing."** Super nimble, real steering feel, quick. (quote)
- **[2023-10-26]** The enthusiast detail: **custom Öhlins dampers in the frunk you literally twist to
  change compression** — "there's nothing in the software that does this, it's just pure car hardware
  porn. You have to be crazy to spend extra on this — but it's got it and it's awesome." (quote)
- **[2023-10-26]** ⚠️ The flaws: a small back seat (he's 6'3", head scraping the roof); no wireless
  CarPlay (Android Automotive built-in only — "nicely integrated, but most people are used to their
  phone's way"); underwhelming optional speakers; a dated-looking big-bezel screen. (paraphrase)
- **[2023-10-26]** ⭐ Verdict: **"You'd have to be insane to buy this — an $80k Polestar 2, when there
  are much faster EVs (Model Y Perf, Mach-E GT, EV6 GT). But they know what I like — a matte-black car
  with a racing stripe, custom dampers, fun suspension tricks — and it's super nimble and quick."** (quote)
