# Reviewing MKBHD Videos! (@mkbhd, 2022-07-02)

## Summary
A retrospective self-review (to celebrate his **Masterclass** launch) in which Marques rewatches his own videos
back to the very first — a rich biography/content-strategy source. The durable thread: **~14 years of video-making,
almost all self-taught — "there's no how-to guide for creating a tech channel and making a business out of it; we
basically created the blueprint,"** now crystallized as the Masterclass (in the company of Neil deGrasse Tyson and
Steph Curry). Key beats: **the Kobe Bryant interview (2015)** — his first Talking Tech, "the most nervous I've ever
been," and what he has "a lot to thank Kobe for"; **the Galaxy Fold break story** (he had the screen-protector-peel
break on camera but published the unboxing first, then chimed in on Twitter and the avalanche hit); **the HTC U
Ultra "poor use of space" common-thread technique** (an early deliberate retention device); the Verge collab era;
and the **2009 origin** — 74 subscribers, the HP Pavilion dv7t in the corner of his parents' bedroom, Animoto
intros, replying to every single subscriber.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-07-02] **Self-taught — "we created the blueprint" → the Masterclass.** "'The thing I'm most proud of is that
  **pretty much all of it is self-taught — there's no how-to guide for creating a tech channel and making a
  business out of it; until now, we basically created the blueprint.** I got to crystallize it in a **Masterclass**
  (special company — I watched NdGT's, and Steph Curry did one). It's nice to look back and know you're getting
  better — and embarrassing that the old stuff is still up.'"
- [2022-07-02] **The Kobe Bryant interview (2015) — the first Talking Tech, most nervous ever.** "'"This is the most
  nervous I've ever been in my life" — a 2015 out-of-the-blue Nike invite to talk to Kobe about his signature shoe
  during his farewell season. **The first of the interviews I ever did — this is what the Talking Tech series is
  all about.** He came over, the nicest guy, broke the ice ("I heard you doing this YouTube thing, this is cool") —
  **I have a lot to thank Kobe for.**' (Also his last video edited in Premiere.)'"
- [2022-07-02] **The Galaxy Fold break — behind the scenes.** "'The 2019 Galaxy Fold unboxing (23M views): **I
  actually got the shot of the screen protector breaking as I peeled it — but I'd already shot/published the
  unboxing, so I only chimed in on Twitter "hey, mine also broke," and that's when the avalanche of Fold-break
  stories hit** (very few review units, all taken back). The magnet paper is Evergreen — everyone loves seeing
  where the magnets are.'"
- [2022-07-02] **The common-thread technique (HTC U Ultra, 2017).** "'One of my favorite videos to put together —
  an early time I spent extra effort on **a common thread throughout ("a poor use of space… a poor use of
  space…"). It doesn't always work, but when it does it's great for retention and for enjoying the video the whole
  way through.**' (A negative/PSA-tier review — "if you don't think I did negative videos, watch this one.")'"
- [2022-07-02] **The 2009 origin.** "'All the way back to the first video (Jan 2009) — **74 subscribers, the HP
  Pavilion dv7t (my first computer, used for the first 400 videos), the corner of my parents' bedroom, Animoto
  intros** (I wasn't skilled at editing, so I used free tools). No intention of it being a job — just a fun thing
  after practice before homework. **Back then I got an email for every subscriber and I'd click their profile and
  thank them.**'"
