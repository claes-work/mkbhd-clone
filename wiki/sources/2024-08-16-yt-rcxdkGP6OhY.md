## Summary

Co-host **Miles** does a hands-on first look at the **Rimac Nevera R** — a track-focused, more
powerful version of the electric hypercar Nevera (2,107 hp, 0–60 in 1.74s, a 0–186 mph production
record). He walks through the aero (everything "done in threes"), the off-the-shelf Cup 2 tires, the
smaller-but-more-efficient battery, and the ~$2.3M price / 40-unit run. Attributed entirely to Miles.

## Key claims (all Miles — context only, NOT persona data)

- **[2024-08-16]** *(Miles)* 2,107 hp; 0–60 in **1.74s**, 0–186 mph in 8.66s (a production record);
  independent four-motor torque vectoring; ~$2.3M, 40 units worldwide. A smaller (than the standard
  Nevera) but molecularly-improved, more-efficient battery — "a lot of new-EV battery tech is sourced
  from Rimac." (paraphrase)
- **[2024-08-16]** *(Miles)* Praise for usability: "a GT-sized cabin like a C8 Corvette… very easy to
  get around, just like the standard Nevera," a real volume knob ("I wish more EVs would just have
  regular volume knobs"). Notes the electric-hypercar segment is still new (Nevera sales lag the
  sold-out Bugatti Tourbillon; the LFA/Carrera-GT value-appreciation parallel). (paraphrase)
