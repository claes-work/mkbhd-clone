# Apple Lost the AI Race ★★

## Summary

A debate-format video (Marques arguing with himself) that reframes whether Apple "lost the AI
race." The resolution: **there are two races.** In **Race #1 — the models/software** — Apple
clearly lost ("company A pays company B $1B/year → company B already won"). But in **Race #2 —
the on-device-AI hardware** — Apple has a head start, because **as on-device models improve you
go to the cloud less, and Apple makes the device you'll run it all on** ("they don't make search
engines either, and they sell you the device you Google on"). The catch: **Siri doesn't excel at
anything the others don't**, and **OpenAI could make a phone.** A landmark AI-strategy piece. ★★.

## Key claims

- **[2026-07-08]** Framed as a two-sided debate. Side A: "**it's so obvious Apple lost**" — after
  ChatGPT, "Google went crazy, Meta turned the burners on, Bing was insane… and Apple did just
  nothing — literally refused to say 'AI' at their events for months." Side B: "**they really won.**"
  (quote)
- **[2026-07-08]** Side B's reframe: "**your definition of the AI race is too narrow.** Being OpenAI
  with ChatGPT is one thing, but **making the hardware everyone runs AI on is a different thing.**"
  Apple's playbook is to **sit back, let early adopters work out the bugs, then step in late with an
  ecosystem-cohesive version** — and "Apple Intelligence is Apple waiting for the tech to get good
  enough after the first hundred million ChatGPT users worked the bugs out." (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2026-07-08]** **Hot take: "Apple Intelligence doesn't have to be that good — it just has to be
  good *enough* for investors to believe Apple's doing something with AI. It doesn't have to be amazing
  for Apple to still be winning, because you're still buying an iPhone."** The rival AI features
  (writing tools, photo editing, Genmoji) "are such a gimmick — no one's buying a new phone just to use
  that stuff." (quote)
- **[2026-07-08]** The counter: **"but what if I'm not buying an iPhone?** If I really care about AI,
  there are so many other phones I'd buy first — a Google, Samsung, or Xiaomi phone" with better AI
  assistants/tools. (quote)
- **[2026-07-08]** The core thesis — **the on-device future**: "the fastest, most secure generative AI
  happens **on device**; the bigger models are what you go to the cloud for. **As on-device models get
  better, you go to the cloud less and less** — so someday you basically never go to the cloud, and
  **who's gonna make that device? Apple.** Just because they don't do the software doesn't mean they're
  losing — **they don't do search engines either, and they sell you the device you should do the
  Googling on.**" (That's why Nvidia wants RTX Spark — a piece of the hardware pie.) (quote)
- **[2026-07-08]** The synthesis — **two races**: **Race #1 (models/software)** Apple "already took the
  L, but can't look like they're losing, so they cobbled together enough of an AI suite to not be
  embarrassing" — "in a world where **company A pays company B $1B/year, company B already won.**"
  **Race #2 (on-device hardware)** the finish line is far out, and **Apple has a head start because it
  makes the iPhone and will protect its position with everything.** (quote) [Ties directly to "The New
  Siri is Google."]
- **[2026-07-08]** The threat and the pattern: **"there's nothing preventing OpenAI from coming along
  and making a phone and taking everything from Apple, the iPhone company"** (rumors exist). And Apple
  enters the AI category **the way it always does — by making it work best with the iPhone** (Siri
  pulls from iMessage/Calendar/Photos "in a way the ChatGPT app never could"). (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2026-07-08]** The honest limit on Siri (from the beta): **"Siri doesn't excel at anything ChatGPT
  or Gemini doesn't already do** — we're not coding with it, **it doesn't work in the background** (no
  come-back-later large requests), and **it doesn't remember anything about you** without notes in the
  Notes app — so it has way less personal context. That's basic stuff you can already do with the
  ChatGPT app on the iPhone." (quote)
- **[2026-07-08]** The closing question: **do you think an AI company makes hardware good enough to pull
  people off the iPhone — or can Apple make its AI good enough to keep them on it?** (paraphrase)
