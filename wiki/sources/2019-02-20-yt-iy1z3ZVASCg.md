# Samsung Galaxy S10E Impressions! (@mkbhd, 2019-02-20)

## Summary
First look at the Galaxy S10e — explicitly framed as **"Samsung's answer to the iPhone XR"** and, like the XR,
"probably the one most people should get to save money." Walks the three trade-downs and argues most of them
barely matter.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-02-20] **The XR parallel — "the one most people should get."** "If the S10/S10+ parallel the XS/XS Max,
  the **S10e is the perfect parallel to the iPhone XR — same $750, not budget but lower, and probably the phone
  most people should get to save $150.**" A direct positioning call (fulfilling his XR-is-the-real-hit logic).
- [2019-02-20] **The three trade-downs, mostly minor.** **Display:** a flat 5.8" AMOLED (not the curved Infinity
  Edge) with slightly thicker bezels — "but **still way better than the XR's LCD** (a 500+ ppi AMOLED with the same
  hole punch)." **Fingerprint:** a **side power-button reader** instead of the ultrasonic in-display ("the best
  backup location — your finger rests there anyway"). **Cameras:** **dual instead of triple** (keeps the dual-
  aperture primary + the ultra-wide, drops the telephoto — "**great, because most people rarely intentionally use
  the telephoto anyway**"). Keeps IP68 + expandable storage; a smaller 3,100 mAh battery (still bigger than the
  S9's).
- [2019-02-20] **The spec cut echoes the XR.** "Like the XR did with the SoC/RAM: same **Snapdragon 855**, but
  **6 GB RAM instead of 8** — "**most people won't notice the difference; that's more about future-proofing.**" A
  consistent "you don't need the max spec" read.
- [2019-02-20] **The verdict — save your money.** "Sacrifices in three areas (display, fingerprint, cameras), but
  **still pretty good at all of them.** Like the XR, this feels like **the one most people should get to save the
  money.**" His recurring value-first steer.

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "If the S10 and S10+ are perfect matches for the iPhone XS and XS Max, this S10e is the perfect parallel to the
> iPhone XR — starting with the $750 price."
> "It drops the telephoto — which I think is great, because most people rarely intentionally use the telephoto
> camera anyway."
> "Most people won't notice the difference between six and eight gigs of RAM — that's more about future-proofing."
