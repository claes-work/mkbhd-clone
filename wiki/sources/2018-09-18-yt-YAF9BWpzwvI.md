# Apple iPhone Xs Review: A (S)mall Step Up! (@mkbhd, 2018-09-18)

## Summary
The full iPhone XS / XS Max review — a "classic Apple S update" (spec bump, no new design). Best material: the
insight that **Apple maintaining old phones well is a "curse it put on itself"** (iOS 12 made the year-old X so
fast the XS feels barely different), the improved depth-map portrait mode, and the buy-a-real-camera advice.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-09-18] **"A classic S upgrade" (+ a naming jab).** "You'd have a hard time telling the XS from the X — new
  gold color, two more visible antenna lines (an oddly **asymmetric bottom speaker grille** — you'd think Apple
  would find a better place), a slightly bigger camera module, and IP68 (up from IP67). Everything else new is
  internal." Aside: "**iPhone XS Max — 'extra small max' — terrible name.**"
- [2018-09-18] **THE INSIGHT — "a curse Apple put on itself."** "The A12 makes the XS extremely fast — **back on the
  responsiveness throne.** But **iOS 12 is also extremely fast on last year's iPhone X** — I'm not noticing a major
  speed difference. It's kind of a **curse Apple put on itself: by maintaining and updating phones so well, it makes
  people with an older phone more satisfied and less likely to upgrade.**" A genuinely sharp, durable observation
  about software longevity and the upgrade cycle.
- [2018-09-18] **Camera — "firmly the second-best smartphone camera" (behind the Pixel 2).** "A real upgrade — a
  bit more than I expected: A+ colors, A+ dynamic range (the more aggressive **Smart HDR**), corner-to-corner
  sharp, good low light. Firmly **#2 behind the Pixel 2.**" (Wants a side-by-side with the Pixel 3 / RED Hydrogen.)
  Camera-ranking continuity.
- [2018-09-18] **The improved portrait mode — teach-the-mechanism.** "The dual cameras build a **depth map** and
  blur **by distance** — farther things blur more — instead of a crude mask + uniform blur. Much more realistic;
  **clearly better than Samsung's generic Live Focus blur.** You can edit the f-stop after the fact." Still "hit or
  miss, so I don't use it much — but when it hits, it's damn good." Plus **best-in-class video** (4K60 + new stereo
  recording).
- [2018-09-18] **The buy-a-real-camera advice.** "**If you have an iPhone X, you don't need the XS** — upgrade to
  iOS 12 (free) and, if you've got a $1000 hole in your pocket itching for a better camera, **buy an RX100 with that
  money — it's a much better camera. Problem solved.**" A characteristic value-honest redirect.
- [2018-09-18] **The Max is "my favorite iPhone ever" — because he's a big-phone person.** "A **6.5" display in a
  body smaller than the Note 9** — that's all I have to say to make it my favorite iPhone ever. I've been a
  big-phone person as long as I can remember." Names the downsides honestly (pocket space, one-handed reach). Also:
  early **iOS-12 notch graphic quirks** at the new resolution (not crashes — will be fixed as devs update); hopes
  **in-glass fingerprint** reaches mainstream flagships next gen (rumored OnePlus 6T / S10 / iPhone 11).
