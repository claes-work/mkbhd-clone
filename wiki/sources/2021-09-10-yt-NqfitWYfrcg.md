# Behind the 1000 Mile Race: Day 1! (@TheStudio, 2021-09-10)

> ⚠️ ATTRIBUTION: **Marques narrates the Tesla portions in the first person**; the Mach-E and Audi
> gas-control cars are driven/narrated by team members (Brandon, Andrew, David, Adam, Tim, Hayato).
> Only Marques's Tesla/experiment lines are subject data. This is BTS of a major MKBHD EV video.

## Summary
The team runs a 1,000-mile road-trip experiment — **Tesla Model S Plaid vs Ford Mustang Mach-E vs
a gas Audi Q5 control** — to measure how much time charging adds to a road trip. Day 1 becomes a
running case study in why non-Tesla charging is painful. Marques drives the Plaid.

## Key claims (Marques-attributed only)
- [2021-09-10] **BIOGRAPHY — owns a Tesla Model S Plaid:** "Tesla Model S Plaid, you're probably
  familiar, I've already done videos about this car, 21-inch wheels, 350-mile range on 100%."
  (biography — refines the Model S ownership from [[2022-02-07-yt-iPhywv1Y52c]]; the Plaid is the
  car in the fleet by late 2021) (ev-cars)
- [2021-09-10] **The experiment's design (methodology):** same start point (a spot near the studio
  with a gas station + Tesla Supercharger + Electrify America charger), same route (Ithaca / Lake
  Placid / Niagara / Scranton), full tank + full battery. "A little unscientific but we've got a
  set of rules." Goal: **"not trying to win by going fast — win by having the best overall road
  trip,"** hard limit 10 mph over. (ev-cars, creator-business)
- [2021-09-10] **Driving method:** runs Waze alongside Tesla's own nav ("Tesla Waze knows the way I
  drive"); autopilots "probably more than I ever have," navigate-on-autopilot; arrives at chargers
  at low state-of-charge for peak charging; watches hill efficiency vs regen. (ev-cars)
- [2021-09-10] **THE CHARGING-NETWORK THESIS (definitive documentation ★):** "we've already figured
  out why it's so annoying to have a network that isn't the Tesla network." Electrify America
  chargers repeatedly **out of order, mislabeled, app/RFID-gated, in Walmart parking lots with
  nothing around**; the car routed them to broken stalls it couldn't see were broken. By contrast,
  Tesla Superchargers "know the ones that don't work" and sit near amenities (Wawa/Starbucks).
  **"This is not a reflection on the car — there's a reflection on the charging network."** (ev-cars)
