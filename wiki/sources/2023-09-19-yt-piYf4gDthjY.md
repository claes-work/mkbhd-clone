# iPhone 15/15 Pro Unboxing and Second Look! (@mkbhd, 2023-09-19)

## Summary
An unboxing + six-days-in "second look" at the whole iPhone 15 lineup. The colors are **super weak** — the blue 15
is **"barely blue"** ("1% blue"), the Plus's yellow is "incredibly pale" — and the box now ships a **braided USB-C
cable** (a first). On the Pro he calls the **Natural Titanium "the one to get"**: the brushed titanium rails are
~10% lighter (noticeable, welcome), **but "I was wrong" that the brushed finish would hide fingerprints** — it still
shows them on dark colors, and the new PVD coating means a deep scratch reveals the light titanium underneath (so
light colors will hide gashes better). He documents the **USB-C charging handshake rules** in detail (a USB-C iPhone
always charges a Lightning one; two USB-C iPhones negotiate which is lower and the higher one becomes a battery bank;
Android phones with USB-PD do the same handshake, ~4.5 W — enough to rescue a phone or charge accessories). The
**Action button** (Pro) has no restrictions — its best use is **Siri Shortcuts** ("lets you do anything," open any
app with zero lag) — **and he maps his to the Google Assistant, "the best assistant on the iPhone."** Cameras: the
new lens coating **did not fix the nighttime flare**; the 1x/1.2x/1.5x is actually **Super-Res-Zoom presets**, not a
pointless crop. Verdict preview: the **base 15 is a bigger upgrade than it looks** because it inherits last year's
Pro features; the Pro is more interesting (genuinely new stuff, slightly bigger batteries). (dbrand Ghost case:
"engineered to never yellow.")

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2023-09-19] **"Barely blue" — and Natural Titanium is the one to get.** "'I'm renaming this blue "barely blue" —
  it's barely giving hints of blue. The colors are super weak across the board. On the Pro, Natural Titanium is the
  one to get — the best in person, and a deep scratch reveals the light titanium underneath, so dark colors could
  look rough if you drop it caseless.'"
- [2023-09-19] **I was wrong — brushed titanium still shows fingerprints.** "'The rails switched from shiny stainless
  to brushed titanium — ~10% lighter, enough for me to notice and enjoy. I thought the brushed finish would
  eliminate fingerprints. Absolutely not — I was wrong. It's less than stainless, but on the darker colors you can
  totally see hand oils marking up the sides.'"
- [2023-09-19] **The USB-C charging handshake rules.** "'A USB-C iPhone always gives battery to a Lightning iPhone.
  Two USB-C iPhones do a handshake — they figure out which has the lower battery and the higher one becomes a battery
  bank. Android phones with USB Power Delivery do the same (~4.5 W out — not fast, but ideal for accessories or
  rescuing a phone in a pinch).'"
- [2023-09-19] **The Action button — Siri Shortcuts does anything; I map it to Google Assistant.** "'The Action
  button has no restrictions I could find — it does anything. My favorite by a mile is the Siri Shortcuts function
  because it lets you do anything else — open any app with zero lag, stack location-dependent functions. For me,
  I'm mapping mine to the Google Assistant, the best assistant available on the iPhone. (Apple, let me map a double
  or triple press too.)'"
- [2023-09-19] **The base 15 is a bigger upgrade than it looks.** "'The lens coating didn't fix the nighttime flare
  — sadly still there. And the 1x/1.2x/1.5x is actually Super-Res-Zoom presets, not just a digital crop. The base
  iPhone 15 is a bigger improvement than it seems on paper — nothing's actually new, but it's bringing last year's
  Pro features (Dynamic Island, the A16, 48 MP), so it's a very solid phone.'"
