# OnePlus 6T Review: New Design, Same Price! (@mkbhd, 2018-10-29)

## Summary
The OnePlus 6T review — "the OnePlus 6 in a prettier, higher-tech package with a bigger battery for the same
price." Notable for the in-display fingerprint reader (with a teach-the-mechanism nitpick), even OnePlus dropping
the headphone jack (he asked them why), and the value story.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-10-29] **The framing — "an incremental update / design refresh, same price."** Six new things: a bigger
  6.4" OLED + smaller chin; a **teardrop notch**; the in-display fingerprint; a bigger battery; **no headphone
  jack**; and **128 GB base for the same $549.** "OnePlus sticking to their guns of competitive pricing." Same
  SD845, OxygenOS, "fast and smooth" (the reason he switched from the Pixel).
- [2018-10-29] **In-display fingerprint — teach-the-mechanism nitpick.** "Faster than the first-gen Vivo I tested,
  but **still slower than Touch ID — somewhere in between; leave your finger a beat and you're in.** Here's the
  catch: **the optical sensor shines a light on your finger, so it goes to full brightness to read — annoying at
  night** (it bleeds around your finger into your face). Super minor, but the kind of thing you notice using it
  daily." Honest, mechanism-grounded.
- [2018-10-29] **Battery champ — the real win.** "The biggest on-paper upgrade: **3300 → 3700 mAh.** In practice
  **5+ hours screen-on, once nearly 6 with 10% left — my battery champ this year so far**" (helped by the 1080p
  OLED + clean software + the famously fast charging). A concrete endorsement.
- [2018-10-29] **Even OnePlus drops the jack — "I asked them why."** "It's 2018; **only Samsung and LG are left on
  the headphone-jack train.** Interesting from OnePlus, who usually listen to users — so **I asked them directly:
  the bigger battery and the under-screen fingerprint took up so much space they felt it made sense to drop the
  jack.**" (A moment of silence.) His get-the-actual-reasoning instinct.
- [2018-10-29] **The verdict — easy recommendation.** "Same great camera (software improvements + a new night
  mode, also coming to the OnePlus 6); no wireless charging despite the glass back. **The OnePlus 6 in a prettier,
  higher-tech package with a bigger battery for the same price — can't really go wrong. Easy recommend.**" Notes
  the first big US carrier push (T-Mobile/Verizon).

## Notable verbatim quotes
