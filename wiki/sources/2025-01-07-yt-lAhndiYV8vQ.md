# The Assisted-Driving Tier List!

## Summary

Marques runs a controlled **back-to-back assisted-driving comparison** — five systems (Ford
BlueCruise, GM Super Cruise, Mercedes Drive Pilot, Tesla FSD, BMW Assisted Driving) on the same
route, same time of day, same traffic — and tier-lists them. **Tesla FSD lands at A-tier**, its
decisive edge being that **it works on essentially every road** (it engages from the parking spot),
while Super Cruise is B and the rest cluster at C. His overarching point: impressive progress, but
**"none of these are full 'forget about it, hands off, seat facing backward' good yet."** Fully
Marques-narrated.

## Key claims

- **[2025-01-07]** Methodology: "same exact route, same time of day, same traffic, same everything…
  back to back to back." (Test run July 2024; factors in off-camera FSD v13.2.1 and Super Cruise
  from a Lyriq.) (quote)
- **[2025-01-07]** ⭐ **Tesla FSD = A** (top): "the one that's iterating by far the most… it basically
  works on every single road," engages leaving the parking lot, "very good human-like reactions to
  traffic slowdowns and getting cut off"; assertive mode is "like a seriously capable New Jersey
  driver / Mad Max." Only real fault: **reads speed limits from signs (visual) so it lags** behind
  GPS-based rivals. **Not S-tier** — "it's not perfect, it has room to grow, it's a little skittish
  sometimes." (quote)
- **[2025-01-07]** **GM Super Cruise = B**: changes lanes on its own confidently, closer follow
  distance, works on more roads than Ford's, "acts like a human." (paraphrase)
- **[2025-01-07]** **Ford BlueCruise = C** ("fine, natural, highway-only, very reluctant to change
  lanes"); **Mercedes Drive Pilot = C** (great HUD, but "often stopped auto-steer" with no chime, "late
  and wide with turns"); **BMW Assisted Driving = C** (only had the non-"Plus" version; "asked for a
  full wiggle on the wheel every 5 seconds — that got annoying"). (paraphrase)
- **[2025-01-07]** ⭐ Big-picture verdict: **"Tesla has the major advantage of just working on more
  roads… but when it comes down to getting on the highway and turning it on, all these systems are very
  capable. It's impressive we've gotten this far, but it's nowhere near done — none of these are full,
  hands-off, no-steering-wheel good yet."** (quote)

## Notable verbatim quotes

- "Tesla has the major advantage of just working on more roads… you can pull out of a parking spot and enable it and it'll navigate out of the parking lot."
- "Assertive mode was like Mad Max out here — a seriously capable New Jersey driver."
- "None of these are full 'forget about it, seat facing the other direction, hands off, no steering wheel' levels of good yet. We're not there yet."
