# The Most Expensive iPhone in the World! (@mkbhd, 2019-12-27)

## Summary
A showcase of a **~$100,000 Caviar gold-and-diamond iPhone 11 Pro** (with a functional mechanical-watch
complication on the back). "Purely a showcase of something ridiculous you don't see every day." The durable beat
is his **"why does this exist? because they can — and a small group appreciates the mechanical-engineering
mastery."**
