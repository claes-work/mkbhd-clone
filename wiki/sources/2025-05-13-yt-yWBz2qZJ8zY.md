# Samsung Galaxy S25 Edge Impressions: Nobody Asked! (@mkbhd, 2025-05-13)

## Summary
A first look at the **Samsung Galaxy S25 Edge — the ultra-thin (5.8 mm) addition to the S25 line** — opening wry:
**"somewhere out there was somebody waiting for the thinnest possible S25... this person exists."** The positioning
question resolves sensibly: it's **not a hyped ultra-premium** but sits **between the base S25/Plus and the Ultra** —
**"an S25 Ultra light."** It borrows the Ultra's **titanium rails and its 200 MP main sensor** (+ software), the Plus's
6.7" 1440p 120 Hz screen, and the same Snapdragon 8 Elite — **but physics extracts a price**: **no telephoto** and a
**3,900 mAh battery** ("bordering on worryingly small" vs the same-size Plus's 4,900), plus likely weaker cooling.
Notably, though, **holding it swayed him**: it looks incredible (the **floating-island camera is his favorite design in
the whole S25 lineup**), and at **163 g it's remarkably light for a big phone** — "much more convincing in the hand
than on video." Two industry beats: **"people aren't asking for thinner phones — most want a slightly thicker one for
more battery,"** so he expects worse battery life; and this **foreshadows the rumored iPhone 17 Air** — but **Samsung
shipped first, which "means a lot to Samsung"**, and Samsung kept an ultra-wide, a physical SIM tray, and its flagship
chip where the iPhone reportedly won't. On the "everyone uses a case, so why thin?" objection: a thin phone + a thin
case/skin still ends up thinner/lighter than a normal phone with a case. The target customer: someone stepping up from
the base phones who likes the Ultra camera but doesn't want the big, thick Ultra with an S Pen they'll never use —
"or someone who sees a shiny thin commercial and buys it." **$1,099** (between Plus and Ultra). "Nobody asked."

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2025-05-13] **An S25 Ultra light, positioned between Plus and Ultra.** "'The positioning makes sense: not a hyped
  ultra-premium, but between the base S25/Plus and the Ultra — an S25 Ultra light. It has the Ultra's titanium rails
  and 200 MP main sensor plus software, the Plus's screen, and the same Snapdragon 8 Elite. $1,099, right between the
  Plus and Ultra.'"
- [2025-05-13] **Physics extracts a price — no telephoto, a worryingly small battery.** "'Make a phone this thin (5.8
  mm) and there's less room inside: no telephoto camera, and a 3,900 mAh battery — bordering on worryingly small vs the
  same-size Plus's 4,900 — plus likely weaker cooling. Samsung's not immune to physics. I expect worse battery life
  than the rest of the S25s.'"
- [2025-05-13] **People aren't asking for thinner phones — but holding it swayed me.** "'People aren't asking for
  thinner phones; most want a slightly thicker one for more battery. But holding it swayed me a little: it looks
  incredible (the floating-island camera is my favorite design in the whole S25 lineup), and at 163 g it's remarkably
  light for a big phone — much more convincing in the hand than on video.'"
- [2025-05-13] **It foreshadows the iPhone 17 Air — and Samsung did it first.** "'There are rumors of an ultra-thin
  iPhone 17 Air at the end of the year; I'm now more confident Apple will also position it mid-lineup. Both will have
  battery issues, but Samsung's is out first — which means a lot to Samsung — and Samsung kept an ultra-wide, a physical
  SIM tray, and its flagship chip, where the iPhone reportedly won't.'"
- [2025-05-13] **The case objection, answered.** "'Everyone puts a case on their phone, so why get a thin one? Because
  with how thin and light they've made this, a thin case or skin still ends up thinner and lighter than a normal phone
  with a case. If you specifically want a thinner, lighter phone without a smaller screen, this is theoretically for
  you.'"
