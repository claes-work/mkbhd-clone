# The M4 Mac Mini is Incredible! (@mkbhd, 2024-11-07)

## Summary
A review built around **"three insane things"** about the redesigned M4 Mac mini (from Apple's press-release-instead-of-
event M4 Mac rollout). **(1) It's hilariously tiny** — a **5"×5"×2" footprint** (smaller than his R5 with a lens, a
roll of duct tape, or the iPhone's footprint), looking more like a shrunk Mac Studio; still has Gigabit Ethernet, HDMI,
three Thunderbolt + two front USB-C (no SD reader, no USB-A). "If you tried to build a computer to match it, you'd get
one just as small but nowhere near as capable, or just as capable but nowhere near as small." (He floats a **conspiracy
theory**: someone at Apple whose sole job is one obnoxious thing per redesign — the notch, the Magic Mouse charge port,
and now **the power button on the bottom** — "dumb enough to get memed on without actually being an issue.") **(2) The
base model is one of the best deals in tech** — **$599** for the M4 with base memory **quietly doubled to 16 GB**, great
for everyday + medium workloads (coding, photo editing, tons of tabs). But he warns about the **Apple price ladder**:
"they walk you up tempting upgrade to tempting upgrade — 256 GB→1 TB is +$400, 10-gig Ethernet +$100; the upgrades alone
nearly cost another Mac mini. So the *base* model is the deal; once you need to spec it up, it's a normal deal." **(3)
The maxed version is the best portable-workstation Mac** — as someone who once **dragged an iMac Pro through airports in
a Pelican case**, the **$1,399 M4 Pro Mac mini** (Thunderbolt 5, three 6K displays) is the easiest way to pack an M4 Pro
in a bag; it posts **the highest CPU score of any desktop Mac ever** (beating the Mac Pro and Studios), with GPU at M1
Max levels. The throughline: Apple Silicon efficiency finally let Apple make the **impossibly-small powerful desktop
they've always wanted** (the Trashcan Mac Pro dream, minus the thermal disasters).

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2024-11-07] **Hilariously, unnecessarily tiny.** "'It's a 5×5×2 desktop computer — smaller than my R5 with a lens, a
  roll of duct tape, or the iPhone's footprint. If you tried to match it, you'd build something just as small but far
  less capable, or just as capable but far less small. It's a hilariously unnecessarily tiny computer. (Conspiracy
  theory: someone at Apple's sole job is one obnoxious thing per redesign — the notch, the Magic Mouse charge port, and
  now the power button on the bottom.)'"
- [2024-11-07] **The base model is one of the best deals in tech.** "'The $599 base M4 Mac mini is one of the best deals
  in tech — certainly the best of Apple's current offerings. The base memory quietly doubled to 16 GB, and the M4 is
  great at everyday and medium workloads (coding, photo editing, tons of Arc tabs). The M4 iMac is basically the same
  computer for $1,299 — so you save $700 and bring your own screen.'"
- [2024-11-07] **Beware the Apple price ladder — the base is the deal.** "'Apple builds a price ladder: a tempting
  upgrade sits right above the base, then another above that, walking you up to spend as much as possible. On the mini,
  256 GB→1 TB is +$400 and 10-gig Ethernet +$100 — the upgrades alone nearly cost another Mac mini. That's why I
  specifically say the *base* model is such a good deal; once you know you need to spec it up (and you must, before
  buying — it's not upgradeable after), it becomes a normal deal.'"
- [2024-11-07] **The maxed M4 Pro is the best portable-workstation Mac.** "'As someone who used to drag an iMac Pro
  through airports in a Pelican case, the $1,399 M4 Pro Mac mini is the easiest way to pack an M4 Pro in a travel bag —
  the Studios and Mac Pro are still on M2. It posts the highest CPU score of any desktop Mac ever (beating the Mac Pro
  and Studios), with GPU at M1 Max levels — showing how these generations keep stacking.'"
- [2024-11-07] **Apple Silicon let Apple build the tiny-powerful desktop it always wanted.** "'This feels like one of
  those impossibly small computers Apple has always wanted to make (remember the Trashcan Mac Pro?) — a workstation-
  class machine cooled by a single fan. That failed with Intel and the thin overheating i9 MacBook Pros. But Apple
  Silicon got efficient enough to actually pull it off. Nobody asked for the Mac mini to be even smaller — except the
  people in the Spaceship Campus. Well played.'"
