# Tesla Self Driving vs Everyday Roads! (@mkbhd, 2022-12-14)

## Summary
An **uncut, real-world test** of Tesla's Full Self-Driving beta: Marques sets a destination and lets the
car drive him to the studio through New Jersey surface streets and highways, narrating and intervening as
little as possible ("I will not cut anything out of this drive"). The verdict: **it's comfortable on
highways and "not much else."** The single most common reason he had to take over was **embarrassment, not
safety** — the car drives like a nervous "old lady," creeping, braking for phantom reasons, and refusing
merges a human would take instantly. He logs **three disengagements** (highway construction cones twice, a
stopped-hazards truck). Structural notes: **Tesla went vision-only (cameras, no radar, no lidar)**, tested
here in easy, well-lit conditions — he wonders aloud whether FSD "takes a meaningful hit" in snow/fog/rain.
The Robo-taxi promise (Elon: cars driving themselves cross-country / earning money like an Uber while you're
away) is **"a very far out promise"** — **"I would not feel comfortable sitting in the back seat of this
car without the ability to intervene."** Overall it's **"sloppy, but it works"** — and **"my heart rate is
definitely higher during this drive than the average normal drive."**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-12-14] **The main reason to intervene was embarrassment, not safety.** "'The number-one reason I've
  had to take over so far is not safety but actually just embarrassment — it slammed on the brakes seeing a
  car backing up; it puts the blinker on way too early; it drives like a cautious old lady. It's a lot more
  making sure the car doesn't do weird things than it is being a relaxed passenger.'"
- [2022-12-14] **Great on highways, not much else.** "'The bread and butter is highway driving — I feel much
  more comfortable with what it does on very obvious, well-lined roads with no construction. On surface
  streets and unusual situations (a single cone blocking a multi-lane merge, a hard 2→5→2 merge) it just
  didn't seem to pick things up, and I'd take over. Highway driving is typically its forte.'"
- [2022-12-14] **Vision-only, tested in easy conditions.** "'Tesla moved their systems entirely to
  vision-based — just cameras, no radar, no lidar. We're doing this in comfortable, well-lit conditions. But
  what if it's snowing, or whiteout fog, or heavy rain where it's hard to see the lines? I wonder if full
  self-driving takes a meaningful hit — if it just works worse there.'"
- [2022-12-14] **It sees more than a human — instantly.** "'The car sees a lot — it can see three, four,
  five cars ahead and the brake lights on all of them, so it knows when cars are braking before a human
  would even recognize it, instantaneously. Theoretically it can respond to things faster than I could.'"
- [2022-12-14] **The Robo-taxi promise is far out; I wouldn't sit in the back.** "'Elon's promise was these
  becoming robotaxis — you commute, then the car drives like an Uber while you're not using it. If
  self-driving were good enough, that could work. I don't think it's that good yet — I would not feel
  comfortable sitting in the back seat without the ability to intervene.'"
- [2022-12-14] **Sloppy, but it works.** "'A big wide lane, a little sloppy — but it works. And I think
  that's kind of the motto of full self-driving beta right now: it's kind of sloppy, but it works.'"
