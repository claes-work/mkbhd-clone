# The Flagship vs Midrange Debate: Galaxy A54 Review! (@mkbhd, 2023-05-01)

## Summary
A methodical flagship-vs-midrange breakdown using the **$400–450 Galaxy A54** against the **$1,200 S23 Ultra** he'd
been daily-driving. His framing note: he **used to say "cheap phones are getting good AND good phones are getting
cheap," but can no longer stand by the second half** (flagships broke the $1,000+ barrier) — the first half still
holds. He scores the A54 against the flagship on his **Five Pillars — build, display, performance, camera, battery —
as percentages**: build **90%** (both glass+aluminum; the flat A54 vs the curved Ultra), display **80%** (a fine
1080p 120→60 Hz AMOLED vs the Ultra's 1440p/1750-nit/LTPO), performance **60%** (the mid-range Exynos 1380 — "a feel
thing," fine for daily tasks, noticeably behind when you multitask heavy apps), camera **70%** ("it's fine — I mean
it"; the tell is **a macro over a zoom — "in what world do you want a macro camera over a zoom"** — the obvious spot
they saved money), battery **90%** (similar daily life; slower 25 W charging, no wireless). Averaged: **~78% of the
flagship for a third of the price** — "a pretty good deal." The recurring analogy: **the A54 is the Corolla to the
Ultra's Supra** — same company, but Corolla buyers were never cross-shopping the Supra, so side-by-side comparison
mainly highlights differences most buyers won't feel. **"Mid-rangers today mitigate the law of diminishing returns,
matching the flagship's finesse for a fraction of the funds."**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2023-05-01] **Can't say "good phones are getting cheap" anymore — but cheap phones are still getting good.** "'I
  used to say cheap phones are getting good and good phones are getting cheap. But flagships broke the $1,000
  barrier, so I can no longer stand by the second half — the first half is still pretty compelling.'"
- [2023-05-01] **The Five Pillars, scored as percentages.** "'I break it into my five pillars of a great smartphone —
  build, display, performance, camera, battery. Nail all five and it's a good phone, period; as price drops you
  expect sacrifices. The A54: ~90% of the build, 80% of the display, 60% of the performance, 70% of the camera, 90%
  of the battery.'"
- [2023-05-01] **A macro over a zoom is where they saved money.** "'The camera is fine — I mean it. But the tell:
  it's a 50 MP main + ultrawide + a 5 MP macro. In what world do you want a macro camera over a zoom? That's the
  obvious version of making it *look* like the expensive phone while saving money — still something there, but not a
  zoom.'"
- [2023-05-01] **~78% of the flagship for a third of the price.** "'Average my assessments over 500 points and you
  get about 78% of the flagship for a third of the price — that seems like a pretty good deal. There are little
  extras the flagship keeps (better haptics, bigger speakers, a stylus, DeX), but the core experience is close.'"
- [2023-05-01] **The Corolla-vs-Supra angle.** "'This comparison always feels weird because most people buying the
  A54 have never touched a $1,200 phone — it's like comparing a Toyota Corolla to a Supra: same company, but Corolla
  buyers weren't cross-shopping the Supra. Me putting them side by side just highlights differences most buyers
  won't feel. Mid-rangers today mitigate the law of diminishing returns — matching the flagship's finesse for a
  fraction of the funds.'"
