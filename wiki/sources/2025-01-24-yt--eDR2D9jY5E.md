## Summary

Co-host **Miles** reviews the **McLaren Artura** (McLaren's V6 plug-in-hybrid supercar) and argues
it's underrated: the 2025-model-year cars fixed the rocky-launch build-quality issues, the new
120°-V6 + axial-flux e-motor deliver ~691hp with smooth "torque infill" that makes it **the easiest,
least-intimidating McLaren to daily-drive**, and the Bowers & Wilkins system + hydraulic steering
are standouts. Attributed entirely to Miles.

## Key claims (all Miles — context only, NOT persona data)

- **[2025-01-24]** *(Miles)* The Artura depreciated to ~half its cost on the secondhand market in ~2.5
  years; the 2025-MY cars have "zero build-quality issues" and are "the most complete, most
  compromise-free McLaren road car they've ever made." (paraphrase)
- **[2025-01-24]** *(Miles)* Powertrain: 3.0L 120°-angle twin-turbo V6 (a brand-new architecture,
  ~zero carryover) + a 94hp axial-flux motor + 7.4kWh battery (~13–14mi real EV range) = 691hp / 531
  lb-ft; the wide-V lets the engine sit low for a better center of gravity; the e-motor spins backward
  for reverse (no reverse gear). McLaren offered free ECU tunes to bring early cars to 691hp.
  (paraphrase)
- **[2025-01-24]** *(Miles)* The hybrid "torque infill" gives instantaneous, smooth, linear
  acceleration → **"the easiest McLaren to drive… not as scary as other McLarens,"** genuinely
  daily-drivable in LA traffic. B&W speakers "top tier… even better than the best Porsche"; hydraulic
  steering "one of the most unique things about McLaren." (paraphrase)
