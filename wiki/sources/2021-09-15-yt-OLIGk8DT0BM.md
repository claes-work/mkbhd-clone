> ⚠️ ATTRIBUTION: **Team live-reaction** to Apple's Sept-2021 event (iPhone 13, iPad mini, Apple

## Summary

his Apple-event media criticism — is the persona keeper.

## Key claims (Marques-attributed only)
- [2021-09-15] **"Grandiose event, incremental product" (recurring media criticism ★):** "Apple's
  events are all very large and grandiose, but the nature of smartphones is they're small year-
  over-year increments. So it's this large grandiose event for a small year-over-year increment —
  it seems like it doesn't match." (consumer-tech-culture)
- [2021-09-15] **iPhone 13 verdict — "it's an S upgrade":** "same design, slightly improved cameras,
  A15 instead of A14 — it's an S upgrade." But "better than last year: we did get ProMotion, better
  cameras, wider apertures, bigger sensors, sensor-shift OIS. I'm excited to try the cameras."
  (tech-reviews)
- [2021-09-15] **Skeptical of the Cinematic-mode demos:** wants to rewatch the focus-pulling demos —
  "that's Apple's perfect use case and I didn't think they were amazing." Voted **no** on 120 Hz in
  the team bet (and lost). (tech-reviews)
- [2021-09-15] **iPad mini:** likes that it inherited the Pro design — "I like small things that are
  high quality." (consumer-tech-culture)
