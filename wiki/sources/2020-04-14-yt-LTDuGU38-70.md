# OnePlus 8 Pro Review: Finally a Flagship! (@mkbhd, 2020-04-14)

## Summary
The OnePlus 8 Pro review — "they **finally made a flagship.**" The two historical OnePlus nitpicks (a not-quite-
flagship camera; missing "fringe" features) are fixed: an improved camera, **wireless charging, and IP68.** His
new daily driver — with a couple of pointed snags.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-04-14] **"Finally a flagship" — the nitpicks fixed.** "'The two OnePlus nitpicks were always (1) the camera
  not quite measuring up and (2) leaving out fringe features (wireless charging, IP68). **They finally added an
  improved camera, wireless charging, and an official IP68.** A bonafide, complete-feeling flagship — at $900, so
  I get to nitpick.'"
- [2020-04-14] **HOT TAKE — "the pop-up camera was better than the hole-punch."** "'Switching back to a hole-punch
  is a **downgrade** — the pop-up gave a **fully uninterrupted screen** and, blocked when retracted, a **security
  benefit**; we've had **no durability problems in two years** of pop-ups. Why remove it? To look like other
  flagships, save money, or guarantee IP68 — maybe all three.'" A dated, contrarian preference (cf. his selfie-
  camera tier list, [[2020-03-17-yt-ASwVyfebu3E]]).
- [2020-04-14] **The proprietary-wireless-charging snag.** "'**30 W wireless charging — but only with their $70
  stand** (proprietary tech; any other Qi phone charges slower on it, and the 8 Pro charges at just **5 W on any
  other Qi charger**). So I'll stick to wired warp charge + my Pixel Stand for overnight. Glad to have wireless,
  but that's a snag.'"
- [2020-04-14] **The camera — flagship-tier, just short of Pixel/iPhone.** "'A **flagship-quality camera, in line
  with the S20 and P40, just short of the Pixel and iPhone.** Big 1/1.4" sensor → sharp photos but **fringing on
  close subjects** (par for the course now); no AF issues; good night mode; **no 8K** (you're not touching the
  iPhone for video). People took "camera was the worst part last year" as a horrific flaw — really the rest was
  just so good.'"
- [2020-04-14] **The two free ideas + "my new daily driver."** "'Flagships with 120 Hz should also offer a **90 Hz
  middle mode** to save battery (like the gaming phones). Still my **least favorite is the curved display**
  (accidental touches — Samsung's perfected the flat-ish balance this year). **This is my new daily driver** — and
  the setup work to make it "mine" is far shorter than on a Samsung.'"

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "Hot take: the pop-up camera was better than the hole punch, and this is a downgrade."

> flagship would."
