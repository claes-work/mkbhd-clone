# The Truth About the Escobar Folding Phone! (@mkbhd, 2020-03-10)

## Summary
An investigation/exposé of the "Escobar Fold" — a $400 folding phone that turns out to be **a Galaxy Fold with gold
tinfoil taped on**, sold by a company that **ships to reviewers and doesn't ship to most buyers.** A strong
creator-ethics source: he **bought two** ($750, got nothing) to investigate, and states his purchase/integrity
policy outright.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-03-10] **The scam mechanics.** "'The Escobar Fold is **an actual $1,800 Galaxy Fold with gold tinfoil
  adhesived on** (peel the hinge foil and the Samsung logo is right there), sold for $400. **My theory: they scan
  orders for names of YouTubers / tech-media people and ship those, and just don't ship the rest** — how they
  "afford" it. The marketing (fake Cameo celebrity endorsements from Chris Hansen and Paula Abdul's ex; a sledge-
  hammer "our IP Samsung" video) is the best part.'"
- [2020-03-10] **The self-experiment — "I took the hit so you don't have to."** "'I quietly **bought one in Dec
  ($350), never got it** (stuck "in production"); then **bought a second under a different name ($400)** — so
  **$750, nothing in return.** Then four days after I tweeted "0/10, didn't get the phone," a box showed up (they
  upgraded my order once my name was attached). **Don't buy anything these guys are associated with.**'"
- [2020-03-10] **INTEGRITY — "I buy pretty much everything I review."** "'People think I get everything free — I
  get **review units and make first-impressions videos with those, but I end up buying pretty much everything I
  review.** (That's why I hadn't reviewed the Moto Razr yet — I sent the review unit back and my bought one was
  delayed.)'" A clear statement of his purchase practice.
- [2020-03-10] **INTEGRITY — refusing guaranteed coverage.** "'They emailed asking for my address, but **wanted a
  guaranteed video in exchange for sending a phone — I wasn't willing to commit to that; that's not something we
  do.**'" A dated no-pay-for-coverage ethic (cf. his not-sponsored transparency thread).
- [2020-03-10] **The reviewer's-lens reframe.** "'I usually focus on the final product — but this product is just
  the Galaxy Fold I've already reviewed. **The interesting part is the behind-the-scenes company trying to sell
  and not-ship them.** Take any **preferential treatment** you get with a giant grain of salt.'" A media-literacy
  note about review-unit incentives.
