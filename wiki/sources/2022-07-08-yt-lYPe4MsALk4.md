# The World's Largest Smartphone Camera! Xiaomi 12S Ultra (@mkbhd, 2022-07-08)

## Summary
A camera-first look at the Xiaomi 12S Ultra — a China-region phone built entirely around **the world's largest
smartphone camera sensor** (a 1-inch **Sony IMX989**, the first one **purpose-built for a phone** rather than
repurposed — beating Samsung to the punch). The whole phone is aimed at one-upping Samsung's "Ultra" (same name).
His **hot take: it has the highest ceiling of any smartphone camera he's ever seen — "hit or miss, but the hits hit
hard."** The huge sensor's light-gathering makes **shutter speed and autofocus S-tier** (it freezes fast-moving
subjects); **Leica optics/color science** help (authentic vs vibrant "photographic styles"). The **cleats photo**
("maybe the most impressive photo I've ever seen from a phone"). The weaknesses are classic big-sensor: **close-up
fringing**, **dramatic color inconsistency between lenses**, and **a zoom-video viewfinder bug** (the viewfinder
crops the primary while the recording actually switches lenses — WYSIWYG breaks). Callback: **a 1-inch sensor
isn't one inch.** "Looks like a minion." Mission accomplished.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-07-08] **The world's largest sensor — first purpose-built for a phone.** "'Almost half the back is the
  camera module — **the world's largest smartphone sensor, a 1-inch Sony IMX989, the first built for a phone**
  (not a repurposed 1-inch sensor), beating Samsung's coming ISOCELL to the punch. **The whole point is to one-up
  Samsung's Ultra in every way and slap the same name on it.** (And yes — **a "1-inch sensor" isn't actually one
  inch**; see my other video.)'"
- [2022-07-08] **The hot take — the highest ceiling I've seen; hit or miss, but the hits hit hard.** "'My hot take:
  **it has the highest ceiling of any camera I've ever seen in a smartphone — it doesn't always hit, but when it
  does, it's amazing.** The huge sensor's light-gathering makes **shutter speed and autofocus consistently S-tier**
  (it freezes fast-moving plants/kids/pets and nails focus — super confidence-inspiring). **The cleats photo — a
  random off-center snap of my feet — might be the most impressive photo I've ever seen from a phone** (tack-sharp,
  natural shallow depth of field with a beautiful roll-off, no portrait mode).'"
- [2022-07-08] **Leica optics + color science.** "'There's real **Leica help on the optics/coatings** (cutting
  fringing and chromatic aberration) — and it won a blind Twitter comparison against the S22 Ultra (better bloom
  control, low noise). Leica "authentic vs vibrant" looks are like the iPhone's photographic styles (neither is
  particularly great to my eye).'"
- [2022-07-08] **The weaknesses — fringing, color inconsistency, and the zoom-video bug.** "'Classic big-sensor
  issues: **ugly fringing when you get too close** (it auto-switches to a worse-looking ultrawide macro); and the
  **color science is dramatically inconsistent between lenses** (switch 4.9x→5.2x and exposure/contrast jump). And
  a genuine bug: **shooting zoom video, the viewfinder just crops the primary while the recording actually switches
  to the telephoto** — so what you see isn't what you record (a WYSIWYG break; fixable in software).'"
- [2022-07-08] **The rest — very "Ultra," and mission accomplished.** "'The rest is aimed straight at Samsung: an
  8+ Gen 1 (months ahead of Samsung), a world-class 6.7" 1440p 120 Hz LTPO (curved sides I'm starting to hate),
  67 W / 50 W wireless / 10 W reverse — all better than Samsung's flagship — for ~$900 in its regions. **"Looks
  like a minion."** As for its mission to out-Ultra Samsung — **mission accomplished; the hits hit so hard, I love
  this thing.**'"
