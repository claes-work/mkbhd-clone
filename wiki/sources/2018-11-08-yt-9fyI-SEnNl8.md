# Let's Talk About the Foldable Smartphone! (@mkbhd, 2018-11-08)

## Summary
A teach-the-mechanism/commentary essay as folding phones first appear (Royole FlexPai, Samsung's Infinity Flex).
The core is a framework for how to think about a brand-new, unpolished form factor: **"why though?" — but give it
time to find its use and iterate**, argued via the **Note Edge precedent.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-11-08] **"It's happening — but why though?"** "**This is dope tech — I like it.** That we can make a device
  that folds in half is really cool. **But why would I want to fold and unfold my phone into a tablet every single
  day?**" He holds genuine excitement and genuine skepticism at once — the honest first reaction.
- [2018-11-08] **The two debuts, ranked.** The **Royole FlexPai** ("roughly the ugliest thing I've ever seen — it
  barely folds, more of a bend; the folding process is brutal, graphics flickering" — likely rushed to "beat
  Samsung to first"); and **Samsung's Infinity Flex** (folds inward, shown dim on stage — "already looks better").
  Notes Android will natively support foldables (helps the graphics).
- [2018-11-08] **"It's a working prototype, not a finished product."** "Samsung showed it in the dark, in a case
  to hide the design — **they don't have a final design yet.** Gen-1 stuff, coming 'hopefully 2019'; don't expect
  anything too polished." Manages expectations honestly (cites the Verge's lights-on rehearsal footage showing the
  crease/bezels).
- [2018-11-08] **THE FRAMEWORK — the Note Edge precedent (reward experimentation, then iterate).** "**This is
  reminiscent of the bezel-less movement.** In 2014 Samsung took a weird step with the curved-edge **Note Edge** —
  it seemed useless, but **it caught on**, and soon every Samsung phone had an Infinity Display. **Gen-1 foldables
  might not be compelling, but if we give it time to find where it's useful and iterate, it could turn out great.**"
  A reusable model for judging first-gen form factors.
- [2018-11-08] **"Thank you for trying the crazy stuff."** "**I applaud you, Samsung** — they're so good at displays
  they can dedicate a team to trying crazy stuff (the Edge, folding OLEDs) and see if it catches on. Maybe this one
  will." His champion-the-risk-taker value, applied to Samsung's display R&D. (Joke close: "technically Apple had
  the first folding phone with the iPhone 6s Plus — weird flex but okay.")
