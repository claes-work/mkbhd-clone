## Key claims (Marques-attributed only)
- [2023-08-18] Why the Nevera: he's interested in it "**because it's all electric**" (the
  EV angle drives the coverage). His reaction after driving it: "**it's at the top of every
  performance list for a reason. It's kind of painful. But it's really fun.**" / "I have
  never been this close to God… I have found a new higher power. It is the Rimac Nevera."
  (ev-cars)
- [2023-08-18] **The McLaren P1 is his dream car** ("Your dream car, Marques?" "Yeah, it's
  right in front of me"). After driving it: "**best driving experience of my life… I think
  that lets me get my fix. I don't [need to buy one]. I'm good for a little while now.**" On
  the drive: "there is no car I've been in where you feel the road more than this thing…
  I feel like I can feel the paint." (ev-cars, biography)

  "**cars will never feel fast again**… that's what I mean by ruining fast cars. It's ruined

  wanting. (ev-cars, voice)
- [2023-08-18] **The electrification-of-flagships thesis:** "the newest Corvette flagship

  we've learned." (ev-cars — corroborates his "electrification of the supercar" thread)
- [2023-08-18] Small takes: the **Porsche 911 Turbo S is "the attainable version"** of that
