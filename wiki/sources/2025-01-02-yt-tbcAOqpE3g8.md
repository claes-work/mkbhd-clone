# A Hot Take on Jaguar's Rebrand

## Summary

While "the whole internet is talking about Jaguar" negatively, Marques offers a **contrarian
defense** of the polarizing rebrand + Type 00 concept. His lens: **electrification is a rare license
to fully rebrand**, and Jaguar — whose customer is "a 60-plus-year-old man" — needs it more than
anyone. He points to Hyundai and Kia as proof that an EV-era rebrand can transform a brand's image,
and argues that a design divisive enough to "bend necks" (like the Cybertruck) may itself be the
win. Fully Marques-narrated.

## Key claims

- **[2025-01-02]** ⭐ Thesis: **"Electrification has given a lot of these companies a unique opportunity
  to also rebrand themselves, and the ones that need it the most have been doing the best job of it —
  and Jaguar needs this more than ever."** (quote)
- **[2025-01-02]** The proof cases: **Hyundai** went from "reliable budget cars, nothing too special"
  to "tech-forward, design-forward" via the Ioniq 5/5N/6 (kept the logo, changed the image); **Kia**
  did the same with the EV6/EV6 GT/EV9 (plus a new logo). "That's what Jaguar really wants." (paraphrase)
- **[2025-01-02]** Jaguar's problem: "the typical Jaguar customer is a 60-plus-year-old man… they sell
  ~100,000 cars a year or less" and can't convert Porsche/Mercedes/Bentley buyers. They were **early to
  EVs with the i-Pace "but it wasn't that great — most i-Paces on the road now are Waymos."** (quote)
- **[2025-01-02]** On the Type 00 concept: he imagines the boardroom said **"leave nothing the same —
  not the logo, not the proportions, nothing is precious, we have no value in Old Jaguar anymore, we're
  a new company now."** Real targets quoted: ~430mi EPA range, ~1,000hp, shipping ~2026–27. (quote)
- **[2025-01-02]** ⭐ The **Cybertruck parallel**: "universally one of the most hated vehicles online…
  but when you see it in person it's a totally flipped ratio — people are curious, it bends necks. **If
  Jaguar can show up with a vehicle so different that when it drives by people actually care about it,
  that by itself is a huge win.**" (quote)

## Notable verbatim quotes
