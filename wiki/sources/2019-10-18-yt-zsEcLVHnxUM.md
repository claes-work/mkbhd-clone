# OnePlus 7T Pro: Tiny Tweaks to Excellence! (@mkbhd, 2019-10-18)

## Summary
A look at the OnePlus 7T Pro — "**maybe the smallest incremental update from phone to phone that I've ever seen**"
over the 7 Pro (one of his favorite phones of 2019). A short "here's literally everything that changed" video.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-10-18] **"The smallest upgrade I've ever seen."** "'Put them side by side and you might not know which is
  which. **The differences:** a slightly-lighter **haze blue** (I like it a bit less — less matte, more
  fingerprints), the laser-autofocus module **moved outside** the camera bump ('a psychological trick — it was
  already there'), Snapdragon **855+**, a **barely-larger 4,085 mAh** battery ('do we even count that?'), faster
  **Warp Charge 30T**, and the **macro mode.** That's it.'"
- [2019-10-18] **What stayed great (why it's still a top phone).** "'The rest is the **already-excellent 7 Pro**:
  the 6.67" 1440p **90 Hz notchless** display ('one of my favorites of the year'), the pop-up camera, UFS 3.0.
  My biggest dislike is still the **curved-edge screen** — not shocked they didn't change it; holding out hope for
  next version.'" Reiterates his flat-over-curved preference.
- [2019-10-18] **The "t-phone" logic — increase the lead.** "'A "t" version of a phone that came out **eight
  months ago** — a slight upgrade that **increases the lead a little on one of the best smartphone experiences of
  2019.** If you have their latest, you don't need to upgrade.'"
- [2019-10-18] **The McLaren Edition + the not-sold-in-US oddity.** "'The **McLaren Edition** ($100 more) has
  forged-carbon-fiber looks + papaya-orange accents (badass, but a fingerprint magnet — wish it were frosted) and
  the only spec bump: **12 GB RAM.** Strangely, they're **not selling the 7T Pro in North America** — pulling back
  from their US higher-priced push.'" A note on OnePlus's shifting market strategy.

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "It might be the smallest incremental update from phone to phone that I've ever seen."
> "Little tweaks here and there just increase the lead a little bit on one of the best smartphone experiences of
> 2019 for me."
