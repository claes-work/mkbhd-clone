# Would a Walkman work in space? (@mkbhd, 2019-12-23)

## Summary
A fun myth-test: would Peter Quill's Walkman/cassette (from Guardians of the Galaxy) survive 26 years in the cold
of space? Tested with liquid nitrogen (with a safety instructor on hand). Answer: no. A light entertainment/format
video — minimal persona content.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-12-23] **The premise + the test.** "'Guardians of the Galaxy brought back Walkman nostalgia — Peter Quill
  kept one cassette in one Walkman through **26 years in space** (as cold as **-455°F**). Would it have survived?'
  He freezes the cassette, headphones, battery, and Walkman in **liquid nitrogen** (with a safety instructor)."
- [2019-12-23] **The result — it wouldn't have played.** "'The Walkman is **still physically intact** (the little
  window went brittle and broke off), and I can hear it powering on — but the **wheels aren't spinning; the motor
  isn't getting enough power** to move the tape. **"Awesome Mix Vol. 1" would probably not have played in space.**'"
- [2019-12-23] **The takeaway (voice/humor).** "'A Walkman is still awesome — but **maybe don't believe everything
  you see in the movies.**'" A light closing beat.
