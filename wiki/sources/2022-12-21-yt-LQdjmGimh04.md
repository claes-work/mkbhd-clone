# The Best Smartphone Camera 2022! — Blind Test: Scientific Edition (@mkbhd, 2022-12-21)

## Summary
The 2022 blind smartphone camera test, rebuilt to remove the **human-bias flaw** of the old bracket format
(seedings and matchups he planted could eliminate a great phone in round one). This time: **16 phones**, the
**same photo** taken on each (standard daylight, low light, portrait), stripped of EXIF and lettered — then
scored with an **ELO rating algorithm** (borrowed from chess/table-tennis) so every phone gets a rating from
its random head-to-heads, no full round-robin required. Beta-tested in the Discord (Discord sponsor: Server
Subscriptions, 90/10 revenue split to creators), then launched publicly: it **peaked at ~35,000 votes/minute
and totaled 21M+ votes in ~3 days** with "essentially zero bad actors." **Overall winner: the Pixel 6A** —
a four-year-old sensor/software combo — "the people's choice camera of 2022," ahead of the Pixel 7 Pro and
Zenfone 9. Lessons: **doing well in one category didn't guarantee the others**; **good exposure wins, but
you can be too bright** (people prefer too-bright over too-dark); if you weight the standard photo 3× the
**Oppo Find X5 Pro** wins; **votes-per-dollar** crowns the Pixel 6A and the $379 Realme 10 Pro+. Biggest
losers: the **$1,600 Sony Xperia 1 IV** (world-class sensor, terrible in full auto) and the Moto Edge 30
Ultra. He also **blind-tested himself** (didn't know which phone took which shot).

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-12-21] **The scientific redesign — ELO, not a bracket.** "'In years past we did blind bracket
  voting — fun, but it depended on matchups I planted, and there's always human input (I might've
  accidentally put the second-best phone against the best in round one). This time: 16 phones, the same
  photo on each, stripped of EXIF, lettered — and an ELO rating algorithm (from chess/table tennis) so you
  hit enough sanctioned matchups to get a statistically significant, customized winner.'"
- [2022-12-21] **21 million votes, essentially zero bad actors.** "'As soon as it went public it peaked at
  ~35,000 votes per minute and totaled more than 21 million votes in about three days, with essentially zero
  bad actors. I don't know if there's any other collection of data like this anywhere on the internet.'"
- [2022-12-21] **The Pixel 6A is the people's choice camera of 2022.** "'Overall winner, highest average ELO
  across all three categories: the Pixel 6A — the people's choice camera of 2022 — ahead of the Pixel 7 Pro
  and the Zenfone 9. And it matches last year's bracket result (the 5A). A four-year-old sensor/software
  combo still nailing the basics better than anyone.'"
- [2022-12-21] **Good exposure wins — but you can be too bright.** "'The three darkest photos were the three
  biggest losers — but right above them were three of the absolute brightest, straight-up overexposed. So
  good exposure wins; but if you're going to miss, people would rather have it too bright than too dark. And
  in portrait mode the three worst photos were the brightest — you *can* overdo it.'"
- [2022-12-21] **Weight the standard photo and the winner changes; votes-per-dollar.** "'Doing well in one
  category didn't guarantee the others. If you say the standard photo is ~3× as important as low-light and
  portrait (which feels about right), the Oppo Find X5 Pro becomes the overall winner. And votes-per-dollar
  crowns the Pixel 6A and the $379 Realme 10 Pro+ — sneaky-good cheap cameras.'"
- [2022-12-21] **The expensive losers.** "'The undisputed biggest losers were the $1,600 Sony Xperia 1 IV
  (world-class sensor and advanced apps, but in full auto it produced the lowest average rating) and the
  Moto Edge 30 Ultra (the most total losses — an over-sharpened low-light photo and a too-dark standard
  one). The iPhone 14 Pro landed in the middle of the pack (6th, 10th, 5th).'"
