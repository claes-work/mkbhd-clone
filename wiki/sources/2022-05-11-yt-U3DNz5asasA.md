# Pixel Watch, Pixel 7 and Google's Hardware Reveal 2022! (@mkbhd, 2022-05-11)

## Summary
Reactions to Google I/O 2022's unusually hardware-heavy keynote, organized as **three levels of announcements**:
**next-up** (the **$449 Pixel 6a** — a slightly smaller/cheaper Pixel 6 with the **same Tensor chip but cheaper
cameras** [the tried-and-true old Sony IMX363 sensor]; the **$199 Pixel Buds Pro**); **later this year** (a fall
preview of the **Pixel 7/7 Pro** — soft-touch glass, next-gen Tensor — and, **finally, the Pixel Watch** — a
circular stainless Wear OS watch built on the Fitbit acquisition, thick bezels, a tactile crown); and **on the
horizon** (a 2023 **Pixel Tablet** pre-announced with almost no detail). His durable read: **Google pre-announcing
so far ahead is about controlling the leak narrative** — rather than let the Pixel 7 leak for six months (and lose
the hype and their own messaging, the Sony problem), they just tell you first. RIP the iPod (discontinued that week).

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-05-11] **Pixel 6a — same Tensor, cheaper cameras.** "'The **$449 Pixel 6a** is a slightly smaller,
  bumped-down Pixel 6 (same camera bar, glass, software, and **the same Tensor chip** — cheaper now that those
  parts are 6+ months old). The real cuts are the display (a smaller 6.1" 1080p 60 Hz) and **the cameras — Google
  told us that's where they saved cost**: it's the **old, tried-and-true 12 MP Sony IMX363** from the Pixel 2/3/4
  (cheaper, but probably not bad). Alongside the **$199 Pixel Buds Pro** (ANC, a transparency mode I hope rivals
  AirPods).'"
- [2022-05-11] **The Pixel 7 fall preview — soft-touch glass.** "'A limited first look at the **Pixel 7/7 Pro** —
  new colors, bolder camera circles, next-gen Tensor, Android 13, and **soft-touch glass (I loved that on the
  Pixel 3)**. That's about all we know.'"
- [2022-05-11] **The Pixel Watch — finally.** "'**Finally, the Pixel Watch** (fall) — a circular stainless Wear OS
  watch from the Fitbit acquisition, a simple silhouette, a tactile crown, interchangeable bands, ~40 mm. **Solid
  bezels** (I'm hoping it's OLED so they blend in, plus good battery/haptics). It acts like a Fitbit for fitness/
  sleep, works with Android phones (not the iPhone — classic Apple lock-in), with a cellular option.'"
- [2022-05-11] **A 2023 Pixel Tablet — pre-announced with nothing.** "'They also **pre-announced a 2023 Pixel
  Tablet** with basically zero info (larger, premium-ish, iPad Air-to-Pro price range).'"
- [2022-05-11] **Why pre-announce so early — controlling the leak.** "'Showing this so early **reminds me of Sony
  (announce a phone, then it ships six months later and the hype's gone) — but this is Google, who leaks
  constantly**, so this feels like them getting ahead of it: **rather than let the Pixel 7 leak for six months and
  lose the hype and their own messaging, just tell people and take advantage of it.**' (RIP the iPod, discontinued
  this week — press F.)'"

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "It kind of reminds me on one hand of Sony, who will announce a phone early in the year and then six months later
> it'll finally come out and all the hype is gone... but on the other hand this is Google, and they've always been
> leaking things constantly."
> "This is the Pixel Watch that we've been hearing so much about for so long and waiting for."
