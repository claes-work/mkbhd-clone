# Mac Studio Review: Double Trouble! (@mkbhd, 2022-03-18)

## Summary
The full review of the Mac Studio (M1 Max / M1 Ultra) — "**the computer I personally got really excited about**"
(he built a video studio; the machine is literally called the Mac Studio, targeted at "someone like me"). His frame:
**the "2x machine"** — the M1 Ultra is two M1 Max fused, so ~2× cores/bandwidth/memory. Benchmarks blow the doors
off every prior Mac (double the M1 Max multi-core), but two honest caveats: **it does NOT beat an RTX 3090 at what
you'd buy a 3090 for (gaming)** — the graph stops where the 3090 keeps drawing power — and **his Mac Pro still
slightly out-exports it** (6:05 vs 7:30). Durable ideas: **"it's just a tool — a benchmark won't make you better at
editing"**; the machine is **not modular** (soldered — he pushes back on Apple's use of the word); and it's
**"$5,000 of overkill vs $25,000 of overkill, so it's a bargain."** He's **still waiting for the Mac Pro tower.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-03-18] **The 2x machine — doubles everything.** "'Basically two Mac minis melted into one thick cube — and
  the **M1 Ultra is literally two M1 Max fused** with enough bandwidth to behave as one chip: **2× the CPU cores,
  GPU cores, memory bandwidth, and total memory.** It blows the doors off every Mac ever, ties the highest single-
  core Geekbench I've seen, and doubles the M1 Max multi-core (more CPU power than even the 28-core Mac Pro).'"
- [2022-03-18] **The honest GPU caveat — it doesn't beat the 3090 at gaming.** "'Apple's graph said "3090
  performance at 200 W less" — **but the graph should continue: the 3090 keeps drawing up to 400 W, so it doesn't
  beat the 3090 at what you'd buy a 3090 for (gaming)** — which I didn't expect it to; you'd never buy this to
  game. But double the cores still do double the work in image processing (~2× the M1 Max on Metal).'"
- [2022-03-18] **Real-world — still slightly behind the Mac Pro.** "'In my Final Cut workflow it gives more headroom
  than the M1 Max laptop — I can drop 8K raw and hit play instantly — but **it still didn't quite beat my Mac Pro
  (my iPhone SE review exported in 7:30 on the Studio vs 6:05 on the Mac Pro).** The more optimized the app, the
  closer to 2×.'"
- [2022-03-18] **Not modular; "it's just a tool."** "'Apple used the word "modularity" — **I don't think it means
  what they think it means; everything inside is soldered, no RAM/GPU upgrades ever** (though that vertical
  integration is what makes it this small/efficient). And at the end of the day **this is just a tool — people look
  for the benchmark that proves it'll make them better at what they do; it can't. If you're bad at editing and get
  this, you'll be bad at editing, but faster.**'"
- [2022-03-18] **"$5,000 of overkill vs $25,000" — and still waiting for the tower.** "'For everyone but the
  heaviest users this is overkill, and **I love that that's the narrative — at least it's $5,000 of overkill vs
  $25,000 of overkill, so it's a bargain.** The Apple-silicon Mac Pro is still coming (the current 40-lb, 28-core
  Mac Pro still slightly outperforms this tiny box) — **I expect the new Mac Pro to double this again. That's the
  one I'll keep waiting for**, but a maxed Studio handles everything we do here.'"
