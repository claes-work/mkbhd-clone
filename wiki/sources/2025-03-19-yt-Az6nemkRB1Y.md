# Reviewing my First Car: Toyota Camry Hybrid!

## Summary

Marques reviews the newest Toyota Camry Hybrid and frames it as a **look back at his own first
car** — a **used red 2014 Toyota Camry XLE** he bought junior year of college. **A canonical
biography source:** he got the car because his **ultimate frisbee team** needed vehicles for
tournament road trips once the seniors graduated; he averaged **37.12 mpg lifetime** and "played
the efficiency graphic like a video game" trying to max his score. The review doubles as praise
for Toyota's stubborn, now-excellent hybrid strategy. Fully Marques-narrated.

## Key claims (biography marked ⬥)

- **[2025-03-19]** ⬥ BIOGRAPHY (canonical): **"My first car ever was junior year of college… I played
  ultimate frisbee, and every time we'd go to a tournament we'd pack up in one of the upperclassmen's
  cars for a road trip. When the seniors graduated we lost all their cars, so I needed one that was
  comfortable, could fit a bunch of people, and had decent gas mileage — so I ended up with a used red
  Toyota Camry XLE, a 2014."** (quote)
- **[2025-03-19]** ⬥ BIOGRAPHY: **"I averaged 37.12 mpg lifetime with that thing. I played the little
  efficiency graphic like a video game — I wanted to max my high score. If I got downhill enough for a
  long enough drive I could get into the 40s. That was my idea of fun as a college student."** (quote)
  — an early, telling instance of his efficiency-as-a-game / optimizer instinct.
- **[2025-03-19]** ⬥ BIOGRAPHY: his Camry had a **tan cloth interior**; "whenever I see a red Camry from
  around this year, I think of mine." He's been "in the backseat of my own car many times." (quote)
- **[2025-03-19]** On the new Camry: 2.5L NA inline-4 + **three electric motors** (two front, one rear →
  electric AWD), 232 combined hp ("surprisingly peppy"), 44mpg highway (~550mi/tank), starts ~$28k
  ("firmly the economical option") but "you can spend $50,000 on a brand-new Camry — don't do that."
  (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-03-19]** Analogy (recurring MKBHD move): specc'ing up a Camry is **"similar to optioning a
  mid-range phone up to $1,000 — you get premium features brought down, but still have the bones of the
  economy option."** (quote)
- **[2025-03-19]** ⬥ Self-reference: **6'3" "with a lot of hair"** — his head is against the sloping
  rear roofline. (paraphrase)
- **[2025-03-19]** Verdict: **"I get why Toyota continues to do the hybrid thing — they've gotten really
  good at it and committed to it… well done Toyota, keep making good cars like this."** (quote)
