# Apple Watch Ultra Review: Worth It Or Nah? (@mkbhd, 2022-09-21)

## Summary
The full review after ~a week — opening with the proof: **two full days and two nights on one charge** (through
two 3-hour ultimate practices), "roughly double" his Series 7. His framing device is **concentric circles of
users**: the SE covers most people, the standard Watch (now Series 8) covers a bit more, and the **$800 Ultra
targets the outskirts — the extreme adventurers** (divers, hikers, marathoners, triathletes) apple still wants in
the ecosystem. He unpacks **what "Pro" (here, "Ultra") means to Apple** — the highest-end version with features a
small fraction can use — via three adds: **ruggedness, features, and battery.** Honest context: the Ultra is huge
and a **"block" that doesn't taper** (he wishes it came in black), and its 2-day battery is "not impressive" next
to a solar Casio's near-infinite life. His verdict mirrors the **ROG Phone parallel**: overkill for almost
everyone, but **"the best Apple Watch they've ever made,"** and its adventure features also make it better for
regular use — **"if you have to ask, you probably don't need it,"** but it's still an everyday Apple Watch.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-09-21] **The proof — two days and two nights.** "'Saturday: 100% → drove to a 3-hr ultimate practice →
  ended the day at 69% → slept with it on. Sunday: 60% → another hotter 3-hr practice, 2,000 cal → slept with it
  on. Monday 11am: **20% left. Two days and two nights into a third day — far better than any Apple Watch, roughly
  double my Series 7.**'"
- [2022-09-21] **What "Pro"/"Ultra" means to Apple — concentric circles.** "'Apple tosses "Pro" on the highest-end
  version of almost everything — each with features a smaller fraction can use. Same with "Ultra": **the SE covers
  most people; the standard Watch (Series 8) covers a bit more; the $800 Ultra is for the outskirts — the extreme
  adventurers** the regular Watch wasn't enough for, whom Apple still wants in the ecosystem. Three adds:
  **ruggedness, features, battery.**'"
- [2022-09-21] **Ruggedness + features.** "'Ruggedness: a bigger 49 mm titanium/ceramic body, bigger glove-friendly
  buttons, a crown guard, a flat sapphire crystal with a raised lip. Features: dual-frequency GPS, WR100 diving (a
  real dive computer with the Oceanic+ app), 2,000 nits, an 86 dB siren, compass waypoints/backtrack, and the
  customizable **orange Action button.**'"
- [2022-09-21] **The honest context — it's a block, and 2 days isn't much elsewhere.** "'It's huge — and unlike
  round watches that taper, **the Apple Watch is a "block" that doesn't taper, so it sticks out** (I wish it came
  in black — black is slimming). And in the watch world **a 2-day battery isn't impressive** — my solar Casio Pro
  Trek has never been charged. The whole "it's huge" thing is context: it's compact next to a Garmin/Suunto.'"
- [2022-09-21] **The verdict — the ROG Phone parallel; if you have to ask.** "'For me (a reviewer + ultimate
  player), the normal Watch is enough — but on the Ultra I'd only drain 20% at practice instead of 40%. **It's
  exactly like the ROG Phone: complete overkill for almost everyone, but all those extreme features also make it
  better for regular use.** Technically **the best Apple Watch they've ever made** (longest battery, biggest/
  brightest display, best mics/speakers, most accurate tracking). **If you have to ask, you probably don't need it
  — but if you know what you're getting into, it's a pretty good Apple Watch.** (I asked a diver, an ultra-runner,
  and an alpinist on Waveform — each concluded it does what they need.)'"
