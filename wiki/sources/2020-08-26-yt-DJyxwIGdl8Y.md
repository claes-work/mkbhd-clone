# Surface Duo Unboxing & Impressions: Hinge Goals! (@mkbhd, 2020-08-26)

## Summary
Hardware-only impressions of the $1,400 Microsoft Surface Duo (Microsoft's disclosure limit: **can't turn it
on until the full review**). Two durable ideas: **the best hinge he's ever used in any piece of tech**, and
**Microsoft's dual-screen bet is "the opposite of the Samsung plan"** — nail the dual-screen experience now
with mature hardware and migrate to a folding display later, rather than build a chunky phone around infant
folding-display tech.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-08-26] **The thinness — "holy smokes."** "'This thing is **incredibly thin** — the USB-C port on the
  bottom is almost the thickness of an entire half of the tablet. All-glass (not metal like other Surfaces),
  no exterior screen, nothing on the back. The hardware **first impression is super positive.**'"
- [2020-08-26] **"The nicest hinge I've ever used in a piece of tech."** "'Within four minutes of unboxing I
  said out loud: **this is the nicest hinge I've ever used in a piece of tech** — including laptops and other
  folding phones. It's a 360° hinge, smooth and firm, and **it can firmly hold itself at any angle** — unlike
  a Galaxy Fold, which really only sticks to closed or fully open. The hinge engineers deserve a round of
  applause.'" His hinge-is-the-most-important-part doctrine, stated at its strongest.
- [2020-08-26] **Why Microsoft limited it to hardware-only.** "'They limited first impressions to hardware
  because **the hardware experience is super positive — once you turn it on and see the bezels, the last-year
  specs and the not-so-impressive camera, that's when you fall back to earth.** Snapdragon 855, 60 Hz screen,
  3,600 mAh across both halves, an 11 MP camera that's also the primary camera, no water resistance, no
  wireless charging, $1,400.'"
- [2020-08-26] **THE STRATEGY CONTRAST — "the opposite of the Samsung plan."** "'Microsoft making a folding
  phone means they believe it's the future — but they bet on **this hinge and dual screens.** They can build
  around dual-screen now and **move to a foldable display later, when the tech is good enough to be this thin.**
  That's **the opposite of the Samsung plan,** which went straight from infant foldable-display tech to a
  chunky phone built around it to make it work.'" A promotable framework for the foldables era.
- [2020-08-26] **Proof of concept, not a finished product.** "'It's so first-gen — like the Galaxy Fold was —
  that **I'm almost already considering not recommending it at all,** because it's not about buying this as a
  finished product; it's a **proof of concept, an attempt to see how this foldable idea works.** Everyone's
  attacking the new frontier of foldables differently (LG dual-screen case, Fold, Z Flip, this), and I actually
  think this is a smart way to do it from Microsoft in 2020.'"
