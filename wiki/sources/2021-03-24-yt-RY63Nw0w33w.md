# OnePlus 9 Review: Sneaky Value! (@mkbhd, 2021-03-24)

## Summary
Full review of the OnePlus 9 — "the phone OnePlus felt they had to make" (the cheaper companion to the hyped 9
Pro). The durable read: **frustratingly close to a great deal** — flagship specs at $240 less than the 9 Pro — but
with a **generic plastic design** you could mistake for a Nord, and a **useless 2 MP monochrome camera** ("a
waste of money, change my mind"). Verdict: **sneaky-good value if you specifically want the OxygenOS experience** —
but not a blanket recommend.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-03-24] **The frame — the phone OnePlus had to make.** "'They can't only make a hyped, expensive flagship —
  **this feels like the phone OnePlus felt they had to make.** As a phone (past the hype), they did what they were
  trying to: a full flagship checklist for less than an S21 Ultra. The Snapdragon 888 at this price is about as
  low as it gets at launch — very competitive.'"
- [2021-03-24] **The generic plastic design — "you could mistake it for a Nord."** "'The design is acceptable but
  **generic — glossy polycarbonate; by weight and feel you could mistake it for a $300–$400 Nord or any glossy
  plastic phone.** As much as I hate the "glastic" term, the S21 (mostly plastic) never feels cheap in the hand;
  this needed to separate itself. Maybe you'll case it anyway.'"
- [2021-03-24] **The 2 MP monochrome camera — "a waste of money, change my mind."** "'Same-size 48 MP main (a
  different Sony sensor), the same excellent ultrawide (one of the best in any phone), but **it drops the
  telephoto and keeps a 2 MP black-and-white camera. Why?** "It adds detail to B&W photos" — it's 2 MP; and for a
  B&W photo I just take a regular one and desaturate it. **It's a useless extra sensor — a waste of money; you
  should have spent it elsewhere. Change my mind.**'" His recurring anti-padded-camera stance, sharpened.
- [2021-03-24] **The honest downgrades vs. the 9 Pro — haptics, the alert slider, the shutter lag.** "'My job is
  to flag what's not the same: **the haptics are weaker/looser, the alert slider is loose (easy to flip to ring
  in your pocket), and the weirdly slow camera performance / shutter lag from the 9 Pro is still here** (hopefully
  software-fixable). But everything in the software is identical to the $969 Pro.'"
- [2021-03-24] **THE VERDICT — sneaky value, not a blanket recommend.** "'Some phones are so good you can blanket-
  recommend them; this isn't quite that. **But for people who value the OxygenOS experience — clean, fast, the 65 W
  charging — this is sneaky-good value: the using-it experience is exactly the same as the phone that costs $240
  more.** Vs. the ~$700 S21: I'd give build/camera to Samsung (real zoom, no wasted sensor), battery/charging to
  OnePlus, and software to OxygenOS — I'd lean OnePlus. It's all about your priorities.'"

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "This extra sensor is a waste of money — a small amount of money, but it's a waste of money, and you should have
> spent that money on something else. Change my mind."
> "The using-the-phone experience, this software experience, is exactly the same — to the spec, to the speed, to
> the performance — as the phone that costs 240 dollars more."
