## Summary

The 2024 Auto Focus year-end awards. Marques and co-host Miles hand out category winners from the
cars they reviewed in 2024. The night's big theme: **the Lucid Air Sapphire sweeps EV of the Year
AND overall MVP/Car of the Year** ("this car is all of that stuff"). Other winners span ICE, hybrid,
efficiency, performance, luxury, bust, and most-improved.

## Key claims (awards = AutoFocus joint picks unless marked ⬥Marques)

- **[2024-12-20]** **MVP / Car of the Year + EV of the Year = Lucid Air Sapphire** — "seems to be
  impossible based on how many things it's good at… 1,200 hp AND 400 miles of range, like a smartphone
  with an overclocked chip and the longest battery life." (joint) The unanimous pick: "we both looked
  at each other and already knew what it was." (joint)
- **[2024-12-20]** **ICE Car of the Year = McLaren 750S** ("the perfect blend of old-school and new
  with hydraulic steering… a crazy go-kart type car"); runner-up Ferrari Purosangue. **Hybrid of the
  Year = Bentley Continental GT Speed** ("multiple personalities — chill GT and sports car"). **Best
  Performance = Lamborghini Revuelto** ("Lamborghini basically fixed every problem with the
  Aventador"); runner-up Hyundai Ioniq 5N ("the most fun attainable EV"). **Best Luxury = Rolls-Royce
  Spectre** ("king of softness, king of quietness"). (joint)
- **[2024-12-20]** ⬥ MARQUES: the **efficiency award** framing — **"it's similar to the battery award I
  give in the smartphone awards; you might not have the biggest capacity, but if you make the best use
  of it, that deserves recognition."** Winner **Tesla Model 3 (base)** ("the most worry-free charging
  experience EV you can get… ~180 Wh/mi on my scale of 200–600"); co-nominee Lucid Air Pure (~420mi).
  (Marques's scale/framing.) (quote)
- **[2024-12-20]** ⬥ MARQUES (biography): **"I've been living with a Rivian R1T since the launch
  edition came out — I clearly love the thing"** (introducing Most Improved runner-up Rivian R1 Gen 2).
  **Most Improved winner = Tesla Model 3 "Highland"** ("the most night-and-day transformation of any
  EV… reestablished itself as one of the best deals"). (quote)
- **[2024-12-20]** ⬥ MARQUES (biography): recounts **living with the Rolls-Royce Spectre** — "driving
  through Jersey City… a garbage truck drove past me and I didn't even hear it, right next to me. The
  luxury experience is not hearing the garbage truck." (matches his week-with-the-Spectre story.) (quote)
- **[2024-12-20]** **Bust of the Year = Fisker Ocean** ("the company doesn't exist anymore for a
  reason… the bones were there, but they rushed it"). (joint)
