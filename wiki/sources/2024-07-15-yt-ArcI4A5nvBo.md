# iOS 18 Hands-On: Top 5 Features! (@mkbhd, 2024-07-15)

## Summary
His annual "top 5 of the new iOS," testing the iOS 18 beta — with **Apple Intelligence deliberately left out** (a
separate video, not yet testable). The framing contrast to last year: **these features aren't ecosystem-locked** — they
don't require a friend with an iPhone or another Apple device; **"they're all just new features to the iPhone, anyway.
I love to see that."** The five: (5) the **new Control Center** (paginated, customizable layouts, resizable controls,
third-party controls coming, a customizable lock screen — "I'm excited to set mine to my to-do list app"); (4) the
**Passwords app** (breaking the password tools out of Settings into their own app, with 2FA/passkeys/shared groups —
but no Android version, so not for him as an Android daily-driver); (3) **home-screen customization** — "welcome to
2024, iPhone users" (place icons anywhere, dark/large/tinted icons) — but **the tinting "feels kind of cursed"** (some
icons/widgets go illegible; Google's Material You went weird its own way too); (2) the **little things** (Game Mode,
much better Photos smart search, Shazam shortcuts, and **RCS is finally live** — "still a green bubble, as I predicted,"
but now high-res media, typing indicators, read receipts between iPhone and Android — **"now that person doesn't have
to buy his mom an iPhone"**); and (1) — "bro, it's a calculator" — the **iPhone Calculator with Math Notes** (handwrite
equations, solved in your own handwriting, variables, graphs, syncing across devices — **"I'm impressed by this more
than anything else in iOS 18"**; "I wish I'd had this instead of a TI-84").

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2024-07-15] **This year's features aren't ecosystem-locked.** "'Last year almost every new feature was
  ecosystem-based — you needed a friend with an iPhone or another Apple device. It's the opposite this year: they're
  all just new features to the iPhone anyway. I love to see that.'"
- [2024-07-15] **Home-screen customization — welcome to 2024 — but the tinting is cursed.** "'iPhone users can finally
  put icons anywhere and go dark/large/tinted. Welcome to 2024. But the tinting feels kind of cursed — I haven't gotten
  any combination to look good; some third-party icons and widgets go completely illegible. Google's Material You went
  weird in its own way too. Apple at least tints all icons at once.'"
- [2024-07-15] **RCS is finally live — still green.** "'RCS support does appear to finally be live on the iPhone in
  2024. As I predicted, it's still a green bubble — but you can now text high-quality media between any modern Android
  phone and an iPhone (I sent myself a full 3 MB image), with typing indicators and read receipts. Apple can now say
  they finally did it — and that guy from the interview doesn't have to buy his mom an iPhone.'"
- [2024-07-15] **The #1 feature is the Calculator (Math Notes).** "'The number-one new feature in iOS 18 is — bro —
  the calculator. It's not just an iPad thing; the iPhone gets Math Notes too: handwrite an equation and it solves it
  in your own handwriting, with variables and graphs (everything I've been asking Wolfram Alpha for), syncing across
  devices, indexed and searchable. I'm impressed by this more than anything else in iOS 18 — I wish I'd had it instead
  of a paper notebook and a TI-84.'"
