# Dear Electric Cars! (@mkbhd, 2020-12-29)

## Summary
A landmark EV manifesto — open "letters" to the whole industry, split into **"Teslas and everything else."** The
durable theses: **range is the #1 baseline metric** (a new 200-mile premium EV is already behind); **the charging
network is half the story** and **arguably needs to be a collaborative effort**; **the only financially
responsible premium EV right now is a Tesla** (because of the Supercharger network); and a message to Tesla —
**your battery/drivetrain lead will eventually erode, so you'll have to invest in build quality.** The unifying
call: **electric cars have to get better together.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-12-29] **EVs are the future — "but they're just that, the future."** "'I honestly believe **electric cars
  are the future of cars — but they're just that, the future; there's a lot of work both Teslas and everything
  else have to do to get there.**' (Apple's rumored EV won't be alone — the biggest companies are all working on
  one. "Are we sure it's going to come with a charger?")'"
- [2020-12-29] **RANGE IS THE BASELINE METRIC — 200 miles is already behind.** "'**Range is the #1 baseline
  indicator of how good a manufacturer's EV program is.** Top EVs go 250–400 miles today (the Model S Long Range:
  402). **A new premium EV launching with 200 miles is already behind** — that's low enough that I still spend
  mental energy on where/when to charge. The goal is to match a gas car's ~400–600+ mile tank.'" A promotable
  EV-evaluation yardstick.
- [2020-12-29] **THE CHARGING NETWORK IS HALF THE STORY — and should be collaborative.** "'**The charging network
  is arguably as important as the car itself** — gas stations are underrated; the confidence one's always nearby
  is huge, and that doesn't exist yet for EVs. My **bold hot take: this needs to be a collaborative effort.**
  Why aren't more EVs compatible with Superchargers? It's a win overall. (I tweeted this; **Elon casually replied
  that Superchargers are being made available to other EVs** — but it's still not common.)'"
- [2020-12-29] **THE VERDICT — the only financially responsible premium EV is a Tesla.** "'Because of road trips,
  **I literally cannot recommend or buy any non-Tesla EV** — the Supercharger network here is as important as
  range or build quality, maybe more. **On record again: if the Porsche Taycan could use Superchargers, I'd take
  it over a Model S** (a couple took 9 hours to go 130 miles in a Taycan, passing Superchargers they couldn't
  use). **A Tesla is the only financially responsible premium EV as far as convenience goes.**'" (Cf. the Taycan
  review, [[2020-09-22-yt-BAZX9p2oGOg]].)
- [2020-12-29] **The Ford-exec "shade" — there's still a compromise, just a different one.** "'A Ford exec threw
  shade (doors fit, bumpers don't fall off) — but **he's leaving out that there's still a compromise: the Mach-E
  tops out at 300 miles and none do Supercharging.** People keep picking the Tesla — the great battery, performance,
  and tech — over the better-built car. **Many buyers would drive a plain car that goes 1,000 miles over the
  best-built one that goes 200** — because there are no chargers everywhere.'"
- [2020-12-29] **DEAR TESLA — your lead will erode; invest in build quality.** "'Tesla, **your battery/drivetrain
  lead is eventually going to go away** as others catch up. I deal with the downside — wind noise, water in the
  taillights, a past drivetrain failure, the Model Y's build-quality quirks — and **the rate of issues is still
  high enough that you'll eventually have to address it.** When the median EV range at a price matches a gas car's,
  **that's when Tesla should finally focus on fit and finish** — the last whack-a-mole.'" A pointed, evidence-
  backed critique.
- [2020-12-29] **THE UNIFYING CALL — get better together (+ the USB-C analogy).** "'**EVs are coming fast, and if
  they're going to make it, they ought to all get better together in the ways they struggle.** Asking EVs to
  adopt Superchargers is like **me asking Apple to add USB-C alongside Lightning — a benefit to the product — but
  everyone has dreams of their own proprietary "Lightning" charging network.**'"
