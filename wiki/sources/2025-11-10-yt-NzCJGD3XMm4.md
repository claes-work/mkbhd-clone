# The Jeep Wagoneer S EV is Scary... (Not in a Good way)

## Summary

Marques reviews the electric **Jeep Wagoneer S** — a car he *wanted* to love because he has "a
soft spot for Jeep" (**his first car, the one he learned to drive in, was a hand-me-down Jeep
Grand Cherokee**). The interior designers "cooked" (nice red leather, McIntosh speakers), but
his verdict is that **"the engineers didn't cook"**: it squanders the EV's natural advantages
with programmed accelerator lag, traction it can't control on skinny efficiency tires, and a
dangerous braking quirk. "Don't buy this for $75k." A biography-rich review, fully Marques-
narrated.

## Key claims

- **[2025-11-10]** ⬥ BIOGRAPHY: **"I have a soft spot in my heart for Jeep. The first car I ever
  drove and learned to drive with and got my driver's-license test with was a Jeep Grand Cherokee —
  a hand-me-down from my dad, from his dad" (a grandfather who liked to modify cars). "That was the
  beginning of my car journey."** (quote)
- **[2025-11-10]** The Wagoneer S (fully electric, dual-motor AWD, ~600hp, ~300mi claimed, ~$70k) —
  "confusingly named" (it feels like a Grand Cherokee, not the bigger gas Wagoneer). (paraphrase)
- **[2025-11-10]** His **standing "an EV should be the best version of that car"** thesis: heavy,
  expensive, quick cars are perfect EV candidates — "a Jeep with the best traction control, best
  all-wheel drive, most torque from being electric — this should be the best Jeep ever. But it feels
  like they just weren't into it." (quote)
- **[2025-11-10]** **"The designers cooked; the engineers didn't."** The interior is the best part
  (red leather, bright ambient lights, heated/ventilated/massaging seats, wireless CarPlay, a
  polarized passenger screen, **A-minus-tier McIntosh speakers** — his first car with them, a quiet
  cabin, cavernous trunk + hidden storage). (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-11-10]** The driving faults (why it wastes the EV advantages): **programmed accelerator
  lag** ("flatten the pedal for a split second and experience zero acceleration" — "there's always
  lag, even in a car that could have instant torque"); **breaks traction in a straight line** from
  low speed (600hp on narrow 235 efficiency tires); and a **dangerous braking quirk** — over a bump
  mid-braking "it thinks it's lost traction, pulls the brakes, then reapplies," so the car lurches —
  **"the only other car I've experienced that in is the Fisker Ocean."** (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-11-10]** Leftover gas-car artifacts on a supposed ground-up EV: an unnecessary power
  button, a big empty center console, **a driveshaft hump in the back seat**, and a tiny frunk. Range
  underwhelms ("never seen a number that starts with a three… I'm getting 270mi in a NJ November").
  (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-11-10]** Verdict: **"as much as I wanted to love this Jeep… this is not the one to get. If
  you're going to buy it for $75k, don't. Just don't."** (a Mustang Mach-E does the same for less,
  more completely). Maybe a second attempt gets it right. (quote)
