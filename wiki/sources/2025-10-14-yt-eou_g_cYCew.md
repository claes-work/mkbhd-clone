# Xiaomi 17 Pro Max: An iPhone... But Better!

## Summary

Marques examines the Xiaomi 17 Pro Max — a phone easy to dismiss as "just another Chinese
iPhone clone" (same shape, iOS-cued software, a dynamic-island, even the skipped number to
match the iPhone's naming). But per his switching-costs video, the clone cues are "just to
get you in the door" — underneath is a **spec monster designed to one-up the iPhone at
every turn**: the world's-fastest Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5, a **7,500mAh silicon-carbon
battery**, and a **full corner-to-corner OLED back display** he considers the first truly
non-gimmick execution of the idea. His frustration: it's China-only, and "it's kind of
embarrassing what it does next to the stuff we have available."

## Key claims

- **[2025-10-14]** It's "very easy to dismiss as just another Chinese iPhone clone, because it
  kinda is" — flat sides, familiar aesthetic, an iOS-cued UI, its own dynamic island, and
  Xiaomi **skipped a number to match the iPhone's** (17 / 17 Pro / 17 Pro Max). But per his
  switching video, "that stuff's just to get you in the door" — then the rest reveals **"a
  spec monster... specifically designed to one-up the iPhone. It's like an iPhone but better."**
  (quote)
- **[2025-10-14]** Display: same 6.9" as the iPhone Pro Max, slightly lower resolution (invisible
  to the eye) but noticeably brighter, with a tiny cutout because it **trades Face ID for a fast
  ultrasonic under-display fingerprint reader**. (paraphrase)
- **[2025-10-14]** Chip: one of the first with the **Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5** — now **matching
  the iPhone's record single-core bar-for-bar and beating it in multi-core and GPU**, giving it
  "credible claim to be the world's fastest mobile chip"; 16GB RAM, UFS 4.1. (paraphrase)
- **[2025-10-14]** Battery: the smaller 17 Pro already has the **highest-density silicon-carbon
  battery he's ever seen (6,300mAh)** — bigger than the S25 Ultra/OnePlus 13/any iPhone — **and
  the Pro Max has 7,500mAh**, 100W wired (charger in box), 50W wireless, and **22.5W reverse
  wireless** (can charge an iPhone to 100%). Silicon-carbon means all that capacity **without
  extra thickness or weight — it's lighter than the iPhone 17 Pro Max.** (paraphrase)
- **[2025-10-14]** Cameras: triple **50MP** (a one-up on the iPhone's 48MP), all three genuinely
  good, crisp, natural bokeh; the 5x telephoto impresses. Tends to overexpose facial skin tones
  "like a lot of phones from this region," but his own skin tone with no face detected is
  accurate. 8K video, an always-there exposure slider "I use a lot," teleprompter mode. (paraphrase)
- **[2025-10-14]** The headline: the **entire back camera plateau is a full corner-to-corner
  3,000-nit 120Hz OLED display** (2.9"). Its #1 use is a **viewfinder for the main cameras** —
  "the highest-quality selfies mankind has ever seen." But it's now big enough for a full clock/
  notifications/timer-with-liquid-animation/media controls, plus a marketplace of Apple-Watch-
  like faces and a retro handheld gaming case. (paraphrase)
- **[2025-10-14]** His verdict on the back screen: **"the most well-thought-out, most complete
  version of this screen-on-the-back idea I think we've ever seen"** — it has "pushed it over
  beyond gimmick into actual useful thing." He acknowledges he still doesn't know *why* you'd
  run a timer or play games there ("looks cool"), but taking selfies with it is a no-brainer.
  Contrasts it with the Xiaomi 11 Ultra, Nothing Phone 3 dot-matrix, and ROG Phone 9 — "none of
  them quite past the threshold of gimmick for me." (quote)
- **[2025-10-14]** It's edge-to-edge with basically no drawbacks (weight distribution fine, IP68,
  screen protectors pre-installed front and back). "I'm very impressed." (quote)
- **[2025-10-14]** The frustration: **China-only**, so he can't daily it (dbrand got him one).
  "I hope we can get some more phones like this over in these parts, 'cause it's kind of
  embarrassing what it does next to the stuff we have available." (quote)
