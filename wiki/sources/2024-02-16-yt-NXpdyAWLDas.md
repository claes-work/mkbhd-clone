# AI Generated Videos Just Changed Forever (OpenAI Sora) (@mkbhd, 2024-02-16)

## Summary
On **OpenAI's Sora** — text-to-video (up to one-minute clips) — which he calls **"another ChatGPT / DALL·E moment
for AI,"** hitting him personally as a video creator ("an AI that's actually doing my job"). The framing: **"remember
Will Smith eating spaghetti? That was a year ago"** — the leap in **one year** is "absolutely ridiculous," and
**"this is the worst this technology is going to be from here on out."** He walks the demo clips (the Tokyo woman,
the SUV, the golden-retriever puppies, the cloud-reading man, the 35 mm movie trailer) — genuinely impressive
lighting/materials/physics, with giveaways if you look for 10 seconds (the odd background walker, the treadmill guy,
and — the recurring tell — **hands** in the AI-grandma clip). Two takeaways. **Now:** these **pass as real to people
not looking for AI** (scary in a US election year) and are **already perfect for stock footage** — "a dent out of
video licensing" (the drone pilot no longer hired, the photographer's footage no longer licensed). There's a **Sora
watermark** in the corner (if not cropped). **The future gets existential:** if it's **trained on all videos humans
have ever made, can it be creative in ways humans haven't?** He closes: "I guess I'll talk to you next year when we
look back and go, 'remember how bad those wolf pups looked.'"

- [2024-02-16] **Another ChatGPT/DALL·E moment — and this is the worst it'll ever be.** "'This does feel like another
  ChatGPT/DALL·E moment for AI. Remember Will Smith eating spaghetti? That was a year ago — the leap is absolutely
  ridiculous. And keep in mind: this is the worst this technology is going to be from here on out.'"
- [2024-02-16] **It passes as real to people not looking for AI.** "'You're about to watch AI-generated videos
  *knowing* they're AI, so your brain is already hunting for imperfections — but not everybody who sees this online
  knows to look. As good as they've gotten, they can and will pass as real videos to people who aren't looking for
  it — insanely sketchy in a US election year.'"
- [2024-02-16] **Perfect for stock footage — a dent in video licensing.** "'It's already perfect for stock footage —
  the Big Sur drone shot, I wouldn't think twice scrolling past it. That has real implications: the drone pilot no
  longer hired, the photographers/videographers whose footage no longer needs to be licensed. Why pay for footage of
  a house on the cliffs when you can generate one? That's the real scary part of what this implies.'"
- [2024-02-16] **The hands are still the giveaway; there's a watermark.** "'Watch the AI-grandma birthday clip
  knowing AI struggles with hands — then it's dead giveaway after dead giveaway. There's a Sora watermark in the
  corner (if it's not cropped out), and they'll need even more safety than DALL·E — you shouldn't be able to make a
  politician look like they're doing something on video, especially this year.'"
- [2024-02-16] **The existential question.** "'It gets pretty existential: if this is trained on all videos humans
  have ever made, surely it can't be innovative or creative in ways humans haven't already been, right? I don't
  know. This is the worst it's going to be — I'll talk to you next year when we look back at how bad those wolf pups
  looked spawning out of nowhere.'"
