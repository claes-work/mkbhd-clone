# The Hyundai IONIQ 5: I Get It Now! (@mkbhd, 2022-08-25)

## Summary
A two-week drive of the Hyundai IONIQ 5 that "converted" him — and doubled as the thesis he kept landing on when
researching EVs: **if the future is electric, we'll eventually need really good AFFORDABLE EVs** (right now the
good ones are all more expensive than their gas counterparts). The IONIQ 5 — a well-rounded AWD crossover, ~250 mi
range, sub-$40K start, **"isn't really bad at anything"** — convinced him **"we're right on the cusp of that."**
He grades it against **his four EV fundamentals: solid range, fast charging, zippy performance, and extra storage**
(and adds a fifth after this: **good in-car maps** — the IONIQ's are "the worst part"). Two durable ideas: the
**retro-future "what the '80s thought the future would look like" design** (electric without looking dorky), and
**"rolling out your first EV is a chance to rebrand"** — Hyundai went from ~$20–30K reasonable cars to one of the
more impressive, easy-to-recommend options in a new category. Also announces the **@AutoFocus car channel** (the
Rivian R1T first).

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-08-25] **The one point — we need good affordable EVs.** "'Researching "the state of EVs," I realized I only
  had one major point: **if the future is electric, we'll eventually need really good affordable EVs** (the good
  ones are all more expensive than their gas counterparts). I asked Elon about a $25,000 mass-production EV in the
  factory once, and he said yes — but **it's never been harder or more expensive to make cars than now** (supply
  chain). **The IONIQ 5 convinced me we're right on the cusp of that** — one of the least expensive options in an
  expensive new category (like the Z Flip 4).'"
- [2022-08-25] **The four EV fundamentals — and a fifth.** "'My pillars for a reasonable EV: **solid range, fast
  charging, zippy performance, extra storage.** The IONIQ nails them — an impressively accurate ~250 mi estimate
  (more accurate than Tesla's), up to 350 kW charging, quick (the 20–40 mph zip matters more than 0–60), and a
  spacious crossover. **The worst part is the in-car maps — brutally slow and clunky** (CarPlay/Android Auto are
  great but don't know your EV range for trip planning) — so **I might have to add "good in-car maps" as a fifth
  fundamental.**'"
- [2022-08-25] **Retro-future design done right.** "'Great retro-future, angular design — **"what people in the '80s
  thought a car of the future would look like"** — with a dot-pixel theme (the pixel tail lights and front lights).
  **It looks electric without looking dorky** — I can't ask for much more.' (The interior is average-to-subpar for
  a $45K car, but spacious. Paddle shifters that adjust regen levels — dope.)'"
- [2022-08-25] **An EV is a chance to rebrand.** "'Underrated in this whole revolution: **rolling out your first EV
  is a chance to rebrand — to create a fresh reputation.** Hyundai is the best example: from ~$20–30K reasonable
  cars (Tucson, Santa Fe) to a $40–50K car that's one of the more impressive, easy-to-recommend options in a new
  category, **for a new, younger audience whose first car may be electric.** If your reputation's already good
  (Ford), just call it the F-150 Lightning — but for companies wanting a reputation change, this is the
  opportunity.'"
- [2022-08-25] **The AutoFocus channel launches.** "'I started **a new car channel, @AutoFocus** — the first video
  is the Rivian R1T I just took delivery of (it beat the Cybertruck to market), and we're using it to shoot this.
  I'll shoot most of that on whatever phone I'm testing. **I get the IONIQ 5 now — and this is the exact car we'll
  need a lot more of to usher in the EV future.**'"
