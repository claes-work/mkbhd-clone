# iPhone SE (2020) Honest Thoughts... (@mkbhd, 2020-04-17)

## Summary
Honest first thoughts on the $399 iPhone SE (an iPhone 8 body + the A13). The durable payload is the **"95% of
people" / tech-bubble** thesis and the **software-support-longevity** advantage — reasons a "parts-bin phone" is
"Apple's best deal ever."

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-04-17] **THE "95% OF PEOPLE" / TECH-BUBBLE THESIS.** "'We're in a **tech bubble** — only maybe **5% of us
  care about bezels, hole-punch-vs-notch, refresh rates.** Ask your parents/grandparents if they care about
  screen-to-body ratio — probably not. For the **other 95%**, a new iPhone with the A13 and a good camera for $400
  looks like a phone with **pretty much no downsides.**'" A key stay-grounded-in-normal-people lens.
- [2020-04-17] **"Apple's best deal ever" — and one of tech's.** "'A **"parts-bin phone"** (iPhone 8 body + A13 +
  the iconic SE name), but at **$399 it's Apple's best deal ever** (not the highest bar from a company selling $700
  wheels) — and **one of the best deals in all of tech.**'"
- [2020-04-17] **The under-rated advantage — software support longevity.** "'Something we don't talk about enough:
  because it's an iPhone, this $399 phone will get **iOS updates for ~4-5 years — probably the best software
  support of any $399 phone.**'" A durable value-per-longevity point.
- [2020-04-17] **The budget-camera + the Android-budget threat.** "'Likely the **best video in a budget phone** and
  a top-two budget camera (the A13's iPhone-11 image processing on the iPhone 8 sensor) — right there with the
  Pixel 3a. And **Apple joining the spec-for-low-budget race is scary for Android budget makers** who've been
  feasting there, because there's never been a *new* iPhone this cheap.'"
- [2020-04-17] **Best compact phone of the year (a Waveform take).** "'On the podcast we landed on **"best compact
  phone" over "best budget phone"** — a 4.7" phone is compact by today's standards, and few other flagship-quality
  small phones are coming.'" (Cross-references the Waveform discussion.)

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "We kind of sometimes catch ourselves in this tech bubble where we're always looking at the bleeding-edge stuff,
> but really it's a small percentage of us — maybe 5% — that care about... all that sort of stuff."
> "This is Apple's best deal ever, which maybe isn't the highest bar from a company that's also selling wheels for
> their computer for $700 — but it is also one of the best deals in all of tech."
