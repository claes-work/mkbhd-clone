# DALLE: AI Made This Thumbnail! (@mkbhd, 2022-05-16)

## Summary
An early, foundational take on **generative AI image models** — OpenAI's **DALL·E 2**, which turns a text prompt
into surprisingly realistic new images (he was given a day of access). He explains the mechanism (**CLIP** matches
images↔text so the model understands concepts; **diffusion** learns to reverse added noise — his "draw the owl"
analogy; kin to thispersondoesnotexist.com), demos a spectrum of prompts, and catalogs the **intentional and
unintentional shortcomings** (no adult/violent content or named real people; weak at relative position and written
text). His durable read: **it's a research project on the road to general AI**, and as a tool it's **"amazing for
brainstorming — the images aren't finished final pieces, but a great starting point"** (this video's thumbnail
started as a DALL·E robot-hand image; the Studio channel pitted DALL·E against Tim the designer). "What a time to
be alive."

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-05-16] **CLIP + diffusion — it understands concepts, not collage.** "'DALL·E 2 (OpenAI) turns a text
  description into a brand-new realistic image — 10 variations, any art style. Two technologies: **CLIP matches
  images to text so the computer understands concepts** (an "astronaut riding a horse" isn't a mosaic of found
  images — it knows what an astronaut, riding, and a horse are, and what's aesthetically pleasing); and
  **diffusion** — teach a model to corrupt an image with noise, and it learns to un-corrupt it (**"step one draw
  the circle, step two draw the rest of the owl"**). Like a generalized thispersondoesnotexist.com for anything.'"
- [2022-05-16] **The imagination surprised him.** "'The crazy part is how much imagination it uses — I ask for "a
  tiger discovering the lost city of Atlantis" and **it adds trees, birds, a moon, accurate lighting and shadows
  all by itself — I'm getting more than I asked for.** "A painting inspired by the Mona Lisa of a goat taking
  pictures with an iPad" — it just gets them right.'"
- [2022-05-16] **Why it exists — the general-AI goal.** "'It's a research project, not a consumer product —
  **OpenAI's goal is good, safe, GENERAL AI, which is hard** (there are great task-specific AIs for cancer
  detection or self-driving, but general AI needs to navigate a ton of situations — think a Tesla robot completing
  tasks). Recognizing and associating objects fast is a big part of that.'"
- [2022-05-16] **The shortcomings — intentional and quirky.** "'Intentional: **no adult content, violence, or
  illegal activity** (that's why the teddy-bear surgeon got scissors, not a knife), and **you can't ask for a
  specific real person** ("a man robbing a bank" yes; "Marquez Brownlee robbing a bank" no — dangerous for obvious
  reasons). Quirks: it's weak at **relative position** (a "blue apple in a bowl of oranges" came back as an orange
  in blue apples) and **written text.** The flip side: diffusion can also transform an existing image toward any
  prompt (un-modernizing an iPhone into older and older phones).'"
- [2022-05-16] **Amazing for brainstorming — not finished pieces.** "'Will it take jobs? (The Studio video pitted
  it against Tim, our designer — give Tim time and he makes something better, but in 10 seconds DALL·E spits out
  variations.) **As presently built it's amazing for brainstorming ideas — the images aren't meant to be finished
  final pieces, but a great starting point** (this video's thumbnail started as a DALL·E robot-hand image). Future
  versions will do higher-res, then quick animations, then video, then whole movies, on the way to general AI.
  **What a time to be alive.**'"
