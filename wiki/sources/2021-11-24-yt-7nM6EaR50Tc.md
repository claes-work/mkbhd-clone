# What is a Google Phone!?! Reviewing Every Pixel/Nexus Ever! (@mkbhd, 2021-11-24)

## Summary
A 13-year retrospective (G1 → Nexus → Pixel 6) framed around one evolving question: **what is a Google phone?**
The through-line: **Google was never a hardware company** — until 2016 every "Google phone" was built by someone
else (HTC, Samsung, LG, Motorola, Huawei) with Google's software on top. Key beats: the **Nexus 6's HDR+ was a
smartphone-camera inflection point** (multi-frame processing we now take for granted); **"peak Pixel was the
Pixel 2"** (the camera that was "the best of all time," the two-tone/colored-power-button design he loves); the
**"disappointing flagship → smash-hit budget phone" cycle** that cemented Pixel as **"the cheap phone that takes
good pictures"**; and his **vertical-integration optimism** — the Tensor Pixel 6 as "the beginning of the new
definition," Google finally doing its own Apple-style hardware+software+chip.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-11-24] **Google was never a hardware company.** "'Until 2016, Google was never the manufacturer of the
  Google phone — the G1 and Nexus One were HTC, the Nexus S/Galaxy Nexus were Samsung, Nexus 4/5 LG, Nexus 6
  Motorola, Nexus 6P Huawei. **Google makes great software and services but had absolutely never been a hardware
  company** — the Nexus name meant stock Android, quick updates straight from Google.'"
- [2021-11-24] **HDR+ on the Nexus 6 was the inflection point.** "'The sneaky-important feature of the Nexus 6 was
  the camera — **the first Google phone with HDR+, combining multiple frames for more dynamic range.** It didn't
  seem huge then, but that was an inflection point for smartphone cameras in general — nowadays if you don't have
  HDR you're not doing it right.'"
- [2021-11-24] **Peak Pixel was the Pixel 2.** "'The Pixel was the first phone designed and built BY Google (the
  rebrand from Nexus). **I think the Pixel 2 was peak Pixel — the panda/stormtrooper two-tone design with the
  colored power button, and a single camera universally praised as the best smartphone camera of all time.**'"
- [2021-11-24] **The cycle — and "the cheap phone that takes good pictures."** "'The 3a at $399 was a hit and
  became most of Pixel sales — but there's an upside and a downside. **The downside: the most successful version
  is the cheap one, so people think of a Pixel as "the cheap phone that takes good pictures," not the fast or
  high-end one.** Then it settled into a rhythm: disappointing flagship, smash-hit budget phone, repeat — which
  keeps cementing Pixel as a budget phone while the hardware got more generic.'"
- [2021-11-24] **The Pixel 6 is the new definition — vertical-integration optimism.** "'Pixel 5/5a felt like
  placeholders while they made serious progress on something new. **The Pixel 6 is the apex of being the most
  Google phone ever — a Google design, Google software, and a Google-designed chip (Tensor).** Like the
  Nexus→Pixel rename when they started designing themselves, this is the beginning of the new definition — Google
  finally doing its own version of what Apple does (controlling hardware, software, chip). I got optimistic about
  competition.'"
