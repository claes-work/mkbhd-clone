# Concept Phones: It's Time to Stop! (@mkbhd, 2023-02-27)

## Summary
A takedown of **concept phones as marketing** — prompted by OnePlus's hyped **"Active CryoFlux"** phone (icy blue
liquid pulsing through channels over the back). It looks incredibly cool... and **"it doesn't work, like at all"** —
the liquid is there to *look* like it's cooling the CPU/GPU/battery, not to actually do it. OnePlus's own best-case
numbers for a *working* future version are tiny (charge ~1 min faster; +2–4 fps). His thesis: **concept phones are
mostly a PR return on R&D that didn't ship** — companies spend big on research; most ideas that don't make it into a
real phone get **"repackaged and turned into a nice concept for people to admire,"** which is fine but is **"just a
PR stunt at the end of the day."** The memorable analogy: **"somewhere in Apple's HQ there's a USB-C iPhone just for
testing — they're just not going to package it up and show it off as a concept."** He runs a **worst-to-best list**
of concept phones by how much he *wanted them to be real* (OnePlus color-changing electrochromic backs; the Xiaomi
attachable-lens; TCL's paper-like matte display; Vivo's unlock-anywhere full-screen fingerprint; the rollable
TCL/Motorola/LG phones) — nearly all killed by cost/reliability (electrochromic glass and huge optical sensors are
"insanely expensive"). Verdict: **"concept phone, kind of just a PR stunt — why not both."**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2023-02-27] **The CryoFlux phone doesn't actually work.** "'It looks super sick — icy blue liquid pulsing over the
  hottest parts of the phone. But it doesn't work, like at all: the liquid flows to make it look like it's cooling
  the CPU, GPU, and battery, but it doesn't actively contribute to cooling. It just looks like it does.'"
- [2023-02-27] **Concept phones are a PR return on R&D that didn't ship.** "'These companies spend a lot on R&D;
  ideas get proposed, mocked up, prototyped, tested — a lot make it into a phone, but the ones that don't get
  repackaged into a nice concept for people to admire the hard work. It's really just about a little positive
  marketing return for the dollars you spent on R&D that didn't make it into products.'"
- [2023-02-27] **The USB-C-iPhone-in-testing analogy.** "'It's kind of like how we can be confident that somewhere
  in Apple's headquarters there's an iPhone with USB-C just for testing — there's no way they haven't tested it. But
  they're not going to turn around and package it up and show it off as a concept phone (it would be funny if they
  did).'"
- [2023-02-27] **The worst-to-best list — killed by cost/reliability.** "'I ranked concept phones by how much I
  wanted them to be real: OnePlus's color-changing electrochromic back (electrochromic glass is insanely expensive);
  Xiaomi's attachable lens (you're still stuck with the 1-inch sensor — just carry a real camera); TCL's paper-like
  matte display (durability/brightness questions); Vivo's unlock-anywhere full-screen fingerprint (an optical sensor
  that big is way too expensive); the rollables (reliability and cost). The ones with real active cooling that
  *shipped* — the ROG Phone, Legion — sacrificed water resistance and thinness.'"
- [2023-02-27] **The verdict — why not both.** "'I can't be salty that they're spending energy on new R&D — it's
  cool that we get new ideas. But also, yeah: a concept phone is kind of just a PR stunt at the end of the day. Why
  not both.'"
