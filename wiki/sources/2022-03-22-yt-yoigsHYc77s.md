# 'The Apple Studio Display is a Bad Deal' (@mkbhd, 2022-03-22)

## Summary
A ~2-week review interrogating whether the $1,600 Apple Studio Display is a good deal. His method: **the appraiser
analogy** — on the spec sheet (27" 5K IPS, 60 Hz, 600 nits, webcam, speakers), it's clearly a **bad deal** vs the
5-year-old LG UltraFine ($1,300) or a $589 4K Dell. But **Apple's habit is to offer a unique combination of things
that defies one-to-one comparison** (like cherry-picked NBA stats) — a genuine 5K iMac-grade panel (218 ppi, clean
integer scaling), **the best-built monitor out** (full-metal, a built-in power supply), and the **best built-in
speakers he's ever heard.** The gripes: a baffling **non-removable power cable** (needs a special tool → thumbs
down), a **mediocre webcam despite a whole A13 Bionic** (still a market for an Opal C1), and a **$400 stand-height
upcharge.** A **rare swing-and-a-miss for a studio person** — but he lands on the Rolls-Royce/self-lacing-Nike
analogy: **if it's exactly what you want (a monitor built for the Apple ecosystem), it doesn't matter that it's not
a good deal — and no, it's not a good deal.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-03-22] **The appraiser analogy — on specs, a bad deal.** "'Most "is it worth it" talk is just the spec
  sheet, like a home appraiser comparing square footage. On specs — 27" 5K IPS, 60 Hz, 600 nits, a webcam, speakers,
  $1,600 — **the tech you're getting for the price is a B [bad] deal** vs the 5-year-old LG UltraFine 5K ($1,300)
  or a well-reviewed $589 4K Dell.'"
- [2022-03-22] **But Apple offers a unique combination that defies comparison.** "'The second you actually consider
  buying, it's the stuff around the panel that makes it — and **Apple's habit is not the best specs but a unique
  combination that's really hard to compare one-to-one, because nobody else has that exact combo** (like the
  cherry-picked NBA stats — "third player ever to get a triple-double before his 26th birthday after eating Apple
  Jacks"). It's a genuine **5K iMac panel** (218 ppi, exactly 2× 1440p so scaling is super clean).'"
- [2022-03-22] **Best-built monitor + best built-in speakers — but a non-removable power cable.** "'It's the
  **best-looking, best-built monitor out** (full-metal, precision perforations, a built-in power supply) — build
  quality that never shows on a spec sheet. The six speakers are **the best built-ins I've ever heard (Apple should
  just make speakers).** But: **a baffling non-removable power cable** (needs a special tool — if it's damaged you
  send the whole thing back → thumbs down), and the webcam **looks substantially worse than expected despite a
  whole A13 Bionic** (flat, no shadows — there's still a market for an Opal C1).'"
- [2022-03-22] **The upcharges + a rare swing-and-a-miss.** "'The $300 nano-texture is impressively good for the
  price (it was $1,000 on the Pro Display) but I still wouldn't recommend it unless a window's behind you. And
  **$400 just to make the stand height-adjustable is ridiculous** on a monitor this expensive. For 2022 you'd
  expect mini-LED or ProMotion or Face ID or a good webcam — **it has none, plus a non-removable cable and a
  non-user-upgradeable stand: a rare swing-and-a-miss for me, someone who works in a studio.**'"
- [2022-03-22] **Is it a good deal? — the Rolls-Royce analogy.** "'Is the Rolls-Royce Ghost a good deal because
  it's the only V12 suicide-door sedan under $400K? Are the self-lacing Nikes a good deal because they're the
  cheapest self-lacing shoes? **Maybe it doesn't matter if it's a good deal if it's exactly what you want** — if
  you're one of the surprisingly large group who wanted a monitor that works great in the Apple ecosystem, this is
  it (made for the Mac Studio). **But no — it's not a good deal.**'"
