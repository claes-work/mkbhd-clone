# Dyson is Back… Why Do These Exist? (@mkbhd, 2024-08-26)

## Summary
A review of the **$500 Dyson OnTrac** headphones — Dyson's second try after the Dyson Zone (the headphones-plus-air-
purifier that remains **"the single dumbest product I've ever reviewed"**); this time they **removed the air purifier**
and kept the big metal headphones. His analytical frame: these only make sense **aimed squarely at the AirPods Max** —
Dyson seems to have seen the AirPods Max "getting dusty in the corner, no updates in four years, still Lightning, still
$550" and decided to **one-up Apple in as many ways as possible** while staying in the same huge-heavy-ANC category:
**$50 cheaper, $50 replaceable ear cups (undercuts Apple), a 55-hour battery (more than double AirPods Max's 20),
cross-platform on iPhone and Android, and a real off button.** The catches: they're **the heaviest headphones he's ever
tested** (451 g, though the battery-on-the-band placement distributes weight surprisingly well), a genuinely clever
**twist-lock swappable ear cups + back plates** (an upsell of the AirPods-Max-mixing trend), and a good back joystick —
but a finicky force-sensor cap control (works ~50% of the time). Sound is **"solid but not amazing — a B+, AirPods Max
beat it by 10–25% in every genre"** (shallow cups → not the widest soundstage), and ANC is decent but **weaker than
Sony/Bose/AirPods**. The real strength is the **world-beating battery**. His **"Business 101"** verdict: Dyson spent
real R&D money on the dumb air-purifier version and **"might as well get their money's worth"** by shipping a larger-
addressable-market product. So **vs. AirPods Max they're not ridiculous** (better-built-value, cleverer, better
battery) — but **vs. any other high-end $350 ANC headphone, they're straight-up overpriced.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2024-08-26] **The Dyson Zone was the dumbest product I've reviewed — this drops the air purifier.** "'A year ago
  Dyson's headphones-plus-air-purifier was, and still is, the single dumbest product I've ever reviewed. So imagine my
  surprise when the new product is just headphones — they removed the stupid air-purifier part and left the giant metal
  Dyson headphones.'"
- [2024-08-26] **Aimed squarely at AirPods Max — and one-ups it.** "'These only make sense once you consider AirPods
  Max — dusty, unchanged in four years, still Lightning, still $550. Dyson aimed to one-up Apple in as many ways as
  possible while staying in the same huge-heavy-ANC category: $50 cheaper, $50 replaceable ear cups (undercuts Apple), a
  55-hour battery (more than double), works on iPhone and Android, and a real off button.'"
- [2024-08-26] **Heaviest headphones ever, but clever swappable design.** "'These are the heaviest headphones I've ever
  tested (451 g), but putting the batteries on the band distributes the weight well — about as comfortable as AirPods
  Max (a low bar). The twist-lock swappable ear cups and back plates are genuinely clever — an upsell of the trend
  people started mixing AirPods Max cups.'"
- [2024-08-26] **Sound is a B+; battery is world-beating.** "'Sound is solid but not amazing — a B+; AirPods Max beat it
  by 10–25% in every genre, and the shallow cups don't give the widest soundstage. ANC is decent but weaker than Sony,
  Bose, and AirPods. The real strength is the world-beating 55+ hour battery — I only charged once in a week and a half
  of heavy testing.'"
- [2024-08-26] **Business 101 — and overpriced vs. anything but AirPods Max.** "'The Business 101 answer to "why do
  these exist" is Dyson spent real R&D money on the dumb air-purifier version and might as well get their money's worth
  with a larger-addressable-market product. Aimed at AirPods Max, they're not ridiculous — better value, cleverer,
  better battery. But compared to any other high-end $350 ANC headphone from the majors, they're straight-up
  overpriced.'"
