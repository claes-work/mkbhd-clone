# iPad Air 2020: The Real iPad Pro Killer! (@mkbhd, 2020-10-21)

## Summary
Review of the $599 4th-gen iPad Air — which he calls the real "iPad Pro killer" because it brings **almost
everything most people care about from the $800 iPad Pro down in price** (with only ProMotion, LiDAR/ultrawide,
and the bigger size as real differences). Threads in **"USB-C is good for everybody, not just the Pro,"** the
**most-people-don't-need-the-Pro** truth, a lament for the **fallen Android-tablet ecosystem**, and Apple's
**attack-a-wider-range-of-prices** strategy.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-10-21] **The Air is the real Pro killer — it takes what most people care about, down in price.** "'The
  iPad Pro is my favorite tablet by a mile, but **most people don't need a Pro, and it starts at $800.** The
  4th-gen Air **starts at $599 and gets pretty much everything most people care about from the Pro** — the boxy
  design, the same Magic Keyboard case (identical dimensions), the quad speakers, the same 12 MP rear camera,
  the A14 chip.'"
- [2020-10-21] **"USB-C is good for everybody" — the dig.** "'iPad Air **now gets USB-C. See, Apple, it's okay
  to put USB-C in products without "Pro" in the name — it's good for everybody.**'" A recurring USB-C jab.
- [2020-10-21] **Touch ID in the power button — "technically backwards, but a non-issue."** "'No Face ID — a
  **7 MP camera and Touch ID built into the power button (like the Pixel 5, technically going backwards on
  biometrics).** But in a mask world a fingerprint is just as convenient. Pro tip that's really common sense:
  **register multiple fingerprints** — both index fingers so any orientation unlocks.'"
- [2020-10-21] **The single camera is fine — tablet cameras are for scanning + FaceTime.** "'One rear 12 MP
  camera — no ultrawide, LiDAR, or flash — but **how often do you need those on a tablet? Pretty much never.**
  I maintain **the most common use of iPad cameras should be document scanning and FaceTime,** and these are
  more than good enough.'"
- [2020-10-21] **ProMotion is the one real gap (his high-refresh bias) — and the Android-tablet lament.** "'The
  only real thing the Pro has that the Air doesn't is **120 Hz ProMotion.** Reviewing an iPad, **I always end up
  reflecting on how the Android tablet ecosystem rose to great heights and fell so far** — all the conjecture
  about what a tablet OS should be — and **now 4 of the top 5 tablets are iPads and they're all the same.**'"
- [2020-10-21] **The buy-advice matrix + the wider-range-of-prices strategy.** "'Get the **Pro over the Air only
  if you want** the ultrawide/LiDAR, 120 Hz ProMotion, or the bigger 13" size — **everyone else should get the
  Air.** This is **Apple's more interesting strategy of attacking a wider range of prices** ($329 iPad up to
  $1,499 Pro), just like the iPhone lineup ($399 SE → $1,299 Pro Max).'" Reinforces the mid-range-tier thesis.

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "See, Apple, it's okay to put USB-C in products without the 'Pro' in the name — it's good for everybody."
> "Most people don't need an iPad Pro, and that thing still starts at 800 bucks."
> "This new iPad more represents Apple's more interesting strategy of attacking a wider range of prices and
> offering more things at more prices."
