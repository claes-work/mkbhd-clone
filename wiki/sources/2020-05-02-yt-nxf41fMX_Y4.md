# iPhone 11 Pro Review: 6 Months Later! (@mkbhd, 2020-05-02)

## Summary
A long-term "6 months later" revisit of the iPhone 11 Pro (a review format he likes). The durable payload is
**"Rule #1 of buying tech: never buy on the promise of future software updates"** — plus his no-case philosophy,
and a candid admission he was **wrong about 3D Touch.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-05-02] **RULE #1 — never buy tech on the promise of future updates.** "'**Never buy a piece of tech based on
  the promise of future software updates — buy it for what it is today**; if it gets better over the years, bonus.
  Especially on iPhones: the **U1 chip's AirDrop-pointing never materialized**; **Deep Fusion shipped and I can't
  tell the difference** (it doesn't even mark which photos used it).'" A promotable buying principle + Apple-stage-
  magic skepticism.
- [2020-05-02] **The long-term-review format + performance headroom.** "'The original review is shot after 1-2
  weeks; there's only so much you learn. **Six months in, the value of the "performance headroom" shows** — the
  A13 will keep this "one of the fastest-feeling 2-3-4-year-old phones." Small specs on paper (4 GB RAM), on pace
  to be fine in four years.'"
- [2020-05-02] **No-case philosophy — "glass is glass."** "'I **never use a case** — it travels in pockets, gym
  bags with cleats, cupholders. The steel sides and matte back held up great, but **the front glass gets micro-
  scratches (worse on iPhones), largely from laying it face-down** — I'm going to stop doing that.'" A stated
  personal habit + a durability observation.
- [2020-05-02] **ADMITTING HE WAS WRONG — "I miss 3D Touch."** "'**I was wrong about 3D Touch — I miss it.** Haptic
  touch (a long press) is nowhere near as good; 3D Touch was a well-done, uniquely-iPhone feature. **I'd trade some
  extra thickness to get it back, and I don't think I'm alone.**'" A candid reversal (dated).
- [2020-05-02] **BIOGRAPHY + the iPhone 12 anticipation.** "'The iPhone SE made me miss small phones, so I **switched
  to the smaller 11 Pro** (from the Max) — first time in ages I'm not on the biggest version. Looking to the
  iPhone 12: boxy iPad-Pro shape, 5G, and a **LiDAR scanner — "a classic case of paying for a feature you may never
  use." If you can keep waiting, wait for the iPhone 12 — that's the one I'm excited to upgrade to.**'"

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "What's rule number one of buying a new piece of tech? Never buy a piece of tech based on the promise of future
> software updates to come. Just buy it for what it is today."
> "I was wrong about 3D Touch. I miss 3D Touch."
