# The Silverado EV Has The Longest Range I've Ever Seen

## Summary

Marques reviews the **Chevy Silverado EV RST** ($100k full-size electric pickup) and calls it a
surprise standout — chiefly for **the longest range he's ever tested on any EV** (rated 440mi;
showed **475mi** at 100%). He praises its "Peak EV" fundamentals (no ignition button, strong regen +
the GM regen paddle, powered frunk, Tesla-Supercharger access) and enormous storage/comfort. Notable
as the **first video shot on the iPhone 16 Pro**, and it corroborates his Cybertruck ownership.

## Key claims (biography marked ⬥)

- **[2024-09-23]** ⬥ Production: **"This is also the first video shot on the iPhone 16 Pro."** (quote)
- **[2024-09-23]** ⭐ Range: 205kWh battery, rated 440mi; **"when I first plugged it up to 100% it was
  showing 475 miles — that makes it the most range I have ever tested on any electric vehicle ever."**
  (Caveat: some Lucids cross 500 but he hadn't driven them yet.) Adjusts up with regen/no towing.
  (quote)
- **[2024-09-23]** "Peak EV" fundamentals he loves: **no ignition button** ("you just put the brake on
  and go… that's actually pretty rare — basically just Teslas"), strong liftoff regen + a two-stage GM
  regen paddle ("you basically don't have to use the brake pedal at all"), a powered frunk, and
  **charging at Tesla Superchargers via adapter** ("~15–17,000 Superchargers, plug-and-play"). Super
  Cruise, Google built-in, physical HVAC. (quote + paraphrase)
- **[2024-09-23]** ⬥ BIOGRAPHY: **"I have a Cybertruck — I realize these trucks are always huge, but
  this just feels like I'm in a house right now."** (Corroborates Cybertruck ownership at this date;
  he later sold it.) Fits behind his own **6'3"** driving position "lounging back." (quote)
- **[2024-09-23]** ⚠️ Trade-off: **"the least athletic of the full-size electric pickups"** (no Sprint/
  sport mode like the Cybertruck's ~800hp or Rivian's ~1,000hp) — "a direct trade-off with the 400+
  miles of range, and I think a lot of people buying a pickup honestly don't care." (quote)
- **[2024-09-23]** Verdict: **"This shot right up to the top of my list of best full-size EV pickups…
  the one with the most range and the most comfort. Well done, Chevy."** (quote)
