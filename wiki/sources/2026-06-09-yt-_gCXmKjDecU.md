# WWDC 2026 Impressions: Yeah, That's About Right ★★

## Summary

Marques's take on WWDC 2026: Apple **breezed through the OS updates** (iOS 27, macOS "Golden
Gate") to focus on two themes — **Trust & Safety for kids** and **the new Apple Intelligence /
Siri**. He *likes* the breezing, because after the sloppy Liquid Glass era it was a "holistic"
pass of under-the-hood fixes (80% faster AirDrop, custom AirPods EQ "finally"). The new Siri is
**"exactly what we expected — straight down Broadway,"** playing it safe (no agentic overreach)
but leaning on its one edge: **it knows what's on your iPhone.** A landmark WWDC read, capped by
the "**Golden Gate** = golden gates on the walled garden" line and a sly **foldable tease**. ★★.

## Key claims

- **[2026-06-09]** Apple **breezed through** the OS updates (iOS/iPadOS 27, watchOS, tvOS, macOS
  **"Golden Gate"**) — and he liked it, because it was a **"holistic"** pass of needed fixes after
  the Liquid Glass era "felt a little sloppy" (battery/performance/readability complaints): faster
  app launch, smoother animations, better Spotlight indexing, **80% faster AirDrop** ("as long as
  you also make AirDrop work more of the time"), a **Liquid Glass transparency slider** ("no one
  asked for" more-clear), and **custom EQ for AirPods "finally… when everyone else has had it for
  years."** (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2026-06-09]** **Trust & Safety for kids**: child accounts, granular Screen Time/scheduling,
  app/website/contact controls. The upside is real parental safety; **the cynical read is "get your
  kid an iPhone"** — the successor to "get your mom an iPhone" — "the youngest possible iPhone users
  means more iPhones in the future." (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2026-06-09]** **The new Siri is "exactly what we were expecting — straight down Broadway. No
  dramatic surprises… no letdowns either."** Trigger by swiping down the Dynamic Island or holding
  power; a more expressive voice; **a conversational chat with a world-knowledge base that cites
  sources**; a **new Siri app** syncing conversation history across the ecosystem. "They played it
  extremely safe — did the stuff you'd expect Apple to do and nothing more." (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2026-06-09]** The **live demos** throughout were "almost as if to say: we know you're rightfully
  skeptical, we learned our lesson from last time." It can read your messages/photos/calendar and
  take basic actions (send, add, remind) — **not** the agentic "buy concert tickets" overreach Google
  showed at I/O ("it's just gonna stop short and add it to your calendar — do the safe thing").
  (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2026-06-09]** The strategic edge: **Apple enters the AI category the way it always does — by
  being the best one to work with the iPhone.** "GPT can't pull from your messages, Claude can't pull
  from your Apple Calendar, Gemini doesn't see this stuff — it's Siri that's most helpful with all the
  personal context that never leaves your device." Plus visual intelligence surfaced in the Camera
  app ("you could see how this would be useful if you were making smart glasses"). (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2026-06-09]** Third-party apps: via **App Intents**, you **can** summon a third-party app if you
  tell Siri to (play it in Spotify/Pocket Casts) but **it can't be a default** — he wants to push how
  far it goes (geofenced recurring tasks in **TickTick** instead of Reminders? WhatsApp group chats?).
  (paraphrase)
- **[2026-06-09]** **The most disappointing slide**: the on-device new-Siri models run **only on the
  iPhone Air and 17 Pro (the 12GB-RAM phones)** — "**kind of hilarious considering the iPhone 16 was
  built from the ground up for Apple Intelligence and is already outdated before that stuff even
  ships**" (the only difference for other phones is the new voice + dictation). (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2026-06-09]** Other AI: **describe-a-Shortcut/Safari-extension in natural language and it builds
  it** ("exact same thing Google did with Home automations"); Safari tab auto-grouping; Apple Home
  notification consolidation ("long overdue"); **AI photo edits that extend the image edges up to 25%**
  and **spatial reframing** — "the closest they've ever gotten to bending the definition of what is a
  photo… a very technically impressive party trick, but I think still a party trick" (limited daily
  use → **pay for more via iCloud**); and a **Passwords app that agentically logs into a site and
  changes your weak password** (which "locks you a little more into that Passwords app"). (paraphrase)
- **[2026-06-09]** **"The irony is not lost on me that they named macOS Golden Gate — because when the
  walls of that walled garden are golden gates, maybe it's not so bad in there."** (quote) Notes **the
  HomePod was not mentioned once**, and a sly **foldable tease**: developer tools for apps that resize
  to multiple aspect ratios — **"tell me you have a foldable coming without telling me."** (paraphrase)
