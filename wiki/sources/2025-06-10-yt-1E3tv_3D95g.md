# WWDC 2025 Impressions: Liquid Glass! (@mkbhd, 2025-06-10)

## Summary
Apple's 2025 software keynote — interesting because, in an AI-everywhere landscape where Apple feels left behind, they
faced two options and **chose to shelf some AI and show a whole bunch of *other* stuff — "and it was actually kind of a
lot."** Everything is **unified to version 26** (matching next year, like Samsung did in 2020) and gets **Liquid
Glass** — a glassy, mostly-transparent UI treatment on every window/icon/sidebar (even a clear home-screen option) —
whose recurring caveat is **readability** ("there's a reason we add backgrounds in video: contrast"). iOS 26: a
redesigned camera app with hidden UI (settings buried — "implies nobody changes these"); the phone app **copies the
Pixel's Call Screening and Hold For Me** ("matched or copied"); iMessage polls/typing-indicators; and **live
translation** in FaceTime/Messages/Phone ("this is the year of live translation — a whole essay brewing"). watchOS: a
wrist-flick dismiss + a "Workout Buddy" ("Spotify DJ for workouts"; still no group fitness challenges). macOS Tahoe: the
new **Spotlight "getting sherlocked" — it copies Raycast** (his favorite productivity app; shortcuts, actions, window
management). visionOS: pinnable widgets, it finally remembers window positions (his old top complaint), better
personas. **The biggest, most fascinating update is iPadOS 26** — a genuine **"game changer" that fits**: Mac-like
free-form multi-windowing (stoplight controls, a real menu bar), a Finder-like Files app, background exports (close
Final Cut and the export continues) — "**I never thought Apple would go this far** (they usually won't cannibalize the
Mac)"; it moves the iPad Pro "way further as a compelling laptop alternative." On AI: **"they said 'Siri' twice in a
90-minute keynote"**; the big LLM Siri upgrade is pushed to **"the coming year" (2026)**; his read — **"Apple's AI
strength, if any, is integration, not model quality"** — plus on-device foundational models for developers, and Visual
Intelligence gaining on-screen (a circle-to-search equivalent).

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2025-06-10] **Apple shelved some AI and showed a lot of other stuff — unified version 26 + Liquid Glass.** "'In an
  AI-everywhere landscape where Apple feels left behind, they could double down on Apple Intelligence or shelf some and
  show other stuff. They did the second — and it was actually kind of a lot. Everything's unified to version 26 and
  gets Liquid Glass, a glassy transparent UI everywhere. My main concern is readability — there's a reason we add
  backgrounds in video: contrast.'"
- [2025-06-10] **iOS 26 copies Pixel features; and it's the year of live translation.** "'The phone app matched or
  copied the Pixel's Call Screening and Hold For Me. And Apple added live translation to FaceTime, Messages, and Phone
  with on-device models — I feel a whole essay brewing about how this is the year of live translation; it's happening
  all at once.'"
- [2025-06-10] **The new Spotlight is "getting sherlocked" — it copies Raycast.** "'macOS Tahoe's new Spotlight does
  almost everything my favorite productivity app, Raycast, does — shortcuts, actions, window management, entire command
  strings without touching the mouse. That's getting sherlocked. It doesn't do the GPT/Gemini tab yet, but who's to say
  next year.'"
- [2025-06-10] **iPadOS 26 is the game changer — Mac-like, and I never thought Apple would go this far.** "'The most
  fascinating update is iPadOS 26 — a genuine game changer that fits: free-form Mac-like multi-windowing with stoplight
  controls and a real menu bar, a Finder-like Files app, background exports (close Final Cut and it keeps exporting). I
  never thought Apple would go this far — they usually won't cannibalize the Mac. It moves the iPad Pro way further as a
  compelling laptop alternative. It feels like, in lieu of AI, they gave us the iPad wishlist.'"
- [2025-06-10] **They said "Siri" twice in 90 minutes — the upgrade is pushed to 2026.** "'They said the word "Siri"
  twice in the entire 90-minute keynote — clearly not a focus. The big LLM Siri upgrade got a blink-and-you-miss-it
  line: "this work required more time... in the coming year" — 2026. To me, Apple's AI strength, if any, is integration,
  not model quality — it's how plugged into the ecosystem the features are. Plus on-device foundational models for
  developers, and Visual Intelligence now works on-screen (a circle-to-search equivalent).'"

## Notable verbatim quotes
