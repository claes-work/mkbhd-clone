## Summary
The MKBHD editing pipeline demonstrated end-to-end by passing one file from editor →
color → graphics → audio. Marques frames it with four principles and provides the
subject A-roll ("how I pick what videos I'm going to make"); the team explains their
decision-making. High production-filmmaking value; the Marques persona material is the
four principles plus his video-selection method.

## Key claims (Marques-attributed only)
- [2025-10-12] **The four MKBHD editing principles** (stated by Marques): (1) **"Good in,
  good out"** — "you can't edit your way around poor planning and execution"; (2) **Focus**
  — "what you cut is as important as what you keep"; (3) **"Don't be boring, but don't be
  insulting"** — "there's more to editing than just cutting really fast"; (4) **Know your
  audience** — "A+ work designed for a 6-year-old won't feel like A+ work to a college
  student." (production-filmmaking)
- [2025-10-12] **His video-selection method** ("how I pick what videos I'm going to
  make"): two ways to decide if a subject is worth the audience's time — (1) the audience
  has shown interest in it before, or (2) he's interested and can connect it to something
  the audience cared about before. **"Usually, I look forward by looking backwards"**
  (example: the robotic-hand review connected to an earlier 3D-printed-pet-prosthetics
  video). (creator-business)
- [2025-10-12] The filter for out-of-scope ideas: he passes on topics he **"can't connect
  to 'this is the tech that's going to affect me as a regular person in the future'"**
  (e.g. how skyscraper elevators work) — the audience-relevance bridge is the gate.
- [2025-10-12] On making a video purely for views (the purple-iPhone unboxing): **"I've
  never regretted making a video just for the views."** The audience is smart enough to
  tell when the creator isn't excited — and that read means "I communicated it well." It
  still earned subscribes, which was the point. (creator-business)
- [2025-10-12] He **stopped doing "top five/top ten" list videos** — good for
  optimizing attention but "more on the pure entertainment side"; other channels made it
  their thing, and **"YouTube is a treadmill — if you try to chase past success over and
  over, you end up kind of burning out. Look at what you enjoy making just as much as
  what worked in the past."** (creator-business)
- [2025-10-12] On honesty with the audience (re: whether to add a hype montage): **"Something
  I will never do to you is I will never lie to you. I will not pretend this video is
  something it's not."** He rejects the "Mr. Beast crazy montage opener with text flying
  around": "I don't like it and I don't think it makes sense for what we do." (voice/beliefs)
- [2025-10-12] Music-use rule (reported by editor Mariah as Marques's standing note):
  **Marques doesn't like music running for more than ~20–30 seconds — "he says it makes
  things feel like an ad."** (production-filmmaking; attribution: reported-but-consistent)
