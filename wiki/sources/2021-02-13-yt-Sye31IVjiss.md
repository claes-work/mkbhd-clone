# How to Upgrade a PlayStation 5! (@mkbhd, 2021-02-13)

## Summary
A practical PS5-accessories guide (lighter ingest) after 3 months with the console. The durable persona signals:
his **matte-black / customization** streak (dbrand "dark plates," ColorWare custom controllers), the honest
"which accessories actually matter" filter, and the caveat that **the SSD/external-storage upgrade only helps PS4
games.** A recurring practical value: **ventilation + cleaning is the best thing you can do for performance.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-02-13] **Dark plates > skins — the easiest way to change the look.** "'The alien-spaceship side panels are
  just plastic and pop right off — **dbrand's matte-black "dark plates" clip on the same way (better than a skin —
  way easier to install, and impressively high quality).** They didn't sponsor this, I just like them; matte black
  is the best color, obviously (I wish there were more colors/shapes).'"
- [2021-02-13] **Controller customization — faceplates + ColorWare.** "'The DualSense black piece is also a
  faceplate (harder to pry). **ColorWare** takes apart, paints, and reassembles devices — "an incredible job every
  time" (a one-of-one MKBHD Edition controller). But **matte black on a controller might not be the move — it gets
  the most wear;** maybe a matte red or blue.'" (These are "some of my favorite controllers ever.")
- [2021-02-13] **The SSD/storage caveat — PS4 games only.** "'You can add an internal M.2 SSD (needs an upcoming
  firmware update) or an external drive — **but external/expanded storage only supports PS4 games, not PS5 games**
  (a bummer, but useful for offloading a PS4 library). Shout-out to Austin Evans's ultimate PS5 video.'"
- [2021-02-13] **The practical value — ventilation + cleaning beats gimmick docks.** "'Docks just hold it upright
  + charge controllers (the fans don't change performance — "don't expect a frame-rate bump"). **The best thing
  you can actually do for a PS5 is keep it in a well-ventilated area and clean out the dust every once in a while**
  (PS4s got loud because the fans work harder to move air).'" A recurring "the boring maintenance matters" note.
