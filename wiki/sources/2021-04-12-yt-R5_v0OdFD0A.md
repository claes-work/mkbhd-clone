# The World's Largest Smartphone Camera! (@mkbhd, 2021-04-12)

## Summary
A look at the Xiaomi Mi 11 Ultra — a phone whose every rear camera is a "world's largest" (the near-1-inch main
sensor, the widest ultrawide, the deepest 120x zoom) plus **a screen on the camera bump.** The durable ideas:
the **big-sensor look** (shallow depth of field all the time, night-mode light-gathering that beats the iPhone) —
but **glass still matters** (it won't match an RX100); **120x is "the novelty drawer"**; and the **seeing-vs-using
distinction** (the huge bump looks like a lot, but your finger rests under it comfortably). Ends with the Retro
Tech Season 2 trailer.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-04-12] **The near-1-inch sensor + the big-sensor look — but glass still matters.** "'The main is a
  **1/1.12" sensor — really close to 1 inch (like the Sony RX100).** Fitting nearly that size in a phone is
  incredible. **Can it hang with an RX100? Still mostly no — the glass makes a huge difference even with a similar
  sensor.** But it delivers the big-sensor look: **high detail + shallow depth of field all the time** (any close
  subject blurs the background — not portrait mode). The trade-off: **fringing on very close subjects** (the plane
  of focus is thin). "Naturally sharp, not over-processed."'"
- [2021-04-12] **Night mode — the light-gathering advantage (faster than the iPhone).** "'The huge sensor lets in
  tons of light — **low-noise night shots at a faster shutter.** Same scene: a **1.5 s exposure on the Mi 11 Ultra
  vs 3 s on the iPhone 12 Pro** — both detailed and similar, but the Xiaomi is brighter and took less time.'"
- [2021-04-12] **The widest ultrawide + "120x is the novelty drawer."** "'Since the LG G5, I've seen a lot of
  ultrawides — **this is the widest yet** (soft/distorted corners, but crop in and it's a normal, flexible
  ultrawide). The 48 MP 5x optical → **120x digital is the biggest zoom today, but past ~50x it's a smear of
  pixels — I file 120x directly into the novelty drawer.** The 5–20x, though, is genuinely sharp with shallow
  depth of field.'"
- [2021-04-12] **The camera works the chip hard — battery + the reverse shutter-lag.** "'Heavy camera use **tanks
  the battery (5,000 mAh, ~45% → 25% in a couple minutes — the software literally blames the camera).** And what
  feels like shutter lag is actually **a lagging shutter animation — the photo captures the instant you tap; the
  opposite of the OnePlus 9's problem (there you think you got it and didn't; here you think you missed it but
  got it).**'"
- [2021-04-12] **The screen on the camera bump — mostly a selfie viewfinder (and it stops the rock).** "'The
  1.1" AMOLED on the bump is literally the **Mi Band 5's display** ("like they had extras left over") — shows time/
  battery/notifications and a custom image (the thumbnail). **The only actually useful thing: a viewfinder for
  high-quality selfies on the massive main sensor.** Bonus: **it brought the bump across the whole back, so it
  doesn't rock on a table.** "Between screen and no screen, I'll take the screen all day."'"
- [2021-04-12] **THE SEEING-vs-USING distinction — and the rest is "sick."** "'A lot of you said "make it thicker,
  10,000 mAh, kill the bump" — but **it's already a heavy ceramic phone; add all that battery and you lose the
  grip and it's one big slick thing.** In use, **my finger naturally rests under the camera and props it up — the
  difference between seeing the phone and using it.** The rest is ultra: 888, QHD 120 Hz, 67 W wired + wireless.'"
  (Ends teasing **Retro Tech Season 2** — dropping daily.)
