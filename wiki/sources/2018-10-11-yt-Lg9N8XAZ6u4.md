# Pixel 3 XL Second Impression: Notch City! (@mkbhd, 2018-10-11)

## Summary
A "second first impressions" after two days with his own SIM in the Pixel 3 XL. The standout is his **user-vs-
YouTuber** framing of the notch — a genuinely useful distinction about who a design flaw actually bothers — plus
notes on the best Android haptics, RAW+JPEG in the stock camera, and a longevity watch tied to his Pixel 2.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-10-11] **THE NOTCH — the user-vs-YouTuber perspective (a real insight).** "**As a person just *using* the
  phone** — scrolling, swiping, tweeting — **I'm numb to the notch; I don't see it anymore**, it's like every other
  notched phone I've used all year (OnePlus 6, iPhone X/XS…). **But when I'm in *YouTuber* mode, pointing a camera
  at it, it's this horrible ugly bathtub — a face staring back at you that you can't ignore.**" A sharp articulation
  of why a design flaw reads differently to a reviewer than to an owner. (Notes many people *are* coming from
  no-notch phones, so it lands differently for them.)
- [2018-10-11] **Android hides the notch by default (the other reason it fades).** "The default is basically to
  **hide it** — the notification tray, the camera app, Spotify, Waze all ignore the notch area; apps that use it
  just color-match a comically big status bar. Google was clever on stage never embracing it." (Some apps still
  need updating — IG Stories, even Google's own YouTube Studio, look off.)
- [2018-10-11] **Best-in-class Android haptics.** "The Pixel 3 haptic engine is **the best I've felt in an Android
  phone** — Google knows it and triggers it often (the tray, typing, every tap); nice, clicky, tight." A specific
  hardware win.
- [2018-10-11] **RAW+JPEG in the stock camera (craft appreciation).** "There's now a **RAW+JPEG option in the stock
  camera** — I love seeing that baked into a phone that knows it'll be used for photography; slow down, shoot RAW,
  edit in Lightroom." Ties to his RAW-workflow values.
- [2018-10-11] **The longevity watch + the coming camera shootout.** Explicitly ties back to his **Pixel 2 slowdown**
  video: "it's got me curious how the Pixel 3 holds up with **4 GB of RAM**." And he plans a **direct camera
  comparison** (iPhone XS vs Pixel 3 vs Note 9 + others — maybe the RED Hydrogen / Huawei) "to see if you can tell
  the difference between the best smartphone cameras." (Also: the YouTube app plays **off-center** on the XL notch —
  fixable in software.)
