# Moto Edge+ Review: We Need to Talk! (@mkbhd, 2020-04-30)

## Summary
Review of the Motorola Edge+ — Motorola's return to flagships. Good bones (SD865, 5,000 mAh, a headphone jack) but
"far from perfect," and the frame is a **formal petition against extreme curved "waterfall" displays** and a
broader **"it's okay to roll back on things"** argument.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-04-30] **THE TED TALK — "it's time to be done with curved displays."** "'The waterfall curve is **prime form
  over function** — accidental touches never fully got ironed out despite years of palm-rejection work; it's not
  worth the extra cost. Samsung's S20 is one of the first going **back to flatter** — and **it's okay to roll back
  on things.** We had capacitive buttons, ring flashes, branded bezels, port flaps — we realized there were better
  ways, and they're gone. My formal petition: **work these extreme 90° waterfall edges back.**'" A promotable
  "it's-okay-to-undo-a-trend" framework.
- [2020-04-30] **The viewfinder-oversharpening problem — you can misfocus.** "'Motorola's camera app **sharpens the
  viewfinder to ~100% (like the Lightroom Clarity slider maxed)** — so you **can't tell if you nailed focus**, and
  I legitimately mis-focused shots multiple times because they looked sharp in the viewfinder. Tone it down.'" A
  specific, dated craft critique.
- [2020-04-30] **The big-sensor problem + the missing dual aperture.** "'Another **108 MP** (like the S20 Ultra /
  Mi Note 10) — nice colors/detail, but the **huge sensor's super-shallow depth of field** means close subjects
  fall out of focus ("half a sign sharp, half blurry"). We could use that dual-aperture feature again.'"
- [2020-04-30] **The Rusty-flagship tells.** "'The **worst under-glass fingerprint reader I've ever seen** (most
  failures, slowest); the **most bloatware on a premium phone in a long time** (a Verizon exclusive — two Candy
  Crushes, etc.); a **rattly $400-tier vibration motor.** Feels like Motorola got rusty making flagships.'"
- [2020-04-30] **The verdict — fine, but the OnePlus 8 Pro beats it.** "'I liked it, didn't love it. Coming from
  the **OnePlus 8 Pro** — higher res, higher refresh, cleaner software, no bloat, faster charging, less curve —
  what reason is there to buy the Moto at $1,000? Motorola's not done with flagships, though — I hope.'"

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "It's okay to roll back on things... this is my formal petition to realize that these curved-over screens, at
> least these extreme 90-degree waterfall edges, we can work it back. Thanks for coming to my TED Talk."
> "Motorola, why are you sharpening the viewfinder this much? You have a good camera — tone it down, relax."
