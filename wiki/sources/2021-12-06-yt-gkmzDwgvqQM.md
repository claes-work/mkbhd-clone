# This Smartphone is Built Different... (Fairphone 4) (@mkbhd, 2021-12-06)

## Summary
A review of the Fairphone 4 — a modular, repairable, sustainably-sourced Android phone ("be the change you want to
see"). His verdict: **not a spec winner, but the furthest anyone has ever gone on sustainability/repairability** —
a **10/10 iFixit repair score** (pop-out battery, 12 screws, user-replaceable modules, a 5-year parts+warranty
pledge). He's honest about the trade-offs vs a Pixel 6 (mid-range chip, smaller battery, 60 Hz LCD, so-so
cameras). Two durable ideas: **there's no 100%-sustainable phone, but Fairphone sets the bar**; and a structural
tension — **to guarantee 5 years of parts, Fairphone must use "mostly average" parts, whereas we (reviewers, the
market) reward the non-average, bleeding-edge phones — which is by definition the opposite of sustainable.** He
makes a **pledge to add a repairability/sustainability section to every future smartphone review.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-12-06] **10/10 repairability — repair like the good old days.** "'~$650, a clean basic 6.3" phone — but
  it's designed from the ground up to be sustainable, modular, repairable, and upgradable by you. **Pop off the
  recycled-plastic back, swap the battery like the good old days, 12 Phillips screws and each part pops out like a
  puzzle piece — I literally removed just the USB-C port via one ribbon cable.** iFixit gives it a 10/10 repair
  score (no glue, no complex screws), with a 5-year warranty and parts pledge. It's a dream.'"
- [2021-12-06] **The honest trade-offs.** "'The instinct to review everything comes out — vs a similarly-priced
  Pixel 6, there are obvious trade-offs: **a mid-range Snapdragon 750G, a smaller 3,900 mAh battery, a 1080p 60 Hz
  LCD (a weak point), acceptable-but-not-great cameras, only IP54.** You have to really value the repairability
  and sustainability to pick it.'"
- [2021-12-06] **No perfect sustainable phone — but the furthest anyone's gone.** "'**There's no such thing as a
  100% sustainable phone** — go back far enough and something's imperfect (even Fairphone: no charger in the box).
  But **Fairphone represents the furthest anyone has ever gone by far** — recycled/responsibly-sourced materials,
  ASI-certified aluminum (no child labor), and an e-waste-neutral pledge (collect equal e-waste per phone sold).
  There are ~300 million phones hibernating in drawers in just Germany and France — imagine the world.'"
- [2021-12-06] **The average-parts tension.** "'A curious thing: **to offer 5 years of replacement parts, Fairphone
  has to keep good relationships with suppliers — so it must use mostly average-size, average-spec parts. But we
  reward the phones pushing the limits, the unique and different ones — which is by definition the opposite of
  average, the opposite of sustainable.** A small company can't design its own silicon/sensors/displays, so it's
  bound by suppliers.'"
- [2021-12-06] **The review pledge + recycle your drawer phone.** "'Fairphone will never out-sell Apple or Samsung
  — but it's proving there are real ways for all companies to do better, and small changes add up. **So I'm making
  a pledge to include a repairability/sustainability section in all my future smartphone reviews** — to shine a
  light and give companies a reason to make those compromises. And you and I: recycle that phone in your drawer.
  We only have one Earth — hot take — we should take better care of it.'"
