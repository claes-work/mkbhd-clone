# The BMW iX is Only Guilty of 1 Sin!

## Summary

Marques reviews the **BMW iX** and reaches a tidy verdict: **its biggest — really, only — sin is that
it's ugly.** Everything else (range, space, comfort, features) is solid; it's a well-executed luxury
EV crossover held back mainly by looks (which slowly "grew on him"). Along the way he flags his usual
critiques (capacitive/haptic buttons, a missing one-pedal mode, gimmicky gesture controls) and notes
it's not a ground-up EV. Fully Marques-narrated.

## Key claims

- **[2023-03-17]** ⭐ The verdict: "I'm adding BMW to my list of companies electrifying well (i4, i7,
  and this iX). **Its biggest sin is just being ugly — that's the worst thing about this car.** It's
  $85k, so you start thinking about what you're paying for — but the fundamentals are all there." (quote)
- **[2023-03-17]** Fundamentals: ~300 mi real range ("surprisingly efficient for the battery size"),
  200+ kW charging, good space, power-folding seats. **Not a ground-up EV** ("no huge frunk, no cavernous
  extra space") but well-tailored. He watched it slowly grow on him over a week ("I still see nostrils
  or teeth"). (paraphrase)
- **[2023-03-17]** ⚠️ The recurring critiques: **the crystal iDrive controller is "prettier than
  functional"**; **haptic buttons "particularly slow — you have to look down"**; HVAC mostly on the
  touchscreen; **no one-pedal-drive setting** he could find (regen stops at ~6 mph); a **slight
  accelerator latency** ("like a kick-down"); and **gimmicky gesture controls** ("clearly a gimmick —
  there are real volume/track buttons on the wheel"). Comfortable, light steering, rear-wheel steer,
  good Harman Kardon speakers after EQ. (quote)
- **[2023-03-17]** ⭐ Verdict/positioning: "At $80–90k you could get a Model X, R1S, or Genesis GV60 —
  they hit different strengths. **You'd get this if you're a fan of BMW, of comfort, light steering, a
  relaxed ride, and 300 miles is enough — some people are just diehard BMW people, and they like
  this.**" (quote)

## Notable verbatim quotes

- "The crystal controller is much prettier than it is functional."
