# Lotus Eletre and Emeya First Look and Drive!

## Summary

Marques spends a day with two new electric Lotuses — the **Emeya** sedan (first look) and the
**Eletre** SUV (first drive) — and reads them as proof of **how obvious the electrification of the
future is**: even Lotus, "known for ultra-light sports cars (they made the original Tesla Roadster's
chassis)," is going straight to electric, not even hybrids. Loaded with tech (and "a lot of moving
parts, maybe some unnecessary"), the heavy Eletre "can't hide its weight" but is **the most fun to
drive** in its segment. Fully Marques-narrated.

## Key claims

- **[2023-09-08]** ⭐ The thesis: "**Lotus is the antithesis of what we know** — they make the lightest
  stuff, they made the original Tesla Roadster chassis — and here they are going right to electric, not
  even hybrids. **It just shows how obvious the electrification of the future of cars is.** Everyone's
  doing it — it's just a matter of *how*." (quote)
- **[2023-09-08]** The Emeya (sedan): "a really good-looking car, no bad angle"; a lot of tech AND analog;
  and **"a lot of moving parts, maybe some unnecessary"** (retracting door handles, retracting lidar,
  active wings, motorized grille flaps, power-opening doors) — "that's kind of the point of an $80–100k
  car: you want it to feel special." Unreal-Engine UI ("the smoothest car UI I've ever seen"), KEF
  speakers, an electrochromic roof; ⚠️ HVAC vent-direction stuck on the touchscreen. (paraphrase)
- **[2023-09-08]** The Eletre (SUV, driven): reminds him of **"somewhere between the Taycan (handling)
  and the EV6 GT (acceleration/fun)"**; ~260–300 mi range; a two-speed rear motor you can **feel shift
  gears** (like the Taycan); ⚠️ **"this is going to be the first Lotus somebody drives where they're
  like, well, it can't really hide how heavy it is — sorry, Lotus, it doesn't feel light."** (quote)
- **[2023-09-08]** ⭐ Verdict: "If you want an electric SUV, the ~$50–70k options are the Ioniq 5 / EV6,
  then up to the Model X Plaid / R1S / this. **The R1S is my favorite in this segment for what it does,
  but this is the most fun to drive** — more stable, more comfortable, better-handling than the Model X
  (which accelerates harder). For their first try, Lotus did this pretty well." (quote)
