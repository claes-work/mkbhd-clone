# Galaxy Z Fold 2 Review: Folding King... But For What? (@mkbhd, 2020-09-12)

## Summary
Full review of the $2,000 Z Fold 2 5G — **"the only phone that folds I can actually recommend any group of
people buy in 2020."** But the review's spine is a repeated rhetorical question: **"it's the king... but for
what?"** — what does it mean to be **the best thing in a category most people should still avoid?** A landmark
articulation of how he reviews category-leading-but-immature tech.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-09-12] **The verdict — the king, best specs of any foldable, "gen 2.5."** "'This is **the best folding
  phone** and the only one I can recommend any group actually buy in 2020. Best specs (865+, 12 GB), best
  cameras (~S20 tier), best screens, best battery, best fingerprint reader, best speakers, one of the best
  hinges — **thanks to Samsung having the most iterations to work on this, I'd call it "gen 2.5."**'"
- [2020-09-12] **THE REFRAIN — "it's the king, but for what?"** "'**But for what, though? What does it mean to
  be the best thing in a category most people should still avoid in 2020?** If this is the best we can do and
  there's still a pronounced crease, a factory screen protector you're told not to remove that collects dust in
  the camera cutout where you can see they took a shortcut, and 75% of apps look like stretched awkward versions
  — **it's the king, but for what?**'" A promotable framing for reviewing immature category leaders.
- [2020-09-12] **THE COVER-SCREEN PARADOX — a bigger outside screen is a "win-lose."** "'The bigger screens are
  spec-for-spec everything you'd want, but in use they're a **win-lose.** Part of the old Fold's tiny cover
  screen was that it was **a barrier to entry to getting lost in your phone** — quick task, then off. Now the
  big outside screen made me **hang out on the outside like a normal phone (a terrible habit),** and reachability
  got worse because it's so tall.'" A nuanced usability insight, not a spec-sheet reading.
- [2020-09-12] **The battery anecdote — improved for a counterintuitive reason.** "'I ended days with more
  battery (35–45%) than on the old Fold — **not from optimization or specs, but because I used the smaller 60 Hz
  outside screen so much more,** which saved battery. A weird usage habit; your mileage may vary.'"
- [2020-09-12] **The inside screen is "all win" — but app optimization lags the hardware.** "'The main display
  is pretty much all win — bigger, brighter, 120 Hz — **every time I unfold it I want to find something to do.**
  But the software/app optimization is still catching up to the odd aspect ratio: videos rarely fill it, lots
  of blown-up phone apps, though tablet-mode apps (Spotify, Gmail) turn it into a productivity machine.'"
- [2020-09-12] **The $2,000 context — "nowhere near the end of the line."** "'It's **nowhere near mature** — the
  outside screen is still slightly worse than a normal phone, the inside slightly worse than a normal tablet.
  For $2,000 you could get an iPad Pro + a Pixel 4a + Sony noise-cancelers with money left over — **but the one
  thing you get here that you can't elsewhere is the most complete, most evolved foldable, all in one pocketable
  device. That's a pretty sweet start.**'"
