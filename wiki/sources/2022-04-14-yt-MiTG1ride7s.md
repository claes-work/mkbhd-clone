# I Gave the Pixel Another Chance... (@mkbhd, 2022-04-14)

## Summary
A follow-up on the Pixel 6 Pro, six months after launch — and a window into a **reviewer's dilemma.** His daily
driver for ~1.5 years has been the **Galaxy S21 Ultra** (he keeps coming back to it). Many assumed he'd switch to
the Pixel 6 — but **it got buggier and buggier after the review window** (by January he tweeted he could no longer
recommend it at $900). The durable insight: **Pixels age concerningly quickly** — the opposite of the normal
"first year is peaches, then slow decline" — which is hard to catch in a **1–2-week review** vs the years people
own a phone (it happened before with the Pixel 2 XL). He re-tested after the updates: **the March update fixed
~95% of the frame drops and the camera speed**, but auto-brightness, the fingerprint reader, and modem/connectivity
are still worse. **Better, but still not enough to recommend at $900** — his SIM goes back in the S21 Ultra. He
stays **optimistic for the next Tensor / Pixel.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-04-14] **The S21 Ultra is still his daily driver.** "'What phone do I actually use? For ~1.5 years on
  Android it's been the **Galaxy S21 Ultra — I keep coming back to it** (the design, display, rock-solid cameras,
  feature-rich software), even after the S22 Ultra. A lot of people assumed I'd switch to the Pixel when it came
  out — but as I said in the review, it's not perfect, and it got more and more buggy.'"
- [2022-04-14] **The reviewer's dilemma — 1–2 weeks vs years of ownership.** "'That's the challenge of reviewing:
  **people own a phone for years, but I have to experience as much as possible in one to two weeks and condense it
  into a video.** A couple months after the review period, the Pixel 6 Pro slowly started unraveling — and it's
  happened before (the same thing with the Pixel 2 XL, until I switched off it).'"
- [2022-04-14] **Pixels age concerningly quickly.** "'This is a very strange trajectory: normally a phone is great
  the first year, then slowly declines — **but the Pixels age concerningly quickly, and Google has to stay on top
  of it with a frantic early software update or two.** I'm not sure why Pixels are more susceptible; I'd hoped their
  own Tensor chip + quick updates would help — I was wrong.'"
- [2022-04-14] **What broke, and what the update fixed.** "'The big problems: **the 120 Hz display constantly
  dropping way below 120 (often under 60) with stuttery animations; terrible auto-brightness and lock screen; a
  slow fingerprint reader; a slowing camera app.** Six months in, on the March update, **the frame drops are ~95%
  fixed and the camera is back to normal — huge — but auto-brightness is still bad, the fingerprint reader is still
  slow, and the modem/connectivity is noticeably worse** (a dead-zone takes longer to reacquire; Wi-Fi calling has
  failed me two days running in the studio).'"
- [2022-04-14] **Better, but still can't recommend at $900 — and still optimistic.** "'If the acceptable bug level
  for a $900 phone was here, it used to be way up here, and now it's here — **better, but still worse than it
  should be. I still don't think people should buy the Pixel 6 Pro for $900.** My SIM goes back in the S21 Ultra.
  But I'm looking forward — a Pixel 6a is rumored for summer, and **I'm very interested in the next-gen Tensor; maybe
  I'm just optimistic, but I trust Google will finally buckle down the Google-phone experience.** How Pixels age is
  now one more factor to consider when reviewing them.'"
