# LG G8 Impressions: Can't Touch This! (@mkbhd, 2019-02-24)

## Summary
First look at the LG G8 ThinQ — "a G7S," a minor update. Best material: his "change for the sake of change" design
principle, the genuinely cool **Crystal Sound OLED** (the screen as earpiece), and the **Air Motion** hand-gesture
feature landing squarely in the gimmick bucket.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-02-24] **"I hate change for the sake of change — but this could've been updated."** "**If it ain't broke,
  don't fix it** — but LG is sticking to their guns a bit too hard: the back fingerprint (not under-glass), the
  **notch and thick bezels (starting to look dated)**, the jack + Quad DAC + boombox speakers. Credit for the
  **rare perfectly-flat back** (zero camera bump)." A design-taste principle applied with a caveat.
- [2019-02-24] **Crystal Sound OLED — the screen IS the earpiece (a genuinely cool idea).** "There's **no earpiece
  cutout** — the **top half of the OLED vibrates as the earpiece** (holeless = better water resistance, a bigger
  sweet spot; in a loud room you can **put it to your cheekbone like bone conduction**). It's not loud enough to be
  a stereo speaker yet — but **how dope would a totally bezel-less phone be that still has stereo front speakers
  because the screen vibrates?**" His extrapolate-the-trajectory enthusiasm.
- [2019-02-24] **Vein ID + Air Motion — the gimmick verdict.** **Vein ID** (wave your hand over the phone; it reads
  the **hemoglobin/vein pattern** to unlock — "silly but dope; worked, though I doubt it's as secure as the 3D face
  unlock"). **Air Motion** (hand-gesture shortcuts via the IR + time-of-flight camera — open apps, adjust volume by
  twisting an imaginary knob): "**very finicky — I was always searching for the sweet spot; it felt gimmicky, and I
  didn't want to go through the learning curve.**" His **gimmick-vs-useful** test, applied.
- [2019-02-24] **"You can open apps — but then what?"** "The endgame (wave over your phone while cooking, hands
  dirty) could be cool — but right now **you open an app and still have to touch it to do anything, and voice
  assistants already do more.** It feels incomplete — **in the gimmick bucket for now.**" A sharp incompleteness
  critique.
- [2019-02-24] **The verdict — "a super minor update / a G7S."** "SD855, 6 GB, a flat 6.1" OLED, ~$800 (like the
  G7). **It's fine — G7 fans will like it, but it won't win over anyone the G7/G6 didn't. Very much in the family.**"
  His recurring sympathetic-but-realistic LG diagnosis.
