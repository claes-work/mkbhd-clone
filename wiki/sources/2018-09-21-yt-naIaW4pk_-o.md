# Apple Watch Series 4 Review: It's About Time! (@mkbhd, 2018-09-21)

## Summary
The Series 4 review — notable as a **personal conversion moment** ("I've never been a watch person… this one's
changing that"). Praises the first real redesign (30% bigger screen, same body), the health features (fall
detection, EKG), and — the video nerd in him — the practical-effects watch faces, while listing the "still very
Apple" gaps.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-09-21] **BIOGRAPHY — the smartwatch convert.** "I've always carried an iPhone but **never been a watch
  person** — on and off with the Moto 360, past Apple Watches, the Galaxy Watch, never consistent. **This one's
  changing that — I'm gonna keep this one on me.**" A dated personal shift.
- [2018-09-21] **The redesign — a bigger screen without a bigger watch.** "The first real redesign: **~30% bigger
  screen area in nearly the same body** thanks to shrunken bezels — no notch needed. It makes every previous Apple
  Watch look terrible; the first one that actually looks **sleek.**" Praises the smart bezel solution.
- [2018-09-21] **PRODUCTION APPRECIATION — the practical-effects watch faces.** "The video nerd in me absolutely
  loves that the **fire/water/vapor faces were made with practical effects and a real camera** — they filled a
  giant watch-sized bin of metal with fire and recorded it. **Probably the most gratuitous Apple thing ever — I
  love it.**" A characteristic craft-appreciation tell.
- [2018-09-21] **The health features — genuinely valuable.** **Fall detection** ("way cooler than the pendant of
  shame — 'I've fallen and I can't get up' — great for people who work at heights too") and the coming **EKG**
  ("hold the crown, it reads your heart, exports a PDF for your doctor, detects AFib — **could legitimately save
  lives**"). Sincere, not gimmick-dismissive.
- [2018-09-21] **"Still very Apple" — the fair gripes.** No sleep tracking ("odd for a health-focused watch"); no
  fully-custom watch faces ("**imagine an App Store for watch faces — could be huge**"); Siri-only ("if I could
  switch to Google Assistant I'd be so happy"); no always-on mode ("I'd take the battery hit as an option").
  Names what they *could* do but don't — his recurring critique shape.
- [2018-09-21] **The verdict.** "The **best smartwatch by far** — the one to get if you have an iPhone; the best
  for productivity, notifications, fitness, and health on your wrist. **Biggest upgrade yet, and I love it.**"
  ($399–$800+; "I wouldn't recommend the top end.")
