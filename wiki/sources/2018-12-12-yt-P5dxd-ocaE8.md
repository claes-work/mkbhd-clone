# The Problem with YouTube Rewind! (@mkbhd, 2018-12-12)

## Summary
A creator-business/platform critique of the widely-hated YouTube Rewind 2018 (briefly the 2nd most-disliked video
ever), from the rare vantage of **someone who was in it.** The thesis: the video failed because **creators and
YouTube want fundamentally different things from it** — and the fix is to leave things out.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-12-12] **THE THESIS — the advertiser-vs-creator Delta.** "**Creators and audiences see Rewind as a
  celebration of the year's biggest YouTubers and moments. YouTube — who makes it — sees it as a showcase for
  ADVERTISERS.** In this era of brand safety and post-adpocalypse, keeping the advertiser relationship strong
  became paramount, so **Rewind turned into a giant ad for YouTube.** That gap is why everyone's upset." A clean
  root-cause framing.
- [2018-12-12] **The exclusions vs inclusions (the evidence).** "**PewDiePie, Shane Dawson, the KSI-vs-Logan-Paul
  fight** — huge on YouTube in 2018, left out. Meanwhile **Will Smith, Trevor Noah, John Oliver** — not really
  YouTubers, not that big *on YouTube* in 2018 — are in it, because **that looks good to advertisers.**" Names the
  pattern concretely.
- [2018-12-12] **The history — from simple to chaotic.** 2011 (Rebecca Black counting down the top videos, simple)
  → 2012 (the Gangnam remix, "maybe peak Rewind") → 2013 (the first Portal A mashup, first advertiser-facing) →
  escalating chaos to 2017. "Every year they **shove more in** — the more you include, the more obvious the
  exclusions." A media-historian read of the format's decline.
- [2018-12-12] **INSIDER/BIOGRAPHY — he was in it (and did a stunt).** "I've been in Rewind a few years. This year I
  **did a stunt for a couple hours — the shot of me falling out of the Fortnite bus was me harnessed in costume on
  a zip line.** We shot 12–14 hours." And: "I was told **Will Smith independently suggested 'Fortnite and MKBHD'** —
  I can't verify it, but I want to believe. (Will, if you're watching, I'd love to collab.)" A rare behind-the-
  scenes look. The problem: "even a full day of shooting got cut to a 4-second clip that **loses the whole point**
  in the montage."
- [2018-12-12] **The fix — leave things out (hand-select).** "**You can't make everyone happy. If anyone could fix
  Rewind, the change is: leave some stuff out. Hand-select the few things that really popped and represented
  YouTube that year — a throwback to what made the old ones great. You can leave me out; I don't mind.**"
  Constructive, self-effacing.
- [2018-12-12] **A biography note — representing the tech community.** "It feels good to **represent the tech
  community on YouTube, which I think is kind of underrepresented.**" His sense of his lane on the platform.
