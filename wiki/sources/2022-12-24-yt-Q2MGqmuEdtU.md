# The Voice Assistant Battle! (2023) (@mkbhd, 2022-12-24)

## Summary
A re-test (first since 2017) of the four phone voice assistants — **Google Assistant, Siri, Bixby, Alexa** —
asked identical questions with identical phrasing on the same Wi-Fi. Categories: simple queries (weather,
timers, facts), device control, conversationalism, and photos. Findings: on **facts there's no runaway
winner** and that's healthy (anything Googleable/Wikipedia-able, they all get). **Google Assistant is still
clearly the best overall** — fastest, best-sourced, most conversational, longest context memory, most app
support — **multiplied on a Pixel** (call screening, Hold for Me). **Device control** has two standouts
(Google and Bixby actually *complete* multi-step tasks — take a selfie, play the right Squid Game episode,
start a recording — where Siri and Alexa just open the app). **Conversationalism** is Google's runaway:
it remembers context across a chain of follow-ups where **Siri "tapped out after about four questions."**
Small Apple miss: **Siri can't run multiple timers on iPhone** (though it can on a HomePod mini). Verdict
ranking: **Google #1, Siri #2** (best plugged into Apple's ecosystem, HomeKit), **Bixby #3** (Samsung-only),
**Alexa clearly worst on a phone** ("Amazon is falling behind... Amazon knows it"). Crucially, it's **"an
ecosystem question almost as much as it is a skills question"** — if you're all-in on one smart home, that
answers it — and the **Matter standard may make this section irrelevant.** (dbrand sponsor: Damascus skin.)

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-12-24] **Google Assistant is still the best on any phone.** "'From my most common queries, across
  every phone — iPhone or Android — the most useful assistant is still Google Assistant. It's most likely to
  be fast, to give a well-sourced answer, to be conversational and remember context longer, and it has tons
  of app support. And it's multiplied on a Pixel with call screening and Hold for Me.'"
- [2022-12-24] **It's an ecosystem question as much as a skills question.** "'This really is an ecosystem
  question almost as much as it is a skills question. If you're all-in on HomeKit, Siri/iPhone is your only
  option for your cameras and lights; if you're all-in on Google Home, you're there. The next-gen Matter
  standard may actually make this section irrelevant.'"
- [2022-12-24] **Device control — Google and Bixby actually finish the task.** "'On complex device control
  Google and Bixby walk away: ask for a selfie and they open the camera, switch to selfie, start a timer and
  take it; "play Squid Game on Netflix" and they open the app and start the right episode. Siri just opens
  the app; Alexa is lost — they aren't making the phone, so there's no vertical integration.'"
- [2022-12-24] **Conversationalism — Google remembers, Siri taps out.** "'Google Assistant is incredible at
  carrying context through a chain of follow-ups about the same subject. Siri tapped out after about four
  questions in a row. If you want to research a subject by voice, your best bet is definitely Google
  Assistant.'"
- [2022-12-24] **Siri can't do multiple timers; Alexa is just bad on a phone.** "'For one weird reason Siri
  can't run multiple timers at once on iPhone — basic stuff Apple hasn't fixed (yet it works on a HomePod
  mini). And Alexa is clearly far behind as a phone assistant — Amazon is falling behind with Alexa, and
  Amazon knows it. I'd avoid Alexa-exclusive stuff right now.'"
