# This Was Ahead of its Time! (@mkbhd, 2022-06-11)

## Summary
An essay on **products that failed because the world wasn't ready** — "Vine walked so TikTok could run" (and the
GM EV-1 before Tesla). His **top three ahead-of-their-time gadgets**: **Google Glass** (2013 — the first genuinely
useful AR glasses, rejected because people weren't ready for a camera on your face; lives on in enterprise, and
AR/VR is exploding again now), the **Samsung Galaxy Camera** (a mirrorless camera running full Android — a great
idea killed because the camera itself wasn't good enough), and the **Motorola Atrix** (a phone that became a laptop
via a Lapdock — the "what is a computer / one core device in bigger shells" dream). Two durable ideas emerge: **the
"internet-connected camera" dream** (an iPhone UI on a real full-frame mirrorless with an M1 — "throw pro in the
name; I'd want one") and **the ecosystem obstacle** (Apple would rather sell you an iPhone AND an iPad AND a Mac
than one do-everything device).

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-06-11] **Ahead of its time — Vine and the EV-1.** "'Vine was the home of short-form vertical video before it
  died in 2016; now TikTok's been the most-downloaded app for 12 straight months — **Vine was ahead of its time.**
  You thank Tesla for EVs, but GM's EV-1 (1990s, a 55-mile two-door) was crushed and forgotten — **ahead of its
  time.** So many products failed just because the world wasn't ready.'"
- [2022-06-11] **#3 Google Glass — rejected, but AR is back.** "'Glass (2013) was the first genuinely useful-looking
  AR glasses (turn-by-turn, Assistant, notifications) — but **people were absolutely not ready for a camera and
  computer on your face** (folks kicked out of theaters, ticketed while driving). It lives on in enterprise. But
  it's 2022, AR/VR is about to explode again (Snap Spectacles, Ray-Ban×Meta, Apple's rumored headset — designed to
  be **accepted by regular people**). **Glass walked so these could run.**'"
- [2022-06-11] **#2 the Samsung Galaxy Camera — the internet-connected-camera dream.** "'A mirrorless camera with a
  Galaxy S3 on the back (a great idea — most camera UIs are bad and can't edit-and-post) — **it failed because the
  camera itself wasn't that good.** (Fun fact: Android was originally a camera OS.) The real problem is **the
  friction between a good shot and getting it online** — most people just use their phone. But for creators/
  enthusiasts, **I maintain we're ready NOW for a good internet-connected camera: imagine an iPhone UI on a real
  full-frame mirrorless with an M1, on-board storage, USB-C — throw "pro" in the name; I'd want one.**'"
- [2022-06-11] **#1 the Motorola Atrix — "what is a computer."** "'The Atrix (2011) plugged into a Lapdock for a
  full laptop experience off the phone — **the "what is a computer" dream: one core device you plug into bigger
  shells** (kin to Samsung DeX). Two hurdles: power (the first dual-core phone wasn't laptop-grade) and software.
  Now smartphone power has come a long way (the M1 is a scaled-up A14) — so plugging a phone into a bigger form
  factor is a very capable machine.'"
- [2022-06-11] **The real obstacle — the ecosystem.** "'The biggest obstacle to the "one computer in bigger shells"
  idea isn't power anymore — **it's the ecosystem: a company like Apple would much rather sell you an iPhone AND an
  iPad AND a MacBook than one device that's everything.** So the whole ecosystem has to play along, which makes it
  much harder than any single gadget.'"
