# My New Daily Driver (the 992 911 Turbo S acquisition)

## Summary

**The canonical acquisition story** — the video where Marques reveals he **bought a 2024 992 Porsche
911 Turbo S** as his new daily driver. He lays out the years-long "I wanted a real sports car" seed,
the key boundary on his EV doctrine (**sports cars can't be electric *yet* — too much weight**), the
**turned-30 trigger**, the one-way flight to Boston to pick up an exactly-as-he'd-spec-it car, and
his decision to **sell the Model S Plaid on Cars & Bids** (he doesn't need two daily drivers). His
verdict frames the 911 as **"a 9-or-9.5 out of 10 at *everything* — the most versatile daily-drivable
sports car,"** his new benchmark. Fully Marques-narrated.

## Key claims (biography marked ⬥)

- **[2024-01-11]** ⬥ BIOGRAPHY (canonical): **he bought a 2024 992-gen 911 Turbo S.** "I've been excited
  to make this video for a long time… the seed started years ago — I wanted an actual sports car; I
  love driving." (quote)
- **[2024-01-11]** ⭐ The doctrine boundary: **"Every time I talk about EVs I end up talking about how
  certain cars make the best electric car — a luxury sedan should be ultra-quiet, smooth, quick, so
  it's the perfect candidate to go electric. But sports cars are not yet one of those things — huge
  horsepower comes with a lot of extra weight. So with an actual sports car on the mind, it couldn't
  really be electric."** (Teslas "are what got me into cars… phenomenal daily drivers, but they're not
  sports cars.") (quote)
- **[2024-01-11]** ⬥ BIOGRAPHY: **"I turned 30 and pulled the trigger."** He watched/read *every* review
  of the 992 Turbo S first ("what anybody shopping for a gigantic purchase would do"); the near-
  universal "it's basically perfect, maybe too good, boring how good it is" praise "set off red flags"
  as a reviewer. A brand-new inventory car "exactly how I would have spec'd it" appeared in Boston; he
  **took a one-way flight up and drove it 200 miles back to the studio** (now 1,200+ miles). (quote)
- **[2024-01-11]** ⬥ The spec (he "nerds out"): jet-black metallic under **stealth PPF (satin-matte
  look)**; a **custom glossy-black two-stripe he worked on with APC**; powder-coated black turbo wheels;
  **painted (black, not yellow) brake calipers** ("probably sacrilegious to Porsche lovers"); lots of
  carbon fiber; sport exhaust. The theme: **satin-black vs. glossy-black contrast.** (quote)
- **[2024-01-11]** ⭐ The verdict/benchmark: **"This is one of the best sports cars ever made — but it's
  not the best at anything, it's right near the top at everything. The most versatile daily-drivable
  sports car. It's not as fast as a Plaid, or a McLaren 765LT — but it's a 9/10 fast; it's not as comfy
  as a Bentley — but it's a 9/10 comfy. A 9 and a 9.5 and a 9.7 at everything, where other cars are a 10
  at one thing and a 6–7 at the rest. This is my new benchmark for gas/sports cars."** (quote)
- **[2024-01-11]** ⬥ BIOGRAPHY: he **still has the Rivian R1T** ("an awesome vehicle for someone who
  doesn't tow"). ⚠️ Nitpicks that seeded his later "not very digital" take: no wireless charger, no
  phone key, no dashcam (he installed a Thinkware U3000), a weak phone app. (paraphrase)
- **[2024-01-11]** ⬥ BIOGRAPHY: **"I don't need two daily drivers anymore, so I'm going to sell the
  Plaid"** — auctioning it on **Cars & Bids** (Doug DeMuro's site, sponsor): **one of the first Plaids
  ever (original 3D badge), carbon-ceramic Track Pack brakes, stealth PPF, ~44,000 miles he daily-drove,
  no reserve.** Sign-off: "catch you in the next one — probably electric." (quote)
