# Sony Xperia 1 III: The Ultimate Enthusiast Phone! (@mkbhd, 2021-07-01)

## Summary
A look at the $1,200 Sony Xperia 1 III — **"the most enthusiast-targeted phone in the world,"** more than any
gaming phone. Sony reached the summit of enthusiast features: the headphone jack, notification LED, microSD, a
dedicated shutter button, a **4K 120 Hz display (locked at 4K)**, and a genuinely novel **variable-optical-zoom
telephoto** (moving glass gives two focal lengths on one sensor). The durable tension: it's **a technical marvel
whose photo quality "really isn't that great"** — and **"almost no one should actually buy it"** (get an S21
Ultra) — the "why people don't buy Sony" thesis, taken to its enthusiast extreme.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-07-01] **"The ultimate enthusiast phone" — Sony doubled down on everything.** "'Sony finally reached the
  top of the mountain — **the most enthusiast-targeted phone in the world.** Start with the name (Xperia 1 III —
  "good luck explaining that"), the 21:9 body, and go down the list: **a headphone jack, a notification LED,
  microSD (the second SIM slot doubles for it), front stereo speakers, a dedicated half-press shutter button, a
  Google-Assistant button — everything an enthusiast wants, IP68.**'"
- [2021-07-01] **The 4K 120 Hz display — locked at 4K, "full maximum enthusiast."** "'A 6.5" **4K 120 Hz OLED —
  and you literally can't change the resolution; it's always 4K.** Full-on maximum enthusiast. The 21:9 means some
  "normie apps" (Instagram, TikTok) get black bars, but an enthusiast will take a narrow, taller cinema display.
  The weak point: **4K 120 Hz all the time eats battery hard** (4,500 mAh, ~4 hrs heavy screen-on — keep a battery
  bank nearby).'"
- [2021-07-01] **THE VARIABLE OPTICAL ZOOM — genuinely novel tech.** "'Quietly, Sony dropped **variable optical
  zoom on a single telephoto — glass that physically moves to snap between 70 mm and 105 mm optically (not
  digitally), on the same sensor.** The result is amazing: two telephoto focal lengths in one camera. (It reminds
  me of Samsung's Galaxy S9 dual-aperture — cool, gone the next year — so I have hope but won't get my hopes up.)'"
- [2021-07-01] **THE TENSION — a technical marvel with mediocre photos.** "'Despite the incredible manual control
  (a full Sony Alpha app, 20 fps burst) and fast, never-miss autofocus, **the actual photo quality really isn't
  that great — soft, a bit muted, sometimes noisy. The best thing about this camera is that it's a technical
  marvel to appreciate.**' (Also: the **slowest auto-rotate I've ever seen in a modern phone** — hopefully a
  fixable bug.)'"
- [2021-07-01] **THE VERDICT — "almost no one should actually buy this."** "'It's a lot — a camera and screen I've
  never seen, a ton of features (Side Sense, battery-care, DualShock 4 support). If you care about smartphones,
  it's exciting. **But almost no one should actually buy this — it's $1,200; an S21 Ultra at that price has better
  battery, charging, cameras, software, and updates.** You have to really want this — unless you're deep in Sony's
  ecosystem.'" (The "why people don't buy Sony" thesis at its extreme.)'"
