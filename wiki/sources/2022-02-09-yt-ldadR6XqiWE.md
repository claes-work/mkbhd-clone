# Galaxy S22 Ultra Impressions: It's a Note! (@mkbhd, 2022-02-09)

## Summary
Hands-on first impressions of the $1,200 Galaxy S22 Ultra — "**it's a Note.**" His theory: after two years with no
new Note, **Samsung effectively made a Note 22 Ultra, saw it was too similar to an S22 Ultra, killed the S22 Ultra
and kept the Note — then just renamed it the S22 Ultra** and slotted it at the top of the lineup. It has the exact
Note shape (flat top/bottom, satin back) and the beloved **built-in S Pen** (all the old software, low latency).
Notes the divisive **individual camera rings** (no shelf/bump), the world-class **LTPO2 display**, and a lot of new
**software camera features** (nona-binning night mode, AI stereo depth portrait, improved 100x stabilization) —
to be tested in the full review.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-02-09] **The Note is back — under the Ultra name.** "'We haven't had a new Note in two years — but I have a
  theory: **Samsung probably made an S22 Ultra and a Note 22 Ultra, looked at them side by side, saw there wasn't
  enough differentiating them, booted the S22 Ultra, kept the Note 22 Ultra at the top, and changed the name.**
  Picking it up, all the memories of previous Notes come back — the exact Note shape (just a satin back now) and
  the built-in S Pen.'"
- [2022-02-09] **The S Pen returns, fully.** "'The biggest new feature is a feature from the old Notes: **the
  built-in S Pen returns** — pops in/out of the bottom corner, with all the software (screen-off notes, super-low
  latency, remote Bluetooth shutter). It really feels like it picks up right where the Notes left off — because,
  other than the name, it does exactly that.'"
- [2022-02-09] **The individual camera rings — divisive.** "'The weird-looking part: **no big camera bump or corner
  shelf like the S21 Ultra — just individual camera rings that each protrude the same amount from the flat back**
  (shiny, matching the rails). I don't think it looks that great (looks a bit like a "P" shape with a case on) —
  a bummer, since the S21 series looked so much better (and the S22/S22+ keep the nicer corner bump).'"
- [2022-02-09] **World-class LTPO2 display + spec bump.** "'The 6.8" 1440p 120 Hz display is everything you'd expect
  — an **LTPO2 panel now (down to 1 Hz)**; I could run adaptive refresh + 1440p at once ("you pay for a lot of
  those pixels, might as well use them"). A Snapdragon 8 Gen 1 (Exynos in some regions), 8→12 GB RAM, up to 1 TB, a
  5,000 mAh battery even with the S Pen cutout, and 45 W charging (no charger in box).'"
- [2022-02-09] **A lot of new software camera features to test.** "'The quad cameras (108 MP main, ultrawide, 3x +
  10x tele) aren't bumped much in hardware, but **there's a lot of new software: a nona-binning high-res night
  mode, an improved Pro mode with new RAW, AI stereo depth-mapping for portraits, and seemingly better 100x
  space-zoom stabilization.** All to test in the full review.'"
