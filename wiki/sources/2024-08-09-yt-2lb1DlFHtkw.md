# The Electric Lexus: Please Try Again (Lexus RZ 450e)

## Summary

Marques reviews the **Lexus RZ 450e** (Lexus's only EV, ~$65k) — shot "in a hurricane" — and lands
on a painful verdict: **"one of the nicest cars I could never recommend."** The interior is genuinely
lovely (soft materials, great HUD, good physical controls), but the EV fundamentals are broken —
**~200 mi of range in 2024 "for 65 grand, that's tough"** and a weirdly low-res central screen. He
closes with constructive "keep this, fix that" notes for Lexus's next attempt. Fully Marques-narrated.

## Key claims (biography marked ⬥)

- **[2024-08-09]** ⬥ META (ties to the accountability thread): opens by mocking a **conspiracy comment
  that he "shot the Fisker video on a cloudy day specifically to make the car look sadder"** — "did you
  know that's a real person? Anyway, I'm shooting this one in a hurricane." (quote)
- **[2024-08-09]** The good: "Lexus is pretty good at this stuff — this interior is nice, soft, well-
  built, leather everywhere," great physical knobs (F-150/Mach-E style) alongside the touchscreen,
  ventilated/heated seats, an electrochromic roof, and **"one of the brightest, best-looking HUDs I've
  seen."** (quote)
- **[2024-08-09]** ⚠️ The dealbreakers: **the central screen is "trash" — incredibly low-res, "like a
  film/fog over it"** (jarring next to the excellent HUD); and **~200 mi of range** ("it almost feels
  like they kneecapped it — they decided they didn't want to sell these"). ~300 hp, soft comfortable
  drive. (quote)
- **[2024-08-09]** Callback: **"I remember the Cadillac Lyriq — I hadn't tested many Cadillacs, got in,
  and thought 'this is really nice,' good performance AND good range, so I ended up calling the Lyriq
  underrated. But this Lexus… with only 200 miles of range, there's no way I can recommend it."** The
  drivetrain is "the one critical thing about an EV," and it's bad here. (quote)
- **[2024-08-09]** ⭐ Constructive close (a characteristic MKBHD move): **"Lexus, if you're watching —
  there are good things here to take to your next attempt: this software, the paddle regen, the
  materials, the HUD, the electrochromic roof. I think there's a way to keep the nice soft luxurious
  feel with a larger battery pack."** (quote)

## Notable verbatim quotes
