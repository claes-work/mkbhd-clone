# The Tri Folding Phone Impressions! (@mkbhd, 2024-10-20)

## Summary
Impressions of the **Huawei Mate XT — the world's first tri-fold phone** (imported from China via a dbrand contact,
since he'll never daily it). He tours the three states: **closed** it's a "really respectable" 6.4" phone (~13 mm thick
but each slice is ~4 mm; thin even bezels; "**an 8 out of 10**, really close to the best single-screen experience I've
seen"); **one fold** opens to a 7.9" near-square tablet (a fine ~7/10, but "why stop halfway when you're one fold from
greatness?"); and **fully open** a full **10.2" OLED tablet** — **"literally a full-sized 10-inch tablet in your
pocket, which no other device can do — a 10 out of 10."** The recurring marvel is the thinness (disorienting to hold a
10" screen that thin) and two impressively-small creases (one inward, one outward). It packs real flagship specs
(Kirin 9010, up to 16 GB/1 TB, a variable-aperture triple camera, 5,600 mAh split across the panes, 66 W wired / 50 W
wireless, an in-box kickstand case) at ~$3,000–$3,500 — "not that much more than the highest-end single folds." The
trade-offs are honest: **the battery is the biggest weakness** (5,600 mAh for a 10" screen), **truly terrible tinny
speakers** ("physics — no room for chambers"), **no IP rating**, the software is China-only, and the price. But his real
point is admiration in a stale market: **"this had the potential to be the world's greatest concept phone — remember
concept phones nobody ships? — but they actually made a bunch and shipped them. In a world where every phone is 'a
little different from last year,' I've got to hand it to projects like this. They didn't have to go this hard, but they
did."**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2024-10-20] **A full 10-inch tablet in your pocket.** "'Closed, it's a really respectable 6.4" phone — an 8/10,
  close to the best single-screen experience I've seen. One fold gives a 7.9" square tablet (a fine 7/10). Fully open
  it's a 10.2" OLED — literally a full-sized 10-inch tablet in your pocket, which no other device can do. That's a
  10/10.'"
- [2024-10-20] **The thinness and creases are a marvel.** "'Each slice is only ~4 mm, adding up to ~13 mm closed
  (similar to a Z Fold 6). Fully open it's disorienting to hold a screen this big with bezels this small and the whole
  thing this thin. There are two creases now — one inward-folding, one outward — and both are impressively small.'"
- [2024-10-20] **Real flagship specs, honest trade-offs.** "'It fits flagship specs (Kirin 9010, up to 16 GB/1 TB, a
  variable-aperture triple camera, 66 W wired / 50 W wireless) at ~$3,000–$3,500 — not much more than the highest-end
  single folds. But the biggest weakness is the battery (5,600 mAh for a 10" screen); the speakers are truly terrible
  and tinny (physics — no room for chambers); there's no IP rating; and the software is China-only, so I can't daily
  it.'"
- [2024-10-20] **The best concept phone — that they actually shipped.** "'This had the potential to be the world's
  greatest concept phone — remember concept phones nobody ever makes? — but they went and actually made a bunch and
  shipped them. In a world where every phone is "a little different from last year, not much has changed," I've got to
  hand it to projects like this. They didn't have to go this hard, but they did.'"
