# Galaxy S22 Impressions: 1 Real Downgrade! (@mkbhd, 2022-02-09)

## Summary
First hands-on with the S22 and S22+ (the volume sellers — "the phones more people will experience"). The theme:
**"if it ain't broke, don't fix it"** — a small update in every department from the S21. The most noticeable
in-hand change (that most case-users won't care about) is **build quality: a real glass back this year instead of
plastic**, plus a boxier, more-compact-feeling shape. The **"1 real downgrade": the batteries got smaller** (both
phones shrank, ~8% less capacity). A genuine improvement: **the high-resolution sensor is now the main camera, not
the telephoto** — so 8K video finally uses the normal wide field of view instead of cropping into the tele.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-02-09] **"If it ain't broke, don't fix it" — a small, safe update.** "'The running theme: **if it ain't
  broke, don't fix it.** A small update in every department from the S21 — same center camera cutout, same button
  and fingerprint placement, still IP68. They played it safe.'"
- [2022-02-09] **The build-quality step up — real glass.** "'The biggest thing I noticed holding it: it's **a bit
  more boxy and square, with tighter corners** — and the **back is real glass this year instead of the S21's
  plastic** (adds some heft/density). Not a huge deal for case users, but a step up. The small S22 (6.1") "feels
  kind of compact" thanks to the boxier shape and tiny bezels.'"
- [2022-02-09] **The one real downgrade — smaller batteries.** "'Since the phones got a bit smaller, **the batteries
  also got smaller: the plus from 4,800→4,500, the S22 from 4,000→3,700 mAh** — an ~8% smaller battery to keep an
  eye on (even if the display is a bit more efficient). I'm not sure why (the camera bumps aren't bigger).'"
- [2022-02-09] **The high-res sensor moved to the main camera — a real win.** "'The cameras are a spec bump (a new
  50 MP main binning to 12.5, an ultrawide, a 3x tele). **The big change: the high-resolution camera is the main
  camera, not the telephoto — so shooting 8K now uses the wider, normal field of view instead of cropping into the
  tele. A big improvement for me.**'"
- [2022-02-09] **The S22 vs S22+ differences.** "'Same 1080p display (a bigger 6.6" on the plus); **the plus adds
  45 W charging (vs 25 W), UWB, and Wi-Fi 6E, plus a bigger battery.** $999 plus / $799 S22, no charger in the box
  for either. A new default messages app that looks just like Google Messages.'"

## Notable verbatim quotes

> camera... that's a big improvement for me."
