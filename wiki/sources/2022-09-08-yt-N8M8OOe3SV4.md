# Apple Watch Ultra Impressions: Polished Overkill! (@mkbhd, 2022-09-08)

## Summary
Hands-on impressions of the September Apple Watch lineup — the SE, the Series 8, and the headline **Apple Watch
Ultra.** The Ultra is **"a regular Apple Watch sent to the gym"** (they ruggedized a normal watch rather than
building down from a Garmin): a 49 mm titanium body, the biggest/brightest (2,000-nit) display, a **customizable
orange Action button**, WR100 diving, dual-band GPS, an 86 dB siren, and a much bigger battery (~36 hrs / a ~3-day
feel; ~60 hrs later). Surprisingly it's **reasonably priced ($799 — Apple isn't usually the one undercutting** a
$900 Garmin). The durable framing (from co-host Andrew on Waveform): **the Ultra is "the GoPro of Apple watches"** —
the commercial is sick, most people don't need it, and it ends up in a drawer. His self-assessment: as an ultimate-
frisbee player he'd love the battery/GPS, **but it's borderline too big for everyday wear** — the regular Watch is
fine for most. Its key advantage over rugged rivals: **it's also an Apple Watch you wear every day.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-09-08] **The Ultra — a regular Apple Watch sent to the gym.** "'A "pro watch" could start from a Garmin-level
  rugged watch and bring it down, or **start with a regular Apple Watch and ruggedize it — they clearly did the
  latter.** A 49 mm titanium/ceramic body (surprisingly light), the biggest/brightest ever (2,000 nits), a bigger
  crown + crown guard, a second speaker/mics, and a **big orange customizable Action button.** They built it by
  asking extreme athletes what the Watch couldn't do: **WR100 diving (a real dive computer), extreme-temp
  ruggedness, compass waypoints, an 86 dB SOS siren, dual-band GPS**, and a much bigger battery (~36 hrs; ~3-day
  feel; up to ~60 hrs later) — a full Iron Man with battery to spare.'"
- [2022-09-08] **Reasonably priced for once.** "'Rumors said ~$1,000 (not crazy after the $10K gold Edition) — but
  **it's reasonably priced at $799.** Still pricey, but for context a Garmin Fenix is ~$900 — **and Apple's not
  usually the one doing the undercutting.** New Wayfinder faces and three durable/comfy bands (Trail, Alpine,
  Ocean) — and the bands are backwards-compatible with the smaller watches.'"
- [2022-09-08] **"The GoPro of Apple watches."** "'The #1 question: am I getting one? As an **ultimate-frisbee
  player who wears my watch while playing**, I'd love the battery/GPS — but it's **approaching too big for everyday
  wear.** On Waveform, co-host Andrew nailed it: **the Ultra is like the GoPro of Apple watches — the commercial is
  sick, you think you'll capture amazing adventures, then it sits in a drawer gathering dust.** Most people don't
  need it, but it'd be sick; for me the regular Watch is fine.' (Aside: I wore a black Apple Watch to the Met
  Gala — it was fine.)'"
- [2022-09-08] **The SE, Series 8, and AirPods Pro 2.** "'The **SE ($250)** replaces the Series 3 (better in every
  way). The **Series 8 ($399)** is a minor but reasonable Series 7 update — a temperature sensor (cycle tracking),
  crash detection, low-power mode. And the **AirPods Pro 2** ($249): the H2 chip, 2× stronger ANC, a 6-hr battery,
  stem volume controls, adaptive transparency (building on their "god-like" transparency lead), and **a much
  better case — a lanyard hook and a speaker so you can find it when you lose it** (like I do).'"
- [2022-09-08] **Its key advantage — it's also an Apple Watch.** "'It's competitive with the other rugged smart
  watches — **but its key advantage is that it's also an Apple Watch you can wear every day and it works really
  well with the iPhone you already have. For the adventurers and those with deep pockets, a pretty sick watch.**'
  (My biggest annoyance with all the watches, per Casey Neistat: sweat/rain/long sleeves touching the screen
  messes with workout tracking — just fix that.)'"
