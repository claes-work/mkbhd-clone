# 'Siri Isn't That Bad' (reacting to hot takes)

## Summary

Marques reacts to viewer-submitted "hot takes," a recurring format. He opens by owning a
wrong take of his own (he once "bet anyone a million dollars" the iPhone would keep the mute
switch — the very next iPhone replaced it with the Action button) — "that's the beauty of the
internet: the hot takes just live out there forever." He then works through five takes, and
the recurring evaluative move is his signature **"in a vacuum vs not in a vacuum"** lens. Also
a **biography anchor**: he states he's **31** (in high school in 2009, a college senior in 2015).

## Key claims

- **[2025-11-28]** ⬥ BIOGRAPHY: **"I'm 31 now"**; the Magic Mouse came out in 2009 "when I was in
  high school," and its 2015 update landed "when I'm a senior in college" — placing his birth ~1993.
  (quote)
- **[2025-11-28]** Owns a wrong prediction: in 2022 he "bet anyone a million dollars" the iPhone
  would keep the mute switch for 5 years — the next iPhone replaced it with the Action button. "That
  that's the beauty of the internet: the hot takes just live out there forever." (quote)
- **[2025-11-28]** **"Humanoid robots are not the optimal form factor and are a bad idea"** — he
  agrees. The pro-humanoid logic (the world is built for the human form) is real, but "you are
  unnecessarily inheriting all of the limitations of the human form." The fork: either **one
  humanoid robot does everything**, or **each appliance becomes its own specialized robot** (the
  dishwasher, the Roomba, the coffee maker) — and he leans to the second. "The self-driving car is
  perfect: do you want a humanoid robot that drives a car, or the car itself to be the robot?"
  "Even if it's one robot that does everything, why not give it six arms and six legs? Why does it
  have to look like a human?" (paraphrase + quote) [Extends the 2025-10-30 NEO video.]
- **[2025-11-28]** **"The bottom charging port on the Magic Mouse is just fine"** — bad take. In a
  vacuum, flip-and-charge-for-5-minutes "doesn't seem that bad, **but we're not in a vacuum** — every
  other mouse ever found a way to charge while you keep using it." And the design hasn't changed since
  he was in high school, still $80. "It's wild that Apple is a multi-trillion-dollar company and
  hasn't updated it." (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-11-28]** **"Within 5 years, smartphones will be obsolete"** — bad take, because **5 years
  is a ridiculously short time** and the take "underestimates the popularity of smartphones today."
  He compares it to a past viewer take that the **Cybertruck would outsell the F-150 by 2026** —
  which "underestimates how popular the F-150 actually is" (best-seller for ~46 years). "Maybe 10,
  15, 20 years... but smartphones still are kind of OP." (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-11-28]** **"We should celebrate companies taking audacious moonshots even if they ship
  before ready / fail"** — a good take, "but be careful." He agrees we should applaud genuine
  attempts (so much tech is reductive/boring), **but as a reviewer his lens is "should you buy it
  today?"** and **"one of the core tenets of how I recommend things is: don't buy it based on the
  promise of future software updates."** "I'm not going to be changing my stance on buying things
  that aren't ready." (quote) [Directly ties to the 2025-10-30 "the gap" video.]
- **[2025-11-28]** **"Siri isn't as bad as people make it out to be"** — surprisingly, a good take,
  *specifically framed*. Siri IS bad, but it's "actually pretty functional at the extreme basics" —
  simple factual questions, sending messages, opening apps, multi-step local commands ("play [song]
  on Spotify"), HomeKit — and it's **gotten better** at those. But "the second you ask anything with
  reasoning or more than one step, it instantly tries to kick it out to ChatGPT." So for the mass of
  users who only do basic tasks, "it does everything you want" — hence the take holds. But it's **"just
  like the Magic Mouse: in a vacuum it seems fine, but the second you're not in a vacuum, you see how
  much better everything else is and realize how bad it is."** (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-11-28]** Context: he frames the AI-assistant gap — Gemini, ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity,
  Amazon Rufus have "blown up" and changed everything in a few years, yet **Apple "feels like they're
  watching from the sidelines."** (quote)
