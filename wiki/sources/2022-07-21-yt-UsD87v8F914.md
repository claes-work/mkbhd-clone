# Google Pixel 6A Review: Can You Feel It? (@mkbhd, 2022-07-21)

## Summary
A review of the $449 Pixel 6a — Google's budget formula (**a flagship chip + old/cheaper hardware, like the iPhone
SE, Google's version**). His one big thought: **the budget space is crowded now, so the 6a is no longer the
hands-down winner — and the reason comes down to the display.** It's a 60 Hz screen, and while the Tensor chip
opens apps fast, **"this one FEELS slow — one of the choppiest 60 Hz displays"** — a feel thing he's confident about
"from a decade of phone testing." Paired with the concern that **Pixels haven't aged gracefully** (what will it be
like in three years?). The camera is the old IMX363 — but **still a top-2 budget camera** thanks to Google's
computational photography ("these cameras are like pizza"). No wireless charging or headphone jack. His steer:
**get it for the Google software + camera; otherwise cross-shop the A53 / Nothing / SE.** "Cheap phones are still
very much getting good."

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-07-21] **The budget formula — new chip, old body.** "'Google's version of the budget play: **the same Tensor
  chip as the flagships and the same software, with cheaper hardware around it (like the iPhone SE, Google's
  version), $449.** Unmistakably Pixel (the two-tone, the camera bar) — a slimmer bump. But it's IP67 (not 68), and
  **no wireless charging and no headphone jack** (the 5a had one).'"
- [2022-07-21] **The display is the problem — 60 Hz "feels slow."** "'Where it most reflects its price is the front:
  a **60 Hz display.** The Tensor makes apps open fast, **but the responsiveness and smoothness — this is not
  smooth; it FEELS slow. Even among 60 Hz phones, this is one of the choppiest** (scrolling, the notification
  shade). You'll either feel it right away or never notice — it's a feel thing, purely from **my decade of phone
  testing.** Hard to show on a 30 fps video.'"
- [2022-07-21] **The aging worry.** "'I combine that with what I've seen: **the last year of Pixels haven't aged
  gracefully** (the 6 / 6 Pro). Three years of guaranteed updates is good, but **what does this phone feel like in
  three years, given how it feels now?**'"
- [2022-07-21] **The camera — the old IMX363, still top-2 in budget.** "'Unlike prior a-series, it doesn't share
  the flagship cameras — it's the **old 12 MP IMX363 (the same sensor since the Pixel 2).** But it's a **tried-and-
  true great camera because of Google's computational photography** — crisp, contrasty, confident shots, good
  colors, minimal fringing. Easily a **top-2 budget camera** (and it has an ultrawide the SE lacks; 4K60 video).
  **These cameras are like pizza — not groundbreaking, but they get the job done.**'"
- [2022-07-21] **The steer — software + camera, else cross-shop; cheap phones getting good.** "'The interesting part
  is the competition at $450. **Get the 6a if you value the Google software experience and/or the camera over
  everything else. If not: a Galaxy A53 ($100 less, 120 Hz, expandable storage), the Nothing Phone (a much smoother
  120 Hz + the lights), or an iPhone SE** (60 Hz single camera, but a flagship chip, wireless charging, and it's an
  iPhone). **Good phones aren't getting cheap, but cheap phones are still very much getting good.**'"
