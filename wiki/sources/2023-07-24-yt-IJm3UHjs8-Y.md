# The Truth about the New Beats by Dre! (Beats Studio Pro) (@mkbhd, 2023-07-24)

## Summary
A review of the **$350 Beats Studio Pro** that opens with the hook: **"these headphones actually had the chance to be
the best Apple headphones ever made by a mile"** — remember **Apple owns Beats** — because on paper they beat the
AirPods Max at nearly everything: **lighter (plastic), actually fold, a better travel case, USB-C, a headphone jack,
longer battery (40 hrs), and ~$200 cheaper.** "If we stopped there, this would be the best Apple headphones ever...
but there's a catch." The quirks: **no Apple H-chip** (unlike previous Beats) — so **no on-head detection, no
multipoint auto-switching** (the "magic moment" of moving between your Mac and iPhone is gone), Find My only shows
the last-connected location, and transparency mode is "a notch behind" the AirPods. There's an EQ, **but only when
plugged in via USB-C** (three baked-in firmware profiles). "These are more luxury features than necessities — but
you're paying luxury prices, and separating them with ecosystem plugs is exactly what I'd expect Apple to do, which
is why it's almost funny they didn't." His verdict: **"a pair of Beats I'd be totally happy living with"** — a very
good, cheaper, cross-platform (fast-pairs with Android too) alternative; a fun V-shaped-bass sound (**"audio is
subjective"**). "It seems like Beats and Apple just don't talk — I wonder what's up with that."

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2023-07-24] **They could've been the best Apple headphones ever — the un-Apple wins.** "'Erase the Beats logo and
  put an Apple logo on these and they'd be insane — they're just so un-Apple. Lighter and actually folding, a real
  travel case, USB-C (plug into a Pixel for lossless), a headphone jack, 40-hour battery, and ~$200 cheaper than the
  AirPods Max. If we stopped here, this would be the best Apple headphones ever made.'"
- [2023-07-24] **But there's a catch — no H-chip.** "'These lack any Apple H1/H2 chip — weird, since previous Beats
  had it. So no on-head detection, and no multipoint: that magic moment of listening on your computer, getting an
  iPhone call, and instantly switching — this won't do it. Find My only shows the last place they were connected,
  not a live ping.'"
- [2023-07-24] **Luxury features, not necessities — but you're paying luxury prices.** "'These are more luxury
  features than necessities, sure — but you're paying in the realm of luxury headphones. Separating these out with
  ecosystem-plug features is exactly what I'd expect Apple to do, which is why it's almost funny that they didn't.'"
- [2023-07-24] **The EQ only works plugged in; audio is subjective.** "'There's an EQ, but only over USB-C — three
  baked-in firmware profiles (signature/entertainment/conversation) you switch by double-pressing the power button.
  I wish I could make my own custom EQ, but most Beats customers would never dig that far. And yes, they're a
  V-shaped bass-heavy signature — but audio is subjective, and for fun listening it's completely fine.'"
- [2023-07-24] **Happy to live with them — and Beats/Apple just don't talk.** "'This is a pair of Beats I'd be
  totally happy living with — very good, almost complete, and cheap enough to be a really good alternative; I expect
  to see these on flights a lot. It also seems like Beats and Apple just don't talk. I wonder what's up with that.'"
