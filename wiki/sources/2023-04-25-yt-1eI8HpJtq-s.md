# I Made My Own Sneaker! M251 (@mkbhd, 2023-04-25)

## Summary
The reveal of Marques's **first designed product** — a sneaker, the **M251**, a two-year collaboration with **Atoms**
(the brand known for its comfortable Model 0 / Model 1). The name answers the recurring "when are you going to design
your own product?": **his very first YouTube tech video was 2 minutes 51 seconds long — M251 — "a new beginning."**
The design approach: **he's not suddenly a shoe designer**, so he combined **bits and pieces from shoes he likes**
(the lightweightness of NMDs, a Jordan-ish silhouette, Atoms' sole and elastic laces, details from Blazers and
Off-White collabs), iterating through **dozens of samples over 2+ years** while Atoms told him what was possible /
what was more expensive. It's a comfortable daily **mid** (synthetic leather + recycled mesh; a proprietary
supportive-but-soft outsole — "the hardest part"; a slight heel-to-toe drop; a copper antimicrobial lining; his
signature on the tongue). He **tested his own creation** for weeks ("it started showing up in videos by accident").
The honest note: **"I can't review my own product"** — he's excited for shoe reviewers to do that. And the one
request: **"wear your kicks, crease your kicks"** — daily them, don't baby them for resale. "This 251 is an exciting
new beginning."

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2023-04-25] **Named for his first video's length — a new beginning.** "'The question always comes up: when are you
  going to design your own product? Two years of materials and design work later, I'm holding it — the M251. My first
  ever YouTube tech video was 2 minutes 51 seconds long. A new beginning — and that's also sort of a new beginning.'"
- [2023-04-25] **I'm not a shoe designer — I combined bits from shoes I like.** "'I'm not suddenly a shoe designer.
  The idea was to take bits and pieces from shoes I like — the lightweightness of NMDs, a Jordan-ish silhouette,
  Atoms' sole and elastic laces, details from Blazers and Off-White collabs — and combine as many as possible in one
  place. It's still a collaboration; I have the ideas, but I need someone to build the shoe (Atoms).'"
- [2023-04-25] **Dozens of samples over 2+ years; the sole was hardest.** "'We went through dozens of samples over 2+
  years — older prototypes had fitment, support, and material issues. The outsole was the hardest part: I'm on my
  feet all day producing in the studio, so it has to be supportive enough not to collapse but soft enough through
  the midsole to be really comfortable.'"
- [2023-04-25] **I can't review my own product — but I tested it.** "'Once we had working prototypes I wore them for
  weeks — they started showing up in videos by accident because I was daily-driving them, testing my own creation. I
  can't review my own product, actually — I'm excited for shoe reviewers to do that — but I can tell you all about
  it.'"
- [2023-04-25] **Wear your kicks, crease your kicks.** "'If you get these, promise me one thing: wear your kicks. I
  hate the culture of babying shoes for resale value. Daily them, crease them, wear them in all four seasons — snow,
  mud. We engineered them to do that; you'll see mine getting dirty on social.'"
