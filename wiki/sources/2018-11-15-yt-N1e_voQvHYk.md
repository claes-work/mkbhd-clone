# iPad Pro Review: The Best Ever... Still an iPad! (@mkbhd, 2018-11-15)

## Summary
The 2018 iPad Pro review, built on one thesis: **incredible hardware, held back by software.** The A12X rivals a
15" MacBook Pro, but iOS 12 keeps it "**still an iPad**" — not a laptop replacement. Includes a precise list of
*why* it can't replace his laptop and a prescient wish for Apple Silicon in Macs.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-11-15] **THE THESIS — "incredible hardware, still an iPad."** "The hardware is the most beautiful, powerful,
  impressive iPad yet — **but thanks to iOS 12, it's just that: still an iPad. It's not any closer to replacing my
  laptop.**" The definitive hardware-held-back-by-software framing.
- [2018-11-15] **The A12X — "the fastest chip I've ever seen in a mobile device."** "Geekbench **rivals the 15"
  MacBook Pro** — faster than many laptops. A+ performance with **headroom for days — very future-proof.**" (Design:
  boxy like the iPhone 5s, stands on its own, Face ID in any orientation, great 120 Hz LCD, still-great speakers.)
- [2018-11-15] **USB-C — "doesn't mean as much as it could, yet."** "The shocker is **USB-C on an iPad** — but
  **virtually nothing is new that I couldn't already do with a Lightning adapter; all I'm really doing is getting
  rid of dongles** (which I love). **Thanks to iOS**, it doesn't mean as much as it could. Thunderbolt accessories
  don't work; plugging in my **Hasselblad X1D just charges it** — I still pull the SD card." A precise deflation of
  the headline feature.
- [2018-11-15] **WHY it can't replace a laptop (the concrete list).** "**No file ecosystem** (it needs Finder, not
  the Files app); **mobile Safari kicks me out of desktop YouTube Studio into the app** every time; **no trackpad/
  mouse support at all**; Thunderbolt accessories don't work. Editing photos with the pencil + 120 Hz is **my
  favorite way to edit photos** — but that's exactly what I could do before with Lightning." Names the real
  workflow blockers, not vague complaints.
- [2018-11-15] **PRESCIENT — "when will Apple put these chips in Macs?"** "This has me thinking two things: maybe
  **iOS 13** turns the iPad into its final form (unlock USB-C, real file management); and **when is Apple going to
  put their incredibly powerful chips in Macs — like the new MacBook Air running a slightly underpowered Intel
  chip?**" A notably early call on the Apple-Silicon-in-Macs transition. Plus the "most Apple-2018 thing ever" —
  **price hikes on everything** (tablet, pencil, keyboard).
- [2018-11-15] **The honest verdict.** "It does everything the last iPad Pro did, **just better** — textbook great
  product. But Apple's constant laptop comparison puts it in a weird space. **If your creative workflow fits — you
  sketch, DJ, or edit video on it — this is incredible, and I'm jealous.** Otherwise it's just **the greatest iPad
  ever.**" Buyer-honest, workflow-dependent.
