# Rivian R2/R3 Hands-On Impressions!

> ⚠️ **ATTRIBUTION — MIXED (interview).** Marques hosts and walks the cars with **RJ Scaringe (Rivian
> founder/CEO)** as guest. Marques's questions/observations ARE persona data; **RJ's answers are

## Summary

At Rivian's launch event, Marques does a **hands-on walkthrough of the new R2, R3, and R3X with RJ
Scaringe**. The R2 ($45k, ~300+ mi) is a smaller two-row R1S "that keeps all the R1 character at a
lower price"; the "one more thing" R3/R3X are smaller still. Marques is most struck by the **new
PlayStation-5-controller-style haptic steering-wheel scroll wheels** and a **camp mode where even the
front seats fold flat.** Fully hosted by Marques (RJ answering).

  CEO of Rivian, about the future and R2"** — and RJ joins him again here. (Confirms the RJ Scaringe
  @Waveform interview.) He closes: **"I know a couple people — one of them's name is Andrew — who's

  R3X (performance). *(Attribute to Rivian/RJ, not Marques.)*
