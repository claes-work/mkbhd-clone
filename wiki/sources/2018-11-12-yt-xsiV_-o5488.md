# iPhone XR Review: No Need to Panic! (@mkbhd, 2018-11-12)

## Summary
The full iPhone XR review — a defense of the "controversial" display against **spec-sheet panic**, plus a genuinely
useful insight about why losing the telephoto lens isn't all bad. Verdict: "**the best-seller iPhone for everyone
— no need to panic.**"

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-11-12] **"Don't judge the display by the spec sheet" — the pixel-density argument.** "The easiest way to
  make the XR display seem bad is to **look only at the numbers**: sub-1080p, **326 ppi**. But you know what else
  has 326 ppi? **The iPhone 8** — and everyone loved that display. **Look at the actual experience, not the spec
  sheet** — in practice it's accurate, vibrant, and crisp enough." A clean media-literacy move (the LCD "Liquid
  Retina" is genuinely good).
- [2018-11-12] **The real noticeable difference is the BEZELS (not res or 3D Touch).** "What I notice every day
  isn't the resolution or the missing 3D Touch (I barely used it) — it's the **slightly thicker bezels.** Having
  used an XS, I much prefer how much more modern shaving 1–2 mm all around feels." Names the true daily tradeoff.
- [2018-11-12] **The telephoto-lens insight — losing it isn't all bad.** "**The iPhone's telephoto has always been
  a worse sensor that lets in less light** — so in low light the phone often falls back to a digital zoom from the
  better primary anyway. And for **portrait mode**, the XR uses the **better primary at a wider angle** — and
  **wide-angle portraits with shallow depth of field are kind of my favorite look** (more swirly, more character).
  Downside: no depth hardware means it's **software-only and limited to human faces** (no object portraits)."
  A counterintuitive, genuinely useful read.
- [2018-11-12] **Excellent battery + the RAM footnote.** "3 GB RAM (one less than the XS) — **fine in iOS pushing
  way fewer pixels.** And the 2,942 mAh battery is **excellent — as good as the XS Max, sometimes better** (the
  A12 + fewer pixels). One of the best parts of the phone." Explains why fewer resources still work here.
- [2018-11-12] **Colors + the slipperiest-phone champ.** "**Red is my favorite, blue is my #2** (coral/yellow
  don't speak to me — the less-saturated rails). They didn't have to do colors — glad they did." Warns the anodized
  aluminum makes it **the new slipperiest phone in the world** (slides out of pockets).
- [2018-11-12] **The verdict — "the best-seller iPhone for everyone."** "**Not a budget phone** ($750). The biggest
  downsides (display, no telephoto) only matter versus the XS. But most people come from an **iPhone 7/8 or older —
  for them this is an upgrade in every way for not much more.** **Nothing to panic about. Thumbs up.**" (If you were
  already getting an XS/XS Max, still do.)

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "The easiest way to make the XR's display seem bad is to focus entirely on the numbers — 326 ppi. You know what
> else has that? The iPhone 8, and that was a great display."
> "The telephoto lens has always been a worse-quality sensor that lets in less light — so we often get better
> results from just using the better camera."
> "Wide-angle portraits with a shallow depth of field is kind of my favorite look — it's the most visually
> interesting version of a portrait."
> "There's nothing to panic about. This is the best-seller iPhone for everyone."
