# The Blind Smartphone Camera Test Winners! (2024) (@mkbhd, 2024-01-09)

## Summary
The 2024 "scientific" blind camera test: **20 phones**, the same three photos each (daytime, low-light, portrait),
stripped of EXIF, scored by an **ELO rating** over **20M+ public votes.** The results: a **"perfect Pixel podium"** —
the **Pixel 7A wins the overall "People's Champ"** (avg ELO 1,660), with the **Pixel 8 Pro second and the Pixel Fold
third** — and, amusingly, **in reverse price order.** By category: **daytime → Pixel 7A** (the iPhone 15 Pro was
**dead last** — "just the darkest one; people pick the brighter photo almost every time," though the S23 Ultra was
second-last for **overexposing**); **low-light → the iPhone 15 Pro** (the plot twist — "the second you get outside
the top four you're in bad-HDR territory"; the Oppo Find X6 Pro "literally painted sloppily over me"); **portrait →
the Pixel 8 Pro** (the Sony Xperia 1 V had the **single lowest ELO in any category** — a broken auto-portrait). The
**Bang-for-the-Buck award → the Pixel 7A** (~$500), with the Nothing Phone 2 second. The standing lesson holds:
**"we weren't shocked by the slightly-brighter photos beating the slightly-less-bright ones"** — and there's much
more to a camera than a staged sit-down photo (autofocus, UI, file format, video).

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2024-01-09] **The method — 20 phones, 20M votes, ELO.** "'We took 20 phones, updated software, took the same three
  photos with each (daytime, low-light, portrait) fully auto — we don't even tap to focus — stripped the EXIF, and
  had you vote head-to-head millions of times. With enough matchups, ELO gives a statistically significant winner.'"
- [2024-01-09] **A perfect Pixel podium — in reverse price order.** "'Average it all and the highest overall ELO —
  the People's Champ — is the Pixel 7A, then the Pixel 8 Pro, then the Pixel Fold. Pixel, Pixel, Pixel — a perfect
  Pixel podium, surprisingly in reverse price order.'"
- [2024-01-09] **The iPhone 15 Pro: dead last in daylight, first in low light.** "'Dead last for the daytime photo
  was the iPhone 15 Pro — not terrible, just the darkest; side by side, people pick the brighter one almost every
  time. But the plot twist: the highest low-light ELO is again the iPhone 15 Pro, with a totally reasonable photo —
  and I fully agree. Outside the top four you get straight into bad-HDR territory.'"
- [2024-01-09] **The losers overexposed or broke.** "'You can absolutely overexpose for this — the S23 Ultra was
  second-last in daylight for a weirdly overexposed masterpiece. And the single lowest ELO in any category was the
  $1,200 Sony Xperia 1 V's auto portrait — it looked like a mistake, and it did it again and again.'"
- [2024-01-09] **Bang for the buck + the standing lesson.** "'Votes-per-dollar winner: the Pixel 7A (~$500), then the
  Nothing Phone 2. The Pixels killed it — one-two-three and great value. And we still weren't shocked that the
  slightly brighter photos beat the slightly less bright ones. There's a lot more to a camera than a staged photo —
  autofocus, UI, file format, video.'"
