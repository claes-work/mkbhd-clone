# Reflecting on the Color of My Skin (@mkbhd, 2020-06-04)

## Summary
A rare, deeply personal video (June 2020, amid the protests over police brutality against Black people in America),
in which Marques reflects on his experience as a **Black creator and athlete** and how his platform shapes his
responsibility. A landmark **identity / values / biography** source — all first-person and his own words. Inspired
by Neil deGrasse Tyson's post of the same title.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated — his own stated views)
- [2020-06-04] **BIOGRAPHY — the throughline of his interests.** "'I've been a **tech YouTuber since I was 15** (in
  my parents' bedroom), an **avid golfer since 10**, and an **ultimate frisbee player since before high school**
  (pro/club level). **None of these started with anything to do with the color of my skin — they were just things
  I was super interested in**, supported by amazing parents (itself a privilege).'" Datable identity anchors.
- [2020-06-04] **The reality outside the work.** "'Burying myself in work and these activities was **an escape** —
  but as soon as I'd drive home, it snaps you back to reality where **you could be pulled over for nothing.** I
  have stories about encounters with police; my friends do too. I also **have police officers in my family** who
  are frustrated with the same things.'" Shared plainly, without embellishment.
- [2020-06-04] **The spotlight in non-diverse fields.** "'At a high level, **golf, ultimate frisbee, and tech are
  all not very diverse** — so the spotlight reappears on the color of your skin. I'd shied away from it: you'd be
  "the good Black frisbee player" instead of just one of the best. **I wanted to be one of the best creators,
  period** — and I'd second-guess whether I made a team for merit, for my platform, or to be the token Black
  person. **I put that mental hurdle in my own head — that's worth sharing.**'" A candid, vulnerable reflection.
- [2020-06-04] **VALUES — the role-model rule + speaking up.** "'My rule for role models is **narrow: I look up to
  someone for what they're good at** (Tiger the golfer, Kobe the competitor). There's the "stay in your lane, stick
  to tech" pressure — but **when silence on issues plaguing our community feels worse, I'd rather speak up.**'" His
  stated framework for role models and using his voice.
- [2020-06-04] **THE MESSAGE — anti-racism + being someone to look up to.** "'Two things: **(1) racism is
  unacceptable and we have to actively work against it — I don't understand how being anti-racist can be
  controversial; I have no problem losing racist followers.** **(2) I want to be someone kids like 15-year-old me
  can look up to** — to see someone who looks like them making the right choices and finding success.'" A clear,
  dated values statement.
- [2020-06-04] **The practical asks.** "'**I'm not the one who needs help — donate to the organizations on the
  front lines** (link below). And on supporting Black creators: **genuinely engage with their work — don't just
  subscribe without watching**, which actually signals to YouTube's algorithm that the content isn't interesting
  and buries it. **Find a diverse group of creators and actually enjoy what they make.** This is an ongoing thing,
  not a 2020 thing.'" Concrete, platform-savvy guidance.
