# Genesis GV70 Electric: Sleeper Pick!

## Summary

Marques reviews the **electric Genesis GV70** and frames it as **direct proof of his standing
doctrine**: **"certain cars are best to go electric — the ones that are already big and heavy but
have the same advantages — and this car proved me right."** Because there's a gas GV70 to compare
against, he can say plainly the electric version is **better than the gas one for all the reasons
he's been arguing** (quieter, smoother, more luxurious). A "sleeper pick," Lyriq-underrated adjacent —
held back only by weak range (236 mi). Fully Marques-narrated.

## Key claims

- **[2024-05-03]** ⭐ Doctrine, explicitly validated: **"One of the things I've been saying this whole
  time — there are certain types of cars that are best with today's tech to go electric, the ones
  already big and heavy that already have the same advantages — and this car proved me right. There's
  a gas GV70; this electric version is better than the gas version, for all the same reasons I've been
  saying."** (Quieter, smoother accelerating AND decelerating, more luxurious.) (quote)
- **[2024-05-03]** "Reminds me of the Lyriq in being a little underrated for what it is." $65–75k, AWD,
  350kW charging, but **236 mi range — "that's the weak point, not great, even worse in winter."** (quote)
- **[2024-05-03]** Nice touches he flags: a **hidden charge port that pops out of the grille** (he
  walked the whole car looking for it), OTA updates, a **touch-sensitive seat-control panel that shows
  on-screen what you're about to adjust before you adjust it** ("one of my favorite features"), good
  materials, a 10-second red-lit boost button. (paraphrase)
- **[2024-05-03]** Character: "this one's not about sporty driving — it's about being comfortable,
  wafting over potholes in Comfort mode… that's what it's best at, that's when it's quiet." (quote)
- **[2024-05-03]** ⭐ Verdict + his consistent fix: **"Good electric crossover, good fundamentals, solid
  price. The only thing I'd change: I'd trade a little of the straight-line performance for more range —
  that would make it the best-rounded, most logical version of itself. Then I'd be okay recommending it.
  Until then, it's the best version of the GV70."** (quote)
