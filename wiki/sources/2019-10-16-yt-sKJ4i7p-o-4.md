# Pixel 4 Impressions: Google and Gaps! (@mkbhd, 2019-10-16)

## Summary
First look at the Pixel 4 ("the most leaked phone in history"). The thesis is in the title — **"Google and
Gaps"**: a solid Google phone with **head-scratching omissions**, chief among them **no ultrawide camera.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-10-16] **THE GAP — no ultrawide, and it stings.** "'The one thing you **can't fake with software is an
  ultrawide** — Google always did the best with one camera, so **now that every phone in this class has an
  ultrawide, I was pumped to see what Google could do with it.** And they didn't. **It's a bigger deal that they
  didn't include it than if they'd just put it in.**' Google's on-stage jab — **"we think telephoto is more
  important"** — 'I definitely feel personally attacked.'" His strongest ultrawide-over-telephoto statement.
- [2019-10-16] **Motion Sense radar — face unlock cool, gestures hit-or-miss.** "The big forehead houses IR +
  **radar** (new in phones). **Face unlock**: the radar preps the sensors as your hand approaches — 'unlocked
  before I got it to my face, pretty cool.' **Gestures**: the alarm-quieting-as-you-reach worked every time, but
  the **swipe gestures worked ~10% of the time** with no feedback on what you did wrong — 'something I'm probably
  never going to use.'" (Removed all other biometrics — no fingerprint anywhere.)
- [2019-10-16] **90 Hz is his favorite feature.** "'My favorite feature about the Pixel 4 without a doubt, easy,
  is the **90 Hz display** — love that. Smooth, high-res, color-accurate, but **not very bright** (struggled
  outdoors).'" Continues his high-refresh advocacy.
- [2019-10-16] **The other gaps.** "'**No matte black** option (the black one is glossy — 'I feel slighted'; 2019
  is 'the year of matte black' otherwise); **only Snapdragon 855, not the 855+** that cheaper phones are shipping;
  and a **small battery** (2,800 mAh on the smaller Pixel 4) to watch with that 90 Hz screen.'"
- [2019-10-16] **The value question.** "'At **$799** the smaller Pixel 4 is a **tough sell — $100 more than the
  iPhone 11.** The **$899 XL** (the one I'm testing) is more compelling.'" Sets up the full review
  ([[2019-10-22-yt-mfbSqTB74xM]]).
