# OnePlus 10 Pro Impressions: What Happened? (@mkbhd, 2022-01-28)

## Summary
Impressions (not a full review; China-only launch) of the OnePlus 10 Pro, framed around the **"Oppo-fication of
OnePlus."** The durable industry thread: OnePlus's continued drift **from an enthusiast-focused company to a
"normal company making normal phones"** — this China unit literally runs **Oppo's ColorOS, not the OxygenOS fans
loved**, and even the charger switched to Oppo's **SuperVOOC** (and, annoyingly, **back to USB-A**). "Their most
normal phone yet" — not bad at all (great performance, an LTPO2 1-Hz display, decent Hasselblad cameras that are
"basically the same as last year"), and the standout is a genuine **battery + charging win** (5,000 mAh + 80 W
wired / 50 W wireless). Verdict withheld pending an international price.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-01-28] **The Oppo-fication — ColorOS, not OxygenOS.** "'This China unit **runs Oppo's ColorOS 12.1, not the
  OxygenOS OnePlus fans have loved for years** (incomplete English menus, un-removable pre-installed Chinese apps).
  It might have a OnePlus box, logo, and red cable, but **this OnePlus 10 Pro is more like an Oppo phone than ever
  before.** Whether the international version gets OxygenOS is unconfirmed — and even OxygenOS is more like ColorOS
  than ever now.'"
- [2022-01-28] **"Their most normal phone yet" — the enthusiast→normal arc.** "'A year ago I did a "What Happened
  to OnePlus" video about them going from a very enthusiast-focused company to a normal company making normal
  phones — **and this is their most normal phone yet.** That doesn't make it bad — the fast/smooth stuff, the high
  refresh display, great performance, the alert slider, great haptics are all still here.'"
- [2022-01-28] **The design + the "stovetop" camera.** "'Solid in person, classic OnePlus cues (centered logo,
  alert slider). The camera layout copies Samsung's S21 "a bit too obviously" — and with the satin back cutting to
  a glossy array, **this one looks more like a stovetop than any phone I've ever seen.** (The "P2D50T" flash text
  = Phone / 2nd-gen-Hasselblad / 50 MP triple.) An LTPO2 panel that modulates down to 1 Hz for more power savings.'"
- [2022-01-28] **Cameras basically the same; the battery is the real win.** "'The housing got a redesign but **the
  camera system is almost the same as last year** (same 48 MP main + 8 MP 3.3x tele, a new 150° ultrawide + X-Pan
  mode) — good daytime shots, a slow-ish shutter (moving subjects blur). But the real upgrade: **without getting
  bigger, a 5,000 mAh battery + 80 W wired + 50 W wireless — combining the best of both worlds** (~60% in 15 min).
  Sadly the SuperVOOC charger switched **back to USB-A.**'"
- [2022-01-28] **Verdict withheld — it's about the price.** "'No international launch, so no real price, so I'll
  withhold judgment. **~$700–900 could be reasonable; higher and it's fighting the big dogs; lower would be a
  surprise.**'"
