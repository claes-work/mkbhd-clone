# Mercedes AMG EQE SUV: Ugly but Comfortable!

## Summary

Marques reviews the **Mercedes-AMG EQE SUV** (~$110k performance EV) and reaches a familiar verdict:
**"really good, but I wouldn't pick it."** Its strengths are the **best interior and driver's seat**
in its class (a class that also includes the Model X Plaid, BMW iX M60, Mach-E GT); its weaknesses
are ~230 mi range, slow charging, no frunk, and a **functional-but-ugly "Windows Vista" UI.** When he
mentally shops the $100k performance-SUV segment, he lands on **range + network + storage → the Model
X Plaid.** Fully Marques-narrated.

## Key claims

- **[2024-05-22]** ⚠️ Looks: "I've never been shy about calling a vehicle ugly — this one's flirting on
  that line… I don't love it, and the bronze color I wouldn't pick." ~$110k, 96kWh, 680 hp, 3.2s 0–60,
  but **~230 mi range and 170kW charging ("its weak point — I'm used to 250/300/350")**; **no front
  trunk.** "One of the loudest EVs I've tested" (lots of lock/drive sound effects). (quote)
- **[2024-05-22]** ⭐ The strength: **"the best interior and the most comfortable one"** in the class —
  incredibly soft headrests, physical tactile vents, a great steering wheel, clever drive-mode dials.
  "If I could only spend time in the back seat versus its competitors, I'd pick the AMG." (quote)
- **[2024-05-22]** ⚠️ The software: **"it's very functional, it's just ugly — it looks like Windows 95 or
  Windows Vista"** (the MBUX Hyperscreen; good haptics, wireless CarPlay, well-laid-out, "but it doesn't
  look good"). The recurring **"Hey Mercedes, open the glovebox"** voice-only annoyance. (quote)
- **[2024-05-22]** Drive: "explosive in a straight line (nearly 700 hp kicks you forward), but once you
  turn, it's not that much better than a Model X Plaid — I might get flamed, but it's true," despite
  rear-wheel steering + great Michelins. Nearly 6,000 lb; air suspension Comfort→Sport→Sport Plus. (quote)
- **[2024-05-22]** ⭐ VERDICT (the Tesla-ecosystem tiebreaker again): **"Comparing this $100k electric
  performance SUV to the Model X Plaid, the Mach-E GT, whatever else — in my head I'm still going with
  the one with the most range, the best network, and the most storage, which is the Model X Plaid. I'd
  maybe take this or the iX next."** He'd only pick the AMG "because it's the best interior." (quote)
