# Every Mistake I Made in 2025 ★★

## Summary

A rare accountability/process video: Marques and his team went back through **every
long-form video uploaded in 2025 (~50+)** — combing comments, Reddit, and everywhere else
— to find and categorize every factual error that made it to the finished product, then
correct them all in one place. It's a landmark source on his **review methodology and
values**: the fact-checking stage he codified, the "reviewer's guide" as his spec source,
the two-types-of-errors framework, his petition for YouTube to bring back text annotations,
and his closing invocation of **Hanlon's razor** against conspiracy comments. ★★.

## Key claims

- **[2025-12-23]** He takes pride in the **writing / storytelling craft** of reviews and
  "doesn't take lightly" the responsibility of "an audience as big and discerning as you."
  Over the past couple years he's added process to minimize errors: **multiple eyeballs on a
  video before it's done**, and **a dedicated fact-checking stage before final export** — a
  process he "codified" at the start of 2025. (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-12-23]** Method: he and his team **re-watched every long-form 2025 video** and
  scoured comments/Reddit "to make sure we were thorough and found everything." His two
  conclusions: "we still have some work to do," and "**I wish YouTube would bring annotations
  back.**" (quote)
- **[2025-12-23]** **Two error types**: (1) technically-incorrect-but-doesn't-change-the-
  conclusion (perfect for annotations); (2) bigger errors needing a pinned comment — but "the
  correction rarely gets as much attention as the original." Hence this video. (paraphrase)
- **[2025-12-23]** The **reviewer's guide** is his spec source: "a PDF you get with the
  unreleased device that breaks down all the specs... **that is my source for the official
  specs because I obviously can't fact-check my specs by Googling it — Google's just going to
  give me rumored specs.**" (quote) He notes cases where the guide itself was wrong (Nothing
  changed the Phone 3's PWM dimming from 2160Hz→960Hz the day before embargo; Beats' misleading
  "most popular headphones in the world" claim later "clarified" to mean within the Beats lineup
  — "which would have just been easier to say, and I hate when companies do that").
- **[2025-12-23]** The corrections list (a window into his standards), e.g.: OnePlus **does**
  work on Verizon (S25 review); Nothing Phone 3a gets **6 years security + 3 OS updates**, not
  6 years of OS updates; called the MacBook Air a "MacBook Pro" once; the **bionic hand's EMG**
  measures muscle electrical activity caused by the brain, **not brain activity directly**;
  the **Pixel 9 reader was already ultrasonic** (still slow); the **S25 Edge does have a vapor
  chamber** (thinner/wider — "they deserve credit"); the base **iPhone 17 does have mmWave**
  (he cut the wrong claim out with YouTube's editor); **AntuTu scores shouldn't be compared
  cross-platform** (iPhone vs Xiaomi) — "I should not have used synthetic benchmark scores from
  a cross-platform test"; the OnePlus 15 **ceramic finish was only on the sandstone colorway**;
  graphic typos ("iPhone 16 Air," "1TB" that doesn't exist). (paraphrase)
- **[2025-12-23]** Root cause: **"it's a lot of these little numbers or specs or facts that I
  think I know, so I'm not double-checking, but I should be."** (quote)
- **[2025-12-23]** Inspiration: **Tom Scott's 2023** "correcting my mistakes" video; same
  takeaway — there will be future mistakes, hopefully fewer — and **Hanlon's razor**: "don't
  attribute to malice what can be explained by stupidity." He directly rebuts conspiracy
  comments that he **plants inaccuracies for engagement bait**: "No, that's not true. I would
  rather not make any mistakes and I'm going to try my best not to." (quote)
- **[2025-12-23]** ⬥ Data point: **~50+ long-form videos uploaded in 2025.** Teases the next
  video as "probably the highest fact-dense video I have ever uploaded" (the M5-scale video).
  (paraphrase)
