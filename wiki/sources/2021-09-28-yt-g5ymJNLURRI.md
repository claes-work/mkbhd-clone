# iPhone 13 Review: Lowkey Great! (@mkbhd, 2021-09-28)

## Summary
Full review of the iPhone 13 / 13 mini, opened with the promotable **Camry analogy**: reviewing this year vs. last
year is **for context, not to tell last-year owners to upgrade** ("if you have a 12, skip the 13"). Apple put its
**three biggest upgrades on the three most important parts — the screen, battery, and cameras.** The standouts: a
**genuinely huge battery improvement** ("I could get two full days of light use — I've never said that about an
iPhone"), **Photographic Styles** (baked-in processing profiles — "Rich Contrast makes it look like the Pixel"),
and **Cinematic Mode, which "isn't it"** for creators. Plus: **the mini is a dying breed** (best year to buy it).

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-09-28] **THE CAMRY ANALOGY — the review is for context, not last-year owners.** "'Most people don't upgrade
  every year — it's like cars: **a new Camry comes out every year, each a bit better, so that when people with
  OLDER cars finally upgrade, they get the newest.** Relentlessly comparing this year's Camry to last year's isn't
  for last-year owners (a small number) — **it's a comparison for context, to put it alongside something you know.**
  If you have a 12, skip the 13. This review is for people with older phones.'" A promotable reviewing framing.
- [2021-09-28] **Three biggest upgrades on the three most important parts.** "'The design is almost identical
  (dismiss it as minor, which it is) — **but they did their three biggest upgrades on the three most important
  parts of a phone: the screen, the battery, and the cameras. Pretty good for a minor update.**'"
- [2021-09-28] **The battery — the standout ("two full days of light use").** "'A bigger physical battery + the
  efficient A15 = **the iPhone's battery got way better — 5–7 hrs screen-on regularly; I only killed it once. I
  think I could get two full days of light use — I've never said that about an iPhone battery before.** It still
  charges slow (20 W) with no split-cell fast charging — just the simple stuff: a bigger battery, an efficient
  chip. Just works.'"
- [2021-09-28] **The smaller-but-taller notch does nothing new.** "'The notch is ~20% smaller (all left-right
  savings; it's actually slightly taller, poking into videos more). But **Apple didn't do anything with the extra
  space — same icons, no battery-percentage option.** We'll wait longer for faster Face ID or an in-display Touch
  ID or always-on.'"
- [2021-09-28] **PHOTOGRAPHIC STYLES — "make the iPhone look like the Pixel."** "'The most interesting new feature:
  **Photographic Styles — five presets (Standard, Rich Contrast, Vibrant, Warm, Cool) that are baked into the
  processing, not a filter on top you can change later** — like a mirrorless PP1/PP3 picture profile. **Someone
  like me leans toward the Pixel's confident contrasty look — now Rich Contrast makes the iPhone look like the
  Pixel; I've left it on full-time. That makes a bigger difference to me than the bigger sensor on a lot of
  shots.**'" A promotable "tune the iPhone to look like the Pixel" trick.
- [2021-09-28] **CINEMATIC MODE — "this ain't it."** "'I got pumped by the name — but **it's not very good at any
  one thing it's supposed to do.** It's locked to **1080p 30 fps** ("checkmate 24fps stans"), the focus tracking is
  shaky, it struggles in low light, no ultrawide, depth only adjustable in post in Apple apps — **not enough manual
  control to be "cinematic."** For a creator learning the fundamentals, **this ain't it — use FiLMiC Pro** and keep
  the iPhone's video quality. It can get better from here.'"
- [2021-09-28] **The mini is a dying breed + "the best camera in any phone."** "'The **13 mini's battery goes from
  bad to perfectly normal — the best year to get a mini, likely the last of a dying breed** (weak sales) of small
  flagship-spec phones. And **the 13 Pro is the best camera system I've ever tested in a phone — straight up** (this
  13 is close, minus the telephoto; could change when the Pixel 6 arrives).'"
- [2021-09-28] **The verdict — "a really good Camry," and the "just the new iPhone" prediction.** "'They took an
  already-great phone, kept the design, and **changed the things that count most — screen, battery, cameras. A
  pretty good minor update.** People complain it should've been the "12S," **but Apple's done with S names — and I
  wouldn't be shocked if before long they stop numbers altogether and it's just "the new iPhone [year]."** For me,
  it's all about the Pros.'"
