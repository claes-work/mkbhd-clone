# AirPods for Android? (@mkbhd, 2018-01-11)

## Summary
Uses Apple's AirPods with an Android phone — and, more interestingly, publicly eats his own earlier
skepticism. AirPods became a genuine hit, and he uses that to re-validate the "removing the jack forces
wireless to actually get good" thesis.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-01-11] **He calls out his own past skepticism.** "When AirPods were announced I was one of the
  skeptics — I tweeted doubts about the goofy-looking stems and losing them. I was wrong: they've turned into
  maybe **the most popular wireless earbuds on the planet.**" A characteristic on-record self-correction.
- [2018-01-11] **On Android, you lose the magic, keep the fundamentals.** With an Android phone you lose the
  W1 instant-pairing, the auto-ear-detection pause, Siri, and the battery widget — but they still work as a
  **very good, truly-wireless Bluetooth earbud** (pair once, good sound, the case is the killer feature). A
  fair cross-platform assessment.
- [2018-01-11] **Re-validates the jack thesis.** "This is the kind of thing I meant when phones started
  dropping the headphone jack — painful in the moment, but it **forced wireless audio to actually get good,
  fast.** AirPods are exhibit A." Ties back to his 2016–17 "the jack is dead, move on" arc
  ([[2016-09-09-yt-headphone-jack]] era).

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "I'll be the first to admit it — I was a skeptic, and I was wrong."
> "This is the wireless future getting good, quickly — which is exactly the point."
