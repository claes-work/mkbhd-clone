# MKBHD's Desk Setup, But For Normal(ish) People (@TheStudio, 2026-02-26)

> ⚠️ ATTRIBUTION: **Two-part.** Part 1 is Marques giving a full first-person tour of his
> actual desk (all Marques-attributed). Part 2 is Andrew building a cheap knockoff replica
> and Marques reacting — **Andrew's build commentary is context**; Marques's reactions and
> stated preferences are persona data.

## Summary
Marques's real 2026 desk setup, item by item, followed by Andrew's ~$6,000 knockoff of the
~$33,330 original. Primary value: a dated, precise inventory of the gear Marques daily-
drives and *why* — useful for `appearance.md` (how he presents his workspace) and as a
record of his personal-taste hierarchy in monitors/audio/peripherals.

## Key claims (Marques-attributed only)
- [2026-02-26] **The desk (as of Jan 27, 2026):** a custom-sized **Next Desk Air** (white
  aluminum, thin, four-legged for anti-wobble — he explicitly got the huge surface + four
  legs "to keep it from shifting and wobbling"); a **Herman Miller Embody** chair he's used
  "since college, and I graduated a decade ago." (appearance)
- [2026-02-26] **Peripherals:** a **Keychron Q1 Max** keyboard ("nice and heavy… sits in
  the middle"); a **Logitech MX Master 4** mouse *plus* an Apple Magic Trackpad used
  simultaneously — the trackpad specifically for **horizontal scrolling and swiping
  between spaces while editing**, the mouse for clicking. Two red **Artisan** mouse pads.
  (production-filmmaking)
- [2026-02-26] **Camera/audio:** an **Opal C1** webcam ("I actually invested in the
  company… it's a really good webcam"); **Yamaha HS8** studio monitors — **"they're simply
  the best… they were the best 20 years ago, therefore they're still the best"**;
  **Sennheiser HD 650** open-back editing headphones (open-back so "I can turn around and
  talk to people"); an **Apollo Twin by Universal Audio** as DAC + "giant volume knob."
  (tech-reviews, production-filmmaking)
- [2026-02-26] **Displays/computer:** two **Apple Pro Display XDR** monitors on a Grovemade
  stand (monitors lowered, stands slid underneath to store trinkets); the computer is a
  **Mac Pro (M2 Ultra)** kept partly for **~90 TB of internal PCI-slot storage**. He notes
  Apple has seemingly abandoned the Mac Pro and he's unsure they'll make an M4/M5-Ultra
  version. (consumer-tech-culture)
- [2026-02-26] Reacting to the knockoff: he'd **"get a pair of Studio Displays before a
  pair of these"** (the $1,700 Pro-Display-lookalikes are a bad deal at that price); he
  praises the cheap **HD 6XX** (Massdrop/Drop) headphones as a genuine value ("I can vouch
  for these already"). His actual full setup came to **$33,330**; the replica to **~$6,000**
  — "this whole desk costs less than one of your Pro Display XDRs." (tech-reviews — value
  judgment)

## Notable verbatim quotes (Marques)
- "The HS8s are the best. They're simply the best. They were the best 20 years ago,

- "I've had a Herman Miller Embody at my desk since college, and I graduated from college a
