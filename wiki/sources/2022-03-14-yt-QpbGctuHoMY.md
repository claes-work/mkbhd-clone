# iPhone SE (2022) Review: Old Dog. New Trick. (@mkbhd, 2022-03-14)

## Summary
The full review of the $429 2022 iPhone SE. His reframe: **SE officially stands for "Special Edition," but this is
the least special phone Apple makes — call it the "Simple Edition."** The formula is unchanged: **new chip, old
body, low price** (the A15 Bionic in an iPhone 8 shell). The durable idea: **the flagship chip is about headroom
and longevity** — "too much engine in a tiny car," but it means **the longest-lasting $400 phone** (years of
updates and consistent performance). The verdict shape: **world-class chip, camera, and haptics; below-average
everything else** (a 720p 60 Hz screen, one camera, mediocre battery). He keeps wondering **"what if they weren't
so locked into this formula?"** — but Apple only knows how to make a cheap phone by reusing the iPhone 8 body.
Still easy to recommend as the cheapest new iPhone, and **"never underestimate the power of the home button."**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-03-14] **The "Simple Edition" — new chip, old body, low price.** "'On paper SE is "Special Edition," but
  **this is the least special phone Apple makes — I'd call it the Simple Edition.** The formula is and has been
  simple: **new chip, old body, low price** — the A15 Bionic (same as the iPhone 13) in the iPhone 8 body, home
  button and all, for $429.'"
- [2022-03-14] **The flagship chip = headroom and longevity.** "'Putting an A15 in a $400 phone means it has a more
  powerful chip than the $1,300 S22 Ultra — **like too much engine in a tiny car.** But the reason is the same as
  always: **headroom for days.** It'll perform the same in 2, 4, 6 years — **the longest-lasting $400 phone**, and
  longevity/updates is exactly what the SE buyer wants.'"
- [2022-03-14] **World-class chip + camera; below-average everything else.** "'The single 12 MP camera is the same
  hardware as the iPhone 8, but **it's straight up the best single camera in this range** (most consistent color/
  noise; Deep Fusion, Smart HDR 4, Photographic Styles from the A15; the best video camera in any $400 phone). But
  **world-class is the chip, camera, and haptics — below-average is everything else**: a 4.7" 720p 60 Hz 600-nit
  screen that feels outdated, one camera (I miss an ultrawide/2x), and mediocre, barely-average battery (I killed
  it more than once by 9 pm).'"
- [2022-03-14] **"What if they weren't so locked into this formula?"** "'The thing I kept wondering: **what if they
  weren't so locked into this exact formula?** A cheap 120 Hz display would make it feel faster for the phone's
  life; a thicker body could fit a much bigger battery; an ultrawide is on nearly every budget rival — but each
  means new tooling and a higher price, by how much I'll wonder forever, **because reusing the iPhone 8 body seems
  to be the only way Apple knows how to make a cheaper phone.** It's boring and predictable, but simple — you know
  exactly what you're getting and not getting.'"
- [2022-03-14] **Still easy to recommend — and the home button.** "'Still easy to recommend as **the cheapest new
  iPhone that'll get updates the longest.** The natural cross-shops are the iPhone 11 (~$100 more: bigger display,
  Face ID, better battery, no 5G) and the 13 mini (~$300 more) — **but neither has what this has: the home button.
  A lot of people still just want a home button — never underestimate the power of the home button.** Glad it got
  any update at all: old dog, new trick.'"
