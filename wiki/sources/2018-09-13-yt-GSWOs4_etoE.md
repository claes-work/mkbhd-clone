# iPhone Xs and iPhone Xs Max Impressions! (@mkbhd, 2018-09-13)

## Summary
Hands-on first look at the iPhone XS / XS Max — framed as "a classic Apple S update" (spec bump, no new design).
Walks the A12 Bionic, the camera/Smart-HDR/editable-portrait-mode upgrade, and closes with the same prediction
he'd expand elsewhere: **the XR might be the real hit.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-09-13] **"A classic S update."** "Same 5.8"/6.5" sizes, same design, same notch/bezels. **The new color is
  gold** — "the only way to tell on the outside that you have the new iPhone is to get the gold one." A spec bump,
  not a redesign — "and I've got no problem with that."
- [2018-09-13] **The A12 Bionic + the camera as the real upgrades.** The **first 7 nm chip** (~7 billion
  transistors, "maybe 30% faster" in app launches/animations — to be verified); and the camera + **new image
  signal processing** ("image processing means a lot — it's a major reason the Pixel 2 is so good"): **Smart HDR**,
  better low light, less noise, and a much-improved **portrait mode** where you can **edit the simulated f-stop**
  ("directional/radial blur based on distance from center — I was really impressed"). Also IP68, louder stereo
  speakers, dual SIM, slightly faster Face ID, modestly longer battery.
- [2018-09-13] **Not a must-buy — and the XR prediction.** "**Starting at $999 / $1099** (up to $1449 for the
  512 GB Max) — is that a big enough change? Honestly **not a must-buy for most, especially if you have the X.**
  **My gut feeling is the iPhone XR might be the real hit this year for Apple**" (a separate video, [[2018-09-13-yt-mgOQgciWLlg]]).
  Between sizes, "**I'm definitely more into the larger phone.**"

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "It's a classic Apple S update — a spec bump, some internal changes, but nothing drastic and no new design."
> "The only way to tell on the outside that you have the new iPhone is to get the gold one — so you do you."
> "My gut feeling is that the iPhone XR might be the real hit this year for Apple."
