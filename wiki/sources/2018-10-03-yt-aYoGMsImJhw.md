# LG V40 Impressions: 5 Cameras on a Phone?! (@mkbhd, 2018-10-03)

## Summary
First impressions of the LG V40 ("essentially a G7 with a whole new camera setup"). The takeaway is his praise
for **five useful cameras across genuinely different focal lengths** — "probably the best use of multiple cameras
I've seen" — with honest nitpicks (the telephoto looks like a different camera; Triple Shot is silly).

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-10-03] **"Best use of multiple cameras I've seen" — different focal lengths, all useful.** "**Three on the
  back (normal / telephoto / super-wide), two on the front (normal / super-wide) — five.** No monochrome sensors,
  no duplicate focal lengths — **it's actually three (and two) genuinely different useful things.**" A clear
  design principle: multiple cameras should each earn their place with a distinct focal length.
- [2018-10-03] **The honest camera nitpicks.** "The telephoto has different color science / white balance, so it
  **looks like a different camera, not just a zoom**; its minimum focus distance is longer (macros fail). And
  **Triple Shot** (fire all three at once) produces "the **worst super-zoom effect of all time** — seems silly; I'd
  rather take my time and choose." Fair, specific criticism.
- [2018-10-03] **Front-facing dual cameras — "a good idea."** "I don't take many selfies, but two front focal
  lengths (normal 8 MP + wide 5 MP) is a **useful difference** for fitting more people in — the wide distorts more,
  so not all the time, but it works." Endorses the concept (revisited on the Pixel 3).
- [2018-10-03] **"Essentially a G7-S."** Same SD845/6 GB/build/boombox speaker/quad DAC + jack/IP68/wireless
  charging/back fingerprint — plus a bigger **6.4" OLED** (vs the G7's LCD) and a bigger battery. And **the
  non-remappable Google Assistant button, again** — "I don't hate it, but I still wish it was remappable." His
  consistent principle.
- [2018-10-03] **The LG-identity verdict.** "You kind of have to **want an LG phone already** — this is the icing;
  it probably won't change your mind if not. Still cool to see, and worth considering at **$900** (not the insane
  $1000)." His recurring sympathetic-but-realistic LG diagnosis.

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "This is probably the best use of multiple cameras that I've seen — no monochrome, no duplicate focal lengths,
> just three different useful things on the back and two on the front."
> "The normal camera versus the telephoto doesn't just look like a zoom — it looks like a different camera."
> "Triple Shot gives you the absolute worst super-zoom effect of all time. Seems silly to me."
