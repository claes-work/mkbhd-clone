# Pixel 6/6 Pro Unboxing & First Look! (@mkbhd, 2021-10-19)

## Summary
Unboxing "maybe the most hyped devices of the year." The durable notes: **the pricing was a pleasant surprise
($599 / $899)** — "a lot better than I expected" — but **the $300 gap makes the 6 Pro a tough sell**; the **camera-
visor design** (unique, rests your finger, doesn't rock — but gathers dust); a **review-embargo caveat** (he can't
show software/photos yet — "a bit of a red flag"); **five years of security updates** ("good looks for the Pixel");
and that he **prefers the look/feel of the non-Pro Pixel 6.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-10-19] **The pricing surprise — but the $300 gap.** "'**$599 for the Pixel 6 / $899 for the 6 Pro — a lot
  better than I expected; $599 just feels like a really good deal.** But then: **there's $300 between them and not
  a whole ton of physical difference — which makes the 6 Pro seem like a bit of a tough sell.**' (No charger in
  the box — "but now we know.")'"
- [2021-10-19] **The differences — more screen, battery, camera, RAM on the Pro.** "'Both have the **Tensor chip
  + Titan M2**, 128 GB base. The 6 Pro adds: a **6.7" 120 Hz 1440p LTPO** (vs the 6's 6.4" 90 Hz 1080p flat), more
  RAM (12 vs 8), a bigger battery (5,000 vs 4,600), and a **folded 4x optical telephoto** (they share the new 50 MP
  primary + ultrawide). Essentially: **more screen, battery, camera, and RAM on the bigger phone — which makes the
  Pixel 6 look like the better deal.**'"
- [2021-10-19] **THE EMBARGO CAVEAT — "a bit of a red flag."** "'For this video **I'm not allowed to share any new
  software features or sample photos yet until the full review — which is pretty annoying, and quite frankly a bit
  of a red flag.**' (I can show what's confirmed + the differences + my reactions.)'" A pointed process note (his
  media-literacy instinct).
- [2021-10-19] **The camera-visor design — unique, functional, but gathers dust.** "'The **full-width camera visor**
  is Google clearly going for a unique design ("not look like any other phone" — no corner bump) — and it works:
  **it's a place to rest your finger, and it won't rock on a table at all. But it's already gathering dust in the
  corners at that 90° angle — annoying to clean.** Usually a strange-looking render grows on me once I hold it —
  this did. **I prefer the look/feel of the non-Pro Pixel 6** (I miss the accent power buttons — RIP).'"
- [2021-10-19] **Five years of security updates — "good looks for the Pixel."** "'Both promise **five years of
  security updates — awesome for an Android phone.** And **the ultrawide selfie camera is back** (I missed it).
  Good looks for the Pixel.'" (The vertical-integration → longer-updates thesis landing.)'"
