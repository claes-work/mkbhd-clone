# OnePlus Nord Reveal! How Much Do Smartphones Actually Cost? (@mkbhd, 2020-07-14)

## Summary
An interview with **Carl Pei** (OnePlus co-founder, guest) that answers the call from his "Budget Phone Blueprint"
video for **real phone-part costs**. **Carl Pei's statements are quarantined**; the value is that Marques's own
framework got answered with data. Ends with the OnePlus Nord design reveal.

> ⚠️ ATTRIBUTION: Interview. **Carl Pei is the guest** — the specific cost figures and OnePlus product-strategy
> statements are **his, context only, not persona data.** Only Marques-attributed framing below.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
### Marques-attributed (persona-eligible)
- [2020-07-14] **The follow-up to his own framework.** "'In the **Budget Phone Blueprint** ([[2020-05-15-yt-u41Zt6z5s7A]])
  I called out any company willing to share **actual part costs** — and OnePlus reached out. **Some of the most
  interesting, informative discussion I've had in the smartphone world in a long time.**' A datable example of his
  framework prompting real-world data." His convening/curiosity in his own voice.
- [2020-07-14] **His guiding questions (what he's always wondered).** "Marques presses on the trade-offs he cares
  about: **how much does NFC cost, IP68 certification, an AMOLED-vs-LCD display, high refresh rate, a headphone
  jack** — the exact levers from his budget-phone game. His **framing** (why these get cut for cost) is the
  persona-eligible part; the numbers are the guest's." His stated interest in the economics behind the compromises.
- [2020-07-14] **The design-locked-6-9-months-before-launch insight (his takeaway).** "'Marques's reflection:
  **the design has to be finalized 6-9 months before launch — "it almost requires a vision of what you want in the
  future long before you're going to share it."** That's why leaks rarely change a phone — tooling has started.'"
  A reusable product-development observation he draws out.

### Guest (Carl Pei) — QUARANTINED, context only
- Carl Pei's figures — **NFC ≈ $4 (BOM); IP68 ≈ $15/phone** (mostly the testing machines + manpower, not the seals;
  "no official certifying body, just agreed standards"); **AMOLED up to 2× an LCD** (curved even more, lower yield);
  high-refresh cost falling with volume; the headphone jack "cheap in BOM but costs design space" — and the design-
  changed-late-by-a-month Nord story — are **guest context, not persona data.**

## Notable verbatim quotes
