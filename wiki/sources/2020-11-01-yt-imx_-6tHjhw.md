# PlayStation 5 Controller: Major Key! (@mkbhd, 2020-11-01)

## Summary
A focused take arguing the **DualSense controller might be the best part of the PS5 — and the most "next-gen"
thing about any upcoming console.** The durable idea: **the thing in your hands connects you to the game more
physically than the graphics do**, and Sony's **strong new haptic motor + adaptive triggers** are a genuine
generational step (demoed by Astro's Playroom). Ties controller-haptics quality to his recurring **phone-haptic-
motor-quality** and **MacBook trackpad** analogies.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-11-01] **THE THESIS — the controller may be the most next-gen thing.** "'The PS5 controller **might be
  the best part of the PS5 — and maybe the most next-gen thing about any of the upcoming consoles.** Graphics
  and games get better every gen, but **the controller evolving is important — it's the one thing that connects
  you to the game maybe more than the screen.**'"
- [2020-11-01] **The two real upgrades — strong haptics + adaptive triggers.** "'**New strong haptic motor** —
  the difference between a new iPhone's haptic motor and an old sloppy vibration motor — plus **adaptive triggers**
  with variable resistance that responds to what's happening on screen. **Together they're the DualSense, and
  it's a feel that's a major step up.**'"
- [2020-11-01] **Astro's Playroom — the tailor-made demo.** "'Sony built Astro's Playroom to show it off:
  **everything you touch feels different** — footsteps on glass vs metal vs sand vs water, different impacts for
  coins vs leaves vs enemies, and a spring you compress via the triggers where **resistance + vibration feel
  exactly like squeezing a real spring.** A very convincing combination.'"
- [2020-11-01] **HAPTIC-MOTOR QUALITY — the cross-device throughline.** "'This reminds me: **whenever I use a
  phone with a really good haptic motor I appreciate it way more — it feels newer and more next-gen** than
  phones with bad vibration motors. Also **the MacBook Pro's Force Touch trackpad — you'd swear the glass moves,
  but it's just a great haptic motor behind it.** With more space in a controller, you can do even more.'" His
  documented obsession with haptic quality.
- [2020-11-01] **The open question — which games will use it?** "'The question now is **what other games will
  take advantage of it** — shooters (different trigger feel per gun), driving games (surface feedback like a
  good wheel), sports (2K — feeling the dribble, the free-throw heartbeat). I'm hoping PS5 devs are as amped as
  I am.'"
