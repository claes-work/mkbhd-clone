# Asus ZenFone 6: Swivel Camera Magic! (@mkbhd, 2019-05-29)

## Summary
Impressions (not a full review) of the $500 Asus ZenFone 6 — "one of the surprises of the year." Its gimmick is
a **motorized flip-up main camera** that doubles as the selfie camera, achieving a notch-free full-screen front.
Marques frames the swivel camera as **another workaround** on the road to bezel-less, not the permanent solution.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-05-29] **"Hardware is back" — the swivel-camera workaround.** "The **motorized main camera flips around**
  to become the selfie camera → **no notch, no cutout, no hole-punch.** But it's firmly in the **same category as
  notches/hole-punches/pop-ups** — 'definitely still a workaround, not the permanent solution on our way to
  bezel-less phones.'" His consistent framing of front-camera-hiding tricks.
- [2019-05-29] **The precedent — the 2013 Oppo N1.** "I reviewed the **Oppo N1** in 2013 with a manual swivel
  camera ('you swiveled it around with your hands like a caveman'); this one is **motorized/electronic** and
  dual-camera (48 MP main + 13 MP ultrawide)." Shows his long institutional memory of hardware.
- [2019-05-29] **The trade-offs of a motor.** "**No IP rating** (dust/water risk), the **motor makes concerning
  noises** under resistance, but it adds **drop protection** (snaps back on free-fall, like OnePlus's pop-up) and
  **auto-panorama** (motor sweeps the lens — genuinely useful) plus gimmicky fine-angle control. Rated 100k
  cycles ≈ a couple years." Balanced engineering read.
- [2019-05-29] **Camera quality + the honest grading scale.** "If the **pixel's camera is my A+, this is a solid
  B-minus to B** — main problem is dynamic range and **inconsistency** ('can't be trusted to take a great shot
  every time'); ultrawide is much worse than main. But **as a selfie camera the main sensor is one of the best**
  (real, non-fake background blur)." Consistent A+/letter-grade camera framework.
- [2019-05-29] **The sleeper package + the ZenUI win.** "**Snapdragon 855, up to 8 GB, a massive 5,000 mAh
  battery** (largest cell he'd used), headphone jack, good speakers — a 'really nice collection of pieces for
  $500.' Biggest win: **Asus finally dropped the heavy ZenUI skin** for near-stock Android." Notes the
  bloatware-removal as a genuine improvement.
- [2019-05-29] **Camera-quality bias, self-aware.** "'I'm a sucker for camera quality over anything else as far
  as practicality — that's why I was so into the pixel 3 for so long despite its issues.'" A self-aware statement
  of his personal reviewing priorities.

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "This is definitely still a workaround — this isn't like the permanent solution on our way to bezel-less
> phones. This falls in the category of notches and hole-punch cutouts and pop-up cameras."
> "I think I'm a sucker for camera quality over like anything else as far as practicality."
