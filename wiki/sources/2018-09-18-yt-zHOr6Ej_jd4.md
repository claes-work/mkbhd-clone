# Gold iPhone Xs Max Unboxing! (@mkbhd, 2018-09-18)

## Summary
The unboxing companion to the XS review — a short video whose durable content is two pointed callouts about
what's **missing** from the box (the dongle, a fast charger) and a wry note on the notch-hiding wallpapers.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-09-18] **No dongle in the box — "done on purpose."** "Every previous jack-less iPhone came with a little
  lightning-to-headphone-jack dongle. **This year it's gone.** You wouldn't blink at an $8 adapter on a $1000+
  phone — but it's **clearly done on purpose: Apple can afford to include it and chose not to**, nudging people
  toward wireless/lightning headphones." (Fun fact: "**dongles have been the #1 best-seller in Apple retail by
  volume for two years.**") A sharp read of a small deletion.
- [2018-09-18] **No fast charger either — "kind of lame."** "By 2018 I hoped for a **fast charger or a USB-C-to-
  lightning cable** in the box — we get neither. It's getting **kind of lame and outdated that Apple, of all
  companies, doesn't ship a fast charger** with such an expensive premium phone." A recurring value gripe.
- [2018-09-18] **The notch-hiding wallpapers (wry).** "As much as they want us to embrace the notch, **the clever
  wallpapers do a lot of work to hide it** — honestly maybe the best use of 3D Touch (the live ones) right now."
  A gentle needle at Apple's own ambivalence about the notch.

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "Apple can afford to put the dongle in there — they chose not to this time, to nudge people toward wireless."
> "It's getting kind of lame that Apple, of all companies, doesn't ship a fast charger in the box of such an
> expensive premium phone."
> "As much as they want us to embrace the notch, that's a lot of work to cleverly hide it."
