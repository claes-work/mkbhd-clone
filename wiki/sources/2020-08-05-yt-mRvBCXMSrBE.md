# Samsung Galaxy Note 20 Ultra Impressions: Ultra Premium! (@mkbhd, 2020-08-05)

## Summary
First impressions of the Note 20 Ultra ($1,299) and Note 20 ($999). The Ultra is a premium standout (best display,
the S Pen's huge latency leap); the durable call is that the **Note 20 (non-Ultra) is overpriced** — a first in
Note history.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-08-05] **The S PEN latency leap — matches the Apple Pencil.** "'The biggest Note 20 Ultra leap: **S Pen
  latency from ~42 ms down to 9 ms — a fifth of the time, matching the Apple Pencil.** Zero perceptible gap; it
  feels like writing directly on the display. A great update for S Pen users.'" A concrete, dated spec win.
- [2020-08-05] **The Ultra — premium, boxy, best display (with the 120Hz-or-1440p caveat).** "'Boxy the way I like
  it, impossibly thin bezels, premium glass/metal, one of the **best displays in any phone.** But — like the S20
  Ultra — **you can't run 120 Hz and 1440p at once**; I'll take **120 Hz at 1080p** (the frames beat the pixels).'"
- [2020-08-05] **The 100x "was a mistake" — 50x periscope now.** "'The periscope tops at **50x (not the 100x
  printed on the S20 Ultra — Samsung realizes that was a mistake)**, and we're all okay with that as long as the
  **3x/5x/10x we actually want are better.** 8K still crops from the telephoto.'" A vindication of his S20 Ultra
  zoom critique.
- [2020-08-05] **THE CALL — the Note 20 (non-Ultra) is overpriced.** "'For the **first time in Note history, the
  Note 20 is a bit overpriced** — **$999 for a 1080p / 60 Hz PLASTIC phone with 128 GB non-expandable storage.**
  People cross-shopping will see the **S20 or OnePlus 8 Pro next to it for less that feel like they give you
  more.** The one reason to get it is the still-unrivaled S Pen (+ wireless DeX).'" A pointed value verdict.
- [2020-08-05] **The Note-20-is-to-the-Ultra-what-the-S20-is-to-the-S20-Ultra framing.** "'A slightly smaller,
  slightly-less-premium flagship with a few cut-downs most people hopefully won't notice (the 30x-vs-50x zoom, the
  26-vs-9 ms pen) — mostly a good idea, but the $999 plastic/1080p/60Hz combo doesn't feel up to the money.'"

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "For the first time in the Note's history, this Note 20 is a bit overpriced — I think you can straight up get
> better phones for a thousand bucks, or get equivalent phones for less."
> "The S Pen has now arrived at Apple Pencil levels of responsiveness — in fact it's matched that 9-millisecond
> number."
