# This is a Crypto Phone. Don't Buy It. (Solana Saga) (@mkbhd, 2023-09-07)

## Summary
A review of the **$1,000 Solana Saga "crypto phone"** with a clear verdict in the title — but a more interesting
argument underneath: **"if you took the crypto out of this phone, it would at least have potential."** The lineage
matters: it traces to the **Essential Phone** (2017, Andy Rubin's promising minimalist startup that flopped —
high price, mediocre cameras, Sprint exclusive — then imploded), whose engineers regrouped as **OSOM** to build the
OV1 (a spiritual successor) — but couldn't get enough hype (**"the classic enthusiast-phone problem: a cool idea
some people really want, but not enough hype to make the whole thing happen"**). Then **Solana** (Web3 cash) backed
it and rebranded it the crypto phone. Stripped of crypto it's an average phone with **two things he genuinely
likes: world-class build (matte steel, ceramic, "a tank") and extremely clean stock Android.** But the three
crypto features (the Solana Mobile Stack, Seed Vault, Dapp store) are **"about the most niche feature possible"**
(Solana-only; the price already crashed $1,000 → $600) — and **"there's basically no reason for 99.99% of people to
buy this"** ($600 gets a Pixel 7/8 or refurb S23). The bittersweet takeaway: **"being a brand-new phone company is
really hard, unless your name is Carl Pei"** — he wishes the OV1 could just exist for a small niche; instead it's
**"the perfect embodiment of crypto in 2023: at best ahead of its time, at worst completely useless to most
people."** (The one gem: the included **USB-C cable with a hardware data-blocker switch** against juice-jacking —
"available on Amazon for $30.")

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2023-09-07] **If you took the crypto out, it'd have potential.** "'I've been notably skeptical on crypto/Web3, so
  call me biased — but I still think if you took the crypto out of this phone, it would at least have potential. Two
  things I genuinely like: world-class build quality (matte steel, ceramic — this phone is a tank) and extremely
  clean stock Android.'"
- [2023-09-07] **The Essential → OSOM lineage and the classic enthusiast-phone problem.** "'It traces to the
  Essential Phone — Andy Rubin's promising minimalist startup that flopped (high price, mediocre cameras, a Sprint
  exclusive) and imploded. Its engineers regrouped as OSOM to build the OV1, but couldn't get enough interest —
  the classic enthusiast-phone problem: a cool idea some people really want, but not enough hype to make the whole
  thing happen. Then Solana brought the cash and rebranded it the crypto phone.'"
- [2023-09-07] **The crypto features are about as niche as possible.** "'The three things that make it a crypto phone
  (the Solana Mobile Stack, Seed Vault, Dapp store) are about the most niche feature possible — Solana-only, and the
  Dapp store is coming to other phones anyway. Remember the Zenfone 10 I love — a shame it won't sell because not
  many people like small phones? Are there more people in the Solana ecosystem who want this than people who want a
  small phone?'"
- [2023-09-07] **No reason for 99.99% of people; being a new phone company is hard.** "'The price crashed from $1,000
  to $600 in under a year, and there's basically no reason for 99.99% of people to buy this — for $600 you can get a
  Pixel 7 or 8, a refurbished S23, any number of OnePlus phones. Being a brand-new phone company is really hard,
  unless your name is Carl Pei, apparently.'"
- [2023-09-07] **The perfect embodiment of crypto in 2023 — plus the data-blocker cable.** "'I wish the OSOM OV1
  could just exist for a small niche community to love — instead this becomes the perfect embodiment of crypto in
  2023: at best ahead of its time, at worst completely useless to most people. The one gem: the included USB-C cable
  has a hardware switch that blocks all data (only power) — great against juice-jacking — but it's on Amazon for
  $30.'"
