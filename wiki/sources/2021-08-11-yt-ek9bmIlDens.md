# Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 3 Impressions: 3 New Features! (@mkbhd, 2021-08-11)

## Summary
Impressions of the $1,800 Galaxy Z Fold 3 — **"the most premium, well-built folding phone I've ever seen."** The
durable notes: a run of **small changes that add up** (thinner/lighter, the hinge tucked further in, a 120 Hz
cover screen); the first **water-resistant folding phone (IPX8 — but not dust-resistant, and dust is the hinge's
nemesis)**; an **under-display selfie camera** that's a real bleeding-edge risk (visible, pixelated, mediocre — on
the least-used camera); and **S Pen support that needs so many special workarounds** that "Samsung should just
keep the Note."

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-08-11] **The most well-built foldable — small changes that add up.** "'Looks like the Fold 2, but **a
  bunch of small changes add up to a new generation: ~2 mm thinner, lighter, Gorilla Glass Victus, a smaller
  camera bump, and the hinge tucked further into the side** — the whole thing feels very firmly, satisfyingly
  well put together.'"
- [2021-08-11] **The first water-resistant foldable — IPX8 (but dust is the nemesis).** "'Surprisingly it's
  **IPX8 water resistant — a first for a new-folding-screen phone; it can be immersed.** Note IPX8, not IP68 — the
  X means **no dust-resistance rating, and dust (the tiny grains getting into the hinge and under the screen) has
  been the nemesis of these hinges.** That they made it watertight enough to certify is impressive.'"
- [2021-08-11] **The 120 Hz cover screen — fixing the Fold 2's win-lose.** "'The cover screen is now **120 Hz to
  match the inside** — on the Fold 2 I never got over the 60 Hz outside feeling worse than the 120 Hz inside,
  which made me use it less. Now it just feels like a really tall high-refresh candy-bar phone.'"
- [2021-08-11] **The under-display selfie camera — a bleeding-edge risk on the least-used camera.** "'The inside
  hole-punch is gone — an **under-display selfie camera in a folding display.** It's a first-gen risk (visible;
  an octagon of pixels that pixelates bright colors off-axis; hazy in the viewfinder, then a defogging algorithm
  snaps the contrast in — still not a great picture). **Samsung's logic: it's the least-used selfie camera (you
  have the cover hole-punch, or open it and use the main cameras), so this was their place to take the risk** for
  the full corner-to-corner inside screen.'"
- [2021-08-11] **S Pen support — but so many workarounds "Samsung should keep the Note."** "'The 80%-more-durable
  film finally makes it **S Pen-compatible — but with an asterisk: you need a special S Pen Fold Edition (soft
  retractable tip, so you can't damage the screen) + an optional case to carry it; other styluses will damage the
  screen.** The only other option is the bigger S Pen Pro (with a "Z Fold mode" switch). **It's all specialized
  workarounds to loop the Fold into the S Pen world — I think Samsung should keep the Galaxy Note around.**'" A
  pointed take (a prediction-adjacent read; the Note line was ending).
- [2021-08-11] **The dock + the price.** "'The multitasking-monster software adds a **pinnable dock on the right —
  like macOS or Windows** — for your shortcuts/recent apps. Snapdragon 888, 12 GB, from $1,800 (a little less than
  before). **The most premium foldable yet — but the Flip is the one I'd recommend to more people.**'"
