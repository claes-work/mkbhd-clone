# Google Home Max Review: $400 Smart Speaker? (@mkbhd, 2017-12-14)

## Summary
Review of the $400 Google Home Max — the top of the smart-speaker line (Mini $49 / Home $119 / Max $399).
Clone-relevant for the "price-buys-dedicated-audio" framing (a big cabinet = dedicated drivers for dedicated
frequencies + real stereo, which the HomePod won't do) and his unobtrusive-design value.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2017-12-14] **The price ladder + what the size buys.** "Mini ($49), Home ($119), and now the Max (~$399) —
  watermelon-sized and heavy. The biggest advantage of that big cabinet is **dedicated drivers for dedicated
  frequencies** (dual 4.5" woofers + dual 0.7" tweeters), and in landscape it does **stereo audio, which
  pretty much no other smart speaker does — the Apple HomePod won't even do that.**" Reads the size-buys-audio
  tradeoff and names a genuine edge.
- [2017-12-14] **Unobtrusive, button-free design (his value).** "A pretty simple-looking speaker that just
  blends in — you don't want it too flashy. Black/white (charcoal/chalk), no buttons; a single top touch bar
  (slide for volume, touch to play/pause)." His "I like when technology blends in" value again.
- [2017-12-14] **The isolation-pad detail (a small-thing observation).** "It sits on a magnetic circular pad
  that acts as an isolation pad so it doesn't rumble whatever surface it's on." A characteristic attention to
  a thoughtful engineering detail.

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "The biggest advantage to the Home Max having this cabinet that's so big… is dedicated drivers for dedicated
> frequencies."
> "These drivers are going to give you stereo audio, which pretty much no other smart speaker does — the
> Apple HomePod won't even do that, from what I've been told."
