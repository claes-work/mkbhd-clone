# Samsung Galaxy S24/Ultra Impressions: More Than Meets The Eye! (@mkbhd, 2024-01-17)

## Summary
A first look at the S24 lineup — **"way more interesting than they look,"** because the story is **the Samsung AI
thing**, not the hardware. Physically small changes: matte finishes (not glossy), flat sides "exactly like an
iPhone," thinner bezels, brighter (2,600-nit) displays (the S24+ jumps to Quad HD at 120 Hz), and — on the Ultra —
**titanium rails, a fully flat display, and the 10x telephoto replaced by a new 50 MP 5x** (his "lens theory":
few people zoom to 10x, so a higher-res 5x can crop to 10x with similar quality). The real news is the **Snapdragon
8 Gen 3's on-device AI** (look for the "barred stars"): **Circle to Search / "instant visual search"** (long-press
and circle anything to Google it — "super useful, people will use it a lot"); **live call translation** (on-device
language packs, real-time two-way — "the smoothest I've used"); AI in the voice recorder/notes (summaries); and the
**photo Magic Editor — "literally a full-on Magic Editor, just like the Pixel"** (fewer restrictions than Google's;
**with a baked-in AI watermark** so you know it's fake). His wry read: some of it "isn't just blatantly copying
Apple's homework"... and Bixby isn't taking any big leaps. "2024 is the year of AI features on phones." (dbrand
Grip Case, now MagSafe-compatible.)

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2024-01-17] **More than meets the eye — it's the AI thing.** "'They look very similar to the S23 lineup design-
  wise, but it's when you get under the hood — especially the software and the whole Samsung AI thing — that it gets
  way more interesting. The list of physically new stuff isn't long.'"
- [2024-01-17] **The 5x replaces the 10x — the lens theory.** "'The Ultra keeps the 200 MP main but replaces the
  10x/10 MP telephoto with a new 5x/50 MP. My theory: not a lot of people actually zoom to 10x, and 5x is a much
  more common range — and the higher megapixels mean hitting 10x just crops the middle 50% with nearly the same
  quality as the old low-res 10x. It's a lens theory; I have to test it.'"
- [2024-01-17] **Circle to Search is the most useful AI here.** "'The big one most people will find most useful:
  instant visual search. Long-press the home button/gesture bar and tap or circle anything on screen to instantly
  Google it. Reverse image search isn't new, but the convenience of circling something on any screen is awesome —
  people are going to use it a lot.'"
- [2024-01-17] **Live call translation, on-device.** "'The phone app gets AI: download a language pack and it does
  real-time two-way call translation — you speak English, they hear Korean, they speak Korean, you immediately hear
  English. All offline with an on-device model. The smoothest live translation I've used (I don't speak Korean, so
  I'll verify accuracy in Spanish for the review).'"
- [2024-01-17] **A photo Magic Editor "just like the Pixel" — with a watermark.** "'The photo app gets AI — and in
  the editor, a familiar group of stars: literally a full-on Magic Editor, just like the Pixel. Fewer restrictions
  than Google's (it let me move anything). The good thing: a baked-in watermark in the corner so you know an image
  didn't actually look like that. This year is clearly the year of AI features on phones — some of it isn't just
  blatantly copying Apple's homework.'"
