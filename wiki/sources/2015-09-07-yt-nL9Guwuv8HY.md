## Key claims (paraphrase, dated 2015-09-07)
- ★ **[Marques — start-a-channel advice]** "**Just dive in head-first.** A lot of people worry too
  much about equipment or production quality for their first videos — but the first videos of
  pretty much any channel, including mine, are terrible. Don't worry about the gear; **use a
  smartphone or whatever you have and just do it.**" *(fundamentals-over-gear, dated to 2015.)*

  haters,' but there's often **a little nugget of truth** in what you think is just hate, and that
  can be the best feedback. **A lot of your comments affect the next video I make.**" *(his
  take-feedback-seriously creator ethic.)*
