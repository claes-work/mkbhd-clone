# Redmi K20 Pro Review: Incredible Value! (@mkbhd, 2019-07-26)

## Summary
Review of the $400 Redmi K20 Pro — "picks up right where the Pocophone left off," but more complete. The reusable
lens: **a long spec list is not the same as quality** — "not all specs are created equal." Ends on his updated
budget-phone thesis: **"cheap phones are getting good."**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-07-26] **THE LENS — "not all specs are created equal."** "'The list of things this phone has is kind of
  amazing, but the **actual quality of all these different things varies.** It has almost everything — but it's
  still a $400 phone.'" A durable reviewing principle: a full spec sheet ≠ a good product (cf. the inverse,
  specs-underselling, in [[2019-05-31-yt-xjFPX7EoINs]]).
- [2019-07-26] **The great-and-awful split.** "**Great:** the display ('best in any mid-range phone' — flat, no
  notch, and 'I'm reminded how much I like this vs. displays that spill over the sides'), the back triple cameras
  (impressed, shoots 4K60 on all lenses incl. ultrawide unlike the OnePlus 7 Pro), and the **battery** (par with
  flagships, fast charger in box). **Awful:** the **selfie camera** ('possibly the worst I can remember using'),
  a cheap loose vibration motor, an easily-blocked mono speaker, and a slow in-glass fingerprint reader."
- [2019-07-26] **The tells that remind you it's cheap.** "'A lot of this is easy to point out on paper, but when
  you **actually use it** there are certain things that keep reminding you it's a cheaper phone' — the vibration
  motor, the speaker, the fingerprint delay 'you might not notice unless you've used a more expensive phone (I
  did).'" His use-it-daily-to-find-the-cracks method.
- [2019-07-26] **THE UPDATED THESIS — "cheap phones are getting good."** "'Like the Pocophone, even if you never
  buy this, it's a **testament to how good mid-range/budget phones have gotten.** Maybe I can't say "good phones
  are getting cheap" anymore — **but I can definitely say cheap phones are getting good.**'" A sharpened,
  quotable reframing of his running budget-phone narrative.
- [2019-07-26] **The naming-confusion bit (voice).** "An extended, self-deprecating riff untangling K20 vs K20
  Pro vs Xiaomi Mi 9 vs Mi 9T — 'if you follow all of that.' A characteristic patient-explainer humor beat."

## Notable verbatim quotes
