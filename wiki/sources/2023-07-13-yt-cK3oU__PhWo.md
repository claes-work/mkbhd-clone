# The Electric Mustang Got 2 Huge Updates! (Mach-E)

## Summary

Marques revisits the **Ford Mustang Mach-E** — one of the most popular, well-liked EVs — to explain
*why* it's succeeded and to cover two updates: **NACS adoption (2025)** and a **software update to
Blue Cruise 1.3.** His central insight: **the best-selling EVs "have to be pretty boring" — people
are most comfortable when a new car acts like their old car**, and Ford nailed that familiarity plus
the fundamentals (a frunk, good range, solid build). Fully Marques-narrated (Flow sponsor).

## Key claims

- **[2023-07-13]** Why it works: the crossover shape "America loves," and it **nails the EV
  fundamentals** for a first-EV buyer (70 kWh, 250–300 mi, a frunk, good storage, Ford build quality).
  (He refuses to relitigate the "Mustang" name.) (paraphrase)
- **[2023-07-13]** Update 1 (hardware): CCS today (~115–120 kW, "not fast in a 250–350 kW world"); **all
  four US EVs adopt Tesla's NACS port in 2025** → Supercharger access + faster charging (current cars
  get an adapter). (paraphrase)
- **[2023-07-13]** Update 2 (software): **Blue Cruise 1.3** (he's "technically beta-testing" it) — a
  Level-2 highway assist, "much closer to the safe end than Tesla's point-A-to-B ambition." The clever
  new feature: **using fleet data, they found people most often disengage when passing/being passed by
  a truck, so the car now nudges within the lane away from a large vehicle** — "some of the smoothest
  highway driving from any system I've ever tried." (quote)
- **[2023-07-13]** ⭐ THE INSIGHT: **"It feels like a lot of what has to be true about the best-selling
  EVs is they have to be pretty boring — and I don't mean that as an insult. People are most comfortable
  when a new car is familiar to an old car. That's a lot of what Ford's done here."** The Mach-E is "one
  of the few rare frunks in a traditional manufacturer's EV." (quote)
- **[2023-07-13]** ⭐ VALUES: "This is one of the few cars that actually gets OTA software updates…
  literally any car that gets meaningful software updates to make it better, I'm a fan of that." (quote)

## Notable verbatim quotes
