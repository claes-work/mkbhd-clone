# The Budget Phone Blueprint! (@mkbhd, 2020-05-15)

## Summary
Built around the Poco X2, a framework video: **"the $17 budget-phone game"** — you get a fixed budget to make one
thing excellent ($5), some average ($3), some poor ($1), across screen/specs/battery/design/camera/software/
marketing. A durable way to reason about budget-phone trade-offs and why the iPhone SE disrupts.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-05-15] **THE FRAMEWORK — the $17 budget-phone game.** "'You have **$17 to build a phone to sell**: $5 =
  excellent, $3 = average, $1 = poor, across **screen, specs, battery, design, camera, software, marketing.** You
  **can't make everything great** — what do you choose? A great screen + good specs already eats $14, forcing
  poor software/marketing/camera.'" A promotable, reusable trade-off model.
- [2020-05-15] **The insight — most people care about software + camera; the marketing wildcard.** "'A poll would
  say **software and camera matter most**, so you have to protect those. Most people spend **$1 on marketing** ("who
  cares") — but that's answering "what would you *buy*," not "what would you *sell the most units* with"; **cutting
  marketing means giving up carrier deals and reach.**'" A seller-vs-buyer reframe.
- [2020-05-15] **Why the iPhone SE disrupts — it "goes over 17 points."** "'The **iPhone SE breaks the game** —
  Apple doesn't pay extra for the software but it's **hugely valuable**, so the SE seems to **go over the allotted
  17 points. When it seems like a phone goes over the budget, that's a good indicator you're getting a good
  deal.**'" A neat way to spot value (cf. the SE's software-support-longevity, [[2020-04-17-yt-UK8HH3_q4yI]]).
- [2020-05-15] **The Poco X2 scored through the game.** "'A **great screen** (120 Hz 1080p — the $4-5 spend), **~$3
  specs** (SD730G), **~$4 battery** (4,500 mAh + 27 W charger), **~$3 hardware/design** (a rebadged Redmi K30;
  "designed by Poco" is weird — it's designed by Xiaomi; fingerprint on the power button — good), **~$3 camera**
  (a surprisingly good main sensor for the price; the recurring **2 MP macro nobody asked for**), and **poor
  software** (MIUI 11).'"
- [2020-05-15] **The community responses.** "'Rene Ritchie built a Pixel 4a (big on software+camera, whoops the
  battery); others prioritized battery, or software ("but then trash specs — how usable is it in two years?"); the
  **Dave2D method: spend everything on marketing and don't ship a phone**; SuperSaf just asked for more money.'" A
  fun crowdsourced illustration.

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "You have 17 [dollars] to build your own smartphone — not to buy, but to sell... for five bucks you make
> something excellent, for three average, for one poor. What feature do you choose [to make special]?"
> "When it seems like you're going over the allotted 17 points, that's a good indicator that you're actually
> getting a good deal."
