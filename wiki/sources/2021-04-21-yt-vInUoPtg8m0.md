# Colored iMacs? Let's Talk About Apple's 4/20 Event! (@mkbhd, 2021-04-21)

## Summary
Reactions to Apple's "Spring Loaded" event (AirTags, the M1 iMacs, the M1 iPad Pros). The durable takes: **AirTags
"just make a lot of sense"** thanks to the Find My network (praise for the user-replaceable battery); the **M1
iMac is "ugly" — only pretty from the back** (the white bezels + chin), with a prediction the higher-end 27" will
fix it; and the **M1 iPad Pro is overkill** ("even if it's nuclear-powerful, it's still an iPad"). Also his running
note: **he watches each keynote three times** (products, specs, then the cinematography).

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-04-21] **"I watch the keynote three times" — the cinematography note.** "'These keynotes are so polished
  I **watch them three times: once for the products, once for the specs, and once for the cinematography** ("how
  is their audio so good on a boat in the middle of the bay?").'" A recurring production-craft appreciation.
- [2021-04-21] **AirTags "just make sense" — plus the user-replaceable-battery praise.** "'A quarter-sized puck
  on Find My; the U1 chip guides you the last few feet. **$29 / $99 for four — about the cheapest Tile price, but
  far better integrated if you have an iPhone.** Best of all: **a user-replaceable CR2032 battery — I could easily
  have seen Apple going the opposite route ("we made a shiny metal one, you can't open it"). They didn't. Nice.**'"
  (What would you tag with four? keys, backpack, a pet collar.)'"
- [2021-04-21] **THE M1 iMac — "it's ugly / only pretty from the back."** "'Technically impressive (11.5 mm thin,
  10 lb) — but **I don't like the way it looks: white bezels "for some reason" and a solid chin.** It's **only
  pretty from the back — you get it out of the box, put it on your desk, and never see the back again; I have no
  idea how thick my monitor is and don't care.** They should've put the computer behind the display, made it a
  bit thicker, nixed the chin, and gone back to black bezels. **I'm pretty sure that's exactly what the higher-end
  27" M1X will do** — this only replaces the outdated baseline 21.5".'"
- [2021-04-21] **THE M1 iPad Pro — overkill; "even nuclear-powerful, it's still an iPad."** "'The M1 in an iPad
  "seems like huge overkill" — the A12Z was already incredible. USB-C becomes **Thunderbolt**, up to 2 TB, 5G, and
  the 12.9" gets **mini-LED** (we were secretly hoping for OLED; this is probably just as good). But **even if it's
  nuclear-powerful, it's still an iPad — amazing at what iPads do, and just as limited.** "There's nothing I'd
  like more than to try Final Cut on a 3K touchscreen with an M1 — but I can't, because iPad."'"
- [2021-04-21] **The other bits — the Apple TV 4K + remote, and the purple iPhone 12.** "'Also a new Apple TV 4K
  + a new remote (nice), and a **new purple iPhone 12** — mid-cycle refresh stuff I'll probably make a video on.'"

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "This iMac is only really pretty from the back... you put it on your desk, and then you never see the back

> "Even if this thing is nuclear-powerful, it's still an iPad — so it's going to be amazing at all the things that
