# Volvo EX90 - Rolls Royce Luxury, Model X Price!

## Summary

Marques argues the three-row **Volvo EX90** is his new answer to "how do you get as close to
101% luxury as possible without spending $300–400k": **"98% of the luxury of a Rolls-Royce for a
quarter of the cost."** He grounds it in four luxury pillars — sound isolation, top-5 speakers,
material/seat comfort, floaty suspension — each ~99% of the Rolls-Royce Spectre he drove for a
week. Biography-dense: he reviewed the **Rolls-Royce Spectre** for a week and the **XC40 Recharge
"a long time ago for Top Gear."** Fully Marques-narrated.

## Key claims (biography marked ⬥)

- **[2025-03-31]** Thesis: Bentleys/Rolls-Royces aren't a good *value*, but they're where you go for
  "the 101% luxury" at ~$300–400k. **"How do you get as close to that as possible without spending
  that kind of money? This is my actual new answer — the Volvo EX90… 98% of the luxury of that car,
  a quarter of the cost."** (quote)
- **[2025-03-31]** ⬥ BIOGRAPHY: "I did review and drive for a week the **Rolls-Royce Spectre**, which
  by all accounts is peak Rolls-Royce — the quietest, smoothest, most soft and plush." (Caveat: he's
  never driven a Rolls-Royce Cullinan, closer to the EX90's size.) (quote)
- **[2025-03-31]** ⬥ BIOGRAPHY: "A long time ago **for Top Gear** I reviewed the **XC40 Recharge**,
  the previous generation of Volvo's electric crossover." (Confirms his Top Gear reviewing history.)
  (quote)
- **[2025-03-31]** Basics: three-row SUV, starts ~$80k (this optioned one is in the $90s), twin motor,
  ~100kWh battery, ~300mi range. Volvo just refreshed the whole lineup (EX30 + EX90). (paraphrase)
- **[2025-03-31]** The **four luxury pillars**: (1) **sound isolation** — "incredibly quiet… the Volvo
  reps said it's about as quiet as a Rolls-Royce Spectre inside; I thought 'yeah right,' but they did
  a 99% job"; (2) **speakers** — Bowers & Wilkins, "top-five speaker system I've ever heard in a car…
  you get to your destination, throw it in park, and sit for 2–3 minutes to finish the song"; (3)
  **materials/seats** — "99% of the Rolls-Royce seat, super soft, heated, ventilated" (edge still to
  Rolls on plushness); (4) **suspension** — "super floaty… completely disassociated from the road…
  very similar to the Spectre." (quote + paraphrase)
- **[2025-03-31]** Praise: **best CarPlay implementation he's seen** ("pull up Waze here, it puts
  directions up in the cluster and lets me keep doing other things"); software better than a
  Rolls-Royce (wireless CarPlay + Google built-in). (quote)
- **[2025-03-31]** ⚠️ Nitpicks: HVAC on the screen instead of physical buttons; **single set of front
  window switches** (press a button to control the rears) — "that was inspired by Tesla and they
  didn't have to do that"; **one-pedal calibration** softer/less direct than Tesla or Rivian. (quote)
- **[2025-03-31]** Recurring hot take: **"I don't think I've ever experienced a good massage from a
  car seat."** (this one has 20-min massage patterns; he never uses them). (quote)
- **[2025-03-31]** ⬥ Self-reference: **6'3"** — fits behind his own 6'3" driving position in the second
  row; third row is two seats "similar to the Rivian R1S," fine for kids, not adults. (paraphrase)
