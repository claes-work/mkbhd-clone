## Key claims (Marques/David reviewer dialogue — Marques-consistent)
- [2024-04-25] **Sharp Aquos Crystal → A tier: "the first thin-bezel phone… the first one
  that got me and a lot of my audience really thinking about smaller bezels."** Credited as
  starting the thin-bezel era (parallel to how the Nokia 1020 tried, but failed, to start
  the big-camera-sensor era before social media made cameras matter). (tech-reviews)
- [2024-04-25] **Essential Phone (PH-1) → S tier: "extremely iconic… ceramic feels amazing
  … basically no phone besides the Aquos had this high a screen-to-body ratio at the
  time."** Praised the team that kept updating it monthly even after the company folded;
  "always wished we'd get a second one." (a documented Marques favorite) (tech-reviews)
- [2024-04-25] **Microsoft Surface Duo / Duo 2 → D tier: "the worst product I've ever
  reviewed so far"** (echoing his Humane-AI-Pin verdict) — "fundamentally flawed": bad
  digitizers, buggy software, an aspect ratio "that literally hurts to use one-handed."
  (tech-reviews)
- [2024-04-25] **RED Hydrogen One → D tier** — a cautionary tale: the promised modular
  camera accessories never came, the cameras were bad, and the gimmicky 3D-holographic
  screen was the point. His reusable lesson: **"any time you release a product with a
  distracting gimmick being its entire point, it's just going to be the [gimmick] of the
  device"** — and "this never got an ecosystem to support how cool it could be." (tech-
  industry-commentary)
- [2024-04-25] **Razer Phone → B tier** — "I'm obviously a sucker for high refresh rate,"
  but the vibration motor was "the worst I've ever [felt], it sounded broken." Blackberry
  Priv → C (over-indexed on the keyboard; bad performance). Royole FlexPai (first folding
  phone) → D ("not a single person should ever spend money on this"). (tech-reviews)
- [2024-04-25] **BIO / creator:** the video/**TikTok he made about the LG Wing "for a
  couple months was the most viewed piece of content I have ever made" — the TikTok hit
  35 million views.** (biography, creator-business)
