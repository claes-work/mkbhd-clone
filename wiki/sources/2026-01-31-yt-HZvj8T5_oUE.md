## Summary
The definitive behind-the-scenes portrait of the MKBHD studio as a company: four channels
(MKBHD, Waveform, AutoFocus, The Studio), a ~3,000 sq-ft space, ~18 people, the annual
"playoffs" (Q3/Q4 product season). Rich biography for the subject and dense entity context
for the team. Marques appears throughout as owner/creator/athlete; the documentary doubles
as the origin-and-culture record of the operation.

## Key claims (Marques-attributed only)
- [2026-01-31] Marques frames the operation: **"You probably know me as the smartphone
  review guy, but this is so much more… four channels with different audiences, a 3,000
  sq-ft studio space and 18 people."** He's "not just the tech review guy, but also the
  co-worker and the friend and boss sometimes and athlete sometimes." (biography,
  creator-business)
- [2026-01-31] On management: **"I'm not the best manager of people, but it's something
  that comes with having such a team."** The "weight of the publish button is heavier than
  ever" — taking the team's livelihoods seriously. (biography)
- [2026-01-31] On staying in his lane re: politics/tariffs (Switch 2 pre-order delay
  segment): **"At the end of the day, I am best at analyzing the product in front of me.
  So that's kind of what I try to do as best I can"** — declining to fold policy/politics
  into reviews. (beliefs — consistent with his documented "product-first" stance)
- [2026-01-31] On tech's purpose (Zipline drones): **"I hope that technology continues to
  actually improve people's lives… I hope we're able to actually cover that and explain
  that in ways that matter. And I don't think there is enough of that right now."**
  (beliefs)
- [2026-01-31] **BIO — athletics:** Marques competed for **Team USA in Ultimate Frisbee at
  the World Games in Chengdu, China, and won gold** (had to miss two podcast episodes and
  a live show for it). His competitiveness "manifests in… how good I want the videos to be
  and how badly I want us to be getting better." (biography)
- [2026-01-31] **BIO — early jobs:** his first job was a **caddy at Essex County Country
  Club for two summers**, then he **volunteered at the Liberty Science Center** for two
  more; "I wasn't very talkative… so I kind of was just walking around reading everything
  and learning way too much about science." (biography)
- [2026-01-31] **BIO — party trick / New Jersey roots:** he can **name every county in New
  Jersey in alphabetical order** (Atlantic, Bergen, Burlington, Camden, Cape May,
  Cumberland, Essex, Gloucester, Hudson, Hunterdon, Mercer, Middlesex, Monmouth, Morris,
  Ocean, Passaic, Salem, Somerset, Sussex, Union, Warren) — "as long as you don't ask me
  why I know this." (biography, voice — dry humor)
