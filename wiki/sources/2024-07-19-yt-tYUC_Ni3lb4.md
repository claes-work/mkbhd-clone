# Samsung Galaxy Ring Review: I Wanted to Love It! (@mkbhd, 2024-07-19)

## Summary
A week with the **$399 Galaxy Ring**, ending in a **smart-ring-vs-smartwatch hot take**. The appeal is real: a smart
ring gets impressively close to a normal ring while packing a small battery + basic sensors into **~2.6 mm**, with a
**7-day battery** you put on and forget — a **24/7, ultra-minimal wearable that never buzzes or notifies** ("a sick
idea"). He learns the best practices (align the sensors to the palm side; **wear it on the index finger, not the ring
finger**) and surfaces a genuinely useful nugget: **the back of the wrist is a horrible place to measure vitals; the
finger is far better** — "a weird secret nobody talks about with smartwatches." But the verdict is personal: **the
ring's numbers were off vs. his Apple Watch Ultra** (over-counting activity, phantom heart-rate readings until 3am,
missed sleep windows), sizing is one-shot (his first was too loose → gaps), and — the deciding factor — **his most
important activities (Ultimate Frisbee, golf, weight training) all require bare hands, so he takes the ring off,
defeating the point of a 24/7 tracker.** The framing that emerges: **wearability vs. functionality** — a smartwatch is
more functional but less wearable; a ring is the reverse. It's not meant to replace a watch, just a different offering
in the ecosystem. The catch vs. Oura: **no subscription (sick)**, but **Samsung-phone-only**, so a Pixel user would
just grab an Oura or Ultrahuman. Plus a real gripe: **it's very hard to find if you lose it** (weak Find My; he
actually lost the smaller loaner). "I like the idea of it, but it's just not for me." (8 Sleep sponsor.)

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2024-07-19] **The 24/7, forget-about-it appeal is real.** "'The whole advantage is you wear it 24/7 — living,
  sleeping, showering, swimming — with a 7-day battery you put on and forget. It's this ultra-minimal wearable that
  never vibrates or notifies, with a stripped-down feature set. That's a sick idea.'"
- [2024-07-19] **The finger beats the wrist for vitals.** "'You wear it on the index finger (not the ring finger),
  sensors to the palm side. And it turns out the back of the wrist is a horrible place to measure heart rate and
  vitals — the finger is much better. That's one of those weird secrets nobody talks about with smartwatches.'"
- [2024-07-19] **The numbers were off — I trust the watch.** "'Wearing my Apple Watch Ultra at the same time, the ring
  was way off — over-counting activity, and it showed phantom heart-rate readings until 3am when I definitely wasn't
  wearing it. After my 1,000-steps test where every watch was within a few percent, I lean toward trusting the watch
  over the ring numbers.'"
- [2024-07-19] **I take the ring off for my most important workouts.** "'It's just not for me. My most important
  activities — Ultimate Frisbee (throwing/catching), golf, and weight training (grip) — all require bare hands, so I
  take the ring off, which defeats the purpose of a tracker meant to know everything about my fitness. It comes down to
  wearability vs functionality: a smartwatch is more functional but less wearable; a ring is the reverse.'"
- [2024-07-19] **No subscription (good), but Samsung-phone-only; and hard to find if lost.** "'The edge over Oura is no
  subscription, which is pretty sick. But it only pairs with a Samsung Galaxy phone — on a Pixel I'd just grab an Oura
  or Ultrahuman with the same feature set. And it's really hard to find if you lose it (weak Find My) — I actually lost
  the smaller loaner ring.'"
