# Nothing Ear(1) Review: Transparent Buds! (@mkbhd, 2021-07-29)

## Summary
Review of the $99 Nothing Ear(1) — the first product from Carl Pei's Nothing (the OnePlus co-founder, now "a
hype-generating mastermind"). The durable ideas: he **deliberately ignored the hype** ("expectations are the
thief of joy"); the **transparent design (with Teenage Engineering) is the best part — and a bigger deal than it
looks, because transparency forces internal neatness** you can normally be sloppy about; and the promotable
observation that **most people at this price buy earbuds for features and looks, not sound quality.** Verdict:
solid $99 buds — "much ado about Nothing."

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-07-29] **Ignore the hype — "expectations are the thief of joy."** "'I chose to **ignore all the hype and
  slow-burn headlines** — **expectations are the thief of joy; why get psyched out when it's only the product that
  matters?** (The original teaser looked totally transparent — these look nothing like that — another reason not
  to over-hype.)'"
- [2021-07-29] **THE TRANSPARENT DESIGN — the best part, and it forces neatness.** "'Easily the best part is the
  transparent design (Nothing + Teenage Engineering). **It's a bigger deal than people realize: the places inside
  earbuds where you can normally be sloppy — battery/PCB placement, the microphones, especially the glue — you
  now have to spend time making neat, because people will see it.** Cut open any AirPods/Galaxy Buds and the glue
  is ugly; here everything is trim. Worth it — they look dope.'" A promotable design-philosophy insight.
- [2021-07-29] **The sound — "pretty good, not amazing" (context matters).** "'They sound like an average $100
  pair — a V-shaped response (tinny highs, big-but-not-punchy bass, light mids). The ANC on max is ~half to
  two-thirds as strong as AirPods Pro — but **those cost 3x as much; that's context, not a criticism.** Best with
  low-frequency white noise. ~4 hrs ANC-on.'"
- [2021-07-29] **THE OBSERVATION — most people buy buds for features and looks, not sound.** "'Companies have
  realized this over the last decade: **most people at this price aren't buying earbuds for the sound quality —
  they buy them for the set of features and how they look, and if they happen to sound good, that's a bonus.**
  These have a good feature set and look great, so they'll do well.'" A durable consumer-behavior read.
- [2021-07-29] **The grades + the verdict.** "'Looks: A/A+. Sound: B. Features: B+ (wanted a real EQ + an
  Assistant long-hold). Had some pre-release connection quirks (one bud connecting, not the other — hopefully
  fixed by launch). **Assuming you didn't fall for the hype thinking these beat $300 flagships — they don't — this
  is a nice place to jump into the market. As for the rest of the hype: much ado about Nothing.**'"
