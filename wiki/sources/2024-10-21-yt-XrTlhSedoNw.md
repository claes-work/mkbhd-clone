# Lucid Air Pure: The Real Tesla Fighter!

## Summary

Marques reviews the **base Lucid Air Pure** (rear-wheel drive, ~$70k) as — finally — an **apples-to-
apples fight with the base Tesla Model S** (both ~$70k, ~400mi range), instead of the Air Sapphire's
3×-a-Plaid mismatch. His verdict is a close, honest split-decision: the Lucid **drives, handles, and
rides better** (and now has CarPlay), but he'd **still take the Tesla "by a hair"** for road-trip
peace of mind and the just-works phone-key experience. Fully Marques-narrated. Contains biography.

## Key claims (biography marked ⬥)

- **[2024-10-21]** Context: **"When I reviewed the Lucid Air I ended up thinking this is it — the peak
  EV, better than the Model S: better engineered, better driving, more range."** But the asterisk was
  always price. The Air Pure (~$70k base) is the first true apples-to-apples vs. the base Model S
  (~$70k, AWD). (quote)
- **[2024-10-21]** Advantage Lucid: **drives/handles/rides better** ("still on steel springs + adaptive
  dampers, not even air suspension, and it handles New Jersey's poor roads better than every Model S"),
  more physical controls, adjustable regen, rear doors open a full 90°, and **"Lucid caved and now
  offers wireless CarPlay — Teslas never have and probably never will."** (quote)
- **[2024-10-21]** Advantage Tesla: quicker (3.1s vs 4.3s 0–60), bigger true-hatchback trunk, two
  wireless chargers, heated steering wheel + ventilated seats, and — the big ones — **the Supercharger
  network + FSD** for road trips, plus "it just works with my phone key, don't have to think too hard."
  (paraphrase)
- **[2024-10-21]** ⬥ BIOGRAPHY: references **buying his own Model S** ("I think you get the heated
  steering wheel with all Model S's… at least when I bought my car") — corroborates his Model S
  ownership. (quote)
- **[2024-10-21]** ⭐ Verdict: **"If you put a threat to me and said I can only buy one — the factors
  that matter are: how much am I going to road-trip (a lot → Tesla, the peace of mind is worth it), and
  do I care about CarPlay (me personally, not really). So I think I'd go with the Tesla by a hair."**
  But huge credit to Lucid for **finally shipping a not-crazy-expensive car** and "walking people up
  the price ladder." (quote)
