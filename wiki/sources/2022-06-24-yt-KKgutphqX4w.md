# The M2 MacBook Pro and Nothing Phone are WEIRD (Waveform, 2022-06-24)

> ⚠️ **Ensemble podcast.** Hosts: Marques Brownlee, Andrew Manganelli, David Imel.
> No speaker labels; only **clearly-Marques** takes train the persona.

## Summary
Why the **13-inch M2 MacBook Pro** is a strange product — and the odd positioning of the
first **Nothing Phone**.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated 2022-06-24)
- ★ **[Marques, clearly — buying advice]** **"Who would buy the 13-inch M2 MacBook Pro? An
  incredibly slim sliver of a target demographic."** The "cheapest Pro so you don't break the
  bank" logic doesn't hold, because the **M2 MacBook Air is cheaper *and* has the whole new
  redesign** (notch, smaller bezels, flat design) — it's the better laptop. The only thing the
  13" M2 Pro adds is **active cooling** (for sustained loads) — so it's only for a narrow
  who-actually-needs-that buyer. *(his recommend-by-who-is-this-actually-for lens.)*
- **[Marques, clearly — Nothing Phone]** The first **Nothing Phone** differentiates on
  **design (the transparent back + Glyph lights)**, not specs (a mid-tier Snapdragon 778G) —
  "definitely not the Carl Pei playbook of best-specs-for-the-money enthusiast phones." *(reads
  the strategy, not just the spec sheet.)*
