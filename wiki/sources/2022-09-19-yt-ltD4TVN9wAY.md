# World's First Intel ARC PC Build! (A770) (@mkbhd, 2022-09-19)

## Summary
A build video — **his first PC built from scratch in ~9 years** (the last, in 2013, was a Hackintosh — "we don't
talk about that"). The hook: it's built around the **Intel Arc A770** discrete GPU (Intel jumping into the
Nvidia-vs-AMD discrete-GPU war; performs around an RTX 3060/3060 Ti), and he builds it **using only YouTuber merch**
(the MKBHD desk mat, the JerryRigEverything knife, and the sponsor — the **Linus Tech Tips screwdriver**). A largely
biographical/light video, but a few durable beats: **awe at how far PC parts have come** (a 4 TB M.2 SSD "in the
palm of your hand" he wouldn't have believed in 2013), a clean matte-black aesthetic ("matte black speaks to me"),
and troubleshooting a too-big 360 mm radiator by improvising (mounting the fans/tubes non-ideally to fit). It boots,
plays Forza at 1440p high in the upper-80s–90s fps — the Arc card "can definitely handle some pretty solid gaming."

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-09-19] **First PC in 9 years — the 2013 Hackintosh.** "'My first PC built from scratch since 2013 — which
  was actually a **Hackintosh (we don't talk about that; we literally couldn't turn it off or it might not turn
  back on).** This one's a proper high-end daily-driver.' Parts: a Corsair 4000X case, an Asus ProArt Z690 board,
  an i9-12900K, an AIO liquid cooler, 64 GB DDR5, multiple fast M.2 SSDs (two 4 TB + two 2 TB), a 1,200 W PSU.'"
- [2022-09-19] **The Intel Arc A770 — Intel enters the discrete-GPU war.** "'The different piece: an **Intel Arc
  A770** — after years of the AMD-vs-Nvidia discrete-GPU wars (and Intel being the good integrated option), **Intel
  is now jumping into discrete GPUs.** It's matte black, simple-looking, and **performs somewhere around an RTX
  3060 / 3060 Ti.**'"
- [2022-09-19] **Built with YouTuber merch — the LTT screwdriver.** "'I decided to build it using only YouTuber
  merch — the MKBHD desk mat, the JerryRigEverything knife (the sharpest I own), and the sponsor, the **Linus Tech
  Tips screwdriver** (3+ years in the making; a magnetic shaft, 12 stored bits — really satisfying).'"
- [2022-09-19] **Awe at the parts — and improvising the fit.** "'If you told 2013-me you could get a **4 TB drive in
  the palm of your hand, I wouldn't have believed you.** The M.2 SSDs keep the build sparse and clean — lots of
  open airflow. **The 360 mm radiator doesn't officially fit this case (only 240) — so I got creative** (replacing
  the three front fans, putting the tubes at the top — not ideal, but making do). **This is what PC building is all
  about.**'"
- [2022-09-19] **It works — and it games.** "'It's alive — a clean red-and-black aesthetic (**"matte black speaks to
  me"**). **Forza at 1440p on high runs in the upper 80s–low 90s fps** — the Arc card can definitely handle some
  pretty solid gaming. My first PC build in a long time, and I'm glad I did it.'"
