# 16" MacBook Pro First Impressions! (@mkbhd, 2019-11-13)

## Summary
First impressions of the 16" MacBook Pro, which **replaces** the 15". The headline fixes: the butterfly keyboard
is gone (back to a scissor "Magic Keyboard"), a bigger display, and a maxed-out battery. A **biography** note — it
becomes his new favorite/most-likely-to-carry editing laptop.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-11-13] **The keyboard fix — "going back in time is an upgrade."** "'The butterfly mechanism didn't do so
  hot. They're **going back to a scissor mechanism** (the new **Magic Keyboard**) — more reliable, clicky, a bit
  more travel — **it's almost as if going back in time is an upgrade here.** Plus a **dedicated physical Escape
  key** (the Touch Bar one "froze so much it was almost useless") and a separate Touch ID button.'" A pointed
  vindication of the walk-back.
- [2019-11-13] **The display + the maxed battery.** "'Same footprint, but the **16" display** pushes the bezels
  out for more real estate — 'I love that.' And **~11 hrs battery** from a new **100 Wh cell — the largest lithium-
  ion battery you're allowed to fly with per TSA** — they maxed it out. **I like this new Apple trend of doing
  better with battery in 2019.**'"
- [2019-11-13] **Thermals + the best laptop speakers.** "'Addresses last year's **i9 thermal throttling** (new
  fans/layout, +28% airflow). And a **new 6-speaker force-cancelling system** — MacBook Pro speakers have been
  top-of-the-line for a while; **best speakers in a laptop right now.** A "studio-quality" mic that's "pretty
  good" — great for video calls, not quite podcast-grade.'"
- [2019-11-13] **BIOGRAPHY — his new editing laptop.** "'This is my **new favorite MacBook Pro, mostly for the
  design and display — the laptop I'm most likely to carry to edit with.**'" A datable personal-gear signal (note:
  this is the same fall he said the Surface Laptop 3 might be his next laptop — [[2019-10-02-yt-aeaBaTqmu8Q]] — a
  contrast worth watching).
- [2019-11-13] **The wishlist — a 14" in the 13" footprint.** "'What I'd love now is a **14" MacBook Pro in the
  current 13"'s footprint** — new specs, slimmer bezels, modernized. That would be my favorite MacBook Pro they
  could make.'" A specific, prescient product wish. (Also notes the **Mac Pro is officially coming in December.**)
