# Android 15 Hands-On: Top 5 Features! (@mkbhd, 2024-05-28)

## Summary
His long-running "top 5 features of the new Android" on the Pixel 8 Pro (Android 15 / "Vanilla Ice Cream" beta), with a
meta-observation: **Android has stabilized** — gone are the huge overhauls and giant visual changes; these are now
**small, nuanced tweaks** ("back in the day this would've been a .0.2 update"), but they're **more well-considered,
more about usefulness** and making owning the phone a little better. The five: (5) **Private Space** — a second,
separately-locked section at the bottom of the app drawer for secret apps/files; (4) **the little things** (rich live
widget previews, a smoother back animation, Bluetooth audio sharing to multiple headphones, high-quality webcam mode,
Bluetooth auto-on); (3) the **new full-screen volume sliders**; (2) **partial screen recordings** — record a *single
app* so background notifications/security codes never leak into your tutorial ("my new default"); and (1) **better
notifications** (Android's long-standing edge) — **Adaptive Vibration** (uses the mic/sensors to set buzz strength for
the surface it's on), a Notification Cooldown that tapers repeat pings (sadly cut in Dev Preview 2), and per-app
vibration patterns (a nod to the old multicolor LED notification lights). His closing note ties to the era's theme:
**the stuff he's most excited about — the big AI features — isn't built into Android anymore; it's the AI the phone is
capable of, sitting outside the OS.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2024-05-28] **Android has stabilized — small but well-considered.** "'There used to be huge new features and massive
  visual overhauls; now it's small, nuanced changes. Back in the day these would've been a .0.2 update. But they're a
  little more well-considered — more about usefulness and little tweaks to make owning your phone a bit better.'"
- [2024-05-28] **Partial screen recordings are the sleeper.** "'You can now record a single app instead of the whole
  screen — background stuff (a text coming in, copying a security code) never shows up. It's my new default for
  recordings; it just shows what I want you to see.'"
- [2024-05-28] **Notifications are still Android's edge.** "'Number one is what Android's been good at for years —
  notifications. Adaptive Vibration uses the mic and sensors to gauge the surface and set buzz strength; there's a
  Notification Cooldown to taper repeat pings from the same app (sadly gone in Dev Preview 2); and per-app vibration
  patterns — a callback to the old multicolor LED lights that told you which app pinged without waking the phone.'"
- [2024-05-28] **The interesting stuff is now AI, and it's not in the OS.** "'I'm still most excited for the bigger
  AI-based features, because that's the stuff expected to make a real difference in how we use our phones. It's just
  not built into Android anymore — it's the AI stuff your phone is capable of, not in the OS itself.'"
