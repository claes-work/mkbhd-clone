## Key claims (Marques-attributed only)
- [2024-04-12] **The Humane AI Pin verdict (the landmark "worst product" review).** The *only* person he'd recommend it to today: someone who **"only really, really wants to reduce their screen time at the expense of their own sanity."** You'll do many things *worse* — "your photos look worse, your videos are less than 60 seconds, less capabilities, a new phone number, another device to charge — all true, but you'll have less screen time. Mission accomplished." Otherwise: **"I just don't enjoy using it."** (This is the review he'd later call maybe the worst product he's reviewed, which sparked the "is it unethical to review a bad product too harshly?" debate — see [[2025-11-25-yt-P-rj56QjASc]].) Demo videos feel like early Google Home ("how tall is the Empire State Building?") — random facts for $800. **Compares to Vision Pro:** "so cool as a tech demo, then you put it on and go 'what do I do?'" — vs tech with obvious use cases (the iPod: "yes, I want to listen to music everywhere").
