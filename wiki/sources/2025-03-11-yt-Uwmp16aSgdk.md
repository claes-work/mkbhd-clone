# M4 Macbook Air Review: Too Easy! (@mkbhd, 2025-03-11)

## Summary
A short-and-sweet review — **"the Mac team is on one."** The M4 MacBook Air is the most capable Air yet with a **price
that dropped back to $999** (from the M2 generation's $1,199), a **16 GB base memory floor**, a slightly larger
battery, Thunderbolt improvements, a Center Stage webcam, and a new color — while **almost nothing else physically
changed** (same keyboard, MagSafe, two USB-C, the 60 Hz notched LCD, the thin aluminum build). The comedy beat: the new
**"Sky Blue" is "barely blue — the LaCroix of MacBook paint colors"** (silver against blue things). The real story is
the **M4 chip**: passively cooled (no fans, so a lower sustained ceiling than the Mac mini), but bursty everyday tasks
are "amazing," and — the recurring point — **"this machine is capable of things that required a Pro chip a couple years
ago"** (loading 4K/8K into a Final Cut timeline and scrubbing with almost no hesitation; exporting just a few beats
slower than a Mac mini) — which "doesn't matter to 99% of Air buyers, but it's good to know you could." His standing
"can you order the base spec and be fine?" question is **yes this year** (16 GB / 256 GB), and he reprises the era's
refrain: **"the single best feature of Apple Intelligence has been improving the amount of RAM in every Apple device."**
His one complaint: **he wants a better display** (Apple reserves Mini-LED / ProMotion / nano-texture for the Pro — "I
think the Air could handle it"). And the last-gen M-Air at **$799** is a great deal for base buyers. Verdict: "boring
that it's a barely-new color and a chip we've seen — but exciting that laptops have gotten this good."

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2025-03-11] **The price dropped back to $999, with a 16 GB base.** "'The M2 generation bumped the Air to $1,199 and
  everyone went "still fine." Now it's 2025, it's got the M4, and the price dropped back to $999 for the base — with 16
  GB base memory. That's sick.'"
- [2025-03-11] **Barely changed — "Sky Blue" is barely blue.** "'Almost nothing else physically changed — same
  keyboard, MagSafe, two USB-C, the 60 Hz notched LCD, the thin build. The main tell is a new color: Sky Blue — I'd
  call it barely blue, the LaCroix of MacBook paint colors; against actually-blue things it just looks silver again.'"
- [2025-03-11] **Capable of what needed a Pro chip a couple years ago.** "'The M4 is passively cooled (no fans, a lower
  sustained ceiling than the Mac mini), but bursty everyday stuff is amazing. This machine is capable of things that
  required a Pro chip a couple years ago — that's not an exaggeration: I loaded 4K and 8K into a Final Cut timeline and
  it scrubbed with almost no hesitation and exported just a few beats slower than a Mac mini. That doesn't matter to
  99% of Air buyers, but it's good to know you could.'"
- [2025-03-11] **The single best feature of Apple Intelligence is more RAM.** "'Doubling to 16 GB base for $999 is huge
  since these computers go into swap so quickly. Shout out to the single best feature of Apple Intelligence, which has
  been improving the amount of RAM in every Apple device.'"
- [2025-03-11] **Can you order the base and be fine? Yes — but I want a better display.** "'Can you order the base spec
  and still be fine? For most people, yes — 16 GB / 256 GB. My only complaint: I'd like a better display. Apple, as
  usual, reserves the Mini-LED, ProMotion, and nano-texture for the Pro, but I think the Air could handle a better
  screen. And the last-gen M-Air at $799 is a really good deal for base buyers. It's boring that this barely changed —
  but exciting that laptops have gotten this good.'"
