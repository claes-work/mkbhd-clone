# Galaxy S21 Review: Would You Notice? (@mkbhd, 2021-02-02)

## Summary
Full review of the $799 Galaxy S21 — built around the question **"would you notice?"** The durable idea: **every
non-top-end phone is a balancing act of checking the right boxes, and the smart sacrifices Samsung made to drop
the price $200 are ones almost no one will notice** (1440p→1080p, glass→plastic). The verdict: the S21 checks the
boxes that matter (performance, battery, camera) — **the best bang for the buck in the lineup** — even as the
enthusiast-missed features (microSD, MST) are gone.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-02-02] **Cheaper than last year = a conscious value decision.** "'We're so used to prices going up that
  when you see a **big jump down in price, it's because a conscious decision was made to offer more value.** Every
  non-flagship phone is **a balancing act — check the right set of boxes to appeal to the most buyers.** The
  customer is more educated than ever, so you have to get it right or they switch on you.'"
- [2021-02-02] **"If you're going to have a camera bump, own it."** "'The contour-cut design has grown on me —
  from "I don't hate it" to "one of the best." **If you're going to have a camera bump, own it** — bringing the
  metal straight to the rails is a good call. The camera plate is the only metal; the rest is plastic — "you'll
  barely notice." I'm more focused on the smaller footprint (6.2") than the weight.'"
- [2021-02-02] **THE SMART SACRIFICES — "would you notice?"** "'They dropped 1440p → 1080p — **most won't notice
  (1080p at 6.2" is 400+ ppi), and last year you had to run 1080p anyway to get 120 Hz. Samsung ran the numbers:
  people take 120 Hz over 1440p every time.** No S Pen, adaptive 48–120 (not 10–120) — small bets weighed against
  the savings.'"
- [2021-02-02] **They kept the flagship chip — "I appreciate that."** "'The one area they went fully high-end: the
  **same Snapdragon 888.** They could have dropped to a 765G at this price and saved more without most people
  noticing — **but they didn't; it's still the fastest chip in a phone. I appreciate that.**' (8 GB vs 12/16 — I
  never noticed.)'"
- [2021-02-02] **HOT TAKE — Samsung's portrait mode is now the best in any phone.** "'Same essentially-S20 triple
  cameras (no laser AF, no 100x — a reasonable 30x max). But **my hot take: Samsung's portrait mode is now the
  best in any smartphone — over the iPhone, over the Pixel.** The s21's ultrawide is even a touch wider (0.5x vs
  the Ultra's 0.6x).'"
- [2021-02-02] **The sorely-missed features — microSD and MST — and the verdict.** "'**Expandable storage and MST
  (magnetic pay-anywhere) are gone from the whole lineup — the two I and a lot of enthusiasts miss most.** But
  the line dropped $200; that's the calculus. **The S21 checks the right boxes — no doubt the best bang for the
  buck in the lineup** — and it's only $50 to double to 256 GB (important now that storage isn't expandable).'"
