# 2023 Mercedes EQS SUV: Luxury, but Not for Me!

## Summary

Marques reviews the **Mercedes EQS SUV 580** and frames it around a **mature-reviewer principle**:
**"just because you don't like something doesn't mean it's bad — it might just not be for you."** The
car is superb at luxury and comfort (quiet, plush, a Burmester system he loves, huge back seat), but
as someone into sportiness/tech he finds it not for him — and he has one genuine complaint that *is*
bad: **the brake pedal physically moves under you while regen-ing.** Fully Marques-narrated.

## Key claims

- **[2022-12-30]** ⭐ THE PRINCIPLE: **"Something that takes maturity to learn is that just because you
  don't like something doesn't mean it's bad — it could just not be for you. Like 'Baby Shark' isn't my
  favorite song, but I won't call it bad — to a lot of toddlers it's amazing."** A ~$130k EQS SUV 580,
  ~530 hp, 108 kWh, ~260 mi. (quote)
- **[2022-12-30]** What it does superbly: **luxury and comfort** — "incredible acoustic isolation,
  massaging seats, a Burmester sound system reminiscent of the Maybach, the softest headrests you'll
  ever feel, a huge comfortable back seat, floaty-but-isolating suspension." Well-built, "a nice heavy
  thunk" doors. (paraphrase)
- **[2022-12-30]** ⚠️ Not-for-him + genuinely-bad: the **Hyperscreen is "so *capable* but not
  *intuitive*"** (a thousand features, but "look how deep you dig to change the exterior sound"; "it
  needs a dock"); HVAC-on-touchscreen; elbow-triggered hazards. The real complaint: **"the brake pedal
  MOVES while I'm driving — when I lift off and it regens, the pedal depresses away from me, so I have to
  hunt for it. Intelligent regen (camera-based) is the craziest. I asked Mercedes — it's intentional. I
  don't like it, I don't like it at all."** (quote)
- **[2022-12-30]** ⭐ The distinction: **"This is focused on luxury over anything else, and that's its
  best asset. It's solid in a straight line (the 580 keeps pulling), but that's *power*, not sportiness —
  I don't want to confuse having a lot of horsepower with a sporty drive."** Who it's for: "someone who
  appreciates the finer things and the little features (a light show on startup, the phone-left-on-
  charger reminder) — and has Mercedes money." (quote)
