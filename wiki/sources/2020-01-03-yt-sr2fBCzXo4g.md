# The Banned Huawei Mate 30 Pro: Best Phone You Shouldn't Buy! (@mkbhd, 2020-01-03)

## Summary
A review of the Huawei Mate 30 Pro — gorgeous hardware, but crippled by the US ban (no Google Play services). The
thesis, stated outright: **a re-emphasis on the importance of software.** "One of my favorite pieces of hardware
of the year, but it still can't make up for the software situation."

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-01-03] **THE THESIS — "a re-emphasis on the importance of software."** "'Moral of the story: the build's
  got to be good, the battery big, the display crisp — but **behind the scenes, the battery optimization, the
  display's responsiveness, the camera quality you get out of a sensor — all these things are software. And that'll
  never be not important. Don't forget that.**'" A crisp, promotable statement of his software-is-decisive belief
  (cf. [[2019-11-19-yt-wegxoNTw0_I]]).
- [2020-01-03] **The biggest hardware-software delta he's ever seen.** "'Importing it here means **Android 10 +
  EMUI 10 but no Google Play services at all** — **no Play Store → no Instagram, no Gmail, no YouTube.** Huawei's
  App Gallery is region-locked and thin. This is the **biggest delta between awesome hardware and software holding
  it back that I've ever seen** — it's gone from a very good alternative to basically **unusable** in the US.'"
- [2020-01-03] **Loves the boxy waterfall display (a stated design taste).** "'One of my favorite designs of the
  year — **I've always been a fan of boxy designs**, and the **next-level waterfall display falls off so steeply it
  gives that boxy square look.** Futuristic. (But the extreme curve has quirks: edge light-falloff, the power
  button pushed nearly to the back, and no volume rocker — you tap/swipe the side of the screen, which fails often
  enough that you wish for real buttons.)'" Reinforces his boxy-design love.
- [2020-01-03] **The best technical ultrawide he'd seen.** "'The quad camera's **ultrawide is the best technical
  performance of any ultrawide I've seen** — the **sensor is so big** I switch to it and shoot with it all the
  time (natural bokeh without portrait mode). Not the best *quality* (weird colors), but technically very
  impressive.'" Continues his pro-ultrawide + big-sensor appreciation.
- [2020-01-03] **THE COMPETITION POINT — bad for us.** "'This phone will be fine in **China (the biggest smartphone
  market)**, but it's **not just bad for Huawei — it's bad for competition, bad for us.** I'm not going to recommend
  anyone import one.'" Ties back to his competition-is-good value ([[2019-05-22-yt-qZGpmWrVSaU]]).
