# The Truth About the Pocophone F1! (@mkbhd, 2018-09-10)

## Summary
Reviews the hyped $300 Pocophone F1 (SD845/6GB/4000mAh, by Xiaomi). Best material: his **budget-phone thesis** —
copying is *fine* at this price — and the "**ride the line**" framework (copy the noticeable premium stuff, cut
corners in the unnoticeable places). Verdict: "a lock for budget phone of the year."

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-09-10] **Why the hype — and the OnePlus lineage.** "SD845 + 6 GB + 4000 mAh, all for **$300** — that's why
  people care. It's exactly what **OnePlus did when they started** (cram high-end specs into a low price — the
  flagship killer); people call the Pocophone **'the flagship-killer killer.'**" Situates it in the value-flagship
  arc (OnePlus has since drifted up to ~$500).
- [2018-09-10] **The budget-phone thesis — "at this price, copy whatever you want."** "At the high end I (and a lot
  of people) ding the copying. But **at this price, copy whatever you want — bring as many expensive-seeming
  features down into this price point as you can. Now it's an advantage.**" A clear, dated principle.
- [2018-09-10] **"Ride the line" (the budget-phone craft).** "You **copy the noticeable premium stuff** (the notch —
  "people associate the notch with a premium look now" — the big display, tapered build, dual cameras) and **cut
  corners in the less-noticeable places** (thicker bezels/a chin, an LCD not OLED, a soft-but-okay camera, no NFC/
  no IP rating). The Pocophone strikes a really good balance of that." His model for how a good budget phone works.
- [2018-09-10] **MIUI surprise + still-prefers-stock.** "**The first time I could see myself using a Xiaomi phone
  every day** — MIUI 10 makes a lot of choices I like (clean theme, app drawer by default, thoughtful little
  features). I still personally prefer stock Android, but I'm excited to see this software on other Xiaomi phones."
  A dated softening on a skin.
- [2018-09-10] **The verdict + the red-and-black aside.** "My **favorite Xiaomi phone to date, probably a lock for
  budget phone of the year** — I was fully ready to not be impressed, and I like it way more than I thought.
  Worthy of the hype." (Aside: "**being red and black doesn't hurt at all for me.**") Nitpick: the annoying
  auto-watermark ("why would anyone want that?").

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "At the high end I've given phones flack for copying — but at this price, copy whatever you want. Now it's an
> advantage."

> a lot."
