# Pixel 6's Tensor Chip: Let's Talk! (@mkbhd, 2021-08-02)

## Summary
Commentary on Google's first custom silicon, **Tensor** (the "White Chapel" project), in the Pixel 6/6 Pro. The
durable thesis: **vertical integration is finally coming to Google** — and because the Pixel is all about
software, controlling the whole stack unlocks a lot: **computational photography applied to video**, better
speech/Assistant, an M1-style efficiency/battery bump, and — the strategic core — **much longer software updates
to keep you on the phone longer** (Google's version of Apple's keep-earning-from-the-device playbook). It could
also **cut out the Qualcomm middleman** and seed an **ecosystem-wide silicon** future.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-08-02] **Why Tensor matters most for the Pixel specifically.** "'Pixels have used off-the-shelf Snapdragons
  like every Android phone (with small custom bits — the Visual/Neural Core, the Titan M). **A whole phone powered
  by Google's own Tensor is a big deal — particularly for the Pixel, because of how much Google does with software
  and optimization.**' (Not new in the abstract — Apple/Huawei/Samsung design chips — but new for Google.)'"
- [2021-08-02] **Camera — computational photography finally applied to VIDEO.** "'Pixel photos have been great for
  years (computational photography), **but video has always been meh. Tensor lets Google apply those computational
  chops to video now** — HDR, noise reduction, stabilization. And since the Pixel 2, they've used **the same
  smallish sensor** (why move to a 50 MP sensor without the chops to back it up?) — **this year it's all-new big
  sensors + Tensor.**'"
- [2021-08-02] **The M1 parallel — efficiency/battery from vertical integration.** "'Remember when the Macs went
  Intel → M1 and battery life roughly doubled? That was optimization/efficiency from vertical integration. **I'm
  not promising that here (both Tensor and Qualcomm are Arm-based), but Google is now approaching the same level
  of vertical integration Apple has** — so a better battery experience, plus dedicating part of the chip solely to
  on-device speech (faster Assistant).'"
- [2021-08-02] **THE STRATEGIC CORE — much longer software updates to keep you on the phone.** "'Android updates
  are usually capped at ~3 years by Qualcomm's support window. **Now that Google controls the whole stack, they
  can optimize Android for as many years as possible — and they WANT to, to keep you on the phone.** Like Apple:
  iPhone sales slowed as people keep phones longer, so Apple leaned into services to keep earning from that
  device. **Google already makes its money from ad services (Search, Gmail, Assistant) — so longer updates keep
  you using Google longer.**'" A promotable vertical-integration-as-retention thesis.
- [2021-08-02] **Cutting out Qualcomm + an ecosystem-wide silicon future.** "'Tensor could **cut out the Qualcomm
  middleman** (Google no longer bargaining for chips whose supply Samsung's bigger orders can disrupt) — potentially
  lowering cost at scale. And this could be **the beginning of an ecosystem-wide silicon thing — a scaled-down
  Tensor in a Pixel Watch, a scaled-up one in a Chromebook** — the same way the M1 is a scaled-up A14 (and gives
  Macs the iPhone's image processing).'"

## Notable verbatim quotes
