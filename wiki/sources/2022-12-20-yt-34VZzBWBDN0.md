# Tesla Model S PLAID Owner's Review: Things I Love and Hate!

## Summary

**The canonical owner's review of his own daily driver** — the **Tesla Model S Plaid** he'd daily-
driven **1.5 years / 30,000 miles** (wrapped satin-black, with the ~$20k carbon-ceramic Track-Pack
brakes). His verdict: **"the best daily driver in the world for me"** — for American straight roads,
the acceleration, the space, and (crucially for his frisbee road trips) the Supercharger network. But
as an *owner* he's blunt about what he hates: the **capacitive yoke blinker buttons** ("the right one
is just bad — I'll merge without a blinker"), touchscreen-only fan speed, and Tesla's unkept promises.
Fully Marques-narrated.

## Key claims (biography marked ⬥)

- **[2022-12-20]** ⬥ BIOGRAPHY (canonical): "I'm finally reviewing the car I've been daily-driving for
  the past year and a half — the **Tesla Model S Plaid. I put 30,000 miles on this car.**" Wrapped satin
  black; triple-motor; **the $20k carbon-ceramic brake package** ("almost no other cars have it — pushed
  to next year, but I got it"); ~8–10% range degradation (390 → 365 mi). Uses the **phone as the key.** (quote)
- **[2022-12-20]** ⭐ THE TWO THINGS HE LOVES: (1) **the yoke's *shape*** ("I get used to it, wouldn't go
  back to a normal wheel — more visibility to the screen"); (2) **the software's responsiveness** ("as
  good as it gets for an EV… no Android Auto or CarPlay, which is bold"). (quote)
- **[2022-12-20]** ⭐ THE TWO THINGS HE HATES: (1) **the capacitive yoke buttons** — **"I hate this. The
  blinkers, horn, headlights are all capacitive touch. My right blinker sometimes won't work for 10
  seconds and I'll just merge without it. Elon keeps saying 'all input is error' — it's 2022, we're not
  there yet; you need real buttons for confident input."**; (2) **touchscreen-only HVAC** ("having to
  slide to hit the exact fan speed while driving is insane"). (quote)
- **[2022-12-20]** ⚠️ Unkept promises (owner's honesty): Cyberpunk "was always advertised, never
  available"; **FSD** "I paid thousands for years ago, still a beta"; the **claimed 200 mph top speed
  "is not real, it never has been" (actually ~175 in track mode).** ⚠️ Build quality "not up to a $130k
  Mercedes/BMW/Porsche" (a broken carbon-fiber console lid, some creaking). No powered frunk, no 360
  camera ("why?!"). (quote)
- **[2022-12-20]** ⭐⬥ THE VERDICT: **"This is the best daily driver in the world for me — American roads
  are lots of straight lines, the acceleration and the ability to place yourself on the road is awesome,
  it's a fun car with tons of space. And as an EV I do a lot of road trips — for Ultimate Frisbee I'll
  go New York to Boston, Ottawa, Montreal, Toronto, back to New York, Philly, DC — and relying on the
  Supercharger network is huge."** Praises **Sentry mode + built-in dashcam** ("more cars should have
  this"). (quote)
