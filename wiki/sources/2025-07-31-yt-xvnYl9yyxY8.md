# Cadillac Celestiq - A Rolls Royce Killer?

## Summary

Marques walks through the **Cadillac Celestiq** — a ~$350k, fully-custom, hand-built ultra-luxury
EV he first mistook for a concept — as **"the peak of EVs, or at least what Cadillac thinks the
peak should be."** His frame is his standing doctrine: the ultra-luxury car "is the exact type
that's meant to go electric and be the quietest, smoothest, best version yet." Co-host **Miles**
does the driving (comparing it favorably to the Rolls-Royce Spectre for far less money) —
quarantined.

## Key claims — SUBJECT-ATTRIBUTED (Marques)

- **[2025-07-31]** "When I first saw this, I didn't think it was real — I thought it was a concept.
  It's very much real… every single one is bespoke and custom." The **~$350k Celestiq** "represents
  the peak of EVs, or at least what Cadillac thinks the peak should be." (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-07-31]** The doctrine (recurring): **"the reason I and a lot of people like the Rolls-Royce
  Spectre and the Maybach and the crazy big electric luxury vehicles is because it's the best version.
  This is the exact type of vehicle that's meant to go electric and be the quietest, smoothest, best
  version yet."** (quote)
- **[2025-07-31]** The interior extremes: 55" of screens, AKG surround, a **zoned electrochromic roof**
  (shade one quadrant/person and not another), a **heated-seat neck vent** ("the hot-breath vent"),
  awesome materials (metal/Alcantara/leather — "other than the piano black"). "My main concern with
  these hand-built cars is they feel like a prototype where things don't work — but on this,
  everything's working well." (paraphrase)
- **[2025-07-31]** The takeaway: **"this car is unobtanium — $350k and harder to buy than a Spectre
  (you've got to get on the list). But the hope is that a lot of this tech trickles down into GM cars
  that mere mortals can afford."** (quote)

## Context (co-host Miles — QUARANTINED, not persona data)
