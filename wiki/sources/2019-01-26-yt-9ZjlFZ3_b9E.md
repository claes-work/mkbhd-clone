# Behind the Scenes of the UnboxTherapy Studio! (@mkbhd, 2019-01-26)

## Summary
Episode 2 of his studio-tour series (now "BTS"; Ep. 1 was Casey Neistat's) — a tour of **Lewis Hilsenteger's
(Unbox Therapy)** studio. Lew (a guest) describes his own setup; the persona-relevant payoff is Marques's
**takeaway: he wants to build a "frictionless" one-button studio.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-01-26] **The series continuation.** "The long-awaited **Episode 2** — it started with the **Casey Neistat**
  studio tour over a year ago, paused, and is back under the new name **BTS.**" (Ties to his 2018 plans.) A dated
  creator-business/format note.
- [2019-01-26] **THE TAKEAWAY — the "frictionless one-button studio" (subject-attributed).** "**The #1 thing I
  learned from Lew's studio: I want to build a set where I can just walk in, sit down, hit ONE button, everything
  turns on, and I record. The ultimate frictionless studio — it removes any barrier of setup between having an idea
  and recording it.**" A direct extension of his **"frictionless — turn footage into videos"** production
  philosophy ([[2018-01-04-yt-6UR_H5XJkeM]]). Also notes Lew's set is **experimental / "might not be permanent"** —
  and Marques admires that iterate-on-the-space ethos.
- [2019-01-26] **Production-craft observations (Marques narrating).** Notes the multi-camera live workflow (a trio
  of cameras + the big monitors so the host can confirm focus, since "everything's live, we rarely redo anything"),
  the bounce-light-off-the-table approach, and the new downstairs set (a wall of stitched 4K OLEDs + wallpaper TVs,
  RGB gradient lighting configurable per-scene via an app). His curator's eye on another creator's production.
