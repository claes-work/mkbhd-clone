# The Fastest Phone of 2020! (@mkbhd, 2020-05-13)

## Summary
Review of the $600 Nubia Red Magic 5G gaming phone — "the fastest phone I've ever used," thanks to a **144 Hz**
screen and active fan cooling. The durable payload: the **law of diminishing returns on refresh rate** and a
prediction that we'll find a sweet spot (as we did with resolution).

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-05-13] **THE FRAMEWORK — diminishing returns on refresh rate.** "'**60→120 Hz was a huge payoff; every step
  past 120 feels a smaller and smaller difference while taking a bigger and bigger chunk of battery.** Like the 4K
  Sony phone, a future 240 Hz phone will probably only run 240 some of the time, crush battery, and not catch on.
  **Just as we came up from 360p→720p→1080p/1440p and found the sweet spot short of 4K, refresh rate will do the
  same** — the sweet spot is around 90-120.'" A promotable, reusable framework.
- [2020-05-13] **The Porsche 911-vs-GT3-RS analogy.** "'Can I see 144 vs 120 Hz? **Yes — but it's like asking a
  professional driver if they can feel the difference between a 911 and a 911 GT3 RS. Someone calibrated can; to a
  normal person they're both just extremely fast.**' The smoothness comes as much from Nubia's tweaked animations
  as the 144 vs 120.'" A characteristic calibrated-vs-normal-person framing.
- [2020-05-13] **The gaming-phone tropes (fun but cheesy).** "'Active **fan cooling** (you can hear it), RGB, a
  glowing logo, shoulder trigger buttons, a red gaming-mode button with cheesy sounds, a headphone jack ("zero-
  latency audio"). Downsides of the extreme: **no water/dust resistance** (holes), awkward button placement.'"
- [2020-05-13] **The camera — megapixels don't mean everything (again).** "'A **serviceable B+ camera** in good
  light; a **64 MP number that struggles for meaningful detail/accurate color** — a perfect example that
  megapixels don't mean everything. The one impressive part: **zero shutter lag** (a split-second faster than the
  iPhone, thanks to 300 Hz touch response).'"
- [2020-05-13] **The 2 MP macro-camera gripe + the worst fingerprint reader (tie).** "'**Who's asking for these 2
  MP macro cameras?** I'd cut it — unnecessary. And the in-display fingerprint reader **ties the Moto Edge+ for the
  worst I've ever used.**'"

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "The payoff from 60 hertz to 120 hertz was huge, but then every step past 120 that we go will feel a smaller and
> smaller amount of difference, but they will continue to take a bigger and bigger chunk out of your battery."
> "It's like asking a professional driver if they can feel the difference between a Porsche 911 and a 911 GT3 RS."
