# Outer Folding Phones: It's Time to Stop! (@mkbhd, 2020-12-15)

## Summary
Impressions of the Royole FlexPai 2 that become an argument about foldable form factors. The FlexPai 2 is
massively improved (a "most improved" award candidate) but he'd still never recommend it — and it leads to a
durable conclusion: **among all the alternate form factors, the outward-folding screen is not the way to go**
because it leaves the delicate display permanently exposed. The 2020 foldables question has shifted from **"can
we make folding phones work?" to "which version of a folding phone is the best way to do it?"**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-12-15] **"Most improved," but still don't recommend it.** "'The FlexPai 2 changed a lot — sharper edges,
  a real triple camera, a combined power/fingerprint button, stereo speakers, a **completely reworked metal-teeth
  hinge that now folds flat with a satisfying clap.** It's a genuine "most improved" candidate — **but I'd still
  never recommend it,** and it made me realize the outer-fold style is the wrong path.'"
- [2020-12-15] **THE ARGUMENT — outer-folding leaves the screen unprotected AND can't hide any tricks.** "'Every
  other foldable **folds IN to protect the soft, delicate display.** Outer-folding **not only stops protecting
  the precious screen — it's also the hardest to make look good, because everything is always exposed; you can't
  hide any tricks** (Motorola tucking the display into the chin, Samsung's hinge brushes). It's the most
  technically difficult version to get right.'" A promotable form-factor verdict.
- [2020-12-15] **THE 2020 QUESTION HAS CHANGED — "which version is best?"** "'It's a very 2020 conversation:
  it's **no longer "can we make folding phones work?" — now it's "which version of a folding phone is the best
  way to do it?"** As alternate form factors evolve, we're finding the ways that AREN'T good and can **eliminate
  them (or wait until the tech is ready to bring the idea back).**'" His discovery-by-elimination view of
  immature tech.
- [2020-12-15] **The cicada-wings metaphor — it needs protection that isn't invented yet.** "'Royole's press
  release compares the thin sides to cicada wings — accidentally perfect: **cicada wings are super thin/light but
  have a natural high-tech self-cleaning, bacteria-killing hydrophobic coating. The FlexPai doesn't have that
  extra protection layer — and that's exactly what outer-folding-screen tech needs to be useful: something that
  hasn't been invented yet.**'"
- [2020-12-15] **His pick — the rollables (most intrigued, unprotected caveat aside).** "'The form factor I'm
  most interested in is actually **the rollables that unfurl like a scroll** — they might have the same
  unprotected problem, but they're not doing a fold-flat/hinge; they're more just variable aspect ratio. I want
  to try one.'" (Also skeptical the Huawei Mate X outer-fold is the best way.)

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "This outside folding screen version, as cool as it can look, is not the way to go."
> "It's not 'can we make folding phones work anymore' — now it's more of 'which version of a folding phone is the
> best way to do it?' That's the new question."
> "It needs that extra layer of protection... it needs something that hasn't been invented yet."
