# Sonos Ace Headphones: You Had One Job! (@mkbhd, 2024-06-03)

## Summary
A month-plus review of the **$450 Sonos Ace** — Sonos (famous for premium Wi-Fi speakers) finally entering the
crowded high-end ANC-headphone game against Sony/Bose/AirPods Max. The verdict: they're **genuinely solid** —
lightweight, comfortable (some of the softest he's worn; magnetic swappable ear cups; only nitpick, slightly shallow
cups), real buttons + a clever "content key" rocker, 40+ hour battery, multipoint, and — the highlights — **A+ noise
cancellation** and **the best transparency mode outside AirPods**. But the title's "one job": everyone waited for Sonos
headphones because Sonos makes **awesome Wi-Fi speaker ecosystems** — and **the Ace is Bluetooth-only, not Wi-Fi
connected**, so it can't join the Sonos speaker ecosystem or stream directly (likely a battery constraint). That,
plus a **"severely neutered" Sonos app** (the EQ is just a bass and a treble slider) and a lackluster case, makes them
**"feel like an overpriced version of the Sonys"** (which are $100–$200 cheaper). He can only shoehorn a recommendation
in **three narrow cases**: you love the brand and don't care about money; you want the best non-AirPods transparency
mode; or you specifically own/plan to buy a Sonos Arc soundbar and often **swap TV audio between speakers and
headphones** with one tap (great for "one person wants to watch TV while the other sleeps"). Otherwise, "just a normal
solid pair of Bluetooth headphones." (8 Sleep Pod 4 sponsor read.)

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2024-06-03] **They're genuinely solid — A+ ANC and transparency.** "'They're really solid: lightweight, some of the
  softest headphones I've worn, real buttons, 40+ hour battery, multipoint. The most impressive thing is the noise
  cancellation — A+, erases white noise and road noise, incredible for flights — and to my surprise the best
  transparency mode I've heard that isn't AirPods.'"
- [2024-06-03] **The one job was Wi-Fi / ecosystem integration — and they missed it.** "'Everyone waited for Sonos to
  do headphones because Sonos makes awesome Wi-Fi speaker ecosystems. But the Ace is Bluetooth-only, not Wi-Fi
  connected — it can't join the Sonos ecosystem or pull from streaming services directly (probably a battery issue).
  For a $450 Sonos, I thought maybe they'd pull it off. They didn't.'"
- [2024-06-03] **So it's an overpriced version of the Sonys.** "'That, plus a severely neutered app where the EQ is
  literally just a bass slider and a treble slider, makes them feel like an overpriced version of the Sonys — which are
  90% of the same headphones for $100–$200 less.'"
- [2024-06-03] **Only three narrow reasons to buy them.** "'The only ways I can shoehorn in a recommendation: (1) you
  love the brand and have the money; (2) you want the best transparency mode that isn't AirPods; or (3) you have (or
  are getting) a Sonos Arc and often swap TV audio between speakers and headphones with one tap — great for when one
  person wants to watch TV without waking the other. Otherwise, just a normal solid pair of Bluetooth headphones.'"
