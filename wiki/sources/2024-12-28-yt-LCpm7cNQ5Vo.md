# WTF Happened in 2024?! (@mkbhd, 2024-12-28)

## Summary
A month-by-month **2024 year-in-review** — "the longest year of my life" — that's a biography goldmine. The beats:
**Jan** Vision Pro launch + the invite to try out for Team USA Ultimate. **Feb** **sold his daily-driver Tesla Model
S**; Sora unveiled; the 43-minute every-Galaxy-S video; the Fisker Ocean "worst car" review controversy; **joined Ridge
as chief creative partner ("the biggest thing I've ever done outside of YouTube")**. **Mar** small phones died; the
Cybertruck (his biggest team production ever); won an Ultimate tournament in Japan under Mt. Fuji; **found out he made
Team USA Mixed for the first time ever**; a **Tesla service van caught fire outside the studio** on Pi Day. **Apr** the
Humane/Rabbit reviews reignited the negative-review debate — **"the weight of the publish button is heavy"** (from a
Colin & Samir talk); the total solar eclipse in Ohio (his bucket-list of natural phenomena) + wisdom teeth out during
the NJ earthquake. **May** the Stevens commencement speech; Team USA training camp in Colombia. **Jun** AutoFocus hit
1M; the Tim Cook interview; the PGA golf-tech video ("a golfer since I was 10"). **Jul** the FBI/Escobar story. **Aug**
the Boox Palma; his longest-ever YouTube break for the **World Ultimate Championships in Australia — GOLD, a USA sweep,
and he scored the championship point**. **Sep** flew Australia→the iPhone 16 Apple event (31 straight awake hours); the
M251 low-top sneaker with Atoms; **Time 100 Most Influential in AI**; the Panels launch + getting roasted ("it was my
turn... I deserved it"). **Oct** Robotaxi/AR glasses. **Nov** the M4 Mac mini "product of the year"; **the Lamborghini
speeding-clip apology** (removed a POV clip going 90 in a 35 — "there's a reason for speed limits, I own it"). **Dec**
the Sora review then Google Veo; Smartphone Awards; Bitcoin 100k. His big lesson, from an Ultimate coach: **"pressure
is a privilege"** — getting to make something and have people care is a privilege. And the 2025 direction: **"a return
to the basics — quality over quantity, good videos rather than a ton of videos."**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2024-12-28] **Pressure is a privilege.** "'The main lesson came from both Ultimate Frisbee and YouTube — a coach
  said it and I never forget it: pressure is a privilege. You don't always get to play for something that really means
  a lot, and to have that opportunity is amazing. Same here: you don't necessarily get to make something new, send it
  to the internet, and have anybody care about it. That's a privilege; I don't want to forget it.'"
- [2024-12-28] **The weight of the publish button is heavy.** "'The Humane and Rabbit reviews reignited the negative-
  review debate — what I mostly learned was the power these reviews actually have. To borrow from a talk with Colin and
  Samir: the weight of the publish button is heavy. As a very visible creator people look up to, everything deserves an
  extra layer of diligence.'"
- [2024-12-28] **World champion + made Team USA Mixed.** "'I made Team USA Mixed for the first time ever in my career,
  which added training camps and the World Championships in Australia — where we won GOLD as part of a USA sweep, and
  I scored the game-and-championship-winning point. I think I need to make an Ultimate Frisbee Explained video.'"
- [2024-12-28] **The Lamborghini speeding-clip apology.** "'A now-removed clip in the DJI action-cam video showed POV
  driving a Lamborghini over 90 mph in a 35 zone — it's stupid and dangerous, and dangerous as a precedent because
  people watch these videos. I apologized and removed it. There's a reason for speed limits, and I own that
  responsibility. Making mistakes and genuinely learning from them is important as a human and as a visible creator.'"
- [2024-12-28] **2025 direction: back to basics, quality over quantity.** "'2024 clarified what I want in 2025 and
  forward: a return to the basics — quality over quantity, a bit more good videos rather than just a ton of videos.
  Also joined Ridge as chief creative partner (the biggest thing outside YouTube), sold my Tesla Model S, and made the
  Time 100 Most Influential in AI (probably the Rabbit/Humane stuff).'"
