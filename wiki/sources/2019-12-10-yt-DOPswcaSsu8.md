# Mac Pro Unboxing & Second Impressions: The Power is Back! (@mkbhd, 2019-12-10)

## Summary
Second impressions of the 2019 Mac Pro after two weeks of daily editing on it — the payoff to a **6-year wait**
since the 2013 trash-can. The durable idea is the **"Headroom unlocker"** philosophy of pro tools, plus jaw-
dropping RED render numbers.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-12-10] **BIOGRAPHY — every recent video edited on it; a 6-year wait.** "'For the past **two weeks I've been
  editing every video on the new Mac Pro** (since the MacBook Pro review). This is something I've **personally been
  waiting for the better part of six years — since the 2013 trash-can.** I'll probably get around this same spec +
  8 TB storage.'" A datable workflow milestone.
- [2019-12-10] **The render numbers — the reason he wanted it.** "'The same **8K RED RAW** render test: **MacBook
  Pro ~20 min → iMac Pro ~11 min → Mac Pro 4:20** — it exported a 5-min clip in **less time than it takes to watch
  it, faster than real-time**, and that's **without even using the Afterburner card** (which is for ProRes).'"
- [2019-12-10] **THE PHILOSOPHY — a "Headroom unlocker," not magic.** "'By 2019/2020 it **takes a lot to inspire me
  as a creator** because my tools are so unlimited. This is **not magic — having a Mac Pro won't make my videos
  better or anyone's videos better.** But it's a **"Headroom unlocker" — it pulls down the barrier for what I'm
  willing to try** as a creator (because of power/speed/time).'" A crystallization of his tools-vs-talent belief
  (cf. [[2019-06-04-yt-QJxbqIo5pC0]]: "no tool makes you better; pros pay for QC and speed").
- [2019-12-10] **Quiet + airflow-by-design.** "'Two takeaways from daily use: **really quiet** (four feet from the
  mic, inaudible — everything is **passively cooled**, just three big front fans; the cheese-grater holes are an
  **airflow design**) and **really fast** (highest Geekbench I've seen; 3 GB/s SSD).'" Notes Linus's dust concern
  (no obvious air filter).
- [2019-12-10] **The port nitpick.** "'Only **two full-size USB-A ports** — instantly full (mouse receiver + SD
  reader). Many legacy Mac Pro peripherals still aren't USB-C; I wish there were two more USB-A on the back.'"
  His recurring "creative pros still need legacy ports" point.

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "It's not like magic — it's not like having this Mac Pro is going to make my videos better or make anyone's
> videos better. But it's sort of a Headroom unlocker — it pulls down the barrier for what I'm willing to try as
> a creator because of power or speed or time."
> "A 5-minute clip on the timeline, exported the whole thing in less time than it would take to just watch it."
