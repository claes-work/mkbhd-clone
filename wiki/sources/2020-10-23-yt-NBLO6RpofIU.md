# Google Pixel 5 Review: Software Special! (@mkbhd, 2020-10-23)

## Summary
Full review of the $699 Pixel 5. The thesis: **2020 is the year Google embraces that the Pixel is all about
software** — no $1,000 hardware flagship, just "a vessel for the best software Google makes," and the result is
"a pretty nice phone." A **masterclass in using great software to turn pretty-good hardware into a really-good
phone.** But the **Pixel camera's lead has narrowed** as hardware elsewhere advanced, and he personally wishes
they'd made a hardware flagship.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-10-23] **THE THESIS — Google finally embraces that the Pixel is about software.** "'Google is a software
  company; **Pixel hardware has never been that great — never the best screens, designs, sensors, or RAM.** 2020
  is the year Google **embraces that the Pixel is all about the software** — no $1,000 flagship, **just a $699
  vessel for the best software they make.** The result is a pretty nice phone — losing last year's hardware
  bells (Soli/squeeze) **actually made it better.**'"
- [2020-10-23] **"I can tell the difference" — 90 Hz vs 120 Hz (the honest reviewer note).** "'It hurts to say,
  but **I can tell the difference between this 90 Hz display and the 120 Hz in other phones this price** (OnePlus
  8T, S20 FE feel noticeably smoother). **Most people won't — but this is my review and you'd want to know what
  I noticed.** It's more the display than the 765G chip.'" His calibrated-eye honesty caveat.
- [2020-10-23] **The 765G "downgrade" was a great idea — for efficiency.** "'People got mad about dropping to a
  765G (weaker on paper), but **I think it was a great idea for the efficiency gains — a performance difference
  no one, including me, would notice.** Battery is **mind-blowing: I failed to kill it in a day for two weeks —
  4 hours screen-on, 50% left. It just sips power.**'"
- [2020-10-23] **THE CAMERA — software-over-everything proof, but the lead has narrowed.** "'The camera is the
  maximum proof of the software-over-everything approach — **same sensor as the Pixel 2, maximized by software,
  plus a new ultrawide.** Still, in my opinion, **the best-looking photos of any smartphone — a confident,
  contrasty look.** But **the lead the Pixel camera used to have has narrowed a lot** — bigger sensors elsewhere
  do natural blur and low-light better. **I wonder how much longer Google can stick to this formula.**'"
- [2020-10-23] **A masterclass — but not for me (the wish-list).** "'For a **masterclass on using great software
  to turn a pretty-good phone into a really-good one, you're looking at it.** But for me? **I wish they'd made a
  high-end flagship version** — neural core (faster processing), 865 + 12 GB, 120 Hz, a bit bigger — even knowing
  it wouldn't sell much. I'm going back to a higher-end phone. **Well played, Google.**'" (Argues it maybe
  should've been $599; the Pixel 4a 5G at $499 is a similar good phone.)

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "2020 is the year Google not just recognizes that, but embraces that the Pixel is all about the software."
> "It hurts me to say this... I can tell the difference between this 90 Hz display and some of the 120 Hz
> displays in some other phones in this price range."
> "If you're ever looking for a master class on how to use great software to turn a pretty good smartphone into
> a really good smartphone, you're looking at it."
