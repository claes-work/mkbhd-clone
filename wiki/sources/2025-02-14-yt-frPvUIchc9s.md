# Rivian Gen 2 Refresh - It's the Little Things

## Summary

Marques, **a launch-edition Rivian R1T owner of a couple years**, lives with the **second-generation
Rivian R1S** and ranks every Gen-2 change by how noticeable it actually is to daily use. The verdict
is a study in **upgrade restraint**: the changes are real (a much-improved suspension with
Sport/All-Purpose modes pushed further apart, ~1 more day of commuting range, dramatically better
cameras) but individually small — **"I'm not going to buy another one just to get these new
features."** Fully Marques-narrated. (References a prior podcast with Rivian CEO RJ Scaringe.)

## Key claims (biography marked ⬥)

- **[2025-02-14]** ⬥ BIOGRAPHY: **"I've been a launch-edition Rivian R1T owner for a couple years now…
  I think they're great."** He was "the most curious" about the Gen-2 refresh because "the second
  generation of everything is the most interesting — it leads you to know what direction they're
  going." (quote)
- **[2025-02-14]** ⬥ BIOGRAPHY: he **commutes "to and from the studio"** in it — the Gen-2's ~350mi
  range means "five full business days of commuting where I could do four on the R1T." (quote)
- **[2025-02-14]** ⬥ BIOGRAPHY: **"remember we did the podcast with RJ"** (Rivian founder/CEO RJ
  Scaringe) "and I talked about the wireless chargers in the middle being basically useless." (quote)
- **[2025-02-14]** Biggest change = **the suspension/motors**: All-Purpose mode is now softer/plusher/
  more luxurious; Sport is firmer (even a bit bouncy on regular roads) — "they've pushed those modes
  further apart." (paraphrase)
- **[2025-02-14]** Cameras "dramatically better" (1080p, high frame rate, great at night); ⚠️ the
  blinker-feed on his own Gen-1 R1T (via the software update) "looks terrible… almost unusable." New
  push-button door latches with a manual override he "still uses out of habit"; the doors now close with
  far less force but sound like "a thud, the G-Wagen sound — doesn't feel as satisfying, in my opinion."
  (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-02-14]** ⚠️ The new flat wireless chargers "still slide around and I don't think they're
  actually still very good — the flat one might be doomed; the successful implementations are tilted."
  The electrochromic roof and roadside-lighting animations are "cool… I don't press it very often." The
  heat pump doesn't heat faster but gets him **closer to advertised range in cold weather**. (quote)
- **[2025-02-14]** ⭐ Verdict (upgrade restraint): **"This being roughly the equivalent of the launch
  edition but making a ton of those small improvements feels like… I'm not going to buy another one just
  to get these new features, but I'm glad I've noticed what they've changed."** (quote)
