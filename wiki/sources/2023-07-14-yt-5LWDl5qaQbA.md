# iOS 17 Hands-On: Top 5 Features! (@mkbhd, 2023-07-14)

## Summary
A top-five of iOS 17 ("one of the biggest, best updates to iOS in a long time"), with a running theme to **keep in
the back of your head: most of these features depend on the other person also having an iPhone** — "that's the
ecosystem at work." The five: **#5 AirDrop/NameDrop** (bump two iPhones to swap contact posters — with control over
exactly what you share; transfers now resume over cellular; he calls it a **Sherlock / "remora fish to Apple shark"**
move, echoing an old app called Bump). **#4 Personal Voice** (an *accessibility* feature — read 150 phrases and the
phone stitches a synthesized copy of your voice **entirely on-device**, for people at risk of losing their voice —
"it kind of still sounds like them"). **#3 the little things** (a Transformer-based smarter keyboard that finally
*learns* your word; **interactive widgets** "finally"; Check-In in Messages). **#2 StandBy mode** — a charging-and-
horizontal iPhone becomes a smart-home display, **"genuinely better than a HomePod for most people who already have
an iPhone,"** boosted by a more conversational, no-wake-word Siri. **#1 the Phone + FaceTime app** (contact posters
that auto-clean everyone's contacts; live voicemail transcription — a throwback to physical answering machines;
FaceTime video voicemail). His close: don't forget how many of these are **"only work if your friend also has an
iPhone — so maybe you pressure them into getting one."**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2023-07-14] **The theme — most of this needs another iPhone.** "'Keep this in the back of your head as we go:
  most, if not all, of these features depend on having another iPhone in the mix — a friend or family member. From
  NameDrop to SharePlay to the iMessage and FaceTime features, that's the ecosystem at work: "here's a cool feature,
  but it only works if your friend also has an iPhone, so maybe pressure them into one."'"
- [2023-07-14] **NameDrop — the Sherlock / remora-shark move.** "'NameDrop lets you bump two iPhones together to
  transfer a contact poster, and you choose exactly what to share (a real number, a fake one). There was an app in
  2009 called Bump that did this; Google bought them — if they still existed, this would be a Sherlock situation,
  another remora fish to the Apple shark.'"
- [2023-07-14] **Personal Voice — an on-device accessibility feature.** "'Personal Voice (in accessibility, not even
  mentioned on stage): read 150 phrases and the phone stitches a synthesized copy of your voice, all on-device, no
  servers. It's for people who may lose their voice — and it kind of still sounds like them, so they can interact
  without a generic Siri voice.'"
- [2023-07-14] **StandBy is genuinely better than a HomePod for most iPhone owners.** "'StandBy mode is this high on
  the list because it's genuinely better than a HomePod for most people who already have an iPhone. Charging +
  horizontal turns it into a smart-home display — a clock, widgets, photos, Apple-Watch-like faces — and a more
  conversational, no-wake-word Siri makes it feel like a smart-home display. If only Siri were actually good at
  other things.'"
- [2023-07-14] **The little things and the phone app.** "'Interactive widgets, finally — the whole point of a widget
  is interacting without jumping into the app. The smarter keyboard finally *learns* your word. And the top one:
  contact posters that auto-clean everyone's contacts, and live voicemail transcription — a throwback to physical
  answering machines where you could hear someone leaving a message and pick up. I'm old now, so I get to say that.'"
