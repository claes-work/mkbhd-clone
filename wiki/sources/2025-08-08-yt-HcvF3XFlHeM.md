# CarPlay Ultra Review - The Closest thing to an Apple Car!

## Summary

Marques reviews **CarPlay Ultra** (in an Aston Martin DBX — the first car to support it) as
"the closest thing to an Apple car." The distinction: regular CarPlay just mirrors your iPhone
on a second screen; **CarPlay Ultra takes over *all* the car's screens and folds in the vehicle
controls**, so you almost never leave CarPlay. He explains the clever mechanisms (punch-through
UI, overlays, the car-designed cluster themes), then poses the strategic question of **which
software-focused carmaker would ever let Apple take over their screens.** Fully Marques-narrated.

## Key claims

- **[2025-08-08]** The framing (with a joke that a plasma-blue Aston DBX 707 is "the Apple car"):
  Apple announced CarPlay Ultra years ago, and this year at WWDC **Aston Martin is the first on
  board.** "This is my first look at CarPlay Ultra as someone who's only used regular CarPlay."
  (paraphrase)
- **[2025-08-08]** The core difference: **regular CarPlay is just a second screen mirroring your
  iPhone; CarPlay Ultra is a collaboration between the car and CarPlay that brings in vehicle
  controls** — so you can change climate/radio/drive modes **without ever leaving CarPlay** (which
  you always had to do before). (paraphrase)
- **[2025-08-08]** The clever mechanisms: **"punch-through UI"** (for settings Apple hasn't built —
  e.g. specific Bowers & Wilkins speaker settings, or the reverse camera — it opens a frame and
  shows the car's native UI, then returns to CarPlay); **overlays** (legally-required items like the
  odometer/airbag/tire-pressure warnings are rendered locally by the car so they stay readable even
  if the phone dies); and **car-designed cluster themes** behind the wheel that "look like Aston
  Martin software but with hints of Apple," swipeable and syncing colors across the displays.
  (paraphrase)
- **[2025-08-08]** Requirements: **wireless-only** (no wired CarPlay Ultra), iPhone 12+/iOS 18.5+;
  existing Aston owners can get it via a service-center software update. (paraphrase)
- **[2025-08-08]** **The strategic question**: "if you're a car company making a car with a huge
  screen and a bunch of cool software, what are the chances you're *also* willing to let Apple take
  over your screens if the user has an iPhone? That's a really weird question." **Tesla and Rivian
  are sure they don't want to** (no CarPlay at all); a "trickling list" of others (Aston) are
  starting to support Ultra. (quote)
- **[2025-08-08]** Verdict: **"to me, it's just a little bit more convenient, but otherwise it's not
  really too special."** (quote)

## Notable verbatim quotes

- "CarPlay is essentially just a second screen mirrored from your iPhone… CarPlay Ultra takes over
  all of the screens in your car to give you access to all of your car's stuff without ever having
  to leave CarPlay."
- "What are the chances you're making a car with a bunch of cool software, but you're also willing
  to let Apple take over the screens in your car if the user has an iPhone?"
- "To me, it's just a little bit more convenient, but otherwise not really too special."
