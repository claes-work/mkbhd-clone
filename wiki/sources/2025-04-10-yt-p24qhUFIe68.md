# Oppo Find X8 Ultra: The Bar's Been Raised! (@mkbhd, 2025-04-10)

## Summary
A hands-on with **"the phone that appears to be as good as a phone spec sheet can possibly be right now"** — framed as
his **"dream-phone spec sheet with unlimited budget"** (the recurring Frankenstein-phone wish), and it matches item for
item: **Snapdragon 8 Elite, 16 GB / 1 TB, a 6,100 mAh silicon-carbon battery (100 W charging → dead-to-full in ~40 min,
plus 50 W wireless + reverse), a chart-topping 1440p 120 Hz LTPO display**, and a **camera system that's "the most
impressive collection of hardware I've ever seen in a phone"**: a **one-inch main sensor** (RX100-class; 63% larger
than the iPhone 16 Pro Max's, 69% larger than the S25 Ultra's), **two huge telephotos** (a 3x that's 300% larger than
the S25 Ultra's + a 6x), a big ultra-wide, and a dedicated **chroma sensor** for white balance — shooting **4K120 Dolby
Vision from both 1x and 3x.** The trade-off he keeps returning to is **Oppo's shamelessness**: ColorOS copies iOS
everywhere (an Action-button clone; a Camera-Control clone — "it only took them a few months"; a "fake AirDrop" that
needs an iPhone app), which he tolerates via **"I don't mind copying as long as they copy the good stuff."** But he
draws a broader point: the copying **highlights the US-vs-other-markets difference** — in the US, phones compete on
**ecosystem/iMessage lock-in**, so there's no pressure to just build the best hardware; in other markets, makers can
grab the iPhone's aesthetics *plus* all of Android's advantages and the big spec numbers — **"it kind of feels like what
we wish the iPhone would be doing here, but it isn't."** It's **never coming to the US** (a bummer). His verdict — a
callback to the OnePlus 13's "the bar has been set" — **"the bar has been raised to a really good spot."**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2025-04-10] **The dream-phone spec sheet, no corners cut.** "'People ask my dream phone and I usually Frankenstein
  specs from different phones. This is basically the max of what humanity is capable of in 2025 if you don't cut a
  single corner: Snapdragon 8 Elite, 16 GB / 1 TB, a 6,100 mAh silicon-carbon battery (100 W → full in ~40 min, plus
  50 W wireless + reverse), a chart-topping display, and a ridiculous camera system.'"
- [2025-04-10] **The most impressive camera hardware I've seen in a phone.** "'A one-inch main sensor (RX100-class —
  63% bigger than the iPhone 16 Pro Max's, 69% bigger than the S25 Ultra's), two huge telephotos (a 3x that's 300%
  bigger than the S25 Ultra's, plus a 6x), a big ultra-wide, and a dedicated chroma sensor for white balance. The
  Snapdragon 8 Elite is putting in work — 4K120 Dolby Vision from both 1x and 3x. It's the most impressive collection
  of hardware I've ever seen in a phone camera.'"
- [2025-04-10] **Shameless Oppo copying — but copy the good stuff.** "'The shamelessness runs deep: ColorOS copies iOS
  everywhere (an Action-button clone; a Camera-Control clone — it only took them a few months; a "fake AirDrop" that
  needs an iPhone app). I don't mind a lot of the copying as long as they're copying the good stuff.'"
- [2025-04-10] **The copying highlights US ecosystem-lock-in vs. hardware markets.** "'It highlights the difference
  between the US and other markets. In the US, phones compete on ecosystem and iMessage lock-in; elsewhere you pick
  your favorite phone on hardware, so why not build one with the iPhone's aesthetics plus all of Android's advantages
  and the big spec numbers? It kind of feels like what we wish the iPhone would be doing here, but it isn't.'"
- [2025-04-10] **The bar has been raised.** "'It's never coming to the US, which is a bummer. But to borrow from the
  OnePlus 13 review earlier this year where "the bar's been set," now I can say the bar has been raised to a really good
  spot.'"
