# Dogecoin: Explained (@mkbhd, 2021-02-16)

## Summary
A crypto explainer (not financial advice) unpacking Dogecoin's rise. The durable ideas: it's **a joke that
outgrew itself** into a real altcoin, and it caught on precisely because it **doesn't take itself seriously** —
making it **"the crypto for the people"** (accessible, less intimidating) in a world where crypto takes itself
too seriously. Contrasted with a **sketchy Tron sponsored-tweet campaign** (insincere, try-hard). Elon's tweets
drive the volume; it's **"the currency of internet attention."**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-02-16] **The framing + the disclaimer.** "'**This is not financial advice — I'm not an expert, and it's
  not the type of thing you should get from a YouTuber anyway.**' Dogecoin is an altcoin (one of thousands built
  on tweaks of Bitcoin's fundamentals — "almost anyone can make an altcoin"). **It started as a joke tweet by
  Jackson Palmer** mocking how many altcoins there were, "and it's one of those jokes that catches on so much it
  outgrows itself and becomes a real thing."'"
- [2021-02-16] **Why it stuck — "crypto for the people" (credit: Graham Stephan).** "'Crypto takes itself very
  seriously, which gets overwhelming — and pushes people into murky young coins, pump-and-dumps, scams. **Because
  Dogecoin started as a harmless joke and the founders obviously aren't in it for the money, it's more accessible,
  less intimidating, more likely to succeed — the crypto for the people.**' (Like Will Rogers's 1928 joke
  presidential campaign.)'"
- [2021-02-16] **THE COUNTER-EXAMPLE — the sketchy Tron sponsored-tweet campaign.** "'The opposite end: I got a
  **sponsorship email for Tron** (the "Ethereum killer") boasting Lindsay Lohan / Lil Yachty participation, asking
  to **"make it look like it's not a sponsored tweet"** — the example tweets weren't even marked as ads. **That
  comes off as so insincere and try-hard that people are already turned off by it** — the opposite of Dogecoin,
  which is boosted by people, and whose owners have donated gains to good causes** (Kenya's water crisis, the
  Jamaican bobsled team — a Cool Runnings reference).'" A media-literacy/authenticity read on marketing.
- [2021-02-16] **Elon drives the volume — "the currency of internet attention."** "'Watch the price and volume go
  up the more Elon tweets, then simmer down — **a volume graph would track lockstep with his tweets.** In a world
  of unregulated crypto, that's fair game. Dogecoin caught on as a meme — rallying against Wall Street, but also
  the meme itself — **it feels like the currency of internet attention;** this meme has a longer shelf life than
  most.'"
- [2021-02-16] **The takeaway — a fun, harmless way to learn; don't risk what you can't lose.** "'Do I think it
  hits a dollar? Probably not. But it's **a fun, harmless crypto to get started with and understand how trading
  works — just don't invest any money you're uncomfortable turning to zero.** Then apply those learnings to future
  investments — **it's always a good idea to have a diverse set of investments; that's Financial 101.** It feels
  good to be in on the joke.'"
