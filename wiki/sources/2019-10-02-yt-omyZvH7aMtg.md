# Hands-on with Microsoft's Future Foldables! (@mkbhd, 2019-10-02)

## Summary
A rare early look (at Microsoft's Redmond HQ) at two 2020 dual-screen devices: the **Surface Neo** (tablet) and
**Surface Duo** (pocketable, runs Android). His angle: the **360° hinge / dual-screen-with-a-bezel** is a
different, more durable answer to foldables than a true folding screen — and he loves the renewed commitment.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-10-02] **The 360° hinge as a smarter foldable path.** "'It's **not technically a folding screen** (there's
  a bezel in the middle), but it has a lot of the **upsides** — the inside screen is also the outside screen — and
  it **avoids all the tricky folding-display magic**, is **safer for durability** (you can cover both sides), and
  you can't fold it the wrong way.' The two things he'd still worry about: **twisting durability** (it's super
  thin) and **battery** (no idea what fits in something this thin)."
- [2019-10-02] **The Surface Duo runs Android — Microsoft + Google collaborating.** "'Possibly the most
  interesting thing: the **Duo runs Android** — Microsoft and Google working together on a dual-screen version, the
  same way they did Windows 10X for the Neo.' Magnets hold a mini keyboard / the Slim Pen; the OS gives a
  touch-bar-like UI when you attach it."
- [2019-10-02] **The value — glad the foldable wave is back "full steam."** "'I **love this — I love the
  commitment.** We had a little hiccup at the beginning of folding phones (the Fold saga), but now we're back to
  full steam: Galaxy Fold shipping, Mate X coming, and now this commitment from Microsoft. **The form factor of
  the future may be big screens getting small and then big again.**'" Continues his reward-experimentation stance
  (cf. [[2019-09-15-yt--5BsYYtyENw]]).
- [2019-10-02] **The taxonomy of foldables (a useful map).** "Names the approaches: the **Galaxy Fold way** (fold
  in, screen out), the **Mate X way** (fold out), vertical foldables (**Motorola Razr** mockups), and now the
  **360° dual-screen** way — a clean mental model of the 2019 foldable landscape."

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "I love this. I love the commitment. I want to see them really make this... we had a little hiccup there in the
> beginning of folding phones, but it seems like now we're back to full steam ahead."
> "The form factor of the future may actually be big screens getting small and then getting big again."
