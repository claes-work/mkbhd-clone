# iPhone 17 Pro Review: Paradox in a Box! ★★

## Summary

The iPhone 17 Pro / Pro Max review, organized around a **paradox**: making the Pro iPhone
*more* pro — thicker, unibody metal, a first-ever vapor chamber, bigger battery, a bigger
4x telephoto — turns out to be **better for the masses**, who buy the Pro Max in huge numbers
and enjoy nicer screens/cameras/batteries just like anyone. He credits the iPhone Air with
"freeing Apple up" to go maximalist here. Landmark for the "gaming-phone effect," the
ProRes-RAW/genlock pro-video additions, and the Scratchgate discussion. ★★.

## Key claims

- **[2025-09-26]** The setup: **"the best thing about the iPhone Air is how much it seems to
  have freed Apple up to make the Pro phones even more pro."** These Pros are thicker, with a
  vapor chamber, bigger battery, bigger camera — "the most maximalist that an iPhone has ever
  felt, and I like it." (quote)
- **[2025-09-26]** Sales reality (via dbrand's model-by-model accessory distribution mirroring
  Apple's): **the Pro Max is by far the best-seller — over half of orders; the two Pros are
  ~90%.** Apple walks the line between "a more pro phone than ever" and "literally the most
  mainstream phone of all time." (paraphrase)
- **[2025-09-26]** Cameras: three now **more evenly matched** 48MP cameras — the **telephoto
  jumps to 48MP and 4x** (from 12MP/5x), "a more natural ~100mm portrait focal length" with far
  more detail. He credits the processed **2x crop increments** but wishes they'd fix the
  **night point-light flares** and add **8K** ("a flip of a switch away" on a 2TB phone).
  (paraphrase)
- **[2025-09-26]** **Pro-video additions**: **ProRes RAW** (change ISO/white balance after the
  shoot in Final Cut 11.2) and **genlock** (multi-device sync) — "a Pro feature they couldn't
  brand because pros already named it." He'll shoot an **electric G-Wagon Auto Focus review on
  the iPhone 17 Pro**. (paraphrase)
- **[2025-09-26]** Design: he loves the **all-unibody-metal jacket** ("not a glass sandwich
  anymore") — more durable, more internal room, and enough space for the **first-ever vapor
  chamber**; the **camera plateau** is a natural finger rest ("where I've been putting my index
  finger on Android phones for years"). (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-09-26]** Nitpicks: **no black Pro color** ("you sickos") — the color wraps the front
  bezel so you see it constantly, and the silver/orange annoy him; and **"Scratchgate"** —
  anodization chipping at the tight corner radii (the camera plateau) — though his no-case unit
  hasn't shown issues. (quote)
- **[2025-09-26]** The **A19 Pro** is ~20% faster; with **12GB RAM** and iOS 26 it's quick.
  The **vapor chamber makes a real thermal difference** (per HGX Studio's thermal-camera tests)
  — vs the Air these "rarely got any more than slightly warm," holding higher frame rates and
  display brightness longer. (paraphrase)
- **[2025-09-26]** The **"gaming-phone effect"**: the things that make a great gaming phone
  (better speakers, performance, screen, big battery) are great for regular use too — same for
  the Pro iPhone. "We've all been saying for years, 'just make a thicker phone and put more
  battery in it.'" The **17 Pro has a 20% bigger battery**; the **Pro Max cracks 5,000mAh for
  the first time** with **40W charging** (half in 20 min). He gets **5+ hours** screen-on with
  30%+ left. (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-09-26]** Other wins: a **PWM-dimming toggle** (first iPhone gen, buried in
  accessibility), a modest anti-reflective coating, **3,000-nit** peak brightness, and expanded
  **satellite** messaging/location. (paraphrase)
- **[2025-09-26]** The **paradox**, stated: **"making the Pro iPhones more pro is actually
  better for the masses. And this is the best one yet."** He'd still take a hypothetical iPhone
  Ultra over it. (quote)
