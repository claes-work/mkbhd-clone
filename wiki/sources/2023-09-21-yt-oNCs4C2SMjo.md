# Apple Watch Series 9 & Ultra 2: What Are We Waiting For?! (@mkbhd, 2023-09-21)

## Summary
A "simple" review: the **Series 9 and Ultra 2 are just a spec bump** — iterative updates for people with no/very-old
Apple Watch. Visually **you'd have to squint**; the only new look is a pink Series 9. The rumored **black Ultra 2
didn't ship** — his read: Apple tested **PVD-coated titanium on a beat-up adventure watch and realized it wouldn't
wear well** (unlike a phone people keep in a case). Everything new **stems from the S9 chip**: faster UI, more
brightness (2,000/3,000 nits — the **brightest display Apple's ever made**) at the same 18-hr battery, a
precision-finding UWB chip, and **on-device Siri** (genuinely faster for local tasks like timers; internet lookups
still need a round trip). The **one genuinely innovative feature is double-tap** (pinch thumb + index to select) —
"a perfect symphony of processing, computation, and sensors" he doesn't expect others to copy soon — and it
**foreshadows the Vision Pro's primary gesture.** (The "why is it S9-only" reason — the algorithm is "too heavy for
older silicon" — is "the most Apple thing ever"; it ships a month later.) The Watch is also **Apple's first fully
carbon-neutral product.** His real complaint: Apple does the **"big silicon upgrade in the same design, then
redesign next generation"** pattern (MacBook Air/Pro) — and he **hopes the Watch is due for that redesign** (a
circular shape, a rotating bezel, or a real battery-life leap) — **"but this ain't it; it's just a spec bump."**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2023-09-21] **Just a spec bump — you'd have to squint.** "'These aren't for people with any recent Apple Watch —
  they're iterative updates for people with no watch or a really old one. I've worn the Ultra 2 for a week and nobody
  can tell; it looks exactly the same. Everything new stems from the S9 chip. I'll make this simple: it's a spec
  bump.'"
- [2023-09-21] **No black Ultra — coated titanium wears badly on an adventure watch.** "'Everyone expected a black
  Ultra 2 — Apple always distinguishes the new one visually. I think they tested PVD-coated titanium and realized
  what happens to it on a durability-focused item you drag through rocks and sand; on a phone you use a case, but on
  an adventure watch it'd have a tough time wearing well. So we just didn't get it.'"
- [2023-09-21] **Double-tap is the one genuinely new thing — and it foreshadows the Vision Pro.** "'The one
  genuinely innovative new thing is double-tap — a perfect symphony of impressive processing, clever computation,
  and high-quality sensors that I don't expect other smartwatches to copy anytime soon. And it's clearly
  foreshadowing the Vision Pro, whose primary gesture is selecting by putting thumb and index finger together.'"
- [2023-09-21] **The S9-only reason is the most Apple thing ever.** "'There was already an accessibility setting
  (AssistiveTouch) doing basically this on older watches. The official reason double-tap is new and S9-only is the
  most Apple thing ever: the algorithm processing the same sensors is "so much heavier now it couldn't possibly run
  on an S8." Convenient. And it ships a month later, not with the watch.'"
- [2023-09-21] **We've been waiting for a redesign for years — but this ain't it.** "'Apple does the big-silicon-
  upgrade-in-the-same-design, then redesign-the-next-generation pattern (MacBook Air/Pro). I'm hoping that's what's
  happening with the Watch — this looks exactly like last year, which looked like the year before, and the year
  before. A circular redesign, a rotating bezel like the Galaxy Watch, or a real battery-life leap would be the
  biggest update in years. But this ain't it — it's just a spec bump. (Also: Apple's first fully carbon-neutral
  product.)'"
