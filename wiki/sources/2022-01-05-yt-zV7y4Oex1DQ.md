# 2022 Tech I'm Ready For! (@mkbhd, 2022-01-05)

## Summary
His annual **look-forward** tradition. Top of the list: **the Apple-silicon Mac Pro** — the two-year Mac transition
should finish this year, and he's **waiting for the tower** (an iMac Pro will tempt him but he wants the "most
unlocked version of Apple silicon" — bigger wattage, bigger thermal envelope). **iPhone 14 rumors** he'd like to
see (and expects the industry to follow): **ProMotion + LTPO across the whole line**, a possible **48 MP camera
"handled the Apple way"** (a Pro mode + 8K, normally binned to 12), and a clever **hole-punch + under-display Face
ID**. Also **Galaxy S22** (hoping they don't re-curve the display; a possible S-Pen/Note revival) and the
**OnePlus 10 Pro**. And **EV trucks** (Rivian/Lightning/Cybertruck) — he wants to redo the 1000-mile gas-vs-electric
test **with trucks, towing.** Notes he's **skipping CES for the first time in a decade** ("not the most responsible
thing right now").

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-01-05] **Waiting for the Apple-silicon Mac Pro.** "'The Mac's two-year Apple-silicon transition (announced
  June 2020) should finish now. The iMac Pro has the simplest formula (drop in M1 Pro/Max, big squared display) and
  will tempt me — **but I won't switch; I'm waiting for the tower.** The Mac Pro is the one I'm most excited for
  because it's **the most unlocked version of Apple silicon possible** — way more wattage, way bigger thermal
  envelope; even my current Mac Pro feels slow in some single-threaded tasks vs an M1 Air.'"
- [2022-01-05] **iPhone 14 — ProMotion across the line, 48 MP the Apple way, hole-punch + under-display Face ID.**
  "'Things I'm looking forward to (and hope the industry copies): **ProMotion/LTPO across the whole lineup, even a
  mini** (funny how Apple went from behind on high refresh to maybe leading it); a possible **48 MP camera —
  handled the Apple way** (a Pro high-res mode + 8K video, normally binned to 12 MP for everyone); and the most
  fascinating rumor, a **hole-punch selfie camera with the Face ID system moved under the display** — clever,
  because Face ID's IR dots don't need high clarity, so you keep a normal selfie camera in the punch-hole.'"
- [2022-01-05] **Galaxy S22 + OnePlus 10 Pro.** "'The S22 (following the S21 Ultra's Phone-of-the-Year win): classic
  Samsung "better everything" + maybe an S-Pen/Note revival — **I just hope they don't re-curve the display**
  (they got it right reducing the curve on the S21 Ultra). The **OnePlus 10 Pro** design is Samsung-inspired but
  its own take; the spec sheet is nuts (80 W wired "SuperVOOC" — the OnePlus-under-Oppo continues).'"
- [2022-01-05] **EV trucks — and a towing 1000-mile test.** "'What I'm most ready for: **EVs, specifically the trio
  of trucks — Rivian R1T (top-5 EV I've driven), F-150 Lightning, Cybertruck** (plus the Hummer). Rivian's
  shipping the R1T (and the R1S SUV is coming). **I'd love to redo the 1000-mile gas-vs-electric road trip — this
  time with trucks, towing something — so we learn something again.**'"
- [2022-01-05] **Skipping CES.** "'This is the **first year in like a decade I'm not going to CES** — as fun as it
  could be, it doesn't seem like the most responsible thing for me to go do right now.' (Mark Gurman is "the only
  one I trust" on the Apple rumors.)'"
