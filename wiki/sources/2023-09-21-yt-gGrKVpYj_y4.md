# Rivian's Electric Delivery Van is Sick!

## Summary

Marques gets rare access to the **Amazon Rivian electric delivery van** ("we see these near our
studio all the time"). It's a study in **function over luxury** — every material chosen to be
lightweight and durable for drivers doing dozens of stops a day — and, quietly, another **"perfect
to go electric"** case: ~150–160 mi range that's totally worry-free because the vans charge overnight
at a fulfillment center, never facing range anxiety. He likes that it introduces people to EVs
positively. Fully Marques-narrated (shot on iPhone 15 Pro).

## Key claims (biography marked ⬥)

- **[2023-09-21]** ⬥ Context: **"near our studio we see these all the time"** (a fulfillment center is
  right by them). Shot on the **iPhone 15 Pro**. (quote)
- **[2023-09-21]** Function-first design: everything is "lightweight and functional, not luxury" — a
  belt-clip key, a **lightweight sliding pocket door** (dozens of uses a day), a **button-operated
  bulkhead door** that auto-opens on park and closes on drive, a big rubberized cargo floor, a huge
  sun shade ("the size of two iPad Pros"), fast heating/cooling ("a driver told me it's one of the best
  seats they've ever had"). He's 6'3" and **walks under the roof without ducking.** (quote)
- **[2023-09-21]** ⭐ The quiet doctrine: **~150–160 mi range, but worry-free** — drivers use 20–40% a
  day and charge overnight at an indoor facility, "ideally no range anxiety at all." (A delivery van is
  a natural fit to go electric — the range downside doesn't bite.) (paraphrase)
- **[2023-09-21]** Branding: the iconic **red-arch "crown" tail light** + big Amazon logo ("unmistakable
  when it pulls up"), and a **gentle chirp lock sound** (not a neighborhood-interrupting horn) — a Rivian
  signature. (paraphrase)
- **[2023-09-21]** ⭐ The bigger point: **"Even outside of buying a personal vehicle, it's awesome that
  people are getting introduced to EVs in a really positive way."** (quote)

## Notable verbatim quotes

- "Everything in here is geared to be as lightweight and functional as possible — it's not about luxury, it's about being functional for the drivers."
- "150 to 160 miles of range every day, and ideally that's worry-free because these come back and charge overnight."
- "It's awesome that people are getting introduced to EVs in a really positive way."
