# Top 5 Android 11 Features: Big Tweaks! (@mkbhd, 2020-06-16)

## Summary
A top-5 walkthrough of the Android 11 beta. The framing: **Android is mature now** — this is "big tweaks," not a
visual overhaul. Highlights the media-controls widget, the notification-shade reorg, and an accessibility feature
he thinks anyone could use.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-06-16] **"Android is mature now" — tweaks, not overhauls.** "'A couple years ago a version update could be
  a **whole visual overhaul; this isn't — it's fine-tuning.** Android as a whole is much more well-baked and mature
  than it was.'" His recurring OS-maturity observation.
- [2020-06-16] **His favorite — the media-controls widget.** "'**My favorite: the media widget at the top of the
  quick-settings pull-down** — an "androidized version of the iPhone's" — so you never scroll far to pause your
  music/podcast (the mini-player color matches the album art). Buggy in beta, not on by default.'"
- [2020-06-16] **Accessibility that anyone could use — Voice Access.** "'**Voice Access** (credit Dieter/The Verge)
  blew my mind — say the *name* of an action, not just a number. **It's an accessibility feature, but anything that
  improves accessibility this much could be used by anyone — and when it gets this good, it's worth it.**'" A
  stated value that accessibility features benefit everyone.
- [2020-06-16] **The #1 change — the notification shade reorg.** "'The classic Android-update headliner: notifications
  split into **conversations / alerting / silent**, plus **bubble (chat-head)** support and power-button smart-home
  toggles.'" His characteristic "the notification shade always changes" note.
- [2020-06-16] **"You need a Pixel to guarantee the latest features."** "'I'm testing on my Pixel 4 — but there's
  **no way to know when a Samsung/HTC/Motorola/OnePlus phone gets it** (carriers decide). If you want to guarantee
  the latest Android features, **you basically have to have a Pixel** — that's been true for a while.'" A recurring
  Pixel-for-updates point.

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "Android as a whole is a lot more well-baked, more mature than it was a couple years ago... this isn't a visual
> overhaul anymore, this is a lot more tweaking, a lot more fine-tuning."
> "Anything that improves accessibility on a phone this much could be used by anyone — and when it gets this good,
> it's worth it."
