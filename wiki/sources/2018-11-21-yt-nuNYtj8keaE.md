# My Favorite Wireless Headphones | 2018! (@mkbhd, 2018-11-21)

## Summary
A wireless-headphone roundup framed by his own conversion: "the jack loss doesn't bother me anymore — I've been
living that wireless life." A dated gear snapshot (Surface Headphones now over the Bose QC35, the M50xBT, Jaybird
X3, AirPods) plus a couple of taste tells.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-11-21] **The wireless-life conversion (context for his jack stance).** "You've heard me say the jack loss
  doesn't bother me anymore — **I've been living that wireless life**, reviewing jack-less phones year after year,
  so you just get on the train. Here are my favorites to make it less painful for you." Frames *why* he made peace
  with the jack.
- [2018-11-21] **Surface Headphones over the Bose QC35 (a switch).** "The **Bose QC35** have been the industry
  standard for noise cancellation since I reviewed them — but I now prefer the **Microsoft Surface Headphones** at
  the same $349: softer/more-modern build, fully **over-ear** (more comfortable on a 6-hour flight), USB-C, and the
  clever **dial (one for volume, one for a noise-cancellation gradient, plus an amplify/transparency mode).** Not
  the best fidelity (weaker mids/highs), but great for Bluetooth streaming." A dated preference change.
- [2018-11-21] **The M50xBT + working-out picks.** The **Audio-Technica M50xBT** = "a revival of a classic — the
  famous M50s with the wire snipped," great for casual listening (no ANC but good passive isolation, cheaper). For
  **working out** he still prefers **Jaybird X3** (wire-in-the-back locks in his ears better than truly-wireless;
  8-hour battery; "**matte black or matte red**, or whatever color you're into"). Appearance tell.
- [2018-11-21] **AirPods + what he's watching.** "On iPhone, **AirPods are still the go-to for portability/
  convenience — some of the most popular earbuds ever** (we've had them **matte black** in our store, wink). Rumors
  of AirPods 2 (maybe wireless charging). I keep hearing about the **Sony 1000XM3** — haven't tried them yet."
  A dated market snapshot.

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "The headphone jack going away doesn't really bother me anymore, because I've been living that wireless life."
> "The Bose have been the industry standard — but I definitely like the Surface Headphones now more than the Bose
> for that money."
> "For working out I still stick with the Jaybird X3 — matte black or matte red, whatever color you're into."
