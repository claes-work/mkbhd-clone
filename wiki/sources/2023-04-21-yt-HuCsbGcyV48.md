sealed, unopened first-generation iPhone** and unboxing it 16 years after its 2007 release

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated 2023-04-21)
- ★ **[Marques, clearly — biography/creator]** He **won an auction for an unopened, sealed

  (dented box, small rips in the wrap, a bit dirty) — which actually **made him more
  confident it was authentic**, not a reseal. *(a signature MKBHD archival stunt.)*

  to do anything. *(documents the archival rigor behind his historical-review videos.)*
