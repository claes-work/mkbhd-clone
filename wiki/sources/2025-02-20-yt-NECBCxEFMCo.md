# Are Foldables Cooked? (@mkbhd, 2025-02-20)

## Summary
A book-style-foldable think-piece via the **OPPO Find N5 — the world's thinnest book-style foldable** (8.93 mm folded,
229 g — basically an iPhone 16 Pro Max that folds in half; razor-thin open). Marques, a foldable defender "since day
one," poses the question the N5 provoked. The engineering marvel delivers on the original dream: **"holy crap, we did
it"** — the 8.1" inner screen has **more screen area than an iPad Mini, in your pocket** (brighter, sharper, higher
refresh). **But the problem is it's a square:** mathematically more screen, yet **anything landscape (a YouTube video,
a keyboard, a browser) is still better on the iPad Mini** because of the black bars. He walks the original success
criterion — a foldable becomes a no-brainer only when it's "just a regular phone that happens to unfold into a tablet"
— and notes that even a hypothetical future fold that's as thin/cheap/capable as a slab **would still be a tough sell
because of the square.** Invoking **Betteridge's law** ("any headline ending in a question mark can be answered no"),
his real answer is nuanced: **"foldables aren't cooked — they're evolving and refining into smaller buckets"** (flip
phones for compactness; book-folds for the multitasking/two-apps-side-by-side crowd) with a real but smaller target
demographic. The N5 has all **three 2025 pillars** (Snapdragon 8 Elite, silicon-carbide battery — 5,600 mAh in that
thinness, ultra-bright dual displays) — though the thin body constrains cooling (**the first Snapdragon 8 Elite phone
he's gotten to overheat**), and cameras stay a notch below flagships (the z-axis limit). His two takeaways: **"we
actually did it — iPad-Mini screens in our pockets,"** and **the next thing to solve is the price coming down.** (He's
sad OnePlus isn't doing a fold this year — "this would make a sick OnePlus Open.")

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2025-02-20] **We did it — more screen than an iPad Mini in your pocket.** "'The original dream was to have an iPad
  Mini you could fold up and put in your pocket. And now we have it — the Find N5's 8.1" inner screen has more screen
  area than an iPad Mini, and it's brighter, sharper, higher refresh. Holy crap, we did it.'"
- [2025-02-20] **But it's a square — the iPad Mini still wins for landscape.** "'The problem is it's a square.
  Mathematically it's more screen, but almost anything you'd actually want a big screen for is still better on the iPad
  Mini — pull up the same YouTube video and the iPad's is bigger thanks to the black bars on the square; the biggest
  keyboard, browser, or media window is a huge iPad advantage.'"
- [2025-02-20] **Foldables aren't cooked — they're refining into smaller buckets.** "'By Betteridge's law, a headline
  ending in a question mark can be answered no. The nuance: at the start we thought foldables might be the no-brainer
  for everyone; they're not, but they still have a place. Flip phones for compactness; book-folds for the
  multitasking/two-apps-side-by-side crowd. They're evolving and refining into smaller buckets — not cooked.'"
- [2025-02-20] **The three 2025 pillars in a fold — but cooling and cameras are constrained.** "'The N5 has all three
  2025 pillars — the Snapdragon 8 Elite, a silicon-carbide battery (5,600 mAh at this thinness — my Pixel 9 Pro Fold
  was 4,650), and ultra-bright dual displays. But this thin body limits cooling volume: I actually got it to overheat
  once — the first time in years I've said that about a new phone, and the first with the Snapdragon 8 Elite. And
  cameras stay a notch below flagships because of the z-axis limit.'"
- [2025-02-20] **The next thing to solve is price.** "'My two learnings: one, we actually did it — iPad-Mini screens in
  our pockets, maximizing this shape. Two, the next thing to solve is the price coming down — this form factor, lower
  price.'"
