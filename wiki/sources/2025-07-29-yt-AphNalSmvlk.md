# iPadOS 26: Ready for Laptop Duty?

## Summary

Marques lives with iPadOS 26 for three weeks, leaving his daily MacBook Pro in the
backpack, to test whether the new window-management overhaul finally makes the iPad a
laptop. His answer reframes the classic question: it's no longer **"can I replace my
laptop?"** (yes, you can) but **"do I want to?"** The new multi-window mode is
dramatically better and very Mac-like, but small walls remain (single audio source,
clunky keyboard case on a lap, ambiguous file locations). His conclusion: the iPad's real
strength is **versatility**, not being a laptop substitute — "the ceiling has been raised,
but it's still an iPad."

## Key claims

- **[2025-07-29]** The reframe: **"The question isn't 'can I replace my laptop with an
  iPad?' Because yes, I can. The question really became 'do I want to?'"** (quote)
- **[2025-07-29]** The new **window management** (menu bar, resize handle, flick-to-half-
  screen, up to **8 windows** vs 4, Mac-style stoplight controls) is "dramatically better
  than before" and "even more [powerful] than Stage Manager was." Crucially it's a
  **mode** you toggle in Control Center — off, the iPad works exactly as it always did.
  (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-07-29]** The remaining **walls**: can't play **two audio sources** at once
  (no background music while watching a video); the keyboard case is still **top-heavy /
  clunky on a lap**; the improved Files app can still be **confusing about where files
  actually are** (AirDropped media doesn't land in Downloads). (paraphrase)
- **[2025-07-29]** Computer-like tasks (e.g. sign a PDF from Slack and send it back) are
  now **possible but higher-friction** — "there's no wall there... it just takes a little
  bit of extra thinking." (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-07-29]** The iPad's genuine advantages: lighter than his laptop by ~a pound,
  a beautiful touch-first single-app focus ("I can't get distracted by Discord or
  Twitter"), cameras, cellular, stylus. (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-07-29]** Buying advice: **"If all you need is a computer, then just get the
  computer"** — it's designed to be better at exactly that (balance, hinge, keyboard). The
  iPad wins when you want something that can be *most* of a computer *and* all these other
  things. (quote)
- **[2025-07-29]** Verdict: **"The ceiling has been raised... but it's still an iPad,"**
  which he means as a compliment — its strength is **versatility**. (quote)
