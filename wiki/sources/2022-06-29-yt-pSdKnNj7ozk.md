# M2 MacBook Pro: Why Does This Exist? (@mkbhd, 2022-06-29)

## Summary
A review staged as **a debate with himself** ("changed my mind") arguing **almost nobody should buy the 13" M2
MacBook Pro.** It's a great computer in a vacuum — but between the new **M2 MacBook Air** and the **14" M1 Pro**,
its reason to exist at this price is squeezed to nearly zero. The problems: a **six-year-old design** (same shell
since the 2016 Touch Bar Intel MBP — old 720p webcam, a Touch Bar, no MagSafe), and the **M2 Air is basically the
same price and better at almost everything** (bigger/brighter display, 1080p webcam, MagSafe + fast charging, more
ports, thinner/lighter). Its **only real edge is active cooling** for sustained workloads — but anyone who needs
that **should just get the 14" M1 Pro** (a whole other level, plus a mini-LED 120 Hz screen, better webcam/speakers,
ports). Its one genuine surprise: **maybe the best battery life in any modern laptop** (16–20 real-world hours).
The verdict punchline: its ideal customer is "someone about to buy the 13" M1 MacBook Pro — **Enterprise.**"

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-06-29] **"Nobody should buy it" — a six-year-old design.** "'This is a great computer, but **I don't think
  anyone should actually buy it.** Apple upgraded the whole lineup (squared design, bigger displays, ports, no
  Touch Bar) — but this refresh got none of it: **the exact same shell as the 2020 M1 MBP, which is the same as the
  2016 Intel Touch Bar MBP — a six-year-old design** (they even reused the M1 boxes with new stickers). Can you
  imagine any other company re-releasing a six-year-old laptop?'"
- [2022-06-29] **The M2 Air is better at almost everything for the same price.** "'In a vacuum it's a perfectly good
  laptop — but next to the **~same-price M2 Air it's worse at basically everything**: the Air has a bigger/
  brighter display, a **1080p webcam (vs a 6-year-old 720p)**, **MagSafe + fast charging** (freeing a port), more
  ports, and it's thinner/lighter. **Why would you buy this? How is it even "Pro" anymore?**'"
- [2022-06-29] **The only real edge is active cooling — but then go 14" M1 Pro.** "'The one reason over the Air is
  the **fan (active cooling) for sustained workloads** — and the M2 is awesome (~10–20% CPU, ~30–40% GPU over M1,
  a better media engine). **But anyone with those sustained workloads should just go straight to the 14" M1 Pro**
  — another level of performance for $200–300 more, plus a mini-LED 120 Hz screen, a better webcam/notch/speakers,
  and actual ports. (And the base 256 GB Pro storage is insulting with clipped SSD speeds — spec it up and you're
  at ~$1,900 anyway.)'"
- [2022-06-29] **The one genuine surprise — the best battery in any laptop.** "'The redeeming factor: **surprisingly
  excellent battery life — maybe the best in any modern laptop (16–20 real-world hours).** But the other Macs
  already have great battery too, so you're not spending just for that and sacrificing everything else.'"
- [2022-06-29] **The verdict — the ideal customer is "Enterprise."** "'The ideal M2 MacBook Pro customer is someone
  who needs slightly better sustained performance than the Air and is willing to sacrifice screen/webcam/speakers/
  ports/thickness/weight to get it — **or someone about to buy the 13" M1 MacBook Pro. Enterprise. That's it.**'"
