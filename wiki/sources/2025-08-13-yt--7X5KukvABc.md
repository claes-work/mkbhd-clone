# Top 5 Sports Tech I Actually Use!

## Summary

A personal favorites video answering a recurring viewer question — is there tech overlap
with his life as an athlete — that doubles as a **major biography source**. Marques
confirms the "professional Ultimate Frisbee player" line in his Twitter bio is literal and
details the four teams he plays for simultaneously. He then walks through the sports tech
he genuinely uses (Apple Watch Ultra 2, Normatec legs, Hyperice, a Xiaomi massage gun,
PowerBeats Pro 2, Nike cleats), consistent with his standing "I only recommend what I
actually use" ethos.

## Key claims (biography-relevant marked ⬥)

- **[2025-08-13]** ⬥ **"I am actually a professional Ultimate Frisbee player"** — the
  Twitter-bio line is "not a joke." (quote)
- **[2025-08-13]** ⬥ He plays for **four teams at once**: **New York Empire** (the pro
  team), **Pride of New York ("PoNY")** for club, **Team USA** for the **World Games**
  ("essentially the Olympics for sports not yet in the Olympics"), and **Team USA Mixed**
  for the beach world championships — "kind of like a second full-time job." (paraphrase)
- **[2025-08-13]** ⬥ He is **off to China to compete for Team USA at the World Games** as
  this video publishes (the World Games are every four years; each country sends 14
  players; "very selective, very high level"). (paraphrase) [See the 2025-08-20 Pixel 10
  video for the gold-medal result.]
- **[2025-08-13]** **Apple Watch Ultra 2** — worn while he lives, trains, and plays; he
  wears it on his **right wrist normally but switches to the left while playing** because
  he doesn't like throwing with something on his right arm. Chosen over Whoop for
  **versatility + gamification** (closing rings, fitness competitions "as silly as it
  sounds, [are] motivating"). (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-08-13]** On accuracy: he's "not convinced the Apple Watch is particularly
  accurate" on calories — "but the real key for me is that it's inaccurate the same way
  every time," so it's still useful for relative tracking. (quote) — a characteristic
  measurement-consistency-over-absolute-accuracy point.
- **[2025-08-13]** **Normatec legs** (Hyperice) for compression recovery between
  tournament days; the **Hyperice knee sleeve** an "MVP" for his patellar tendinitis; a
  Nike-collab compression/heat boot he's "less sold on." (paraphrase)
- **[2025-08-13]** A small **Xiaomi mini massage gun** ("Xiaomi basically makes
  everything") and his workout earbuds of choice, the **PowerBeats Pro 2 in mango** —
  "basically perfect" for working out (seal, hook, light, ANC). (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-08-13]** ⬥ He's worn **the same style of cleat every year since 2007** — the
  lightest soccer cleat he can find, usually **Nike Mercurial Vapor / Superfly**; a pair
  never lasts more than one season. (paraphrase)
- **[2025-08-13]** Ultimate Frisbee is "my home away from being online all the time and
  being super immersed in tech... really important for me to have." (quote)
