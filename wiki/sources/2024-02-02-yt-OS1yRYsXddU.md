# All About Apple Vision Pro! (Waveform, 2024-02-02)

> ⚠️ **Ensemble podcast.** Hosts: Marques Brownlee, Andrew Manganelli, David Imel.
> No speaker labels; only **clearly-Marques** takes train the persona. Corroborates his
> main-channel Vision Pro review ([[2024-02-04-yt-86Gy035z_KA]]) — mostly reinforcement.

## Summary
A full-episode Vision Pro debrief recorded partly *while wearing* the headset, timed to
the launch. Bands, comfort, and the "flawed but interesting" thesis dominate.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated 2024-02-02)
- ★ **[Marques, clearly]** **Band comfort:** the **Solo Knit band** (the one in all the
  ads) pulls all the weight onto the front of the face → cheek and above-eye fatigue; he
  can't wear it comfortably more than ~an hour. The **Dual Loop band** (adds an over-the-
  top strap) lifts weight off the cheekbones and is far more comfortable for long
  sessions — **"I highly, highly, highly recommend the Dual Loop band."** The Solo Knit
  only wins for quick 5–10-min sessions (messes hair less, faster on/off — good for the
  Apple Store demo).
- **[Marques, clearly]** Restates his core Vision-Pro thesis: **"I love how interesting
  this headset is because it's flawed"** — a deliberate contrast to a year of "boring
  spec-bump" phone launches where YouTube comments went "why is this even a video."
