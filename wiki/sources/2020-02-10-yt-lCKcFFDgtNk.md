# Foldable Moto RAZR Unboxing & Second Thoughts! (@mkbhd, 2020-02-10)

## Summary
A retail unboxing + "second thoughts" on the $1,500 folding Moto Razr, now that the shiny-new factor has worn off.
The durable idea is a **form-factor critique**: is a normal-sized phone folding *smaller* the best use of a fold?

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-02-10] **THE FORM-FACTOR CRITIQUE — fold to gain size, not to shrink.** "'My general question: **is a 6.2"
  phone that folds in half the best way to do a folding phone?** I can already fit a 6.2" phone in my pocket. Given
  the choice, **I'd rather a fold that lets me pocket something I couldn't before — a 7-8" iPad-mini-size screen —
  than a normal phone getting even smaller.**'" A sharp, reusable take on what folding should be *for*.
- [2020-02-10] **"Design first, then engineering" — the compromise chain.** "'Motorola told me they **picked the
  iconic Razr design first, then went to engineering to fit what they could into that thin form.** That's why you
  get the cool thin look — but also the compromises: a **split 2,500 mAh battery** (tiny), thin buttons, **15 W
  charging**, no wireless charging, no water resistance, eSIM-only.'" A read on how a design-led constraint cascades.
- [2020-02-10] **The hinge + less crease (the engineering win).** "'The complex hinge **bends the display taut over
  the middle** so you get **less of a crease than the Galaxy Fold** — really smart. (There's a moment mid-fold you
  can see straight behind the display — a durability thing to watch.)'"
- [2020-02-10] **The one-handed problem.** "'Open, it's one-handable (the narrow 21:9 helps). But **I absolutely
  cannot open it with one hand** — so every time you want past the outside screen you need two free hands. Not sure
  yet how annoying that gets.'"
- [2020-02-10] **The satisfying close + the daily-driver plan.** "'Does it end the call when you slam it shut? **It
  does.** There's only one way to find out if $1,500 is worth it — **use it — so I'm going to daily-drive the
  Razr.**' (And won't do a robot fold-test: "we didn't see that actually demonstrate the Fold's durability.")"

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "I would rather have a fold to enable putting something in my pocket that I couldn't before — like a 7- or 8-inch
> screen, an iPad-mini size — than a normal-sized phone getting even smaller."
> "They first picked the iconic Razr design and then went to engineering to figure out what they could fit... not
> the other way around."
