# Best Back to School Tech 2022! (@mkbhd, 2022-08-08)

## Summary
A Best-Buy-sponsored back-to-school buyer's guide — his **first-ever back-to-school video** (he graduated ~7 years
ago; in college everyone got the same HP laptop as part of tuition, so students spiced up the dorm instead). A
lighter ingest, but with biography value. Ten picks, led by a **computer framed as an investment ("a computer I'm
hoping to use for the next 4–6 years")**: a **Dell XPS 15** (Windows), the **M2 MacBook Air** (Mac, "the solid
no-brainer"), or a **Galaxy Chromebook 2** (budget) — the Mac-vs-Windows choice "mostly comes down to whatever
you're majoring in." Plus the **MX Master 3S** mouse, an **iPad Air + Apple Pencil** (great for handwritten math
notes), a **Galaxy S22** (mid-cycle discounts; "we forget the January phones"), desk accessories (wireless-charging
lamps), and NC headphones (the **Sony XM4** over the XM5 because they fold and cost less) + **Beats Studio Buds** for
workouts. Notes his **current daily driver is the S21 Ultra.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-08-08] **His first back-to-school video — the college context.** "'All the years I was in college I never
  made a back-to-school video — I graduated 7 years ago (makes me feel old). **My college gave every undergrad the
  same HP laptop** (engineering/business) or the same MacBook Pro (music-tech/visual-arts) as part of tuition — so
  we did "back to school everything else," spicing up the dorm.'"
- [2022-08-08] **A computer is an investment — three options.** "'The computer is the big one. **The way I think
  about it: this is a computer I'm hoping to use for the next 4–6 years straight — an investment in the future.**
  Windows: the **XPS 15** (splurge for the 4K OLED / RTX, handles demanding coding/rendering). Mac: the **M2
  MacBook Air, the solid no-brainer** (the new chip/screen/design/MagSafe are worth it over the M1 Air). Budget:
  a **Galaxy Chromebook 2** if your major works in web apps. **The Mac-vs-Windows choice mostly comes down to
  whatever you're majoring in — certain software just works with certain computers.**'"
- [2022-08-08] **The accessories — mouse, iPad for math notes.** "'One mouse: the **MX Master 3S** (the quieter
  update — the most versatile, easy-to-recommend mouse for years). And if a stylus/tablet fits your note-taking,
  the **iPad Air + 2nd-gen Apple Pencil** — **I always give the pencil a shot; for math notes with formulas and
  structure, keeping up on a keyboard doesn't cut it, so a virtual notebook is great** (the M1 Air; iOS 16 is iOS
  16, so an M2 version wouldn't matter much).'"
- [2022-08-08] **The phone — a Galaxy S22 (and his daily driver).** "'A new phone: the **base Galaxy S22** — now
  mid-cycle, so it's discounting. **We tend to forget the January phones, but the S22 still holds up** (top
  cameras/display, great battery, DeX). **My own daily driver lately has been an S21 Ultra** — maybe worth a
  separate video.'"
- [2022-08-08] **Desk + headphones.** "'Desk accessories (a wireless-charging Audi light lamp; a Belkin multi-device
  charger — **pro tip: for a Series 7+ Apple Watch, get a charger that supports the new 15%-faster speed**). And
  headphones — **the Sony XM4 (not the XM5) as the deal: they fold, cost less, and sound about as good** — plus
  **Beats Studio Buds** for workouts (the fit stays in).'"
