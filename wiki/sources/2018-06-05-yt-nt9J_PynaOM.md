# Top 5 WWDC 2018 Announcements! (@mkbhd, 2018-06-05)

## Summary
A recap of WWDC 2018 — a software-only year (no hardware, unlike 2017's iMac Pro/HomePod tease). His Top 5:
Apple TV (minor), the AR push, watchOS 5, **macOS Mojave (dark mode)**, and **iOS 12** at #1. Useful mostly for
his enthusiasms (dark mode, AR/Measure) and a couple of taste-tells.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-06-05] **A software-only WWDC.** "Every year we hope for a hardware nugget — last year we got the iMac Pro
  sneak peek and HomePod. This year: **zero new hardware.**" Sets expectations; opens with a self-aware "hey Siri,
  any hardware today? — no, of course not" gag.
- [2018-06-05] **#4 AR — "a big push."** New **USDZ** shared-AR file format (Adobe support), ARKit 2.0, and the new
  **Measure app** (an AR ruler — "I'm going to start measuring how tall people and buildings are"). The most
  technically impressive demo: **shared multi-device AR** (two iPads seeing the same scene from different angles).
  Genuine enthusiasm for the trajectory.
- [2018-06-05] **#2 macOS Mojave — DARK MODE is the headline.** "The coolest feature by far is **dark mode** — and
  it's thorough (Finder, apps, dock, everything). **That's going to look super sick on the space gray iMac Pro.**
  Side note: **they should do an iOS dark mode too**, especially now there are OLEDs on the iPhone X." A clear
  personal want (predates iOS dark mode by a year). Also: Desktop Stacks ("not me — I keep a **clean, pristine
  desktop**") and iOS apps coming to Mac.
- [2018-06-05] **#1 iOS 12 — crowd-pleasers.** Performance focus (back to the iPhone 5s); **grouped notifications
  "finally"** ("iOS notifications are a fire hose of redundant garbage"); **group FaceTime** (up to 32);
  **Siri Shortcuts** ("the one thing Bixby had going for Samsung — sorry, Bixby"); **Memoji** + convincing eye
  tracking. "I'm putting iOS 12 on my phone right after this video just for grouped notifications."
- [2018-06-05] **The recurring Apple TV gripe.** Praises the new ISS-shot 4K screensavers but reiterates: "**you
  still can't watch YouTube in 4K on the Apple TV 4K** — a Google/Apple stalemate. Frustrating." A consistent
  pointed complaint.
