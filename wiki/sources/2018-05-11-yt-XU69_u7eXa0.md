# Google Pixel 2 Revisited: Android P Beta! (@mkbhd, 2018-05-11)

## Summary
A **"Revisited"** long-term look at the Pixel 2 XL — the phone that spent the most time in his pocket (viewers
pointed this out after the iPhone X Revisited). Verdict: aged really well, **still his preferred camera "period,"**
and still worth buying. Also seeds the (upcoming) Blind Camera Test and a few durable positions.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-05-11] **BIOGRAPHY — his actual daily driver.** "The only phone in my pocket more than that iPhone X was
  this — the **panda Pixel 2 XL**. When people ask what I use most, I keep coming back to this one." A dated
  confirmation of his primary phone (white, no case, "naked").
- [2018-05-11] **Still the best camera — "period."** "Single camera on the back, and this is **still the best camera
  in any smartphone, period** — even now that it doesn't have the highest DxOMark score anymore. It's still my
  preferred camera." Reaffirms his software-first Pixel-2 stance; **teases doing an updated Blind Test** of the best
  smartphone cameras.
- [2018-05-11] **"A camera that encourages you to take photos."** "Most of the photos I've shared on Twitter/
  Instagram lately came from this — I used to reach for a **Sony A7R**. This is one of those cameras that just
  **encourages you to take photos**, and they're often great." A nice articulation of why a good phone camera
  matters (lowers friction). Notes he's a **"zoom with my feet" guy** (rarely misses telephoto); Pixel's
  software portrait mode beats most despite one lens.
- [2018-05-11] **Front-facing stereo speakers are "severely underrated."** "As we all move to bezel-less phones,
  **speakers will suffer** — the days of big front-facing stereo speakers are pretty much over (maybe the Razer
  Phone 2 is the last dinosaur). I'm enjoying it while it lasts." A design-tradeoff lament.
- [2018-05-11] **The wireless-charging "moonshot."** Loves the durable mostly-metal body but wishes it had wireless
  charging: "**A moonshot goal of mine — even a little glass, and just set it on a mat and it works. I don't need
  the whole back to be glass** for that." A concrete personal tech wish.
- [2018-05-11] **The screen is the one real weakness.** "My biggest annoyance since day one — the panel isn't the
  brightest or most color-accurate, has a slight blue cast. **You have to calibrate to it** (I use Boosted, not
  Saturated). When a phone has a notable quality like this, you have to remember it when editing photos others
  will see on different screens. **Not ideal.**" Honest about the well-documented panel drama.
- [2018-05-11] **Pixel 3 wishlist.** Build on the already-great camera; get a **quality OLED (maybe a Samsung
  panel)**; catch up on **modern design** ("which probably means a notch — that's where we're at in 2018"). Still
  recommends buying the Pixel 2 now (price hasn't dropped) unless you can wait for October.
