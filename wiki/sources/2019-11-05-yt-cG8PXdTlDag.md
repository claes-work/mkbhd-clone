# AirPods Pro Review: Imperfectly Perfect! (@mkbhd, 2019-11-05)

## Summary
Review of the $250 AirPods Pro. The verdict — captured in the title, **"imperfectly perfect"** — is the essence of
his **"if I could only pick one"** framework: technically-better earbuds exist (Sony XM3), but AirPods Pro win on
everyday-use fundamentals + ecosystem, so they're the one he'd pick.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-11-05] **The silicone tips solve the #1 problem — staying in.** "'The silicone tips are a **major win for
  people like me who literally can't wear regular AirPods for more than three minutes** before they slide out.
  These stay in, create a seal, and the **vent equalizes pressure** so the seal isn't uncomfortable — it's easy to
  forget you're wearing them.'"
- [2019-11-05] **Noise cancellation is the biggest strength; sound is seal-dependent.** "'ANC is **really
  impressive for earbuds**, best with consistent white noise (coffee shop, car, plane). **Sound: off it's just
  like regular AirPods; on it's dramatically better** — mostly because you finally have a seal.' (Defers to Snazzy
  Labs's Quinn for the audiophile take: 'mid-heavy, I agree.')"
- [2019-11-05] **THE FRAMEWORK — "if I could only pick one."** "'Are they the best wireless earbuds? **Yes but no
  but yes but no.** The **Sony WF-1000XM3 are technically better** — better sound, better ANC, longer battery,
  better app. But **AirPods Pro nail so many everyday-use things** — the **case size** (still pocketable when
  rivals aren't), the H1 instant pairing, blending into iOS 13. **If I could only pick one pair of earbuds to do
  all this, I'd pick AirPods Pro** — and a lot of people will.'" A recurring decision lens (cf.
  [[2019-06-27-yt-JdTBIHX-r0M]]).
- [2019-11-05] **"Imperfectly perfect" — the thesis.** "'They're **not audiophile-grade, not great dedicated
  workout headphones, not the perfect flying headphones** — but if you want **one pair to do everything, this is
  it.** Imperfectly perfect.'" The everyday-fundamentals-beat-spec-superiority argument.
- [2019-11-05] **The nitpicks.** "'**No volume control on the buds at all** — my least favorite thing; I don't
  want to ask Siri every time. Only **~4 hrs** battery (not always a full flight). **Lightning, not USB-C.
  Proprietary tips** (can't use third-party foam) — but $4 replacements from Apple, reasonably priced.'"
