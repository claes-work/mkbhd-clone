# This Car Has No Key! (Genesis GV60 Performance)

## Summary

Marques spends a week with the **Genesis GV60 Performance** having **never carried a key fob** — using
**face unlock (a camera in the A-pillar) + a fingerprint reader + an Ultra-Wideband phone key.** His
verdict: face unlock is neat but too variable (height, weather, a frozen camera) to fully trust; the
**UWB phone key is the future** ("~100% success, and it knows which driver you are"), far better than
the flaky Bluetooth phone keys. Fully Marques-narrated; contains a memorable biography anecdote.

## Key claims (biography marked ⬥)

- **[2023-06-27]** ⬥ CONTEXT: opens **"shout out to Miles — you guys already know about the miles-per-
  hour intro"** (an @AutoFocus co-host reference). "I've never driven a Genesis before — the high-end
  Hyundai." The GV60 reminds him of the Kia EV6 ("the EV6 GT is still more fun to drive"). (quote)
- **[2023-06-27]** ⚠️ Face unlock: neat and works sometimes, but **"too many variables — everyone's a
  different height (this camera barely gets me at 6'3" if I hunch), and in weather (a mask, rain, or a
  frozen-over camera) it won't work. I much prefer the phone key."** (quote)
- **[2023-06-27]** ⭐ UWB phone key: **"unlike Bluetooth phone keys (Tesla/Rivian ~75–85% success —
  often I stand there and nothing happens), this uses Ultra-Wideband from the iPhone — ~100% success,
  quick, and it knows *which* driver you are, so it pre-adjusts the car's settings."** (My Apple Watch
  even tells me it unlocked with the phone key.) (quote)
- **[2023-06-27]** ⬥ BIOGRAPHY (memorable anecdote): **"A couple weeks ago I drove the Rivian R1T to the
  studio using the Bluetooth phone key — no fob with me. While I was there I got an email that the phone
  key system was down. I was literally stuck at the studio for a couple hours until they fixed it."**
  (Why he trusts UWB more.) (quote)
- **[2023-06-27]** Notes: a satin-white factory paint, a "crystal ball" gear selector ("between gaudy
  and neat"), a drawer glovebox, B&O speakers; ~220 mi range. "I'd pick the EV6 GT (more fun), but if
  you prefer softer/more-luxury, this." (paraphrase)

## Notable verbatim quotes

- "I got an email that the phone-key system was down. I was literally stuck at the studio for a couple hours."
- "Face unlock is neat when it works — but there are too many variables to be seamless every time."
