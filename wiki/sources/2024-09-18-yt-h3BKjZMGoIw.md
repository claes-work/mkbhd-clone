# iPhone 16/16 Pro Unboxing: End of an Era! (@mkbhd, 2024-09-18)

## Summary
The traditional day-one iPhone unboxing, hunting for the "one single difference" vs. last year — and it's a subtraction:
**no stickers in the box this year. "Truly the end of an era — they just can't stop removing things."** Otherwise the
familiar braided USB-C cable, no SIM tool; new poppy base-phone colors (Ultramarine "the flagship color," pink Plus)
and a new "Desert Titanium" Pro. His first-week FAQ notes: the **base phones are the same size, but both Pro phones grew
(6.3" / 6.9")** — the Pro Max is now "borderline tablet-sized, the biggest phone they've ever made"; the new corner
cameras make them **"big-time table rockers"**; **8 GB RAM across the entire line** now (for the eventual Apple
Intelligence + a stronger neural engine); and — the rant-for-another-day — they **ship with iOS 18.0, exactly zero Apple
Intelligence features** despite "built from the ground up for Apple Intelligence." The centerpiece is the new **Camera
Control**: "Apple refused to call it a button in the keynote and doubled down in executive interviews" — but **it is a
real, physically-clicking button** (with a capacitive touch surface + vibration motor that mimics a half-press) that
only controls camera things; finicky momentum-physics swiping; third-party apps (Kino, Snapchat) can hook in. He
covers how **cases** handle it (a cutout, or Apple's/Beats' capacitance-transferring glass insert) — and clocks the
oddity that **Beats now makes iPhone cases** ("since when does Beats make cases?"). He wishes it were also Touch ID.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2024-09-18] **The one difference: no stickers — the end of an era.** "'Upholding the unboxing tradition to find one
  single difference vs last year: there are no stickers included in the iPhone 16 box at all. Truly the end of an era.
  They just can't stop removing things — they can't help themselves.'"
- [2024-09-18] **The Pro phones grew; 8 GB RAM across the line; table rockers.** "'The base phones are the same size,
  but both Pro phones grew — 6.3" and 6.9". The Pro Max is now borderline tablet-sized, the biggest phone they've ever
  made, with more battery (good). All the phones now have 8 GB of RAM (there used to be different amounts Pro vs
  non-Pro) — clearly for the AI models and a stronger neural engine. And the new corner cameras make them big-time
  table rockers.'"
- [2024-09-18] **Ships with iOS 18.0 — zero Apple Intelligence.** "'Apple gave us a lot of talk about these being the
  first phones built from the ground up for Apple Intelligence — yet they all launch with iOS 18.0, which has exactly
  zero Apple Intelligence features out of the box. But that's a rant for another day.'"
- [2024-09-18] **Camera Control — it's a real button Apple refused to call a button.** "'Apple refused to call it a
  button in the keynote and doubled down in executive interviews. But it is an actual moving, clicking physical button,
  called Camera Control — plus a capacitive touch surface with a vibration motor that mimics a half-press. It only
  controls camera things (open, shoot, hold to record; double-tap to flip zoom/styles/tone), the swipes have momentum
  physics, and third-party apps (Kino, soon Snapchat) can hook in. I wish it were also Touch ID.'"
- [2024-09-18] **Beats makes iPhone cases now.** "'Cases handle the new button two ways: a cutout, or Apple's insert
  with glass that transfers the capacitance through the case to keep it flush — and the Beats by Dre cases already have
  it. Since when does Beats make cases? Why does Beats make cases? Maybe now they're just iPhone accessories.'"
