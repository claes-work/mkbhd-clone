# Google Pixel 4a Review: Simple and Clean! (@mkbhd, 2020-08-03)

## Summary
Review of the $350 Pixel 4a — "Google's budget phone with an excellent camera, and that's really it." The formula
is **keep it simple**: one size/color/spec, one great rear camera, no useless extras. The camera is the win; the
performance is the concern.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-08-03] **"Keep it simple" — the whole thesis.** "'The 3a was the budget phone with a great camera; **double
  down. Everything here is clean and simple**: one-piece matte-black plastic (no seams), **one size, one color, one
  spec**, a flat 5.8" 1080p 60 Hz OLED, the fingerprint **still on the back — the tried-and-true place** (Nexus 5X
  → Pixel 4a), and a **headphone jack.** No high refresh isn't a dealbreaker at this price.'"
- [2020-08-03] **THE CAMERA — the budget-camera king.** "'A **single 12 MP rear camera** — "resembles the multi-
  camera setups without spending on extra useless cameras, nice." **Great Pixel-style confident, contrasty, HDR
  photos with great color** (Night Sight still clean) — **great photos for a $350 phone.** I don't miss the
  ultrawide much, and **I don't miss the depth sensor or the 2 MP macro at all** — keeping it simple just works.'"
  Reinforces the no-useless-cameras + budget-camera-king thread.
- [2020-08-03] **The downside — performance (and longevity concern).** "'The **SD730G isn't very smooth** — 60 Hz
  shouldn't be a death sentence (phones were all 60 Hz fine a couple years ago), so **the chip is more to blame.
  The phone just doesn't feel fast**, which concerns me for how long it'll last — the value-over-time question you
  face against the iPhone SE.'" His honest weak-point call.
- [2020-08-03] **Battery surprise + the plastic-bends note.** "'Pleasantly surprised — **5 hrs SOT, ending days at
  10-20%** (a 1080p 60 Hz screen helps). The **plastic unibody bends a bit** — "I'm predicting JerryRig snaps it" —
  but it **won't shatter like glass.**'"
- [2020-08-03] **The verdict + the competitive hope.** "'It **doubled down on everything the 3a had and lowered the
  price — $350 is insane for this much phone. It undercuts the Nord and the iPhone SE** (which are better
  performers / better video, but you get Google features + a better stills camera here). **I love that Google's
  getting competitive — I just hope it translates into a true high-end Pixel 5 flagship** (their flagship track
  record isn't great).'"

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "It resembles the multi-camera setups... but without spending the extra money for extra useless cameras. Nice."
> "Keeping it simple just works great here — I don't think anyone's going to be mad this isn't quad cameras."
