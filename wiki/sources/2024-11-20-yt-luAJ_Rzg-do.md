# Acura ZDX Type-S - It's about the little things

## Summary

Marques reviews the **Acura ZDX Type-S** — a ~$75k sporty electric crossover (a GM-platform sibling
of the Honda Prologue / Chevy Blazer EV) — and frames it around **how the small livability
differences add up** once you live with a car in this now-crowded segment. His verdict: a very
livable, well-equipped, genuinely sporty (~500hp) package let down mainly by range (~280mi). Fully
Marques-narrated; notable for two small biography/taste tells. Fully Marques-narrated.

## Key claims (biography/taste marked ⬥)

- **[2024-11-20]** Framing: at $75k for an electric crossover to commute / haul family / never road-
  trip, "there's the Honda Prologue, the Chevy Blazer, and this — kind of all the same thing with a
  different skin, but this is the sporty one (Type-S)." Less range for the sportiness (~280mi, 190kW).
  (paraphrase)
- **[2024-11-20]** ⬥ TASTE/BIO: **"I've reviewed a few Acuras… I like the NSX, so I have a soft spot
  for Acura."** And: **"this color, called Tiger Eye, is maybe my favorite color I've seen in a car all
  of this year — great name, great color."** (repeated; "more cars of this color please"). (quote)
- **[2024-11-20]** ⭐ Thesis: **"There are so many cars of this shape and size that the differences
  start to feel subtle — but when you live with them, they add up to a big difference."** Wins on the
  little things: a hooded gauge screen that kills glare, a genuinely useful physical volume knob, a
  regen paddle + toggleable one-pedal driving, HUD, heated/ventilated seats. (quote)
- **[2024-11-20]** ⚠️ The little misses: **only one wireless charger** ("a lot of them have two"); a
  closed center console he "can't just drop a grocery bag into" (he prefers wide-open EV consoles); a
  low reversing camera; **Bang & Olufsen speakers "just okay… nothing to write home about"** (he flags
  he may be biased by "another car in this price range with insanely good speakers" — i.e. the
  Polestar 3). (quote + paraphrase)
- **[2024-11-20]** ⬥ Self-reference: fits behind his own **6'3"** driving position with "plenty of
  space." Verdict: **"very livable… as a package this one's really good — I just wish it had more range
  so I could road-trip it once in a while, but I don't think I would."** (paraphrase + quote)
