# iPhone 17 Review: No Asterisks!

## Summary

The base iPhone 17 review — opened by his recurring **"Honesty Wireless" salesman skit**
(the world's most honest salesman tells a lottery winner to just buy the plain 17, not the
Pro Max). Thesis: this is **"the best base iPhone since they added the Pro to the lineup,"**
and a straight-up good deal "no asterisks." Apple finally gave the base phone the long-
withheld flagship features — a 120Hz VRR display, a genuinely innovative square selfie
camera, bigger battery, 256GB base — which paradoxically makes the pricier Pros harder to
justify.

## Key claims

- **[2025-10-01]** Cold-open **"Honesty Wireless"** skit: even a money-no-object lottery winner
  should "just buy the 17 and use it the exact same way you were going to" rather than a $2,000
  Pro Max. (paraphrase)
- **[2025-10-01]** Thesis: **"This is the best base iPhone since they added the Pro to the
  lineup, and I don't think it's particularly close."** Companies normally keep one or two
  features exclusive to the Pro so the phones don't overlap — "but this year they just went ahead
  and added all the stuff we thought they should, and didn't half-ass it." (quote)
- **[2025-10-01]** **Finally a top-tier display**: up to **3,000 nits**, thin bezels, and — most
  importantly — **120Hz variable refresh** (down to 1Hz, enabling always-on), plus PWM dimming
  and Ceramic Shield 2. "We can finally say goodbye to the $800 60Hz iPhone... dare I say a
  flagship-quality display finally." (quote)
- **[2025-10-01]** **The new selfie camera is the standout — "everyone should copy it."** A 24MP
  **square sensor** ("Center Stage") that shoots an 18MP vertical or horizontal crop and
  **auto-rotates to landscape for group selfies without turning the phone**; quality is
  noticeably better than the 16 Pro, and the extra sensor area gives **much smoother selfie-video
  stabilization** even without action mode. It's on **all** the new iPhones (base, Air, Pro).
  (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-10-01]** **Better battery** (every 2025 iPhone has a physically larger cell; the base
  also gains efficiency from the new VRR display) — **~5h screen-on time**, and it charges
  noticeably faster; plus the **A19** ("not the Pro, but one of the most powerful single-core
  chips on the planet") and **doubled base storage: 128→256GB**, which effectively makes every
  tier feel $100 cheaper. (paraphrase)
- **[2025-10-01]** **Value math**: the base iPhone 17 (256GB) is **$100 cheaper than the
  equivalent $900 Pixel 10** yet has a better screen, main camera, selfie camera, battery life,
  and a far more powerful chip — "just one less telephoto camera." "The new iPhone 17 is just a
  straight-up good deal now. **No asterisks.**" (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-10-01]** Minor complaints (not major missing features anymore): can't record the full
  square selfie sensor in the stock app; the new dual-capture bakes in the front-feed position;
  **USB 2.0** transfer speeds; **no telephoto** in a bracket where rivals have one; and **still
  no real Apple Intelligence improvement**. The **camera control button** (Apple was
  "suspiciously quiet" about it) gives him "3D Touch vibes — I wouldn't be shocked if it quietly
  disappeared in two years." (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-10-01]** The core **market effect**: **"when the cheapest phone in a lineup is a really
  good deal, it makes all the more expensive versions feel like worse deals — because they are."**
  Why spend $300 more on a Pro? You get the vapor chamber + telephoto + Pro chip/ProRes — things
  **only actual pros (gamers, long-video shooters) will notice**, which he says makes the Pro
  phones "actually appropriately named." **"This is the phone most people should get."** (quote)
