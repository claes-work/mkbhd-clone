# OnePlus 7: Way Under the Radar! (@mkbhd, 2019-06-21)

## Summary
A look at the overshadowed standard OnePlus 7 ("a refreshed OnePlus 6t," his 2018 phone of the year). Two
throughlines: OnePlus's **brand repositioning from budget to premium**, and — stated explicitly again —
**competition is good for consumers.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-06-21] **"A OnePlus 6t-t" — steady, not exciting.** "No Pro features: teardrop notch (not pop-up), 1080p
  60 Hz (not 1440p/90 Hz), 20 W (not 30 W warp), regular vibration motor, 6/8 GB (not 12), and **one fewer camera
  — dropped the ultrawide for a telephoto**, which I disagree with: **'the ultrawide is way more useful and more
  fun than a telephoto, and everyone's realizing this in 2019.'**" Reiterates his ultrawide-over-telephoto stance.
- [2019-06-21] **REASON 1 it's under the radar — OnePlus's deliberate repositioning (the one he doesn't love).**
  "'OnePlus is actively moving their brand positioning from **straight-up budget to much more premium** — the 7
  Pro legitimizes their more expensive products. They're going from **flagship killer to "hey, we also make a
  flagship."**'" A sharp industry-strategy read.
- [2019-06-21] **REASON 2 — competition put it there (the one he loves) → THE THESIS.** "'There has **never been
  more competition** at this premium mid-tier price' (ZenFone 6, Galaxy A70, Redmi K20 Pro, Pixel 3a...). The
  OnePlus 7 is now 'an **option** to import, not the clear front-runner.' It 'represents a very crowded radar,
  which **if you know what I know about competition, is a good thing for all of us.**'" Explicit pro-competition
  value (cf. [[2019-05-22-yt-qZGpmWrVSaU]], [[2019-07-02-yt-YPln3JP_gKs]]).
- [2019-06-21] **The flat-display + smaller-size preference.** "Certain things are **better** than the Pro for
  some people: **smaller/more pocketable** (the 7 Pro is 'a little on the big side'), and a **flat display** — 'I'm
  not the biggest fan of the curved glass, the extra glare and accidental touches.'" More of his stated taste.
