# Techtober Reactions: Rivian, MacBook Pro, and Pixel 6! (@TheStudio, 2021-11-02)

> ⚠️ ATTRIBUTION: **Marques frames and appears** throughout ("the stream Marques is watching");
> mixed with heavy team banter. Only Marques-attributed lines are subject data; the "resident

## Summary
A Techtober-week roundup: hands-on with the Rivian R1T, live reaction to Apple's Oct-2021 M1 Pro/
Max MacBook Pro event + unboxing, and the walk-up to the Pixel 6 event. Marques's genuine takes
are on EVs, the return of MacBook ports, and his enduring Mac-Pro wait.

## Key claims (Marques-attributed only)
- [2021-11-02] **Rivian R1T — instant enthusiasm:** "I could not be more in love with this truck,
  I want this so bad." (ev-cars — predates his eventual R1T ownership; see [[2022-08-25-yt-OKjyz7Jp4kA]])
- [2021-11-02] **The non-Tesla-EV charging problem (consistent thesis):** the R1T's worst thing is
  "the same thing as every other non-Tesla EV — it's the charging network," "a really hard sell
  right now especially after our road trip and seeing the charging network in the tri-state area."
  (ev-cars — his standing take that charging infrastructure, not the vehicle, is the EV blocker)
- [2021-11-02] **2021 MacBook Pro — the ports comeback:** delight that Apple brought back HDMI +
  SD-card slot + MagSafe and killed the Touch Bar ("I'm more pumped for the SD card slot than
  anything"); jokes as a "benevolent dictator... I have brought back everything you wanted." On
  AirPods 3 he shrugs — "I don't really care, I just want new MacBooks... but I still want Apple
  to put USB-C in everything." (tech-reviews, consumer-tech-culture)
- [2021-11-02] **Still waiting for the Apple-Silicon Mac Pro (the recurring beat):** even amid the
  M1 Max hype, "I still need to see that Apple Silicon Mac Pro — it's coming." (consumer-tech-
  culture — same tension as [[2022-03-09-yt-E3QJt43zkaY]])
