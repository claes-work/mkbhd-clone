# The MKBHD Gear Tour 2019! (@mkbhd, 2019-04-12)

## Summary
The definitive production-gear tour — a biography/production goldmine detailing exactly the cameras, lenses,
lighting, audio, and motion tools he uses, plus his philosophy ("is this overkill? yeah — you can make great
videos with none of this"). For gearheads; a deep record of his 2019 setup.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-04-12] **The premise — "overkill, and I've accepted almost no one will watch this."** "This started when I
  found the **Sennheiser MKH 416** shotgun mic and loved it — but **who cares about a $900 mic?** So I just used it.
  Same with a tripod, a slider. **You can make great videos with none of this** — but for my fellow gearheads,
  here's all of it." His honest, self-aware framing.
- [2019-04-12] **CAMERAS + the "big three" lenses.** **RED DSMC2 bodies (Monstro 8K sensor)** — "more camera than I
  can ever use, but I love them"; the talking-head lens is the **Sigma 24–35 Cine** (covers full-frame). His "big
  three": the **Zeiss Otus 55** (super sharp, but ~2 ft minimum focus), the **Canon 100mm macro** ("puts you closer
  than your eye — I see dust I have to re-clean"), plus the everyday **Sigma 18–35**. Plus niche lenses: the vintage
  **Helios 44** ($50, "the most spirally anamorphic bokeh in a non-anamorphic lens"), the Canon 11–24 (car
  interiors), a 15mm macro tilt-shift ("minimum focus is touching the glass").
- [2019-04-12] **LIGHTING + the "everything on wheels" theme.** Key: an **ARRI SkyPanel S60-C** (huge 4-ft softbox,
  "makes any color, insanely bright" — a Sun replacement); fill: an **Aputure 300D**; **Quasar Science RGB tubes**
  and a **Westcott Flex** for accents. A recurring studio principle: **"put as many things on wheels as possible."**
- [2019-04-12] **AUDIO.** The **MKH 416 undersling** (just below the sight line) into a **Sound Devices MixPre-6**
  recorder; **Sanken COS-11D** lavs (via Electrosonics recorders) "for a collab or interview — one for me, one for
  Lew or Bill or Elon."
- [2019-04-12] **MOTION — the camera ROBOT + the discipline.** The **Motorized Precision robot** (a RED Epic-W +
  motorized **FIZ** — focus/iris/zoom — keyframed via an **Xbox controller** and Windows software): "the crazy
  orbital moves a human couldn't do." His self-imposed rule: **"it's a specific challenge of mine NOT to use the
  robot all the time"** (so it stays special). Plus the **Sirui Magic Carpet Pro slider** (modular track), the
  **pedestal** (a crab-steering rolling tripod, discovered on the Model 3 shoot — the Apple Watch hallway shot),
  a **Kessler pocket jib** (top-down set), the **Sachtler flowtech** tripod ("the greatest ever — the leg
  adjustment"), and the **DJI Ronin 2** (car follow-shots; the factory tour with Elon). The **top-down set** trick:
  a big soft **Kino Flo** through a sheet + dbrand-skinned aluminum "surface" sheets.
- [2019-04-12] **The point — "tell the story in a more interesting way."** "Most of it is ridiculous — a camera on
  a robot doing keyframed moves — but it's **too much fun not to share.** The whole point of all this gear is to
  **tell a story or show a visual in a more interesting way.**" B&H sponsored ("the go-to for video gear since
  before college — right across the river in NYC").

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "Is this overkill? Yeah. A lot of this stuff is — you can make great videos with a lot less than this, with none
> of this honestly, and people do every single day."
