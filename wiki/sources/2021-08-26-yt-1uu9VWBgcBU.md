# Samsung Z Flip 3 Review: The First Big Step! (@mkbhd, 2021-08-26)

## Summary
Full review of the $999 Z Flip 3 — framed as the **first big step** toward the folding-endgame he's stated since
the start: **for foldables to catch on, folding-in-half has to become "just another feature" — a normal phone in
every other way, with no huge compromises or price tag.** The Flip 3 delivers a normal-phone experience that folds
smaller, and it's **"fine" everywhere (cameras, battery, calls) — which is itself an accomplishment for a folding
screen.** The durable verdict: **"a folding phone I can actually start recommending — getting this far is
crucial."** The next fix: minimizing the crease.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-08-26] **THE FOLDING-ENDGAME restatement.** "'What I've said since the start: **if foldables are the
  future, the tech has to get so good that folding-in-half is just another feature — no huge compromises, no
  massive price tag, a normal phone in every other way, except it also folds.** That's when everyone starts trying
  them. The Flip's ambition is simple: a normal phone that folds shut.'" A promotable crystallization.
- [2021-08-26] **The design + build — the panda-Pixel-2 look, a sturdy hinge, IPX8.** "'The refreshed design
  reminds me of **the classic Panda Pixel 2 (one of my favorite designs ever)** — squared sides, two-tone, matte
  black available. The hinge holds almost any angle then magnets snap it shut with a satisfying clap; **now IPX8
  water resistant** (keep it from sand/dust); much better stereo speakers. (Gripes: fingerprinty; the volume
  rocker's too high to reach one-handed.)'"
- [2021-08-26] **120 Hz finally + the crease is the next fix.** "'The 6.7" inside display is finally **120 Hz —
  my biggest ask; it doesn't seem right to charge flagship money for 60 Hz** (Apple somehow still pulls it off).
  But **the crease is still here, right where your finger runs while scrolling — visually and physically there;
  I scroll extra to move text off the middle.** Minimizing the crease should be the next big improvement (plus
  trimming the plastic edge).'"
- [2021-08-26] **The bigger cover screen — 4× the area.** "'The cover screen is **4× the last one's area** (not
  Razr-sized, but genuinely changes how you use it) — six widgets (music/weather/timer), notifications, a decent
  camera viewfinder on the primary cameras. (Missed opportunity: Google Assistant on the outside — it uses Bixby
  voice instead.)'"
- [2021-08-26] **"Fine everywhere" is the accomplishment.** "'The cameras are fine (flush — I love that), the
  battery is fine (~4 hrs screen-on), call quality fine, heat fine. **It might sound like I'm disappointed saying
  "it's fine" — but that's kind of an accomplishment on a phone whose screen folds in half.** We're working toward
  no major compromises, delivering the one promise of folding down to something more pocketable — and it does it.'"
- [2021-08-26] **THE VERDICT — the recommendable foldable; getting this far is crucial.** "'It's not as ambitious
  as the Fold 3, but **it gives you a normal-phone experience and folds in half — very close to what I've been
  saying since the beginning of the foldables thing. This is a folding phone I can actually start recommending to
  people, and the fact that we got this far is crucial for them to move forward.**'"
