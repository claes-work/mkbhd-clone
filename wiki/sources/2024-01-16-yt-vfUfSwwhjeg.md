# Can A Gaming Phone Go Mainstream? (ROG Phone 8 Pro) (@mkbhd, 2024-01-16)

## Summary
A review of the **Asus ROG Phone 8 Pro** as the first real attempt to **package a great gaming phone for the
masses** — because "the thing that makes gaming phones good at gaming also makes them great daily drivers." Asus
toned it down: **thinner bezels + the first-ever hole-punch** (more one-handable), **thinner + slightly smaller
battery**, its **first IP68 and first wireless charging** in an ROG Phone, while keeping the gamer stuff (a
2,500-nit 165 Hz screen, Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 with up to 24 GB, the second side USB-C, shoulder triggers, a headphone
jack, and a toned-down **functional back "AniMe Vision" dot-matrix display** — "certifiably gimmicky but I like it,
even more useful than the Nothing Phone"). The verdict hinges on **the one hump they couldn't clear: the camera** —
in a phone sold as mainstream, **the camera goes from an afterthought to "one of the most important things,"** and
this one (the same IMX890 as $600–700 phones, over-aggressive HDR/motion-blur) "is good but not over that hump."
Also flagged: **"you're going to start noticing AI features in basically every phone this year with the Snapdragon 8
Gen 3 — that's the theme of 2024."** At $1,100–1,500, it "remains a really good gamer phone" more than a mainstream
one.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2024-01-16] **Packaging the gaming phone for the masses.** "'The thing that makes gaming phones so good at gaming
  also makes them really good daily drivers. Asus finally said: what if we just package our great gaming phone for
  the masses? This is the first one where they really committed — thinner bezels, the first hole-punch, thinner
  body, and for the first time IP68 water resistance and wireless charging.'"
- [2024-01-16] **The functional back display beats the Nothing Phone's.** "'They couldn't help themselves — there's
  still a display on the back (AniMe Vision). It's certifiably gimmicky, but I like it: it's minimal, and it's
  actually functional — giving you useful info on the back, even more than the Nothing Phone.'"
- [2024-01-16] **The camera is the one hump they couldn't clear.** "'In previous gaming phones I'd say the camera's
  bad but it's not why you buy this. But in a phone sold as mainstream, the camera goes from an afterthought to one
  of the most important pieces. This is the same IMX890 as $600–700 phones — decent in good light, but too much
  motion blur and HDR, aggressive processing like it's running algorithms for a five-year-old sensor. It leveled up,
  but it's not over the hump.'"
- [2024-01-16] **AI features are the theme of 2024.** "'You're going to start noticing AI features in basically
  every phone this year with the Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 — Qualcomm built AI capabilities into the chip, so every
  manufacturer has new AI stuff. Trust me on this one; that's the theme of 2024.'"
- [2024-01-16] **A really good gamer phone, not quite mainstream.** "'They traded some gamery edge for mass appeal,
  and a lot of the decisions were smart (thinner, IP68, wireless charging while keeping the back display). But the
  one hump they couldn't get over is the camera — one of the most important things about a mainstream phone. So this
  remains a really good gamer phone.'"
