# iOS 15 Hands-On: Top 5 New Features! (@mkbhd, 2021-07-02)

## Summary
A top-5 hands-on of iOS 15. The durable notes: **Focus modes are the best feature** (near-profiles that sync across
all your Apple devices — the ecosystem-integration payoff); **Live Text is catching up to Google Lens** (but even
more intuitive); **iOS notifications still have a long way to go vs. Android**; and a pointed jab — **"I still hate
that Apple bought Dark Sky and stole it from Android."** The sponsored outro reveals a biography note: **he's
"mostly self-taught"** and made a **Skillshare class on his video process** ("what I wish I'd had").

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-07-02] **#1 — Focus modes (the best iPhone customization in a while).** "'As someone who likes
  customization (I use Android for it), **Focus modes are the best version of that I've seen on the iPhone in a
  while — almost like separate profiles** (work/personal/sleep/DND): each lets in different apps/people, can
  trigger by time/location, and can **show a specific home screen.** And **they sync across all your devices** —
  set "work" on the phone, everything switches, and your iMessage status shows it.'" (The ecosystem-integration
  payoff, per [[2021-06-08-yt-wGKb3oUo8go]].)'"
- [2021-07-02] **#3 — Live Text (catching up to Google Lens, more intuitively).** "'**On-device: long-press text
  in any photo to copy/translate/look it up** (even IDs a cat's breed). **It's catching up to what Google Lens has
  done for a while — but even more intuitive, since you just long-press a photo without a separate app.** (Lens
  still IDs more — shoes, cars; this is more limited.)'"
- [2021-07-02] **#2 — notifications (better, but "still a long way to go").** "'A fresh coat of paint (bigger app
  icons) + a **notification summary** for low-priority stuff. **But Android's notifications have forever been way
  better than iOS's — just facts — and they still have a long way to go.**'"
- [2021-07-02] **The pointed jab — "Apple bought Dark Sky and stole it from Android."** "'The Weather app is nicer
  now — **but I still can't forgive Apple for buying Dark Sky and then just stealing it from Android; I literally
  hate that every day. That was the best Android weather app.**' (Also: Apple Maps and FaceTime SharePlay/links
  keep catching up — "you can technically say you have FaceTime on Android now.")'"
- [2021-07-02] **BIOGRAPHY — "mostly self-taught" + the Skillshare class.** "'(Sponsored) I made **a Skillshare
  class on how I create videos end-to-end. I identify as mostly self-taught — I didn't go to school for making
  tech videos — and I always wished there was something laying out the steps and fundamentals, so I made what I
  wish I'd had.**'" A datable persona/creator-history note.
