# Apple Card Unboxing & Impressions! (@mkbhd, 2019-08-09)

## Summary
A look at the titanium Apple Card. Framed as "what would it be like if Apple made a credit card." The durable
payload is a **walled-garden warning** — the Card is another tool that raises the switching cost of leaving the
Apple ecosystem. Also a biography anchor: the **Waveform podcast officially launched.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-08-09] **THE WALLED-GARDEN WARNING (the key takeaway).** "'**Beware of the ecosystem.** If you thought the
  walls of the garden weren't high enough — people don't want to leave the iPhone because of iMessage bubbles and
  iCloud storage; now **imagine trying to switch to Android but you don't want to because you'd have to give up
  your credit card too.** This is obviously **another tool to trap you in the Apple ecosystem** — but you already
  knew that.'" A canonical statement of his recurring walled-garden lens.
- [2019-08-09] **"A credit card for iPhone users" — the product framing.** "'This is essentially **a credit card
  for iPhone users** — an extension of Apple Pay in physical form, for the places that don't take Apple Pay.' The
  hardware is pure Apple: **titanium, matte white, laser-etched name, no card number** (in the app, more secure)."
- [2019-08-09] **The genuinely-nice software (and where Apple out-does banks).** "The wallet UI has an **interest
  slider** where 'the **red color actively discourages you from making the minimum payment**,' a map-per-
  transaction history ('stuff I wish my banking app did'), **Face ID security** (a thief can't use the card
  without your face), and a **color gradient** that fills in by spending category (confirmed live)." Approves of
  the UX.
- [2019-08-09] **Honest: it's a mediocre credit card / a flex.** "'As a credit card it's **not that amazing** —
  ~12%+ interest, **1% cash back** (2% on Apple Pay, 3% at Apple — 'who's spending that much at Apple?'). You
  won't see this on any best-value-cards list. I kind of just see it as a **status symbol / flex of the decade** —
  it's very gratuitous.'" Won't oversell the financials despite the nice hardware.
- [2019-08-09] **BIOGRAPHY — the Waveform podcast launch.** "'Shout out to the **Waveform podcast — now officially
  launched, episode 1 is up.**'" A datable milestone (Aug 2019) for a major MKBHD-brand property.
