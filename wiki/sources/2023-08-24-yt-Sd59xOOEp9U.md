# Ford made a Matte Black F150 Lightning!

## Summary

A quick look at the limited **F-150 Lightning Platinum Black edition** (2,000 units) — a factory
matte-black-wrapped truck. Marques walks the blacked-out details and gives it his stamp: **"MKBHD-
mobile approved."** Notable for a **biography detail** — he explains his own car's matte-black is
**XPEL Stealth PPF (a gloss-black car with a protective clear layer), not a vinyl wrap** — and for a
values note: he's a fan of any car (like the Lightning) that gets **meaningful OTA software updates.**
Fully Marques-narrated.

## Key claims (biography marked ⬥)

- **[2023-08-24]** The car: a 2024 F-150 Lightning Platinum (AWD, ~130 kWh, 300 mi) as a **factory
  matte-black-wrapped limited edition (2,000 units)** — a vinyl wrap over a gloss-black truck, blacked-
  out badges/logos/wheels, a fogged aesthetic light bar (the actual headlights aren't tinted), numbered
  front-trunk badge. "Still really only two EV pickups in mid-2023 — the R1T and this (no Cybertruck
  yet)." (paraphrase)
- **[2023-08-24]** ⬥ BIOGRAPHY: **"My car — I've shown you many times — is matte black, but it's a
  gloss-black car with a clear layer over the top: XPEL Stealth, a protective thicker layer. This
  [Ford] is not that — it's just a vinyl wrap; I'd want to ceramic-coat it."** (Confirms his 911 wears
  XPEL Stealth PPF, matching the "stealth PPF" spec from the daily-driver reveal.) (quote)
- **[2023-08-24]** ⬥ Taste/verdict: **"If I could drive a vehicle this big all the time, this would
  probably be the one I picked… I'll give it my stamp of approval — this is MKBHD-mobile approved. I
  like the all-matte-black look."** The frunk is "still the gold standard." (quote)
- **[2023-08-24]** ⭐ VALUES: **"Ford is pushing real, meaningful software updates to this car"** (e.g.
  enabling the bed outlets while the truck is off) — "any car that gets meaningful software updates to
  make it better, just like a smartphone gets updates, I'm a fan of that." (quote)
