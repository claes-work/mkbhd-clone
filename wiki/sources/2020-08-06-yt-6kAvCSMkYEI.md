# The Last Intel Mac VS The Osborne Effect! (@mkbhd, 2020-08-06)

## Summary
A look at the 2020 27" iMac refresh framed by a buying dilemma: with the Apple Silicon transition looming, should
you buy an Intel Mac now? Introduces the **Osborne Effect** and a **need-it-now-vs-keep-it-long** decision
framework.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-08-06] **THE OSBORNE EFFECT — pre-announcing a future product defers current sales.** "'The **Osborne
  Effect**: customers cancel/defer orders for a soon-to-be-obsolete product when a company pre-announces its
  successor. Apply it here — people **deferring a perfectly-good iMac because of the Intel→Apple-Silicon transition
  ~a year out.** Apple thought about this, but the iMac was road-mapped to ship anyway.'" A promotable
  framework.
- [2020-08-06] **THE DECISION FRAMEWORK — how immediately + how long.** "'For any Intel Mac between now and the
  transition, **consider two things: (1) how immediately do you need a new computer, and (2) how long do you plan
  to keep it.** Quick updaters (3-4 years) or need-it-now buyers: get it. But **people who keep an iMac 7-10 years
  should probably wait a year or two** for Apple Silicon and/or the redesign.'" A reusable buy-now-vs-wait lens.
- [2020-08-06] **Frequent updates are good — "like Tesla."** "'I **love that Apple is doing more consistent
  updates** — in my ideal world **tech just keeps getting updated so whatever you buy is the latest, kind of like
  Tesla.** Thank you for keeping the iMac updated.'" His pro-frequent-updates advocacy (cf. the Tesla OTA thesis,
  [[2020-05-23-yt-_SoviSNZjso]]).
- [2020-08-06] **The genuine wins — SSDs, nano-texture, a better webcam.** "'Finally **all-SSD** (kill the last
  Fusion drive, Apple); the **$500 nano-texture matte glass** (from the Pro Display XDR) is one I love — "an order
  of magnitude easier to work with in a well-lit studio, no glare"; and a **physically better 1080p webcam** (the
  bigger chassis has room — "I'll keep asking for a better laptop webcam").'"
- [2020-08-06] **The redesign is overdue.** "'The iMac's design is **~8 years old (since 2012) and dated** — those
  bezels/chin are the biggest in Apple's lineup; a Pro-Display-XDR-style redesign is rumored. That, or Apple
  Silicon, would make it feel more worth the money.'"

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "There's something called the Osborne Effect... a social phenomenon of customers canceling or deferring orders
> for a current, soon-to-be-obsolete product because a company announced a future product prematurely."
> "In my ideal world, the tech just keeps getting updated over and over regardless of whatever cycle we're on...
> kind of like what Tesla does."
