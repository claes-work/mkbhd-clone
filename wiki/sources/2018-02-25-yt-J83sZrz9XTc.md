# Samsung Galaxy S9 Impressions! (@mkbhd, 2018-02-25)

## Summary
First-look at the Galaxy S9 / S9+ — framed as a **refinement of the S8** whose one headline is the camera. The
teachable bit is the **variable-aperture lens** (a first for smartphones) and the photography principle behind
it; also super-slow-mo, the "Animoji ripoff" AR Emoji, and a note on the widening S9-vs-S9+ gap.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-02-25] **"A refinement of the S8" — camera is the one real change.** Same shape, bezels, button layout
  (including Bixby); still IP68, wireless charging, USB-C, **still a headphone jack**. The one hardware move he
  likes: the fingerprint reader is now **under the camera** (more reachable) instead of beside it. Measured, not
  hyped.
- [2018-02-25] **The variable aperture — a teach-the-photography-mechanism.** "The S9 is the **first smartphone
  camera I've seen that changes apertures** — physically switching between **f/1.5 wide open** (max light) and
  **f/2.4** (more detail). Here's why it matters: **the wider you go, the softer it generally is** — photographers
  stop down to f/2.4–2.8 for sharpness. Doing that in a camera this small is genuinely impressive." Explains the
  optics, not just the spec.
- [2018-02-25] **Super slow-mo — impressive but caveated.** 960 fps at 720p stretches **0.2 s → 6 s** ("turning
  this into a movie"); also 240 fps at 1080p. But the 0.2 s capture window means you must time it (there's a
  motion-detection box to auto-trigger), and low-light grain is real — "I threw the air quotes on 'quality' for a
  reason." Honest about the limits.
- [2018-02-25] **AR Emoji = "Animoji ripoff," and creepy.** The front-camera avatar is closer to Bitmoji but
  worse; "**I don't see many people using it past the first few days.**" A dismissive-but-fair read (reinforced in
  the same-week camera deep-dive).
- [2018-02-25] **The widening S9 vs S9+ gap.** "I almost gave the S8 an award for being so close to its bigger
  brother. This year the **S9+ gets more screen, more battery, more RAM, AND more camera (dual)** — a bigger
  separation, a bigger buying decision." A reviewer's-eye structural observation.
- [2018-02-25] **Aesthetic aside — the mirror-finish/skin routine.** "Still the super-shiny mirror finish — a
  phone you'll end up putting a **dbrand skin** on to keep it from looking like a mess six seconds out of the
  box." His recurring glossy-back gripe.

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "This is the first smartphone camera I've ever seen that can change apertures — f/1.5 wide open, or stop down
> to f/2.4 for more detail."
> "The wider you go, the softer it generally is — that's why photographers stop down."
> "AR Emoji — Animoji ripoff. It's pretty creepy, if I'm being honest."
