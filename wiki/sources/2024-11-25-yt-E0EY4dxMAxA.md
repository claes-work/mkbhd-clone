# The Polestar 3 is Amazing

## Summary

Marques reviews the **Polestar 3** and captures its defining paradox: **"how could one car have so
many awesome design choices and also have such poor ones."** He loves the design so much it earns
his highest compliment — **"if I designed a car, it would look a lot like this"** — and rates its
Bowers & Wilkins system **the best or second-best car speakers he's ever heard**. But weak range
(~270mi), an over-digital UI (the glovebox opens only via touchscreen), and cost-cut single window
switches hold it back. Fully Marques-narrated.

## Key claims

- **[2024-11-25]** ⭐ Design: **"The best compliment I can give a car is that this is how I would have
  designed it. If I was designing a car, this is what I would have done."** Functional front + rear
  wings, low sloping roofline, thin-line aesthetic, yellow accents. ~$67k start (single motor) up to
  the $80,000s (this dual-motor Performance spec). (quote)
- **[2024-11-25]** ⭐ Speakers: **"This is — I'm tempted to say — either the second-best or the best
  speaker system I've ever heard in a car, period."** Bowers & Wilkins with a headrest speaker; "the
  one neck-and-neck with it is the one from the Maybach, and the Maybach costs way more." "I'll finish
  a song sitting in Park before going inside." (quote)
- **[2024-11-25]** ⚠️ The paradox: **"This car is amazing… and it's amazing how one car could have so
  many awesome design elements and also cheap out on a window and do things like the glovebox that only
  opens with a button on the touchscreen — there's no handle."** Too-digital: unlabeled capacitive
  steering-wheel buttons, single set of front window switches ("a money-saving measure I don't love"),
  HVAC buried multiple clicks deep. (quote)
- **[2024-11-25]** ⚠️ Range: even with clever tech (it decouples the front/rear motors for a
  front-drive efficiency mode, ~7s to re-engage), the dual-motor gets **only ~270mi**. "Kind of a
  mixed bag — beautifully designed but not the most functional." (paraphrase)
- **[2024-11-25]** ⬥ Verdict: **"I have a crush on this car, but I know it's not for me"** — the
  design is everything he'd want, but the weak range means "I just don't want to drive this car for
  road trips. Keep working on these, Polestar, please don't stop." (quote)
