## Genuinely-new micro-takes (each is one short)
- ★ **iPhone-update PSA: software updates do NOT "nuke" your phone.** Debunking the widely-believed myth
  that "updating your iPhone lets Apple slow it down to make you buy a new one." His take: **that's not what
  updates do** — the throttling controversy (batterygate) was **a specific, disclosed-badly power-management
  fix for aging batteries**, not a scheme baked into every update. *(corroborates his batterygate stance —
  defensible intent, bad disclosure — and his anti-planned-obsolescence-myth skepticism.)* [2023-09-22]
  (yt-bDuzEwInD7U)
- ★ **PlayStation Portal "seems… terrible."** Sony's $200 handheld is **Wi-Fi Remote-Play only** (no cloud,
  no local games, no Bluetooth audio) — "for the very specific person who wants to stream their own PS5 to
  another room, and no one else." *(a rare blunt pan; his does-this-need-to-exist lens.)* [2023-08-23]
  (yt-VlIeANvowso)
- **iMessage on Android — two ways.** Explainer: one route needs **a Mac as a relay**; the other is a
  third-party service — both are workarounds, neither is native. *(corroborates his interop / iMessage-lock-in
  interest ahead of RCS.)* [2023-09-04] (yt-yqOuowxeOOw)
- **AirDrop on Android** — you can get an AirDrop-like ecosystem feature on Android (via a third-party app he
  saw on Twitter). *(same interop thread — the walled-garden features are getting bridged.)* [2023-08-28]
  (yt-zsxviDibcUg)
- **Wireless-industry deceptive pricing** (Visible sponsor read, but a real consumer-advocacy angle): "free
  phone" promos + expensive multi-line plans **hinge on customers being too complacent to look into a simpler
  option.** *(the anti-carrier-games consumer-advocacy register.)* [2023-10-06] (yt-RlJYdxqPG-I)
- **Emergency-alert PSA** — heads-up that every US phone would ring loudly at 2:20 PM ET on Oct 4 for the
  nationwide test. [2023-10-03] (yt-IbhnTf-EqRY)
- **iPhone 15 event recap** — a fast standalone rundown of everything announced (iPhone 15 / 15 Plus / 15 Pro /
  15 Pro Max, USB-C, the Action button, titanium). [2023-09-13] (yt-ltpbEQA8Fwc)
- **The Samsung Galaxy S23 FE** — recaps the up-and-down "Fan Edition" trend (S20 FE the people's champ, S21
  FE late, S22 FE never came) and asks whether the S23 FE is worth picking. [2023-10-26] (yt-L_9CmbTTckc)
- **The Infinite Machine P1 "Cyber scooter"** — an electric scooter whose faceted design is clearly
  Cybertruck-inspired. [2023-10-12] (yt-VaV0Uk8eRbk)
- **The Zenon Flex monitor** — an ultrawide that mechanically bends into a curved monitor. [2023-10-07]
  (yt-Bq0FAsWmOys)
- **Samsung Galaxy Tab S9 Ultra** — a first look at the giant flagship Android tablet (successor to the S8
  Ultra). [2023-07-31] (yt-pHhpXrIOwsY)
