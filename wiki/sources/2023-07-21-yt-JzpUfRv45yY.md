# The Worst Hot Takes in Tech (@mkbhd, 2023-07-21)

## Summary
A reaction format (concept credited to Jimmy/basketball-hot-takes) grading audience tech hot takes. **(1) "Cybertruck
will outsell the F-150 by 2026" → bad take:** the **F-150 has been America's #1 truck for 46 years** (a truck sold
every 49 seconds; 600k–1M/year); the ~1.5M "pre-orders" are **refundable $100 deposits ("I'm one of them") — reserved
spots, not sales** — and Tesla can't build a million of a brand-new vehicle overnight (production ramps). **(2) "Apple
Maps is now better than Google Maps" → good take:** after the disastrous 2012 launch (Tim Cook apologized), Apple
caught up — often higher-quality Street View, better design, and now offline maps in iOS 17; **"they've caught up,
they're competitors now"** (though Google's decades-long data head-start means Apple isn't *better* yet). **(3) "The
Magic Mouse is the best mouse" → bad take:** the charging port is on the *bottom* (unusable while charging) and it's
ergonomically bad — but the "kernel of truth" is that **Apple makes it impossible to build a better accessory than
its own** (you can't out-integrate the Watch, out-pair AirPods, or match the Magic Mouse's uniquely smooth inertial
scrolling). **(4) "Tensor sucks, Google should've stuck with Snapdragon" → half-right:** Tensor isn't great (weak raw
perf, poor efficiency), and **today he'd pick a Snapdragon Pixel** — but **don't give up the custom-silicon dream**
(2–3 generations out it could be the best-optimized experience). **(5) "Beats have always been good" → decent take:**
**audio is one of the most subjective things in tech** (loudness is the only universally-impressive trait); Beats
were once overpriced cheaply-built, but "decent headphones today."

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2023-07-21] **Cybertruck won't dethrone the F-150 by 2026 — pre-orders aren't sales.** "'The F-150 has been
  America's best-selling truck for 46 years — a truck sold every 49 seconds, 600k to a million a year. The ~1.5
  million Cybertruck "pre-orders" are refundable $100 deposits (I'm one of them) — reserved spots, not sales — and
  Tesla can't build a million of something it's never made overnight; production ramps. Love the optimism, but bad
  take.'"
- [2023-07-21] **Apple Maps has caught up to Google Maps — a good take.** "'After the 2012 disaster (Tim Cook
  apologized), Apple plugged away at the data and added features. Nine times out of ten Apple's Street View is
  higher quality and smoother, it's better designed, and iOS 17 finally adds offline maps. I won't say it's *better*
  yet — Google has a decades-long data head-start — but it's caught up; they're competitors now, which is good
  take.'"
- [2023-07-21] **The Magic Mouse — bad take, but Apple makes it impossible to beat their accessory.** "'The charging
  port is on the *bottom* — you can't use it while charging — and it's ergonomically bad, a fatal flaw in more than
  one category. But the kernel of truth: Apple does to accessories what it does everywhere — makes it impossible to
  build a better one than its own. You can't out-integrate the Watch, out-pair AirPods, or match the Magic Mouse's
  uniquely smooth inertial scrolling (every other Mac mouse scrolls in jittery integer chunks).'"
- [2023-07-21] **Tensor — half-right; don't give up the dream.** "'Tensor isn't great — weak raw performance, poor
  efficiency, two generations in. If Google offered a Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 Pixel and a Tensor G2 Pixel today, I'd pick
  the Snapdragon. But I don't think they should give up the tensor dream — 2–3 generations out, the optimization
  could give the best battery life and experience you could never get from off-the-shelf Snapdragon.'"
- [2023-07-21] **Beats — audio is subjective; decent take.** "'Audio is one of the most subjective things in all of
  tech — the only trait people universally find impressive is loudness. People would pick Beats over a better-sounding
  cheaper pair and love them; a first nice pair with a V-shaped bass-heavy signature is exciting. Beats were once
  overpriced, cheaply built — but decent headphones today. Decent take.'"
