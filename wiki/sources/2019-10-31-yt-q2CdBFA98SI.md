# LG G8X Proves Foldables are Coming! (@mkbhd, 2019-10-31)

## Summary
A look at the $700 LG G8X and its dual-screen accessory case. The frame is a rebuttal to the "foldables are a
gimmick" sentiment: as the market matures, companies differentiate — and **folding devices (screen or not) are
coming and here to stay.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-10-31] **THE THESIS — "foldables aren't a gimmick; they're coming and here to stay."** "'A popular
  sentiment is that foldables are a gimmick with no point. I see why — they **stumbled out of the gate** (the
  $2,000 re-released Galaxy Fold, the delayed Mate X, the FlexPai nobody should buy). But **when a market grows
  and matures, there become different ways to differentiate** — and folding devices (a folding *screen*, or just
  a foldable *device*) **are coming, and they're here to stay. I believe it.**'" His pro-experimentation optimism.
- [2019-10-31] **LG's angle — a removable dual-screen, not a folding display.** "'LG isn't doing a folding display
  — they're **doubling down on "a bigger screen that fits in your pocket"** via a removable dual-screen case with a
  360° hinge. The phone itself is unremarkable (plain design, one spec: SD855/6GB); **the dual-screen case is what
  sells it** — $700 for the flagship + the case included.'"
- [2019-10-31] **The empty-notch critique (easy to mock).** "'To fix last year's mismatched color temperature,
  they used the **same panel for both screens — so there's a notch on the second screen even though there's
  nothing in it.** Symmetrical, I guess, but really easy to make fun of.' (Also: why is the whole front glossy? why
  the cutout window just to see the logo?)" His characteristic detail-level nitpicks.
- [2019-10-31] **Apps have to catch up — restraint on judging early.** "'With two screens (vs one folding
  screen), you have to **acknowledge the bezel and embrace it.** It's **up to app developers** to make apps take
  advantage — but it's early days, so **I'm trying not to be too harsh on the app situation for foldables yet.**'
  The Gamepad feature (a screen as a controller) = 'one of the more forward-thinking uses of dual screens — bravo.'"

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "When a market starts to grow and mature, there become different ways to differentiate yourself inside of it."
> "This first generation of folding products... is coming back. The apps have to come along, everything has to
> come along with it — but they're coming, and they're here to stay. I believe it."
