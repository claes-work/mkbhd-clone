# The Ferrari Purosangue is the Easiest SUV to Love AND Hate

> ⚠️ **ATTRIBUTION — MIXED.** Co-host **Miles** narrates the exterior, engine, and driving-dynamics
> sections (and the "my girlfriend in the passenger seat" / "young and goofy boy" / "NA cars are what
> I've always owned" lines — Miles, quarantined). **Marques narrates the interior / tech / livability
> walkthrough** (handed off at "come with me into the interior") — that portion IS persona data. Not
> a Ferrari V12 (that's Miles); the ⬥Marques portion is the infotainment critique.

## Summary

Marques and Miles co-review the **Ferrari Purosangue** — Ferrari's first four-door ("FUV"), a
naturally-aspirated 6.5L V12 SUV built ground-up (not borrowing parts like the Urus/Bentayga/Cullinan).
Miles handles the "definitively the best-driving performance SUV ever" case; **Marques handles the
interior tech, and grades it harshly.**

## Key claims (⬥ = Marques / persona data; Miles = quarantined)

- **[2024-07-18]** ⬥ MARQUES (the interior/tech, his lane): Ferrari puts **almost all controls on the
  steering wheel** via tiny capacitive buttons — **"some of the most difficult-to-use UI/UX while
  driving that I've seen; I do not like it."** CarPlay/Android Auto only show on the cluster screen; the
  HVAC resets to full-blast automatic every start; audio/EQ can only be set from the passenger screen.
  **His grade for the front-seat UI: "like a C, C-minus."** He praises the one good bit — a physical
  HVAC dial that "always controls the amount." The **back seat is "actually better in a Ferrari — weird
  thing to say."** (quote)
- **[2024-07-18]** ⬥ MARQUES: "I don't think anybody buying this is pretending they're getting it for
  practicality — but once you buy it, you should still have decent practicality." Notes the odd wheel-
  arch fitment ("one of the weirdest fitments of any SUV"). (quote)
- **[2024-07-18]** *(Miles — QUARANTINED)* the case for it: a ground-up 6.5L NA V12 (725 hp, revs to
  8,250) "wins the performance-SUV war on paper"; individual rear-wheel steering + electric anti-roll
  actuators kill body roll; "the most fun performance SUV to drive of all time." His nitpicks: a touch
  too quiet, aggressive downshifts ("my girlfriend felt she needed a helmet"). **(NOT persona data.)**

## Notable verbatim quotes (⬥ Marques only)
