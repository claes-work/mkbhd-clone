# Is a $25,000 Electric Car Even Possible?

## Summary

Using the **Fiat 500e** ($32k, ~140 mi range) as his example, Marques writes an essay-review asking
whether the long-promised **$25,000 good-range EV is even achievable** — because **the battery is the
most expensive part of any EV**, so to cut price you cut battery, and you quickly hit "pure economic
physics." His tentative answer: we'll get affordable EVs eventually, but **the bar for that price
tag will keep creeping up with inflation**. Fully Marques-narrated; contains two biography beats.

## Key claims (biography marked ⬥)

- **[2024-05-28]** The framing: the "mythical $25,000 EV" (people imagine a "Tesla Model 2"). His bar
  for **"the cheapest good EV" is the Model 3** (~300+ mi under ~$40k), with the Ioniq 6 just under it;
  below that (Leaf, Bolt, Kona, Mini Cooper SE at ~100 mi) "you run up against pure economic physics."
  (paraphrase)
- **[2024-05-28]** The Fiat 500e: $32k, ~140 mi, ~7s 0–60, "zippy, solid, with cute design details" —
  but **cheap materials sitting on an expensive battery**: "you've gone through all the work of
  minimizing cost, and here's what you end up with — a lot of plastic for a $32,000 car," no CarPlay,
  a tiny back seat. The question: "how do you cut another $7,000 out of this? Smaller battery → even
  less range." (quote + paraphrase)
- **[2024-05-28]** ⬥ BIOGRAPHY: **"I have also sat in the back seat of my own Porsche 911 once, just to
  say I did"** — corroborates 911 ownership; the Fiat's back seat "was a lot like this." (quote)
- **[2024-05-28]** ⬥ BIOGRAPHY: **"I remember that first interview I did with Elon on video, where I
  asked about the master plan and eventually getting to a cheap $25,000 car — that must have been about
  a decade ago, and $25,000 seemed realistic at the time."** (Places an on-camera Elon Musk interview
  ~2014–15.) (quote)
- **[2024-05-28]** ⭐ Conclusion: **"I tend to think we will eventually get to sustainable electric cars
  for everyone — but the bar for what that price tag is is just going to slowly creep up over time"**
  (inflation vs. battery progress). "Right now it doesn't exist, so we could argue it's impossible
  right now." (quote)
