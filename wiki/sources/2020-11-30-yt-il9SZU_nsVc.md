# What's in my Tech Bag! [2020] (@mkbhd, 2020-11-30)

## Summary
An everyday-carry ("**tech I choose**") video — a rich **biography + gear-philosophy** source. The durable idea:
**"it's not always the best thing or the best version, and that's fine — I have my needs met, that's enough."**
Reveals his real daily kit, his **two-phone (Android + iPhone) habit**, his **LASIK** (a datable biographical
fact), and his **ICONS** product line + Peak Design MKBHD-edition bag.

- [2020-11-30] **THE PHILOSOPHY — "not always the best thing, and that's fine."** "'This is stuff I've enjoyed
  most and choose to use — **it's not always the best thing or the best version of everything, and that's fine.

  game-changer this year, hasn't let me down.**" The **11" iPad Pro** (cellular, ICONS skin) — "I don't need the
  bigger one; mostly I just watch videos on it."'" (Extends the M1 review — [[2020-11-11-yt-b6g6rDDt9x8]].)

  15–35 RF I carry everywhere** — "not the camera I shoot professionally; for video we go **Sony A7S III** or
  **Komodo**, technically better — but **I take a lot of photos, and I'm a big fan of Canon's color science.**
  Shout-out to **Peter McKinnon** for putting me on it."'" A documented gear + creator-network detail.
- [2020-11-30] **BIOGRAPHY — LASIK, and one-charger-for-everything.** "'"I **used to keep my contacts here —

- [2020-11-30] **ICONS + the end-of-year projects.** "'End-of-year projects are ramping — **the Smartphone
