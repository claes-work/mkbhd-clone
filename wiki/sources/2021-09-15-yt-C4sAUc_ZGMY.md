# iPhone 13 Event Reaction: Everything New! (@mkbhd, 2021-09-15)

## Summary
Reactions to Apple's September 2021 event — the iPhone 13 lineup plus a refreshed base iPad, a redesigned iPad
mini, and the Apple Watch Series 7. The durable notes: it's **a small update wrapped in a huge, grandiose show**
(the presentation didn't match the modest updates); **ProMotion (120 Hz) finally arrives — but only on the Pros**
(a real 13-vs-13-Pro differentiator); a **20% smaller notch**; the base iPad is "still the best deal in the whole
tablet world," and the **iPad mini redesign is the biggest refresh of the day.** Plus a pointed gripe: **ProRes is
locked out of the 128 GB base Pro** ("kind of brutal").

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-09-15] **A small update, a huge show.** "'Apple's events are always grandiose — tons of drone shots,
  amazing transitions, cinematography through the roof — **all to announce a couple of small updates. It didn't
  quite match.**'" (His recurring production-craft-vs-substance observation.)'"
- [2021-09-15] **ProMotion FINALLY — but only on the Pros.** "'The 13 Pro/Pro Max **finally add a 120 Hz ProMotion
  display (variable 10–120 Hz) — I suspected they would.** This is now **a real differentiator between the 13 and
  13 Pro; we'll see if regular people notice and upgrade for it** (I think a lot will). And Apple expects
  **1.5–2.5 hrs better battery** despite the brighter 120 Hz screen.'"
- [2021-09-15] **A 20% smaller notch + the cameras.** "'All the new iPhones get **brighter screens and a 20%
  smaller notch** (mainly from moving the earpiece up — no extra info in the reclaimed space). Cameras: the 13/mini
  get **bigger sensors + sensor-shift OIS**; the Pros get **matching, all-wider-aperture triple cameras, a 3x
  telephoto, night mode on all.** Video (already the best in any phone) adds **Cinematic mode** (AI focus racking —
  "Apple's demos are best-case; these didn't look that good — the focus still hunted") and **ProRes.**'"
- [2021-09-15] **The gripe — ProRes locked out of the 128 GB base Pro.** "'You can shoot **4K30 ProRes — unless
  you get the 128 GB base Pro, in which case only 1080. Instead of moving base storage to 256, they just disabled
  the feature — kind of brutal. There might be a technical reason (write speeds), but they should explain.**'" A
  pointed value critique.
- [2021-09-15] **The iPads — base is the best deal; the mini is the biggest refresh.** "'The refreshed **base iPad
  ($329, A13, Center Stage) is still the best deal in the whole tablet world — the #1 entry point.** The **iPad
  mini is the biggest redesign of the day — a tiny iPad Air (full metal, USB-C, A15, 5G, an 8.3" screen) — but
  it's 60 Hz, and I'll miss 120 Hz.** Now all iPads have USB-C except the base (still Lightning), and the mini
  officially out-specs the Air (which now needs an update).'"
- [2021-09-15] **The verdict — don't upgrade from a 12.** "'A bunch of small upgrades — **you probably don't need
  to run out and get a 13 if you have a 12. But the 120 Hz on the Pros — I'm into it; that might be a good reason
  for people on older iPhones (even an 11 or 12) to consider the Pro.**' ($699 mini → $1,099 Pro Max.)'"

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "Apple's events are always these huge, grandiose things... but then it's all to announce like a couple small
> updates to a few things, so it felt like it didn't quite match."
> "The 13 Pro and 13 Pro Max have finally done it — they finally added a 120 Hz display to these Pro phones... this
> is now a real differentiator between the 13 and the 13 Pro."
> "Instead of moving the baseline storage up to 256, they just literally disable this one feature [ProRes], and
> it's kind of brutal."
