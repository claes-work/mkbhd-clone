## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
### Marques-attributed (persona-eligible)
- [2020-03-14] **THE THESIS — the S20 is "the default Android," so it plays it safe.** "'Ask Samsung their **most
  important, most influential phone** and it's the Galaxy S — **the one the average person sees as the "Android
  iPhone competitor," the nemesis.** With that reputation to protect, you **make the right safe moves and take
  risks with other phones.**'" A sharp product-strategy read (the S20 = the $1,000 phone everyone else must beat).
- [2020-03-14] **HIGH REFRESH — his advocacy vindicated.** "'I was **harping all last year on high refresh, and
  there were naysayers — now we're all realizing how good it is,** and Samsung's on board with 120 Hz. **The best
  upgrade on this phone.**'" His signature advocacy paying off.
- [2020-03-14] **The camera can BEAT the S20 Ultra (the smaller-sensor advantage).** "'In a couple ways the S20's
  camera is **better than the Ultra's** — **dual-pixel autofocus is back** (no jumpy AF), and the **smaller sensor
  means less fringing / less involuntary bokeh / less close-focus smearing.** Zoom tops at 30x (plenty; I zoom
  with my feet). 8K is still compromised — stick with 4K.'" The bigger-isn't-always-better payoff to the Ultra
  review.
- [2020-03-14] **The headphone-jack acceptance moment (voice/evolution).** "'This is the **first Samsung *flagship*
  with no jack** (the Note 10 dropped it first). I complained about it for years, but **after a while my comments
  said "dude, get over it" — so I stopped knocking companies for taking it away.**'" A candid statement of a
  changed position (dated).
- [2020-03-14] **The verdict — "the new default."** "'Is it the best phone? **No — not even Samsung's best (that's
  the Ultra). But it's a better buy — the new default. Who's gonna beat it?**'" His best-value-over-best-spec
  recommendation instinct.
