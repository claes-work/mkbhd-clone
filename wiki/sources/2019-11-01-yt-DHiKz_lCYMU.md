# Beats Solo Pro: Return to Excellence! (@mkbhd, 2019-11-01)

## Summary
Review of the $300 Beats Solo Pro on-ear ANC headphones. Notable for the self-aware **"I thought you hate Beats"**
bit (his loved→hated→came-back Beats arc) and a clean **build-quality-vs-comfort** tradeoff read.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-11-01] **His Beats arc (voice/biography).** "'I actually **started off really loving Beats, then swung the
  other way and hated them for a while, then sort of came back.**' A self-aware framing of his evolving
  relationship with the brand." A characteristic candid-about-my-own-bias beat.
- [2019-11-01] **Best-built Beats ever — but that causes the comfort problem.** "'**My favorite build of Beats
  headphones of all time** — sturdy top band, metal side pieces, rigid. But that **biggest improvement may have
  led to the biggest downside: comfort.** They're **on-ear**, don't adjust much, and the **clamp force is tight**
  and doesn't break in — comfortable enough for a couple hours but they squeeze your ears.'" A clean cause-effect.
- [2019-11-01] **The sound is genuinely good now (not bass-garbage).** "'These are a **totally different world**
  from the previous Solos — not "super bass-heavy overwhelming garbage." **Pretty fun** — still bass-forward with
  recessed mids (a little shrill vs neutral), but **bright, clear, vivid — like a photo from a Samsung phone.** If
  you were worried Beats sound terrible, you don't have to anymore.' Still ranks Sony/Bose/Sennheiser above them."
- [2019-11-01] **"More matte" as a marketing feature + the Lightning gripe.** "'The box literally says **"more
  matte"** as if it's a huge feature — every color is matte soft-touch, shows no fingerprints.' The bummer:
  **charges via Lightning, not USB-C** ('for someone like me pairing them with an iPad Pro and an Android phone,
  USB-C would've been great — but these are made by Apple, why do I get my hopes up')." The recurring USB-C wish.
- [2019-11-01] **The ranking — 2nd-favorite Beats.** "'My **second-favorite Beats ever** — the PowerBeats Pro
  (workout fit) are still #1. Pleasantly surprised, competitive at $300, but **I'll still switch back to my
  Sonys** (more comfortable, over-ear, sound a bit better).'" Consistent with his Sony-as-daily preference.
