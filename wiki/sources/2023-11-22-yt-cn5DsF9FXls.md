# This Smartwatch is $69! (CMF Watch Pro) (@mkbhd, 2023-11-22)

## Summary
A review of the **$69 CMF Watch Pro** (CMF is **Nothing's ultra-budget sub-brand**; CMF = **color, material,
finish**). His frame: **"just the fact that you can get a fully functioning smartwatch for $69 in 2023 is amazing"**
— the basics (time, notifications, steps, heart rate, sleep) are **"good enough for 95% of people."** It's a
plastic-feeling (nominally aluminum) square with a bright 1.96" display, IP68, a big app list (calls, blood oxygen,
a Google-Assistant relay), a magnetic-pin charger, and **genuinely great battery (3+ days easy, better than an
Apple Watch)** because it's just a lightweight Bluetooth accessory. It **works with any Android phone** ("better
than Apple can say about their own smartwatch"). The twist he learned: **$69 is actually the *premium* end for a
basic smartwatch** — the cheapest are $20–50 and "more or less the same product" — so this is a **"slightly better
packaged, slightly better designed version for a slight upcharge, the same way Nothing charges a bit more for a
phone with the same specs, just for the design."** "If you squint it kind of looks like an Apple Watch Ultra — and
if that's your comparison point, it's a total bargain." (Eight Sleep sponsor: he now uses the Pod cover for sleep
tracking instead of a wearable.)

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2023-11-22] **The basics are good enough for 95%.** "'Just the fact that you can get a fully functioning
  smartwatch for $69 in 2023 is kind of amazing. The simple stuff — time, notifications, steps, heart rate, calories,
  sleep — on its face that's good enough for 95% of people.'"
- [2023-11-22] **Better battery than an Apple Watch; works with any Android phone.** "'Because it's just a
  lightweight Bluetooth accessory, battery life is really good — 3 straight days easy, better than an Apple Watch.
  And because it's a generic Bluetooth accessory, it works with basically any Android phone — which is at least
  better than Apple can say about their own smartwatch.'"
- [2023-11-22] **$69 is actually premium in this segment.** "'The main thing I learned: $69 is actually on the
  premium end for a very basic smartwatch. The cheapest, most basic ones are $20–50, and at that point they're all
  more or less the same product — a plastic Bluetooth accessory. This is a slightly better-packaged, better-designed
  version for a slight upcharge.'"
- [2023-11-22] **The Nothing/CMF playbook — design markup.** "'CMF is Nothing's ultra-budget sub-brand — CMF stands
  for color, material, finish. They mark up extremely cheap products a little for nice packaging and design, the
  same way Nothing charges a bit more than you'd expect for a phone with the same specs, just for the design.'"
- [2023-11-22] **If you squint, it's an Apple Watch Ultra.** "'If you squint enough, this square kind of looks like
  an Apple Watch Ultra — and if that's your comparison point, it's a total bargain. I think that was their real
  goal.'"
