# Surface Laptop Studio Impressions: Windows 11 With a Twist! (@mkbhd, 2021-10-05)

## Summary
Impressions of the Surface Laptop Studio — **a laptop "trying to be three things at once"** (laptop / easel "stage"
mode / flat tablet). The durable notes: his recurring **"who uses each mode?"** question; the **double-decker-
MacBook-Pro** design (with a build quirk — an inconsistent air gap); a **creator-laptop ports gripe** (no SD card
reader, only two USB-C); a **trackpad that "reached MacBook level"** (haptic + 120 Hz); the **Slim Pen 2** (a
paper-simulating vibration motor); and pricing that **"falls apart" once you add the GPU** ($1,599 → $2,100+).
"Glad it exists" for a smaller group.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-10-05] **Three modes — "who uses each?"** "'Three modes: **laptop, the easel "stage" mode (folded down 45°,
  covering the keyboard but keeping the trackpad/touchscreen), and flat tablet.** The idea's cool — but my first
  thought is always **who uses each mode?** From my own use, the stage mode is for videos, controller gaming, and
  touch-first apps (Android apps are coming to Windows 11 but don't work yet) — but there aren't many touch-first
  apps I use.'"
- [2021-10-05] **The double-decker design + a build quirk.** "'It looks like a **double-decker MacBook Pro** (a
  slim full-width top with all the ports, a smaller inset bottom for internal volume — the illusion of a thinner
  laptop hovering off the desk). Rigid magnesium/aluminum — **but a weird build quirk: an inconsistent air gap all
  the way around the display that I wanted to squeeze shut.**'"
- [2021-10-05] **The ports gripe — a creator laptop with no SD card reader.** "'For something positioned as a
  creator laptop: **no SD card reader (every creative pro I know uses one), and only two USB-C** (both Thunderbolt
  4 — great for my SD dongle, but that leaves one port for a mouse).'"
- [2021-10-05] **The trackpad "reached MacBook level" + the Slim Pen 2.** "'The **trackpad is really nice — the
  responsiveness and haptic feedback have reached MacBook level for me** (helped by the 120 Hz display) — something
  I was hoping to see from Windows 11 + optimized Surfaces. The webcam is decent (2 MP / 1080p). The speakers are
  the letdown (no grilles, almost no bass). The **Slim Pen 2 (a vibration motor to simulate paper)** — not Apple
  Pencil levels, but usable; it snaps under the front lip with a strong magnet ("very satisfying").'"
- [2021-10-05] **The pricing "falls apart" — and "glad it exists."** "'It starts at $1,599, but **you'd never want
  this without the dedicated GPU — so at least $2,100** (i7 / 16 GB / RTX 3050 Ti), and the AMD world dramatically
  outperforms it. **I didn't get this expecting to recommend it to most people — it's for a smaller group who find
  the easel/pen useful — but I'm glad it exists; where else do you find a laptop with this design?**'"

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "Every time I see something like this, my first thought is always: okay, what do you use each mode for?"
> "The trackpad's responsiveness and the haptic feedback — it's honestly, it's on MacBook level for me. It's
> reached that level."
> "I didn't get this laptop expecting to recommend it to most people... but I'm also glad it exists."
