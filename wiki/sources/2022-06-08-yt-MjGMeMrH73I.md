# M2 MacBook Air Impressions: Midnight Madness! (@mkbhd, 2022-06-08)

## Summary
Hands-on first impressions of the redesigned M2 MacBook Air. Two takeaways: **one color warning and one lineup
question.** He loves the design refresh (the flat, squared "two iPads sandwiched" look, still razor-thin, **MagSafe
back with a color-matched cable**, a bigger 500-nit display with a notch + 1080p webcam). The color warning: **the
new Midnight (like the Apple Watch S7 — not quite black, slightly blue) picks up a LOT of fingerprints** and is
hard to clean (lean Starlight or Space Gray). The M2 is the 2nd-gen baseline (~20% CPU / ~30–35% GPU over M1). But
the price rose to **$1,199** (the M1 Air stays at $999), and **the weirdest part of the whole lineup is the M2
MacBook Pro** — same price ballpark, but the Air is **thinner, lighter, with a better display, MagSafe, fast
charging, a better webcam, and more ports — a better computer for ~90%+ of people.** One quirk: still **only one
external display**.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-06-08] **The design + MagSafe color-matched cable.** "'I love the refresh — no longer wedge-shaped, flat and
  squared like the MacBook Pros, "two iPads sandwiched," still razor-thin (11.3 mm, 2.7 lb). **MagSafe is back with
  a color-matched cable** (a really nice touch) + fast charging (67 W optional brick), a quad-speaker setup, and a
  bigger 500-nit display with a notch and a new 1080p webcam. The notch is dorky staring at it, but we're used to
  it.'"
- [2022-06-08] **The color warning — Midnight picks up fingerprints.** "'Reluctantly (we love a matte black): **the
  new Midnight might not be the way to go.** Like the Apple Watch S7, it's not quite black — slightly blue — and in
  my limited time it **picked up a LOT of fingerprints and was hard to clean.** It leaned me toward Space Gray or
  Starlight (which won't show them). Can't say I didn't warn you.'"
- [2022-06-08] **The M2 — the baseline chip.** "'The M2 is important: ~20% better CPU and ~30–35% GPU over the
  already-capable M1, same 18 hrs of video playback, 100 GB/s bandwidth, up to 24 GB. But **remember it's still the
  baseline chip** (M1 Pro / M1 Ultra are higher-end; expect M2 Pro/Ultra later) — targeted at everyday computing
  with occasional demanding stuff.'"
- [2022-06-08] **The lineup question — the M2 Pro makes no sense.** "'It's pricier now — **$1,199** (the 2-year-old
  M1 Air stays at $999, interesting it didn't drop). The weirdest part: **the other machine that got the M2 is the
  M2 MacBook Pro (no redesign) — and I'd argue the cheaper M2 Air is actually the better computer for 90%+ of
  people** (thinner, lighter, better design/display, Touch ID + a real function row over a Touch Bar, MagSafe +
  fast charging, better webcam/speakers, MORE ports). **The Pro's only edge is a fan for sustained loads — but
  those people should get the 14" M1 Pro.**'"
- [2022-06-08] **The one quirk — one external display.** "'A weird quirk: **even with two ports, it still only
  supports ONE external display (up to 6K).** But it's a good machine — fun to hold and use.'"
