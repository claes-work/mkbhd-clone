# Living with the 2022 Electric Hummer so you don't have to!

## Summary

Marques lives a week with the **GMC Hummer EV** and lands on a signature tech-analogy verdict: **"this
is the ROG Phone of trucks"** — absurdly excessive, more specs/features than you'll ever use, but
genuinely enthusiastic and (thanks to a **212 kWh battery**) with real 300+ mi range. He fixates on
the **9,046-lb weight** (roads he can't drive down) and calls **crab-walk mode "kind of a gimmick."**
Fully Marques-narrated.

## Key claims

- **[2022-09-04]** ⭐ The weight: **"The stat that sums it up: a Civic is ~3,000 lb, a Model 3 ~4,000, a
  Model X ~5–6,000, an R1T ~7,000 — this has a 212 kWh battery (the biggest I've ever seen) and weighs
  9,046 lb. There are roads in my neighborhood I can't drive down because it weighs too many tons."** It
  barely fits a parking space ("you have to choose whom to screw over"). (quote)
- **[2022-09-04]** Who's it for: "people who bought and loved the original H1 — that's the closest thing.
  It's for someone who doesn't need a truck but wants a big fun tank. I'd still take the cheaper F-150
  Lightning." (paraphrase)
- **[2022-09-04]** Praise (good UI!): **"These are actual physical buttons, not touchscreen — really good
  UI"** (physical HVAC + expandable menus); a tailgate Bluetooth speaker ("the ultimate tailgate
  vehicle"); a strong regen paddle (~240 kW). ⚠️ The vibrating-seat alerts ("I'd prefer the steering
  wheel"). (quote)
- **[2022-09-04]** ⚠️ Crab walk: "I'll hold it down so you see the ridiculous 5-second animation to
  unlock crab-walk mode… **I'm here to tell you it's kind of a gimmick. It works, but I'd never actually
  do it.** The four-wheel steering for a *tighter turning radius*, though, is the real game-changer." The
  **video-game drive-mode animations** ("towing a rocket… is that on the moon?"). (quote)
- **[2022-09-04]** ⭐ THE VERDICT (signature analogy): **"This is the ROG Phone of trucks — absolutely
  gigantic, way more specs and features than you'll ever use, but still cool to have them if you're an
  enthusiast; a huge battery giving legit 300–330 mi range; excessive but very enthusiastic, with crazy
  screen animations. It's a gamer's truck. It took me a week to learn that, but I'm not even mad."** ~$110k.
  (quote)
