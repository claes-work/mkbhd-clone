# M3 MacBook Pro Impressions: Is It Enough? (@mkbhd, 2023-10-31)

## Summary
Filmed from a hotel at the **Ultimate Frisbee Beach World Championships** (he's playing on **mixed Team USA** — "an
opportunity of a lifetime, a career goal"), reacting to Apple's M3 event. He frames it with **the tech-adoption
arc**: every tech has an explosive-growth phase then flattens into maturity — smartphones are on the flat part
(iPhone 15 barely beats 14), folding phones and EVs are near the beginning; but **computers had an unusual curve** —
stable Intel machines, then Apple Silicon made a **big out-of-nowhere leap** (the M1 Max he still dailies), then M2
leveled back to a ~30% bump. Now, with **Qualcomm claiming chips beyond the M2** and flat Mac sales, the interesting
part is **"the convincing Apple has to do"** to give people reasons to buy. His read of the event: M3/Pro/Max on
3nm, but **Apple verbally compared the M3 to the M1, not the M2** (M2 is only two years old — "trying to make the
improvement look as large as possible"); vs M2 it's ~10–25% raw. The **space black** color is "the most classic
Apple thing — give it a new look so you know you have the new one" (it speaks to him — "I might want to upgrade... I
don't need it"). Still no USB-C on the Magic accessories. And the kicker: the whole keynote was **"shot on an
iPhone"** (revealed at the end) — "amazing production; I've wanted to see behind the scenes of an Apple keynote for
years, more than any other, this one."

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2023-10-31] **The tech-adoption arc — computers had an unusual curve.** "'Every tech has an arc — explosive
  growth, then it flattens into maturity. Smartphones are on the flat part; folding phones and EVs are near the
  beginning. Computers are mature, but Apple Silicon made a big out-of-nowhere leap on the graph (the M1 Max I still
  daily), then M2 leveled back to a reasonable ~30% bump.'"
- [2023-10-31] **The story around the event is the interesting part.** "'With Qualcomm claiming chips beyond the M2
  and flat Mac sales, the interesting part isn't just the products — it's the convincing Apple has to do to give us
  reasons to keep buying new Macs.'"
- [2023-10-31] **Apple compared the M3 to the M1, not the M2.** "'Something sneaky: in the broad performance stuff
  they almost always verbally compared the M3 family to the M1, not the M2 — which makes sense, they're trying to
  make the improvement look as large as possible. The M2 is only two years old; vs M2 most numbers were more like
  10–25% raw.'"
- [2023-10-31] **Space black — a new look so you know you have the new one.** "'Apple's very clever: if they just do
  the new chip, not as many people upgrade — but give it a new look and you know you have the new one just by
  looking at it. The most classic Apple thing. This black one speaks to me. I might want to upgrade... I don't need
  it though. I don't need it.'"
- [2023-10-31] **The keynote was shot on an iPhone.** "'At the very end, black background, white text: "this event
  was shot on an iPhone." I did not see that coming — I watched it fully believing it was regular cameras (drone
  shots, CGI, beautifully colored). Amazing production — I've wanted to see behind the scenes of an Apple keynote for
  years, and more than any other, I want to see this one.'"
