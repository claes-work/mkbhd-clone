# The Lucid Air One-Ups Tesla and Maxes Out Range!

## Summary

**The origin of his "Lucid one-ups Tesla" thread.** Marques lives a week with the **Lucid Air Grand
Touring** and reads Lucid's whole strategy as **"take what Tesla's doing and one-up as many meaningful
things as possible"** — more range (446 mi), faster charging (350 kW), and a **luxury interior that
beats Tesla by a handy margin.** The catches: no charging network of its own, a weird small trunk, a
very high price, and his hot take — **"it's ugly"** (a whale-shark face). Fully Marques-narrated.

## Key claims

- **[2022-11-11]** ⭐ THE FRAME: **"Lucid's strategy is to take what Tesla's doing and one-up as many
  meaningful things as possible — and they've done a pretty good job."** The Air GT Performance: **446
  mi honest range, 350 kW charging, a super-low drag coefficient** — "faster charging and more range
  than Tesla" (but you still deal with the public network — no Lucid Supercharger equivalent). ~$150–160k.
  (quote)
- **[2022-11-11]** ⭐ Luxury beats Tesla: "This is where it beats Tesla by a handy margin — Alcantara/
  leather, a great back seat (a real trunk, not a hatchback → way more legroom than a Model S), a
  21-speaker system with amazing separation, quieter framed windows, massaging seats, rear window shades.
  **They won the luxury and materials game.**" ⚠️ "But Tesla still has better *software.*" (quote)
- **[2022-11-11]** The engineering trade: "They gave you all that back-seat space in the same wheelbase
  as a Model S by cutting into the trunk — so you get a **weirdly small trunk opening** — but a **huge
  powered front trunk** (they made the motors/parts so efficient), which I'll be tempted to use all the
  time." (paraphrase)
- **[2022-11-11]** ⚠️ HOT TAKE: **"It's ugly. Yeah, this is an ugly car."** (Studio colleague Andrew: "it
  looks like a whale shark from the front" — "I kind of agree, because of this huge visor.") He notes the
  coming **Sapphire (2023, triple-motor Plaid-killer, ~$250k)** "looks a lot better — black wheels, no
  aero caps, stealth." (quote)
- **[2022-11-11]** ⭐ The takeaway: **"Electric cars are getting really interesting — the fact that this
  beats the flagships in so many things is impressive. It does it at the expense of the trunk and the
  cost. Lucid's proving you can just put a lot of money into the electricity and R&D and make a nice
  car."** (quote)
