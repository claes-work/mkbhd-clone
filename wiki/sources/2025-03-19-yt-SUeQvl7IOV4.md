# Google Pixel 9A Impressions: One Great Spec! (@mkbhd, 2025-03-19)

## Summary
Hands-on with the **$499 Pixel 9a** — "the leaks are always true." The headline wins: **the price stays the same
($499)** despite inflation/tariffs, and **there's no camera bump at all** — "how long have we been asking phone makers
to make it thicker and fill the space with battery? They did it" — giving it an 8.9 mm body and **the largest battery
ever in a Pixel (5,100 mAh, more than the 9 Pro XL).** He has mixed feelings on the design (**the "one great spec" is
the battery, but losing the visor sacrifices the Pixel's hard-won identity** — "where did the identity go?"). It's the
same Tensor G4 as the flagship 9s, a 6.3" 120 Hz display (nearly the Pixel 9's — off-at-60-by-default), IP68 now
(up from the 8a's IP67), a plastic back, and all the Pixel software (Gemini everywhere, Magic Eraser, call screening,
**seven years of updates**) with dual cameras (48 MP + 13 MP UW, Night Sight). His framing: **"think of the 9a as a
Pixel 9 minus some camera chops, plus a way bigger battery"** — and unlike last year's awkwardly-placed 8a, there's now
**real separation** (the 9a makes fewer sacrifices at the same price, while the 9 rose to $799 and only later dropped),
so it's **genuinely good value.** (Odd new embargo: he could show no software, only home screens.) Two closing beats:
**it validates calling the iPhone 16e "ridiculous"** ("we've got all these other pretty-good budget phones"), and a
**wait-for-the-Pixel-10 caveat** for performance-per-dollar chasers (a rumored big Tensor G5 jump) — immediately hedged
with **"expectations are the thief of joy; the 9a is a perfectly good phone, go be happy."**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2025-03-19] **Same $499 — and they finally killed the camera bump.** "'The price stays the same at $499 despite
  inflation and tariffs — nice. And there's no camera bump at all. How long have we been asking makers to make the
  phone thicker and fill the space with battery? They did exactly that — an 8.9 mm body with the largest battery ever in
  a Pixel (5,100 mAh, more than the 9 Pro XL). That's become the main value proposition.'"
- [2025-03-19] **The "one great spec" is the battery — but it lost the Pixel identity.** "'I have mixed thoughts on the
  design. I just praised the Pixel line for locking in its identity as the phone with the visor — you look at a Pixel
  and it's recognizable. This comes out and… where did the identity go? But the trade is no camera bump and a huge
  battery, so I'll take it.'"
- [2025-03-19] **A Pixel 9 minus camera chops, plus a way bigger battery.** "'It's the same Tensor G4 as the flagship
  9s, nearly the same 6.3" 120 Hz display, IP68 now, all the Pixel software (Gemini, Magic Eraser, call screening,
  seven years of updates), and dual cameras with Night Sight. Think of the 9a as a Pixel 9 minus some camera chops, plus
  a way bigger battery — a sacrifice a lot of people are totally cool with, and it's way cheaper.'"
- [2025-03-19] **Better positioned than the 8a — and it validates calling the 16e ridiculous.** "'Last year the 8a was
  awkward — the Pixel 8 above it was a better deal. This year there's real separation: the 9a makes fewer sacrifices at
  the same price, while the 9 launched higher. It's genuinely good bang for the buck. And the 9a drops into a world
  where the iPhone 16e was looking ridiculous — now we feel validated calling it that, with all these good budget
  phones.'"
- [2025-03-19] **The wait-for-Pixel-10 caveat — "expectations are the thief of joy."** "'We're a few months from Pixel
  10 leaks, and the rumors suggest a big Tensor G5 performance jump that could make it much more competitive with
  Snapdragon. If you're a performance-per-dollar person like me, it might be worth waiting — but I already regret saying
  that, because the 9a is a perfectly good phone. Expectations are the thief of joy; go get it and be happy.'"
