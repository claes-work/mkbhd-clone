# The Blind Smartphone Camera Test 2020! (@mkbhd, 2020-12-03)

## Summary
The third annual Blind Smartphone Camera Test — 16 cameras, bracket-style, ~10M votes. What started as crowning a
best camera has become **"a philosophical, psychological test on social media."** The durable findings: **people
pick the brighter photo, then the more-saturated one; white balance is underrated (and why the iPhone loses round
one every year); and Twitter vs. Instagram compress photos differently** — so the meta-lesson is **"just make
what you want, post it, and be happy — do the best with what you've got."** Winner: the ASUS Zenfone 7 Pro.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-12-03] **It's become a psychology test, not a science test.** "'It started as crowning an unbiased best
  camera, but it's turned into a **philosophical, psychological test on social media** — the opposite of
  scientific. Three years running: **no phone or company has ever won more than once, and the iPhone has never
  made it out of the first round.** We shoot the photos most people actually take (human subjects), and **we
  don't tap to focus — Google's data says most people don't.**'"
- [2020-12-03] **FINDING #1 — people pick the brighter, then the more-saturated photo.** "'Hold everything else
  the same and **people just pick the brighter photo** (makes sense). And with brightness equal, **they pick the
  slightly more saturated/vibrant one — but only to an extent; you can't crank saturation through the roof.**
  A paler photo loses.'" A durable perception insight.
- [2020-12-03] **FINDING #2 — WHITE BALANCE IS UNDERRATED (and why the iPhone keeps losing round one).** "'White
  balance has been the major new factor — **I'd go so far as to say it's why the iPhone loses in the first round
  every single time.** The iPhone runs cool/blue; in warm-colored scenes (skin tones, warm light) **a cooler
  white balance makes warm things look less saturated and a black shirt go blue.** My theory: on fairer skin
  tones (most people) it doesn't hurt as much, and blue skies look bluer.'"
- [2020-12-03] **The photographer's counterpoint — white balance is just a slider; chase information.** "'A
  photographer wouldn't be so concerned about white balance — **it's just a slider you can fix, and so is
  exposure if it's close. What they really want is as much information, detail, and dynamic range as possible so
  you can make the photo you want in the edit.**'"
- [2020-12-03] **FINDING #3 — Twitter vs. Instagram compress differently.** "'The same finale photo **won on
  Instagram but got crushed on Twitter** — a huge swing. Twitter boosts saturation (so the over-saturated one
  looked worse); Instagram makes both a bit paler. Everywhere you post, **a photo just looks different — this is
  true of my YouTube videos too, chewed up and spit out differently on every screen.**'"
- [2020-12-03] **THE META-LESSON — just make what you want, post it, be happy.** "'I'm not going to run around
  saying the Zenfone 7 Pro is the new camera king (though it's very consistent — "he don't miss"). The point is:
  **it doesn't really matter exactly what your photo looks like when you take or edit it, as long as you like
  it — make what you want, post it, be happy, and do the best you can with what you've got. That's what we're
  all doing.**'" A promotable creator philosophy.
