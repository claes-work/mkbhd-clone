# Samsung Galaxy Note 10: But Why Tho? (@mkbhd, 2019-08-27)

## Summary
Review of the smaller, $950 Galaxy Note 10 (vs. the 10+). Two throughlines: the **1080p "spec matters more on
paper than in real life"** principle, and a **"who is this actually for?"** framing that lands on a narrow Venn
diagram. He surprises himself by liking the smaller flagship — but still recommends the OnePlus 7 Pro over both.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-08-27] **"Should've been the Note 10e" + the refreshing smallness.** "'A trimmed-down version, like the
  S10e was to the S10. Usually I'd go for the biggest phone, but **so many phones have gotten so huge that a
  smaller flagship is actually refreshing** — I can reach all four corners, no one-handed-assist needed.'" A
  dated shift toward valuing reachability.
- [2019-08-27] **THE PRINCIPLE — 1080p is a spec that "matters on paper more than in real life."** "'This is a
  perfect example of something that **matters to people a lot more on paper than in real life.** On the spec sheet
  a $950 phone with only 1080p sticks out like a sore thumb — but in reality it's **still pretty sharp at 400+
  PPI**, extremely bright, good colors.'" Reinforces the specs-skepticism thread ([[2019-05-31-yt-xjFPX7EoINs]],
  [[2019-07-26-yt-WpPw7lUXyI0]]).
- [2019-08-27] **The self-undercutting honesty — the S10+ is better for less.** "'Samsung is a smart company,
  but back in **March they made a phone that's better in a lot of ways for less money — the Galaxy S10+**: bigger
  higher-res display, up to 12 GB, better speakers, a headphone jack, a bigger battery, a remappable button —
  starting at $800 vs the Note's $950. **You trade the S Pen for all that.**'" Will point buyers away from the
  phone he's reviewing.
- [2019-08-27] **"Who is this for?" — the narrow Venn diagram.** "'So **why does the smaller Note 10 exist?** For
  the specific group that **likes the Note/S Pen experience but wants it smaller** and has $950 — a really
  specific Venn overlap. Could be you.'" His who-is-this-actually-for review structure.
- [2019-08-27] **Still recommends the OnePlus 7 Pro over both — the high-refresh raving.** "'I'd **still go for
  the OnePlus 7 Pro** over this Note and over the S10+ — costs less than both and has that **higher refresh-rate
  display I've been raving over, which I really hope a lot of phones pick up on.**' (You'd miss the better cameras
  and wireless charging.)" Consistent 90 Hz advocacy.
