# Forgot About Beats by Dre? (@mkbhd, 2021-06-17)

## Summary
A commentary on the arc of Beats by Dre — from a **fashion-accessory-with-too-much-bass** (a callback to his 2014
"The Truth About Beats by Dre") to a newly competitive Apple sub-brand. The durable ideas: Beats won ~70% of the
high-end headphone market on **marketing over R&D** and **brand cachet ("like Tesla — getting people who don't
care about audio to overspend")**; Apple bought it for **$3B (still its largest acquisition)** and kept it
**alongside AirPods, not competing**; and the new dual-native Studio Buds (USB-C, no H1, works on Android) show
**"the competition woke up."**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-06-17] **The origin — marketing over R&D bought 70% of the market.** "'For its first ~8 years Beats was a
  **stylish, borderline-fashion accessory with plenty of bass but not great fidelity** (no one mixing music
  actually used them). **They got them on every influential celebrity's head — and clearly spent way more on
  marketing than R&D.** It paid off: **they charged $300–$500 and captured ~70% of the high-end headphone
  market** from buyers who'd never spend $500 on any other pair.'"
- [2021-06-17] **The Tesla parallel — brand cachet as buying power.** "'Beats created a **commanding buying power
  / brand cachet — kind of like how Tesla today gets people who were never into cars to spend way more than they
  normally would.**'" A promotable brand-power analogy.
- [2021-06-17] **The $3B acquisition — kept alive alongside AirPods.** "'Apple bought Beats for **$3 billion —
  still their largest acquisition ever** — turned Beats Music into Apple Music, then started making wireless audio
  (AirPods, Pro, Max). **But they kept Beats alive as a high-fashion alternative that lives alongside AirPods, not
  competing with them** (Beats slowly switched to Lightning, added W1/H1 chips) — "it's all Apple; Beats success
  is Apple success."'"
- [2021-06-17] **The Studio Buds — dual-native, and "the competition woke up."** "'The new $149-with-ANC Studio
  Buds are the most interesting Beats since the acquisition: **they go dual-native — USB-C, no H1 chip, an equally
  quick pairing process on Android — the first Beats I can actually recommend for Android users.** Weird for an
  Apple-owned company, but **that's more competitive for all of us. The competition woke up.**' (The sound is
  still the classic bass-heavy, mids-light Beats signature — not the best, but more competitive than ever.)'"
- [2021-06-17] **"People did forget about Dre."** "'The Beats name is still big but doesn't have the pull it did
  10 years ago — **people aren't about to spend $400 on heavy headphones that don't sound great in 2021; YouTube
  reviews would call them out fast.** They still lean into fashion (send LeBron a pair — free real estate), but
  they're changing it up, and I like where it's going. Yes, **people did forget about Dre.**'"
