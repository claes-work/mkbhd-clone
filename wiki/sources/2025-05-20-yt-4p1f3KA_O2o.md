# Cadillac Optiq

## Summary

Marques reviews the **Cadillac Optiq**, the smaller/cheaper "Lyriq Junior," and reframes how to
think about it. Its one strength is decisive: **luxury feel for the price**. Against the stark,
minimal EX30 / Model Y / Ioniq in its class, the Optiq is the one that "feels like luxury for this
price for an EV" — heated/ventilated/massaging seats, real materials, Super Cruise standard. He
notes it's essentially "a fancy Chevy Equinox," the drive is unremarkable/soft-not-sporty, and
adds an asterisk: the full-size Lyriq isn't much more and rides better. Fully Marques-narrated.

## Key claims

- **[2025-05-20]** Callback: months ago he called the **Cadillac Lyriq underrated** ("I loved that
  thing, super good for the price") and now sees them everywhere — "I was justified." (quote)
- **[2025-05-20]** Reframe: it's NOT the Model-3-to-Model-S story. **"The Optiq is a Lyriq Junior…
  same category, a little cheaper, a lot smaller,"** starting in the $50,000s. (quote)
- **[2025-05-20]** ⭐ Core thesis: **"If you look at [the Volvo EX30, Model Y, Ioniq 5] and think
  they're so simplistic, so minimal inside, and you really want something that feels like luxury for
  this price — for an EV, that is the strength of the Optiq."** Heated/ventilated/massaging seats,
  leather + metal-feeling materials, blue trim. (quote)
- **[2025-05-20]** Under the skin: **"essentially a fancy Chevy Equinox, the same way the Lyriq is a
  fancy Chevy Blazer"** — same platform, very different interior, and that interior "makes a really
  big difference in the way you think about a vehicle." (quote)
- **[2025-05-20]** Praise: **Super Cruise standard** ("lovely"). ⚠️ **No CarPlay / Android Auto** —
  Google built-in only (Play Store, Maps, sign in with your Google account). (paraphrase)
- **[2025-05-20]** ⚠️ Weakness: **19 AKG speakers with Dolby Atmos "don't sound that good… an odd
  weakness."** (quote)
- **[2025-05-20]** Drive: "fully unremarkable… tuned for luxury over sportiness… lacks the zip the
  simpler EVs (Ioniq, Tesla, EX30) have." Soft, floaty, indirect — "that's what you're paying for."
  (quote)
- **[2025-05-20]** ⬥ Self-reference: **6'3"** — his head touches the rear ceiling trim. (paraphrase)
- **[2025-05-20]** ⚠️ Asterisk verdict: **"The Lyriq rides better and has more storage… it's only a
  few grand more. Because the Optiq is smaller, it's probably better for more people (city drivers),
  but if you have the space, the Lyriq is not that much more."** (quote)
