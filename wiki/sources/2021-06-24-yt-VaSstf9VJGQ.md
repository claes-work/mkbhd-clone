# Windows 11 Reactions: It Runs Android Apps! (Kinda) (@mkbhd, 2021-06-24)

## Summary
Reactions to the Windows 11 reveal, framed by the **Windows odd/even quality cycle** (XP good, Vista bad, 7 good,
8 bad, 10 good — "11, this is your chance to break the cycle"). Durable notes: a biography reveal — **he's a Mac
user because of Final Cut Pro, not macOS** (happy in Windows); the headline **Android apps running natively** (via
the Amazon App Store + Intel Bridge — "the most convoluted version") and the principle **"meet people where their
apps are"**; and the verdict that Microsoft **"might have just broken the cycle."**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-06-24] **The odd/even quality cycle.** "'**Windows XP great, Vista not, 7 great, 8 (tiles) not, 10 great —
  and now 11: no pressure, Microsoft, but this is your chance to break that cycle.**'" A memorable framing.
- [2021-06-24] **BIOGRAPHY — "I'm a Mac user because of Final Cut, not macOS."** "'A lot of you know me as a Mac
  user — but **that's not because of macOS; I switched at work because of an app: Final Cut Pro. I'm perfectly
  happy to spend time in Windows,** and I felt more compelled to react to this Windows reveal than anything Apple
  has done in desktop lately.'" A datable persona clarification.
- [2021-06-24] **The refreshed glassy aesthetic + great window management.** "'A dramatically refreshed, **glassy/
  translucent** design (centered Start by default; a widgets pane). **Window management stays a Windows strength —
  new snap presets on hover, and displays now memorize window positions when you unplug/replug a monitor.**' (Teams
  built-in is odd — "they own Skype, which they paid $8.5B for.")'"
- [2021-06-24] **THE HEADLINE — Android apps run natively (kinda).** "'**Android apps can now run natively in
  Windows 11 — but really it's the Amazon App Store loaded into the Windows Store, run through Intel Bridge (you
  need a Microsoft AND Amazon account) — "a super official side-load," the most convoluted version of it.**' It
  won't be perfect (bad for non-touch input), but you can say you offer Android apps on Windows.'"
- [2021-06-24] **THE PRINCIPLE — "meet people where their apps are."** "'The move makes perfect sense: **meet the
  people where you want them to be with the apps they already like** — the same reason you can run Android apps on
  a Chromebook and iOS apps on a Mac.' Verdict: **"Not bad — I'm impressed. I think they might have just broken
  the cycle."**'" A promotable platform-strategy read.

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "I switched to macOS for the most part at work because of an app — because of Final Cut Pro. So I'm perfectly
> happy to spend time in Windows all the time."
> "Meet the people where you want them to be with their apps that they already like — it's the same reason you can
> run Android apps on a Chromebook and iOS apps on a Mac."
> "I think they might have just broken the cycle."
