# Smart Home Tech I ACTUALLY Use! (@mkbhd, 2022-10-03)

## Summary
A tour of the smart-home gear he actually uses — from his **new basement home studio** (a lighter ingest, but with
strong biography). His framing: **the "smart homes of the future" mostly don't exist yet** (even the $30M NYC
skyscraper apartments he toured had almost nothing), and **he deliberately kept his simple** (mostly regular bulbs;
one set of smart lights). The key early decision was **which ecosystem — he chose Google** (his main phone is
Android; he uses Assistant all the time), with the caveat that **Google buying Nest made the app worse** (the great
Nest app's features scattered into Google Home). The gear: a Nest Hub Max, Nest thermostats (with **Nest Renew** for
cleaner power), a Nest doorbell + cameras, a Yale smart lock, Philips Hue lamps (**pointed at the wall** to bounce
light onto his face for the webcam), Netgear Orbi mesh (tripled his speeds), a Kasa smart plug, and Sonos. The
durable habits: his **"hey Google, how's my day" morning routine** and the **"don't touch my phone first thing in
the morning"** practice. Biography beats: the **new basement studio (in progress)** and a **first $0 electricity
bill from rooftop solar + battery.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-10-03] **The smart homes of the future mostly don't exist — so keep it simple.** "'A few years ago I toured
  $30M NYC skyscraper apartments expecting cool tech — and there was almost nothing (an iPad mini bolted to a wall).
  **The "smart homes of the future" we hoped for mostly don't exist yet.** I kept mine pretty simple — **mostly
  regular bulbs, one set of smart lights** — no wall of robot vacuums.'"
- [2022-10-03] **The key decision — the ecosystem (I chose Google).** "'The real early choice is **which ecosystem —
  Google, Apple, or Amazon — because you set everything up in one place. I chose Google, no regrets** (my main phone
  is Android; I use Assistant all the time). The downside: **Google bought Nest, and the amazing Nest app's features
  scattered into the Google Home app** (I'm still hoping they re-add the thermostat schedule, older Nest pairing).'"
- [2022-10-03] **The gear — and pointing lights at the wall.** "'A Nest Hub Max (rock-solid for years), Nest
  thermostats (learn your habits; **Nest Renew** optimizes for cleaner grid power), a Nest battery doorbell (~3
  weeks/charge for me) + indoor/outdoor cameras with smart zones, a **Yale smart lock** (peace of mind). Smart
  lights: two Philips Hue lamps I **point at the wall** — one bounces light onto my face for the webcam, one lights
  the room (I like pointing lights at the wall). Netgear Orbi mesh **tripled my speeds**; a cheap Kasa smart plug;
  and Sonos (the best-sounding speakers in my house).'"
- [2022-10-03] **Routines + "don't touch your phone first thing."** "'The fun part is routines — **"hey Google,
  how's my day" reads the weather, calendar, my phone's battery, the news, traffic, and opens the shades.** It ties
  to a habit I highly recommend: **I don't touch my phone first thing in the morning — I get up, touch the ground,
  walk away from my bed without it** — so it's nice to ask Google what's going on instead of scrolling.'"
- [2022-10-03] **Biography — the new studio and the $0 electricity bill.** "'Coming at you from **the new basement
  home studio (a work in progress; I'll do more videos from here).** And baked into the home itself: **I recently
  got my first $0 electricity bill now that I have solar on the roof** (with a battery setup that works with the
  Nest thermostat) — maybe a separate video.'"
