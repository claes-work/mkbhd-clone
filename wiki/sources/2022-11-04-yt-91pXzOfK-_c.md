# Hasselblad X2D: The Best, Worst Camera (@TheStudio, 2022-11-04)

> ⚠️ ATTRIBUTION: **Marques-narrated** (distinct from David's technical camera essays): the
> old-Porsche-craftsmanship framing, "going directly into the **Mac Pro**," "I leave most of
> the editing to the professionals," and the sleep-on-it rule are Marques tells. A camera-as-
> *experience* appreciation, not a specs teardown.

## Summary
Marques on the $9,000 Hasselblad X2D — a "best, worst camera" paradox: objectively worse
than an R5/A7S for most people (no video, slow AF, tiny lens ecosystem), but he loves it for
the craftsmanship and the deliberate one-photo-at-a-time *experience*. A window into how he
relates to gear as an object.

## Key claims (Marques-attributed only)
- [2022-11-04] **The "best, worst camera" paradox / experience-over-specs.** A $9k camera
  that "doesn't shoot video, has slow autofocus, one photo every few seconds, a tiny lens
  ecosystem — but the experience, that's what it's about." He frames it exactly like
  **"the way I hear people talk about old sports cars, like 1970s Porsche 911s"** — you'd
  be better off with a cheaper R5/A7S, "but if you're into the craftsmanship and the process
  of capturing one photo, there's something about these cameras." (tech-reviews, voice)
- [2022-11-04] **How he works with the files** (corroborating documented habits): the X2D's
  medium-format 100-MP DNGs are "a thicker file, more to play with — if I was going to edit a
  photo, I'd pick the Hasselblad"; but he **"leaves most of the editing of the photos to the
  professionals"** and just "has fun with the dynamic range." Ingests **directly into the
  Mac Pro**. Sleep-on-it: with a Hasselblad file "I want to look at it, sit on it, sleep on
  it, wake up and edit some more." He even notes **Canon color straight-out-of-camera often
  looks better**. (tech-reviews, production-filmmaking)
- [2022-11-04] The event-vs-craft distinction: bringing a Hasselblad to CES was "a mistake —
  an event is about utility, capture it quickly and move on," which "is not what this is
  about." (tech-reviews)

## Notable verbatim quotes (Marques)
- "The experience — that's what it's about… it sounds like an old guy talking about an old Porsche, right?"
- "If I was going to edit a photo, I'd pick the Hasselblad."
- "I leave most of the editing of the photos to the professionals, and I have some fun with it."
