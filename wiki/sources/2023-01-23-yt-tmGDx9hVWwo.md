# M2 Max MacBook Pro Review: Back to Bumps! (@mkbhd, 2023-01-23)

## Summary
A review of the M2 Pro/M2 Max 14"/16" MacBook Pros — launched by press release plus an 18-minute video that
"looked like it was cut out of another event" (the URL, file name, and AR files suggest it was ready months
earlier). The takeaway: **externally identical to the M1 versions; the change is the chip** (Apple quotes
~20% CPU / ~30% GPU), plus small extras (Wi-Fi 6E, Bluetooth 5.3, **HDMI 2.1** finally, an extra hour of
battery, a color-matched MagSafe cable). The framing lesson: **the big leap was Intel → Apple Silicon; now
it's back to incremental "bumps."** He grounds "Pro" in a real-workflow benchmark he actually notices — a
**3.5-min screen recording exported to ProRes 422**: a $15,000 Intel Mac Pro did it in 56s, the M1 Max in
32s, and the **M2 Max in 23s.** His recommendation: **these aren't for people who already have M1 Pro/Max
laptops** — they're for people on older machines, and those M1 owners should hunt for discounted M1 Pro/Max
units. On waiting for M3 (rumored on 3nm this year): **don't** — "you should actually wait till Q3 2027 for
the M5s... if you're gonna wait for the next thing, you'll just be waiting forever, because that's just the
way tech is." Two running bits: **Apple "forgot about the iMac"** (still on M1), and he honors a prior
wager — **wearing socks-and-sandals all day** because the notch stayed and Face ID didn't arrive.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2023-01-23] **Incremental — the real leap already happened.** "'The first Apple Silicon MacBook Pros
  (Oct 2021) were some of the most unanimously well-liked computers I've ever seen — the big leap was Intel
  to Apple Silicon. A year and a half later there isn't a ton we expect to change; we're back to incremental
  improvements. Completely identical on the outside; the bulk of what's new is the M2 Pro/M2 Max chip.'"
- [2023-01-23] **"Pro" is about what it lets you do faster — the export benchmark.** "'It's not so much the
  raw processing power as the things it lets you do better or faster — hence the name Pro. My screen-
  recording-to-ProRes-422 export: a $15,000+ Intel Mac Pro did a 3.5-minute recording in 56 seconds, the M1
  Max laptop in 32, and this M2 Max in 23. A pretty substantial improvement you instantly notice.'"
- [2023-01-23] **Who it's for — not M1 Pro/Max owners.** "'These laptops aren't for people who already have
  the M1 Pro/Max laptops that just came out — they're for people on older machines who now have a better one
  to upgrade to (unless you're the tiny fraction pushing the old one to its limits, in which case you know
  who you are). If you have an M1, look for sales on the M1 Pro/Max now — those are great laptops.'"
- [2023-01-23] **Don't wait for the next thing.** "'You might wonder if you should wait for the M3 later
  this year. You should actually wait till Q3 2027 for the M5s — 30% faster than the M4s, which are 30%
  faster than the M3s, and you see where this goes. If you're going to wait for the next thing, you'll just
  be waiting forever — that's just the way tech is.'"
- [2023-01-23] **They forgot about the iMac; the notch/Face ID bet.** "'The transition still isn't complete
  — no Mac Pro yet, and it feels like they forgot about the iMac, still stuck at M1. And I said if the next
  version still has a notch this big and no Face ID, I'd wear socks and sandals for a day like Linus — I'm a
  man of my word, so all day today.'"
