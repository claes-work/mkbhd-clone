# OnePlus 8 Review: Special No More! (@mkbhd, 2020-04-16)

## Summary
The $699 OnePlus 8 review — "**special no more.**" A good phone that's no longer the singular flagship-killer,
which sets up a durable industry framework: the **stratification of phone prices** has crowded the middle OnePlus
used to own.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-04-16] **THE FRAMEWORK — the stratification of phone prices.** "'A couple years ago high-end phones were
  ~$600-700, so you were **either racing to the top (best flagship) or racing to the bottom (best budget)** —
  OnePlus slipped into the middle as the "flagship killer." **Now there's chaos at every tier: $200-300 budget /
  $400-600 mid / $700-900 premium / $1,000-1,400 ultra-premium** — the middle OnePlus owned is crowded.'" A
  reusable map of the 2020 phone market.
- [2020-04-16] **"Flagship killer no more."** "'OnePlus is **moving away from "flagship killer"** (new slogan "fast
  and smooth") now that they actually make a flagship. This is exactly the OnePlus we've always known — high-end
  chip, cut a few things to hit the price.'" Continues the OnePlus budget→premium arc.
- [2020-04-16] **The smart cost-saving trade-offs (90 Hz / 1080p).** "'**90 Hz (not 120) and 1080p (not 1440p)** —
  smart moves. **I can see the 90-vs-120 difference but it's small; both crush 60 Hz.** With a 4,300 mAh battery it
  gets **slightly better battery life than the maxed-out 8 Pro.**'" His high-refresh nuance.
- [2020-04-16] **The classic OnePlus camera problem + no wireless charging.** "'Classic OnePlus: **amazing
  everything else, sub-par camera** (a step down from flagship). **If you're serious about camera quality, don't
  look here — get a Pixel 3a or the new iPhone SE** (an iPhone-quality camera for $399). And **no wireless charging
  at all** — could they have just swallowed their pride and added a coil?'"
- [2020-04-16] **METHODOLOGY — "I'm going to start including price in reviews."** "'I usually don't go deep on
  price (it changes over time), but **I'm going to change that — because the positioning of these phones is
  sometimes the reason they live or die** (ever since the Essential Phone).'" A dated shift in review method.

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "It's the stratification of phone prices over these past couple years that has caused this situation... now
> there's more chaos, more activity than ever at all of these different price ranges."
> "It's a nice phone, it's not special anymore, but it is one of the favorites in this awkward middle-child price
> point."
