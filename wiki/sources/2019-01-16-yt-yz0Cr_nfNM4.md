# First Look at the Hole-Punch Display! (@mkbhd, 2019-01-16)

## Summary
A first-look at the hole-punch display (via the Honor View 20) — framed as **the 2019 way to avoid the notch.**
Marques assesses the implementation honestly (it's bigger than renders; off-center; a software shadow) and
restates his wish to put the cutout in the middle.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-01-16] **"This is how we get away from the notch in 2019."** "The most interesting designs the past couple
  months have been phones **avoiding the notch** — and the **hole punch** is the 2019 answer (Samsung's 'Infinity-O',
  this Honor View 20). Push the screen to the corners and put a **camera cutout in the top-left** instead of a
  notch." A dated design-trend read.
- [2019-01-16] **The implementation critiques (honest first-look).** "**It's bigger than the renders suggested** —
  a full camera module needs a full opening. On this phone it's **not centered even in the rounded corner** (tucked
  above center to sit in the notification bar) — **hard to un-notice; it looks a little off.** And there's a
  **darkened shadow ring around it** — I reached out to Huawei; I *think* it's added in software (the UI has similar
  shadows elsewhere), but if it's the LCD/backlight hardware, **OLED may solve it** in future phones." Specific,
  reported-out criticism.
- [2019-01-16] **Apps treat it like a notch.** "So far apps play with the hole punch **the same as a notch** —
  they ignore the top space (you can hide it in settings). Full-screen apps mostly work ("just a hole in the UI");
  some corner text/overlaps look weird for now, but people will get used to it and apps will update." Calibrated.
- [2019-01-16] **The recurring wish — "put it in the MIDDLE."** "I **still wish someone would do it in the middle** —
  there's no time or notifications in the middle; **that's where no info usually needs to be** (that's where the
  notch was). But the **corner cutout is the most popular 2019 implementation.**" His consistent design opinion.
- [2019-01-16] **What's coming.** Notes the **Huawei Nova 4** (already at CES) and the **Galaxy S10** (announced for
  late February) — "the S10 should have a single *and* a dual (S10+) cutout on the highest-end OLEDs; **I'm super
  ready for that.**" Predicts clever **dark-wallpaper/gradient** hiding as the trend spreads.

## Notable verbatim quotes
