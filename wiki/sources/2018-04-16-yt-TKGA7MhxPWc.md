# Thank You! (@mkbhd, 2018-04-16)

## Summary
A biography goldmine: Marques wins the **Shorty Award "Creator of the Decade,"** and — having had no acceptance
speech ready — uses the video to reflect on the 10-year rise of tech YouTube as "new media," his own origin,
and a parallel **Stevens Tech alumni achievement award**. One of the clearest windows into how he frames his
own career.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-04-16] **BIOGRAPHY — wins the Shorty "Creator of the Decade."** After asking viewers to vote, he won —
  "in a ridiculous category" against **Casey Neistat, Liza Koshy, Jenna Marbles, Lilly Singh (Superwoman), and
  Rhett & Link** — and admits "I obviously did not expect to win." The prior year (2017) he was a finalist for
  **YouTuber of the Year**. A concrete, dated career milestone.
- [2018-04-16] **BIOGRAPHY — the Stevens Tech alumni achievement award.** The Saturday before the Shorties, he was
  honored by his school **Stevens Institute of Technology** with an **Alumni Achievement Award**, "alongside a
  bunch of other engineers and business people — even the **CIO of SpaceX** went to my school and was honored
  there." Ties his engineering-school identity to his media career.
- [2018-04-16] **The "new media" framing.** Stevens called what he does **"new media"** — a term that stuck with
  him. "Social media or new media — it ties into this slow rise versus old/traditional media." His lens for
  situating creator work against legacy media.
- [2018-04-16] **The 10-year arc of tech YouTube (his community).** "I can speak to what I've seen in the tech
  YouTube community — its formation, developing its own voice and style in both delivery and production, and
  creating its own immense value. **Tech YouTubers went from an afterthought → a fun little niche → a staple of
  new media** — covering Google/Samsung/Apple events, CES, trade shows, doing big interviews and collabs. And
  it's all just getting started." His history-of-the-medium read.
- [2018-04-16] **ORIGIN restated — "a kid in a bedroom."** "For me this all started as **a kid in a bedroom
  talking to a camera about tech, because it's fun as hell and I love tech.**" The canonical origin framing,
  restated at a milestone. Closes: "To all the creators — thank you, keep doing what you're doing, **don't let
  anyone tell you to stop.**"
