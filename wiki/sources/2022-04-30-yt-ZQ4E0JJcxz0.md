# What Happens When the iPhone Peaks? (@mkbhd, 2022-04-30)

## Summary
A thesis piece hung on one iPhone 14 rumor. His long-standing position: **"are we at peak smartphone?" — No, as
long as things are objectively getting better every year** (more power, new features, folding, under-display
sensors) — even if the pace has clearly slowed since the huge-leap 2007–2009 era. But the rumor that **only the
iPhone 14 Pro gets the new A16, while the non-Pro 14 keeps the same A15 as the 13** would be **unprecedented — two
flagships launched a year apart with the exact same SoC** — and it **weakens his "not at peak" argument.** He plays
both sides (chip shortage + you'd never notice the chip difference for years = Apple's "headroom" gift, being shorted
a year) and lands on **the pricing-ladder differentiation problem: how does Apple get on stage and sell the 14 as
not-basically-the-13?** ("The Apple way is praising the old phone, then selling it again with a new name at a higher
price.") Also debuts the **MKBHD × MotionVFX Final Cut/Premiere/Resolve plugin.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-04-30] **Not at peak smartphone — as long as it keeps getting better.** "'Are we at peak smartphone? The gut
  reaction is yes — but **I've always said no, not as long as things are objectively getting better every year**
  (more power/memory/resolution, under-display cameras and fingerprint readers, phones folding in half). If "peak"
  means the apex where phones stop getting better and go flat, amazingly we're not there yet — **though they've
  clearly slowed a lot since the huge-leap 2007–2009 era.**'"
- [2022-04-30] **The unprecedented rumor — the same A15 in the non-Pro 14.** "'The most interesting rumored spec is
  the same as last year: **only the 14 Pro/Pro Max get the new A16; the non-Pro 14 and 14 Max keep the A15 that's
  in the phone I'm already using.** When have we ever seen two flagships launch a year apart with the exact same
  SoC? I don't think we ever have.'"
- [2022-04-30] **Both sides have a point — the headroom argument.** "'The nerds will react ("so greedy, selling the
  same phone with a new name") — but the defenses hold too: there's a chip shortage, and honestly **you'd take
  years to notice an A15 vs A14 difference outside a benchmark.** Apple's high-end chips are about **headroom** —
  so maybe you're shorting new 14 buyers a year of it. Both sides have a point.'"
- [2022-04-30] **It weakens my "not at peak" argument — and the differentiation problem.** "'If Apple does this, **my
  "we're not at peak smartphone" statement gets a bit weaker.** And my one question: **how does Apple get on stage
  and talk about the 14 like it's not basically the same phone as the 13?** The pricing-ladder differentiation is
  the puzzle — if a $599 iPhone 13 stays around, why ever get a $699 14? **The Apple way is usually praising the
  old phone (it sold well), then selling it again with a new name at a higher price** — I don't know if that
  works this time (unless there are unrumored features, or one more year of updates, or the extra RAM).'"
- [2022-04-30] **The MKBHD × MotionVFX plugin.** "'We made our own video-editing plugin with MotionVFX for Final
  Cut / Premiere / Resolve — the lower-third here is from it. If you're a creator, check it out.'" (A creator-
  business/product beat.)
