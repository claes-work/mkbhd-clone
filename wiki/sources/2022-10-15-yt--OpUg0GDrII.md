# Pixel Watch Review: First Gen Fumbles! (@mkbhd, 2022-10-15)

## Summary
A review of Google's first smartwatch — the long-hyped $350 Pixel Watch, and a disappointment. The **hardware is
beautiful** (a lightweight 41 mm "literal pebble," a domed Gorilla Glass circle, a great clicky crown, a clever
lugless band system) — **but two things hold it back: the small screen/substantial bezels and, above all, the
battery, which is "trash."** The 24-hour claim is really ~18 hrs because it **measures heart rate every single
second and never reduces the pings** (the way mature watches save battery) — so **sleep trackers must charge it
twice a day.** The other fumble: **Fitbit is "bolted on, not built in"** (a separate Fitbit account, key metrics
behind a **$10/mo paywall**, Google Fit orphaned, no synced Do Not Disturb). His durable meta-observation:
**"reviewing products you're hoping to be good makes you sound really harsh — like a disappointed parent."** There's
real potential (Assistant on the wrist, the most-accurate heart-rate tracking he's used) — but **"far from
finished."**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-10-15] **Beautiful pebble hardware — but a small screen.** "'For $350 you get a lightweight 41 mm circular
  watch — **a literal pebble** — with a domed Gorilla Glass front, a great clicky crown, and a **clever lugless
  push-and-slide band system** (important to nail in gen 1 so bands stay compatible). Hardware-wise it's beautiful.
  **But it's small — the 41 mm is the casing; the screen is ~29 mm with substantial bezels** (the mostly-black UI
  hides them, like a notch — you only notice when looking) — I wish there were a larger version.'"
- [2022-10-15] **The battery is trash — it never reduces the heart-rate pings.** "'The bezel worry pales next to the
  battery: **it's trash.** The 24-hr claim (one day + one night) should have been a red flag; with the always-on
  display on and **it measuring heart rate every single second all day**, I get ~18 hrs — not enough for a day plus
  sleep tracking, so **you have to charge it twice a day.** The Apple Watch/Galaxy Watch save battery by pinging
  sensors LESS often (battery-saver = fewer heart-rate/GPS pings, no always-on) — **this pings constantly, which is
  cool (the most accurate heart-rate tracking I've tried) but nukes the battery.** (Saving grace: it charges very
  fast — 10→40% in 15 min.)'"
- [2022-10-15] **Fitbit is bolted on, not built in.** "'The clumsiest thing: **Fitbit isn't built into the watch so
  much as bolted onto the side.** Wear OS 3.5 is fine and familiar — but you make a **Fitbit account**, the fitness
  data dumps into the Fitbit app (not Google Fit, which is orphaned), and a **ton of it (breathing rate, HRV, sleep
  stages/scores) goes behind a $10/mo Fitbit paywall** after the 6-month trial. You can see the arc: Google bought
  Fitbit, people love the name, so it can't be removed — now there's a Google watch with Fitbit in it AND Google
  Fit in the background, and they don't talk to each other (even Do Not Disturb doesn't sync from the phone).'"
- [2022-10-15] **Reviewing things you hope are good — the disappointed-parent problem.** "'This is the funny thing
  about **reviewing products you're hoping to be good — you end up sounding really harsh, like a disappointed
  parent.** There's real potential and parts I enjoy (Assistant on my wrist, offline music, Maps turn-by-turn,
  voice-to-text) — but the first-gen learnings are clear (the charger, the constant heart-rate monitoring, the
  Fitbit integration, connectivity issues some reviewers had).'"
- [2022-10-15] **Far from finished.** "'I was hoping this would be amazing from the start — but first-gen stuff
  never is, and this starts from zero (few bands/accessories). **For $350 you could get a more mature smartwatch
  with more features, better battery, and things that aren't behind a paywall.** For their first foray into forearm
  fitness and fashion — **functionally it's still far from finished.**' (Biography: I change watch bands every
  single day, and sleep in a different band.)'"
