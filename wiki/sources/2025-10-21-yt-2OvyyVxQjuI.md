# The Weirdest 'Car' I've Ever Driven

## Summary

Marques becomes the first person outside Aptera to drive the **Aptera** — a three-wheeled solar
EV that isn't legally a car (it's registered as an "autocycle"/motorcycle). The pitch is deeply
appealing: **400mi range, 2,200 lb, solar panels for up to 40 free miles/day, ~$35k** — but the
execution is "an unbelievable amount of quirks" (Doug-DeMuro-style) and it's a not-final
prototype. His verdict: a compelling efficiency idea "built around nice-weather regions," but
maybe "too different to get people to commit." The driving section is a dialogue with a co-driver
(quarantined).

## Key claims — SUBJECT-ATTRIBUTED (Marques)

- **[2025-10-21]** People have sent him the Aptera "for years" ("it's coming soon"), and now there's
  real momentum — Aptera let him be **the first outside the company to drive it.** "Spoiler: the idea
  is really good, but so many things about it are very different from a normal car." (paraphrase)
- **[2025-10-21]** What it is: a **three-wheeled autocycle** (motorcycle plate/insurance/registration,
  no special license needed), **41 usable kWh**, **~400mi base range**, **solar panels everywhere**
  (~4.5kWh/~40mi per sunny day → "if you commute under 40 miles a day and park in the sun, you drive
  for free"), a forged-carbon body (one of the biggest single carbon pieces in the world), an NACS
  port, and a teardrop shape for maximal aero (cf. the Mercedes EQXX). (paraphrase)
- **[2025-10-21]** The Doug-style "quirks and features": spider-up doors with a **secret-knock** entry
  (no handles), a split window, a single center rear wheel, and **an extremely minimal touchscreen-
  everything interior** (windows/AC/park-reverse-drive all on-screen) — "I'd rather have a window
  switch and a door lock switch." (paraphrase)
- **[2025-10-21]** The critique (recurring): **"my initial reaction to any car like this is it feels
  so clearly built around nice-weather regions"** — like the solar Prius (one of Auto Focus's most
  popular videos), the panels only help in sunny places, and secret-knock doors + covered wheels
  won't work in ice/snow. "Good for Californians and warm climates." (quote)
- **[2025-10-21]** ⬥ A real safety flaw he flags: **the solar dashboard glare "blinds me to whatever's
  in front of me… that could be dangerous — I don't think they can keep that."** (quote)
- **[2025-10-21]** His theory: ~50,000 pre-orders but not more because **"it's different, and that's
  what's getting attention — but maybe too different to get people to commit to buying it. Still,
  it's interesting enough that it could push the industry a little, which is exciting."** (quote)
