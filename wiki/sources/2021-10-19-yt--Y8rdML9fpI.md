## Summary
Team first look at the Pixel 6 / 6 Pro on launch day. Marques's substantive contribution is his
design read (it looks intentionally un-iPhone) and a pricing analysis he calls aggressive.

## Key claims (Marques-attributed only)
- [2021-10-19] **Design — deliberately doesn't look like anything else:** "the one thing I
  remember talking to them [Google] about was — it doesn't look like any other phone, and that's
  on purpose. You know what an iPhone looks like... this just doesn't look like any of those. And
  that's true, for better or for worse: it looks like a Pixel." (tech-reviews)
- [2021-10-19] **Pricing verdict — aggressive:** $599 (Pixel 6) / $899 (6 Pro). "$899 is pretty
  good and... $599 is really good"; "$300 difference — how are they going to upsell people to
  [the Pro]?"; notes the 6 Pro is "$100 less than the iPhone 13 Pro." (tech-reviews, consumer-
  tech-culture — reads the 6 as the value pick, questions the Pro's upsell)
- [2021-10-19] **Ergonomics — the horizontal camera bar as a grip:** unlike a corner bump, the
  full-width bar gives "a nice place to rest your finger" where he already picks up his phone.
  Notes the 6 Pro "feels more like the Pixel 4 XL than the Pixel 5" (big). (tech-reviews)
- [2021-10-19] **On pre-review price ignorance (his method):** "go back and watch my first-
  impressions video when I didn't know the prices — I kind of was guessing... I guessed [the Pro]
  would be a thousand somewhere in there." (creator-business — he forms first impressions before
  seeing price on purpose)
