# I Tried Samsung's Secret Android XR Headset! (@mkbhd, 2025-01-27)

## Summary
An exclusive first look at **Project Moohan** — the **first Android XR headset** (a deep Google+Samsung collaboration,
"the Nexus/Pixel but for headsets"), an unfinished prototype Samsung intends to ship in 2025. It **looks a lot like a
Vision Pro** (light fabrics, a big reflective front glass — but no outward eye display), with a Quest-Pro-style rear
head-cup + dial, a Snapdragon chip, hand + eye tracking (no controllers needed in the demo), and an **external battery
pack — but with a removable USB-C cable** (so you can swap in any battery, unlike Apple's). His key reframe: **the
hardware isn't what makes it cool — Android XR is.** It fixes the **VR-OS fragmentation** (Vision Pro/visionOS, Quest/
Horizon OS, and a pile of other CES headsets with nowhere to go) by giving any headset a high-baseline OS. Its two big
advantages: **the Play Store** (all phone/tablet apps work, resizable to any aspect ratio, plus optimized "spatial"
apps like YouTube) and **Gemini** — which he found **the most interesting part**: multimodal (it sees what you see —
translate signs, answer trivia), **it navigates the UI and apps *for* you** (organize/close windows, jump around Google
Maps by voice), and **circle-to-search in real life** — and crucially it **reduces the arm-fatigue** of pinching and
reaching ("surprisingly high on my list of useful features"). He calls this **"a massive difference vs. the Vision Pro,
which has no AI features at all and probably won't soon."** Display/FOV are "fine, a notch behind Vision Pro." His
takeaway: it's less about the hardware and more about **Google's first genuinely new platform/OS in ~10 years** — "like
test-driving a concept car." If this is **"the Pixel 1 of Android XR,"** it opens room for higher- and lower-end
headsets to run the same OS, inspiring more spatial apps that make **every Android XR headset better.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2025-01-27] **The first Android XR headset — the Pixel of headsets, fixing fragmentation.** "'Project Moohan is the
  first Android XR headset — a deep Google+Samsung collaboration, think the Nexus or Pixel but for headsets. It's a
  potentially huge step toward fixing the fragmentation in VR/mixed-reality: Vision Pro runs visionOS, Quest runs
  Horizon OS, and all the other CES headsets have nowhere to go. Android XR gives any headset a high-baseline OS.'"
- [2025-01-27] **It's the software, not the hardware.** "'It looks like a Vision Pro, but I came away not thinking much
  about the hardware — because that's not what makes it cool, it's Android XR. If you're Google and Samsung building the
  Pixel of headsets, your move to give people a reason over a Vision Pro is the same as the Pixel: software.'"
- [2025-01-27] **Gemini is the most interesting part.** "'The Gemini integration was the most interesting part.
  Multimodal — it sees what you see (translate a sign, answer trivia). It navigates the UI and apps *for* you — organize
  and close windows, jump around Google Maps by voice. And there's circle-to-search in real life. Best of all, it
  reduces the arm-fatigue of pinching and reaching — surprisingly high on my list of useful features.'"
- [2025-01-27] **A massive difference vs. the Vision Pro.** "'This is a massive difference versus the Vision Pro, which
  has no Apple Intelligence, no AI features at all, and probably won't be getting any soon. The removable USB-C battery
  cable is also better than Apple's — swap in any battery.'"
- [2025-01-27] **The Pixel 1 of Android XR — the platform is the point.** "'It's been more fascinating to see what
  Google's doing at a software level with their first genuinely new platform/OS in ~10 years — like test-driving a
  concept car. If this is the Pixel 1 of Android XR, it leaves room for higher- and lower-end headsets to run the same
  OS, inspiring more spatial apps that make every Android XR headset better.'"
