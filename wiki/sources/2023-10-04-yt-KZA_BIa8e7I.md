# Google Pixel 8/8 Pro Impressions: Software Magic! (@mkbhd, 2023-10-04)

## Summary
A hands-on with the Pixel 8 / 8 Pro (opening with sarcasm about how thoroughly they leaked). The hardware improves —
rounder design, brighter displays (2,000 / **2,400 nits**, "the highest I've ever seen"), the small Pixel 8 now
**120 Hz**, and the new **Tensor G3** — but Google "didn't flex raw performance": the G3's story is **way more TPU
for AI**, because **"the Pixel is basically the smartest smartphone in the room"** — it wows you with clever
software, not specs. The software pile: Assistant summarizing a webpage, faster on-device speech-to-text (that drops
"ums"), a more-human **Call Screen voice**, and the camera magic — **Magic Editor** (select subjects and move/resize
them, change the sky/shadows — "nothing seems off limits"), **Best Take** (stitch everyone's best face from a burst
— "a Frankenstein photo of a moment that never happened — *what is a photo?*"), and **Audio Eraser**. The headline
non-camera news: **seven years of OS/security/feature updates** ("industry-leading... they're leading the way" — a
phone updated until 2030) — plus a **$100 price bump** ($699 / $999) he thinks is "a fair price, even if not the
bargain of the year." Weird extras: a **temperature sensor**, exceptional refined **haptics**, and the odd
cloud-processed **Video Boost** (coming later). Also a Pixel Watch 2 spec bump. (dbrand Ghost case.)

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2023-10-04] **Tensor G3 is about AI, not raw power — the smartest smartphone.** "'Google didn't flex raw
  performance much; the G3 story is way more TPU and processing for AI. That checks out — the Pixel has always been
  about software. It's basically the smartest smartphone in the room: it won't wow you with numbers or build
  quality, it'll wow you with very clever software at every corner.'"
- [2023-10-04] **Best Take — a moment that never happened; what is a photo?** "'Best Take detects a burst of a group
  photo and lets you tap each person's face and swap to a different instance from a different time — stitching a
  Frankenstein photo with everyone having perfect faces, even though it's a moment that never happened. What is a
  photo?'"
- [2023-10-04] **Magic Editor — nothing seems off limits.** "'Magic Editor lets you select subjects and just move
  them around, change the sky, change shadows — completely alter the photo with a few taps. Honestly, nothing seems
  off limits. All of it is software magic enabled by the Tensor G3's neural cores.'"
- [2023-10-04] **Seven years of updates — leading the way.** "'The massive update for both phones: seven years of OS,
  security, and feature-drop updates — a phone that gets its last update in 2030. In the Android world that's
  unprecedented; they're leading the way. Good looks, Google.'"
- [2023-10-04] **A $100 bump — a fair price, not the bargain of the year.** "'The Pixel 8 is $699 ($100 more) and the
  8 Pro $999. I think you can justify it — the base got a 120 Hz, much brighter display, thinner bezels, the Tensor
  G3, and the same primary camera as the more expensive phone. It's a fair price, even if it's not the bargain of
  the year it used to be.'"
