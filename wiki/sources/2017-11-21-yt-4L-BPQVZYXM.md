# Razer Phone Review: The Real Deal! (@mkbhd, 2017-11-21)

## Summary
The full Razer Phone review — "the real deal." A gaming-first phone that impressed him more the longer he used
it. Clone-relevant for the **"opposite of the iPhone X" design read** (boxy matte-black brick, purposeful big
bezels + huge front speakers, 120Hz), his matte-black taste, and a candid gripe (the buttons).

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2017-11-21] **"The real deal" — impressed more over time.** "It came out of nowhere (the Razer/Nextbit
  acquisition), I didn't expect much, and it impressed me a lot. Everything I liked in the first impressions —
  the build, the industrial-design choices, how unique it is — I still like a lot. I actually love it."
  Substance-over-first-impressions confirmed by living with it.
- [2017-11-21] **"The opposite of the iPhone X" — and I dig it.** "Where all modern phones chase a sleek,
  curvy 'pebble' look, the Razer Phone is a boxy, squared-off brick — and I kind of dig it. At this point no
  other phone looks like it." Values a phone that stands out against the converging-design trend. (Consistent
  with his "big bezels on purpose" first-impressions read.)
- [2017-11-21] **The matte-black taste — a pleasant surprise.** "One color — the same matte black as Razer's
  laptops — and it's been surprisingly good at not catching fingerprints (matte black usually catches a lot),
  which was a pleasant surprise." His matte-black value, with an honest caveat resolved.
- [2017-11-21] **The design nitpick (the logo cutout).** "I wish the logo weren't in a little cutout — it's
  laser-etched but sits in a recess, so it feels like a sticker I could scrape off; feels cheaper. Just put
  the etching on the outside of the metal." A specific, feel-over-looks critique.
- [2017-11-21] **What's prime vs. what's terrible.** "Everything else outside is prime — **huge front-facing
  speakers**, a **Quad HD IPS display (120Hz)**, clean lines, well-hidden antenna lines; way more of an
  all-metal beast than the flimsy plastic Nextbit Robin it descends from. **But the buttons are terrible** —
  I wish they'd ditched them." His good-buttons value, inverted (bad buttons named). The gaming build makes
  everyday use great too.
