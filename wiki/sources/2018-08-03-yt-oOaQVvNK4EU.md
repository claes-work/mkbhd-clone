# Prime Picks! - The #1 Headphones on Amazon! (@mkbhd, 2018-08-03)

## Summary
Prime Picks episode 2 (the series is now officially named): the #1 best-selling earbuds, over-ears, and wireless
headphones on Amazon — all cheap ($9–35). The takeaway isn't the products but his **framework for why cheap
headphones sell** and why **EarPods may be the most popular headphones in the world by volume.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-08-03] **The buyer-priority framework for cheap headphones.** "People buying $9–35 headphones care, in
  order: **(1) functional/convenient, (2) comfortable, (3) durable, and only then (4) how they sound.** Notice
  sound is **low on the list** — by the time you're replacing something that broke, you're mostly just happy it
  works." A crisp consumer-behavior model.
- [2018-08-03] **EarPods — "the most popular headphones in the world by volume."** "This priority list explains
  what may be the most popular headphones on earth — ones **you probably never consciously bought: EarPods.** They
  come in the box, are built for the iPhone, are light/comfortable — durability and sound are barely average, but
  they're **so convenient.**" A sharp market observation.
- [2018-08-03] **"Expectations, positioning, and a little luck" — how things reach #1.** How a product tops Amazon:
  "it's about as good as the competition, around the same (low) price, and then **rises to the top by whatever
  means necessary and stays there because it's good enough.**" (Notes his unease about **one company — mpow —
  dominating Amazon's accessory best-sellers**, teasing "a whole other video.")
- [2018-08-03] **The honest product read.** The $9 Panasonic ErgoFit (fine, thin fragile cable), the $35 mpow
  over-ears (a "listening-through-a-bed-sheet" hiss, but decent passive isolation, and "**not super bass-heavy,
  which I like — headphones that over-exaggerate bass are easy to rip on, they're clearly doing it wrong**"), and
  the $20 **mpow Flame** (his pick — sweatproof IPX7, best durability, "a package you could actually recommend").
- [2018-08-03] **APPEARANCE tell.** Picked the over-ears in **"red and black, naturally."** His signature color
  scheme surfaces again.
