# Cadillac Vistiq EV - Cadillac Can't Stop Cooking!

## Summary

Marques reviews the **Cadillac Vistiq**, a ~$80k full-size three-row electric SUV that slots
between the Lyriq and the Escalade. He opens with a genuine shout-out to Cadillac for **staying
committed to EVs while others pull back**, praises the materials/build/ride/space, but flags the
big drawback: **GM dropped CarPlay/Android Auto**, so the Vistiq has neither (its native software
is good, but that's a dealbreaker for CarPlay users). Fully Marques-narrated (a rainy-NJ-winter
review).

## Key claims

- **[2025-12-01]** **Shout-out to Cadillac's EV team for continuing to add to their lineup "when so
  many companies are pulling back" on EVs** — "the demand's not there, it's very scary. But not
  Cadillac." (quote)
- **[2025-12-01]** The Vistiq (~$80k, dual-motor AWD, ~100kWh, ~300mi) sits **between the Lyriq
  ("actually pretty underrated") and the Escalade** — most of the Lyriq's good software/layout with
  nearly the Escalade's space, in his red-and-black-with-black-roof spec. (paraphrase)
- **[2025-12-01]** Materials/build are great (heated/ventilated/massaging seats with headrest
  speakers; **"B+ speakers"**; leather everywhere; good isolation) — "at $80k vs a Model X, it's not
  even a question." But it moves closer to Escalade with **more piano-black/touchscreen HVAC** where
  he'd prefer the Lyriq's buttons. (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-12-01]** **The big drawback: GM is abandoning CarPlay/Android Auto — the Vistiq has
  neither** (the Lyriq had it). The native software is "the best software that isn't CarPlay/Android
  Auto I've seen in a while" (Play Store, Google Maps, smooth), "but no CarPlay is kind of a bummer —
  if you were hoping to use CarPlay, the Vistiq is immediately off your list." (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-12-01]** ⬥ BIOGRAPHY / habits: checks the second row **"behind a 6'3 driving position"**;
  the third row is tiny (like the EX90/Gravity); **"one of the best spots for a road trip for me is
  all the way in the back so I can extend my legs."** Rides like the Escalade; touring mode = "limo
  mode," sport firms up. Minor gripes: no hardware drive-mode switch, no hardware glovebox button.
  (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-12-01]** ⬥ BIOGRAPHY: **"my parents loved riding around in the back of this this weekend."**
  His final three-row-EV pick under $90k: **#1 the Volvo EX90; then a tie between the Vistiq, the
  Rivian R1S, and the Lucid Gravity** — "I'd probably want to chauffeur people around most in this."
  (quote)
- **[2025-12-01]** Aside: a recurring **NJ-winter-weather** motif — "shout-out to everyone else who
  does car videos on the East Coast." (paraphrase)
