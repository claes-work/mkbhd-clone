# The Tesla Charging Takeover! (NACS)

## Summary

A follow-up to his earlier "EVs are held back by charging" video: Marques explains **the NACS
takeover** — Ford, then GM, then Rivian agreeing to adopt Tesla's charging port and open the
Supercharger network to their cars. His thesis: **it's a win for everyone** — for EV drivers
(confident road-tripping), for the carmakers (their already-better vehicles get better), and for
Tesla ("the new gas station," plus government subsidy money) — with a **monopoly caveat.** Fully
Marques-narrated; includes a clip of his RJ Scaringe podcast.

## Key claims (biography marked ⬥)

- **[2023-06-20]** The setup: Tesla's Supercharger network is the huge advantage ("they spent billions;
  it's so much better than any public/Electrify America network"). After a few magic-dock CCS chargers
  ("made my Rivian 3% better"), the real dominoes fell: **Ford → GM → Rivian all adopting NACS in
  2024–25**, opening thousands of Superchargers. (paraphrase)
- **[2023-06-20]** ⭐ THE THESIS — **a win for everyone**: (1) **EV drivers** — "you can confidently road-
  trip a Rivian in North America now; the uptime/reliability is that much better — a no-brainer"; (2)
  **the carmakers** (Ford/GM/Rivian) — "a lot of people would agree these vehicles are *better* than
  what Tesla makes (a pickup, a 7-seat SUV), so now those better vehicles get better and sell more"; (3)
  **Tesla** — "they get paid ($10/mo + charging — **Tesla's the new gas station maybe**) and are
  eligible for government subsidy money for opening to other cars." (quote)
- **[2023-06-20]** ⚠️ The caveat: **"Might be a little too much of a win for Tesla when you look forward
  at potential monopoly status."** He still hopes public/Level-2 chargers keep improving, and expects
  **Tesla will keep *some* Superchargers Tesla-only as its remaining advantage.** (quote)
- **[2023-06-20]** ⬥ BIOGRAPHY: plays a clip from **his @Waveform podcast interview with RJ Scaringe**
  (Rivian CEO), who says Rivian will keep investing in its own **Adventure Network** (chargers at
  national parks/campsites where Superchargers aren't) even after adopting NACS. (paraphrase)
- **[2023-06-20]** "Who's the next domino? Could be Lucid, Hyundai, Mercedes, Polestar." (It was.) (quote)
