# Tesla Model Y Impressions! (@mkbhd, 2019-03-15)

## Summary
First look at the **Tesla Model Y** at its Hawthorne reveal — "one of the few tech products of the past year that
didn't leak." Completes the S3XY lineup. His headline call: **the Model Y will likely outsell the Model 3**, plus
a raft of design observations and a first look at the 2020 Roadster.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-03-15] **Completes the S3XY lineup — "an SUV version of the Model 3."** The 4th car (S/X = big premium
  car/SUV; 3 = smaller affordable car; **Y = the affordable slightly-larger crossover**). Specs: **230–300 mi
  range; $39k base (but higher-margin versions first — base by 2021); 3.5s 0–60 performance.** A dated reveal record.
- [2019-03-15] **THE CALL — "the Model Y could outsell the Model 3."** "The Model 3 has risen up the best-selling-
  car charts — but **best-selling SUVs constantly outsell best-selling cars, especially in the US. So the Model Y
  has the potential to be a much bigger seller than the Model 3.**" A sharp market prediction (grounded in the SUV-
  vs-car sales reality).
- [2019-03-15] **The design read — "a hybrid of the Model X and Model 3."** "It's mostly a Model 3 (shared parts),
  but bigger, with a **glass panoramic roof**, a higher ride height (on purpose), and — the one I love — an
  **automatic hatchback liftgate** ("I love the Model S hatchback; I had all four tires in the back the other
  day"). The **third-row option is extremely cramped — I don't recommend it; if you need three rows, get a Model X.**"
  Practical, honest observations.
- [2019-03-15] **The design detail he loved — factory chrome delete.** "All the pieces that are normally shiny
  chrome on the Model 3 (door handles, window trim, mirrors) are **satin matte black** on the Model Y — a **factory
  chrome delete, so you don't have to wrap the car to get rid of the chrome anymore. Pretty sweet.**" A characteristic
  matte-black appreciation (ties his wrap/matte-black identity).
- [2019-03-15] **Honest about the scope + the Roadster bonus.** "I got the same 30-second, pitch-black test ride as
  everyone (useless footage) — hopefully an **Auto Focus episode in daylight** soon." And: "I got a hot minute with
  the **2020 Roadster** — first time seeing it in person. **Sweet baby Jesus, that car looks incredible — I want it
  to exist so bad. I really hope it's on time.**" (His deposit is in.) Genuine enthusiasm.
