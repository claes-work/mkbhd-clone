# 2023 Nissan Ariya First Impressions: Priced to Compete!

## Summary

A first look at the **Nissan Ariya** (one of the first US pre-production units), which Marques
cross-shops against the **VW ID.4 and Hyundai Ioniq 5** — and **would pick either of those over it.**
The Ariya is well-built with cool ideas (a copper theme, cherry-blossom Easter eggs, a clever center
console), but the **capacitive climate buttons** and an **"awful"-looking UI** hold it back, and its
range/charging lose to the Ioniq 5. Fully Marques-narrated; notable for an **early statement of his
channel-collaboration vision.**

## Key claims (biography marked ⬥)

- **[2022-09-12]** Context: "The first year I noticed EV commercials in the Super Bowl — a big awareness
  moment." Cross-shops the Ariya vs. the **VW ID.4 and Ioniq 5** (long-wheelbase crossovers, first EVs
  from these makers). **"Spoiler: I'd personally pick either of those two over this one."** ~$50k, no
  frunk (electronics moved forward → a "glowing lantern" open cubby). (quote)
- **[2022-09-12]** The good: well-built; a consistent **copper** theme inside and out; a motorized
  center console + a genuinely clever **cable-wrap phone-charging spot**; three hidden **cherry-blossom
  Easter eggs**; dual-pane glass. (paraphrase)
- **[2022-09-12]** ⚠️ The bad (his consistent critiques): **capacitive haptic climate buttons** ("I want
  buttons I can just reach over and hit — same level as the Ioniq 5's mistake"); **"this UI looks
  terrible — I'm not even going to sugarcoat it"** (so he's glad it has wireless CarPlay, but you must
  use the ugly built-in maps to route to chargers); a middling ~7s 0–60 drive "like the ID.4." (quote)
- **[2022-09-12]** ⭐ THE VERDICT: **"Why I'd pick the Ioniq 5 over this: it has the edge in range, looks,
  storage/organization, and most importantly charging speed — the Ariya maxes at 130 kW and <300 mi, not
  a car I'd road-trip. And this isn't Nissan's first rodeo (the Leaf) — I expect more from them."**
  A middling score. (quote)
- **[2022-09-12]** ⬥ CREATOR-BUSINESS (early channel vision): **"I've seen comments about who I should
  collaborate with — obviously the Doug DeMuros and Sandy Munros of the world, we should make that
  happen; the Kilowatts have reached out. When I said I have a lot of thoughts on cars, I meant it."**
  (Documents his early @AutoFocus collaboration plans.) (quote)
