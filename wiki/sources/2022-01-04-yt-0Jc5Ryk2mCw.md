# Galaxy S21 FE Impressions: Great Phone, Odd Price! (@mkbhd, 2022-01-04)

## Summary
Impressions of the $699 Samsung Galaxy S21 Fan Edition (a lighter ingest). His read: **a great, super-safe, maybe
slightly boring phone — undone by price and timing.** The FE formula (one-year-old flagship parts repackaged
cheaper, like Apple's SE) worked in 2020 (the S20 FE won his Phone of the Year at that price) — but **this one is
a "swing and a miss"**: it launches at $699 right when the S22 is a month away and the regular S21 is already $599
(refurbished on Amazon), sandwiched by the $599 Pixel 6. Invokes the **Note 20 "Bust of the Year" parallel** (a
fine phone that priced itself out of its slot). The lesson: **buy it with blinders on and you'll love it; take the
blinders off and the landscape is more interesting.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-01-04] **The FE formula — safe, even a little boring.** "'A super-safe bet — very tame, doesn't make
  mistakes, maybe even a little boring, but a lot of people will like it. **The Fan Edition formula is one-year-old
  flagship parts put back together in a cheaper phone (like Apple's SE), and the biggest repeated part of the
  formula is the price.** Flagship-level inside (888, 120 Hz AMOLED — "a win for Samsung the display company"),
  then the corners get cut (plastic back, an optical instead of ultrasonic fingerprint reader, a step-down on
  haptics).'"
- [2022-01-04] **A B+ camera.** "'Solid triple cameras with OIS — sharp, shallow depth of field, dependable in
  good light, sometimes noisy in low light. **An easy B+ camera — hanging with a lot of flagships but not beating
  any.**'"
- [2022-01-04] **The swing and a miss — price + timing.** "'The real question is whether those little cuts take
  away from the flagship experience — and mostly they don't. **But this would all be fine if the S21 weren't
  already sitting at $699 (refurbished $599 on Amazon), and if the S22 weren't right around the corner.** The
  S20 FE lit the market on fire with flagship stuff for ~$300 less than expected — **this one, launching so close
  to the S22 at this price, just blends into the market. A swing and a miss.**'"
- [2022-01-04] **The Note 20 "Bust" parallel + "take your blinders off."** "'This has happened to Samsung before —
  **the Note 20 got my Bust of the Year trophy** not because it was a bad phone (it wasn't) but because it priced
  itself out of its slot. **Buy the S21 FE with blinders on and you'll really like it; take the blinders off and
  there's a lot more around this price** (Pixel 6 at $599, a OnePlus 9, even an iPhone SE) — a more interesting
  decision.'"
- [2022-01-04] **No charger in the box.** "'On a sustainability note, **no charger in the box** — that small box
  again. More "flagship experience" stuff, if you want to look at it that way.'"

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "This phone would have obviously killed [at] $549 or even $599... but it didn't. This launch price and timing is
> what sort of just blends it in with the rest of the market."
> "Buy it with the blinders on, they're gonna really like this phone. But if you take your blinders off, there's a
> lot more around in this landscape around this price."
