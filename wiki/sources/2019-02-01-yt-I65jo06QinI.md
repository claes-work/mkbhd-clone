# Sony WH1000XM3 Review: A New ANC King! (@mkbhd, 2019-02-01)

## Summary
The Sony WH-1000XM3 becomes his new all-time favorite wireless ANC headphones — beating both the Bose QC35 II and
the Microsoft Surface Headphones on sound AND noise cancellation. A clean "what this category needs to nail"
review with honest flaws (touch controls, the cold-weather bug, single-device pairing).

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-02-01] **"A new ANC king" — the switch.** "**Better sound and better noise cancellation than both the Bose
  and the Surface Headphones**, plus a great design (lightweight, well-padded, USB-C, 20+ hr battery, comfortable
  for 5.5 hours straight on a flight). **My new all-time favorite wireless noise-cancelling cans.**" A dated
  preference change (he'd switched Bose → Surface, now → Sony).
- [2019-02-01] **"What this category has to nail."** "There's not much to a great over-ear ANC pair: **sound,
  noise cancellation, comfort, and a decent feature set.** These hit all of them." A crisp evaluation framework.
- [2019-02-01] **The honest flaws.** **Touch controls** (swipe/tap on the right cup — "fidgety, doesn't work every
  time; I prefer the Surface's physical dials"); **no auto-pause on removal** (the Surface did); the **cold-weather
  phantom-touch bug** ("in sub-zero weather the touchpad registers ghost touches — I have to flag it; may not be
  software-fixable"); wind on the top mics; and **only pairs one device at a time** — "**I really miss seamless
  multi-device switching** (music from the phone, hit play on the iPad and it takes over)." Balanced, thorough.
- [2019-02-01] **The aesthetic + the credit.** "**Matte black** mostly all around (which I love) with a copper
  accent — permanent backpack material." Credits the comments/viewers (and a livestreamer) who pushed him to buy
  them. (Aside: he's in Seattle for **a top-secret project.**)

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "These sound better than both, are better at noise cancellation than both, and have a great design. It's already
> my new all-time favorite pair of wireless noise-cancelling cans."
> "When you get used to headphones with multiple-device connectivity and seamless switching, that's a big
> convenience — I really miss it with these."
> "There's a cold-weather issue — in sub-zero weather the touchpads register phantom touches. I have to let you
> guys know about it."
