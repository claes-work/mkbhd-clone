# The MKBHD Desk Setup Tour 2021! (@TheStudio, 2021-08-06)

> ⚠️ ATTRIBUTION: **Pure Marques** — a first-person tour of his own desk. Subject data throughout
> (Andrew's one "can confirm" aside is context). ★★ gear / workflow / biography goldmine.

## Summary
Marques walks through everything on and under his desk. Rich, specific gear + workflow +
biography material, plus two durable philosophy nuggets (his footage-backup rule and his chair-
recommendation heuristic) and his stated authenticity ethos for the tour itself.

## Key claims (Marques-attributed)
- [2021-08-06] **Authenticity ethos (meta):** "this isn't going to be one of those super-highly-
  produced desk tours with glorious B-roll of everything — I'm going to actually get into
  everything that's actually on my desk. I didn't clean it. Everything I actually use on the
  regular to get stuff done." (creator-business, production-filmmaking)
- [2021-08-06] **The desk:** a custom-sized **Xdesk Air Pro** standing desk (motorized telescoping
  legs, presets for standing/sitting). Accepts that a height-changing desk can't be perfectly
  cable-managed for both heights. (production-filmmaking)
- [2021-08-06] **Audio:** **Yamaha HS8** studio monitors ("might be the longest-running thing in
  the setup") on Auralex foam isolation pads, driven by a **Universal Audio Apollo Twin MkII**
  ("a glorified volume knob" + preamp — he doesn't use the plugins). (production-filmmaking)
- [2021-08-06] **He edits on wireless earbuds:** matte-black **ColorWare AirPods Pro** that "never
  leave the studio" — "yes, I do edit with these wireless headphones. The sound quality and the
  lack of lag is just good enough for me," and transparency mode lets teammates talk to him.
  (production-filmmaking — a notable workflow admission)
- [2021-08-06] **BACKUP PHILOSOPHY (durable ★):** a ~96 TB **Promise Pegasus** array, but "I only
  save typically the final exported video file, the thumbnail, and some key clips... other than
  that, get rid of it all." Interviews are worth keeping; most raw footage isn't. Local backup +
  Google Drive / Dropbox / "youtube.com." (creator-business, production-filmmaking)
- [2021-08-06] **Displays:** dual **Pro Display XDR** with the **nano-texture coating** — "a huge
  win" over his old glossy monitors, because the studio lights/windows would otherwise reflect
  (consistent anti-reflection obsession). A **side-dock** person (switched from bottom dock).
  (production-filmmaking — the reflection theme recurs in his camera choices)
- [2021-08-06] **Peripherals:** **Logitech MX Master 3** (laser-etched "MKBHD"); pro tip — use the
  dongle and keep it close (the Mac Pro's metal sheet caused lag). **Keychron K2** wired mechanical
  keyboard + Blackmagic/Magic trackpad — "a three-input type of person"; only got into mechanical
  keyboards "the last year or two" and "won't go back." (tech-reviews)
- [2021-08-06] **CHAIR HEURISTIC (durable ★):** **Herman Miller Embody** (~a decade). "Anytime
  anyone asks for a chair recommendation, I go: what's your budget, and how long do you spend in a
  chair every day? For the ~$1,000–1,200 for the decade I've had it, it's been worth it. Easily my
  favorite chair ever." (creator-business, biography)
- [2021-08-06] **Compute:** a **Mac Pro** powers everything (mounted facing backward for airflow so
  it doesn't blow hot air on the wall) + a 16" MacBook Pro "workhorse" that roams room-to-room.
  **Final Cut Pro**, latest macOS. A **Tesla key card** sits on the desk (corroborates Model S
  ownership). Shout-out to **Peter McKinnon** for the ProTech unboxing knife. (biography)
