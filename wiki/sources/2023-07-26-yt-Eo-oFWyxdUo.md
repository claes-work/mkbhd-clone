# Samsung Z Fold 5 and Z Flip 5 Impressions: They Settled! (@mkbhd, 2023-07-26)

## Summary
A first look at Samsung's Z Fold 5 / Z Flip 5 — **"this is what it looks like when Samsung settles."** They're so
similar to last year that "it'd be faster to tell you what's different": **a new chip (Snapdragon 8 Gen 2), Gorilla
Glass Victus 2, and a new hinge** (fewer moving parts, folds completely flat) on both; the **Flip's larger 3.4"
Flex Window** cover screen (up from 1.9") is the headline; the **Fold's brighter 1,750-nit inner display** and a 40%
thinner S Pen. **"Everything else is the same as last year,"** at the same prices ($1,000 / $1,799). His framing:
**"this is just the Samsung way"** — not laziness, but a company **so settled into its two form factors that any
drastic change would just be a whole new phone.** He backs it with history: Samsung **threw stuff at the wall** for
years (the Galaxy Mega's "gigantic" 6.3" screen is a normal flagship size today; the Note Edge's curved display),
then **settles in and iterates.** Having now used the Oppo and especially the **Pixel Fold**, he's **"struck by how
tall and narrow Samsung's outside cover screen is"** — it doesn't feel as natural for typing or content. He'd have
loved improvements to the under-display selfie camera or a bigger battery, but "it's still going to be a pretty safe
phone." (Aside: he loves the S9 Ultra tablet.)

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2023-07-26] **This is what it looks like when Samsung settles.** "'You probably didn't even realize this is last
  year's phone. Same shape, dimensions, aspect ratios, cameras, batteries. It'd be faster to just tell you what's
  different: a new chip, a new hinge that folds flat, and — on the Flip — the larger Flex Window. Everything else is
  the same, at the same price.'"
- [2023-07-26] **It's the Samsung way — a drastic change would just be a new phone.** "'This isn't laziness. Samsung
  is so settled into these two form factors, being safe and churning along, that any major change I'd want would
  probably just be a whole new phone. In the past they threw stuff at the wall to see what sticks — they're not
  afraid to have 1,000 phones in the lineup.'"
- [2023-07-26] **The Galaxy Mega / Note Edge history.** "'Remember the Galaxy Mega? One day Samsung decided to make a
  gigantic phone — it looked absurd next to normal phones then, but that 6.3" is a normal-sized flagship today. The
  Note Edge curved the display over the side before anyone else, and they've settled into that ever since. Same with
  the Note and styluses. They invent a form factor, then settle in and iterate.'"
- [2023-07-26] **Samsung's cover screen is too tall and narrow.** "'Now that I've held the Oppo and especially the
  Pixel Fold, I'm struck by how tall and narrow Samsung's outside cover screen is — it doesn't feel nearly as natural
  as the Pixel Fold; I don't enjoy typing or watching content on something that awkwardly shaped. If Samsung wanted a
  more passport-shaped foldable, I think they'd just make a whole other phone.'"
