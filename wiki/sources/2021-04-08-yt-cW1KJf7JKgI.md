# The Ultimate Gaming Phone! (@mkbhd, 2021-04-08)

## Summary
A look at the Lenovo Legion Duel 2 — "one of the weirdest phones I've ever seen." The durable framing: **this one's
for the people who say phones are boring** — "sometimes you have to try a bunch of weird stuff to land on a great
idea." Notable ideas: a **split left/right battery** (real thermal + weight-distribution benefits), **dual-USB-C
90 W charging** (plug it in twice), **dual haptic motors** (the strongest vibration he's felt in any phone), and a
**selfie camera inside the power button** (a horizontal streaming webcam) — a phone where the phone functionality
is "an afterthought." "Not for me, but I'm glad they made it."

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-04-08] **"This one's for the phones-are-boring people."** "'To those who say every couple of weeks that
  **phones are getting boring and there's no innovation — this one's for you.** One of the weirdest phones I've
  seen; not every idea is fantastic, but **sometimes you have to try a bunch of weird stuff to land on a great
  idea. I'm here to point the camera at the thing and show you what they've tried.**'" A recurring pro-experiment
  value.
- [2021-04-08] **The split battery — a genuinely good idea (thermal + weight).** "'The 5,500 mAh battery is
  **split left/right (2,750 each), laid out horizontally** — real benefits: **the sides where your fingers are
  stay cooler** (hot components centered), **perfect weight distribution**, and cooling focused on the middle
  (a turbine fan + side vents). No separate camera bump — the cameras are centered too.'"
- [2021-04-08] **Dual-USB-C 90 W charging — "a hilarious extreme."** "'Two USB-C ports on the phone + a dual-port
  90 W brick: **plug it in twice and charge at 90 W (0–100 in ~30 min).** Kind of hilarious seeing a phone plugged
  in twice, but satisfying. Plus **bypass charging** (power from the cable to avoid heat, like the ROG 5) and
  **charging limiters for battery health — a nice trend to see baked in.**'"
- [2021-04-08] **Dual haptic motors — the strongest vibration in any phone.** "'It has **one linear haptic motor
  on each side; together they make a really strong, thumpy, punchy vibration — the most impressive I've felt in
  any phone** (I don't think any other has two). Gaming feels strong, and even everyday typing/alarms.'" (Notes
  his haptic-motor-quality obsession again.)
- [2021-04-08] **The selfie cam in the power button + "the phone is an afterthought."** "'The **44 MP motorized
  selfie camera lives in the power button — oriented horizontally because its primary purpose is to be your webcam
  for streaming games to Twitch/YouTube** (extremely average quality — "I'd expect nothing less"). Everything
  else — a 64 MP main camera your fingers block, texts, calls — **feels like it just happens to be true. It's the
  ultimate all-in gaming phone: a mobile gaming handheld with cellular.**'"
- [2021-04-08] **The verdict — "not for me, but I'm glad they made it."** "'It doesn't bother with wireless
  charging or water resistance — doesn't matter at this level. **Even if it's not for me, I'm glad they made it,
  and I'd happily recommend it to people I know who are much more into mobile gaming.**' (He handed it to Andrew —
  a PUBG player — who won his first game, chicken dinner.)'"
