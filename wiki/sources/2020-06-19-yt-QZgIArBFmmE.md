# Samsung Galaxy Buds Plus: The Go-To! (@mkbhd, 2020-06-19)

## Summary
A review of the $150 Galaxy Buds+ built around his **"the go-to" theory**: every mature product category has a
checks-all-the-boxes safe-bet starting point. For non-AirPods wireless earbuds, the Buds+ are it.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-06-19] **THE "GO-TO" THEORY.** "'Every well-established category has **a go-to — an easy-to-recommend, safe-
  bet, straight-down-the-middle option** (the Logitech MX mouse, the Shure SM7B mic, the iPhone for phones). **It's
  not the one for everyone — it's a starting point;** then you learn your values and veer off to alternatives you'd
  pay more for. **For non-AirPods wireless earbuds, the go-to is the Galaxy Buds+, 100%.**'" A promotable,
  reusable recommendation framework.
- [2020-06-19] **Why — it nails the fundamentals.** "'Small, lightweight, **fits well (rubber tips, a passive seal
  I don't miss ANC that much with)**, a **best-in-class 11-hour** battery (a full week of casual listening, no
  battery anxiety), a **pocketable USB-C + wireless-charging case** with a satisfying click, and it stays put
  enough to work out in (IPX2).'" The fundamentals thread again (cf. [[2020-05-05-yt-48bK3mmjgRE]]).
- [2020-06-19] **The sound — best non-ANC he's tried.** "'Tuned by **AKG** — a full, rich bass, crisp mids/highs,
  no hiss. **I'd go so far as to say the best-sounding non-noise-canceling wireless earbuds I've tried** (a touch
  bass-heavy, but easily EQ'd — though I won't; it's already great for music).'"
- [2020-06-19] **The go-to as a launch pad for your real choice.** "'Now that you know these, you might decide "I
  want ANC" → go Sony/AirPods Pro; or "I want workout-focused" → go Jaybird/PowerBeats. **Other headphones do
  plenty better — but if you're wondering what the de-facto non-AirPods set is, it's the Galaxy Buds+.**'" The
  starting-point-then-branch model in action.

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "Every product category that's well established enough has a go-to — an easy-to-recommend, checks-all-the-boxes,
> safe-bet, straight shot right down the middle."
> "You use that as a sort of a starting point, and then when you find the values you have and the things you like,
> you veer off into the alternatives you might pay more for."
