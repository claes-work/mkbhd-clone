# iPad Mini 2021 Review: Pocketable Power! (@mkbhd, 2021-09-22)

## Summary
Review of the $499 2021 iPad mini (a lighter ingest) — essentially **a tiny iPad Air** (USB-C, a Touch ID power
button, Apple Pencil 2, the latest A15). The durable idea: the iPad mini is a **"know your use case" device** — the
people who get it already have a specific job in mind (hobbyist pilots, note-takers, a HomeKit wall hub). "Not for
me," but a real option again. The weak points: **a "fine" 60 Hz LCD** and **a mini battery.** He suggests many
should consider the cheaper base iPad, and wishes for an **"iPad mini Pro"** (120 Hz + Face ID + Pro cameras).

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-09-22] **"Know your use case" — who buys a mini.** "'The iPad mini fascinates me — I always wonder who buys
  them and what for. **The people getting one already have a specific thing in mind: hobbyist pilots (plotting a
  flight map in a small cockpit), students note-taking with the Pencil, someone making it a HomeKit wall hub.**
  Not for me (I'm an iPad Pro fan), but this refresh makes it a real option again. **Know your use case.**'"
- [2021-09-22] **A tiny iPad Air.** "'The refresh is mostly design: **unibody aluminum, squared sides, drop the
  home button (Touch ID in the power button), USB-C (thank you), a first-ever camera bump, and Apple Pencil 2
  support** (the pencil is the size of the whole side, so the volume buttons moved to the top). The **A15 (5-core
  GPU) — better than the iPad Air's — so it's smooth and future-proofed for years of updates.**'"
- [2021-09-22] **The screen is "fine"; the mini battery is the weak point.** "'The 8.3" screen is **fine — a 60 Hz
  LCD, same not-great 500 nits, not for outdoor sun.** The worst thing is **the mini battery — I needed to charge
  it at the end of every full day (~7–8 hrs screen-on).** Good stereo speakers, a decent 12 MP camera + Center
  Stage, the same full iPad software as the $800 Pros. (The UI padding makes it feel really mini — the weather
  widget is bigger on the iPhone.)'"
- [2021-09-22] **The advice — consider the base iPad; and the "iPad mini Pro" wish.** "'For your use case, **consider
  the 9th-gen base iPad — $170 cheaper, bigger screen** (a different type of tablet). The mini starts at 64 GB
  ($499). And selfishly I was imagining an **"iPad mini Pro" — this size with 120 Hz + Face ID + the Pro cameras +
  a bigger battery — but that's ~$200–300 extra, which is probably why it doesn't exist.** Cross-shop a Galaxy Tab,
  a Surface Go, or the new Kindle. **Know your use case — that's the advice.**'"
