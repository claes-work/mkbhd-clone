# Why I Ordered a Tesla CyberTruck! (@mkbhd, 2019-11-24)

## Summary
Marques explains why — after initially saying he didn't need a pickup — he **ordered a (triple-motor) CyberTruck.**
The reason is the design thesis: to break into a locked-in, mature market you **have to be radically different**,
and the CyberTruck's polarizing look is that on purpose.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-11-24] **THE DESIGN-DISRUPTION THESIS — you have to be different to break in.** "'Pickup trucks have all
  looked the same forever (the top-5 US best-sellers for ~30 years are the F-150 and trucks that look like it),
  and buyers are **fiercely loyal** — an F-150 owner just buys another F-150. So a Tesla truck that **looks like an
  F-150 with a battery wouldn't sell** — it **had to be different, striking, and stand out,** and they leaned into
  it **unashamedly and never looked back.**'" A durable market-disruption framework.
- [2019-11-24] **THE APPLE PARALLEL — intentionally jarring design, knowing the memes will come.** "'It's the same
  way **I'm convinced Apple lately makes design choices that are intentionally a little jarring — knowing the memes
  will come — because soon enough, when people get used to it, that's the reason it's recognizable and even
  iconic.**'" A sharp, promotable cross-brand insight.
- [2019-11-24] **The verdict — possibly Tesla's most important/disruptive vehicle.** "'Next to the Model Y, the
  **CyberTruck could be the most important vehicle Tesla makes — and possibly the most disruptive** in the tech
  world. I still don't think it's pretty — it's more **brutalist** — but other pickups aren't pretty either; it
  just **had to be different.**'"
- [2019-11-24] **BIOGRAPHY — the order + embracing the tech-reviewer role.** "'I put down my **$100 refundable
  deposit** — I'm in line for a **triple-motor CyberTruck, all in.** I'm finally **embracing my position as a tech
  reviewer on the bleeding edge — investigating it to figure out if it's good.**'" A datable personal purchase +
  self-positioning statement (cf. his Tesla Model S ownership, [[2019-09-05-yt-gNK_C78sBWY]]).
- [2019-11-24] **The community-polarization read.** "'The 30,000+ comments on my first-impressions video
  **alternate hilariously** between "this is dope, think outside the box" and "this is the dumbest truck ever."
  When you look at it, **there isn't a single curve — all angles and lines, "like how I drew a truck at five" —**
  and that simplicity makes it striking.' (146k orders as he recorded — 'people who didn't even wait for it to
  grow on them.')"
