# Huawei Mate 20 Pro Review: The People's Choice! (@mkbhd, 2018-12-15)

## Summary
The full Mate 20 Pro review (his last before the 2018 Smartphone Awards) — he daily-drove the blind-test winner.
Verdict: "**I like it a lot, don't love it — and that's really only because of one thing: the software (EMUI).**"
Everything else, especially the battery, is excellent.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-12-15] **"I like it a lot, don't love it — because of one thing: the software."** After it won his blind
  test and racked up phone-of-the-year awards, he daily-drove it. Great hardware, but **EMUI is the dealbreaker** —
  "I had to put in extra work (Nova Launcher, uninstalling/disabling duplicate apps, changing defaults). Bugs:
  **opening the second-most-recent app closes multitasking; the notification tray's always full**; plus the
  **blatant iOS copying** (Huawei Share vs AirDrop) and bloatware." His software-is-the-flaw thesis.
- [2018-12-15] **A++ battery — "the best I've used this year."** "The best part is **not the camera — it's the
  battery.** A 4,200 mAh cell + aggressive power management = **a two-day phone a couple times — I never think
  about charging it.** Other 2018 flagships got 5–6 hrs screen-on; this ended a day at 1 a.m. with 4.5 hrs
  screen-on and **58% left.**" A standout endorsement + the fun **reverse wireless charging** gimmick.
- [2018-12-15] **Camera — A-minus, "sticks to what won the blind test."** "Bright (sometimes overexposed) photos
  with **basically no shadows/blacks in lots of light**, A+ detail/sharpness, slightly muted colors (different
  Huawei color science). The best part is the **extra focal lengths** (super-wide especially). Probably the highest
  DxO score." Ties directly to his Blind-Test "brighter wins" finding.
- [2018-12-15] **The hardware nitpicks.** The **speaker behind the USB-C port** ("a cool idea for 5 seconds, then a
  horrible one — muffled while charging"); the first-gen **in-glass fingerprint is a little slow**; a bit of
  edge light-fall-off / off-axis color shift on the OLED. Otherwise "all-around great hardware." The **red back
  matches the red power button** ("a strong move" — appearance tell).
- [2018-12-15] **The near-stock wish + verdict.** "**I'd love this phone with OxygenOS or stock Android** with a
  few of these features. Either way it's **the best phone Huawei's ever made, hands down — better than the P20
  Pro**; if you don't mind the premium and importing it, worth a look." Restates his near-stock preference; sets up
  the Smartphone Awards.
