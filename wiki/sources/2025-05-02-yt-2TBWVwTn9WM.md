# You could wait for the Rivian R3X or... (Volvo EX30)

## Summary

Framed as a comparison to a car that doesn't exist yet: everyone's been lusting after the
**Rivian R3X** (compact, sporty, ~$47k, decent range), and Marques' point is that **the Volvo EX30
does all of that TODAY**, with 2025 tech. Short wheelbase like the R3X, ~$47k like the R3X, but
real. 422hp that "feels like 600," a 3.4s 0–60, a clean Scandinavian minimal interior with clever
center-console storage — and its one real weakness is range (250mi). His verdict lands it firmly on
his personal-favorites list: **"Volvo seems to be on my nice list this year."** Fully Marques-narrated.

## Key claims

- **[2025-05-02]** Thesis: people have lusted over the unreleased **Rivian R3/R3X** (compact sporty
  crossover, sub-$50k). **"There is something that actually accomplishes all of that exact same stuff
  today that people don't talk about much… this is the Volvo EX30"** — short wheelbase "nearly the
  same as the R3X," ~$47k start "like the R3X," "but with today's 2025 tech." (quote)
- **[2025-05-02]** ⬥ BIOGRAPHY: **"I'd come from testing Volvo's EX90"** (his Rolls-Royce-luxury pick)
  — establishes the back-to-back Volvo run. (quote)
- **[2025-05-02]** Specs: dual-motor, **422hp, 0–60 in 3.4s**, 69kWh battery, **250mi range (its
  biggest weakness)**, 153kW charging. "To hit this price with a compact crossover in 2025, you have to
  have a smaller battery." (paraphrase)
- **[2025-05-02]** Design love: **"I don't think there's anything not to like about the outside of the
  EX30… it just doesn't have any bad angles."** Clean Scandinavian, no fake grille, real air vents.
  (quote)
- **[2025-05-02]** Interior: minimal (single central 12.5" screen, no gauge cluster — "some people
  might be turned off like in Teslas by only having speed on that screen"); clever center console with
  a magnetic sub-tray + reconfigurable cup holders; **Harman Kardon sound "somehow still pretty good…
  Volvo knows how to do a good sound system in all their cars, no matter what."** Google built-in +
  optional CarPlay. (quote)
- **[2025-05-02]** Drive surprise: **"It's got some zip — I didn't expect it to be this fun. If you
  made me guess the horsepower I'd have said 600, not 400."** Feels nimble, 4,100lb ("~1,000lb lighter
  than some EVs"); communicative, forgiving, non-firm suspension. (quote)
- **[2025-05-02]** ⚠️ Nitpicks (software-fixable): **one-pedal calibration "a lot worse than the
  EX90"** (coasts too far, herky-jerky stop); the **driver-attention monitor is over-sensitive** ("I'd
  check my rearview mirror and get a ding saying pay attention to the road"), and lane-keep + driver
  alert re-enable every drive. Same **single-set-of-window-switches** gripe as the EX90. (paraphrase)
- **[2025-05-02]** ⬥ Verdict: "Would never road-trip it (not enough range), but it's plenty fun as a
  commuter and has plenty of range as a daily driver… **Volvo seems to be on my nice list this year.**"
  (quote)
