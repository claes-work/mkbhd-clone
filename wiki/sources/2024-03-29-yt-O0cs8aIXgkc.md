# Tesla Cybertruck Review: Already Iconic? (@mkbhd, 2024-03-29)

## Summary
A month / 1,500-mile review of the **Tesla Cybertruck** structured as an **"overrated or underrated" game** per feature.
The hook: **"the most dangerous thing about the Cybertruck is the way it makes other people around you behave"** (not
the sharp edges or the glare). He genuinely **can't tell if it's good-looking** — online comments hate it
("refrigerator on wheels"), but strangers who've never heard of it call it a movie prop; the **looks distract from
everything else** (100% of people who guessed the price guessed **too high**). The verdicts: **steer-by-wire →
underrated** (variable ratio + four-wheel steer make it "the most nimble, agile truck I've ever driven"; Tesla didn't
have to do it); **bulletproof stainless steel → overrated** (durable, yes, but a fingerprint magnet, no normal door
handles, hard to stamp — and the windows/tires aren't bulletproof); **truck stuff (tow/haul/off-road) → overrated**
(**"63% of truck drivers admit to rarely or never towing"**; most will drive it like a **glorified SUV**);
**performance → overrated** (a one-trick straight-line pony — the drag-strip time slip shows **"quick vs fast"**: it
out-launches a 911 Turbo S but the geared car passes it by half-track; Tesla's tow-a-911 ad quietly ran the *cheapest*
Carrera T over an **eighth**-mile then quoted the quarter-mile time). His **panel-gap door latch actually fell off**
(under-torqued bolts, gaffe-taped back by Miles; Musk said only 15 trucks affected) — **"the Foundation Series badge
kind of means willing beta tester."** Verdict: as a product person, **it's already an iconic design** — but iconic
cars are almost always *rare*, and this is slotted for mass production, so the awe **has to wear off** (the **PT
Cruiser arc**: crazy at launch, humanized once a million are on the road). "Only time will tell." And under all the
new tech, **"it's still a Tesla under the hood."**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2024-03-29] **The most dangerous thing is how it makes others behave.** "'The most dangerous thing about the
  Cybertruck isn't the pointy corners, the three-ton weight, or the glare off the steel — it's the way it makes other
  people around you on the road behave.'"
- [2024-03-29] **The looks distract from everything else.** "'I genuinely can't tell if it's good-looking, and I'm
  serious. Online it's mostly negative; in person strangers call it a movie prop. 100% of people who guessed the price
  guessed too high — the looks are so distracting they hide the rest of what makes it what it is.'"
- [2024-03-29] **Steer-by-wire — underrated.** "'Variable steering ratio plus four-wheel steer make this the most
  nimble, agile-feeling truck I've ever driven — you point the nose and pivot around a small axis. Tesla didn't have
  to ship a full steer-by-wire system with all its redundancies, but they did. Underrated.'"
- [2024-03-29] **Bulletproof steel — overrated; early trucks are beta.** "'Durable, sure — shopping carts and fender
  benders are fine. But the windows aren't bulletproof, the tires can still be slashed, it's a fingerprint magnet with
  no normal door handles, and steel is hard to stamp. My door latch literally fell out — under-torqued bolts. The
  Foundation Series badge is clout, but it also kind of means willing beta tester.'"
- [2024-03-29] **Truck stuff — overrated; most will drive it like an SUV.** "'63% of truck drivers admit to rarely or
  never towing. If you're buying a truck for towing, your short list has no EVs on it. Most of the millions of
  pre-orders will use this like a glorified SUV because it looks cool — and that's fine.'"
- [2024-03-29] **Performance — overrated; quick vs fast.** "'EVs commoditized straight-line acceleration. The time
  slip shows quick isn't fast: the Cyberbeast out-launches a 911 Turbo S off the line, but the lighter geared car
  shifts and passes it by half-track and pulls into the 140s while the truck hits its 114-mph limit. Tesla's tow-a-911
  ad ran the cheapest, slowest Carrera T over an eighth-mile and quoted the quarter-mile time. It's a one-trick pony —
  a very fun one — but then you get to a turn and it's a truck again.'"
- [2024-03-29] **Iconic — but iconic cars are rare, and this is mass-produced.** "'As a product person, I think this is
  already an iconic design — Countach, Veyron air. But those are almost always rare; there were ~2,000 Countaches, <500
  Veyrons, and there are 2M+ Cybertruck pre-orders. Seeing your 50th of the day hits different. It's like the PT
  Cruiser — a crazy design that got attention at launch, then Chrysler made a million and it got humanized. Is that
  the arc for the Cybertruck? Only time will tell.'"
