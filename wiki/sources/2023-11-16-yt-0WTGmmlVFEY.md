# Lucid Gravity Has 440 miles and 3 Rows!

## Summary

An early **first look at the Lucid Gravity prototype** — Lucid's first SUV, a three-row that Marques
sees as a bit sporty, a bit luxury, and a lot of high-tech. The headline: **440+ miles of range**
(sub-0.24 drag) at ~$85k+ base. He walks it front-to-back — a **front-trunk that turns into a
seat**, a repositioned higher driver display, a genuinely usable third row (better than the R1S),
and cavernous fold-flat storage. Fully Marques-narrated (opens "MKBHD here, welcome to the Batcave").

## Key claims

- **[2023-11-16]** The basics: Lucid's first SUV, three-row, shipping before end of 2024; ~same battery
  as the Air but very efficient (**sub-0.24 Cd → 440+ mi range**); ~$85k base ("well into six figures
  as you spec"); likely a 3-second 0–60. "A bit sporty, a bit luxury, a lot of high-tech." (paraphrase)
- **[2023-11-16]** Front: a Lucid-Air-but-taller face, a glowing logo light strip, huge staggered 21/22"
  front + 23" rear wheels; a **frunk that fits a folding seat accessory** — "you could hang out in the
  front trunk of your car, shielded from the rain. I never thought I'd say that." (quote)
- **[2023-11-16]** Interior: a **higher-mounted driver display + flatter wheel** for full visibility;
  soft materials, a sliding tempered-glass center console; **no CarPlay/Android Auto** ("don't expect
  it in a Lucid anytime soon"); customizable buttons under the screen; a Model-X-like uninterrupted
  windshield; color-changing ambient LEDs. ⚠️ He's unsure about the **new flatter steering wheel** losing
  the Air's clever wheels/knobs. (paraphrase)
- **[2023-11-16]** Space: **a better third row than the Rivian R1S** ("a little more room; adults fit
  reasonably for ~an hour"); a **second-row airplane-style laptop/tablet tray** he prefers to a built-in
  screen ("a screen would age quickly and look tacky — my own laptop always works"); huge fold-flat
  storage ("bicycle, surfboard"). (quote)
- **[2023-11-16]** Framing: competition right now is just the Model X and R1S; "we'll get more soon (a
  Kia, the Escalade)." "Is this enough to save the company / get people who'd never buy a Lucid?" (quote)

## Notable verbatim quotes

- "You could hang out in the front trunk of your car and be shielded from the rain — I never thought I'd say that."
- "A screen on the back of the seat would age quickly and look tacky — I'd rather put my own laptop on this tray, and it'll always work."
