# 2024 Corvette E-Ray: Everyday Supercar, Electrified! (preview)

## Summary

A **preview/ride-along** at GM's Detroit Proving Grounds of the **Corvette E-Ray** — his early take on
the performance-hybrid before the full review. It fits his **electrification-of-the-sports-car** thread
(cf. the Ferrari 296): a small 1.9 kWh battery drives a front-axle e-motor (the rear stays V8), making
the **first AWD Corvette and quickest ever.** His non-hot-take: **"this will be the best everyday-
drivable Corvette ever made,"** because the e-motor adds traction (better in rain/snow) and it ships
with all-season tires. Fully Marques-narrated (he'd only ridden along, not driven).

## Key claims

- **[2023-01-17]** The setup: at the Detroit Proving Grounds; ties to "the electrification of the sports
  car I talked about in the Ferrari 296 video — the NSX is out, the hybrid 911 is coming, and each team
  balances it differently." The E-Ray's approach: a 1.9 kWh battery + a 160 hp **front**-axle e-motor,
  separate from the 6.2L V8 (**rear**), adding ~100 kg → **first AWD Corvette, quickest ever (2.5s 0–60),
  in the wide Z06 body.** Not a plug-in (regen only). (paraphrase)
- **[2023-01-17]** ⭐ The take: **"My hot take — it's not even a hot take — this will be the best everyday-
  drivable Corvette ever made, and it's because of the electrification."** The e-motor adjusts traction
  control hundreds of times/sec (vs. ~once for a gas car) → better traction in rain/snow, quicker off
  low speeds; **all-season tires + carbon-ceramics standard.** "I live in the Northeast, so this is the
  best possible Corvette for someone like me — I'd take it over the Z06." (quote)
- **[2023-01-17]** The quirks (previewing the full review): **stealth mode** (silent electric start, ~3–4
  mi to ~45 mph) with a **~5-second flying-start engine-sync** because the axles "don't talk to each
  other"; a first-in-Corvette **max-regen button** to refill to 100%. ⚠️ His one concern: the **tiny 1.9
  kWh battery will do a lot of deep cycles if driven hard → aging.** (paraphrase)
- **[2023-01-17]** ⭐ Verdict: **"Basically the best Corvette ever made — the C8 was already killing it;
  this is just a quicker, more responsive, more versatile version that doesn't add much weight or lose
  storage (they built the C8 platform with space for this motor). Track guys will still take the lighter
  rear-drive gas car — but to own and daily-drive, this is it."** ~$105k. (quote)
