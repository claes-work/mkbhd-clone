# Samsung Galaxy Z Fold 2 Unboxing: 3 Major Upgrades! (@mkbhd, 2020-09-03)

## Summary
First impressions of the $2,000 Galaxy Z Fold 2 5G, filmed in a trailer in the Arizona desert (hosting David
Blaine's "Ascension"), shot on Komodo. The durable idea: **Samsung's willingness to experiment in the open and
learn from Fold-1 mistakes** produced a phone whose three big upgrades — **screens, hinge, cameras** — are all
lessons learned directly from the first Fold.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-09-03] **"You learn a lot more from mistakes than success" — the benefit of experimenting in the open.**
  "'A lot happened with the first Galaxy Fold, but **the benefit of being Samsung and experimenting out in the
  open is you make mistakes and learn a lot from them — you learn more from mistakes than success.** The Z Fold
  2's changes are learned directly from the Fold 1.'" His pro-iterate-in-public thesis for Samsung.
- [2020-09-03] **The full-size cover screen changes behavior.** "'The worst-looking part of the first Fold —
  that tiny 4.6" cover screen with huge bezels — becomes a **corner-to-corner 6.2" display.** This alone changes
  how I use the Fold: the tiny screen was limiting and made me not want to use the phone closed; **a full-size
  outside screen changes the behavior before you even open it.**'"
- [2020-09-03] **Inside screen + 120 Hz — "what I was hoping for."** "'Inside: thinner bezels, a **7.6" square
  display,** the huge corner cutout replaced by a single hole-punch, and now **120 Hz adaptive (11–120 Hz).**
  This is what I was dreaming of for folding phones. **The crease is still there** (same ultra-thin glass as
  the Z Flip) — you can feel it — but it's not a big problem if you're looking at content, not for the crease.'"
- [2020-09-03] **The hinge — reversing his earlier take.** "'I was trashing the first Fold's hinge, but **this
  second-gen zero-gap hinge now does a lot of the same stuff I just raved about on the Surface Duo** — freestand
  from ~2–115°, plus Flex Mode software. **The hinge is the most important part of a folding device.**'"
- [2020-09-03] **Cameras — the smarter layout is the argument for the form factor.** "'Same flagship-quality
  triple 12 MP system, but the big update is the **layout** — the clunky Fold-1 cutout is gone. Best of all:
  no more compromised inner selfie camera — **you take "rear-camera selfies" using the big cover display as the
  viewfinder,** which is much smarter (and coming to the old Fold too).'"
- [2020-09-03] **The screen-protector caveat.** "'The front protector is fine to remove yourself, but Samsung
  **recommends an official Samsung person remove the pre-installed main-display protector** — I don't love that,
  it has a slight haze, and I'll probably remove it anyway.'" (Recurs as a criticism in the full review.)

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "The benefit of being Samsung and just experimenting out in the open like that is you can make mistakes and
> learn a lot from them — you learn a lot more from mistakes usually than success."
> "What a difference that cover screen makes."
> "The hinge to me feels like the most important part of a folding device."
