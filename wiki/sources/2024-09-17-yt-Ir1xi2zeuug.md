# Apple Watch Series 10 Review: This is It? (@mkbhd, 2024-09-17)

## Summary
A review anchored to a dashed expectation: **rumors said the 10th-gen Apple Watch would be the big "iPhone X"-style
redesign** — and it isn't. It's **"definitely another incremental upgrade"**: slightly thinner/lighter (10% aluminum,
20% via a **titanium** swap replacing stainless), a **bigger screen** (the 46 mm now matches the Ultra's screen size),
a new one-piece metal back and 30%-smaller re-engineered speaker — "like someone squashed last year's watch down a
little." watchOS 11 (Vitals energy score, FDA-approved **sleep-apnea detection**, training load) also comes to older
watches. The **four Series-10-only** reasons to upgrade: (1) **media out loud** from the new speaker; (2) an **LTPO 3
display** down to 1 Hz (always-on second hand + better off-axis viewing); (3) a water-temperature sensor; and (4)
**faster charging** (~0–65% in 30 min). But the **S10 chip isn't noticeably faster** than the S9. His durable
reviewer's-take: **now that Apple has called *this* a "redesign," it's fairly sure sign this is what the Apple Watch
will look like for the next 5–10 years** — meaning even smaller changes ahead, and no sign of the long-wished features
(a rotating bezel, a round shape, a meaningful battery leap — "we didn't get any of that"). So: an easy no-brainer
upgrade from a Series 4/5/6/7; but as a statement, **"this is the Apple Watch — the squircle, no rotating bezel, these
buttons, this shape, 18-hour battery, over and over. Take it or leave it."** (He notes he doesn't sleep-track with it —
he uses the 8 Sleep, "an advantage over something on the back of your wrist.")

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2024-09-17] **The 10th gen wasn't the big redesign — still incremental.** "'There were serious rumors the 10th-gen
  Apple Watch would get the huge iPhone-X-style redesign — 10 is such a nice round number. But it's definitely another
  incremental upgrade: thinner, lighter, a bigger screen, titanium replacing stainless. Like someone squashed last
  year's watch down a little.'"
- [2024-09-17] **The four Series-10-only features.** "'Four things are actually new to this watch: media out loud from
  the redesigned speaker; an LTPO 3 display down to 1 Hz (always-on second hand, better off-axis viewing); a water-
  temperature sensor; and faster charging (~0–65% in 30 minutes). The S10 chip itself isn't noticeably faster than the
  S9.'"
- [2024-09-17] **Now that they've called this a "redesign," this is the watch for 5–10 years.** "'My reviewer's-take:
  now that Apple has called this a big redesign, I'm fairly sure that means this is what the Apple Watch will look like
  for the next 5–10 years — so we're back to even smaller changes from here. All the features we keep wishing for — a
  rotating bezel, a round shape, a meaningful battery improvement — we didn't get any of that, so it feels like they're
  not planning to.'"
- [2024-09-17] **This is Apple's statement of "this is the Apple Watch."** "'This feels like Apple's big statement:
  this is the Apple Watch — the squircle, no rotating bezel, these are the buttons, this is the shape, 18-hour battery,
  over and over again. Take it or leave it. If you have a Series 4–7 and want a new one, this is the no-brainer upgrade.
  But that's it, that's the review.'"
