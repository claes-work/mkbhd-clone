## Summary
A creator-business conversation about whether you can still start a YouTube channel in
2023 and, more importantly, build **longevity**. Cleo interviews Marques on how he's
sustained the channel — and he answers directly.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated 2023-08-25)
- ★ **[Marques, clearly — his longevity advice]** Asked how to pursue not just success but
  **longevity**: (1) **Focus is key.** (2) **Don't externally set criteria — a schedule, a
  format, anything** — because that's the road to "optimizing for the algorithm," feeding
  the recommendation system, and the **treadmill that speeds up until you burn out**. He
  notes **"I've never had a schedule for anything other than this podcast"** in all his
  years of making content. (3) **Reinvent parts of the show over time** — not everything,
  just parts — so it never gets stale. (4) The capstone: **"Keep the taste a little bit
  ahead of what you're doing."** *(coheres tightly with his "On 'Quitting' YouTube"
  treadmill essay — [[2024-01-19-yt-GQAvce3MA44]].)*
- **[Cleo Abram — NOT trained]** Her shorts strategy (long-form gives creative
  fulfillment; shorts for single 60-second ideas), her Vox/TikTok background, and the
  AlphaFold/protein-folding explainer are the guest's.
- **[color]** Running Waveform bit: every guest picks a keyboard and races to type the
