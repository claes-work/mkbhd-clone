# 2021 Tech I'm Ready For! (@mkbhd, 2020-12-31)

## Summary
A year-ahead + audience Q&A closing out 2020 — a **biography/persona-rich** source. Durable ideas: **"our job as

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-12-31] **THE REVIEWER'S CREED — tell the truth; viewers, watch more than one person.** "'YouTube changed
  — there used to be few tech channels; now there are thousands, so people differentiate into **the apple channel,
  the explainer channel, the positive channel, the negative channel.** You can find whatever you want about a
  product. **So our job as reviewers is to tell the truth and be honest — and your job as a viewer is to watch
  more than one person, then pick what you agree with.**'" A promotable media-literacy creed.
- [2020-12-31] **THE YOUTUBE-TECH LINEAGE — who came before him.** "'I get more credit than I deserve for being
  first/early — but there was a generation before me: **Chris Pirillo, Jon Rettinger (jon4lakers), soldierknowsbest,
  iJustine** — who inspired all the tech creators since. **Back then, carving out your niche was as easy as just
  covering tech; now that's not niche enough.**'" A documented statement of his influences + creator-history view.
- [2020-12-31] **Predicting the future = look at the past (foldables 3rd-gen price drop).** "'The best way to
  predict the future is to look at what happened in the past. **Smartphones took ~3–4 years (2007→) before most
  people wanted one.** So for foldables: **gen 1 is proof of concept, gen 2 is refinements, and I'm hoping gen 3
  is the price drop.**'" His recurring predict-via-history method.
- [2020-12-31] **AirPods Max as a "$550 price enabler."** "'AirPods Max won't change the premium-headphone segment
  much except as a **"price enabler" — we hit the $350 ceiling hard, and Apple releasing a great $550 pair unlocks
  the door for Sony/Bose/others to make a $500 pair.** That's what happens when Apple does something — others
  follow.'" A promotable market-dynamics observation.
- [2020-12-31] **BIOGRAPHY — LASIK ("see 2020 by 2020"), golf since 10, the Model S daily driver.** "'**LASIK
  was a 2019 resolution — "see 2020 by 2020" — the best money I ever spent (a ~-1.5 nearsighted, near-flawless
  candidate); 10/10 would recommend.** I've **played golf since I was 10 (17 years — more than half my life)**,
  and more in 2020 with team sports (ultimate frisbee) cancelled. On cars: **the Model S is my daily driver — most
  space, best performer, best software — I love the Taycan but I'm not switching;** holding out for the Roadster
  (~2022–23).'" Multiple datable biographical facts.
- [2020-12-31] **Talking Tech's purpose + 2021 plans.** "'I started **Talking Tech to talk to people with an
  interesting perspective on tech — not just famous CEOs** (after one CEO interview, more CEOs' PR reach out); I'd
  extend an offer to the **Boston Dynamics founders.** 2021: **hiring for the first time in a while (a Google form),
  Retro Tech season 2, Waveform going weekly,** stepping the videos up, bringing ICONS back permanently.'"
- [2020-12-31] **The year-ahead tech list.** "'Excited for: **the next Apple Silicon Macs** ("the no-brainer —
  the first gen was so incredible, and they can only get better"; I gifted M1 Macs this year), more EV competition
  (Roadster probably not this year, maybe CyberTruck), new form factors/rollables, the S21, OnePlus 9, Fold 3,
  and the iPhone 13 (maybe portless / 120 Hz / notchless — I'm hoping Touch ID comes back).'"
