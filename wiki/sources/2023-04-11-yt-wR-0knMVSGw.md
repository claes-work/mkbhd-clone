# The Rivian R1S Is... The Best SUV Ever? (@mkbhd, 2023-04-11)

## Summary
A review of the **Rivian R1S** (the R1T truck's platform with the bed/gear-tunnel swapped for a **third row**) that
doubles as an EV-strategy explainer. The provocative open — **"the best SUV you can buy right now just happens to be
electric"** — is "actually a strategy": because an electric drivetrain is **new and expensive**, a company adding it
faces a choice — **compete at the highest end (make the best overall product) or at the lowest end on price** — and
**business 101 says when the tech is expensive you compete at the high end** (which is why EVs are expensive and
cheap EVs are scarce). The R1S makes a compelling case: a genuinely great three-row SUV — clever split tailgate,
huge frunk + sub-trunk space (fits a mattress folded down), 1,500 W outlets, an **800 hp / ~7,000 lb quad-motor
missile** that's also relaxed in all-purpose mode. It **"really fits the adventure-vehicle mantra."** Its one
possible knock: it sways a touch more than the R1T and "isn't the sportiest," but 99% of drivers will never notice.
The open question is **when this fun, good EV experience comes down in price** — a tech/development question (Rivian
also funds a charging network and delivery vans) — teased via the **R2 lower-priced platform** (he interviewed CEO
RJ Scaringe on his podcast). "R1T and R1S set the tone for what we can expect from Rivian."

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2023-04-11] **Leading with "the best SUV is electric" is a strategy.** "'Starting with "the best SUV you can buy
  happens to be electric" is actually a strategy. An electric drivetrain is new and expensive, so a carmaker chooses:
  compete at the highest end to make the best possible product, or compete at the lowest end on price. Business 101 —
  when the tech is expensive, it makes more sense to compete at the high end with a no-brainer better product; the
  cheap ones come later as the tech gets cheaper. That's why EVs are expensive and cheap EVs are scarce.'"
- [2023-04-11] **The R1S is the R1T with a third row — and a lot of clever space.** "'The front half is the same R1T
  (go watch that video); the back trades the truck bed for a third row. Clever split tailgate, a huge frunk plus
  sub-trunk space, 1,500 W outlets and an air compressor kept from the truck, and folded down it's a giant flat area
  that fits a mattress.'"
- [2023-04-11] **An 800 hp, 7,000 lb missile — but relaxed when you want.** "'It's a quad-motor 800 hp missile in a
  straight line — absurd, it has no right being this fast at 7,000 lbs. Shorter wheelbase than the R1T, so it feels
  more nimble. In all-purpose mode it drives as good as any SUV you can imagine; sport mode drops the air suspension.
  The only knock: it sways a touch more than the R1T and isn't the sportiest — but 99% of drivers will never
  notice.'"
- [2023-04-11] **The adventure-vehicle mantra.** "'It really fits the adventure-vehicle mantra — quietest, most
  responsive, most efficient, most space over the other six/seven-seat SUVs. If the mission is the most fun thing
  for people who don't really have a budget, I don't think there's much else I'd take over this.'"
- [2023-04-11] **The open question — when does the good EV experience get cheaper?** "'The question for everyone else
  is when the fun, good EV experience comes down in price — a tech and development question (this company also funds
  a charging network and the delivery vans). I talked to CEO RJ on my podcast about the R2 platform, a lower-priced
  version of this same experience; if they pull it off in as soon as a year, we're moving in the right direction.'"
