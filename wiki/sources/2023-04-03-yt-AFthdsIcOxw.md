# This Voice is Entirely AI... (@mkbhd, 2023-04-03)

## Summary
A framework video ("I have a theory... I don't know what to call it"): **the two levels of AI-generated content.**
**Level one** — AI fools you *when you're not looking for AI* (you scroll past the AI "Pope in a puffer jacket" photo
and only when told it's fake does your skeptical eye kick in — the hands are weird, details garbled). **Level two** —
**AI fools you even when you *know* you're supposed to be looking at AI, and it still works.** His level-two example:
an artist (Mr. J. Maderos / "Vince") sent him a **track with an AI-generated Jay-Z voice** — and even knowing it's
AI, **"I can still hear it and sort of enjoy it like I'm actually listening to Jay-Z."** The unsettling part: **"this
is the worst this technology will ever be."** The bigger point: **every one of these technologies is trying to
advance from level one to level two** — the chatbot's goal is to pass as a human in conversation; the image
generator's, to make usable art; the self-driving car's, to drive among humans. Is there a solution? **"Not yet"** —
he doesn't believe in outright bans; likely there'll be a **parallel development of AI-detection tools** you deploy
when there's reason to. **"For now, let's enjoy level one — you won't have just that for much longer."**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2023-04-03] **The two levels of AI.** "'My theory: there are two levels of success for sufficiently advanced AI.
  Level one is when AI-generated content fools you because you weren't looking for AI (the Pope photo you just
  scrolled past). Level two is when you *know* you're supposed to be looking at AI and it still fools you — that's
  when it gets scary.'"
- [2023-04-03] **The AI Jay-Z verse — a level-two example.** "'An artist sent me a track with an AI-generated Jay-Z
  voice. Even knowing I'm listening to AI-generated content, I can still hear it and sort of enjoy it like I'm
  actually listening to Jay-Z. That's what opens the can of worms — it's borderline scary knowing how good it is.'"
- [2023-04-03] **This is the worst it will ever be.** "'And the cherry on top: this is the worst this technology will
  ever be. There are level-one examples everywhere (low-stakes content, chatbot-written emails) and it's kind of
  fine because it's just an email — but the goal of all of these technologies is to advance to level two.'"
- [2023-04-03] **Everything is aiming at level two.** "'The chatbot's goal, when it gets better, is to pass as a
  human in conversation. The image generator's goal is to produce usable paintings like a human. The self-driving
  car's goal is to drive alongside humans. That's wild — but that's what they're all working toward.'"
- [2023-04-03] **The solution — not yet; detection tools will have to develop.** "'Is there a solution? Really, not
  yet — it's so new there's no answer. Some think regulation, some think banning; I don't believe in that. I think
  there has to be a parallel development of tools designed to detect AI content, and you learn to deploy them when
  there's a reason. For now, let's enjoy level one — you won't have just that for much longer.'"
