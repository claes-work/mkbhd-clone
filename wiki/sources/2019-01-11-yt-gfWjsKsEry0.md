# Riding in a Driverless Taxi at CES 2019! (@mkbhd, 2019-01-11)

## Summary
A first-hand ride in a **fully driverless taxi** (Yandex's retrofitted Prius) on real Las Vegas streets at CES
2019 — his most concrete encounter with autonomy. Contrasts it with the "useless-while-still-concepts" autonomous
show cars, explains the sensor fusion, and comes away optimistic.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-01-11] **Concept autonomous interiors "feel useless while they're still concepts."** "Every hall has
  open-room autonomous-future concepts — turn around, face your friends, a moving room. But **as long as they're
  concepts, they don't feel real yet.** BMW's **iNext** was a happy medium (futuristic but still a normal car) —
  but without the actual self-driving tech in action, it's half the story." A demand for the real thing over
  vaporware.
- [2019-01-11] **The real ride — "this really feels like the future."** "**Yandex** (the Russian tech company) had a
  **fully driverless car on the streets with regular traffic** — a retrofitted Prius with a radar/lidar/camera
  array. A safety engineer sat in the passenger seat with a **kill switch**, but **no one in the driver's seat** for
  a ~15-minute loop on real streets, real drivers, real pedestrians, real traffic lights — **everything smooth. It
  really feels like the future.**" A dated, first-hand autonomy milestone.
- [2019-01-11] **Teach-the-mechanism — the sensor-fusion visualization.** "The nerdy part: **iPad Pros showing what
  the sensors see in real time** — radar/lidar/cameras combining to identify cars, pedestrians, stationary objects
  up to **250 m in every direction** (things 6–7 cars away I couldn't see out the window). **Red paths and green
  paths** — it evaluates what's happening and what's about to happen, and takes the green path to a predetermined
  destination." A clear explanation of how it works.
- [2019-01-11] **The taxi model makes sense.** "It's a **pre-mapped area with predetermined routes** — but the road
  conditions, other cars, pedestrians, lights, turns, speed changes were **all the car's decisions.** And that
  **makes perfect sense for a taxi: map the city, set pickup/drop-off routes, and the taxis go driverless.**" A
  practical read of the deployment path.
- [2019-01-11] **The optimism (+ the human reactions).** "People pulled out phones to record it; Vegas cops think
  it's funny. The 15 minutes were a lot of fun and **gave me optimism for the future of self-driving.**" (Ties his
  Tesla-Autopilot familiarity — "it helped me not be as nervous in the backseat.") A hopeful, grounded close.

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "This really feels like the future. It might not be the crazy self-driving interior everyone else is talking
> about, but this tech part is cool to me."
> "The iPads are a visualization of what the sensors see — radar, lidar, and cameras combining to identify cars,
> pedestrians, and stationary objects up to 250 meters away in every direction."
> "That all makes sense for a taxi: you map the city, you have predetermined routes, and the taxis are driverless."
