# 2019 Mac Pro Impressions: Cheese Grater is Back! (@mkbhd, 2019-06-04)

## Summary
First impressions from WWDC of the long-awaited 2019 Mac Pro ("cheese grater") and the Pro Display XDR — the
end of the multi-year "please don't cancel the Mac Pro" saga. The throughline: **true modular, upgradeable pro
hardware is back**, and **"pro" gear is genuinely, unapologetically expensive** because pros pay for
speed/quality-control, not for it to make them better.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-06-04] **Modularity is the point — "the Mac Pro we've been waiting for."** "A **rebuild from the ground
  up**: 8→28-core Xeon, up to **1.5 TB RAM**, up to 4 GPUs, **8 PCI slots**, MPX modules (incl. an **Afterburner
  card** offloading ProRes/ProRes RAW encoding from the CPU). 'That is the kind of **modularity people were asking
  for and waiting for for so long.'** You **buy the tier you need and upgrade later** — unlike the iMac Pro where
  you had to buy the highest end." Resolves the trash-can-Mac-Pro complaint. "I'll take the cheese grater over a
  trash can any day."
- [2019-06-04] **PRO gear is supposed to be expensive — the "pro" framework.** "'The word **pro** I think is sort
  of overused (OnePlus 7 Pro, iPad Pro) — but for **actual professional-grade equipment, that stuff is very
  expensive and clearly not for everyone.'** Starts at **$6,000.** **No amount of money on a creative tool makes
  you better at creating** — but it helps with **quality control and speed, which is what professionals pay a lot
  of money for.**" A durable belief about tools-vs-talent.
- [2019-06-04] **The Pro Display XDR + the stand-pricing PR lesson.** "A **32" 6K HDR** LCD, 1,600-nit peak, 1M:1
  contrast, nano-texture matte option — **$5,000** ($6k for matte), competing against **$40,000 reference
  monitors** where it 'can look like a bargain.' But announcing the **$1,000 stand separately on stage was
  terrible PR** — 'why would you do it like that?' They should have said '$6,000 monitor, $1,000 off without the
  stand.' Pros who wall-mount buy monitors sans stand all the time; normal viewers don't think that way." A
  marketing-framing critique.
- [2019-06-04] **PERSONAL WORKFLOW — his most-anticipated pro tool.** "'I've been **waiting for this for a
  while**'; looking forward to seeing **how it fits into my workflow** (the Afterburner handles ProRes but not 3D
  transcoding). Guesses a **maxed-out config will hit ~$40,000.**" Connects the product to his own
  production pipeline ([[production-filmmaking]]).
- [2019-06-04] **The Tesla-Roadster comparison for spec absurdity.** "'It felt kind of like the **Tesla 2020
  Roadster announcement** when they went through these specs — they're just absolutely ridiculous.'" A
  characteristic cross-domain reference tying Apple pro-spec excess to EV hype.
