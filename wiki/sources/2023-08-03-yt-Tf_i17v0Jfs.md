## Key claims (David-attributed — CONTEXT ONLY)
- [2023-08-03] The **exposure triangle** (aperture / ISO / shutter speed) taught via a
  camera obscura; David's standing advice: "**shoot manual and understand these things
  really well**" to make intentional decisions. Film ISO = physical silver-halide grain
  size; digital ISO = signal-to-noise.
- [2023-08-03] The 3D-printed camera's light-leak saga (one printer's lower filament
  density let light through the plastic); Angus shipped a fixed one. "3D printing something
  is easy, but having the know-how to make a camera obscura, not so easy." ~£1,000.
