# iPhone 15 Pro: 3 Months Later! (@mkbhd, 2023-12-28)

## Summary
An 88-days-later long-term review of his titanium iPhone 15 Pro (no case) — the kind of review only time enables
("the longer I use a phone, the more I uncover, especially about the way *I* use it"). He's happy with his spec
(titanium "looks way better than stainless, and lighter"; the light color hides fingerprints best). Three
long-term behaviors: he **basically never uses the new multi-focal-length 1x/1.2x/1.5x** ("I just frame at 1x"); the
**Action button** he cycled through many mappings (Google Assistant → ChatGPT voice → a TickTick task box) and
**ended on just opening the camera** — "muscle memory to hit the button and take the photo"; and **USB-C is great**
(one cable for everything; USB 3.2 on the Pro helps ProRes imports) — but he **still can't ditch the Lightning
cable** because the AirPods Max, Magic Keyboard, and Trackpad are still Lightning. Verdict: **"you don't have to
upgrade every year, but this is one of the good years"** — the **upgrade sweet spot** (cameras + USB-C + titanium),
especially from an older iPhone. The closer is a biography beat: he'd compared the 15 Pro to a **Porsche 911** (refine
the same thing generation to generation) — and **for his 30th birthday he bought a matte-black 911**, and "once you
live with a 911, you appreciate that strategy — it's boring, predictable, familiar, confidence-inspiring." Shot
entirely on the iPhone 15 Pro.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2023-12-28] **The long-term review uncovers how you actually use it.** "'The usual review is one to two weeks
  before I summarize everything. But the longer I use a phone, the more I uncover — especially about the way I use
  it. As someone who carries two phones, the iPhone's in my pocket a long time, so I have a lot more to say.'"
- [2023-12-28] **The Action button ended on the camera.** "'I went nuts with the possibilities — first mapped it to
  Google Assistant (I never use Siri), then a ChatGPT voice chat, then a TickTick new-task box. But I ended up just
  setting it to open the camera — the fastest way to get a shot when something's about to go away; I have the muscle
  memory now to hit the button and take the photo.'"
- [2023-12-28] **USB-C is great — but I still can't ditch Lightning.** "'Thank you, Europe. One cable for the iPhone,
  any Android, the iPad Pro, my laptop — much nicer. But I still carry a Lightning cable everywhere because the
  AirPods Max (my travel headphones), the Magic Keyboard, and the Trackpad are still Lightning.'"
- [2023-12-28] **The upgrade sweet spot.** "'You don't have to upgrade every year or two. But the more I use the 15
  Pro, the more confident I am this is one of the good years to upgrade — the cameras, USB-C, titanium. There's a
  concept of an upgrade sweet spot, and this phone hit it, especially for people on much older iPhones.'"
- [2023-12-28] **Turned 30, bought a matte-black Porsche 911 — the same strategy.** "'In the review I compared this
  to the Porsche 911 — the design barely changes generation to generation; they spend a long time refining a
  slightly better version of the same thing. For my 30th I took that plunge too (matte black). Once you live with a
  911, you appreciate that strategy — it's not flashy, it's boring, predictable, familiar, confidence-inspiring.'"
