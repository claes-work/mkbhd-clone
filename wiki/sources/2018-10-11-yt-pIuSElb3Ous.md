# Razer Phone 2 Impressions! (@mkbhd, 2018-10-11)

## Summary
"Techtober" first-look at the Razer Phone 2 — a gaming phone deliberately softened toward "a normal everyday
flagship." Best material: the **waterproofing-vs-speakers** teach-the-mechanism (does IP-rating kill the big
speakers?) and the "team no notch / team anti-$1000-flagship" framing.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-10-11] **The strategy — "a normal everyday flagship that happens to be good at gaming."** "Every tweak is
  aimed at appealing to the masses, not just gamers." New: a **glass back** (enables **wireless charging**), a
  glowing **RGB Razer logo** (usable as a notification LED — "actually pretty sick"), **IP67**, upgraded cameras,
  and a **new vibration motor** (he'd hated the last one). Still the unique **120 Hz LCD** ("the only one in a
  phone again this year"), SD845/8 GB/4000 mAh.
- [2018-10-11] **TEACH-THE-MECHANISM — waterproofing vs speakers.** "**Waterproofing a phone always hits the
  speakers** — a watertight diaphragm in front of the driver is a big deal. Samsung's phones used to have better
  speakers before they waterproofed them; HTC's boom-sound monsters were never IP-rated. So my big question:
  would going IP67 **kill the Razer's amazing speakers?**" His answer: "**I don't think so** — still very loud,
  hard to block, no distortion at high volume; maybe slightly lower peak." A clean explanation of a real
  engineering tradeoff.
- [2018-10-11] **The 120 Hz tradeoff, restated.** "The 120 Hz LCD is brighter now (645 nits) but **still not as
  bright as the 60 Hz OLEDs** in mainstream flagships — that's the trade-off for the ultra-smooth frame rate."
  Consistent with his ProMotion enthusiasm + honesty.
- [2018-10-11] **The audience framing.** "If you're **team no notch**, this is one of your **last** options. If
  you're **team 'I hate every $1000 flagship because they're not worth it,'** this is one of your latest — and one
  of your **best**." A characteristic map-the-buyer close.

## Notable verbatim quotes
