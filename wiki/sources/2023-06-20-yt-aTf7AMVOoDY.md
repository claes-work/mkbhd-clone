# Pixel Tablet Review: Is Google Back?! (@mkbhd, 2023-06-20)

## Summary
The full review of the **$499 Pixel Tablet**, framed by history: a decade ago there was a vibrant tablet ecosystem
anchored by Google's Nexus 7/10, then Google made a few bad ones and **"literally just gave up."** Meanwhile
smart-home displays (Nest Hub) took off — **"a smart home display is basically just a cheap tablet"** — so Google
made a cheap tablet that becomes a smart-home display. His verdict: **"tame your expectations"** — it's a decent
mid-range Android tablet (soft-touch metal, an 11" 60 Hz LCD, Tensor G2 that he "cannot call fast") and a
**slightly-below-average speaker-dock hub** — and that's fine because **two-in-one devices are always slightly worse
at one of the things** they try to be, but these two are close enough. Software highlights: the new swipe-up dock;
**multi-user support** ("so sick — the iPad should seriously be taking notes"); Hub mode. But the recurring
weakness: **the Android-tablet app situation** — Google's own apps are optimized, but Instagram/Twitter/TikTok/etc.
are **"still just big phone apps"** — the biggest weakness of any Android tablet, because the developer incentive
(one iPad app covers all iPads; one Android tablet app won't) is still tough. The one petty win: **"they got the
calculator optimized before the iPad — your move, Apple."** He'd love a high-end docking tablet, but knows it
wouldn't sell; this is the version that makes sense.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2023-06-20] **A cheap tablet that becomes a smart-home display.** "'Google gave up on tablets, but smart-home
  displays took off — and a smart home display is basically just a cheap tablet. So they thought: what if we made a
  cheap tablet that could turn into a smart home display? That's the Pixel Tablet. It's not amazing or premium —
  tame your expectations — but it's interesting and underrated.'"
- [2023-06-20] **Two-in-one devices are always slightly worse at one thing.** "'Two-in-one devices are funny because
  they're always slightly worse at one of the things they're trying to be. This is a tablet and a smart-home hub — it
  turns out to be a decent cheap Android tablet and a slightly-below-average speaker-dock hub. But those two are
  close enough and simple enough that it makes sense.'"
- [2023-06-20] **Multi-user support — the iPad should take notes.** "'Multi-user support is so sick, and the iPad
  should seriously be taking notes. From the lock screen or notification tray you switch users with your
  fingerprint; each person sets up their own apps and home screen. Tablets are used at home by families — logging
  into your kid's account so they don't buy $30,000 of Minecraft credits is obviously great.'"
- [2023-06-20] **The app situation is the biggest weakness.** "'Google's own apps are optimized, but all the
  third-party apps that make the world go round — Instagram, Twitter, TikTok, Expedia — are still just big phone
  apps. The Android-tablet app ecosystem is still, even now, the biggest weakness. The incentive is tough: one iPad
  app covers all iPads, but one Android tablet app won't work on all Android tablets.'"
- [2023-06-20] **The petty win — the calculator.** "'There is one app Google got optimized before the iPad: the
  calculator. Full readout, history, light/dark theme. Your move, Apple.'"
