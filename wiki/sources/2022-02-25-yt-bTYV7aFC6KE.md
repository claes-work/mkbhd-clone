# Galaxy Tab S8 Ultra: A Monster Tablet! (@mkbhd, 2022-02-25)

## Summary
A review of the 14.6" Galaxy Tab S8 Ultra — "**the best Android tablet out right now**," an impossibly-thin
1.6-lb machine that "looks like it's from the future." The standout is the **largest OLED touchscreen he's ever
used** (every pixel self-lit, inky blacks). He notes the **irony of a notch** — the company that made ads mocking
the iPhone's notch is the first to put one on a tablet (but "it disappears" and it's in the right place for a
horizontal-first device). The recurring tablet tension: it's still an Android tablet with the **app-scaling
problem** (Instagram, TickTick, SoundCloud don't scale), but **Samsung DeX** (a desktop windowed UI) makes it far
more computer-like — and in several ways **it's a better attempt at being a computer than the iPad** (floating
windows, a microSD slot, better-placed webcams). Verdict: **know what you want** — the first tablet that actually
earns the "Ultra" name.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-02-25] **The best Android tablet — the largest OLED touchscreen.** "'Show this to someone 15 years ago and
  they'd think it's impossible — an impossibly thin 1.6-lb machine, room-filling speakers, a desktop interface
  inside. **The standout is the 14.6" display — probably the largest OLED touchscreen I've ever used** (every
  pixel self-lit, pitch-black blacks, incredible contrast). The one non-world-class thing: it's not LTPO, and it
  maxes at ~380 nits (fine inside, weak in the sun).'"
- [2022-02-25] **The notch irony — but it disappears.** "'A hilarious irony: **the same company that made
  commercials mocking the notch is the first to put one on a tablet** — but honestly it doesn't bother me; use the
  screen for 3 seconds and it disappears. And they put it in the right place (the long horizontal side) — this is
  a horizontal-first device.'"
- [2022-02-25] **We don't actually hold big tablets — the case is the kickstand.** "'Dave2D made a smart point: **we
  don't usually hold these huge tablets — most of the time they're propped up or in a folio.** My iPad Pro hasn't
  left its folio in a year. So the $350 book-cover case (your kickstand + keyboard) is basically part of the
  package — a ~$450+ total.'"
- [2022-02-25] **DeX vs the Android-tablet app problem.** "'It's still a large Android tablet, so it has the
  **app-scaling problem** (no properly-scaling Instagram, TickTick doesn't use the whole screen, SoundCloud
  doesn't work — that's on developers). **But Samsung DeX is a way around it** — a desktop windowed UI (feels like
  a Chromebook) with a dock, file management, floating windows — a next level of versatility for making it your
  primary computer.'"
- [2022-02-25] **Better at being a computer than the iPad — but know what you want.** "'It's still top-heavy and
  hard to use on your lap (same as the iPad) — **but this is clearly better than the iPad's attempt at being a
  computer**: floating-window support, a microSD slot the iPad never had, and horizontally-placed, higher-quality
  webcams (good for calls). The pen's included; 2.8 ms latency, real-paper feel; second-best tablet speakers I've
  heard (behind the big iPad Pro). Do I recommend it? **Know what you want — if you wanted a tablet that actually
  fits the "Ultra" name, this is the first one that does it.**'"
