# Sony WH-1000XM6 Review: Back on the Throne! (@mkbhd, 2025-05-15)

## Summary
A review of the **Sony WH-1000XM6** — Sony's flagship ANC headphones, which **fix what the Mark 5s got wrong** (the
XM5s "made some things worse," which was odd for the long-time king of the hill). The comfort stays elite (very light,
soft vegan-leather pads, a flexible headband), and — the headline fix — **they fold again**, into a **significantly
smaller case that closes with a magnetic buckle instead of a zipper** ("pretty much perfect — about to be copied
everywhere"); they also rotate flat around the neck again. Under the hood: **12 mics (up from 8), a more powerful QN3
chip, all-new drivers, and AI trained on 500M voice samples** — yielding **even stronger noise cancellation** than the
"already GOATed" Mark 5s (a much stronger low end/sub-bass), and — the real highlight — **a transparency mode that went
from muddy to "up there with AirPods Max levels of crystal clear and natural."** The refreshed Sound Connect app adds a
fun **listening mode** (cafe/living-room/"my room" that makes music sound like tinny speakers 20 ft away, plus a cinema
upmix). His verdict on the **$450 price** (up from the XM5's $400, now ~$350) is the crux: **"I actually still think
they're worth it"** — not because the price is comfortable, but because **the whole premium-ANC category is full of
rivals that try to beat these and don't, which makes these still feel like the deal** (the Bose QC Ultra at $429, the
Sonos at $450, JBL, Beats Studio — "this smokes those"; AirPods Max are $550 and Apple-only, while these do multi-device
including an Apple + non-Apple device at once). **"If you just want the flat-out best headphones to fly, work, and
commute with — the most comfort, the best noise cancellation — these are back on the throne."** (Minor gripes: sweaty
ears after a couple hours; the finicky touch controls.)

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2025-05-15] **They fix the Mark 5s — and fold again.** "'The XM5s left room for improvement — they made some things
  worse, odd for the long-time king. The Mark 6s fix a lot: they fold again into a significantly smaller case that
  closes with a magnetic buckle instead of a zipper — pretty much perfect, about to be copied everywhere — and they
  rotate flat around the neck again. Back near the top for travel.'"
- [2025-05-15] **Stronger ANC and a much better transparency mode.** "'Under the hood: 12 mics (up from 8), a more
  powerful QN3 chip, all-new drivers, AI trained on 500M voice samples. Even stronger noise cancellation than the
  already-GOATed Mark 5s (a much stronger low end/sub-bass). And the real highlight — the transparency mode went from
  muddy to up there with AirPods Max levels of crystal clear and natural.'"
- [2025-05-15] **$450 is expensive but still worth it — because the competition doesn't beat them.** "'They launch at
  $450 (up from the XM5's $400, now ~$350) — really expensive, maybe partly tariffs. I still think they're worth it,
  because the whole premium-ANC category is full of rivals that try to beat these and don't, which makes these still
  feel like the deal: the Bose QC Ultra ($429) — these are better; the Sonos ($450) — better; JBL — smokes it; the Beats
  Studio — smokes it. AirPods Max are $550 and Apple-only, while these do multi-device including an Apple and a non-Apple
  device at once.'"
- [2025-05-15] **Back on the throne.** "'If you just want the flat-out best headphones to fly, work, and commute with —
  the most comfort, the best noise cancellation — that's just how much they cost. These are back on the throne.'"
