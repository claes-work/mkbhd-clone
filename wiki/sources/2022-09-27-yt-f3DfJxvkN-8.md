# AirPods Pro 2 Review: 1 Underrated Thing! (@mkbhd, 2022-09-27)

## Summary
A review he wasn't even planning to do — until he tried them and was won over, **"but not by what you think."** The
new AirPods Pro 2 look near-identical, and the obvious upgrades are real but not the point: **dramatically better
sound** (a new driver — the best-sounding earbuds Apple's made), 33% longer battery, a new XS ear tip, and **a
findable case** (a lanyard loop + a speaker so you can locate it, U1 precision finding, an Apple Watch charger).
The thing that actually convinced him is **the H2 chip** — which enables **much stronger ANC** (he'd now wear
AirPods on a plane) and, above all, **adaptive transparency**: still passing through outside sound but **real-time
trimming any harsh sound above 85 dB** (an ambulance, a jackhammer, a barking dog) while keeping its signature —
sampling outside sound 48,000×/sec. Since **AirPods' transparency mode was already best-by-a-mile**, this levels it
up again. The takeaway: they're **"just flat-out better than I expected"** — and perfect for **"AirPods people."**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-09-27] **Better sound + a findable case — but not the point.** "'Near-identical design (only the dual top
  vents give them away), but **an entirely new driver — dramatically better sound (more lows/mids, little
  distortion); the best-sounding earbuds Apple's made** (still no lossless, but good 16-bit/48 kHz). Plus 33%
  longer battery (6 hrs ANC), a new XS ear tip, and **a findable case** — Apple clearly had a meeting about how
  often people lose these: a lanyard loop, **a speaker to play a sound**, U1 precision finding, IPX4, memoji
  engraving, and it works with an Apple Watch charger. Even stem volume controls. **But none of that convinced
  me.**'"
- [2022-09-27] **The H2 chip — much stronger ANC.** "'The thing that convinced me is **the H2 chip** (2nd-gen, ~2×
  the ANC). Combined with the new drivers, **it erases white noise (ACs, transportation) immediately — I now
  understand why people wear these on a plane** (I put the ANC on the level of the Sony XM5, just below the Bose
  QC Earbuds 2, and above the AirPods Max).'"
- [2022-09-27] **Adaptive transparency — the underrated thing.** "'AirPods' transparency mode has long been **best
  by a mile** — and the H2 levels it up with **adaptive transparency**: it still passes all outside sound through,
  but **anytime a harsh sound goes above 85 dB (an ambulance, a jackhammer, a barking dog) it trims it in real time
  while keeping its sound signature** — sampling outside sound **48,000×/sec (240× faster than last gen)**, all on
  the earbuds, no delay. **A real hack: instead of concert earplugs, wear these in transparency and it drops the
  whole thing to a safe level while you still feel the bass.**'"
- [2022-09-27] **The other H2 touches.** "'It also talks to the Watch's Noise app in real time, chimes on a
  **mismatched-AirPod** (someone else's in your case), and does an **ear scan (Face ID depth sensors) for
  personalized spatial audio** — which mostly just widened the soundstage for me (a lot of computational audio is
  already happening — the EQ adapts to every track/volume, which makes them hard to A/B).'"
- [2022-09-27] **For "AirPods people."** "'A significant upgrade — flat-out better than I expected. At the beginning
  I said "AirPods people" — the folks who wear AirPods everywhere, all the time. **These are amazing for them:
  better battery, sound, ANC, much better transparency, a findable case, stem volume — all at the same price. I get
  it. AirPods Pro 2 are really, really good.**' (Samsung's and the Pixel Buds are also good.)'"
