# Google Pixel 5A: Spot the Difference! (@mkbhd, 2021-08-17)

## Summary
Review of the $449 Pixel 5A — **"the smallest year-over-year refresh I've ever seen"** (nearly identical to the 4a
5G: same chip, RAM, storage, cameras). The durable content is the analysis of **why Google would release an almost-
identical phone**: the pressure to keep an annual line updated, and — crucially — **Tensor was saved for the Pixel
6** (you don't debut new custom silicon in a mid-ranger; Google actually wanted Tensor in the Pixel 5, but it
slipped). His prediction: the future A-series should **use the same Tensor chip as the flagship, like Apple's
iPhone SE strategy** — but **a lot is riding on the Pixel 6.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-08-17] **"Spot the difference" — the smallest refresh ever.** "'This feels like a spot-the-difference
  newspaper game. **The smallest year-over-year refresh I've ever seen** — same 765G, 6 GB, 128 GB, same cameras,
  fingerprint, speakers, headphone jack, 18 W charging. What changed: a slightly greener "Mostly Black," a hair
  bigger (6.3"), a **metal unibody (IP67), a substantially bigger ~4,700 mAh battery** (its biggest improvement is
  something it already did well), Gorilla Glass 6 — but they **removed the laser autofocus** (a cost cut). $499 →
  $449.'"
- [2021-08-17] **Why release an almost-identical phone? — the annual-line pressure.** "'Reviewing it is easy;
  figuring out **why** was the challenge — companies don't do things for no reason. **(1) There's pressure to keep
  an annual line updated** even for a minor bump. And a good all-around ~$500 phone with the best camera in its
  class (vs the A-series, SE, Nord) is still a safe bet.'"
- [2021-08-17] **THE REAL REASON — Tensor was saved for the Pixel 6.** "'Their choices were: this, don't release
  a 5A at all, or **put Tensor in the 5A first — but you don't debut your new custom silicon in a mid-ranger,
  because it inherits a mid-range-silicon reputation; you put it in the shiny Pixel 6.** From conversations with
  people at Google, **they actually wanted Tensor in the Pixel 5 — it slipped, so they pushed the Pixel 5 out with
  whatever Qualcomm chip worked (the 765G, rushed, even missing its Neural Core).** So the 5A is potentially the
  last non-Tensor Pixel.'" An insider-informed strategic read.
- [2021-08-17] **THE PREDICTION — use the flagship's Tensor in the 6A, like Apple's SE.** "'My bet for the
  A-series future: **do what Apple does — put the same chip in the cheaper phone (the iPhone SE used the iPhone
  11's chip: seemingly overpowered, but future-proof).** A toned-down A-series body with a Tensor chip should be
  great sub-$500. But that requires the Pixel 6 to sell well at scale so the chip price comes down.'"
- [2021-08-17] **"A lot is riding on the Pixel 6."** "'**A lot is riding on the Pixel 6** — they're ramping up
  marketing and really trying to sell a lot. That's what I'm excited for in Google land.'"
