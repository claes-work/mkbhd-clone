# iMac Pro Setup Tour 2018! (@mkbhd, 2018-01-04)

## Summary
A full early-2018 desk/setup tour — a biography/production goldmine detailing exactly what he uses to make
videos, now built around the **iMac Pro** (which replaced the trash-can Mac Pro). Also his design philosophy
("minimal, clean, frictionless — turn footage into videos") and a nice meta-note on how to *watch* a setup
tour (gather inspiration, don't copy).

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-01-04] **The editing rig, in detail (PRODUCTION/BIOGRAPHY).** The **custom X-Desk (formerly NextDesk)
  Air Pro** motorized sit-stand desk (four motors, ~6 years old — one of the first pieces in the studio; he
  uses the bare metal top, black legs, a Philips Hue strip behind it); **Yamaha HS8** studio monitors (flat/
  neutral reference, on RLX isolation pads); an **80 TB Promise Pegasus R3** Thunderbolt-3 RAID (archive);
  the **iMac Pro** (10-core W, 128 GB RAM, 2 TB SSD, 16 GB Vega — with a **maxed 18-core version ordered**),
  on a space-gray 12 South stand; **Sennheiser HD800 ColorWare** headphones (open-back, flat, great for
  editing) driven by an **Apollo Twin Mk2 Duo** preamp ("dead silent"); a **Herman Miller Embody** chair
  ("everyone in the studio has this now"); the **Hasselblad X1D** kept near him; the acoustic foam/fiberglass
  panel array. A definitive 2018 rig record.
- [2018-01-04] **His unconventional peripheral setup (a personal-taste tell).** "Mouse on the right (Logitech
  MX Master 2S, space gray), trackpad on the left (mostly for zooming/scrolling between macOS Spaces in a
  three-panel setup). **I'm not lefty — I just have a certain weird way of doing things** people find odd."
- [2018-01-04] **Daily drivers (dated).** "My phone of choice is sometimes on the desk — right now the **panda
  Pixel 2 XL** (my main), an **iPhone X**, and the new **white OnePlus 5T** (I'm a huge fan of the colored
  power button — hoping that becomes a thing)." His two-phone system, current state.
- [2018-01-04] **The design philosophy + the "it comes together over time" honesty.** "Minimal, pretty clean,
  the way I like it — all for me to make it **as frictionless as possible to turn footage into videos.**
  Before you make a spreadsheet of the cost: this didn't happen all at once — I've had the desk ~6 years, the
  speakers about as long, versions of this chair for years; it comes together with time." Anti-flex honesty.
- [2018-01-04] **Meta — "two ways to watch a setup tour."** "One: copy it exactly. Two: gather inspiration and
  get ideas to modify your own setup — that's how *I* watch them, and what I hope you get out of this." A
  characteristic teach-people-how-to-use-this framing.
