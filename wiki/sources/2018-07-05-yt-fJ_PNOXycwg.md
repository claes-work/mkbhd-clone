# The Truth About Colored Smartphones! (@mkbhd, 2018-07-05)

## Summary
A teach-the-mechanism essay (framed via the red OnePlus 6) on why manufacturers stagger color releases —
decoding the **SKU-minimization** and **press-cycle** economics behind it, and reading Apple's color strategy
as a signaling tool. "I love colored phones, but I also kind of hate the way they exist."

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-07-05] **The tension — "I love them but I also kind of hate them."** Sets up the essay: colored phones are
  genuinely exciting, but the *way* they're released is a manipulation worth explaining.
- [2018-07-05] **Why not launch all colors at once — reason #1: SKU minimization.** "Companies want to sell **as
  few variations of a product as possible.** If they could sell one color and one storage tier, they would — same
  reason **car companies bundle everything into option packages** rather than offering 75 build combos." A clean
  operations-economics explanation.
- [2018-07-05] **Reason #2: the press cycle (the real trick).** "Every phone has a **press cycle** — a spike of
  attention at launch, then a long tail. Releasing a **new color of the same phone, months later, restarts that
  cycle** — a fresh wave of videos, articles, and sales. **OnePlus does it, Samsung does it, Apple does it** —
  almost everyone's guilty." Names the mechanism plainly (and implicates his own coverage).
- [2018-07-05] **Apple's color strategy, decoded — color as a status signal.** "Apple uses new colors specifically
  when a new model has the **same design / a spec bump**: **rose gold on the 6s, jet black on the 7, a red dot on
  the LTE Watch crown** — so people can tell who has the new one. Rumors say the same-design next iPhone gets
  **blue/orange/red** — same play." A sharp media-literacy read.
- [2018-07-05] **Still pumped anyway — the honest coda.** "I love it and I hate it. I love that colored phones are
  personal and everyone does them differently (favorites: **Apple Product RED, then the new OnePlus red, then —
  believe it or not — the BlackBerry Passport red**). I hate that it plays perfectly into the manufacturer's
  hands. But **I'm not going to stop covering them — I'm crossing my fingers for more.**" Self-aware without being
  cynical.
- [2018-07-05] **Product RED credit + the Moto Maker nostalgia.** Credit to Apple's **(PRODUCT)RED** for the AIDS-
  research donation ("more companies should do this"), even if the six-months-later red drop "is pretty hilarious."
  And a wish: "**I miss Moto Maker** — customizing every material, back, button color. That was **peak smartphone
  customization**, and that's where I want it to be."
