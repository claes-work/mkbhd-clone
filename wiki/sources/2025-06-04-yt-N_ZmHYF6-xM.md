# Nintendo Switch 2 Unboxing & First Gameplay! (@mkbhd, 2025-06-04)

## Summary
The retail-unit unboxing + first gameplay of the **$449 Nintendo Switch 2** (his box is the **$499 Mario Kart World
bundle**, plus the $55 camera, the $85 Pro Controller, and a physical Mario Kart World cart — "a pricey box"). In the
box: the console (a big 7.9" screen), the magnetic Joy-Cons + mouse-mode bumpers, an HDMI cable, the charger, the
Joy-Con grip, and the **4K dock with a fan**. Two first-impression takeaways stand out. First, comparing all three
consoles side by side (Switch 1, Switch OLED, Switch 2), **the Switch 2's LCD isn't OLED — but "most people just want a
bigger screen, and that's what the Switch 2 delivers"** (the same recurring pixel-peeper-vs-mainstream lesson from
phones/tablets), and **a Switch 2 OLED will almost certainly come in a couple years** (even more expensive). Second —
the surprise — **"the new Joy-Cons are almost as much of an upgrade as the new screen"**: they're bigger, every button
and stick is bigger, the magnetic attach is "so satisfying," the haptics are better, and it's genuinely better to game
on an individual Joy-Con. He confirms the details he flagged at the hands-on: a **pre-installed screen protector** (Nintendo
says don't remove it), the mouse-mode Joy-Cons work (scroll/select), a **day-one update**, 4K-auto to the TV, and — the
gripe he keeps hitting — **the paid Welcome Tour tutorial game "isn't free and isn't here."** Verdict for anyone
wondering "is it really that much of an upgrade?": **"it's gonna feel different because of the Joy-Cons just as much as
the screen."** (dbrand Killswitch travel-case sponsor.)

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2025-06-04] **Not OLED — but most people just want a bigger screen.** "'Side by side with the Switch 1 and Switch
  OLED: the Switch 2's screen is LCD, not OLED. But this has happened so many times in phones and tablets — we nerds
  pixel-peep for higher fidelity, but most people just want a bigger screen, and that's what the Switch 2 delivers.
  There's almost certainly a Switch 2 OLED in a couple years, even more expensive.'"
- [2025-06-04] **The new Joy-Cons are almost as big an upgrade as the screen.** "'The new Joy-Cons are almost as much of
  an upgrade as the new screen — bigger, every button and stick bigger, the magnetic attach is so satisfying, better
  haptics. It's genuinely better to game on an individual Joy-Con. If you're wondering "is it really that much of an
  upgrade?", it's gonna feel different because of the Joy-Cons just as much as the screen.'"
- [2025-06-04] **The details confirmed — and the paid tutorial game still isn't here.** "'Confirmed: a pre-installed
  screen protector (Nintendo says don't remove it), mouse-mode Joy-Cons that work (scroll/select), a day-one update,
  4K-auto to the TV. And the big talking point from last time — the game that acts as a tutorial to learn your Switch 2
  doesn't come with it, it's not free, and it's not here.'"
- [2025-06-04] **A pricey box.** "'It's a pricey box — the $499 Mario Kart World bundle, the $55 camera, the $85 Pro
  Controller, and a physical cart. So much plastic for this tiny cartridge. But the console is the obvious upgrade:
  bigger, better-looking, higher refresh, more storage.'"
