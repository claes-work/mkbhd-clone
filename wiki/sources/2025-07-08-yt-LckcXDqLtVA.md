# Rivian R1T Quad Gen 2 - The Perfect Upgrade?

## Summary

Marques — **a first-gen R1T Quad owner** — lives with the Gen-2 R1T Quad for a week (~1,000 miles)
to answer whether he'd upgrade. His verdict: **no, he's keeping his truck** — the suspension is
the biggest improvement, but everything else is only slightly different, and he prefers his own
setup (all-terrain tires for hopping over sand/grass). The video includes a drag-race segment with
co-host **Miles** (Miles's lines and the Lamborghini Urus SE he brings are quarantined). Biography-
rich.

## Key claims — SUBJECT-ATTRIBUTED (Marques)

- **[2025-07-08]** ⬥ BIOGRAPHY: **he owns and dailies a first-gen launch-edition R1T Quad** (~830hp,
  0–60 ~3.1s), and lived with the Gen-2 Quad for a week ("put like 1,000 miles on it, fully moved
  into it, dailied it"). His truck gets **285mi on all-terrain tires**; he **prefers all-terrains
  "because I like to go on sand or grass or hop over a mound"** (the Gen-2's sticky staggered street
  tires would defeat that). (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-07-08]** The Gen-2 differences: much better **suspension/ride** (the biggest change; "the
  R1T's suspension is somehow way better than the R1S"); new headlights, teal Quad accents, an
  electrochromic roof, a push-button door handle he calls **"a bit of a downgrade"**, and — "the
  first non-Tesla EV I've tested with a **NACS port**" (all Gen-2 Rivians will ship with it). The
  **Gen-2 speakers are slightly *worse*** than Gen-1 ("I'm willing to admit that"). (paraphrase)
- **[2025-07-08]** The Cybertruck framing (recurring): "the Cyberbeast is the top of Tesla's lineup
  and ridiculously quick, so when Rivian does their fastest, they just say 'beat that' — and
  accidentally made a ridiculously quick truck" (1,000hp, a ~10.5s quarter). (paraphrase)
- **[2025-07-08]** ⬥ Verdict: **"would I actually upgrade from my first-gen launch edition? No, I
  don't feel the need to. The suspension is by far the biggest difference; everything else is so
  similar. I'm going to keep my truck."** (For a new buyer, the Quad "makes all the others seem like
  a better deal, or get a Gen 1 at a discount.") (quote)
