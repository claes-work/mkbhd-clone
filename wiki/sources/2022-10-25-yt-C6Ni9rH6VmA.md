# The New iPad is Weird! (@mkbhd, 2022-10-25)

## Summary
A look at the confusing 10th-gen base iPad — "**I've never been so confused by so many choices in one new
product.**" The modernization is fine (no home button, flat sides, saturated colors, a larger 11" LCD, an A14).
The weirdness: it **finally goes USB-C — but supports only the Gen-1 Apple Pencil, which now needs a USB-C-to-
Lightning adapter to charge** ("I'm sure no one will ever lose this"). The likely reason is **the webcam finally
moved to the long side** ("low-key the best change" — you look at the camera on video calls) — which took the spot
the Gen-2 Pencil magnetically attaches to (so it's a compromise: the new Pencil OR the long-side webcam, not both).
It gets a nice new two-piece Magic Keyboard folio but **no Stage Manager.** The real problem is **the pricing:
$450 (up from the $329 9th-gen, which stays in the lineup)** — and **64 GB base "isn't real computer storage in
2022,"** so bumping to usable storage + the keyboard hits **$850 ("what")**. Verdict: **for a basic iPad, get the
$329 9th-gen; who is this one for?** — "sweet and simple, sufficient for some but pretty silly for most."

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-10-25] **USB-C — but the Gen-1 Pencil needs an adapter.** "'The modernization is great (no home button, flat
  sides, saturated colors, a bigger 11" LCD, an A14) — and it **finally goes USB-C** (flash drives, fast charging,
  external display). But the weirdness: **it supports the Gen-1 Apple Pencil, which is Lightning — so it needs a
  USB-C-to-Lightning adapter to charge** ("I'm sure no one will ever lose this," in separate wasteful packaging).
  Why not support the Gen-2 Pencil? Por qué no los dos?'"
- [2022-10-25] **The real reason — the webcam moved to the long side.** "'The real answer: **this is the first iPad
  to move the webcam to the long bezel — low-key the best change here** (on video calls you actually look at the
  camera, not off to the side). But that spot is where the Gen-2 Pencil magnetically attaches — **so it's a
  compromise: the new Pencil OR the long-side webcam, not both** (and the M2 iPad Pro kept the webcam on the short
  side, so we may never get an iPad Pro with the webcam in the right spot).'"
- [2022-10-25] **A nice keyboard — but no Stage Manager.** "'A new **two-piece Magic Keyboard folio** — a magnetic
  back-plate kickstand + a detachable keyboard with a function row (nice and modular, though less lap-able). But
  weirdly it's not coming to the iPad Pro, and **this iPad doesn't get Stage Manager** (which would've suited the
  USB-C external-display multitasking).'"
- [2022-10-25] **The pricing is the head-scratcher.** "'The biggest head-scratcher: **$450 (a big bump from the
  9th-gen, which stays at $329), plus a $250 keyboard.** And **64 GB base in 2022 isn't real computer storage** — so
  bump to usable storage + the keyboard and you're at **$850. What.** You could get an iPad Air, an iPad Pro, or a
  computer for that.'"
- [2022-10-25] **Who is this for?** "'Not a bad product — a perfectly good iPad. But for the "I just want a basic
  iPad" person (schools, businesses, a foam case for the kid), **the $329 9th-gen is $100+ cheaper and the better
  deal.** And the second you want it to be a real computer, the storage/keyboard math gets silly. **A strange
  combination slotted between sweet and simple — sufficient for some, but pretty silly for most.**'"
