# Asus Zenfone 9: The New Compact King! (@mkbhd, 2022-08-01)

## Summary
A review of the Asus Zenfone 9 — **"the most well-rounded compact phone in the world,"** and **his favorite release
of the year so far.** The frame: it's **contrarian / against-the-grain** in an era of same-y phones — refreshingly
compact (a 5.9" display), a grippy textured-plastic back, a headphone jack, and it's red. The clever bit: the
**power button does four things** (fingerprint reader + a customizable smart key + a swipe-down notification pull +
an edge app-launcher). Two standouts: **seriously incredible battery life** (the efficient 8+ Gen 1 in a small
phone) and **cameras that punch above their weight** — the same IMX766 sensor as the ROG/Nothing phones, but with
gimbal stabilization and Asus's software tuning that give it **"the closest look to a Pixel of any non-Pixel
camera."** At $699 it's like a **"Pixel 5 Pro two years later."** The nitpicks: no wireless charging, a display a
step below flagships, and a **color-fade concern** (the red is already fading at the edges in two weeks).

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-08-01] **Contrarian and compact — the four-in-one power button.** "'One of the most against-the-grain sets of
  features in a while: **refreshingly compact (a 5.9" display), a grippy soft-touch textured-plastic back, a
  headphone jack, and it's red.** And the **power button quadruples as a fingerprint reader, a customizable smart
  key (double/long-press to launch anything), a swipe-down notification pull (like the old Galaxy S10e), and an
  edge app-launcher** — Asus went the extra mile for one-handed reach.'"
- [2022-08-01] **Seriously incredible battery life.** "'I expected the usual small-flagship weak battery — instead
  **it has seriously incredible battery life; it sips power** (a great phone to put the efficient 8+ Gen 1 in). I
  had heavy days — high-brightness screen-on, navigation, gaming — and got 7+ hrs of screen-on time, ending with
  10–20% left.'"
- [2022-08-01] **Cameras that punch above their weight — the closest to a Pixel look.** "'The cameras are better than
  you'd expect — the **same IMX766 sensor as the ROG/Nothing phones (which were B/B+), but with a six-axis gimbal
  stabilization setup and Asus's software.** A typical Zenfone 9 shot is bold, contrasty, saturated — **it has the
  closest look to a Pixel of any non-Pixel camera** (sometimes over-HDR/harsh in high dynamic range). Out of all
  the phones with this sensor, this one looks the best — maybe Asus should have a meeting with the ROG team.'"
- [2022-08-01] **$699 = a "Pixel 5 Pro two years later" — my favorite release this year.** "'The price is really
  good — **$699; I'd have guessed higher.** It reminds me of the Pixel 5 (the best Pixel then, but a 7-series chip,
  90 Hz) — **this feels like a Pixel 5 Pro two years later: the highest-end chip, a nice screen, incredible
  battery, not a lot of downside. My favorite release this year.**'"
- [2022-08-01] **The nitpicks — no wireless charging, and the color-fade concern.** "'No phone is perfect: **30 W
  charging but no wireless charging** (most phones at this price at least have the option); the 120 Hz display is a
  step below flagships (800 nits). And the biggest: **the soft-touch red is fading and darkening at the edges/
  corners after only two weeks — I can't vouch for this plastic finish aging well** (a phone with a soft-touch back
  shouldn't need a case, though one's included). This'll be extremely underrated in the US (no carrier presence) —
  but if you want a great compact Android around $700, don't think twice.'"
