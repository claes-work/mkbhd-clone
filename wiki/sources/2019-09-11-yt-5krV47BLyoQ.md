# iPhone 11 Pro Impressions: What a Name! (@mkbhd, 2019-09-11)

## Summary
First look at the iPhone 11 Pro / Pro Max. The core beat is an extended **"pro is overused"** argument — a list of
what *would* have justified the name — alongside genuine enthusiasm for the matte finish, the display, the battery,
and the triple cameras.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-09-11] **THE "PRO" CRITIQUE — what would have earned the name.** "'It really just feels like they added the
  **pro name because that's what's hot right now.** The features that would've made me think "pro" **aren't there**:
  no high-refresh ProMotion display, no USB-C, no reverse wireless charging, no in-display fingerprint, no Apple
  Pencil support — **not even a little pro software** like they did with iPad OS on the iPad Pro (e.g. camera
  settings in the camera app instead of buried in Settings).'" His most detailed version of the peeve
  (cf. [[2019-06-27-yt-JdTBIHX-r0M]], [[2019-06-04-yt-QJxbqIo5pC0]]).
- [2019-09-11] **What IS new — the four things: matte, display, battery, cameras.** "Loves the **matte/frosted
  back** ('I absolutely love it' — though the camera square oddly goes glossy). Display: **not** 120 Hz, but
  brighter (**1,200-nit peak, 2M:1 contrast**). Battery: Apple claims **+4/+5 hrs** vs the XS, mostly from the
  **A13's efficiency** not a bigger cell. Finally an **18 W USB-C-to-Lightning fast charger in the box** ('it
  took a pro phone to get that')."
- [2019-09-11] **The camera is the whole pitch.** "**Triple cameras** (normal + 2x tele + 120° ultrawide), 4K60
  on all three, portrait mode from either the tele **or the primary** ('I'll choose the primary way more often
  now'). 'Apple is banking on you wanting the best photos/videos of any phone — **for video I think it will be;
  for photos it's debatable** (Pixel 4 and Huawei's new flagship are around the corner).'"
- [2019-09-11] **The honest bottom line.** "'It's not a bad phone at all — probably one of the **best-performing,
  best-battery, best-display** phones of the year, and I love that finish. It's just my expectation of the word
  **pro** that makes me feel I should expect more.' 'Even if it looks like a kitchen stove on the corner of your
  phone.'"

## Notable verbatim quotes

> "I love the new matte finish... I will take a matte option in any phone every time."
