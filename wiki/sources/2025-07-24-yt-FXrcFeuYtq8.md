# iOS 26 Hands-On: Liquid Glass!

## Summary

A hands-on with the iOS 26 public beta, structured as a top-five-features countdown
(his standard "in order of how big of a deal it is" format). The headline is **Liquid
Glass**, the transparency/refraction visual overhaul unifying Apple's platforms around
visionOS — polarizing enough that Apple is already walking back some transparency for
readability. Along the way Marques notes how many of the best new features are **Google
Pixel features Apple has now adopted** (call screening, Hold Assist, circle-to-search-
style visual intelligence), and repeats his standing wish that Apple would let him pick
non-default apps and chatbots (Gemini/Perplexity/Claude, Google Calendar).

## Key claims

- **[2025-07-24]** #1 is **Liquid Glass** — an OS-wide redesign in a transparency /
  refraction / reflection material, centered on visionOS's frosted-glass aesthetic.
  It's **polarizing**; Apple is **already walking it back** (Beta 3 adds background blur /
  less transparency for readability). (paraphrase)
- **[2025-07-24]** On the all-clear icon/widget theme: "you do have to admit it is a
  really, really good, detailed, well-executed **theme engine**" — but "that's all this
  really feels like it is... just a theme you might apply or might not." (quote)
- **[2025-07-24]** He personally prefers **clean, flat design** — "that's my bias" — so
  the bubbly glassy elements (volume slider, quick settings) aren't for him, though the
  **lock screen is his favorite part** (the dynamic extended clock that fades behind
  subjects). (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-07-24]** #2 CarPlay: a **massive update** — phone calls no longer take the
  whole screen, Tapback reactions to texts, a widgets column, and Live Activities.
  "It's about damn time." (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-07-24]** #3 Phone app: the iPhone **inherits two of the best Pixel features** —
  **Call Screening** and **Hold Assist** (Pixel's Hold For Me) — plus a unified layout.
  "Welcome to the party, iPhone friends." (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-07-24]** #4 Visual Intelligence now works on **on-screen** content via the
  screenshot UI (Ask / Image Search / circle-to-search) — "basically the same thing as
  Android['s circle to search]," which he calls "kind of like a superpower." Apple
  Intelligence has been "mostly miss" (Genmoji, Image Playground), but this has a **high
  ceiling**. (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-07-24]** Recurring wish: let him change defaults — add to **Google Calendar**,
  make the assistant **Gemini / Perplexity / Claude** if he wants. "I know none of this
  stuff is ever actually gonna happen, but I can dream." (quote)
- **[2025-07-24]** #5 the redesigned Camera app (simplified; pick a format setting
  directly instead of tap-cycling — "so much better for video"); plus spatial-scene 3D
  photos he says are clean enough that he "doesn't put it in the gimmick bucket anymore."
  (paraphrase + quote)
