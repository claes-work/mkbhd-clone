# Tesla Roadster - Living with the Original!

## Summary

Marques lives for a few days with **the original 2008 Tesla Roadster** — "the car that started it
all" — **borrowed from a viewer (Justin)** whose daily driver it still is at 97,000 miles. Less a
review than a fascinated look at "what made electric cars interesting over gas": a Lotus-Elise-based
EV that's **the most *analog* electric car he's ever sat in** (key-turn ignition, no power steering,
tiny screen) yet strikingly **ahead of its time**. Fully Marques-narrated. (This is the Roadster he
references in the later Sacrilege-EV video.)

## Key claims (biography marked ⬥)

- **[2022-10-30]** ⬥ BIOGRAPHY: **he borrowed the 2008 Roadster from a viewer — "shout out to Justin,
  who emailed me and daily-drives this car (97,000 miles, still works like normal)."** "I've never
  reviewed a car from 2008 before, so this is more my first impressions of something fascinating." (quote)
- **[2022-10-30]** The origin story: "Tesla took a **Lotus Elise** (an existing gas car), took a bunch
  out, put a bunch in — and started selling electric cars that were actually *cool* instead of the
  boring ones before it. This shares **less than 10% of its parts** with the Lotus." (paraphrase)
- **[2022-10-30]** ⭐ Ahead of its time: "A car this small couldn't have the skateboard battery
  architecture, so the batteries sit behind the driver. Battery upgrades over the years took owners to
  ~350 mi (this one, at 97k mi, still gets ~250). **The best software of any EV of its time** — a tiny
  screen with speed, battery %, a 5/15/30-mi graph, live torque/hp/G's. **The lack of huge screens
  actually makes it feel timeless.** Already had regen good enough for one-pedal driving + brake lights
  on lift-off." (quote)
- **[2022-10-30]** ⚠️ But also the **most analog EV**: "you turn a physical key in the ignition; no
  power steering (heavy at low speed); stiff, raw — you feel every bump (I bet you could run over a dime
  and tell heads from tails). A fun piece for a while, then it gets harsh." **It's tiny** ("the lowest
  car I've ever driven — I barely fit; a kid ran up asking 'how does anybody fit in that thing?'"). (quote)
- **[2022-10-30]** ⭐ The point: "A peek behind the curtain at what made EVs compelling — they only sold
  ~2–3,000, but many are still on the road and some have gone *up* in value. It says something about
  Tesla, and about the car. I appreciate the look into the past to appreciate the present." (quote)
