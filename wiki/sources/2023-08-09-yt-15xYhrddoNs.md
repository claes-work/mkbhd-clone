# The Electric Cadillac Escalade IQ: 450 Mile Range!

## Summary

Marques covers **GM's unveiling of the Escalade IQ** (a 2025 model — "a visual representation of a
bunch of promises") and reads it straight through his doctrine: **the Escalade is popular to *ride*
in (Uber Black, limos), so it's a perfect match to go electric** — big, luxurious, smooth, quiet,
expensive either way. A jumbo Lyriq with a 200+ kWh battery, **450-mile targeted range**, four-wheel
steering + crab walk, and a polarized passenger screen. "GM's kind of cooking right now." Fully
Marques-narrated.

## Key claims

- **[2023-08-09]** ⭐ Doctrine: "You can't go 60 seconds in NYC without seeing an Escalade — **not
  popular to drive, popular to *ride in* (Uber Black, limos)**. I've been saying they should make an
  electric one because it matches too well: what do you want from an Escalade AND from a new electric
  SUV? Big, luxurious, smooth, quiet, expensive. It just matches too well." (quote)
- **[2023-08-09]** Design: **"a jumbo-size Lyriq"** ("someone scaled up a Lyriq 150% and added Escalade
  touches") — the Lyriq he'd called underrated. (quote)
- **[2023-08-09]** Specs: dual-motor AWD, a **200+ kWh battery**, **450-mile targeted range** even on
  24" wheels, 750 hp in Max Velocity mode, 100 mi in 10 min charging, bidirectional; **four-wheel steer
  (10°) + a Hummer-style "crab walk" (Arrival mode)**; a real 12.2-cu-ft frunk; adaptive air suspension;
  a **55" continuous display** (a 35" driver + a **polarized 12" passenger screen the driver can't see**
  — "never seen that before"); up to 40 AKG speakers; auto-opening doors ("if that isn't an Uber
  feature…"). $130k start. (paraphrase)
- **[2023-08-09]** ⭐ Verdict: "A whole price bracket above the R1S (which I called the best SUV you can
  buy). I can see this quickly becoming well-liked among people who already drive Escalades — a very
  popular NYC limo. **GM's kind of cooking right now — well played, the Lyriq and now this.**" (quote)
