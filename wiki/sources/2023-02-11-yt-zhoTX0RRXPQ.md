# The Galaxy S23 Ultra is Better Than You Think! (@mkbhd, 2023-02-11)

## Summary
The full review, arguing the S23 Ultra is **too easily written off as incremental** — aesthetically it's basically
the S22 Ultra, but the two things that changed are **arguably the two most important in a phone: the chip and the
cameras.** The chip (**Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 for Galaxy**) brings modest benchmark gains but two real wins:
**efficiency → "incredible" battery life** ("a battery monster" — two packed days at 1440p... actually 1080p, ~8.5 hr
screen-on), and **"bye-bye Exynos"** (every S23 Ultra ships Qualcomm worldwide, so everyone gets the gains). The
camera story is the **200 MP main** — but the point isn't resolution (it bins 16→1 for a 12.5 MP shot); it's
**doubled optical image stabilization (correcting ~3° vs 1.5°)**, which lets the shutter stay open longer for
sharper, brighter photos, plus the best super-steady video mode he's seen and usable 8K/30 (a sample shot on
@AutoFocus). A recurring lesson: **the new 12 MP selfie beats the old 40 MP one — "rock-solid proof that megapixels
don't matter."** His analogy: **"the Porsche 911 of smartphones"** — boring/understated, but it nails every mark
across the board; other phones will one-up it in single categories, but none match the overall package. Still gripes
about **shutter lag** (distinct from slow shutter speed).

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2023-02-11] **Easy to write off — but the two things that matter changed.** "'It was the easiest thing to write it
  off as incremental — aesthetically it's basically the same phone. But there are two real upgrades, and they're two
  of arguably the most important things you can change: the chip that runs it all and the cameras.'"
- [2023-02-11] **A battery monster + bye-bye Exynos.** "'The 8 Gen 2 for Galaxy is more efficient, so battery life is
  incredible — a battery monster; two packed days of high-brightness use with ~8.5 hours of screen-on. And it's
  bye-bye Exynos: every S23 Ultra ships with the Qualcomm chip worldwide, so everyone gets the efficiency and
  performance gains. Spend $1,200 — just switch it to 1440p and treat yourself; it lasts forever.'"
- [2023-02-11] **200 MP is really about stabilization.** "'We keep saying cameras are mostly software, and for the
  look that's still true — but the improvement to stabilization is what matters here. The 200 MP sensor bins 16
  pixels into one for a 12.5 MP shot, and the OIS doubled (correcting ~3° vs 1.5°), so the shutter can stay open
  longer to collect more light and still be sharp. Best super-steady video mode I've seen, and usable 8K/30.'"
- [2023-02-11] **Megapixels don't matter — the selfie proof.** "'The new 12 MP selfie is better than the old 40 MP
  one. It's just better — rock-solid proof that megapixels don't matter. Sharper, more detailed, nice color, with
  better software processing.'"
- [2023-02-11] **The Porsche 911 of smartphones.** "'My analogy is it feels like the Porsche 911 of smartphones —
  if you're going to charge $1,200, it better set high marks all the way across the board, and it does; it's pretty
  underrated, kind of boring, but it does. Other phones will one-up it in single categories (120× zoom, faster
  charging), but for nailing every mark, this does it. Understated is the better word than boring.'"
- [2023-02-11] **The one gripe — shutter lag.** "'The one thing I'll still complain about is shutter lag (different
  from slow shutter speed) — the gap between hitting the button and capturing, more pronounced on the 50/200 MP
  modes; you can capture a crisp photo of the wrong moment.'"
