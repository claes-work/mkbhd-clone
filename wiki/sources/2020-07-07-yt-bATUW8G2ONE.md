# iPhone 12: What to Expect! (@mkbhd, 2020-07-07)

## Summary
A pre-release "what I'm hoping for" on the iPhone 12, using dummy models. The blanket theme: **iPad Pro** — flat
squared sides, and a wishlist topped by a 120 Hz display. Notable for his **flat-sides-feel-better-naked** point
and continued high-refresh advocacy.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-07-07] **The iPad-Pro theme — flat sides feel better (especially caseless).** "'Squared-off **flat sides**
  straight from the iPad Pro — it stands on its own. To the **small minority of us who carry a phone naked**: the
  rounded sides were less satisfying; **flat sides feel more secure and confidence-inspiring** — it genuinely
  feels better, not just looks better.'" His stated caseless-user preference.
- [2020-07-07] **The three sizes — the small one matters.** "'Three sizes (~5.4" / 6.2" / 6.7"), and this time the
  **non-Pro is the smallest.** 5.4" is surprisingly compact (last Samsung flagship that size was the S7). **My
  "best small phone" award always goes to the smallest iPhone** because so few make small flagships — likely again
  this year. The 6.7" would be the biggest iPhone (and biggest battery) ever — probably the one I end up with.'"
  Biography: he's been using the smaller iPhone lately.
- [2020-07-07] **THE WISHLIST — 120 Hz #1, USB-C, smaller notch, matte black.** "'**#1: a 120 Hz ProMotion
  display** (like the iPad Pro and half the phones this year) — rumored on at least the Pro models. Also **USB-C**
  ("Apple's most stubborn about Lightning; USB-C is just better and more universal"), a **smaller notch**, **better
  Face ID angles** (still have to lean at 45°), and — wishlist — a **matte black** non-glossy finish.'" His
  recurring high-refresh + USB-C + matte-black wishes.
- [2020-07-07] **The design lineage + the 4K240 flex.** "'Reminds me not of the beloved iPhone 5s but the **iPhone
  4** — a straight glass sandwich (maybe they'll add the chamfer back for the 13). Code suggests **4K240fps video —
  "my RED can barely do that" — Apple flexing their image pipeline.** A mystery button and LiDAR are unknowns.'"
- [2020-07-07] **The verdict — potentially the biggest iPhone change in a while.** "'This hardware design + iOS 14
  is **potentially one of the biggest changes to the iPhone in a long time** — a lot to look forward to.'"

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "To the small minority of us who carry a phone naked... the flat sides just feel more secure and more
> confidence-inspiring — it feels better to have flat sides."
> "Number one item on my wishlist for the new iPhones is a ProMotion 120 hertz display."
