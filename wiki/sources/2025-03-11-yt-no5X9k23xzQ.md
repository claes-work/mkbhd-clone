# Dodge Charger Daytona EV is a Fratzonic Flop

## Summary

Marques tests the electric **Dodge Charger Daytona** and applies his recurring doctrine — **a muscle
car is "perfect to go electric" because the electric version should be the best version ever**
(bigger, more power, swift in a straight line). But the execution disappoints: the headline
**Fratzonic chambered-exhaust fake-engine noise** is a one-note gimmick you turn off, it's not a
ground-up EV (huge center console, comically small frunk), the range is weak (~200mi real), and
straight-line rivals cost far less. Its real strength turns out to be a comfortable cruiser. Fully
Marques-narrated.

## Key claims

- **[2025-03-11]** ⭐ Doctrine restated: **"The types of cars that are best to go electric are the ones
  where it would be the best version ever — bigger, more power, swifter than ever. So how about a muscle
  car? The Dodge Charger Daytona is perfect to go electric."** But "the execution… didn't actually end
  up being that good of a car." (quote)
- **[2025-03-11]** ⚠️ The **Fratzonic exhaust**: "the world's loudest electric car," 126 dB (same as
  the gas Charger) via a rear speaker system that imitates a gas engine. His verdict: **"it's an
  acquired taste… it doesn't sound as good as the gas car, so if you just want the noise you'll just
  get the gas car"**; it drones "like you're riding around in second gear all the time"; "fun at first,
  but you pretty quickly get over it and just turn it off." (quote)
- **[2025-03-11]** ⚠️ Not a ground-up EV: it shares a body with the gas Charger → a huge unnecessary
  center console and a **"comically small" frunk**; "a ground-up EV wouldn't have this." (quote)
- **[2025-03-11]** Value critique: 670hp, 0–60 in 3.3s (fastest Charger), but at **$85–86k**, "a Model 3
  Performance kind of smokes this, even a Kia EV6 GT beats it, a Model Y Performance is on par — none of
  those cost $86k; for 80 grand you could get a used Plaid." (quote)
- **[2025-03-11]** ⚠️ Range: 100kWh but only ~241mi rated, ~183kW charging; woke up to "201 miles" at
  100%, "185 in sport mode." "Not for road trips." Can't even do the Charger's signature standing
  burnout (5,900lb, only 335 lb-ft at the rear axle). (paraphrase)
- **[2025-03-11]** The twist verdict: **"what it ended up being good at — I commuted in it every day —
  is a comfortable cruiser."** Plush Alcantara/leather interior, roomy, soft forgiving suspension, big
  visibility, hatchback space. ⚠️ "The speakers are not that good." "It was supposed to be fun… but you
  turn the sound off and it's actually kind of just a boat." (quote)
