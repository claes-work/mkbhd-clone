# Porsche Taycan Turbo: I Almost Switched! [Auto Focus Ep 6] (@mkbhd, 2020-09-22)

## Summary
Auto Focus Ep. 6: two weeks with the $100k+ Porsche Taycan Turbo, framed against Tesla. A landmark EV episode —
he becomes **"a believer."** The durable ideas: **interior quality vs. interior layout** are two different things
(Porsche wins quality, Tesla still wins layout); **Tesla's efficiency and Supercharger network are still real
moats**; and Porsche is special because they **put effort into the EV driving experience instead of coughing out
an electric version of an existing car** — "there's more than one right answer, kind of like folding phones."

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-09-22] **The framing — Tesla QC pushes people to the Taycan.** "'Two types of people see a red Tesla:
  "wow, a red Tesla" or **"what an uneven paint job, panel-gapped, swirly QC nightmare."** If you're the second
  person, **maybe the Taycan is for you.** Specs: 670 hp, 250 mi range, 0–60 in 3 s — but there's a lot more to
  a car than the numbers.'"
- [2020-09-22] **INTERIOR QUALITY vs LAYOUT — a self-correction.** "'I used to say Tesla interiors are fine,
  people exaggerate — but sitting in this, **there's a difference between interior quality and interior layout.**
  I **still prefer the Tesla/Mach-E layout, but the quality of materials here is amazing.** Instead of just
  saying it, let me show you — screens and (nice metal) buttons everywhere, haptic touchscreen, adjustable
  seats. I hope Tesla gets to this level someday.'" A promotable distinction + a documented mind-change.
- [2020-09-22] **TESLA'S EFFICIENCY MOAT — watt-hours per mile.** "'Efficiency is a Tesla specialty (millions
  of miles of powertrain R&D) — the Model 3 averages ~350 Wh/mi. **The Taycan is significantly less efficient —
  I can't get it under 400, typically 400–550+** — so the rated 250 mi feels more like 210–220 in sportier
  modes.'" A concrete, dated efficiency comparison.
- [2020-09-22] **THE SUPERCHARGER MOAT — and the open question.** "'The Taycan uses Electrify America — you can
  charge just as fast (0–100 in ~25 min), but **there just aren't nearly as many high-powered chargers; compare
  the two maps.** The extra chargers + longer range make Tesla road-trips more convenient. **I wonder why more
  new EVs don't use Tesla's network** — Tesla said they'd allow it, people have even hacked non-Teslas onto it —
  **I'd drive the Taycan confidently every day if I could just pull up to a Supercharger.**'"
- [2020-09-22] **"The best-driving electric car out right now, period."** "'I said it on Twitter and I meant it:
  **the Taycan is the best-driving EV out right now.** It weighs over 5,000 lbs — more than an F-150 — yet feels
  more **direct, flat and planted** than anything in its class, more than a Model 3 or S. Four modes; the
  **nice metal drive-mode knob** matters ("if it was a dumb plastic knob I'd ask why").'"
- [2020-09-22] **WHY IT'S SPECIAL — effort into the EV experience; "more than one right answer."** "'What makes
  it special isn't just that they made an EV — it's that **they actually put effort into thinking about the EV
  driving experience and didn't just cough out an electric version of another car they make.** There are
  different ways to attack a great electric experience — **kind of like folding phones, there's more than one
  right answer in these first generations.**'" A promotable cross-domain framework.
- [2020-09-22] **The closing question.** "'Taycan or an equivalent fully-loaded Tesla in your driveway right
  now — which? **For me: the Taycan WITH Supercharging; without it, I'd still take the Tesla.**'"

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "There's a difference between interior quality and interior layout. I still prefer the layout of a Tesla...
> but there is no doubt that the quality of the interior is amazing."
> "The Porsche Taycan is the best-driving electric car out right now, period."
> "I just really like that they actually put some effort into thinking about the electric car driving
> experience — they didn't just cough out an electric version of another one of the cars they make."
