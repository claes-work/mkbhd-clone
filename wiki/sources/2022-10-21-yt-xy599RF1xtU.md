# The GMC Sierra Electric Pickup Truck: 400 Miles?

## Summary

A **first look** at the 2024 **GMC Sierra EV Denali Edition 1** (a year+ out — "promises, take the
specs with a grain of salt"). Marques frames it as GM's second EV truck (after the Hummer EV) that
**"feels like they actually learned something from the Hummer."** Highlights: a **MultiPro midgate**
enabling ~11 ft of flat storage, a big powered frunk, **vehicle-to-home power**, and promised 400 mi /
755 hp. His verdict: "the best GMC Sierra they've ever made." Fully Marques-narrated.

## Key claims

- **[2022-10-21]** Frame: a 2024 model (a year+ away) — "the specs are promises we're hoping they hit."
  GM is already shipping the Hummer EV; this lands later than the F-150 Lightning but maybe before the
  Cybertruck. "Shades of baby Hummer." (paraphrase)
- **[2022-10-21]** ⭐ The clever storage: **the MultiPro midgate** (between cab and bed) opens to pass
  through to folded rear seats → combined with the tailgate down, **~11 ft of flat storage**; plus a
  **large powered front trunk** with a drain + 110V outlet. "They're optimizing the space (front seats
  forward, rear seats back — no engine)." (paraphrase)
- **[2022-10-21]** Promised specs: dual-motor, **400 mi range (on 24" wheels — smaller = more), 350 kW
  charging, 755 hp, 9,500 lb towing, 4.5s 0–60**, ~212 kWh battery (Hummer-size). ⭐ **Vehicle-to-home:
  "it supports the inverter to power your house — underrated; more trucks and EVs in general should have
  this."** Up to 10.2 kW of off-board power. (quote)
- **[2022-10-21]** Interior/pricing: Denali Edition 1 = ~$107k (the top spec first; a ~$50k Elevation
  spec comes in 2025). An 11" driver screen + a full-color HUD + a 17" vertical touchscreen with
  physical HVAC switches (like the Hummer). "It feels well thought out — like they learned from the
  Hummer being crazy." Crab-walk mode too ("not just a Hummer thing"). (paraphrase)
- **[2022-10-21]** ⭐ Verdict: **"The success here is that this Sierra electric is the best GMC Sierra
  they've ever made — the fastest, one of the most powerful, the quietest, the torquiest and most fun to
  drive, the first with a panoramic sunroof and EV-only features like a frunk. A win just for being a
  good option."** (quote)
