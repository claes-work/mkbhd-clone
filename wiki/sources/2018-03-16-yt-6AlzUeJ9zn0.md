# Top 5 Android Pie Features! (@mkbhd, 2018-03-16)

## Summary
A walkthrough of the **Android P (Pie) Developer Preview** on the Pixel 2 — an incremental "refinement era"
update. Five main changes plus a set of privacy/quality touches. Light on strong opinion; useful mostly as a
dated snapshot of his Android read and a couple of small taste-tells.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-03-16] **The "refinement era" framing.** "New letter versions aren't massive drastic changes anymore —
  which makes sense when they've refined Android for so long. They're **tweaking things at this point.**" His
  standard mature-platform read.
- [2018-03-16] **The five features.** (1) **Visual refresh** — clock moved left, adaptive light/dark, and a theme
  of **rounded corners everywhere** (mixed feelings: "could look cleaner with rectangles"); (2) **Quick settings**
  redesign (circles, blue/white, vertical scroll) — but he **dislikes losing long-press-to-expand** toggles;
  (3) **New volume controls — his favorite change**: a side vertical bar that matches the physical buttons, and
  media volume by default; (4) **Settings app** with colorful round icons ("not too cartoonish"); (5) **Notch
  support** — themes that simulate small/medium/"unibrow" notches so devs can test — "more an **indication of our
  future** than a feature," welcome-to-2018.
- [2018-03-16] **The privacy/quality touches he flags.** **Background apps can no longer use the mic/camera/sensors**
  (OS-wide — "we always wanted that"); **lockdown mode** (disables biometrics until PIN); auto-battery-saver at any
  5–70% threshold; screenshot markup; text-selection magnifier. He consistently calls out the privacy items as the
  meaningful ones.
- [2018-03-16] **The running "P = ?" bit.** Guesses **"Peppermint"** from the swirls in the Easter egg (it later
  shipped as **Pie** — a light, dated miss, characteristic of his playful update videos). Also credits **Android
  Police** for their deep changelog digs — a habit of pointing viewers to good sources.

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "New letter versions of Android aren't those massive drastic changes anymore — they're kind of tweaking things
> at this point."
> "The new volume controls are definitely my favorite change so far."
> "Background apps can't use the microphone or camera anymore — we'd always wanted that to be a thing we could
> control; now it's OS-wide."
