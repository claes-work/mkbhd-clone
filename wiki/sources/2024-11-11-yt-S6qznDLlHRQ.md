# How My Video Gear is Changing! (@mkbhd, 2024-11-11)

## Summary
A DJI-sponsored piece (the Action 5 Pro) that's really a **statement about his filmmaking philosophy going into 2025**.
The counterintuitive premise: after **15+ years** of making videos, **"people are more drawn to lower-production-value
videos — they want to see real life, what's happening in front of you with whatever camera you have."** He notes that
as he **"graduated" from his first camera → DSLR → cinema camera, the cameras got harder and harder to use** — and he
wants to **get back to shooting more spontaneously, in more places and experiences, not just the talking-head stuff
we've gotten really good at.** The **apex tool for that is the action camera** (not the smartphone) — small, rugged,
waterproof, shoots everything all the time, and crucially **you're not risking your actual phone** in precarious
angles. He uses the Action 5 Pro to walk through what enables it (dual bright OLED screens, **47 GB internal storage** —
"petition for all cameras to have internal recording"; a 1/1.3" sensor, 4K120, 10-bit D-Log M, 155° FOV, magnetic
quick-release, webcam mode). The through-line is the famous line **"the best camera is the one you have with you"** —
he keeps recalling review moments where something cool happened on a device and he could only *tell* you after the fact
instead of *showing* you, because the Red/cinema camera wasn't with him. So the goal going forward: **have a camera on
him more often** to capture those moments. He's explicit it's **not** abandoning the high-end — "there's a reason we
shoot so much with the Reds and in the studio; that always has its place" — it's about **capturing more.** (Callback:
the 2014 dog-cam POV.)

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2024-11-11] **Lower production value draws people — they want real life.** "'After 15+ years of making videos,
  something counterintuitive happens on YouTube: people are more drawn to almost lower-production-value videos. Big
  lights and cameras are fun, but people just want to see real life — what's happening in front of you with whatever
  camera you have with you.'"
- [2024-11-11] **Cameras got harder to use as I graduated — I want to shoot more spontaneously.** "'I graduated from my
  first camera to a DSLR to a cinema camera, and these cameras actually get harder and harder to use. I want to get
  back to shooting more spontaneously, more places, more experiences — not just the talking-head stuff we've gotten
  really good at.'"
- [2024-11-11] **The action camera is the apex tool for that.** "'You might've thought I'd say smartphones, but the apex
  is an action camera — even smaller, more rugged, shoots everything all the time, and I'm not risking my actual phone
  in precarious angles where it could fall or take a beating. Petition for all cameras to have internal recording, by
  the way — it's saved me when I forgot a card.'"
- [2024-11-11] **The best camera is the one you have with you.** "'The famous quote — "the best camera is the one you
  have with you" — I've been leaning into that, because of how often I don't have the cinema/Red camera with me. So
  many times in a review something cool happens on a device and I can only tell you about it after the fact instead of
  pointing a camera at it and showing you. I want to have the camera with me more often so I can capture it and put it
  in the video.'"
- [2024-11-11] **Not abandoning the high-end — just capturing more.** "'What I'm not saying is that I'm done with the
  super-high-end fun cameras — I still love them, there's a reason we shoot so much with the Reds and in the studio,
  and that always has its place. The goal is just to have a camera with me more often so I don't miss the moment.'"
