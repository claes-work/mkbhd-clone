# iPhone XR Impressions: All of the Colors! (@mkbhd, 2018-09-13)

## Summary
First look at the iPhone XR — with a "sneaky feeling this is the way to go for a lot of people." Positions it as
"the new iPhone 8," walks the specific trade-downs from the XS, and runs a fun **color mini-review** format
(scoring all six colors), landing on **Product RED, day one**.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-09-13] **"The real hit" — the new iPhone 8.** "Same A12, RAM, Face ID, notch, iOS 12, speakers as the XS —
  I have a **sneaky feeling this is the way to go for a lot of people.** Think of it as **the new iPhone 8: the
  ideal upgrade for someone on an iPhone 8 or older — not an X.**" $749. A clear positioning call.
- [2018-09-13] **The trade-downs, named honestly.** **LCD "Liquid Retina"** (not OLED) at a **lower 828p res**
  ("you can't even watch 1080p HD on it — keep that in mind") with **slightly thicker bezels/a chin** (you can't
  wrap an LCD to the edge like an OLED); **no 3D Touch** ("I barely use it — won't miss it; they built long-press
  around it"); **single camera** (but same primary sensor + software portrait mode — "you're really not missing
  much except the telephoto"); IP67 not IP68.
- [2018-09-13] **The color mini-reviews (a fun format).** Scores each: **coral 6/10**, **white 6.5** ("the clean
  look"), **blue 8** ("iPhone 5c vibes, bonus points if you wear blue jeans"), **black 8** ("soft spot for matte
  black — stealthy, dark-mode, Batman art"), **yellow 4** ("taxi vibe… the worst iPhone 5c"), and **Product RED
  9.5** — "a deep rich Ferrari red, **and we're getting it day one instead of waiting six months**, with a black
  front; **easily my favorite.**" A playful, opinionated appearance segment.
- [2018-09-13] **"A funky Frankenstein iPhone."** "Some things from the iPhone 8, some from the X — it technically
  qualifies as a new design, and a wallpaper matching the aluminum frame color makes a real difference." Nails the
  in-between identity.

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "I have a sneaky feeling this is going to be the way to go for a lot of people this year."
> "You can look at this as the new iPhone 8 — the ideal upgrade for someone on an iPhone 8 or older, not an X."
> "Product RED — and we're actually getting it on day one instead of waiting six months. This one's a winner —
> easily my favorite. 9.5 out of 10."
