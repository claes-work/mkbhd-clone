# A Second Look at the Tesla Cybertruck!

## Summary

At LA's Petersen Museum, Marques gets an up-close **second look at the 2019 Cybertruck prototype**
(the reveal-stage car). Beyond details (a likely charge-port panel, aero-cap wheels, genuinely sharp
corners, a huge suspension-clearance range), his real takeaway is **how massive it is** — big enough
that a chunk of the hundreds-of-thousands of pre-orderers "aren't ready for a truck this big." And,
key: since the delayed reveal, **real EV-truck competition has arrived**, so **the Cybertruck's edge
now has to be price + range.** Fully Marques-narrated.

## Key claims

- **[2022-11-17]** It's the 2019 prototype ("don't put too much stock in small details — they'll change;
  we've seen test trucks with side mirrors and a giant single wiper"). Aero-cap wheels, **no curves at
  all — hard lines and genuinely sharp corners**, "the easiest vehicle in the world to wrap." The
  loading-mode suspension shows a huge clearance range. (paraphrase)
- **[2022-11-17]** ⭐ Massiveness: "**It's huge** — tall and wide. Sitting at the front bumper like I do
  with other cars, a driver couldn't see me at all." **"A guaranteed, non-zero percentage of the
  hundreds of thousands of pre-orders are people not ready for a truck this big — it won't fit in their
  driveway or garage."** He wouldn't be shocked if they shrink it slightly. (quote)
- **[2022-11-17]** ⭐ The strategic point: **"They revealed this years ago and it still hasn't shipped —
  so the landscape is meaningfully different now: there's real competition (F-150 Lightning, Rivian R1T,
  Hummer EV). So what's the Cybertruck's advantage going to be? Besides the Tesla hype and the unique
  look, it basically has to be price and range — IF they actually deliver ~$50–60k with the Supercharger
  network and 350 mi (a huge if given the market since then)."** (quote)
- **[2022-11-17]** Also spotted at the museum: a first look at the **Optimus robot** ("looks like a robot
  you'd put in a museum that would never walk — but apparently they want it in people's houses") and the
  Roadster/Semi. (paraphrase)

## Notable verbatim quotes
