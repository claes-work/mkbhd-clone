# The Ultimate High Tech Luxury Electric Car! (BMW i7)

## Summary

Marques calls the **BMW i7** **"probably the most high-tech, impressive luxury electric car I've ever
seen — I'd take this over the Lucid Air, the Maybach, and the Bentley Flying Spur, and I mean that."**
It's the full articulation of his **perfect-to-go-electric doctrine** for luxury sedans (explicitly
tying back to the Bentley video), plus a tech tour de force — auto-opening doors, whisper-quiet cabin,
an **executive back seat with a 30" 8K "theater" screen** and a lounge mode. Fully Marques-narrated.
(This i7 is literally what underpins the Rolls-Royce Spectre.)

## Key claims

- **[2023-05-23]** ⭐ Verdict up front: **"This is probably the most high-tech, impressive luxury EV I've
  ever seen. The Lucid Air would be close, the Maybach, the Bentley Flying Spur — I'd take this over all
  of them. And that says a lot."** ~530 hp, 4.5s 0–60, ~270–280 mi real (300–320 claimed), ~$120k start
  ("half the price of the Maybach, undercutting the Lucid/S-Class/Model S"). (quote)
- **[2023-05-23]** ⭐ The doctrine (fully stated): **"This all comes down to the fact that this is still
  one of the best types of vehicles to go electric — what I was saying in the Bentley video. A large
  luxurious four-door sedan: people want it comfortable, spacious, quiet, smooth; they already know it's
  heavy and expensive. That's perfect to go electric — you end up with the quietest, smoothest, best
  version of everything."** (quote)
- **[2023-05-23]** The tech: **doors that auto-open (sensing clearance) and close on brake**; framed
  windows + huge sound deadening → **"a whisper-quiet conversation at 50 mph — you don't even hear
  potholes"**; a surprisingly-good voice assistant ("Hey BMW…" — mics per seat know which passenger is
  talking); Hans-Zimmer acceleration sounds; ⚠️ the crystal iDrive controller / capacitive buttons
  ("prettier than functional — I accidentally opened the door"), and **mediocre B&W speakers** ("the
  most underwhelming luxury in the car"). (quote)
- **[2023-05-23]** The **executive back seat**: fully-reclining jet seats, a per-seat screen, and
  **theater mode** — shades rise and a **30" 8K touchscreen** drops down (an Amazon-Fire-Stick UI, the
  car has its own SIM); **lounge mode** reclines you into a "passenger princess" position. ⚠️ "The only
  challenge is the UI — there's so much tech, but the UI is a little tricky." (paraphrase)
- **[2023-05-23]** ⭐ "This might be the best luxury sedan on the planet right now." He'd rather be
  driven in it than drive it. (quote)
