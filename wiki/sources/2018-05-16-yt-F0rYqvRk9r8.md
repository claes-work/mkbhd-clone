# OnePlus 6 Impressions! (@mkbhd, 2018-05-16)

## Summary
First look at the OnePlus 6 ($600-ish, SD845) — a "classic OnePlus" value play. Notable mostly for his read on
OxygenOS as the rare near-stock skin done right, the OnePlus flagship-killer arc, and the by-now-familiar 2018
notch-and-glass design convergence.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-05-16] **"Classic OnePlus" — big specs, lower-than-expected price.** SD845, 8 GB RAM, 6.3" AMOLED, dual
  cameras, **under $600** — "they're sticking to their guns: impressive specs for a lower-than-expected price."
  Situates it in the OnePlus One "flagship killer / never settle" lineage.
- [2018-05-16] **The 2018 design convergence — and he's made peace with the notch.** "Dual cameras, glass back,
  a notch — just like every phone the last six months and the next six. **Honestly it looks pretty good** — maybe
  I've just gotten used to notches, or this one's smaller." Notes the "**frosted black glass**" matte-ish option
  but "**I'm still putting a dbrand skin on it**" (grip + fingerprints). No wireless charging is the turn-off.
- [2018-05-16] **OxygenOS — the near-stock skin done right (his favorite approach).** "OxygenOS is great — you can
  barely call it a skin. It's **near-stock**, **one of the first with updates**, with a few smart tweaks (the
  Shelf, a notch-hide toggle, new gestures — though the gestures aren't as smooth as the iPhone's yet)." A clear
  statement of his near-stock-Android preference; keeps the **alert slider** and **headphone jack**.
- [2018-05-16] **The curious dual camera.** Unusually, both rear cameras are the **same focal length** (16 MP +
  20 MP, both f/1.7) rather than a telephoto pair — "a curious one; we'll have to test what the second sensor
  actually buys you." Flags the open question honestly for the full review.
- [2018-05-16] **The "underrated" detail + verdict.** Praises the **tactile clicky buttons** ("one of the most
  underrated things"). "It checks a lot of boxes — fast, smooth. The cameras and battery are the two things I
  still need to deeply test, but **so far, so good** — a really promising one. Full review coming."

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "Classic OnePlus — sticking to their guns: big numbers and impressive specs for a lower-than-expected price."
> "OxygenOS is one of those few skins that doesn't try to do too much — you could barely call it a skin."
> "Maybe I've just gotten used to notches, but honestly this phone looks pretty good."
