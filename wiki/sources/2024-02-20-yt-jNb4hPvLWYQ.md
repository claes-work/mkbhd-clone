## Key claims (Marques-attributed)
- [2024-02-20] **The X100V/VI is his everyday pocket camera:** "over the last 4 years I've
  shot with cameras with massive resolutions, massive price tags, massive aspect ratios,
  but **none of those were the camera that stayed in my pocket every single day. This
  was.**" It fits in his jacket pocket and he "pretty much never leaves the house without
  it." (biography, tech-reviews)
- [2024-02-20] **Why Fujifilm wins = film simulations + JPEG philosophy:** Fuji is the rare
  company that made cameras, glass *and* film, so it employs color scientists; it turned
  that film-stock IP into in-camera JPEG **film simulations** (Provia, Velvia, Classic
  Chrome, etc.). His take: **"I don't think photography is just for professional
  photographers"** — a lot of people just want a *vibe* without heavy editing. (tech-
  reviews, consumer-tech-culture)
- [2024-02-20] **A personal preference:** **"I hate editing photos. I hate spending a lot
  of time in Lightroom making tweaks. So I really like that I can just focus on the image I
  want to take and let the color scientist deal with the color."** He shoots **raw + JPEG
  just in case, but most of the time just shoots JPEG.** (biography, voice)
- [2024-02-20] Praises the X100VI's **HEIF (10-bit) support** as an underrated upgrade
  (bridges raw and JPEG — up to a billion colors, less banding) and the digital rangefinder
  usability (dedicated aperture/shutter/ISO dials so you can adjust the whole exposure
  triangle without moving your eye). (tech-reviews)
- [2024-02-20] **A wish:** he really wishes Fuji would make a **full-frame** version ("I'd
  feel a lot more comfortable shooting that 40-megapixel sensor if it was full-frame"),
  while acknowledging the business/optics reasons they won't. Verdict: at $1,600 "definitely
  the best" everyday pocket camera at that price range, but existing X100V owners don't need
  to upgrade. (tech-reviews)
