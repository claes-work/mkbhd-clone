# Prime Picks! - The #1 Unlocked Smartphone on Amazon! (@mkbhd, 2018-07-13)

## Summary
The **origin of the "Prime Picks" series** (later named): buy the #1 best-seller in an Amazon category and see
if it's actually worth it. Episode 1 is the **$250 Moto G6** — a vehicle for his budget-phone philosophy,
near-stock praise, a public call-out of Motorola's update record, and his hatred of front logos.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-07-13] **SERIES ORIGIN — "Prime Picks."** "New series: pick a category on Amazon, find the **#1 best-seller,
  buy it, and see if it's actually worth it.**" (Named "Prime Picks" by episode 2.) A dated creator-business/format
  node.
- [2018-07-13] **Budget-phone philosophy.** "With budget phones it's all about **where you strategically bring
  features down from a $900 flagship and where you don't.** The G6 brings up 18:9, a glass back, dual cameras,
  USB-C — but keeps the headphone jack, skips the notch, doesn't shrink bezels. A **well-balanced checklist.**"
  His framework for judging cheap phones on their own terms.
- [2018-07-13] **Near-stock Android praise — with a public call-out.** "Motorola's **near-stock Android** (Moto
  Display, Moto Actions — chop for flashlight, twist for camera) is clean, the way a lot of us like it. **BUT**
  near-stock usually means fast updates, and **lately Motorola's been slacking hard — sometimes not updating at
  all. Don't buy this if you want a long tail of updates. I'm calling them out — they should change that.**" Holds
  a company to account (a recurring move).
- [2018-07-13] **"I will never not hate logos on the front of phones."** Dings Motorola for shoving the logo
  between the fingerprint reader and the screen ("looks like they shrank the reader to make room for a logo").
  A clear, quotable design-taste position.
- [2018-07-13] **Honest budget verdict.** "Decent-at-best camera (weak dynamic range) — I'd call it weak on a $900
  phone, but it **gets the job done** here. The front-firing earpiece as the only speaker is actually louder than
  most bottom-firing ones. **Thumbs up** — but it's **no longer clearly the budget king** (Nokia, Xiaomi, Redmi
  are all fighting for this price now)." Calibrated, context-aware.
