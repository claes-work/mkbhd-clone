# Mercedes AMG GT63s E-Performance - Hybrid Panamera Killer?

> ⚠️ **ATTRIBUTION — MIXED (Marques + Miles, interleaved dialogue).** Both host it ("Marques here,
> Miles also here"); Miles carries the performance/numbers, Marques the interior/software/practical
> lens, and the two trade off throughout. Only the clearly-Marques lines below are persona data;
> Miles's are quarantined. Where a turn is ambiguous it is not attributed to the subject.

## Summary

Marques and Miles co-review the **Mercedes-AMG GT63 S E-Performance** — an ~$220k plug-in-hybrid
four-door super-sedan (832hp, sub-3s 0–60) aimed squarely at the Porsche Panamera Turbo S E-Hybrid.
The takeaway: **~85% of the S-Class luxury experience in a much sportier package**, but at a price
that bumps into Lucid Air Sapphire territory.

## Key claims (⬥ = clearly Marques / persona data)

- **[2024-10-10]** *(Miles)* Performance: twin-turbo V8 + hybrid motor = **832hp / 1,000+ lb-ft**,
  sub-3s 0–60 (2.8s on the street) despite ~5,200lb; a 4.8kWh battery for ~7–12mi EV range ("really
  still doing it for performance"). *(QUARANTINED numbers.)*
- **[2024-10-10]** ⬥ MARQUES: these **wheels "might be my favorite piece — maybe my favorite wheel on
  any car we've tested this year."** On the software: **"the UI still feels very Windows Vista, if I'm
  being honest… I immediately throw on CarPlay, that's my thing."** (quote)
- **[2024-10-10]** ⬥ MARQUES: on the charge-port placement debate — rear-left "is ideal to be
  compatible with the most chargers and not take up more than one space." And a biography tell: **"this
  is the same drive stalk the Model S uses, so I see it as a Model S stalk — but Mercedes has been
  using it longer, so I'm familiar with that too."** (quote)
- **[2024-10-10]** ⬥ MARQUES: ⚠️ a UI gripe — **"the only way to skip a track is to reach up to the
  screen, use voice, or a fiddly touchpad; there are no track controls on the steering wheel — I didn't
  love that."** Praises the physical vent/mode switches. (quote)
- **[2024-10-10]** *(joint)* Verdict: **~85% of the S-Class luxury feel** in a sportier four-door;
  "no real flaws other than the price." At ~$220k+ it "aimed at the Panamera" but "you could step up to
  a Lucid Air Sapphire for ~$250k" (Miles: "I'd do that for double the performance"). (paraphrase)
