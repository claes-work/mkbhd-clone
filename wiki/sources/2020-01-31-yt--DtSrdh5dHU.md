# Are we at Peak Smartphone? (@mkbhd, 2020-01-31)

## Summary
A video-essay answering the recurring "are we at peak smartphone?" question. The verdict — "**yes, but no**" —
develops his mature-market thesis: the plateau is real, but don't let it blind you to the bleeding edge that's still
moving fast. Anchored by a **"peak car"** parallel.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-01-31] **THE THESIS — "yes, but no."** "'Yes, we're seeing **fewer big revolutionary jumps** — we've all
  evolved into glass rectangles; **that's what a mature market is** (the greatest rate of change is at the start of
  the curve). But that's **not to say we don't get seriously impressive improvements every year.**'" His crisp
  framing of the plateau.
- [2020-01-31] **Don't lose the edges when you zoom in.** "'The iPhone 11 next to the XR looks like the same phone —
  but zooming in that far, you **lose the peripheral that's still pushing things forward**: ultrawide lenses, high
  refresh rates, **computational photography** (HDR, night modes — photos that were impossible a few years ago are
  now effortless), 65 W charging, and **phones that literally fold in half** (a whole first-gen of fitting a bigger
  screen in your pocket).'"
- [2020-01-31] **THE "PEAK CAR" PARALLEL.** "'Same question: **have we reached peak car?** Technically yes — same
  plateau (four wheels, an engine, a trunk). **But saying so ignores the most exciting parts** — the EV revolution,
  sustainability, autonomy, the Rivian R1T's quad-motor tank turn, the supercharger network, the CyberTruck.'" A
  reusable cross-domain lens: the plateau hides the revolution at the edge.
- [2020-01-31] **"You don't need a new $1,000 phone every year" — the healthy plateau.** "'We've reached a point
  where **software updates are better and phones just last longer** — you can use a 3-4-year-old phone and do
  everything you need. **But don't forget the bleeding edge** — the Galaxy Fold 2, the CyberTruck — or you'll miss
  it.'" Ties to the "why year-over-year phones exist" thesis.
- [2020-01-31] **The combination is the innovation (Galaxy S20 Ultra preview).** "'The rumored S20 Ultra — 120 Hz,
  SD865, 16 GB, 108 MP + four cameras — **no single spec is drastically new, but that combination in one phone is
  new; show that spec sheet to someone three years ago and they'd say it's impossible.**'" A dated forecast + a way
  to see progress in aggregate.

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "This is what we call a mature market... you always have the greatest rate of change at the beginning of the
> curve, and then as you reach the ideal, it slows down."
> "Don't let that plateau make you ignore all the incredible innovations still happening in these industries that
> are driving us forward, or you might miss it."
