# Rewind: Best Gadgets of 2021! (@mkbhd, 2021-12-29)

## Summary
A Best-Buy-sponsored **top-10 of the year's most gettable gadgets** (the companion to Tech Rewind 2021 — this one
is "stuff you can actually get right now," not off-the-wall influential tech). A lighter ingest. The list, counting
up: 10 Beats Fit Pro (his favorite workout buds — the wingtip fit), 9 Z Fold 3, 8 Sony A1 ("a beast" — the Model Y
review + the Studio setup tour were shot on it), 7 Galaxy S21 Ultra, 6 Switch OLED ("they nailed it"), 5 AirTags
("the Apple effect" — how a category ignites once Apple enters), 4 the iPhone 13 mini ("the mini done right" — same
specs as the bigger phones, only size/battery differ), 3 the DJI FPV drone ("never had more fun with a drone"), 2
the **Pixel 6** ("a great phone and a great deal"), and 1 the **M1 Pro/Max MacBook Pros.** Restates **"the notch is
like the Samsung crease — it fades away when you're actually using the device."**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-12-29] **#1 M1 Pro/Max MacBook Pros — and the notch fades like the crease.** "'The new MacBook Pros made
  the biggest splash (the incredible performance/efficiency, and how it applied to my own workflow — I've edited a
  lot on the 16" M1 Max). World-class displays, best-in-class speakers and battery, and all the ports. **The notch
  is kind of like the crease in the Samsung folding phones — when you're actually using the device it fades away
  to the eye and becomes a non-factor, even if it's a head-scratcher why it's there.**'"
- [2021-12-29] **#2 the Pixel 6 — a great phone and a great deal.** "'Mainly the smaller, cheaper $599 Pixel 6 — a
  great deal; Google's first chip (Tensor), quite good cameras for the price, and the most convenient Google
  features (Assistant holding on calls). One reason I choose to use a Pixel so often as my main phone. (I daily the
  6 Pro, but there's not a whole ton of difference.)'"
- [2021-12-29] **#5 AirTags — "the Apple effect."** "'One of the most influential products of the year, mainly
  because it shined a huge spotlight on the category and we got to see how competitors (Tile) reacted. **I'm always
  surprised how many people suddenly have deep thoughts about a category as soon as Apple enters it — the Apple
  effect is real**, and they pulled a lot of companies into their orbit (AirTag wallets, sleeves, clamps).'"
- [2021-12-29] **#4 the iPhone 13 mini — the mini "done right."** "'A spotlight on the mini: **tiny flagships are a
  dying breed, but Apple did the mini the correct way — the whole lineup has the same specs, so the only
  difference is screen and battery size** (the mini has the same chip and primary sensor, and a bigger battery
  than the 12 mini). If there were ever a good time to get a mini, it's now (rumors say there may not be another).'"
- [2021-12-29] **#8 the Sony A1 — "a beast."** "'A great year for cameras; the Sony A1 did a lot right (I look
  forward to shooting with it — the Studio setup tour and the Tesla Model Y review were shot entirely on the A1).'"
