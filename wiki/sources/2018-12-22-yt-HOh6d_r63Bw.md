# Google Pixel Slate: This Ain't It Chief! (@mkbhd, 2018-12-22)

## Summary
A disappointed review of the Google Pixel Slate — his "tablet bust of the year." Great hardware undone by
**Chrome OS lag** on the baseline Celeron. Reprises the **expectation-Delta** framing (like the RED Hydrogen) and
lands a useful observation: with its keyboard it's closer to replacing a laptop than the iPad Pro is.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-12-22] **"Tablet bust of the year" + the expectation-Delta framing.** "This made me **sad, confused, and
  curious — mostly sad.** A letdown hurts more **when the expectations and potential were sky high — that Delta.**
  Same as the RED Hydrogen (phone bust): I respect the company, my expectations were way up, and the gap was
  drastic." His recurring disappointment framework, restated.
- [2018-12-22] **Great hardware — high hopes.** Matte-black-blue, symmetrical, no camera bump; front stereo
  speakers; a sharp 3000×2000 "molecular" display; **dual USB-C**; a power-button fingerprint reader; a magnetic
  keyboard with **infinite adjustment angles**; Chrome OS's "power-user paradise" promise. "I was pumped — maybe
  this unhooks me from the iPad Pro."
- [2018-12-22] **"Welcome to lag city" — the software kills it.** "Then I used it. The multitasking animation takes
  **several seconds**; the whole UI runs at **~12–20 fps**; scrolling and loading are slow. **I couldn't force
  myself to use it long enough to even discover the features.** The opposite of what makes the iPad Pro (A12X) so
  effortless." Likely the baseline **Celeron** bottleneck — "**they shouldn't ship this one.**"
- [2018-12-22] **"Software can be fixed, but…"** "In theory a software update fixes all of it — but **I've seen
  good products get better, not a lot of terrible things get to great.**" A calibrated, low-hope caveat.
- [2018-12-22] **The one real insight — better as a laptop than the iPad.** "With the **$200 keyboard** (premium
  soft-touch, stronger magnets = infinite angles, backlit keys, **a trackpad and mouse** — which I missed on the
  iPad), the **Pixel Slate is closer to replacing an actual laptop than the iPad Pro is with its keyboard.** But as
  a standalone tablet? **This ain't it, chief — don't spend $600 on this; just get an iPad**" (or try the i5/i7)."
  A genuinely useful comparative point.

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "It's an even bigger disappointment when the expectations and the potential were sky high — that's the Delta."
> "Welcome to lag city, population: Pixel Slate."
> "I've seen good products get better, but not a lot of terrible things get to be great."
> "Without the keyboard, if you were planning to use this as your daily tablet — this ain't it, chief."
