# New Year New MKBHD! (@mkbhd, 2018-01-02)

## Summary
A New-Year channel update — a major biography/creator-business node. Reveals **5 million subscribers**, the
2017 stats, the shift from a one-man operation to a **team ("we")**, and the 2018 plans (more car videos, the
Studio Tours series, merch expanding).

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-01-02] **BIOGRAPHY — 5 million subscribers + 2017 stats.** "In 2017 the channel passed **5 million
  subscribers** and rolled past **800 million total video views**. Of the **95 videos** uploaded in 2017 (the
  goal was **100** — got close), they were watched ~266 million times = **1.1 billion total minutes of watch
  time.**" Concrete, dated milestone (up from 4M in Dec 2016).
- [2018-01-02] **The shift to a team — "I keep saying *we*."** "It gradually turned into a team effort — it
  used to be just me, my camera, my computer, and not a lot. With more hands you can do third-person shots,
  moving shots, all kinds of angles — capturing things I couldn't while focusing on the camera myself. We're
  expanding on that in 2018; you'll notice the difference in the videos." A key creator-business/biography
  inflection (the studio becomes a team operation).
- [2018-01-02] **The 2018 plans.** More **car videos** that actually ship ("a guarantee, with a good playlist
  name"); continuing **Dope Tech**; the **Studio Tours** series (Casey Neistat was episode 1; episode 2 was
  planned around **Deadmau5's** studio — but "Linus made his video, basically the same as we were, so we're
  back to working on that"); and **merch** ("that beast will be entirely different in 2018"). His roadmap.
- [2018-01-02] **What he's looking forward to (industry).** "The new **Mac Pro** allegedly this year; non-stop
  questions about the **RED Hydrogen One**; maybe an 8K TV or beyond." His anticipation slate.
- [2018-01-02] **Behind-the-scenes candor.** Notes he "shot some car videos in 2017 that will never see the
  light of day — took a couple days, learned a lot," and that the **Project Loveday** video was the car video
  that did ship (and won). Honest about the unfinished work behind the finished output.
