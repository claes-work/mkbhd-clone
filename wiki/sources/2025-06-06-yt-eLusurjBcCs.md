# One Plus 13S Review: What's the S For? (@mkbhd, 2025-06-06)

## Summary
A review of the **OnePlus 13S** whose "S is for **small**" (also a nod: the channel just hit **20 million
subscribers**). His framing: small phones died with the Zenfone 10 ("the last sub-6" flagship"), so the **new "small
flagship" bar is 6.3"** (iPhone 16 = 6.1", Galaxy S25 = 6.2"), and at 6.3" the 13S is **"one of the best small
flagships — the Zenfone 10 reincarnated for 2025."** (Asterisk: **not coming to the US.**) Three things to know: (1)
**full flagship specs** in a compact, rock-solid body (Snapdragon 8 Elite, 12 GB, up to 512 GB, a small-but-uncut 6.3"
1440p 120 Hz LTPO); (2) **"the OnePlus thing"** — no wireless charging, and cameras that "aren't the reason you buy
this"; and (3) **the battery is the highlight, and it's the shocker** — a **5,850 mAh cell in a tiny phone, 2-day life,
80 W charging** — and **stunningly NOT silicon-carbon** ("I literally assumed it was, because it's impossible in a
phone this small — but it's a regular lithium-ion, which was shocking"; the China version swaps the selfie cam for a
silicon-carbon **6,260 mAh**). Plus **OnePlus's AI arrived** via a customizable **"plus key"** (replacing the beloved
alert slider — an Action-button clone with its own dark settings UI) defaulting to **Mind Space** (a screenshot memory
bank — like Nothing's Essential Space) — which **"breaks my #1 rule of organizational software: not multi-platform"**
(no sync/laptop triage). The other AI (a photo unblur/reflection eraser, live audio translation) is "nothing we haven't
seen" but "nice OnePlus has it built in now instead of relying on Google." At **~$650**, notes: an optical (not
ultrasonic) reader, oddly no double-press-for-camera, and the clever OxygenOS icon-pull-down reachability gesture.
Verdict: **the small-flagship bar has moved to ~6.3", and for the best battery life, "there's no question this is it"**
— just not for camera people, and not in the US.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2025-06-06] **The S is for small — the new small-flagship bar is 6.3".** "'The S stands for small. Small phones died
  with the Zenfone 10, the last sub-6" flagship. So the new bar for a small flagship is 6.3" (iPhone 16 is 6.1", Galaxy
  S25 is 6.2"). At 6.3", the 13S is one of the best small flagships — the Zenfone 10 reincarnated for 2025. (Also: we
  just hit 20 million subscribers.)'"
- [2025-06-06] **The battery is the highlight — and shockingly not silicon-carbon.** "'The battery is the strongest part
  — a 5,850 mAh cell in a tiny phone, easy 2-day life, 80 W charging. I literally assumed it was silicon-carbon because
  it's impossible in a phone this small — but it's a regular lithium-ion, which was shocking. The China version even
  swaps the selfie cam for a silicon-carbon 6,260 mAh.'"
- [2025-06-06] **"The OnePlus thing" — no wireless charging, mid cameras.** "'It does the OnePlus thing, which means no
  wireless charging (an easy ding) and cameras that aren't the reason you buy this phone (the same sensor as the ROG
  Phone 9 / Moto Edge 50, paired oddly with a 2x tele instead of an ultra-wide). You'll get more out of an S25's triple
  cameras.'"
- [2025-06-06] **OnePlus AI arrived via the plus key / Mind Space — but it breaks my multi-platform rule.** "'OnePlus's
  AI arrived: they replaced the beloved alert slider with a customizable "plus key" (an Action-button clone with its
  own dark settings UI), defaulting to Mind Space — a screenshot memory bank, pretty much Nothing's Essential Space. It
  breaks my #1 rule of organizational software: it's not multi-platform, so I can't triage it on a laptop.'"
- [2025-06-06] **The small-flagship bar moved to ~6.3"; best battery, no question.** "'The moral: the benchmark for
  compact/small flagships has moved up to just over 6". But if you want the one with the best battery life, there's no
  question this is it — super-smooth software, super-fast charging, AI built in now. If it's cameras you're after, this
  isn't the one; and if you're in the US, sorry.'"
