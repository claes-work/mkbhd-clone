# Every Phone Should Copy This! (@mkbhd, 2025-02-14)

## Summary
A PSA built around the **HMD Skyline** (HMD owns the Nokia brand; a Lumia-ish $500 mid-ranger) — a phone "you've
probably never heard of" with **two features every phone should copy, but won't.** **(1) Repairability:** one visible
screw + an **iFixit partnership** lets you do real repairs yourself (battery, charge port, back or *front* glass — a
front-glass swap is $95 + $5 for the toolkit); "it took me a minute to get in and replace the front glass." His durable
framing: you don't have to choose between the **Fairphone extreme** (fully modular but thick, less performant, iffy
water resistance) and **Apple's "the most sustainable phone is one you never replace parts in"** ("an interesting
theory — or an excuse from the most successful phone company in the US to tightly integrate everything"). **"You can
land somewhere in the middle"** — the Skyline is easily openable yet still thin and IP54; a simple battery swap extends
a phone's life by years. **(2) Full Qi2 with magnets built into the phone** — the Skyline is **"the only phone in the
world with full Qi2 magnets in the body"** (so it snaps onto MagSafe chargers/mounts; the only non-iPhone he can use
with MagSafe accessories). MagSafe was an obviously-good idea he "expected every Android maker to copy straight away —
just copy the good things," and Qi2 (2023) standardized the magnets — yet makers only ship **"Qi2 ready"** (magnets in
a *case*, like the OnePlus 13 / S25 Ultra). The meta-point: **flagships follow trends, and repairability + in-body Qi2
"aren't trends" and — like small phones — haven't proven they sell phones, so we probably won't see them; but the
Skyline proves it's possible without charging a fortune, so vote with your wallet.** (A solid $500 mid-ranger otherwise
— a mappable Action-button clone; worst vibration motor since the original Razer Phone.)

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2025-02-14] **Repairability — one screw, and you can land in the middle.** "'One visible screw plus an iFixit
  partnership lets you do real repairs yourself — a front-glass swap is $95. You don't have to choose between the
  Fairphone extreme (modular but thick and less performant) and Apple's "the most sustainable phone is one you never
  replace parts in" (an interesting theory, or an excuse to tightly integrate everything). You can land in the middle —
  easily openable yet still thin and IP54. A battery swap extends a phone's life by years.'"
- [2025-02-14] **Full Qi2 magnets in the phone — the only non-iPhone that does it.** "'The Skyline is the only phone in
  the world with full Qi2 magnets built into the body — it snaps onto MagSafe chargers and mounts. MagSafe was an
  obviously-good idea I expected every Android maker to copy straight away — just copy the good things — and Qi2
  standardized the magnets back in 2023. Yet everyone only ships "Qi2 ready" (magnets in a case). Is Apple really going
  to be the only one to put the magnets in the phone?'"
- [2025-02-14] **Flagships follow trends — and these aren't trends.** "'Lots of flagships just follow trends, so they
  have no reason to offer repairability or in-body Qi2 because those aren't trends. Just like YouTube comments beg for
  small phones but nobody makes them because they don't sell — none of these have proven they sell phones either. So we
  probably won't see them. But this phone proves it's possible without charging a fortune — so vote with your wallet
  and prove everyone wrong.'"
