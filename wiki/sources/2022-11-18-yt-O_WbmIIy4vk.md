# iPad Pro M2: What Does 'Pro' Even Mean? (@mkbhd, 2022-11-18)

## Summary
A review of the M2 iPad Pro — staged partly as a **skit** (a friend keeps asking what's new and getting "the same
stuff, but faster"). It's **externally identical to the M1 iPad Pro** (the only visible change: the text now says
"iPad Pro"); inside, the M2 is ~10–15% faster CPU / 30–40% faster GPU — but it runs the same iPadOS 16 everything
else runs. The three real new bits: **ProRes video (but NOT in the stock camera app — needs a third-party app like
Filmic Pro)**, Wi-Fi 6E, and **pencil hover (M2-exclusive "for some reason")**. This triggers his central question:
**what does "Pro" even mean in Apple's lineups?** He finds **two buckets** — **"professional" (people who make money
using it) vs "enthusiast" (people who like the high-end version)** — and concludes **the word "Pro" is really just
used to sell you / make you feel like you're getting the best.** The iPad Pro is the blurriest case (real pros use
Procreate/DaVinci, but he still can't run Final Cut Pro on it). Verdict: **the best iPad you can get — but if ProRes/
Pencil aren't on your list, the M1 is $300 off the same experience.** "**The Paradox of iPad Pro prevails**" — he
keeps using his ~3-year-old A12Z iPad Pro.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-11-18] **Same stuff, but faster.** "'From the outside you cannot tell it's new (only the "iPad Pro" text is
  new). Inside, the M2 does the **same stuff, faster** (~10–15% CPU, 30–40% GPU, same battery) — more raw power
  than a lot of laptops, even Apple's M1 iMac. But it runs the same iPadOS 16 as every iPad. "**If some app the M1
  couldn't handle... just kidding, that's probably not a real scenario.**"'"
- [2022-11-18] **The three real new bits — and ProRes isn't in the camera app.** "'The real new stuff: **ProRes
  video capture — but it's NOT in Apple's own stock camera app; you need a third-party app like Filmic Pro** (so
  much for making it your out-of-the-box mobile video studio). **Wi-Fi 6E.** And **pencil hover — M2-exclusive "for
  some reason"** (a 12 mm preview of your stroke, mixing watercolor colors, pinch-to-resize — genuinely cool, and
  now in some third-party apps).'"
- [2022-11-18] **What does "Pro" even mean? — two buckets.** "'It got me on the existential question: **what does
  "Pro" mean in Apple's lineups? I went through every Pro product and found two buckets: (1) professional — people
  who actually make money using it; (2) an enthusiast who likes the high-end version.** The lines bend (some MacBook
  Pro buyers just wanted the nicer screen). **The word "Pro" is clearly just used to sell you, to make you feel
  like you're getting the best thing — professionals like nice things, so I'd like the "Pro" thing too.**' (Others
  use "Ultra" or "Plus.")'"
- [2022-11-18] **The iPad Pro is the blurriest case.** "'The iPad Pro is the blurriest mix — real pros use
  Procreate/Pixelmator, and DaVinci Resolve is coming — **but our graphic designer would laugh at using it over a
  Wacom, and I'm a video editor who still can't run Final Cut Pro on the iPad Pro.** So don't put yourself in a
  bucket.'"
- [2022-11-18] **The verdict — the Paradox of iPad Pro.** "'If you're iPad shopping, **yes, this is the best iPad you
  can get, phenomenal. But if ProRes never crosses your mind and drawing isn't on your list, get the same
  experience for $300 off with the M1.** I'd like to think a bigger update is coming (the webcam on the long side,
  display/battery/speaker improvements) — **until then, I'll keep using my nearly-3-year-old A12Z iPad Pro that
  does all the same stuff I want. The Paradox of iPad Pro prevails.**'"
