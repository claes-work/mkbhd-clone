# Galaxy S22 Ultra Review: Separating from the Pack! (@mkbhd, 2022-02-22)

## Summary
The full review after ~2 weeks. The bottom line: **"if you took the name off, what Samsung's giving you for the
money is a Galaxy Note"** — "Ultra" now means the highest ambitions/specs/features, and **it has swallowed the
Note.** Combining the Note stuff with the phone **accomplishes the mission of separating the Ultra from the regular
S22s** (a genuinely more-different phone). The S Pen is the best stylus in any phone (latency down to **2.8 ms with
AI prediction — "as close to 1:1 as it gets"**). Repeats the **x-axis vibration-motor downgrade** gripe (he missed
calls) and notes **8K crops ~2x on the Ultra** (worse than the regular S22). Verdict: it delivers on the extras
(display, S Pen, camera tweaks) — but **he's sticking with his S21 Ultra** (basically the same phone, with a design
he prefers).

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-02-22] **"Ultra" swallowed the Note — mission accomplished.** "'Take the name off and **what Samsung's
  giving you for the money is a Galaxy Note.** "Ultra" now just means the highest ambitions, biggest numbers, best
  specs — and it's swallowed the Note (the squared design, the built-in pen). **Combining the Note stuff with this
  phone accomplishes the mission: it separates the Ultra from the regular S22s, makes it a more obviously different
  phone in the lineup.**'"
- [2022-02-22] **The S Pen — 2.8 ms with AI prediction.** "'I'm not the biggest S Pen user (I signed one PDF last
  week), but it's still the best stylus in any phone — all the old features, and **latency down from 9 ms to
  2.8 ms; it uses AI to predict where the pen's going and draws pixels slightly ahead, so it's as close to 1:1 as
  it gets.** And if you won't use it, slot it back — the phone made no sacrifices to include it (still a 5,000 mAh
  battery).'"
- [2022-02-22] **Brightness is logarithmic; the curve is the one dislike.** "'The big number is 1,750 nits max —
  but **brightness is logarithmic, so it's not dramatically brighter than last year's 1,500** (both are very
  sunlight-readable); still, credit for improving an already best-in-class display. **My only real dislike is the
  display curving more over the edges** than the S21 Ultra (worse for watching video), which I don't like.'"
- [2022-02-22] **The vibration downgrade + the 8K crop.** "'Same gripe as the S22 review: **the vibration motor
  went from z-axis to x-axis — just as strong in the hand, but way weaker on a desk/in a pocket** (I literally
  missed phone calls with it right in front of me). And **8K on the Ultra crops ~2x** (into the middle 33 MP of
  the 108 MP sensor) — worse than the regular S22; still 24 fps; there's noticeable shutter lag on the main
  camera.'"
- [2022-02-22] **The verdict — it delivers, but I'm keeping my S21 Ultra.** "'Mission accomplished: if you want the
  extras (the super-high-res display, the S Pen, the camera tweaks — a really good, consistent portrait mode; the
  brighter "Nightography"; an Expert RAW app), it delivers, and the S22+ is a great $200-cheaper standard flagship.
  **That said, I'm sticking with the S21 Ultra as my daily driver — basically the same phone on software/battery/
  cameras, with a design I prefer and a vibration motor that notifies me more often.**'"
