# Let's Talk About Porsche Taycan! (@mkbhd, 2019-09-05)

## Summary
A reaction to the Porsche Taycan reveal, benchmarked against the Tesla Model S. Two durable EV frameworks: **"range
+ charging are the two things you must nail first,"** and the **supercharger network as Tesla's everyday-driving
moat.** Ends on the competition thesis (EV edition) and pushes back on "Tesla killer" framing.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-09-05] **THE EV FRAMEWORK — nail range + charging first.** "'With **every** new electric car, the first two
  things I look at are **(1) range** (how far on a charge) and **(2) charging** (how fast, and where). **You gotta
  nail those two things and the rest will follow** — if you don't, you're fighting an uphill battle against the
  rest of the EV world.'" His canonical EV-evaluation lens.
- [2019-09-05] **The spec face-off — competitive on paper.** "Taycan: 100 kWh battery, **280 mi range** ('300 is
  about the benchmark for a high-priced one'), 0-60 in 2.8s (vs Tesla's 2.4 — 'small on paper, big in the seat'),
  and an impressive **270 kW peak charging** (higher than the Model 3's 250; 800V architecture = thinner wires,
  less cooling, repeatable launches). A **two-speed transmission** — 'I don't think I've seen that in an EV
  before.'"
- [2019-09-05] **THE INTANGIBLE MOAT — the supercharger network + everyday driving.** "'The Model S has the
  **longer range AND the supercharger network** — I go on road trips all the time and **never once have to think
  "am I going to make it to the next charger."** That's the beauty of an always-growing network. It would've been
  sick if the Porsche could use it — Tesla opened it up, but no one takes them up on it.' Tesla wins **everyday
  driving**; the Porsche will win **track driving** (Nürburgring 7:42, better dynamics, repeat performance)." A
  clear everyday-vs-track split.
- [2019-09-05] **THE THESIS — "room for more than one," and "Tesla killer" is lazy.** "'Car people **hate Tesla
  or love Tesla, no in-between.** But there's **room in the EV market for more than one premium sedan** — I'm glad
  we finally have choice, because **competition back and forth makes better products and we win.** Calling it a
  "Tesla killer" is unnecessary — honestly **Tesla pushed Porsche to work this hard** on their first EV in the
  first place.'" The competition value, EV edition (cf. [[2019-07-02-yt-YPln3JP_gKs]]).
- [2019-09-05] **BIOGRAPHY + format tease.** "'I **wrapped my Tesla** (I'm biased, I love it).' Road trips: **NYC↔
  Philadelphia for ultimate frisbee**, **NYC↔Providence for the 5G video.** Teases an **Auto Focus episode** on
  the Taycan ('Porsche, you thinking what I'm thinking?')." Datable personal + channel data.
