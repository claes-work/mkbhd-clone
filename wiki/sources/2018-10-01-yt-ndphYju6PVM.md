# iPhone 'Gates' Explained! (@mkbhd, 2018-10-01)

## Summary
A media-literacy essay on the annual iPhone "-gate" controversies — why they happen, why they matter (the
**scale argument**), why he personally doesn't chase each one, and why the real story is always **how Apple
responds.** One of his clearest statements on holding companies to account without hysteria.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-10-01] **The scale argument — why any iPhone flaw is a big deal.** "The '-gate' suffix (from Watergate)
  gets slapped on any iPhone problem. They matter because of **scale**: Apple sold ~217 million iPhones in FY2017,
  so **a defect in just 0.001% still means 2,000+ problems.** Any issue, no matter how small, has to be treated as
  potentially large." A crisp, quantified media-literacy lens.
- [2018-10-01] **Accountability is legitimate — that's what warranties are for.** "Quality control is real —
  that's why warranty/insurance/AppleCare exist. When something goes wrong, **you have to be able to hold the
  company accountable.**" Frames scrutiny as healthy, not cynical.
- [2018-10-01] **The 2018 gates, calmly assessed.** **BeautyGate** (front-camera skin-smoothing — "very un-Apple;
  they've long been the realistic-selfie standard; but it's more an exaggerated-HDR effect, less harsh on darker
  faces, easily fixed in software") and **ChargeGate** (standby-not-charging-until-you-wake-it — "more serious;
  see Lew's deeper test"). Measured, not alarmist.
- [2018-10-01] **TRANSPARENCY — why he didn't make dedicated videos (+ the not-sponsored disclosure).** "Not
  because **I'm sponsored by Apple — you know that's illegal, right?** It's just that it wasn't a huge deal to me:
  ChargeGate didn't affect any of my devices (I checked), the beauty smoothing isn't as harsh on darker faces, and
  I'm not a big selfie-taker — so it slipped my radar." A rare on-record explanation of his coverage choices +ethics.
- [2018-10-01] **The real story — how Apple RESPONDS ("you're holding it wrong").** "Gates blow up because of **how
  Apple handles them — steering as far from acknowledging a problem as possible.** 'You're holding it wrong'; 'your
  pants shouldn't be that tight.'" He reads the actual **iPhone 4 antennagate statement** aloud as parody and
  predicts a similar ChargeGate statement. A sharp critique of corporate non-response.
- [2018-10-01] **The payoff — "thanks, gates."** "People keep buying iPhones anyway (Apple has the best loyalty
  in the world — like Samsung after the exploding Note 7, 95% of trade-ins went to another Samsung). But scrutiny
  **forces companies to respond and fix things — and we all win.** So: **thanks, gates.**" A characteristic
  net-positive close.
