# Samsung Galaxy S10e Review: Why Not? (@mkbhd, 2019-03-12)

## Summary
The full S10e review — "not a better phone on paper than the S10/S10+, but a better **deal**." Argues the three
trade-downs barely matter (and one is arguably an *upgrade*), and lands on it as "the one most people should get."

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-03-12] **"Not a better phone, but a better deal — why not just get the S10?"** "**Hardware-wise it's a step
  up in every direction except battery** vs the iPhone XR it directly answers — same $750." The three differences:
  cameras, fingerprint, display (+ a naming gripe: "$750 isn't budget, isn't flagship — a **premium mid-range**").
- [2019-03-12] **Cameras — dropping the telephoto is fine.** "Same primary + same **ultra-wide** (the fun new
  Samsung camera this year), just **no telephoto** — **I don't miss it at all** (zoom becomes digital). Keeping the
  ultra-wide is the sweet spot." Consistent with his telephoto-rarely-used read.
- [2019-03-12] **The capacitive fingerprint is arguably BETTER than the S10's ultrasonic.** "Instead of the
  under-glass ultrasonic, the S10e has a **classic capacitive reader in the side power button — and coming back to
  it, man, it's easy to forget how good we got at these: one of the fastest readers I've ever used, way faster than
  the S10's ultrasonic.** You could argue it's actually **better** than what you get on the S10." A counterintuitive
  take (the older tech wins on speed). Plus a swipe-down-for-notifications gesture on it.
- [2019-03-12] **Display — flat, barely worse.** "The smallest (5.8"), still a sharp Dynamic AMOLED with the hole
  punch, but **flat** (slightly thicker bezels — "barely; same as the iPhone XS, which curved its OLED for
  nothing"). **Way better than the XR's LCD.** No user-selectable resolution (a small quirk)."
- [2019-03-12] **6 GB RAM is fine (not 4).** "6 GB not 8 — **thanks to my Pixel concerns I'd worry if it were 4,
  but 6 seems like enough.**" Ties his RAM thread. Plus **Flamingo Pink** (colored rails + camera module — a nice
  touch vs the S10's black).
- [2019-03-12] **The verdict — "the one most people should get."** "On paper, not a better phone — **but a better
  deal, and a better deal than the iPhone XR** (better display + a headphone jack + the ultra-wide). Get the bigger
  phone only if you **need** the ultrasonic reader, the Infinity display, or the 2× telephoto. **Otherwise the S10e
  is probably the one most people should get.**" His value-first steer (echoing the XR).

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "On paper, is it a better phone? No. But it is a better deal."
> "Coming back to this capacitive fingerprint reader from the S10's ultrasonic — man, it's way faster. You could
