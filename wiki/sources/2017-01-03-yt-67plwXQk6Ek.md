# Hiring People? Biased Reviews? Ask MKBHD V12! (@mkbhd, 2017-01-03)

> **SOLO @mkbhd video — the subject directly.** Two landmark answers: the **solo→team transition**
> and the **"reviews are biased on purpose"** philosophy.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated 2017-01-03)
- ★★ **[Marques — the solo→team transition]** **"Up until this point everything you've seen on the
  channel has been made by me — but I think it's time to expand the operation"** to make "not just
  more stuff but better stuff, to enable things that take me too long." **2017 is the year he starts
  hiring** (an assistant first). *(dates the end of the 100%-solo era.)*
- ★★ **[Marques — review philosophy: bias is the point]** "**Why do my reviews seem biased? Because
  they are** — that's inherent to being a reviewer. **I'm not just a spec sheet.** If you want
  something completely unbiased, look at the spec list. The whole point is you get my unique
  perspective — and even if you don't share it, you can **calibrate yourself to my videos** (if I
  like something for a reason you don't, next time you'll know). Every opinion I have is a bias
  because I'm human." *(a core articulation of his reviewer identity.)*
- ★ **[Marques — biography: East Coast]** "I really like LA… but I wouldn't move there anytime soon.
  There's too much good about this **East Coast thing, this New York City being right over there** —
  it's part of who I am now. **East Coast Beast Coast.**" *(committed to the NJ/NYC-area home.)*
- ★ **[Marques — Apollo]** The Model S power-steering issue was a **contaminant on the wiring**
  (misdiagnosed by the first engineer, fixed by the one Tesla flew in) — "I've been driving it ever
  since and it's been fantastic."
