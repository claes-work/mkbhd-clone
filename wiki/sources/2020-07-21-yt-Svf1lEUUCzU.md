# OnePlus Nord Impressions: Back to Budget! (@mkbhd, 2020-07-21)

## Summary
First impressions of the €399 OnePlus Nord — OnePlus returning to the budget space that built their fanbase. His
take: a **balanced package** that doesn't go all-in on one spec, unlike a lot of budget phones. (Notes the two-
embargo impressions/review strategy that "creates two press waves.")

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-07-21] **"Back to budget" — the balanced-package read.** "'OnePlus crept up in price toward flagship
  status; **Nord brings them back.** Unlike a lot of budget phones I've reviewed lately that **go all-in on one
  spec and skimp on the rest, this feels like a much more balanced package** — build quality, the alert slider, a
  **90 Hz flat OLED**, a good battery with warp charging, decent specs (SD765G).'" A distinct budget-phone read.
- [2020-07-21] **Mixed on the design — preferred the scrapped prototypes.** "'I **liked the scrapped prototype
  designs more** (more character, matte finish) — they went back to a **recognizable OnePlus vertical camera
  module**, which takes away character. "This is just the Oppo Reno 3 Pro" — another version of the same phone.'"
- [2020-07-21] **The camera — "I wouldn't call it a flagship camera."** "'OnePlus called it "flagship-quality" —
  **I wouldn't.** The 48 MP main is the **same hardware as the OnePlus 8 (not the 8 Pro)** — a step below flagship.
  But OnePlus has a good track record of **improving cameras over software updates**, and it'll beat the garbage in
  other $400 Androids.'" His measured push-back on the marketing.
- [2020-07-21] **Dual selfie cameras > a useless depth sensor.** "'The first time OnePlus does **two front cameras
  (standard + ultrawide)** — good, since **if you're doing a dual cutout, make use of it** rather than a
  borderline-useless depth sensor.'"
- [2020-07-21] **The value verdict + the iPhone-SE shadow.** "'A steady package — nothing too crazy, but **not
  skimping on almost anything** (big battery, fast charging, nice 90 Hz screen, 5G). **One of the better well-
  rounded things you can get at the price — if you take out the iPhone SE, the other gorilla at this exact
  price.**'" Not coming to the US (a Nord version may come later).

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "Unlike a lot of the other budget phones I've reviewed lately, this doesn't go super all in on one spec and then
> kind of skimp on the rest — this definitely feels like a much more balanced package."
> "I wouldn't call it a flagship camera... the OnePlus 8 camera was a step below [flagship]."
