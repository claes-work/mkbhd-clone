# The Electric Rolls Royce Spectre is Ridiculous!

## Summary

**The canonical "perfect to go electric" case study.** Marques spends a week with the fully-electric
**Rolls-Royce Spectre** and argues it's the exact vindication of his doctrine: a luxury car should be
big, smooth, quiet, and heavy — so **the electric version is the quietest, smoothest, softest
Rolls-Royce ever built.** His overriding impression is **soft** (suspension, steering, seats,
lambswool carpets, pedal mapping) and, above all, **"the quietest car I have ever sat in in my
life."** ~$500k. His favorite Rolls-Royce ever. Fully Marques-narrated.

## Key claims

- **[2024-01-24]** ⭐ The doctrine, vindicated: **"You've heard me talk about luxury sedans — what more
  perfect vehicle to go electric? I want it big, smooth, quiet, spacious; it'll be heavy and expensive,
  comes with the territory. This would be the quietest, smoothest, softest, most spacious Rolls-Royce
  ever built. Rolls-Royce knows that too — their whole lineup is electric by 2030. So I feel like I was
  right."** (quote)
- **[2024-01-24]** Specs: 577 hp, ~7,000 lb (≈ a Rivian R1T), 0–60 low fours, ~260 mi range (~190–200 in
  cold). Underneath it's **a BMW i7 battery + motors** with a custom Rolls suspension on top. (paraphrase)
- **[2024-01-24]** The theme is **soft**: the **Planar Suspension** decouples the anti-roll bars in a
  straight line so a single wheel takes a pothole without the whole car wobbling ("a cloud/boat feeling,
  masks speed like the Maybach"); soft steering; **a "limo stop"** off regen; power you "roll into,"
  never spin-and-slam; lambswool carpets. (paraphrase)
- **[2024-01-24]** ⭐ The standout: **"This is the quietest car I have ever sat in in my life — on
  23-inch wheels, which should be impossible. Hundreds of pounds of noise-isolating material; you can
  have whisper-quiet conversations at highway speed."** The $11k bespoke speakers "sound amazing not
  because they're $11k, but because it's so quiet in here you hear everything." (quote)
- **[2024-01-24]** Unexpected tech wins: wireless CarPlay; a **built-in multi-camera Drive Recorder
  (dashcam)** — "a car this expensive, I'd want to see everything. Good job, Rolls-Royce." (quote)
- **[2024-01-24]** Price: $420k base → **~$540k as-optioned (~$120k of options** — a $13,100 paint job,
  $13,300 door starlight, $10,675 wheels, even $1,775 for a black umbrella canopy). "For the options
  alone you could get a whole BMW i7." (paraphrase)
- **[2024-01-24]** ⭐ The Doug-DeMuro-endorsed thesis: **"The difference between a regular luxury car and
  a $500k Rolls is a level-up that takes a lot of extra money — the price isn't linear, it goes up like
  this. You don't NEED more than an S-Class; but if you want an actual level-up (the sound deadening,
  the lambswool, the quiet), that's how you get there."** "My favorite Rolls-Royce ever." (quote)
