# Inside the Rimac Nevera: The $2.5 Million Dollar Electric Supercar

## Summary

The **@AutoFocus companion video** to Marques's main-channel piece on the **Rimac Nevera** — the
$2.5M, 2,000-hp, quad-motor electric hypercar he **got to drive** (in Ohio, via the Triple F
Collection). His verdict: **"speed is forever ruined for me — fast cars are now ruined."** This is
the deep-dive on the details/Easter eggs; it holds 23 records set in one day (0–60 in 1.7s) and
**"smokes the Chiron, the Plaid, basically everything."** He frames it as the real electric successor
to the perpetually-delayed Tesla Roadster. Fully Marques-narrated.

## Key claims (biography marked ⬥)

- **[2023-08-18]** ⭐ The reaction: **"Speed is forever ruined for me — I can't see cars as fast anymore
  because of this thing."** The Nevera: Croatian company Rimac's second car, quad-motor, 120 kWh,
  ~2,000 hp; **23 records in one day (0–60 in 1.7s)**; "it smokes the Chiron, the Plaid, basically
  everything that exists." (quote)
- **[2023-08-18]** ⬥ BIOGRAPHY: **he got to drive it in Ohio, courtesy of the Triple F Collection**
  (this is a companion to "the video on the main MKBHD channel"). At **6'3", "six-three, six-four might
  be the limit"** to fit in the car. He fits a **Pixel Fold** in the cramped cabin ("first time a Pixel
  Fold's ever been in a Rimac"). (quote)
- **[2023-08-18]** Build quality: **"the number one thing that's hard to explain in person is how
  well-built and quality this is — basically every part is carbon fiber or metal."** Bugatti influence
  (VW Group merged Rimac with Bugatti between the first and second cars). Easter eggs: state-of-charge
  side lights, a selectable Croatia/US/UK flag animation, dial-in front/rear power split, drift mode.
  Claimed future OTA CarPlay/Android Auto. (paraphrase)
- **[2023-08-18]** Honest range: "71% battery showing 73 miles of range — it knows how hard it's been
  driven and adapts (~662 Wh/mi). This car has not been babied." (paraphrase)
- **[2023-08-18]** ⭐ The framing: **"When Elon unveiled the Roadster 2.0 in 2017, it was supposed to be
  the hardcore smackdown to gas cars — then Tesla got other priorities (Cybertruck). This car came out
  instead. It's far less attainable ($2M vs. $200k), but it's the actual king of performance now."** (quote)
