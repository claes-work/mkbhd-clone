## Genuinely-new micro-takes (each is one short)
- ★ **AirTag stalking → Apple and Google "had to work together."** On the joint **cross-platform
  unwanted-tracking-detection standard**: Apple + Google agreeing on a spec so an iPhone can warn you about an
  Android-paired tracker and vice-versa — "**two of the biggest rivals in tech had to sit down and agree**"
  because the safety problem crossed ecosystems. *(an interop/privacy node — rare-alignment-when-safety-demands-it,
  same walled-garden-bridging thread as his RCS/iMessage takes.)* [2023-05-12] (yt-DO1PJYAQj0c)
- ★ **The Magic Mouse design flaw (again).** A third-party **case that fixes the Magic Mouse** by relocating so
  you can use it while charging — the running joke/critique that Apple put **the charging port on the bottom**,
  so it's unusable while it charges. *(corroborates his long-standing Magic-Mouse-is-badly-designed take.)*
  [2023-03-31] (yt-KG6Nu72Avqg)
- ★ **iOS 17 "Personal Voice" (accessibility).** "Maybe the wildest new feature ever added to the iPhone" —
  buried in **Accessibility → Speech**, it lets you **train the phone to recreate your own voice** (for people
  at risk of losing it), reading ~150 phrases. *(ties to his interest in accessibility features; genuinely
  impressed register.)* [2023-07-12] (yt-bDtuVp1ywTo)
- ★ **The Vision Pro reveal-shot tell.** The very first time Apple showed the Vision Pro in real life, they
  chose **a specific shot** — a deliberate framing choice he flags as meaningful. *(his "read the marketing
  choices" eye; corroborates the Vision-Pro-reveal thesis.)* [2023-06-08] (yt-tciqW8VKfOU)
- **Mid-cycle color strategy — the Yellow iPhone 14 Plus.** "Apple does these mid-cycle new colors just to keep
  the iPhone fresh and talked-about." *(a small product-marketing framework: color drops as attention
  maintenance.)* [2023-03-09] (yt-a01edYZIfjc)
- **Google Assistant "hum to search"** — hum/sing a stuck song and Assistant identifies it. Feature spotlight.
  [2023-04-14] (yt-K6gtEGnbR6I)
- **The BMW i7** — "the most tech I've ever seen in a car" (a full-size electric 7-series longer than an
  Escalade, with the theater screen). [2023-05-19] (yt-UXB9bnuyZb4)
- **Tesla's $300 wireless charger** — hailed as "Tesla making AirPower where Apple couldn't"; a multi-device
  wireless pad. [2023-03-22] (yt-4PZSB9bV1Zg)
