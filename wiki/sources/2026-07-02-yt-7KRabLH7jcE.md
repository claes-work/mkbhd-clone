# The Best Car I've Ever Driven: McLaren W1 ★★

## Summary

Marques drives the **McLaren W1** — the successor to the legendary F1 and P1 — at **Mugello
Circuit**, "one of the first people outside McLaren" to do so. It's the payoff of his 2024
hybrid-supercar thesis (electrification arrived at the top). A **1,200+hp twin-turbo V8 hybrid
that weighs ~3,000 lbs** ("a Formula One car with a license plate"), it lands the aesthetics +
drive + character and reminds him most of his **all-time favorite car, the P1**. The verdict is
in the title — **his new poster car** — a landmark car review and a big biography beat. ★★.

## Key claims

- **[2026-07-02]** The W1 is **the successor to the McLaren F1 and P1** — "some big shoes to fill" —
  and he's "one of the first people outside McLaren to drive it." It's the payoff of his ~2024 hybrids
  video ("electrification is here… the holy trinity of 2015 was just the beginning"). (paraphrase)
- **[2026-07-02]** ⬥ **His favorite car of all time is the McLaren P1** (a hybrid holy-trinity member
  he was "lucky enough to drive" — "the type of car where owners with 20–30 others still say it's their
  favorite to drive"). The W1 is "closer than ever to a literal Formula One car with a license plate."
  (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2026-07-02]** Still a purist McLaren: **extreme lightweight, rear-wheel drive, hydraulic steering,
  3D-printed double-wishbone suspension.** The exterior is "an aerodynamic masterpiece" (an active
  front splitter/DRS, an active rear wing, a huge diffuser — "the air leaves the back twice as fast as
  it entered the front"). (paraphrase)
- **[2026-07-02]** **His best-looking, most-interesting McLaren interior ever** (the historic knock on
  McLaren was reliability/build quality) — seats built into the carbon tub, a knit concept-car material,
  fixed seats where **you move the pedals/wheel to you**, anhedral doors, wired CarPlay ("not that
  you'll do much tech in this car"). (paraphrase)
- **[2026-07-02]** The numbers, vs his benchmark (the ~700hp Porsche 911 Turbo S): a new in-house
  **twin-turbo flat-plane-crank 4.0L V8 making 900+hp alone**, plus a **Formula-E-derived radial-flux
  e-motor adding 342hp** (a tiny 1.4kWh battery, 1–2 EV-only miles); **~1,450 kg (~3,200 lbs)** — an
  insane power-to-weight. **0–186 mph: ~12.7s (the Porsche ~22s).** (paraphrase)
- **[2026-07-02]** On the road (tight Italian mountain roads, comfort mode): rides well, absorbs
  potholes, not twitchy, **B+ visibility** (a rearview camera a quarter-blocked by the rear wing). **"McLaren
  continues to do the best steering in any supercar with their hydraulic steering."** In sport, the
  sound and torque-filled long pulls come alive. (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2026-07-02]** The honest tension: like much of the hypercar world it's "a bit less communicative,
  a bit less emotion than some older cars" (1,200hp, "crazy numbers"), **but** it keeps McLaren's
  lightweight/hydraulic-steering purism — **"it's both things at once."** (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2026-07-02]** At **Mugello** (an F1-grade track; race mode drops the suspension and extends the
  wing so far it's "no longer street-legal"): locked to the ground on Trofeo RS tires, **over 190 mph
  on the straight — "the same speed Lewis Hamilton hit here in an F1 car" at the 2020 Tuscan GP.** Fun
  fact: **the G-forces literally pulled the glass in his lens, moving him out of focus.** (paraphrase +
  quote)
- **[2026-07-02]** ⬥ Verdict (~3 hours in the car, notes taken on his phone): highlights are the
  **aesthetics/build** and **the drive/character** (road-trip-able-to-track-monster). Still a hybrid
  (a few faults + door-latch quirks on this **prototype**, "I'll give it a pass"); McLaren "has gotten
  way better with build quality." It reminds him most of the P1 (sounds like it, nearly lag-free — the
  hybrid deletes turbo lag). **399 built, $2M+ — "we'll probably never see it on the road, just like the
  P1." "I think I have a new poster car for the bedroom now."** (quote)
