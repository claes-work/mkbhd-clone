# ChatGPT: Should Creators be Worried? (Waveform, 2022-12-09)

> ⚠️ **Ensemble podcast.** Hosts: Marques Brownlee, Andrew Manganelli, David Imel.
> No speaker labels; only **clearly-Marques** takes train the persona. Marques's **earliest
> ChatGPT reaction** (days after its Nov-2022 launch).

## Summary
The show's first dive into **ChatGPT** (just launched) and AI avatars — and whether creators
should worry — plus a milestone: their ~145th consecutive weekly episode.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated 2022-12-09)
- ★ **[Marques, clearly — early ChatGPT/AI-and-creators]** ChatGPT is "a taste of the
  future" — a chatbot drawing on a huge catalog of the world's information, "kind of
  terrifying" in what it can do. **"I want to make a video about this because I have thoughts
  about all the AI stuff — how it's used, what we can use it for, how it might evolve."**
  Notably: **people are already sending them "full MKBHD videos" written by ChatGPT — "and
  it does a pretty dang good job."** *(his first-take on AI's threat/opportunity for
  creators; the show is his sandbox for it.)*
- ★ **[Marques, clearly — the podcast + himself]** The Waveform streak hit **~145 weekly
  episodes / almost 3 years unbroken** — **"this is my longest relationship with anything in
  my life."** The only near-miss was episode 1 (he **flew back from California to record on
  Thursday, then flew back ~2 hours later**). Aside corroborating the productivity thread:
  **"Marques has been through like 14 different project [to-do] apps since we started."**
- **[Marques, clearly — sarcasm]** On a Huawei smartwatch that pops open to reveal two
  earbuds inside: **"The two things I want in a smartwatch are for it to be thicker and have
  worse battery life — so I don't understand this at all"** (and people on Twitter disagreed).
