# Apple Watch Series 5 Review: One Big Tradeoff! (@mkbhd, 2019-10-09)

## Summary
Review of the Apple Watch Series 5, whose one real new feature is the **always-on display** — and its one big
tradeoff is **battery life.** Like the iPhone 11 review, it's "same design, subtle tweaks, one main feature."
His workaround: toggle always-on on/off depending on the day.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-10-09] **The always-on display is the one feature — and it's a battery tradeoff.** "The **LTPO variable-
  refresh** display drops to **1 Hz** when idle, but 'having always-on **always on does hurt battery** — I went
  from ~50% left to 20-30% left; a long outdoor GPS workout can nearly kill it.' **With it off, I hit noon at 90%+
  — legitimately two-day battery.**' His solution: **toggle it on during the workday, off at night** (and 'it's
  slightly annoying to be the guy with the glowing wrist all night')." A balancing-act verdict.
- [2019-10-09] **The glanceability case for always-on.** "Explains why people wanted it — 'sometimes the **wrist-
  raise gesture is inconvenient or rude** (driving, biking, typing, a social situation); you just want to glance
  at the time.'" Frames the feature from real use.
- [2019-10-09] **"The default" — the Apple Watch's position.** "'I bet I'm not the only one to interview
  Microsoft's and Bill Gates's CEOs while wearing an Apple Watch — it's not trolling, **it's just the default at
  this point: the default smartwatch for the iPhone, which is also everywhere.**'" A market-position observation.
- [2019-10-09] **"The worst UI in any tech product in a decade" — the blob app chooser.** "'The **blob app-chooser
  interface** might be the **worst UI in any Apple product, any tech product, in like the last decade** — I've
  changed it to list mode. Apple, figure out a grid mode.'" A pointed, quotable design critique.
- [2019-10-09] **The "same as the iPhone 11" verdict.** "'It's kind of like that iPhone 11 review — **you already
  know what it does; same design, subtle tweaks, one main feature.** If you won't use always-on much, a **Series 3
  or 4 at a discount is a better deal.** The Apple Watch continues to firmly deliver on what you expect.'"
