# Huawei Mate X2 Impressions: I Was Right! (@mkbhd, 2021-03-19)

## Summary
Impressions of the Huawei Mate X2 — a validation lap: **"I was right."** Weeks after concluding the outer-folding
Mate Xs wasn't practical and they should switch, **Huawei went to an inside fold** (like the Galaxy Fold). The
durable ideas: this is **the best folding-phone hardware right now** (a bigger, near-normal-aspect-ratio outer
screen, a great crease-minimizing hinge, near-flagship P40-Pro-Plus cameras), a clever **wedge design** (weight
near your hand), and the crystallized **folding-endgame thesis**: make the fold "just another feature."

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-03-19] **"I was right" — the inside-fold switch.** "'I told you: the outer-folding Mate Xs was hot but not
  practical, so maybe stop the outside-fold thing until the tech gets better. **And they did the thing — they went
  from an outside fold to an inside folding display**, doing a lot similarly to the Z Fold 2 (which I liked).'"
- [2021-03-19] **The best folding hardware right now — the outer screen + hinge.** "'The outer screen is **even
  bigger and higher-quality than Samsung's — a full 6.45", wider, close to a normal 15:9 aspect ratio, so it feels
  like a full smartphone** (the Fold 2's front screen was a poor excuse by comparison). The hinge firmly pops it
  open to flat with **hardly any crease at all — much better than Samsung's. Shout out to the hinge engineers.**'"
- [2021-03-19] **THE WEDGE DESIGN — center of gravity near your hand.** "'It's a **wedge (4.4 mm thin side → the
  thick, component-heavy side).** Huawei's reasoning: on a Z Fold the center of gravity is far from your hand, so
  the wobble feels extra heavy; **putting the weight closer to your hand makes it feel lighter. I buy it — it
  feels less heavy than the Fold 2** (still a heavy phone; it rocks on a table from the camera bump).'" A
  promotable design-rationale read.
- [2021-03-19] **Near-flagship everything (the caveat: no Google, small battery).** "'It uses **very close to
  flagship bits** — the same great P40-Pro-Plus / Mate-40-Pro-Plus camera system (50 MP ~1/1.28" primary, 3x +
  10x periscope), Kirin 9000, 55 W charging. Caveats: **the concerningly small 4,500 mAh battery** for an 8" 90 Hz
  display, no wireless charging, and **the same no-Google-services software problem** (not coming to the US).'"
- [2021-03-19] **THE FOLDING-ENDGAME THESIS.** "'I keep this in my head with every foldable: **the end game is to
  get the fold as close to "just another feature" as possible — remove as many caveats as you can.** A lot of
  gen-1 ones were cool that they folded but not a great idea otherwise. **When it's just as usable unfolded, with
  just as good battery/specs/cameras, and the price is close to a regular phone — that's when people will want
  them.** This is a step toward that — a refined experience.'" A crystallized foldables framework.
