# The Death of AirPower: Explained! (@mkbhd, 2019-03-30)

## Summary
A commentary/explainer on Apple officially cancelling AirPower — the multi-device wireless charging mat that was
never shipped. Walks the 18-month saga, explains the thermal engineering failure, and reads what it says about
Apple (with a running Mac Pro-paranoia bit).

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-03-30] **The saga — announced 2017, quietly erased, now dead.** "Announced with the iPhone X (Sep 2017),
  'coming next year' — all of 2018 passed. At the 2017 hands-on area, **nobody could actually get it to charge.**
  Apple slowly **erased it from its site** (which only drew more attention); the AirPods 2 box **still diagrams it.**
  Now, March 2019, they've admitted it won't work." A clean media-history walkthrough.
- [2019-03-30] **THE MECHANISM — why it failed (thermal).** "The magic was **place any device anywhere on the mat**
  and it charges — which required a **20–30+ coil** design for total X-Y freedom. That many coils in one small mat
  = **it ran way too hot** (per John Gruber's report). **Apple and thermals just don't seem to get along lately.**"
  A clear engineering explanation.
- [2019-03-30] **The accountability read (both camps).** "Apple had two options — redesign it or cancel it, and
  **it's pretty Apple-like to cancel the whole thing rather than ship a different mat.** You can be **mad they
  didn't ship a product they promised** (not a good look) OR **glad they didn't ship something thermally dangerous**
  (like a certain exploding Samsung). Which camp are you in?" Balanced, as usual.
- [2019-03-30] **The running Mac-Pro-paranoia bit.** "It wasn't a huge deal — it's a ~$180–200 mat, not the iPhone
  11 or **the Mac Pro. Wait — they could cancel unannounced products... the Mac Pro is unannounced... please, God,
  don't cancel the Mac Pro (nobody buys it though — please don't).**" A characteristic comedic aside (his most-
  anticipated 2019 product).

## Notable verbatim quotes
