# Google Pixel 8A Impressions: Just Get The 8! (@mkbhd, 2024-05-07)

## Summary
Hands-on with the **$500 Pixel 8A** — Google "mostly sticking to the script": a good mid-ranger, a few improvements
where it counts, same price. New: slightly rounder corners ("round-SpongeBob vibes"), a matte back with vibrant new
Aloe/Bay colors, the **Tensor G3** (capable if inefficient), a first-time 256 GB option, and an improved **Actua
display** (40% brighter, now pOLED up to **120 Hz** — though **Smooth Display ships off by default**, so most buyers
never see it). Same great Pixel software story: the best **speech-to-text** he's used, **seven years of updates**, and
cameras basically unchanged from the **7A that topped his blind camera test's Elo** against flagships. His two worries:
(1) a **two-assistants-side-by-side mess** — the Gemini app is now promoted on the home screen while the Google Search
bar's mic still routes to the *separate* Google Assistant; Google is developing two overlapping assistants and "will
eventually have to consolidate them" (he expects Gemini to do everything). (2) The **price / self-cannibalization** —
you can still buy last year's *phone-of-the-year* **Pixel 8 for $550**, which gets you thinner bezels, a brighter/
higher-quality display, arguably better cameras, bigger battery, faster charging, IP68 and the same G3 — **"is that
not a way better-feeling deal?"** The 8 was so good it makes phones both above and below it feel like worse buys.
Verdict: **"a solid mid-ranger" — but the recommendation is basically "just get the 8"**, and this weird six-month
release cadence is odd.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2024-05-07] **Sticking to the script — a solid $500 mid-ranger.** "'Google's keeping it simple: last year's phone
  was already really good, so make a few key improvements and keep the price at $500. A brighter Actua pOLED that now
  hits 120 Hz, the Tensor G3, a first 256 GB option, new matte colors. Solid.'"
- [2024-05-07] **Smooth Display ships off by default.** "'A lot of people will argue 120 Hz doesn't matter much to
  Pixel buyers — and you're right, because many won't even notice: Smooth Display is off by default, so it's a 60 Hz
  phone out of the box, and even on it's dynamic up to 120, not locked.'"
- [2024-05-07] **The Pixel software story still carries it.** "'It's the one with the smart AI features, easily the
  best speech-to-text speed and accuracy I've used on a phone, seven years of updates, and cameras basically unchanged
  from the 7A — which got the highest Elo in our blind camera test against top flagships. A pretty good baseline.'"
- [2024-05-07] **Two assistants side by side — a mess Google will have to consolidate.** "'The Gemini app is now
  promoted on the home screen, but the Google Search bar's mic still goes to the separate Google Voice Search with
  Assistant. Google is literally developing two assistants side by side with overlapping-but-not-congruent
  capabilities. It's inconsistent; they'll eventually have to consolidate — I think Gemini does everything at some
  point.'"
- [2024-05-07] **The 8A cannibalizes itself against the $550 Pixel 8.** "'You can still buy last year's phone-of-the-
  year Pixel 8, brand new, for $550 — thinner bezels, a brighter/higher-quality display, arguably better cameras with
  bigger sensors, a bigger battery, faster charging, IP68, the same G3. Is that not a way better-feeling deal? The 8
  was so good it makes phones both more and less expensive feel like worse buys — including making the 8A look a little
  high-priced. Just get the 8.'"
