# Apple Watch Series 6 Review: It's Bait! (@mkbhd, 2020-10-02)

## Summary
Full review after living with the Series 6. The landmark idea: **one of the biggest mistakes in tech is comparing
the new thing to last year's thing when most buyers aren't upgrading from last year.** The verdict: if you have a
Series 4/5 you don't need this, but if you don't have one yet, **this is the most complete "ecosystem bait" yet.**
Blood oxygen is dismissed; the S6 chip and the haptic alarm are the real wins; **"if it ain't broke, don't fix
it" — and Apple didn't.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-10-02] **THE MATURE-CATEGORY RULE — don't compare to last year's model.** "'One of the **biggest mistakes
  we make in tech, so often in these mature categories, is comparing the new thing to last year's thing — when
  most people buying it aren't upgrading from last year.** That's a rare use case; **most people have 3–4-year-old
  versions.** If you have a Series 4/5, you don't need to upgrade.'" A promotable reviewing principle.
- [2020-10-02] **"The most complete ecosystem BAIT yet."** "'If you don't have an Apple Watch yet, **this is the
  most complete ecosystem bait yet.**' The Apple effect got me sleeping in a watch I don't like wearing to bed —
  "I guess that's the Apple effect, getting people to try things."'" His framing of Apple's pull.
- [2020-10-02] **BLOOD-OXYGEN SKEPTIC — buy a $25 pulse oximeter instead.** "'A dedicated sensor clamps your
  fingertip and reads through it; **the watch reads reflections off the back of your wrist — a non-ideal spot,
  which is why readings are inconsistent.** If you care that much about accuracy, **grab a $25 pulse oximeter
  from Amazon — it'll be way more accurate than any smartwatch.** This is a casual gamify-it sensor; **don't take
  it to the doctor.**'" (I learned dedicated ones **working with David Blaine on Ascension** — he needed accuracy
  to tell if he was hypoxic.)
- [2020-10-02] **The real best feature — the S6 chip (and the underrated haptic alarm).** "'The **real best
  feature is the S6 chip** — the efficiency for all-day battery, the brighter always-on, faster charging,
  snappier watchOS 7 animations. And the **massively underrated haptic alarm** — it just taps you awake, no
  sound. Sleep tracking is casual; hand-washing detection is hilarious.'"
- [2020-10-02] **THE CHARGING PUZZLE + the 20–85% habit.** "'If you wear it all day AND to sleep, **when do you
  charge it?** I found two partial windows (shower at night, morning routine) — **I'm always bouncing between 20
  and 85%, which is actually better for battery health.**'"
- [2020-10-02] **"If it ain't broke, don't fix it" — well played, Apple.** "'Apple **did not fix anything that
  wasn't broken** — no new shape, no circular version, no Qi charging, no third-party watch-face store. Series 6
  for 2020, conveniently available for the holidays. **Well played, Apple** — I'm going back to my black Series 5.'"
