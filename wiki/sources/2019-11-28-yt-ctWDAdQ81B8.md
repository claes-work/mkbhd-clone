# 16" MacBook Pro Review: Now Do It Again! (@mkbhd, 2019-11-28)

## Summary
The full 16" MacBook Pro review — "the best laptop Apple's ever made (not the one I'd personally get)." The frame
is the **"listen to the people team"** thesis: Apple is systematically fixing its Pro lineup based on user
complaints. Ends with a wishlist and "now do it again" to the 13".

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-11-28] **THE THESIS — Apple's "listen to the people" committee.** "'It's like Apple hired a **"listen to
  the people" team** going through the lineup fixing each product: **iPad Pro** → functional USB-C; **AirPods Pro**
  → better fit/sound/ANC; **iPhone 11 Pro** → ultrawide + bigger battery; now the **MacBook Pro** → fixed keyboard,
  bigger battery, fixed thermals, bigger screen. Whoever this imaginary team is, good looks.'" A durable read on
  Apple's 2019 course-correction (ties [[2019-06-08-yt-O2iorLuSjOg]]).
- [2019-11-28] **The fixes deliver — keyboard, thermals, battery, speakers.** "**Scissor keys** ('significantly
  better, real key travel'), **+28% thermal efficiency** (same i9, but bigger heatsink/fans → stays at higher
  clocks under sustained loads — rendering, compiling, exporting), the **100 Wh TSA-max battery** ('a 5-hr flight
  of email/browsing/writing landed at 60%'), and **speakers improved to "holy crap level" — best in any laptop.**'"
- [2019-11-28] **STILL won't edit RED on a laptop — needs the desktop.** "'I **still refuse to throw RED RAW at my
  laptop** — my render times don't make sense (EOS R 4K was fine, but I shoot RED RAW, not ProRes). **That's why I
  travel with an iMac Pro when I shoot RED. I do this to myself.**'" A hard, datable constraint of his pipeline
  (cf. [[2019-06-28-yt-dZ_Ab1XxxKg]]).
- [2019-11-28] **The wishlist — and "now do it to the 13"."** "'Two more USB-C ports (give us six), **bring back
  the SD card reader** ('~100% of the creative pros I know use SD cards'), an **LTE option** (like I use on the
  iPad Pro), a **black/matte-black option** (space gray is 'a slightly darker silver'). And **take every
  improvement and do it to the 13" — make it a 14" MacBook Pro** — because that's the size I'd actually want.'"
  His prescient 14"-MBP wish, stated again.
- [2019-11-28] **The universal USB-C charger note.** "'The 96 W brick becomes my **universal fast charger** — the
  laptop, iPad Pro, phone, and Sony headphones all charge via USB-C; it just works with everything.'" A stated
  appreciation for USB-C standardization.
