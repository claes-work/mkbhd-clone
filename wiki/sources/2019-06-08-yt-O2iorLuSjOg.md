# iPad OS Impressions: They Listened! (@mkbhd, 2019-06-08)

## Summary
His "favorite announcement from the entire WWDC 2019 keynote." iPad OS 13 directly fixes the complaints from his
iPad Pro review — the software update "kind of feels like it saves the iPad Pro." This is the payoff to the
recurring **"incredible hardware held back by software"** thread.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-06-08] **RESOLUTION of "held back by software."** "'There were a lot of complaints in my iPad Pro review
  about how this is clearly the best iPad ever — incredible hardware, great screen/battery/chip — **but still
  held back by the software, by iOS.** iPad OS **directly addresses so many of those complaints** and brings it
  up to a much more impressive computer. This update kind of feels like it **saves the iPad Pro** for me.'"
- [2019-06-08] **The killer features — desktop-class browsing + USB-C file import.** "**Desktop-class Safari**
  (Google Drive, the YouTube creator studio — 'literally one of the things I would carry a MacBook Pro to do'),
  a **download manager**, and finally **plug in a flash drive / SD card and import directly** ('originally what I
  was expecting when I saw USB-C on the iPad Pro')." Concrete workflow wins.
- [2019-06-08] **Sidecar + Apple Pencil latency.** "**Sidecar** turns the iPad into a second Mac display (Pencil-
  compatible); the Pencil latency dropped **20 ms → 9 ms** — 'incredibly fast, feels like writing directly onto
  the screen.'"
- [2019-06-08] **THE AMBIVALENCE — hold-back-then-deliver vs. listening.** "'I don't know whether to be **mad at
  Apple for holding these features back** and delivering them later to blow our minds, or **super happy for Apple
  for listening** to our feedback.'" A dated, characteristic tension in how he reads Apple's release strategy.
- [2019-06-08] **PERSONAL — the practical upshot.** "'Today I'm taking my laptop out of my backpack and just
  carrying the iPad Pro.' Since I don't edit video on the go anymore (I do it between desktops), **it can do
  everything I need mobile.**" (Aside: he downloads **New York Empire** ultimate-frisbee game footage from a
  shared Google Drive — a biography data point; see [[2019-06-28-yt-dZ_Ab1XxxKg]].)
