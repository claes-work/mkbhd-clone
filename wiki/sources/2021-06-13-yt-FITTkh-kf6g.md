# Top 5 Android 12 Features: Huge Redesign! (@mkbhd, 2021-06-13)

## Summary
A top-5 walkthrough of the Android 12 beta — **"easily the biggest Android redesign since maybe the beginning."**
The durable notes: **Material You** (wallpaper-based theming that recolors the whole system) is the headline; the
**privacy controls go "above and beyond the iPhone"** (camera/mic indicators + a privacy dashboard + approximate
location); and the recurring **"close the gap with iOS on smoothness"** welcome (a 22% CPU-time cut).

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-06-13] **"The biggest Android redesign since the beginning."** "'Android's been maturing with subtle
  tweaks for years, but **this is truly the biggest — visually, literally everything is different.**' Top 5, in
  order of importance: (5) smoothness, (4) redesigned widgets, (3) privacy, (2) new quick settings, (1) Material
  You.'"
- [2021-06-13] **#5 — smoothness (close the gap with iOS).** "'A 22% cut in CPU time for core system services —
  **no matter your chip, things feel smoother, less jank.** I always welcome this; **you want Android to close
  the gap with iOS on smoothness.** (Plus the rumor Google may design its own silicon for the next Pixel.)'"
- [2021-06-13] **#3 — privacy "above and beyond the iPhone."** "'Commendable privacy controls — **the key word is
  control:** camera/mic indicators (like iOS), quick toggles to kill all camera/mic access, a **privacy dashboard
  timeline** of which apps used which sensor and when, and **approximate-vs-precise location** per app. "That's
  another level above and beyond the iPhone."'"
- [2021-06-13] **#1 — Material You (wallpaper-based theming).** "'The evolution of Material Design (Google's since
  2014) — **big buttons/sliders, larger text, and wallpaper-based theming: Material You takes your wallpaper's
  color and picks matching accent colors** for the clock and quick settings. "Change your wallpaper, change your
  life." (Note: Material You is Pixel-only for now; other phones get the features with a different aesthetic.)'"
