# The Electric MINI Maxes out on Quirks!

## Summary

Marques reviews the **electric MINI Countryman (All4)** and deliberately breaks his own format —
"you didn't come here for quirks and features; if you wanted that you'd watch Doug DeMuro's videos,
but the second I saw this car my brain went straight to 'this is quirky.'" A circular dinner-plate
OLED, a go-kart mode with a live G-force meter, a key-twist start, customizable tail-light
patterns, and a magnetic little "MINI" box — lots of **character for the sake of character**, done
well enough and cheaply enough that "MINI fans are going to love this." His personal pick in the
class remains the Volvo EX30. Fully Marques-narrated.

## Key claims

- **[2025-05-15]** Format nod: **"You didn't come here for quirks and features — if you wanted that
  you'd watch Doug DeMuro's videos. But the second I saw this car, my brain went straight to: this is
  quirky."** So he does a mix. (quote)
- **[2025-05-15]** Basics: not a Mini Cooper — **a "big Mini," a literal crossover**; two rows, four
  doors, same segment as the Volvo EX30; ~$41k start, ~$50k specced; ~260–280mi range, 150kW charging.
  Shares a platform with the gas Countryman. (paraphrase)
- **[2025-05-15]** The quirks: a **circular dinner-plate-sized OLED touchscreen** ("you don't see
  circular screens often because most UIs are rectangles" — Android Auto is a square inside the
  circle); **key-twist start** (twist left off / right on); a **go-kart mode** with a "woohoo" chime +
  live G-force/torque/horsepower needles; **customizable tail-light LED patterns** ("what other car
  are you out here changing the tail-light patterns on?"); a magnetic **"MINI" box** and ~5 places to
  put your phone. "Some are just character for the sake of character — they don't serve a purpose, but
  they're interesting." (quote + paraphrase)
- **[2025-05-15]** Drive: ~5s 0–60 (All4 dual-motor), smooth, soft suspension. ⚠️ Feedback: the
  **auto-regen calibration** (coast-to-braking with no input) is hard to get used to; the **brake
  pedal has little bite at the top** then grabs. (paraphrase)
- **[2025-05-15]** ⬥ Taste note: **"As much as I like the color red, I typically don't love an all-red
  car"** — but the black trim + white roof break it up well. (quote)
- **[2025-05-15]** ⬥ Production note: **"Shout out to the iPhone 16 Pro's camera."** (quote)
- **[2025-05-15]** Verdict: a Model 3 is "probably a better deal," and **"for me personally, I like the
  Volvo EX30 a little more"** — but "MINI fans are going to love this… they've got a hit here. Well
  done, MINI — I never thought I'd say that." (quote)
