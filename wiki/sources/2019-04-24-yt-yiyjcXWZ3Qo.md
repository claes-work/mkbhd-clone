# Huawei P30 Pro Review: Optical Excellence! (@mkbhd, 2019-04-24)

## Summary
The full P30 Pro review — "the best phone not sold in the US, that's just facts." The durable payoff is the
crystallized framework: **there is more than one "best smartphone camera."** Also the RYYB night mode, the
periscope zoom (useful-vs-gimmick), and a new battery champ.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-04-24] **THE FRAMEWORK — "there is more than one best smartphone camera (and that's not a cop-out)."** "**If
  you want the absolute best image quality, it's the Pixel 3** (color science, DR, detail, white balance — in an
  aging, slow phone). **If you want the best/fastest low-light or the most versatile, it's the P30 Pro. If you want
  the best video, it's the iPhone XS. You have to know what you want** — that determines the best for you." A
  reusable, crystallized version of his camera-judgment thinking.
- [2019-04-24] **The night mode is the real game-changer.** "The **RYYB Super Spectrum sensor** (40% more light) —
  in **near-pitch-black, photos look like you shined a light into the scene; it beats the Pixel's Night Sight**
  (class-leading until now). The catch: it's **auto-mode always**, so dim bar shots can come out too bright." His
  standout praise.
- [2019-04-24] **The periscope zoom — "borderline useful-vs-gimmick."** "**5× optical → 50× 'super creep mode'** (the
  periscope bends light down + a mirror). It works, but **I topped out at ~10× before it got soft and gimmicky.**
  Funny: knowing I had it, I took **way more zoomed photos than usual.** The **ultra-wide was far more useful.**"
  Honest about the hype feature.
- [2019-04-24] **Battery — "the new undisputed heavyweight champ."** "The 4,200 mAh cell — **I end days at 40–45%,
  7+ hrs screen-on; a comfortable 2-day phone for light users. I don't even think about charging.**" (Huawei's
  aggressive background-killing is "fine because apps reopen fast.")
- [2019-04-24] **Software = EMUI, the weak point.** "Great features (**dark mode everywhere**; a great one-handed
  swipe gesture) but the settings are cluttered, there's bloat, the notification-group **requires an extra tap**,
  and a **home-button-doesn't-respond bug hits me multiple times a day.** It's **propped up AND held back by the
  software — the UX isn't the focus. Samsung fixed this with One UI; Huawei is due for that.**" His recurring
  near-stock/UX critique.
- [2019-04-24] **Daytime photos + the verdict.** Daytime is "coming down from hyper-over-processed to a more
  toned-down realistic look" (like the Mate 20 Pro), but with a **magenta cast on skin that's hard to edit out** —
  "still not quite the best image quality." Verdict: "**the best phone not sold in the US** — get it if you want
  great battery + a fast versatile low-light camera; the Pixel still wins pure image quality, the iPhone wins video."
