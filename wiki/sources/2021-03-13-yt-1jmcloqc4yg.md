# The Smartphone With a Microscope Camera?! (@mkbhd, 2021-03-13)

## Summary
A look at the Oppo Find X3 Pro built around the **feature-vs-gimmick debate**. The durable framings: **not all
gimmicks are created equal** — the 60x **microscope camera is "one of the most fun gimmicks I've ever seen"**
because Oppo put real effort into it (not a cheap padded macro); **"I hate BAD macro cameras, not the idea of a
good one"**; and **a gimmick becomes a feature by iterating on it.** Plus **ColorOS has risen to near the top of
his favorite Android skins** (a hot take), and the billion-color 10-bit display is a real (if niche) feature.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-03-13] **The frame — feature vs. gimmick, the way every company chases differentiation.** "'Every flagship
  is great now, so companies need **one more thing to give you a reason to buy** — a 100x zoom, a stylus, gaming
  buttons: **gimmick or feature? A lot is up to you to decide.**' Enter the Find X3 Pro — the flagship with a
  microscope.'"
- [2021-03-13] **The design — "own the camera bump" (like the S21).** "'The whole back is **one seamless piece of
  glass curved up into the camera module** (~40 hours to make one sheet) — worth it. **Same reason I like the S21
  designs: intentional about embracing the cameras, versus a bump that looks stapled on.**'"
- [2021-03-13] **HOT TAKE — ColorOS is now near the top of his favorite Android skins.** "'This might be a hot
  take, but **ColorOS has risen to near the top of my favorite versions of Android — I'd put it right above
  OxygenOS.** A big part is the animation/physics — "snappy, smooth, responsive" — plus Google feed + stock-app
  integration.'" A notable, dated software-preference shift.
- [2021-03-13] **The billion-color / 10-bit display — a feature (niche).** "'"First billion-color smartphone" is
  a sexy tagline for **a 10-bit display (1.07 billion colors vs 8-bit's 16.7 million). The impressive part isn't
  the panel — it's the pipeline preserving 10-bit from capture through to display,** which reduces banding for
  pro photo/video work. But it's off by default; **a very small sliver will use it — feature, but niche.**'"
- [2021-03-13] **THE MICROSCOPE — "I hate BAD macro cameras, not the idea of a good one."** "'To clarify how I
  feel about macro cameras: **I hate BAD macro cameras — a cheap 2 MP sensor slapped on to look premium, a waste
  of money. The idea of a GOOD macro camera could be fun.** This is a **60x microscope** (1–3 mm from the subject,
  a ring light so you don't shadow it) — "one of the most fun gimmicks I've ever seen in a phone." **Oppo put real
  time, effort, and money into a much-better-than-average experience, and it shows.**'" A key nuance of his
  long-standing anti-padded-camera stance.
- [2021-03-13] **GIMMICK → FEATURE — the test is iteration.** "'**One of the biggest differences between a gimmick
  and a feature is iterating and keeping going with it** — making a new version (maybe a future dual-aperture f8
  microscope for more in-focus). It's still a gimmick, but one of the most fun ones — and I'm glad they did it.'"
  A promotable gimmick-vs-feature criterion. (Note: **charger included in the box** — "in 2021 you actually have
  to say it.")'"
