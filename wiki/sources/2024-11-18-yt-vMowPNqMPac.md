# The Best EV You Can't Buy (Li Auto Mega)

## Summary

Marques tests the **Li Auto Mega** — a Chinese three-row electric minivan **you can't buy in the
US** — and calls it **"the most features, the most tech I think I've ever seen or experienced in an
EV."** Built around simplicity, luxury, and family: a "Magic Carpet" air suspension he ranks with
the Rolls-Royce Spectre and Maybach, ~350mi range, claimed ~500kW charging, second-row nap mode,
gesture-controlled screens, a fridge/heater. It's a window into how far ahead China's EV space has
gotten. Fully Marques-narrated.

## Key claims

- **[2024-11-18]** ⭐ **"This thing has the most features, the most tech I think I've ever seen or
  experienced in an EV… all these things would be thousands of dollars of options on basically any
  other car I review, but they're all part of the Mega."** Est. equivalent ~$60–80k (not sold here).
  (quote)
- **[2024-11-18]** The **"Magic Carpet" air suspension**: **"I'm not exaggerating — I think it's the
  softest, best-riding air suspension I've ever driven. I'm thinking of things like the Rolls-Royce
  Spectre, the Maybach."** ~350mi range; **claimed ~500kW charging (10–80% in ~10 min), "basically the
  fastest-charging EV in the world."** (quote)
- **[2024-11-18]** The tech tour: dual 3K OLED touchscreens ("not a lot of cars are more responsive"),
  a GPT-powered assistant, heated/ventilated/massaging seats front and second row, **a second-row
  fridge/heater** (for baby formula), **gesture-controlled rear screen**, Sentry-mode dashcams, 21
  speakers w/ Dolby Atmos, and **"nap mode"** (seats recline, shades rise, forest sounds, a countdown
  timer). "That's just the front seat" (recurring beat). (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2024-11-18]** ⚠️ Caveats: much of the UI is in Chinese/kilometers, not networked in the US (maps/
  streaming won't load). He also shows the **Li Auto L9** (the same three-row interior in a more
  traditional SUV shape) — notably an **EREV/series hybrid**: "always driving on EV power, but the gas
  engine charges the battery… that constant peace of mind." (paraphrase)
- **[2024-11-18]** ⭐ Bigger point: **"it really has opened our eyes to what's happening overseas in
  the electric vehicle space."** Asks viewers whether they'd pick this over a Model X or R1S at ~$80k.
  (quote)
