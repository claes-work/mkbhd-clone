# Samsung Galaxy Nope 20! (@mkbhd, 2020-08-26)

## Summary
A pointed review of the $999 (b&h ~$1,086 w/ tax) non-Ultra Note 20 — titled "Galaxy **Nope** 20," "an episode
of **Nope Tech**." A running rhetorical structure: **every meaningful feature is cut down a bit, but none of
those is the worst part** — the worst part is the **$1,000 launch price.** The phone itself is unique and fine;
the price + Note branding make it a confusing, bad deal. It **should have been ~$700.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-08-26] **The confused-lineup thesis — Samsung never decided if this was premium.** "'It feels like
  **Samsung never made up their mind on whether this was supposed to be a premium phone.** Every meaningful
  feature is cut down a bit from the Ultra — 1080p/60 Hz screen, smaller cut-down camera array, 128 GB
  non-expandable, plastic — but the Snapdragon 865+ keeps it "still high-end." **They cut down too far.**'"
- [2020-08-26] **The "worst part" refrain — it's never the specs, it's the price.** "'The 1080p/60 Hz screen
  on a $1,000 flagship? Not the worst part. The 26 ms pen? Not the worst part. The cut-down cameras, storage,
  plastic build? **Not the worst part. The worst part about the Galaxy Note 20 is the launch price they picked
  of a thousand dollars** — as flossy Carter would say, the price is too damn high.'" A rhetorically structured
  value verdict.
- [2020-08-26] **"Plastic has its place" — a defense, not a knock.** "'Plastic gets a bad rap, and I've been
  guilty of it, but **plastic has its place** — on a Pixel 4a you'll never hear me complain. As Zack (JerryRig)
  says, glass is glass and glass breaks; **plastic is lighter, makes this unwieldy phone more manageable, and
  is less likely to shatter.** When 80% of you use a case anyway, it makes sense as a place to save money.'"
  Nuances his historical materials snobbery.
- [2020-08-26] **The verdict + the two paths Samsung should have taken.** "'Remove the price and the Note name
  and it's a pretty unique phone (S Pen, wireless DeX). **It should have been ~$700, not $1,000.** Two better
  paths: (1) **don't cut down as much and earn the $1,000** (add 120 Hz + more base storage), or (2) **commit
  to a cheaper Note** — swap the 865+ for a 765G, make it a $700 "Note 20 Lite" that would've eaten a lot of
  ~$700 phones alive (like the LG Velvet). They straddled and got a confusing bad deal.'"
- [2020-08-26] **Buy-advice — get an S20+ / OnePlus 8 Pro instead (unless you must have the S Pen).** "'I
  can't recommend most people buy this at this price as long as an **S20+ or OnePlus 8 Pro is more phone for
  the same or less.** But Samsung now promises **three major Android updates** starting with these flagships
  (the Note 10 won't get that), so if you're in the tiny sliver who must have the S Pen and can't spend $1,300,
  fine — otherwise, nope.'"
