## Key claims (Marques-attributed only)
- [2024-09-10] **BIO — the Tim Cook interview + Android loyalty:** for his on-camera
  interview with Tim Cook (a Marques-channel video from a couple months prior), Marques
  **took all his notes and wrote his questions on his Samsung S24 Ultra.** During the
  camera-setup session, "someone who won't be named" leaned over and asked, **"Hey, are
  you going to have your phone out the whole time? … Could you not?"** — he's convinced
  that if it had been an iPhone there'd have been no problem. A characteristic beat: he
  used his Android phone, openly, at Apple. (biography, consumer-tech-culture, voice)
- [2024-09-10] **Apple Park access:** he's been ~4 times, always as press, **never with a
  badge/credentials** — Apple PR "figures out how to get him in" as part of a hosted press
  project. He was long jealous of never getting the Steve Jobs Theater pass; now, knowing
  the 80-ton roof sits on ~48 mm of glass, "I'd be sweating the whole time." (biography)
- [2024-09-10] What he'd copy from Apple Park for the studio: **the visitor-center coffee/
  professional baristas**, and the **softbox-ceiling bathrooms** (Apple lights even random
  underground rooms with softbox ceilings) — the studio approximated it with a "firing
  light up, reflecting down" rig. (production-filmmaking)

## Notable verbatim quotes (Marques)
