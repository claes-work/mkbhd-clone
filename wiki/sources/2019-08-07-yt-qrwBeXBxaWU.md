# Samsung Galaxy Note 10/10+ Impressions: A Great Duo! (@mkbhd, 2019-08-07)

## Summary
Hands-on impressions of the two 2019 Notes. Two durable notes: the **headphone-jack removal is "a choice, not a
space issue,"** and Samsung's **two-tier segmentation deliberately pushes power users up to the 10+.** Also two
biography anchors — Retro Tech S1 wrapped, and the channel crossed 9M subscribers.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-08-07] **HEADPHONE JACK — "a constant choice," not a space issue.** "The Note is **Samsung's first
  flagship with no headphone jack.** 'They say it's an issue with space for a bigger battery — I say **if you can
  fit an entire pen in the side of the phone, you could have fit a headphone jack.** This is no longer a space
  issue — it's a constant choice they're making. It's the beginning of the end; **the headphone jack is leaving
  and it's not coming back.**'" A pointed, quotable stance.
- [2019-08-07] **The two-tier segmentation read.** "'If you paid attention, there's a bunch of little things that
  **push you toward the 10+**: 1440p (vs 1080p), an extra color, 5G only on the 10+, up to 12 GB (vs 8), 512 GB +
  microSD (vs 256/none), 45 W charging, extra depth sensors. Those extras make the **10+ the king of the hill —
  the flagship they want power users to spend extra on.**'" A crisp product-strategy observation.
- [2019-08-07] **The Bixby-button gripe (voice).** "'The power button is now **also the Bixby button** — did you
  not think I was gonna bring that up, Samsung? Hold it and it opens Bixby; it won't even turn off the phone.'"
  Recurring anti-Bixby humor.
- [2019-08-07] **The camera + S-Pen upgrades.** "Triple cameras (main + 2x tele + ultrawide, **video on the
  ultrawide again**), a **zoom-in mic** (mic array focuses as you zoom — 'a Spy Kids gadget'), and — the standout
  — **handwriting-to-text search** ('take a note with the stylus, then search the word you wrote and it shows
  up — super useful'). Air-action gestures 'file in the gimmicks folder.'"
- [2019-08-07] **The wish + the smaller-phone tease.** "'The one thing I wish they did to make it the "everything
  phone" is a **high-refresh display** (90/120 Hz) — not happening, still 60 Hz.' And 'this might be the **first
  time I actually really like the smaller phone** as far as feel in the hand' (foreshadows [[2019-08-27-yt-pyldH6sefTQ]])."
- [2019-08-07] **BIOGRAPHY anchors.** "'We've **finished shooting Retro Tech Season 1** — it's now in editing.'
  And 'the channel just **rolled over 9 million subscribers.**'" Two datable milestones (Aug 2019).
