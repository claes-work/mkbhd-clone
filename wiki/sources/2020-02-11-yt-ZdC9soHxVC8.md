# Galaxy S20 Impressions: New Year, New Samsung! (@mkbhd, 2020-02-11)

## Summary
First look at the Galaxy S20 / S20+ (the true S10 successors; the S20 Ultra is separate). "One of the biggest jumps
I've seen Samsung take" — big upgrades in the three things that matter most (screen, battery, cameras), plus his
running high-refresh advocacy paying off ("the train I told you flagships would all get on in 2020").

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-02-11] **HIGH REFRESH ARRIVES ON THE FLAGSHIPS (his 2019 prediction).** "'**120 Hz QHD OLED** — "this is the
  train I've been telling you flagships would all get on in 2020" — plus a **240 Hz touch sample rate**; instantly
  noticeably smoother, and **I can tell the difference vs 90 Hz.**' ⚠️ The asterisk: **120 Hz locks the resolution
  to 1080p** (out of the box it's 1440p/60 Hz)." His high-refresh advocacy vindicated, with the honest caveat.
- [2020-02-11] **"New decade, new Samsung" — the FLAT display.** "'The hole-punch is **center** (nothing's usually
  there — I prefer it) and the display is **much flatter** — so unlike Samsung, who always pushed the curved glass.
  Much more usable.'" His flat-over-curved preference finally met by Samsung.
- [2020-02-11] **5G is NOT the game-changer people think.** "'The whole lineup is 5G, but the S20/S20+ are **sub-6
  only (no mmWave) — up to ~20% faster than good 4G on a good day. I wouldn't buy this phone just for the 5G.**'"
  Consistent with his low-band-5G realism.
- [2020-02-11] **Cameras — Space Zoom + the 8K-video catch.** "'Bigger sensors across the board; **Space Zoom**
  (30x hybrid on the S20, 100x on the Ultra). **8K24 video — but the catch:** 8K needs a ≥33 MP sensor, so it comes
  from the **64 MP telephoto, not the 12 MP main** — it punches in, and **8K drops tracking autofocus/effects.** Not
  the dream of streaming 8K over 5G yet.'" His characteristic look-past-the-headline-spec analysis.
- [2020-02-11] **"I like taking the photos myself" (Single Take).** "'New **Single Take** — point, hit the shutter
  once, and it decides when to grab photos/videos. Seems cool, but **I like taking the photos myself.**' The button
  moved back to the right and **the Bixby button is gone.**" A small stated preference (manual control).
- [2020-02-11] **The verdict — most-improved front-runner.** "'Hard to find a weakness aside from price. **Too early
  to call Samsung a front-runner for most improved? I don't know.**'" A dated award-season note.
