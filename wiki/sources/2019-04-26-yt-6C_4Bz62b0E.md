# Samsung Galaxy Fold Review: We Gotta Talk! (@mkbhd, 2019-04-26)

## Summary
The (pre-)review of the ill-fated first Galaxy Fold — "the weirdest set of circumstances I've ever had to review a
phone." Amid the review-units-breaking saga, he lands a reusable **2-in-1 framework** and a full-throated defense
of first-gen experimentation.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-04-26] **"The weirdest circumstances I've ever reviewed a phone in."** "Hands-on → impressions → took one
  home → **broke it** → got it replaced → found **other reviewers' units broke** for related reasons → made a
  video → **now Samsung is delaying it and changing it before it ships.** So this is a **pre-review** of the rest of
  the execution." Frames the fiasco honestly.
- [2019-04-26] **The fragility — the hinge + the soft plastic screen.** "It's fragile — you feel it. **Glass doesn't
  fold, so the inner screen is soft plastic** (the part Samsung's changing); the **hinge is a complex gear mechanism
  where dust/dirt can slide in and break the display.** Rated for 200,000 folds by a robot — but **you can wobble it
  with your bare hands. I'm terrified for the JerryRig test — I'd bet real money it snaps in half.**" Blunt about the
  first-gen weak points.
- [2019-04-26] **The crease — a notch-like split.** "**People complaining about the crease are mostly watching a
  video of it; people who *have* one mostly say it's not a big deal. I'm in the middle** — like a notch: stare at it
  (light wallpaper, off-axis) and it's bad; focus on content straight-on and I don't see it." A media-literate read.
- [2019-04-26] **What's good — the S10 DNA + continuity.** Shares the S10's specs/materials; **stereo speakers
  actually better than the S10** ("a hidden advantage of a giant device — the speakers are far apart"). **Continuity
  (small screen → big screen and back) works better than any other folding-phone demo** and is fast. A neat
  observation: **the tiny front screen means "you don't get lost mindlessly scrolling Instagram"** — its badness is
  almost a feature. (The Bixby button under the fingerprint reader is **"straight up rude"** — you trigger it
  constantly folding/unfolding.)
- [2019-04-26] **THE 2-IN-1 FRAMEWORK — better than, or cheaper than.** "For a 2-in-1 to be worth it, it must be
  **better than or cheaper than buying both things.** Convertible laptops are a better laptop, a better tablet, or
  cheaper than both. **The Fold is not a better phone than most, not a better tablet than most, and at $2,000 sure
  isn't cheaper than an S10 + a tablet. So I won't tell you to buy it — but go try one in a store.**" A clean,
  reusable framework.
- [2019-04-26] **The defense of first-gen experimentation.** "**A big screen that folds into your pocket feels like
  the future** — this is one of the few **truly new form factors** in a long time. **It's so easy to hate on ($2,000,
  a hinge, breaking) — but for those of us who get excited about new tech, this having this much promise this early
  is exciting. I'm thankful Samsung jumped in early. This is everything the LG Voyager ever wanted to be** — and if
  phones improved as much as they did from the Voyager to now, I'm excited for what folding phones become." His
  champion-the-risk-taker value, fully stated.
