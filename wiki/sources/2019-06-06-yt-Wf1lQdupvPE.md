# Top 5 iOS 13 Features! (@mkbhd, 2019-06-06)

## Summary
WWDC 2019 coverage — his top 5 iOS 13 features from the beta. Dark mode is #1; the sleeper standout is the
privacy-first **Sign in with Apple.** Format note: a "so you don't have to" service-listicle framing.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-06-06] **Dark mode is #1 — the most-anticipated feature.** "System-wide dark mode got the loudest cheer.
  The default all-white iOS 'can be blinding.' It only affects stock apps (third-party apps toggle themselves)
  but the quick toggle is easy, and since **iPhones use OLED, black pixels save a little battery.**"
- [2019-06-06] **Sign in with Apple — a privacy standout.** "'A really impressive dedication to shielding your
  information from the companies that keep asking for your login' — it **generates a random dedicated email each
  time** so they can't grab yours." Flags Apple's privacy positioning approvingly (a recurring MKBHD lens).
- [2019-06-06] **The Photos overhaul — video editing parity.** "The best part: the same edits (color, contrast,
  sharpness, noise reduction) now **apply to video**, and you can finally **rotate a video** (impossible in iOS
  12). 'I hope Google Photos builds this in eventually.'"
- [2019-06-06] **The "new" keyboard — Android had it a decade ago.** "Swipe typing is now built in — 'new in air
  quotes because Android's had this for maybe a decade now.'" His characteristic cross-platform honesty about who
  did it first.
- [2019-06-06] **The methodology aside — "I sat through the keynote so you don't have to."** "I installed the
  'super buggy' beta so you don't have to; the '2× faster app launch' claim is 'tough to see because the beta is
  so buggy' — I can't verify it." Careful about not over-claiming from a beta.
