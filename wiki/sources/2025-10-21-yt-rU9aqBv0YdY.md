# What's Going on with Apple Vision Pro? ★★

## Summary

Ostensibly a review of the M5 Vision Pro (a new chip + a new Dual Knit Band, otherwise
identical to the 2-year-old original), this is really a **tech-industry-commentary landmark**
on the state of face computers. Marques argues the interesting story isn't the spec bump —
it's the **competition and the strange lack of it**, and the way **smart glasses have raced
past headsets**. Two years in, the Vision Pro turned out to be "watching movies and gathering
dust"; Apple has reportedly scrapped a lightweight "Vision Pro Air" and shifted resources to
smart glasses — validating his Oct-2023 "build-down vs build-up" framing. ★★.

## Key claims

- **[2025-10-21]** The M5 Vision Pro is "more or less the same as the original from two years
  ago" — same dimensions, frame, displays, cameras, R1, battery, **same heavy weight, same
  $3,500 price**. The **M5 chip** brings incremental wins (faster app loading, ~10% more pixels
  you "really aren't gonna notice," 120Hz vs 100Hz, ~20% longer battery). (paraphrase)
- **[2025-10-21]** "100% of their effort" on comfort went into the new **Dual Knit Band** —
  cleverly engineered, distributes weight across the head, with **tungsten counterweight** in
  the back (the reason the listed weight went *up*); "significantly more comfortable" and
  sold separately for $99 for old units. (paraphrase)
- **[2025-10-21]** Why it's interesting: **"the competition and the strange lack of competition."**
  Samsung finally shipped its Android XR headset (Project Moohan), but **almost no one else is
  chasing a premium $3,500 XR headset** — and that tracks with the explosion in **smart glasses**.
  (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-10-21]** The pivotal comparison: the **Meta Ray-Ban Display** progress "makes the
  Vision Pro look like it's sitting still," and glasses "are much more appealing to way more
  people" because they carry **a much lesser social penalty** in public while still giving a
  first-person camera, music, questions, directions. "Yes, they are still nerdy, but not as
  nerdy as this." (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-10-21]** Two years of data: **"most of the use of Vision Pro has been watching movies
  and gathering dust, which is a shame because it's capable of so, so much more."** He reads
  Apple as having seen the sales/usage numbers, concluded "this isn't gonna be some gigantic
  smash hit," and **trimmed it back** — reportedly **scrapping a lightweight "Vision Pro Air"**
  and **shifting resources to develop smart glasses** to jump on that train. (quote)
- **[2025-10-21]** This validates his **October 2023 framing**: companies chase the face-computer
  future from **two directions — building big headsets and trimming them down, or building smart
  glasses up** — and "maybe, just maybe, one of those directions has way more traction now than
  the other." The reason: **far fewer people want to spend significant time inside a face-computer**,
  however impressive; "there's just nothing quite like having actual reality through some glass
  with some stuff overlaid on top." (paraphrase + quote)
