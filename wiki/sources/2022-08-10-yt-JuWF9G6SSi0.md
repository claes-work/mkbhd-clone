# Samsung Z Fold 4/ Flip 4 Impressions + Watch 5 Pro! (@mkbhd, 2022-08-10)

## Summary
First hands-on with Samsung's 2022 foldables and watches. The theme for the phones is **refinement** — Samsung
**dominates the (still-young) foldable space** (the Flip is the most popular folding phone in the world), so the
Flip 4 and Fold 4 are subtle updates: trimmer bezels/hinges, more durability, an 8+ Gen 1 (likely the biggest
year-over-year gain is **battery life**), and one new software feature each. The **Flip 4's** is more cover-screen
widgets/lock-screen customization; the **Fold 4's** is a **Windows-like taskbar/dock** on the inside screen (big for
quick multitasking) plus a **2nd-gen under-display selfie camera** (hides better, but the photos are still the weak
point). Same prices ($999 / $1,799) — so **"if you weren't convinced on foldables, this won't change your mind"**;
they're **"settling into their form."** The surprise standout for him is the **Watch 5 Pro** (a fitness/adventure
watch with an ~80-hour battery) — right ahead of the rumored Apple Watch Pro.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-08-10] **Refinement — Samsung dominates foldables.** "'The theme is refinement (as it was the last two
  years). In the young foldable world, **Samsung is dominating — the Flip is apparently the most popular folding
  phone in the world.** The **Flip 4** is very much a Flip 3 with three subtle changes: slight aesthetic trimming,
  a bit more durability (a trimmer, still-sturdy hinge), and updated specs — and **the most noticeable improvement
  might be battery life** (the efficient 8+ Gen 1 + a bump to 3,700 mAh). One big software feature: more cover-
  screen widgets/lock-screen customization.'"
- [2022-08-10] **The Fold 4 taskbar — the one I'm most interested in.** "'The **Fold 4** (the one I'm most
  interested in) gets a bit more: a slimmer hinge (satisfying to clap shut), Gorilla Glass Victus+, a new camera
  (a 50 MP primary + a 3x tele up to 30x space zoom — probably ~S22+ quality), and **one big software feature: a
  Windows-like taskbar/dock on the inside screen** that shrinks to the bottom — easy quick-launch and jumping
  straight into two/three-app multitasking. **On a 7" inner screen I just want to multitask — the taskbar makes a
  big difference.**'"
- [2022-08-10] **The 2nd-gen under-display camera — hides better, still bad photos.** "'The **2nd-gen under-display
  selfie camera** hides itself even more impressively than last year — **but the images still don't look any
  better** (that was the weakness last year and it appears to be the weakness again; a video call won't look
  pretty). The crease is a touch less visible than on the Flip (you don't drag your finger over it while
  scrolling, unless you're a heavy S-Pen user).'"
- [2022-08-10] **Same prices — it won't change your mind; settling into its form.** "'Same prices — **$999 Flip /
  $1,799 Fold.** So **if you weren't convinced on foldables (the crease, having to open it) this won't change your
  mind** — but for $999 the Flip folds in half and is the most appealing folding phone to most people. Nothing
  fundamentally different across the board — **it's very much the productivity-tablet-in-your-pocket, just settling
  into its form** (a different shape would be a whole new model).'"
- [2022-08-10] **The surprise standout — the Watch 5 Pro.** "'Low-key the most interesting new hardware is the
  watches — right ahead of a rumored Apple Watch / Apple Watch Pro. The **Watch 5** has a flatter contact area for
  more accurate readings, sapphire glass, sleep stages. The **Watch 5 Pro** is a thicker, ruggedized fitness/
  adventure version (titanium bezels, a 590 mAh cell) promising **~80 hours of battery — that's what I'm curious to
  test.** ($279 / $449.) (I haven't reviewed many smartwatches — leave a comment if you want that.)'"
