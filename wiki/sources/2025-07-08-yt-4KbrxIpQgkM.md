# Nothing Phone 3 Review: They Lied! ★★

## Summary

A pointed review of the Nothing Phone 3, marketed by Nothing as its first "true
flagship." Marques builds the review around a memorable evolutionary metaphor —
**carcinisation**, the tendency of unrelated crustaceans to independently evolve toward
crab-like forms — arguing Nothing set out to make something distinctive but keeps
converging on the same crab everyone else makes. He picks apart the ways the phone is
flagship "in marketing" but cut in the details, and lands the thesis that **"flagship is
a marketing term."** A landmark for his design-company critique of Nothing and his
skepticism of spec-sheet branding. ★★ L3-candidate.

## Key claims

- **[2025-07-08]** Frames the phone via **"carcinisation"** — the real biological
  phenomenon where different crustaceans independently evolve into crab-like shapes —
  as a metaphor for phones all converging on the same form. "You want lobster, not
  another crab." (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-07-08]** Nothing calls it a **true flagship**, but Marques catalogs the
  **flagship-cut playbook**: a Snapdragon **8s Gen 4** (not the 8 Elite), **Gorilla
  Glass 7i** (not Victus), and an **LTPS** display (not LTPO). Flagship label,
  mid-tier-flagship internals. (paraphrase)
- **[2025-07-08]** The signature new feature — the **Glyph Matrix**, a small 489-LED
  dot-matrix display on the back — is "surrounded by gimmicks" (mini-games, a spin-the-
  bottle widget) rather than a clear utility. (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-07-08]** **Essential Space** (Nothing's AI capture feature) "doesn't sync, so
  I don't use it." (quote)
- **[2025-07-08]** Thesis: **"Flagship is a marketing term."** The gap between the
  marketing claim and the componentry is the story. (quote)
