# Apple Vision Pro Unboxing! (@mkbhd, 2024-01-30)

## Summary
The unboxing of the **$3,500 Apple Vision Pro** (his is the **$3,900 1 TB**), "the beginning of Apple's spatial
computing journey" and of his own Vision Pro video series. In the box: the headset (with the commercial-famous
**Solo Knit Band** attached, plus the over-the-top **Dual Loop Band** — Apple ships both because of the weight), a
thin and a thick **Light Seal Cushion** (which is fitted via an **iPhone Face-ID face scan** — not molded, but
sized from presets), the **Zeiss optical inserts** for glasses-wearers ($100 readers / $150 prescription, magnetic),
a **Vision Pro polishing cloth**, and the **external battery** (a heavy 3,166 mAh, ~2.5–3 hours, a proprietary
connector into the headset, charged over USB-C via a 30 W brick). He notes the front is all glass ("you can imagine
why people carry the cloth"), the whole computer is small but very front-heavy, and — trying both bands — the **Dual
Loop is immediately far more comfortable** ("much more well distributed"). He defends the mocked **$200 travel
case**: "I wouldn't travel with this without it, and I do think people will want to travel with it — the number-one
use case I'm looking forward to is airplanes." (The full review is teased as the next video; Anker sponsor.)

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2024-01-30] **$3,500 (this is the $3,900 1 TB) — the start of the series.** "'This is a representation of what
  you'd get for $3,500 — or in this case $3,900, because this is the 1 TB Vision Pro. This is very much the
  beginning of our Apple Vision Pro investigation and series of videos.'"
- [2024-01-30] **Two bands — the Dual Loop is far more comfortable.** "'The Solo Knit Band is the one in all the
  commercials — soft, quick to adjust. But there's been a lot of talk about the weight, which is why Apple includes
  a second Dual Loop Band (over the top and behind your head). Trying it — that's so much more well distributed;
  much easier to wear for longer. I'm probably using that more often.'"
- [2024-01-30] **The face scan and the Light Seal.** "'Everyone goes through a face-scanning process with the
  iPhone's Face ID sensors, and you get a custom-fit Light Seal — not exactly molded, but they have preset sizes and
  the scan picks the best match for the best immersion. You also get a thin and a thick Light Seal Cushion.'"
- [2024-01-30] **The heavy external battery.** "'The battery is 3,166 mAh — 2.5-to-3 hours — and it's pretty heavy;
  I know phones with 5,000 mAh batteries that feel much lighter, so I'm curious why this feels so heavy. A
  proprietary connector plugs into the headset; you charge it over USB-C with a 30 W brick (not super fast).'"
- [2024-01-30] **Defending the $200 case — the airplane use case.** "'People made fun of the $200 accessory case,
  but I wouldn't want to travel with this without it, and I do think people will want to travel with it. The
  number-one use case I've been looking forward to is airplanes — I'll need a big backpack, but I'm going to try
  that.'"
