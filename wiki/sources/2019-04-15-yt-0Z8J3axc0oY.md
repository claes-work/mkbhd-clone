# Samsung Galaxy Fold Impressions! (@mkbhd, 2019-04-15)

## Summary
First hands-on with the original Galaxy Fold ($1,980) — before the review units started breaking. "More like a
folding tablet than a folding phone." Comes away "pretty impressed," with the front screen as the main gripe.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-04-15] **"A folding tablet, not a folding phone."** "A **4.6" cover display** (tall, narrow, big bezels) +
  a **7.3" 4:3 inner OLED.** It's **chunky** — the feel/weight of two narrow Samsung phones back-to-back with a
  hinge; it doesn't fold fully flat (a small triangle gap)." Names the form factor honestly.
- [2019-04-15] **The crease + the satisfying close.** "**Yes you can see the crease** off-axis or with a light
  wallpaper, and feel it — but straight-on you forget it (much better than the Royole FlexPai). The **magnetic
  'thunk' close is extremely satisfying** (strong enough to pick up paper clips). **Opening/closing is a two-handed
  operation.**" Balanced first read.
- [2019-04-15] **The quirks.** A **notch + no headphone jack** (but comes with the Galaxy Buds); a fingerprint
  reader that **doubles as a Bixby button — easy to trigger by accident**; **6 camera sensors**; a **split battery**
  (one cell in each half, 4,380 mAh total); SD855, 12 GB, 512 GB.
- [2019-04-15] **The two folding features — continuity + multi-window.** "**Continuity** (open an app on the small
  screen, unfold, and it picks up where you left off — Play Store, Maps) **works fast and surprisingly well.**
  **Multi-window** lets you run up to **8 apps** at once ("I don't know who's doing that much multitasking on a 7"
  screen — that person needs a tablet — but it's cool they don't limit you")."
- [2019-04-15] **The verdict — a step toward the future.** "If I nitpick, the **front screen is the main issue —
  small, big bezels, hard to type on.** But **even if you never use it, a big screen that folds in half and fits in
  your pocket — that's the part that feels like the future** (a better viewfinder/browser/video experience). **A
  step in the right direction.**" Optimistic (a full review would follow — and the breaking saga).

## Notable verbatim quotes
