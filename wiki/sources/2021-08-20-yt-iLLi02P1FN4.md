# Samsung Z Fold 3 Review: Let's Talk Ambition! (@mkbhd, 2021-08-20)

## Summary
Full review of the $1,800 Z Fold 3, built around a promotable frame: **ambition.** The Fold's goal (a normal phone
AND a mini-tablet in your pocket) is far higher than the Flip's (a normal phone that folds smaller) — and **because
its ambition is so high, the Fold 3 is actually worse at some normal-phone things** (an awkward size, a cramped
cover keyboard, falls out of car mounts, worse battery, B+ cameras) **on its way to doing something no other phone
can.** The verdict: the second-best foldable (the Flip is more recommendable), but the one he loves for its
ambition — and **"Samsung should keep the Note."**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-08-20] **THE AMBITION FRAME.** "'The **Flip 3 has one goal — be as close to a normal phone as possible, then
  fold smaller. The Fold wants to be a normal phone AND give you a mini-tablet screen — a much bigger ambition. And
  because the ambition is so high, the Fold 3 is actually worse at some normal-phone things on its way to doing
  things no other phone can.**'" A promotable way to evaluate ambitious hardware.
- [2021-08-20] **Worse at normal-phone things — the cost of ambition.** "'Used closed, **the cover screen is
  better than ever (corner-to-corner, 120 Hz) but narrow — a cramped keyboard, awkward for video.** It's much
  thicker, the camera bump rocks it, it **falls out of most car mounts, and it's an absolute unit in your
  pocket.** Objectively an awkward size/shape for regular use.'"
- [2021-08-20] **The build + IPX8 (dust is still the killer).** "'The folding hardware is better than ever — the
  hinge protrudes less, feels firmer; the whole phone (hinge included) is **IPX8 water resistant** (they sealed
  the components, not the hinge) — **but IPX8 isn't dust resistance; keep it away from a beach or a woodworking
  shop.** B+ cameras (last year's Fold 2 set). The biggest sacrifice: **battery** — 4,500 → 4,400 mAh powering a
  120 Hz cover + inside screen; I was near-dead by end of day (~4 hrs screen-on).'"
- [2021-08-20] **The payoff — the inside screen no other phone can offer.** "'All the sacrifices are for **that
  big, corner-to-corner, uninterrupted 7.6" inside display — something no other pocketable device offers.** I feel
  more productive; flex modes (a YouTube video propped up on a table); and **a pinnable app dock on the right, like
  a desktop OS.** The under-display selfie camera is decently hidden but the photos are mediocre (dehazing) — use
  the hole-punch or the main cameras. A Labs feature can force any app into the aspect ratio (Instagram finally
  fills it).'"
- [2021-08-20] **S Pen — great, but "keep the Note."** "'S Pen support finally (30%-harder film) — but a special
  **$50 S Pen Fold Edition (soft retractable tip) + an $80 case to carry it** — a lot of extra work for a screen
  barely bigger than the Note 20 Ultra. **The best S Pen experience is still the Galaxy Note, where it lives inside
  the phone — Samsung should keep the Note around.**'"
- [2021-08-20] **THE VERDICT — high ambition, but not the best in any single area.** "'If you weren't willing to
  deal with the Fold 1/2's shortcomings, the Fold 3 won't change your mind. **With $1,800 you'd want the best in as
  many areas as possible — best specs (yes, easy), but also best cameras/battery/ergonomics — and this phone
  straight up doesn't have those** (even Samsung's own lineup does more). **The trade-off: you get one thing no
  other phone has. Fold 3 gets closest to that high ambition — and I love that about it.**'"
