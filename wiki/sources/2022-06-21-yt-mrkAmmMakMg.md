# The Nothing Phone LOOKS Different (@mkbhd, 2022-06-21)

## Summary
A first look at the back of the Nothing Phone (1) — the startup's debut phone (after the Ear 1 buds). His frame:
in an age when the top phones all look the same (glass slabs made to appeal to the masses), **a startup that made
another average phone would give no reason to switch — so Nothing made a phone that stands out.** The standout is
the **Glyph interface**: 900+ LEDs behind a transparent back — **"the ultimate notification LED"** (a dying breed),
plus a USB-C charging progress bar (wiggle to check), a camera fill light, and **Glyph ringtones** (each with a
matching light pattern, so you know who's calling by the light even when muted). The durable industry read: **this
"stand out with a cool design/feature" strategy is a customer-acquisition cost — steal a small slice of the most
loyal customers, then broaden phone by phone until you've made a mass-appeal phone that sells the most but abandons
your original fanbase — "sound familiar?"** (the OnePlus playbook).

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-06-21] **Standing out is the point.** "'Most top phones are designed to appeal to the masses, so they all
  look the same — a glass slab. **A startup making another average phone would fit in, but give no reason to switch.
  So Nothing made a phone that stands out** with cool features around a unique look. It looks like a movie prop in
  certain light.'"
- [2022-06-21] **The Glyph interface — "the ultimate notification LED."** "'The standout is the **Glyph interface:
  900+ LEDs across the transparent back** (around the cameras, the wireless-charging pad, the corner). **It's the
  ultimate notification LED** (a dying breed) — it pulses for notifications, glows while (reverse) wireless
  charging, and acts as **a USB-C charging progress bar (wiggle the phone to check).** It can also fire as a camera
  fill light, and there's a recording indicator.'"
- [2022-06-21] **Glyph ringtones — the impressive part.** "'The most impressive part: **10 built-in ringtones, each
  with a matching light pattern**, pairable with contacts — **so even muted, you know who's calling by the light**
  (and different patterns per app). A genuinely new smartphone feature, which we don't see often.'"
- [2022-06-21] **The strategy — customer acquisition, then broaden (sound familiar?).** "'Does looking different
  actually work? **It's a real strategy — like the Naruto / League of Legends / Dragon Ball Z collab phones:
  standing out to steal a small slice of the most loyal customers is literally a customer-acquisition cost.** If
  the first run succeeds, make a slightly more broadly-appealing phone, then more, then more — **until you've made a
  very broadly-appealing phone that sells the most but abandons your original fan base. Sound familiar?**' (the
  OnePlus arc.)'"
