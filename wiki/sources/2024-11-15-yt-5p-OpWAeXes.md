## Summary

Co-host **Miles** reviews the new **plug-in-hybrid BMW M5** and answers the "have they ruined it?"
question with a qualified no: it's much heavier (~5,400lb) but delivers 717hp, is one of the most
comfortable/livable modern M cars, and does everything an M5 fundamentally should (high-speed
Autobahn cruising in comfort). Attributed entirely to Miles.

## Key claims (all Miles — context only, NOT persona data)

- **[2024-11-15]** *(Miles)* The weight (~5,400lb) is real but "99% of buyers aren't tracking these";
  717hp / 738 lb-ft (up from ~620) but ~3.4s 0–60 (about the same as the last CS) because of the
  weight. Plug-in hybrid: 14.8kWh, ~25mi real EV range. (paraphrase)
- **[2024-11-15]** *(Miles)* Highlights: X-Drive lets you switch to rear-wheel-drive; one of the most
  comfortable/livable M cars ever; good Bowers & Wilkins; carbon roof. "Would I personally buy one?
  Probably not, but it does everything an M5 is supposed to." (paraphrase)
