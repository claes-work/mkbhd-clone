# Google Pixel Fold Impressions! $1800 of Pixel?! (@mkbhd, 2023-05-10)

## Summary
A first hands-on with **Google's first foldable** — "a Pixel with a folding screen inside." Three categories that
matter (design, hardware specs, software) plus the overshadowing **price**. **Design:** a compact book-style fold
(like the Oppo Find N2 but a bit bigger and thinner), with a notable engineering choice — **the hinge parts sit
above and below the fold rather than behind it**, which makes the whole phone thin but adds **extra bezel above and
below the inner display** (the internet "will freak out about the bezels"; he's fine with them — room for a good
inner selfie camera and a thumb rest). **Hardware:** mostly-flagship (a bright 5.8" outer + 7.6" 120 Hz inner
display, Tensor G2, 12 GB), but the **thinness trade-off means a smaller 4,821 mAh battery than the Pixel 7 Pro's
5,000 — with a bigger, brighter screen — so battery is the open question.** **Software** is the Pixel strength ("the
smartest smartphone"), with new large-screen work (a long-press dock, 50+ optimized Google apps). The catch:
**$1,799** — "1,800 bucks is a lot" — **"a tough sell for anyone who isn't specifically interested in the Pixel
software experience"** (he's one of those people), the same tough sell as the 7 Pro; is it another first-gen phone
"people probably shouldn't buy"?

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2023-05-10] **The hinge choice makes it thin but adds bezel.** "'Google put a lot of the folding hardware above
  and below the middle instead of behind it — that's what makes the hinge and the whole phone so thin, but it also
  means more bezel above and below the inner display. People will freak out about the bezels — that's one thing you
  can count on from the internet — but I'm totally fine with it: it lets them put a good inner selfie camera in the
  bezel and gives you something to rest your thumb on.'"
- [2023-05-10] **The thinness-vs-battery trade-off is the big question.** "'It's a little bigger and a little thinner
  than the Find N2, which naturally means not so much battery. It has a 4,821 mAh battery — the largest ever in a
  foldable — but the Pixel 7 Pro has 5,000 mAh, the same Tensor G2, and only average battery life. A smaller battery
  and a much bigger, brighter screen gives me pause; battery is the big question in my head.'"
- [2023-05-10] **The Pixel software strength, extended to a big screen.** "'It has all the Pixel stuff that makes it
  the smartest smartphone. On the inside it's basically a tablet, and Android tablets have always been lacking in
  software — but there's a new long-press dock, and they've optimized ~50 Google apps for the larger display
  (multi-column layouts, not stretched phone apps). Good news on software, TBD when we test it.'"
- [2023-05-10] **The price overshadows it — a tough sell outside the Pixel faithful.** "'$1,799 — 1,800 bucks is a
  lot for a phone at the top of the market (the same price the Fold 4 launched at, which has since dropped). From
  first impressions this is a tough sell for anyone not specifically interested in the Pixel software experience —
  the same tough sell the 7 Pro was — except the 7 Pro undercut the Ultras, and this is the same price as the best
  flagship folding phones.'"
