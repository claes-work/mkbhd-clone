# Hyundai Ioniq 9 - A Killer 3-Row with NACS!

## Summary

Marques lives with the **Hyundai Ioniq 9** for a week and calls it **the best bang-for-buck in the
crowded three-row electric SUV segment** — "a really well-specced Ioniq 9 for under 80 grand" vs.
six-figure rivals (Lucid Gravity, Rivian R1S, EQS, Cadillac Escalade IQ). Well-equipped, honest
~320–340mi range, and it's **one of the first non-Teslas he's tested with a native NACS port**
(plug into some Superchargers with no adapter). Fully Marques-narrated.

## Key claims

- **[2025-06-24]** Segment framing: EVs used to all get compared to Tesla; now every segment is
  crowded. For a three-row electric SUV, **"this one stands out as the best bang for the buck… a
  really well-specced Hyundai Ioniq 9 for under 80 grand"** vs. Lucid Gravity / R1S / EQS at six
  figures. "It might sound weird to say bang for the buck when it's $70–80k, but this category is
  expensive." (quote)
- **[2025-06-24]** Specs: 110kWh battery, dual-motor AWD, ~420hp / 500 lb-ft, ~320–340mi real range
  ("an honest 300+ miles"); shares the E-GMP platform with the Kia EV9. (paraphrase)
- **[2025-06-24]** ⬥ Milestone: **"one of the first EVs I've seen/tested with the NACS port — a
  non-Tesla with the Tesla charge port in the US… we're going to start seeing these more in 2025 and
  2026."** (caveat: only some Superchargers work without an adapter — check the map). (quote)
- **[2025-06-24]** Design: the Ioniq "pixel" aesthetic; he'd probably pick the **Kia EV9's looks
  slightly over it**. Bose speakers rate a **"B."** (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-06-24]** Standout: **"well equipped"** — power-folding AND power-*un*folding second and
  third rows ("both rows will power-fold themselves back into place"), 110V outlet, huge flat load
  floor ("I can fit a bicycle back here"). "When I say well equipped, that really means a lot in a
  three-row." (quote)
- **[2025-06-24]** ⚠️ Recurring skepticism: **"Has anyone ever bought a car because of the massaging
  seats? I've never experienced a good massage from a car seat."** (quote)
- **[2025-06-24]** ⬥ Self-reference: **6'3"** — third row possible behind his own driving position but
  not roomy. Downsides are minor: a couple of cheap-feeling trim pieces + a tiny useless frunk.
  (paraphrase)
- **[2025-06-24]** Verdict: "Is this the best-equipped sub-$80,000 fully electric three-row SUV?…
  I think this might be the one." (quote)
