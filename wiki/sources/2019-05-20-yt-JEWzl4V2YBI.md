# OnePlus 7 Pro: What You Didn't Know! (@mkbhd, 2019-05-20)

## Summary
A follow-up "loose ends" video to the OnePlus 7 Pro review — details that bubbled up after the fact. Notable
for two reusable methodology points: how MKBHD thinks about **un-certified water resistance** and why
**screen-on-time is not directly comparable between reviewers.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-05-20] **Un-certified water resistance — paying for the seals, not the certificate.** "OnePlus **adds the
  rubber and seals internally** but **doesn't pay for the independent IP testing**, passing the ~$10–30/unit
  saving to buyers. I'm OK with that **as long as third-party testing** (e.g. Dave2D submerging it in a bowl)
  shows it holds up." A framework for reading marketing-vs-certification claims. (Dave2D = other creator, context.)
- [2019-05-20] **Screen-on-time is relative — don't compare reviewers directly.** "People disliked my **4–4.5 hrs**
  screen-on-time when others got 6–7. But **you can't compare one person's SOT to another's** — different apps,
  accessories, brightness. **My** usage is taxing (high brightness, Bluetooth/NFC/GPS always on, heavy
  multitasking), so compare **my** number across devices, not against other reviewers." A recurring
  measurement-integrity point.
- [2019-05-20] **The pop-up selfie camera free-fall math.** "It **retracts on detected free-fall** (~0.5 s to
  fully retract ≈ 1.2 m / 4 ft of fall). Dropped from selfie height it's retracted before impact; from counter
  height it may not fully retract — but **half-retracted still beats fully exposed.**" His characteristic
  literal-engineering breakdown.
- [2019-05-20] **RAM right-sizing + variable refresh clarification.** "My **medium-heavy use averages ~5 GB**, so
  6 GB is fine but I'd pay OnePlus's **$30 to go to 8 GB** for headroom; 12 GB is overkill/future-proofing. The
  90 Hz panel is **already variable** — it drops to 60 Hz on calls/video/camera, not fully adaptive yet."
- [2019-05-20] **The price framing.** "If **Samsung** shipped a special S10+ with a no-hole-punch 90 Hz display
  and 12 GB RAM, you'd expect **$1,000+.** Because it's OnePlus at **$669**, the price is still kind of amazing."

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "You can't really just directly compare one person's screen-on time to another person's screen-on time,
> because these people don't use the same apps or the same accessories or the same brightness even."
> "Half-way retracted is still better than fully exposed if it happens to land on it."
