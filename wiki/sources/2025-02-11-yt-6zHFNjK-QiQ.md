# PowerBeats Pro 2 Review: Still Better than AirPods!? (@mkbhd, 2025-02-11)

## Summary
A review that opens by **breaking his own rule of never quoting a pre-brief**: Apple told him the PowerBeats Pro are
**"the best-selling headphones in the world"** (he can't verify it, but they're clearly very successful $250 buds). The
point: **the sequel is a hell of a refresh** — Beats took those buds and added **ANC + transparency + heart-rate
tracking**, made them **smaller/lighter (20%) with better sound and comfort**, and put them in a **smaller case with
more battery + USB-C + wireless charging — all at the same $250.** His verdict: **"more clearly than ever, better than
AirPods Pro at more things than ever"** ("Apple should be taking notes"). The standout is the fit — **the ear hooks
make them the de-facto premium workout earbuds** ("AirPods Pro fit my ear well; these are a lock"). They run the **H2
chip** (instant pairing, easy on Android too), so **ANC and transparency are both really good but "a smidge below
AirPods Pro"** (which he still calls the industry best); sound is an 8/10 — "AirPods plus a bit more bass and energy,"
tuned for workouts, not a flat signature. The novel feature is **heart-rate tracking** (a sensor updating every 5 s,
tech borrowed from the Apple Watch; pairs to gym equipment — though apps default to the Watch's data if you have one).
The case is 33% smaller with ~3 charges (a month of daily 2-hr workouts; 5 min → 1+ hour fast charge). His conclusion:
**"here I am in 2025 recommending Beats — and they're not overpriced for what you get. Beats should be studied — it's
the opposite of a downfall."** (The old PowerBeats Pro are ~$150 now but he "can't recommend them anymore" — Lightning,
no wireless charging.)
