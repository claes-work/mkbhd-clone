# Pixel 7/Pro and Watch Impressions! (@mkbhd, 2022-10-07)

## Summary
Hands-on impressions from Google's fall event — the Pixel 7/7 Pro, the Pixel Watch, and a preview of the Pixel
Tablet. Opens with the **"companies throw subtle shots at each other on stage even though they're all copying each
other in a giant circle"** observation (Google's shade slides vs Apple). The phones look like the Pixel 6 (a
**metal visor now** — one seamless piece, better built; the 7 Pro's is glossy/fingerprinty), with a flat Pixel 7
screen and a less-curved 7 Pro screen. Per his benchmarks video, he's more interested in **features** — the
standout being **photo-unblur** (AI that fixes blurry shots in any Google Photos library) plus a cinematic mode and
a Pro camera system (a new ultrawide + a 48 MP 5x tele with software zoom). His pricing worry: **same $300 gap — the
7 will feel like a good deal, the 7 Pro a tougher sell again.** The **Pixel Watch** gets a **B+ design** (nice
circle, but bezels/battery worries; "a Fitbit smartwatch"), and the **Pixel Tablet** has one killer idea — **it
docks to a magnetic speaker and becomes a Nest Hub Max** ("the ultimate house tablet"; he predicts $499).

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-10-07] **Everyone's copying each other in a circle.** "'I love how tech companies throw subtle shots at each
  other on stage (Google's slides shading Apple's Face ID / cinematic mode) **even though we all know they're just
  copying each other in a giant circle until we have the same features across everything.**'"
- [2022-10-07] **The metal visor + more interested in features.** "'They look like the Pixel 6 (an established
  identity), but the **visor is metal now — one continuous seam-free piece, better built** (the 7 Pro's is glossy,
  so I'll get it covered in fingerprints; I miss accented power buttons). Per the benchmarks video, the general
  perf bump will be slight — **I'm more interested in the features the Tensor G2 enables.**'"
- [2022-10-07] **Photo-unblur — the coolest feature.** "'The coolest to me is **unblur**: open a slightly blurry
  photo, hit a button, and the Tensor chip uses AI to sharpen it — a genuinely impressive result. **And it works on
  any photo in your entire Google Photos library** (remaster old Polaroids / 30-year-old yearbook photos). Plus a
  cinematic mode (like Apple's), transcribed voice messages, and 2× faster Night Sight.'"
- [2022-10-07] **The Pro camera + the $300-gap worry.** "'The Pro adds a new autofocus ultrawide (21% wider, good
  macro) and a **48 MP 5x telephoto** with lots of software-zoom magic (a 2×/10× crop + super-res, haptic stops,
  a stabilized preview at 30×). But same prices ($599/$899) — **I have a feeling the $300 gap will feel big again:
  the 7 will feel like a good deal, and the 7 Pro a tougher sell.**'"
- [2022-10-07] **The Pixel Watch (B+) and the Pixel Tablet (the house tablet).** "'The **Pixel Watch: a B+ design**
  (a nice inconspicuous circle, seamless bands) — B+ not A because of **bezels and battery** (24-hr claim = a red
  flag). Functionally **it's a Fitbit smartwatch** ($349/$400 — more than the Apple Watch SE or Galaxy Watch 5).
  The **Pixel Tablet** has one killer idea: **it docks to a magnetic speaker and becomes a Nest Hub Max** — "the
  ultimate house tablet" for the 80% of tablets that just sit around the house (not a Pro rival; I'll guess $499).'"
