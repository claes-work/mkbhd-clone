# The Electric Hummer: Hands-On & Impressions! (@mkbhd, 2021-07-30)

## Summary
An exclusive hands-on with the two only-working GMC Hummer EVs (SUV + pickup), brought to the studio. The durable
notes: it's a **9,200-lb truck that does 0–60 in 3 seconds** (up to 1,000 hp), with wild features (**crab walk**,
a **removable convertible roof**, the **biggest frunk he's ever seen**, **multi-color charge-indicator
headlights**, and 20+ "Hummer EV" logos everywhere). It sits **between the CyberTruck and the F-150 Lightning** on
the electric-truck spectrum; an "electric Hummer is kind of an oxymoron." He closes with his recurring conviction:
**every year for the next 10 will be the most interesting EV year yet.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-07-30] **The scale — 9,200 lb, 0–60 in 3 s, crab walk.** "'The truck weighs **over 9,200 lb and still does
  0–60 in ~3 seconds (performance-Model-3 fast) — terrifying** — up to 1,000 hp. For a Hummer it's not about
  speed but torque and towing. Wild features: **crab walk (all wheels turn the same way to move diagonally),** an
  adjustable air suspension, and **three windshield wipers** (one would be too tall, a pair too short).'"
- [2021-07-30] **The EV-truck wins — the biggest frunk + charge-indicator headlights.** "'The **biggest front
  trunk I've ever seen in person** (water-sealed, deep — I loved this on the F-150 Lightning too). The **front
  running lights are multi-color to indicate charge level** (blue dots move across) and turn orange for blinkers —
  "honestly my favorite part of this truck."'"
- [2021-07-30] **The removable roof + storage everywhere + moon-landing pride.** "'It's a **convertible — a
  4-part removable roof** turns it from a military-looking vehicle to an open-air one. Storage everywhere (under
  and inside the back seats). And **lots of GM pride** — made-in-USA flags, and moon-landing references (GM made
  Apollo 11 rover parts): a topographic moon map on the floor, a boot-print on the Sea of Tranquility. **20+
  "Hummer EV" logos** on the truck ("you'll never forget which car you're in").'"
- [2021-07-30] **Where it sits + "an electric Hummer is kind of an oxymoron."** "'It feels **between the
  CyberTruck and the F-150 Lightning** on the electric-truck spectrum ($80k dual-motor base up to ~$112k+ for the
  loaded Edition 1). **An electric Hummer is kind of an oxymoron — the typical Hummer buyer won't care about the
  350-mile range or 350 kW charging; they care about off-roading, power, towing, cargo — and it checks those.**'"
- [2021-07-30] **The recurring conviction — "every year for the next 10 will be the most interesting EV year
  yet."** "'I'm **still very confident that every year for the next 10 straight years is going to be the most
  interesting year yet for electric vehicles — we're just getting started.**'" (Maybe the Rivian soon.)'"
