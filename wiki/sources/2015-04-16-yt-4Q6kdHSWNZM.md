## Key claims (paraphrase, dated 2015-04-16)
- ★ **[Marques — biography: the jersey wardrobe]** **"90% of the time [my on-camera shirt] is an
  ultimate frisbee jersey — I have so many"**; the other 10% is "some cool shirt design from RVCA
  or Amazon." He's now launching **MKBHD-designed shirts** (the first: a basic MKBHD logo, black
  or red; limited-edition runs; made by the same company as his Tesla shirt). *(explains the
  jersey-heavy on-camera look; the origin of MKBHD merch.)*
- ★ **[Marques — biography: THREE Ultimate teams]** He plays for **three teams at once**: a club
  team, **Garden State** (summer); a pro team, the **New York Rumble**; and his **college team**
  (currently in the postseason — won its section 2–0, moving to Metro-East Regionals). *(fully
  explains the multi-team Ultimate life.)*
- ★ **[Marques — pre-graduation goal]** **"I want the *quality* to increase after graduation"** —
  right now videos are shot in fragments between classes (shoot one day, finish the next after
  class, edit that night), which makes it hard to hold "focus or a goal or an image for what I
  want it to be." Continuous time will make them better. *(his quality-over-quantity instinct,
  dated pre-graduation 2015.)*
- **[Marques — manufacturer that should make a phone]** **Tesla** — "I wouldn't put it past a
  company run by Elon Musk to build something impressive" (they already build touchscreen
  interfaces and employ software engineers). *(the same instinct behind his later Tesla coverage.)*
- **[Marques — 4K phones]** A 4K/~800-ppi phone screen isn't *necessary* (nor were 1080p/2K) "but
  they're beautiful and pushing the limits — I can't say no to that"; great for **VR** headsets.
