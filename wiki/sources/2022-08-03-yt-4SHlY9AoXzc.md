# OnePlus 10T Impressions: Somebody That You Used to Know (@mkbhd, 2022-08-03)

## Summary
First impressions of the $649 OnePlus 10T — **"the most generic OnePlus phone of all time."** It's a mid-cycle
spec-bump (an 8+ Gen 1, up to 16 GB, flat 1080p 120 Hz) at a lower price than the 10 Pro — fine on paper, but the
cuts read as an identity crisis. Two land hardest: it's the **first OnePlus phone ever without the alert slider**
(one of his favorite OnePlus features), and it **dropped all Hasselblad branding/color science despite the
$150M+ deal.** Anchored by **the iPhone-mute-switch analogy** — "I'd bet a million dollars the next five years of
iPhones all keep the mute switch, because it's part of the identity." The standout feature is **125 W SuperVOOC
charging (0→100 in ~20 min).** His conclusion ties to the enthusiast→normal arc: **"more generic than ever" means
it appeals to more people than ever** — and the moral: **"don't be a fan of the company, be a fan of the product."**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-08-03] **The most generic OnePlus ever.** "'This isn't a 10T Pro, it's just the 10T ($649). Some cuts make
  sense (1080p/adaptive instead of 1440p/LTPO — those are reserved for the highest end) — but **it feels more
  generic than ever**: generic chrome rails instead of the 10 Pro's dark satin, a smoothed camera slope "I first
  liked on an Oppo," an OxygenOS that's more like ColorOS than ever.'"
- [2022-08-03] **The first OnePlus without the alert slider — the iPhone-mute-switch analogy.** "'The head-scratcher:
  **this is the first OnePlus phone ever without the alert slider** — one of my favorite OnePlus features for years
  (they say it took up space they gave to battery). **Can you imagine the iPhone dropping the mute switch to save
  space? I'd bet anyone a million dollars the next five years of iPhones all have a mute switch — because it's such
  a part of the identity.** To people who followed OnePlus, this is kind of sad.'"
- [2022-08-03] **Dropped Hasselblad despite the deal.** "'OnePlus signed a **$150M+, three-year Hasselblad deal**;
  the 10 Pro had the branding and a Hasselblad camera app. Less than two years in, **the 10T has no Hasselblad
  branding, no app, and as far as I can tell none of the color science/processing either.** Is the cheaper phone
  not worthy of the Hasselblad sauce, or did they stop caring? If you paid for it, put it in all your phones.'
  (A generic IMX766 primary + a 2 MP macro.)'"
- [2022-08-03] **The one standout — 125 W SuperVOOC.** "'The headline feature is the battery/charging: a 4,800 mAh
  dual cell with **Oppo's 125 W SuperVOOC — 0→100 in literally 20 minutes** (truly convenient). But — **no wireless
  charging** (fast-charging peace of mind, but you lose the wireless convenience).'"
- [2022-08-03] **More generic = appeals to more people; "be a fan of the product."** "'This is perfectly fine for a
  lot of people (decent screen, high-end chip, lots of RAM, four years of updates) — **and being more generic than
  ever means, by definition, it appeals to more people than ever** (right in line with my "What Happened to
  OnePlus" video). A OnePlus without an alert slider feels like giving up their identity — but the moral is:
  **don't be a fan of the company, be a fan of the product.**'"
