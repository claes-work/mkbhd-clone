# OnePlus 13 Review: The Bar Has Been Set!! (@mkbhd, 2025-01-07)

## Summary
Three weeks with the **OnePlus 13**, framed around **three things in it that will be smartphone trends for all of
2025** — so "even if this phone isn't for you, there'll be a lot more phones like it." **(1) The battery** — a **6,000
mAh silicon-carbon battery** (double the Galaxy S8's) in a slim body, thanks to a new silicon-carbon anode (more energy
density, faster charging, wider temperature range) — a genuine **two-day phone** he "basically can't kill in a day";
**"the best smartphone batteries have been since the beginning of the modern smartphone,"** and a wave is coming
(great for small phones and foldables). **(2) The Snapdragon 8 Elite chip** — a bigger-than-usual jump (31% higher
clock, 44% higher single-core Geekbench), great for both sustained performance and everyday efficiency. **(3) A record-
breaking display** — 4,500-nit peak / 1,600-nit high-brightness, the first A++ from DisplayMate, QHD+ 120 Hz LTPO,
2,160 Hz PWM, ultrasonic reader. That's **three of his five pillars of a great smartphone** (build and cameras the
other two): build is nice (alert slider, great haptics, **IP69** — funny given OnePlus once refused any IP rating), and
he notes the official cases have **MagSafe-compatible magnet rings** ("unlocking a whole world of accessories"). The
**cameras are a notch below other flagships — "the OnePlus story"** (fast phone, great charging, cameras trail; drops
hard in low light, the make-every-dark-photo-bright syndrome). OxygenOS is fast and adds a **Dynamic Island copycat**
("if you're gonna copy, copy the good stuff"), and he's wry about the **app-open/close animation-test genre** ("that's
not how anyone uses their phone, but OnePlus optimized for it — thanks?"). Verdict: OnePlus's undercut-the-flagships
formula holds, and the exciting part is **everyone will get better batteries, chips, and displays this year. "The bar
has been set."**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2025-01-07] **Silicon-carbon batteries are the 2025 story.** "'The 6,000 mAh silicon-carbon battery is the best
  smartphone batteries have been since the beginning of the modern smartphone. A new silicon-carbon anode gives more
  energy density, faster charging, and a wider temperature range — so this slim phone is a two-day phone I basically
  can't kill in a day. A whole wave of phones is shipping with these, great for small phones and foldables too.'"
- [2025-01-07] **The Snapdragon 8 Elite is a bigger-than-usual jump.** "'This year's chip jump is way bigger than the
  usual 5–10% — 31% higher clock, 44% higher single-core Geekbench. For casual use you mostly won't notice, but the
  power+efficiency jump means sustained performance without throttling and much better battery efficiency for everyday
  use. Lots of 2025 flagships will have it.'"
- [2025-01-07] **Three of my five pillars — cameras are the OnePlus story.** "'Battery, chip, and a record-breaking
  4,500-nit display are three of my five pillars of a great smartphone. Build is the fourth (nice — alert slider, great
  haptics, IP69, MagSafe-compatible cases). Cameras are the fifth, and they're a notch below other flagships — that's
  the OnePlus story: fast phone, great charging, cameras trail, dropping hard in low light.'"
- [2025-01-07] **Copy the good stuff.** "'OxygenOS is fast and smooth and added a Dynamic Island copycat ("Live Alert
  capsules" — it literally shows up in settings if you search "Island"). If you're gonna copy stuff, copy the good
  stuff.'"
- [2025-01-07] **The bar has been set for 2025.** "'OnePlus's formula is to undercut the other flagships by being a bit
  cheaper, and they're sticking to it. But the exciting part is everyone else can stick to their formula too and it
  still means we get much better batteries, chips, and displays across the board this year. The bar has been set.'"
