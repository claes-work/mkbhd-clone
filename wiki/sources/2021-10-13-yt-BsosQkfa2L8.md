# Apple Watch Series 7 Review: Spot the Differences! (@mkbhd, 2021-10-13)

## Summary
Review of the Apple Watch Series 7 (a lighter ingest) — **a minor update ("basically a Series 6S")**: a 20% larger
screen, the most durable Apple Watch (thicker glass, IP6X dust), and 33% faster charging (the one change that
applied to him, since he **wears it 24/7 as an alarm**). The durable persona note: his **biggest disappointment is
the lack of a neutral-color baseline aluminum** — a recurring MKBHD taste gripe (he wants a matte-black option).
Verdict: the best smartwatch for the iPhone, but **"the SE is great for almost everyone."**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-10-13] **"Basically a Series 6S" — a minor update.** "'If watches did S updates, this would be a 6S. **It's
  basically a Series 6 with a slightly larger display and slightly faster charging, same price** — plus a bunch of
  little quirks. The screen is 20% larger (a bit more text everywhere; two exclusive faces — Contour, Modular Duo;
  a QWERTY swipe keyboard I barely used).'"
- [2021-10-13] **The edge-curving display "didn't make much difference."** "'Apple's promos show text bleeding
  over the curved edges — but **in real life, with reflections, it didn't make much of a difference** (most stuff
  is centered). The one place it hurts is the Contour face, which puts numbers on the most reflective edges.'"
- [2021-10-13] **Small changes add up (or don't) — durability + faster charging.** "'"If enough small changes
  apply to you, they add up." **Most durable ever (thicker cover glass, now IP6X dust resistant — Apple just
  hadn't tested for dust before; it's already 50 m water resistant). Slightly brighter in the dim always-on
  state.** The one that applied to me: **33% faster charging (with the new USB-C puck) — 8 min = 8 hours of sleep
  tracking — which matters because I wear it 24/7 (it's my haptic alarm), so I only charge in brief windows.**'"
- [2021-10-13] **THE BIGGEST DISAPPOINTMENT — no neutral-color baseline aluminum.** "'My biggest disappointment is
  the color choices. **There's no neutral (black/gray/white) baseline aluminum — Starlight is warm, Midnight is
  blue-ish; a genuine neutral gray means jumping to heavier, pricier titanium/stainless.** With a watch you wear
  every day you want it neutral and spice it up with the band. **It bothers me that I can't get a baseline black
  aluminum Apple Watch — so I'll probably stick with my Series 6.**'" A durable MKBHD matte-black/neutral taste gripe.
- [2021-10-13] **The verdict — the best for iPhone, but "the SE is great for almost everyone."** "'It's the best
  overall smartwatch for the iPhone — no crazy new features (no iPhone-back charging, no square redesign we hoped
  for). **The SE is great for almost everyone; the Series 7 adds the larger always-on display, colors, and the
  blood-oxygen/ECG sensors.**'"
