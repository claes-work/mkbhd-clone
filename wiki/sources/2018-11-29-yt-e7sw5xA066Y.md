# 2018 Macbook Air Review: No Risk! (@mkbhd, 2018-11-29)

## Summary
The 2018 MacBook Air review — "the smartest no-risk laptop Apple can make," which he frames (again) as **the
curse of a beloved first-gen you can't live up to**. Opens with a useful account of **what he actually does on a
laptop**, and closes with a prescient wish for **ARM chips** in Macs.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-11-29] **PRODUCTION — what he actually does on a laptop.** "Email, docs, YouTube Studio, research, **light
  Lightroom (one image at a time)**, file management. **I stopped video editing on my laptop — I refuse to render
  8K/RAW on a laptop** (I'll toss together 1080p in Final Cut at most). So my use is non-intensive — I can get away
  with the **cheapest Mac laptop.**" A dated, concrete workflow.
- [2018-11-29] **"Don't buy it for its thinness" (the honest debunk).** "It's not noticeably thinner or lighter
  than the 13" MacBook Pro I've been using — **fun fact, at its thickest point the Air is actually *thicker* than
  the 13" Pro**; the wedge just makes it feel thin." A characteristic spec-vs-reality correction.
- [2018-11-29] **The Retina display — the real (overdue) upgrade.** "The biggest downside of the old Air was the
  low-res display; **now it's a 2560×1440 Retina with thin black bezels — a big upgrade we asked for.**" One gripe:
  "**at max brightness it's not as bright as I want** (the Pro/Surface get brighter), and I use it maxed all the
  time, which hits the B+ battery." Names the one real weakness.
- [2018-11-29] **The laptop-camera gripe.** "**Why doesn't anyone put a smartphone-quality camera in a laptop?**
  It should just have the iPhone's front camera and **Face ID** (like the iPad Pro or Windows Hello). Touch ID's
  fine, but Face ID would be more futuristic." A recurring "here's what they could easily do but don't."
- [2018-11-29] **"No risk is the curse Apple gets."** "Apple took **no risk** with this — and it's actually **the
  smartest no-risk laptop they could make** (fans asked for new internals + a Retina display; they delivered
  exactly that + Touch ID). **It's weird that 'incremental' sounds like a bad thing — it's not — it's just the
  curse of never living up to the revolutionary first-gen Air.**" Ties his XS-"curse Apple put on itself" +
  "incremental is a compliment" threads to laptops.
- [2018-11-29] **PRESCIENT — the ARM-chips wish + the value gap.** "I'd have loved an **$800–1,000** price to get
  excited about. And I'm waiting for the big upgrade: **maybe they inevitably switch to those ARM chips**, thinner
  bezels, Face ID, 120 Hz. For now I'll **keep my 13" MacBook Pro + iPad Pro** (and I've been trying the Surface
  Laptop 2)." An early call on Apple Silicon (echoing the iPad Pro review).
