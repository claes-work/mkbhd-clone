# The Kia EV9 is Surprisingly Good!

## Summary

Marques lives with the **Kia EV9** — a full-size three-row electric SUV — and frames it as the
ideal **"minivan in disguise"**: all the space and practicality of a minivan, none of the stigma,
and **"it's not missing anything."** The recurring Kia catch (a ~$75k GT trim with a lot of plastic)
is offset by how much it crams in for the money (it's the cheapest three-row EV). A hot take: **Kia's
software is top-three most responsive he's used.** Fully Marques-narrated.

## Key claims

- **[2024-02-24]** ⭐ The frame: "Nobody really *wants* a minivan, but what if you could get a practical
  three-row SUV with all a minivan's features that isn't a minivan? Now we're talking. **The EV9 is not
  missing anything.**" Trims from $55k (RWD "Light") to ~$75k (GT); "the cheapest three-row fully-
  electric SUV — take the Kia logo off and it's very compelling." (quote)
- **[2024-02-24]** Specs: 99.8kWh, ~270 mi rated (~250 real), 379 hp (GT); a ground-up EV (has a frunk);
  power-folding rows; the remote-control park-out key; pixel welcome-light animations. (paraphrase)
- **[2024-02-24]** ⭐ HOT TAKE: **"Kia software might be the most responsive — definitely top-three most
  responsive car software I've ever used… you know how people say 'smooth and responsive' then swipe
  and it's 12 fps? This is actually really good."** Built-in nav preconditions the battery en route to a
  charger. (quote)
- **[2024-02-24]** The "everything" list: wireless CarPlay/Android Auto, physical HVAC buttons, a
  USB-C data/charge toggle button ("never seen that before"), fingerprint reader, HUD, camera mirror,
  **gel Herman-Miller-like headrests ("the softest I've ever felt")**, active bolsters. The catch:
  **no real leather; lots of plastic for $75k.** (paraphrase)
- **[2024-02-24]** ⭐ Verdict + a brand-reinvention note: **"Kia has been shockingly high on my power
  rankings of EVs I'd buy — they've truly reinvented themselves with the aesthetics and feature set."**
  In the three-row power rankings (vs. the ~$90k R1S and Model X), "I'm finding it hard to say I'd pick
  the R1S for much more than build quality." Third row is small (like the R1S); "basically a minivan,
  fully electric, silent, smooth, three rows, tons of features — pretty good." (quote)
