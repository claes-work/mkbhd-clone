# Xbox Series X/S Design Impressions! (@mkbhd, 2020-09-18)

## Summary
Design-only first look at the (gutless) Xbox Series X ($499) and Series S ($299), before the Nov 10 launch. The
durable ideas: Microsoft's **two-tier strategy** (a cheaper console undercutting every PlayStation) is the more
interesting launch, **fps over resolution** is the right priority for most people, but **amortized over 4–5
years, the extra $200 for the future-proof console is probably worth it.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-09-18] **The design read — the plain black box is what people want under the TV.** "'The Series X is a
  **matte-black box — honestly plain, but that's what people want under their TVs;** they don't want an
  alien-spaceship thing.' The little green up top is paint, not a light. **The Series S (matte white, black
  grille) is my favorite-looking of all the new consoles** — cleaner, more modern — though we'll see if it
  yellows. Both are ridiculously small.'"
- [2020-09-18] **The two-tier strategy is the more interesting launch.** "'Different computers inside each,
  same identical experience at two resolutions: **Series X = 120 fps at 4K, Series S = 120 fps at 1440p**
  (less GPU, 10 GB vs 16 GB RAM). Microsoft's strategy of a **cheaper Xbox undercutting all the current
  PlayStations at launch is a really interesting one** — more interesting than Sony's.'"
- [2020-09-18] **FPS OVER RESOLUTION — the right priority for most people.** "'For most people without a 4K TV,
  the **$300 Series S is going to be fine — 120 fps, and the frame rate, as we can all agree, is more important
  in gaming.** The idea is **fps over resolution.** I love a crispy screen — I'm a different customer — but on a
  high-refresh monitor it'll look amazing.'"
- [2020-09-18] **BUT — amortize the cost, go future-proof.** "'I wonder how many actually buy the cheaper one,
  because **when you divide the cost over the 3–5 years you'll own it, it's probably not a big deal to spend the
  extra $200 for the future-proof console** — then it does 4K too.' If you have (or might get) a 4K TV, splurge
  for the X.'" A durable amortize-over-lifetime buying lens (cf. the Osborne-Effect framework).

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "It does look pretty plain, but that's what a lot of people want under their TVs — that's why a lot of people
> don't want like an alien-spaceship-looking thing."
> "The frame rate, as we can all agree in gaming, is more important... the idea is fps over resolution."
> "When you divide out the cost of the console over the three, four, five years you'll probably have it, it's
> probably not that big a deal to just go for the 200 extra dollars and have the future-proof console."
