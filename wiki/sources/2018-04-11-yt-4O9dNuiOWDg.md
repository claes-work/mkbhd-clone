# Product RED iPhone 8 Unboxing! (@mkbhd, 2018-04-11)

## Summary
A short unboxing of the (PRODUCT)RED iPhone 8/8+ — notable mainly for a pure statement of his personal aesthetic:
because Apple gave this year's red model a **black front** (last year's had a white front), it's effectively a
**"red and black MKBHD Edition iPhone"** — his signature color scheme, straight from Apple.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-04-11] **APPEARANCE — "a red and black MKBHD Edition iPhone."** "Apple actually listened — the big
  complaint about last year's red iPhone was the **white front**; this year it's a straight-up **red back, all-
  black front**. There's no other Apple device like this. **Red on black — I really, really like the look.** We
  all want it." A direct articulation of his red-and-black identity, validated by a mainstream product.
- [2018-04-11] **The red is different this year.** Last year's was an **anodized matte red** aluminum finish; this

  camera can barely do it justice."
- [2018-04-11] **Wants a red iPhone X.** "Now I really want an iPhone X in this red — I brought it up, they
  wouldn't say. It's the same glass on the back of the 8 and X, so a red iPhone X could happen." (Answered in the
  same-batch [[2018-04-28-yt-OJfOsoUYPts]]: the steel band likely can't be dyed red.)
- [2018-04-11] **The (PRODUCT)RED cause + buyer note.** Buying any (PRODUCT)RED item donates a portion to **HIV/
  AIDS programs and research** — "for sure a good cause." Buyer caveat: it's mid-cycle ("a new iPhone within ~6
  months, like clockwork"), and "if you just want a little red in your life, you can always grab a **skin.**"
- [2018-04-11] **Internals unchanged.** "It's the exact same phone as any other iPhone 8, just a new color — all
