# The iPhone 11 Models! (@mkbhd, 2019-07-17)

## Summary
A pre-release look at dimensionally-accurate iPhone 11 dummy models (case-maker leaks; credit Sonny Dixon /
Ben Geskin). His verdict: excited for the triple-camera/ultrawide upgrade, but the design is a disappointing
"mailed it in" — and it prompts his recurring wondering about the post-Jony-Ive Apple.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-07-17] **Excited about the camera, hates the bump.** "'I **love the mini-trend of adding an ultrawide** to
  flagships — imagine ultrawide 4K from an iPhone.' The rumor that the **Pixel 4 won't have an ultrawide makes me
  sad.** But the new **square camera bump** is 'awkward, chunky, alien-eye-looking' — and 'you can make the phone
  a little thicker and have no bump, that would be cool with me (I remember the iPhone 5s with no bump).'" His
  consistent anti-camera-bump / pro-ultrawide stance.
- [2019-07-17] **"They mailed it in" — the design critique + the post-Ive question.** "'Apple usually has the
  tick-tock strategy (6/6s look same, new design for 7... 10/10s). But this is **the first time they're not doing
  a major redesign after the S version** — same design with a new camera. **It makes me wonder when we're going
  to start to see iPhones not designed by Jony Ive.**'" A dated industry-watch data point (echoes
  [[2019-06-28-yt-dZ_Ab1XxxKg]]).
- [2019-07-17] **The predictions (mostly right).** "Three models (11 / 11 Max / 11R), same 5.8"/6.5" OLED sizes,
  **still a big notch** 'in a world where almost everyone is rapidly getting rid of theirs,' likely **dropping 3D
  Touch** ('fine — you get the shortcuts with a long press anyway; I just wish it made it cheaper'), **still
  Lightning, no USB-C** ('thanks Apple'). Predicts the 11R gets **standard + ultrawide** (not telephoto)."
- [2019-07-17] **METHODOLOGY — honesty about the visualization.** "'Usually we'd go super hard with After Effects
  to show it on a working phone, but the differences are **so minimal you almost can't tell** — it looks just like
  a 10/10s.'" Won't manufacture excitement the models don't support.

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "It kind of seems like they just mailed it in — it's just the same design with the new camera. It makes me
> wonder when we're going to start to see iPhones not designed by Jony Ive."
> "You can make the phone a little thicker and have no [camera] bump, and that would be cool with me. I remember
> the days of the iPhone 5s not having a camera bump."
