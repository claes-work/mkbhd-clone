# MacBook Neo Review: Better than you Think! ★★

## Summary

The full MacBook Neo review — and Marques's boldest claim about it: **"potentially Apple's
most disruptive product in the last 10+ years,"** precisely because it's the *opposite* of
flashy. Nothing in it is new, but the **combination** — an iPhone-class A18 Pro (benchmarking
**around M1**) in a well-built aluminum $599 Mac — is about to "make the entire Windows and
Chromebook laptop industry very nervous." He structures the back half as a **per-profession
rating system** (students A+, writers, photographers B−, editors, coders, etc.), grounded in a
week of real use. A landmark on Apple's economics-of-scale disruption. ★★.

## Key claims

- **[2026-03-10]** The thesis: **"not only is this really good, I think it's potentially Apple's
  most disruptive product in the last 10+ years"** — not the flashy stuff (iPhone X, Vision Pro),
  "actually the exact opposite." Nothing is fundamentally new, but "this combination of things is
  about to make the entire Windows and Chromebook laptop industry very nervous." (quote)
- **[2026-03-10]** Performance: the **A18 Pro benchmarks around M1** — Geekbench multi-core ~8,500
  (~M1); GPU ~M1; **single-core is its strongest dimension, closer to M3.** "A very capable chip"
  (it edits 4K on the iPhone 16 Pro). All the bursty everyday stuff (web, spreadsheets, music,
  email) is "literally just like any other Mac." (paraphrase)
- **[2026-03-10]** On 8GB RAM: "not a lot," but **swap makes it generally okay** (the SSD reads/
  writes ~1,500MB/s) "the same way it was on the M1 MacBook Air" — the worry is only as the SSD
  fills over years. "Definitely faster than any Intel Mac, which is more than good enough for the
  average person." (paraphrase)
- **[2026-03-10]** **The economics are the disruption**: Apple "can just crank out millions of
  these chips at scale" (they already do for the iPhone) at a **ridiculously low unit cost** — so
  low that they put an **A19 Pro just to run the webcam/speakers in the Studio Display** (giving
  that monitor 12GB RAM, more than this laptop). "They print these things in the millions and build
  a perfectly reasonable budget laptop around it." (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2026-03-10]** Build/design: the **aluminum chassis** makes it "the best-feeling, best-built
  laptop in its price class" (no keyboard flex, a one-finger hinge); the **same keyboard as the
  $3,000 MacBook Pro minus the backlight** (off-white "barely yellow" keys tinted to the color);
  a real clicking trackpad; Touch ID on the 512GB; playful colors (**"citrus" = lemon-lime
  Gatorade**); software accent colors matched per model. (paraphrase)
- **[2026-03-10]** Battery: **just shy of the MacBook Air** but with a **much smaller physical
  battery** — Apple "correctly" used a cheaper smaller **lithium-ion** cell because the chip "sips
  battery." A 20W charger in the box (0→100 in ~an hour). (paraphrase)
- **[2026-03-10]** ⬥ The **per-profession rating** (with biography beats): **Students A+** ("this
  laptop was literally made for you"); **college students A**; **writers** — "as a bit of a
  part-time writer myself, I did all my writing for this video on the Neo"; **photographers B−**
  (handles the edit, but the display doesn't cover full P3 — plug into a better one); **coders B**
  (great for basics, not for heavy local LLMs); **editors** — "not an A" because it lacks the
  iPhone's faster SSD and OLED, but **Final Cut 4K edits are no problem** until plugins hit the
  wall (then get the MacBook Air); **podcasters B+** ("I have a pretty serious podcast… we wouldn't
  use it for this," but a basic 1–2-mic podcast is fine); **gamers** — no; **Grandma** — yes.
  (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2026-03-10]** Prediction: the **next Neo gets an A19 Pro + 12GB RAM** (as early as next year),
  plus a **True Tone ambient sensor and a keyboard backlight** (the biggest missing quality-of-life
  items). The reason it's so good: **it's the easiest laptop for Apple to make and the easiest for
  reviewers to recommend** — "90-something percent of the MacBook Air for hundreds less. Well played,
  Apple." (paraphrase + quote)
