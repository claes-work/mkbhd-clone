# On the iPhone With No Charger... (@mkbhd, 2020-07-01)

## Summary
A think-piece on the (then-)rumor that the iPhone 12 would ship without a charger or headphones. He steelmans
**both reactions** (greed vs. e-waste), lands **in between**, and predicts **how Apple will spin it** — a
media-literacy exercise.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-07-01] **REACTION 1 — "create a problem, sell the solution."** "'Removing the charger (the one necessary
  accessory) **optically looks like pure greed** — classic Apple creating a problem and selling the solution
  ($20-30 for the charger after it dies). We've never seen a phone ship without one.'"
- [2020-07-01] **REACTION 2 — the e-waste case.** "'On the other hand: most iPhone buyers **already own an iPhone
  charger**, and at the iPhone's scale the **e-waste savings are massive** (per Anker's CEO / Dieter's article,
  discarded in-box chargers across phones/tablets/laptops are ~**300,000 tons/year**). Plus a **smaller box** = more
  per truck/plane = real environmental impact. "It would be a better smartphone world if Apple stopped shipping the
  charger."'" A steelman of the pro side.
- [2020-07-01] **HIS TAKE — in between, via the DSLR-lens analogy.** "'I'm in between. **Adjust the price** (a
  basic charger ~$15 + headphones ~$30 off → ~$955; and precedent exists — the XR launched at $750, the 11 at
  $700). It's like **a DSLR shipping without a lens: most buyers already have lenses, or grab a discounted kit
  lens** — just as Apple could discount a basic charger. The people with fast/wireless chargers don't need the
  slow in-box one they'd throw away.'" A promotable reframe.
- [2020-07-01] **"How will Apple spin it?" — the media-literacy angle.** "'Apple has run a **master class in tilting
  how people view their products** (the headphone jack seemed ridiculous — then everyone copied it). Two paths: **(1)
  don't mention it** (avoid another "Pro Stand moment") and let customers discover it, or **(2) go straight at it
  from the environmental angle** (the eco slide they always show before price) — I think #2 is more likely. Though
  **if they truly cared about the environment, they'd support user repair, or make it USB-C.**'"
- [2020-07-01] **The portless-iPhone theory (why this year).** "'Per MG Siegler: maybe it's a **one-year-early move
  to get people used to no charger before the rumored completely portless, all-wireless iPhone** — apple's goal.'"
  A dated forward-looking read.
