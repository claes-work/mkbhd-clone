# The Ultimate Pocket Camera: Insta360 X3! (@mkbhd, 2022-09-09)

## Summary
A sponsored look at the Insta360 X3 360 camera — **"the most versatile camera I've ever used."** The one great
trick: two back-to-back >180° sensors capture a **full sphere of video**, and the software **stitches out the
selfie stick and the camera itself** (an "invisible ball of 360° video"), then lets you **point at any piece of
that sphere after the fact** (flow-state stabilization locks onto a subject). That unlocks a range of shots — **me
mode** (a floating pseudo-drone selfie), **"GTA mode"** (a car chase-cam), **first-person mode** (camera on your
head/in your mouth — safer than holding a phone while driving), and **bullet time** (swing it on a string). The
durable point for creators: **it's all edited on your phone in one app** (sky-swaps and reframing faster than any
Adobe tool) — the accessible, phone-first workflow he keeps preaching. He's **adding it to the @AutoFocus arsenal.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-09-09] **The one trick — a sphere of video with the stick stitched out.** "'Two back-to-back >180° sensors
  capture a super-wide feed each; the camera stitches them into one and — knowing its own dimensions and the mount
  point — **stitches out the selfie stick and itself, creating an invisible ball of 360° video.** But the point
  isn't spherical video — **it's a sphere you can point any part of, after the fact** (flow-state stabilization
  finds a horizon and locks onto a target). **That's what makes it the most versatile camera I've ever used.**'"
- [2022-09-09] **The range of shots.** "'From simplest to craziest: **me mode** (a floating pseudo-drone selfie),
  **"GTA mode"** (a stick off the back of a car for a first-person chase cam), **first-person mode** (camera on
  your head or in your mouth — since holding a phone while driving isn't safe; "nose mode" is the other side), and
  **bullet time** (swing it on Insta360's cord for a slow-mo 360 reveal).'"
- [2022-09-09] **The specs.** "'The X3: new half-inch sensors (5.7K 360 HDR, 8K time-lapses), a 2.3" touchscreen,
  waterproof to 33 ft (IPX8), a removable 1,800 mAh battery — plus loop recording (a dash cam) and accessories (a
  card reader, a mic-jack adapter).'"
- [2022-09-09] **The killer feature — all edited on your phone.** "'The craziest part: **it's all edited on your
  phone in the Insta360 app** — the next level of accessible (I keep saying you can make videos on your phone).
  Tracking renders faster than real time; **a convincing sky-swap in a few seconds — much faster than any Adobe/
  motion-graphics tool I've tried.** Any effect — bullet time, keyframed reframing, using your phone's gyroscope to
  point — is done in-app, smoothly.'"
- [2022-09-09] **The one weakness — and adding it to the arsenal.** "'The only weakness: put a close subject right
  on the **stitch seam** and you get distortion — but it's easy to shoot around (just keep the subject somewhere in
  the sphere). A $449 camera + the app + a selfie stick, that's the whole system. **I'm adding it to the @AutoFocus
  arsenal — first-person driving shots, selfie-stick-outside-the-car stuff.**'"
