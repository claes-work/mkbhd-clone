# The iPhone with a Piece of the Moon? The Caviar Story (@mkbhd, 2020-12-12)

## Summary
Ostensibly about a $6,940 Caviar custom iPhone 12 Pro with a claimed piece of the moon, but really an
investigation into **Caviar as a headlines/free-marketing machine.** The durable idea: **luxury-tech companies
thrive on "all press is good press" — attach a limited-edition art piece to the most-talked-about tech (the
iPhone) and tech YouTubers will make the videos for free.** Includes a **Neil deGrasse Tyson guest interview**
(moon-rock facts attributed to Tyson, not the subject).

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-12-12] **This is his THIRD Caviar video — because the model works.** "'This is the **third video I've made
  about a ridiculous Caviar iPhone** — they reach out and send one. Search YouTube for any custom/gold/crazy-
  expensive iPhone and **almost all those glorious-thumbnail videos are about a Caviar iPhone.** Why do they keep
  sending them? **Because we'll keep making videos about them — it's free marketing.**'"
- [2020-12-12] **"All press is good press" — the machine explained.** "'The more you look into Caviar (a Russian
  luxury-tech company), the more it makes sense: **they thrive on headlines — they're everything the Escobar
  company wanted to be.** They attach a piece of the moon / Mars / Steve Jobs's turtleneck to the **most talked-
  about new tech every year, the iPhone, for a constant free supply of hype, headlines, and YouTube videos.**'"
  A promotable read on attention-as-a-business.
- [2020-12-12] **The verification problem + the collector logic.** "'Aside from precious metals, **there's no way
  for me or most regular people to verify the authenticity of any of it** (moon, Mars, Steve Jobs's turtleneck) —
  and even if real, it's overpriced. **People obviously shouldn't buy this for value — but collectors care more
  about limited editions / low supply than anything,** and everything Caviar makes is one of a small number (this
  is 1 of 19).'"
- [2020-12-12] **The tells — arrives already set up, and the phantom "famous clients."** "'It's weird to spend
  thousands on a phone that **arrives already set up and turned on out of the box just for a wallpaper — the only
  others I've seen do that are Escobar.** And their "famous clients" page: **googling each person, the only photo
  that exists of them with a Caviar phone is the one on Caviar's own site.**'" His investigative skepticism.
- [2020-12-12] **The verdict — "just grab an icon skin" / "just buy a piece of the moon."** "'It's cool that
  people make custom art pieces with a real piece of the moon — but **what's the point of attaching it to a
  working, disposable iPhone you'll throw away when the next one comes out?** If you want a custom iPhone, **just
  grab an ICON skin (way cheaper); if you want a piece of the moon, just buy a piece of the moon.**'"

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "If you send a crazy hundred-thousand-dollar gold iPhone to someone who makes videos about phones for a living,
> you're probably going to get a video... it's kind of a no-brainer. All press is good press."
> "The only real reason they have for constantly attaching this stuff to new iPhones... is a constant free supply
> of new hype and headlines and YouTube videos."
> "If you want a custom iPhone, just grab an icon skin... if you want a piece of the moon, just buy a piece of
> the moon."
