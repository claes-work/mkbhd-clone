# Reviewing Old MKBHD Thumbnails! (@TheStudio, 2023-09-08)

> ⚠️ ATTRIBUTION: **Marques + Tim** (the studio's lead graphic designer) review old MKBHD
> thumbnails, with a host interviewing. Marques's own lines about *his* thumbnail-making
> are subject data; Tim's descriptions of *his* designs are context (attribute to Tim).

## Summary
A thumbnail retrospective — high-value for how Marques thinks about thumbnails and his
hands-on, self-taught workflow. His thumbnail philosophy and the early-era craft are strong
creator-business/production material.

## Key claims (Marques-attributed only)
- [2023-09-08] **He makes his own rough thumbnails in Final Cut Pro:** "if I'm cranking out
  a rough draft or a quick thumbnail, I literally make it in **Final Cut Pro** — it's just
  what I'm most comfortable with." Previously used a free Photoshop-mimic; "**I never really
  learned [Photoshop].**" (Tim's ribbing: "you're like the painters that paint using
  Microsoft Paint and Excel.") (biography, production-filmmaking)
- [2023-09-08] **His thumbnail philosophy:** **"There's two different things for a
  thumbnail. Something that works really great as a thumbnail isn't necessarily a beautiful
  image; and things that are a beautiful image don't do well as thumbnails. There's a
  balancing act."** A thumbnail must condense a lot of information and still read at tiny
  size. (creator-business)
- [2023-09-08] **BIO — the early workflow:** "For a long time in videos, I didn't even
  think about making a thumbnail… I didn't decide to make a thumbnail until I was uploading
  the video, when I'd go 'oh right, I don't have a thumbnail' " — he'd grab an A-roll frame
  and hand-draw an outline around himself (click-drag, zoomed in). (biography)
- [2023-09-08] **His DIY craft:** the classic **two-phone top-down** was practical — a red
  poster-board (red/white sides), phones laid on it, then a white-PNG line dropped on the
  edge in post to clean it up; the drop shadow is real. He'd "punch in harder" (bigger
  products) if redoing them today. (production-filmmaking)
- [2023-09-08] **The "handsome Squidward" idea (Marques's concept):** for the
  smartphone-cameras-vs-reality video, he walked up to Tim and just said "handsome
  Squidward" — illustrating phone beautification with a real person in the background and an
  over-beautified version in front. "I can almost guarantee someone at Apple was mad at
  this thumbnail." (creator-business, voice — his idea-generation shorthand)
- [2023-09-08] Closing thought: **"Thumbnails, even though everyone knows they're
  important, are still kind of underrated — they're supposed to be simple on their surface
  but are deceivingly complicated."** (creator-business)
