# Toyota BZ4X: They'd Rather Make Hybrids!

## Summary

Marques lives with the **Toyota bZ4X** — Toyota's one EV — and finds it a solid Toyota crossover
with **one deal-breaking flaw: ~220 mi range (199 in winter)**. It reads like "an EV they made
because they had to," which becomes a lens on **Toyota's all-in-on-hybrids strategy** vs. the
Hyundai/Kia EV push. His open question: with EVs still young and expensive, **is hybrid-camp actually
the smart play right now?** Fully Marques-narrated; contains a Camry-Hybrid biography callback and a
Denvix sponsor read.

## Key claims (biography marked ⬥)

- **[2024-04-19]** The one flaw: **~220 mi range (199 in the winter), slow charging — "the deal-breaker
  for me."** "$43–50k, same class as the Ioniq 5; everything else is pretty solid because it's a Toyota
  — but this being their one EV, it feels like they missed the chance to show a great EV drivetrain."
  (quote)
- **[2024-04-19]** ⭐ The "compliance vehicle" read: "It feels like they made an EV because they had to
  and then just shipped it and didn't care too much… Toyota's strategy is notably to ship a lot of
  hybrids." (Technically not a compliance car, but it feels like one.) (quote)
- **[2024-04-19]** ⬥ BIOGRAPHY: **"I used to drive a Camry Hybrid for a while"** (corroborates the
  first-car Camry story) — and **"the Prius is one of the most popular videos on this entire channel,
  and one of the most recognizable cars on the street; the bZ4X's driver's seat has a lot of Prius to
  it."** He calls the bZ4X essentially **"a Prius XL."** (quote)
- **[2024-04-19]** ⚠️ Nitpicks: too much piano black; capacitive (not physical) buttons; **no true one-
  pedal driving** ("I wish I could, at least as an option"); an over-wide center console. Praise: great
  camera system, good Toyota software (wireless Android Auto), JBL speakers. (paraphrase)
- **[2024-04-19]** ⭐ The strategic question: **"Toyota's strategy is a fascinating one — they're so
  all-in with hybrids while Hyundai/Kia are flag-shipping EVs. I wonder if that's the way to go right
  now, because EVs are still so young and expensive for what you get — this feels like a good example of
  that (only 220–230 miles when you can get a Prius that does a lot of the same stuff for less)."** (quote)
- **[2024-04-19]** Contains a **Denvix tire-inflator/battery sponsor read** (flagged). (paraphrase)
