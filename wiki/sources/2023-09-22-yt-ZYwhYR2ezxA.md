## Key claims (paraphrase, dated 2023-09-22)
- ★ **[Marques, clearly — sustainability skepticism]** He's wearing the **pink Apple Watch
  Series 9** with the **recycled Nike sport band** (each one unique — made of flecks of old
  bands). He keeps circling back to Apple's **"carbon-neutral" claim** and is openly
  skeptical: the claim covers the *entire* lifecycle (materials, construction, packaging,
  shipping, and the electricity used over the ownership lifetime) — **"it's got to be
  mostly credits, right? I need to know how they're doing this."** He grants the *genuinely*
  easier wins (recycled aluminum in the housing, motor, buttons) but presses on the parts
  you *can't* control (delivery trucks, always-on Apple Stores, the grid you charge from).
  *(a characteristic "show me the mechanism, not the marketing" probe of green claims.)*
