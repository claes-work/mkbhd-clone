# Behind the Blind Smartphone Camera Test! (@TheStudio, 2021-12-17)

> ⚠️ ATTRIBUTION: **Marques-led** behind-the-scenes of his own annual **Blind Smartphone
> Camera Test** (a documented MKBHD franchise). He narrates, sets the rules, and is himself the
> photo subject; Andrew operates the shutter. Subject data. Andrew's asides are context.

## Summary
The BTS of the 2021 blind smartphone camera test — 16 phones, bracket format, community votes.
The valuable part is Marques laying out the **methodology and fairness rules** for how the test
is designed, plus his repeated thesis that smartphone camera quality is now mostly *processing*.
★ L3-candidate for camera-testing methodology.

## Key claims (Marques-attributed only)
- [2021-12-17] **The fairness rule for the first/setup photo (methodology ★):** "the first
  photo is always the most interesting one 'cause we don't want to pick a photo that really
  emphasizes just dynamic range... or that's just greens or blues." The setup shot deliberately
  loads *every* variable at once — "the subject is me and it's with a bunch of things around me
  with different colors, different environments; you've got light on me, but also a dynamic
  range test... a little bit of cool, a little bit of warm, a little bit of sun, a little bit of
  indoors, a little bit of skin tones, a little bit of shadows, a little bit of highlights. It's
  all the things." (production-filmmaking, tech-reviews)
- [2021-12-17] **Shooting technique for a fair side-by-side:** they shoot a tripod reference
  frame first as an "eye test / framing reference" (a fixed tripod alone doesn't work "because
  of focal lengths"), and "always shoot a little wide because then we are cropping it in to fit
  two pictures in the same [frame]." (production-filmmaking)
- [2021-12-17] **The core thesis — it's all processing:** "honestly this whole thing is all
  about processing, and they kind of all process their stuff the same." (tech-reviews — his
  consistent modern-smartphone-camera position: the sensor matters less than the pipeline)
- [2021-12-17] **On why the community reliably out-picks the "spec" favorites:** "the ones that
  I think are good are the ones that usually lose" — brand reputation ≠ blind-pick winner; the
  iPhone historically "has not done great" and "never made it out of the first round" (this year
  the iPhone 13 Pro got "its first win ever"). Winner: **Pixel 5a** (an unexpected, mid-range
  winner over the OnePlus 9 Pro). (tech-reviews)
- [2021-12-17] **"This is my favorite video of the year, every year."** (biography — he says the
  team is "twice as big" now, so points are totaled on brackets) (creator-business)
