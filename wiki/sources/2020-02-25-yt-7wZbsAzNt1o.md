# The 2020 Mac Pro: A Silent Killer! (@mkbhd, 2020-02-25)

## Summary
The full 2020 Mac Pro review after a couple months of daily editing. Three things: **over-engineered, finally
modular, really fast.** The inverted verdict — "**you probably shouldn't buy it, most people will never need it —
but I'm really glad they made it.**"

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-02-25] **THE INVERTED VERDICT — "you probably shouldn't buy it, but I'm glad they made it."** "'My usual
  "really good → you should buy it" ending flips here: **most people will never need this.** But I'm in the tiny
  sliver (a Final Cut RED editor) where it's exactly right.'" A characteristic who-is-this-for framing at the
  extreme end.
- [2020-02-25] **"Over-engineered — in the name of aesthetics and noise."** "'The **most over-engineered desktop
  I've ever seen** — a completely **cableless interior**, MPX modules that slide in on pins, a satisfying PCI lock,
  every button metal, labeled/numbered parts. The fan controller **modulates each fan to a different, never-
  matching speed so you never hear the harmonics** — genuinely silent. Tons of choices you'd never notice on
  another desktop.'" His appreciation of invisible fit-and-finish.
- [2020-02-25] **"Apple chose to make a very expensive machine" — not just an expensive one.** "'It's not that
  the Mac Pro is expensive — it's that **Apple chose to take no shortcuts and take everything to the max** (build,
  performance). Those other desktops are for most people; **Apple made the one that took no shortcuts.**'" A
  reframe of the price outrage.
- [2020-02-25] **Powerful but not invincible — the RED ceiling + the Afterburner bet.** "'Better than my maxed
  18-core iMac Pro — but shoot a **5:1 8K RED clip, drop it on a timeline at high quality, and it still chokes.**
  The **$2,000 Afterburner card** decodes ProRes (up to 23 streams) — useless to me since I shoot RedCode — **but
  it's a reprogrammable FPGA, so Apple + RED could enable RedCode acceleration** (rumored in the works).'"
- [2020-02-25] **"A downside of Apple, not the Mac Pro" + the wishlist.** "'Apple **just doesn't make a $900-1,200
  modular tower** — that's a downside of *Apple*, not this product. There's a list of products I wish Apple made
  and doesn't: a **mirrorless camera** ("I already make some of the best smartphone cameras"), **computer
  speakers**, a **multi-device wireless charger.**'" His recurring "here's what Apple should make" bit. BIOGRAPHY:
  gave Apple **$40k+ of his own money**; hopes it lasts a decade. (Aside: "the $400 wheels need locks.")
