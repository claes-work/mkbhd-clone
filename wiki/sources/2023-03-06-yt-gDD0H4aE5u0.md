# Kia EV6 GT: Almost Perfect!

## Summary

Marques reviews the **Kia EV6 GT** and lands on the verdict that recurs whenever he mentions it later:
**"almost perfect… I love it, but I could not recommend it — there's one dealbreaker: 200 miles of
range."** It's **the most fun EV you can drive under $70k** (~580 hp, sub-3.5s 0–60, a neon-green "GT"
mode that drops traction control), but the performance version didn't get a bigger battery. His plea:
"Kia, please make a high-battery version." Fully Marques-narrated.

## Key claims (biography marked ⬥)

- **[2023-03-06]** ⭐ The verdict: **"This car is almost perfect. It's so good, I love it… but I could
  not recommend it, because there is one dealbreaker — a pretty classic one."** ~$65k; the GT is the
  faster, sportier EV6 (neon-green brake calipers/badging). "I never thought I'd like a Kia this much."
  (quote)
- **[2023-03-06]** ⚠️ THE DEALBREAKER: **"The GT got more powerful motors but no bigger battery — so with
  its 77 kWh pack it has a max range of 200 miles. That's about half of what I'd hope for at $65k."**
  ⬥ "I have a 30-mile commute and I have to recharge before the end of the week." (quote)
- **[2023-03-06]** ⭐ The joy: **"This is probably the most fun EV you can drive under $70k"** — ~580 hp
  dual-motor, low-to-mid-3s 0–60, "it keeps pulling, a blast." The **GT button** turns everything neon
  green, drops traction control, and "the car shrinks around you"; a **customizable MyDrive mode** lets
  you dial each parameter. "It really lets you stay on the power through curves and play." (quote)
- **[2023-03-06]** The ground-up tell: "a telltale sign a car wasn't built ground-up to be electric is
  it lacks extra features over the gas version — this has a tiny 20L frunk." ⚠️ Wired (not wireless)
  CarPlay ("so your phone ends up sliding around in the tray"); a fiddly dual-function volume/temp knob
  ("Ioniq 5 learned the same lesson"). Tech gets a "B-/C+." (paraphrase)
- **[2023-03-06]** ⭐ Verdict + plea: "Buyers at $65k are also cross-shopping the Mach-E GT, Model 3/Y
  Performance — all with 100+ miles more range. **It's so much fun, and it's just such a pain to charge
  60% more often. Kia, please make a 110 kWh version — it's my only request.**" (quote)
