# Top 5 OnePlus 5T Features! (@mkbhd, 2017-11-16)

## Summary
A Top-5 of the OnePlus 5T — "literally the 2017-eyes version of the OnePlus 5" (which was only months old).
Clone-relevant beats: the bezel-less display that finally makes it "feel 2017," the fingerprint reader moved
to the correct back spot, a "good buttons" call-out, an honestly-flagged trust-the-marketing moment (the
low-light second camera), and the $499-undercuts-the-flagships value.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2017-11-16] **"The 2017-eyes version of the OnePlus 5" — the redesign makes it feel new.** "The OnePlus 5
  just came out in June — a lot going for it, but it didn't feel very 2017. The 5T's biggest change is the
  6" 1080p AMOLED that gets way closer to every edge; screen-to-body ratio matters now, and this feels a lot
  more modern." (OnePlus "sticks with lower resolution longer for battery/performance — 1080p doesn't look
  bad.")
- [2017-11-16] **Fingerprint moved to the back — "the correct place."** "First OnePlus to move the reader to
  the back (no room up front with the edge-to-edge display) — and they put it just above the middle, right
  where your finger rests naturally, like the Pixel and V30. Still ceramic, extremely fast." The
  fingerprint-in-the-right-spot value.
- [2017-11-16] **"Good buttons" — the underrated call-out.** "Something usually underrated with phones is
  good buttons — we don't talk about them unless they're loose/bad. The 5T has exceptionally clicky,
  convincing, crisp tactile buttons." His standing tactility-matters value.
- [2017-11-16] **Honest trust-the-marketing flag (the low-light camera).** "Instead of a telephoto, the 5T's
  second camera is another normal-focal-length 20 MP f/1.7 for low light — but the main is *already* f/1.7
  with bigger pixels, so how is this better? OnePlus says under 10 lux it switches and uses 'intelligent
  pixel technology' (4 pixels merged into 1). **I can't find a better explanation than what OnePlus says…
  at this point we just kind of trust it. I hope it works the way they say.**" Names where he can't verify.
- [2017-11-16] **Super-fast face unlock — but the security question.** "The new facial recognition is
  insanely fast — 5–10× faster than Face ID; it unlocks before you even see the lock screen. But **how can
  it be that fast AND as secure? Not sure yet.**" Fair, reserve-judgment take (contrast with the X's Face ID).
- [2017-11-16] **What they kept = the value pitch.** "OnePlus is hell-bent on being a good phone for the
  price: they kept the **headphone jack** ('a lot of enthusiasts refused to let it die'), the alert slider,
  Dash charging, near-stock OxygenOS, and — most importantly — the **$499 price, clearly undercutting the
  flagships**." The value-flagship read.
