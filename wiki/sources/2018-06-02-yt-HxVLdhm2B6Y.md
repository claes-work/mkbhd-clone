# Driving Lamborghini Aventador S! [Auto Focus Ep. 2] (@mkbhd, 2018-06-02)

## Summary
**Auto Focus Episode 2** — the first supercar on the young car series, framed (characteristically) as a **tech
review**: the real hook is trying **Apple CarPlay** (Teslas don't have it). Reviews CarPlay through a $500k
Aventador S, calls the car "the opposite of a Tesla," and corrects a viral headline about it having Android Auto.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-06-02] **AUTO FOCUS Ep. 2 — the tech-reviewer's lens on a supercar.** "The real reason this car caught my
  attention was **Apple CarPlay** — I've never tried it; Teslas don't have it." He reviews the half-million-dollar
  Lamborghini primarily as a vehicle for a **software** feature — the defining Auto Focus move (a phone reviewer
  pointed at cars). Confirms the series is rolling (Ep. 1 was the Model 3, [[2018-01-18-yt-9O5PhuW927w]]).
- [2018-06-02] **CarPlay/Android Auto as the fix for bad car UIs.** "**In-car systems are almost all bad at
  navigation** — CarPlay and Android Auto are the two best chances new cars have at connecting to your phone in a
  way people actually use." CarPlay turns the Lambo's dial-and-keyboard UI into your familiar iPhone home screen +
  Siri; a 1:1 mirror of compatible apps. Nitpick: Siri doesn't show the text of your dictated reply. (Notes
  **Android Auto's compatible-app list is longer.**)
- [2018-06-02] **"The opposite of a Tesla."** Driving impressions: everything is **low** (forehead-level roofline,
  a shimmy to get in/out under the scissor doors); driving modes **Strada / Sport / Corsa (race) / Ego**;
  "**sensory overload** — huge wheels, grip, brakes, power, and sound, louder than you think, all the time. **It's
  kind of the opposite of a Tesla.**" A memorable EV-vs-ICE contrast from an EV guy. Aside: "even in 2018, **nice
  to see a screen without a notch.**"
- [2018-06-02] **The headline correction (his fact-check instinct).** "The headlines said this is the most
  expensive car with **Android Auto** — that's actually one of the reasons it got on our radar. We plugged in and
  tried; **as far as we, and Lamborghini, could tell, it does NOT have Android Auto — just CarPlay** (and it's not
  even on Apple's official CarPlay car list). The headlines are wrong." Publicly corrects the record.
- [2018-06-02] **No verdict (honest scope).** "If you want a buy rec, I'm not there yet — I only had a day and a
  half. But it was a lot of fun." Refuses to over-claim from short exposure.
