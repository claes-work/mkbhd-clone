# The State of Bezels! (2018) (@mkbhd, 2018-03-14)

## Summary
A teach-the-mechanism essay on the industry-wide **march to bezel-less** and why the **notch** exists as an
awkward transitional stage. Walks through every component that has to be relocated to kill the bezel, uses the
Vivo Apex concept as the proof-of-concept, and lands a memorable read on the notch: "not the future, not even a
feature — just an awkward middle stage."

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-03-14] **The framing — a march to 100% screen-to-body.** "2018 phone design is all about killing bezels.
  We used to have more bezel than screen and not care; now we hate them. The question is: **can we get rid of
  bezels, and how?**" Sets up the component-by-component teardown.
- [2018-03-14] **The mechanism — what must be relocated.** Side bezels are easy (nothing's in them). The hard
  parts are top/bottom: the **fingerprint reader** (→ in-glass optical, see [[2018-02-21-yt-dRtqFjYYUY0]]), the
  **earpiece** (→ a **piezoelectric speaker** that vibrates the glass, as in the Xiaomi Mi Mix), the mics/light
  sensors (small, movable), and the **front camera** — "the last step; we can't hide it under the glass yet, so
  you get creative." A clean systems explanation.
- [2018-03-14] **The Vivo Apex concept as proof.** The Apex relocates everything — piezoelectric earpiece,
  a huge under-display fingerprint sensor, and a **motorized pop-up front camera** — for a true bezel-less 6"
  screen. But "**it's a concept for a reason**": it likely cost thousands to build, and moving parts are bad for
  durability and waterproofing. Optimistic about the tech, realistic about the product.
- [2018-03-14] **The notch verdict (MEMORABLE).** "This notch **isn't the future — it isn't even a feature.** It
  represents an **awkward middle stage** between the phones of the past and the bezel-less future we want, until
  we figure out where to put the front camera." One of his sharpest one-liners on transitional design.
- [2018-03-14] **Samsung way vs Apple way.** "Two camps: the **Samsung way** — no notch, just shrink and pack the
  bezels tight (better-looking now, more stable); the **Apple way** — aggressive, uglier, but pushing toward the
  bezel-less future." He frames it as a genuine tradeoff, not a dunk.
- [2018-03-14] **"It's a shame people have to copy it" — the headphone-jack parallel.** "It's like when Apple
  decided wireless audio was the future and killed the jack — weird and awkward at first, but it moves us
  forward. The shame is everyone **copying** the notch." Ties to his recurring copy-the-good-stuff / jack-is-dead
  arcs.
- [2018-03-14] **Award callback + a wish.** He gave the **Essential Phone** his 2017 design/build award for doing
  the notch best with today's tech (tiny centered notch, earpiece shoved to the top); hopes a next Essential just
  shrinks the chin further. Consistency with his Smartphone Awards reasoning.
