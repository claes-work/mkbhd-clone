# $750 Mini Boosted Board Impressions! (@mkbhd, 2018-04-18)

## Summary
First look at the **Boosted Mini** electric skateboard, framed through his recurring **"Boosted is the Tesla of
electric skateboards"** analogy — the Mini as Boosted's "Model 3": their most important, cheapest, most-portable
board yet. Casey Neistat (a Boosted expert) appears and helped inform his take (guest — attribution-gated).

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-04-18] **The framing — "Boosted is the Tesla of electric skateboards."** "I've often thought of Boosted as
  the **Tesla** of electric skateboards — both led electrifying their category, both are pricey, and their biggest
  challenge is **getting the price down into a lower market.** If the longboard is their **Model S**, this Mini is
  their **Model 3** — their most important and cheapest yet." His signature cross-domain analogy.
- [2018-04-18] **The Mini's tradeoffs, explained.** 29" (vs 39"), concave deck with a **tail** (kick-turn like a
  normal skateboard), no wheel-gap flex (the battery spans it) so **the tail becomes your suspension/control**.
  Three real advantages from being smaller: **carrying** (doesn't drag), **turning radius** ("shorter wheelbase =
  turn faster — just like Model 3 vs Model S"), and **ride style**. A clear tradeoff walkthrough.
- [2018-04-18] **The lineup — Tesla-style range tiers.** **Mini S** ($750, 7-mile range, orange wheels) vs **Mini
  X** ($1000, ~14-mile range) — "the Mini X is like what **Tesla would call the long-range version** — you're
  paying for range." Extends the analogy to the product ladder.
- [2018-04-18] **BIOGRAPHY — his daily transport switch.** "It's **my new favorite mobile electric skateboard** —
  the quality is that much better than the others I've tried. **I'm switching from the longboard to the Mini** as
  my main form of mobile transportation." A dated personal-habit data point.
- [2018-04-18] **Industry read.** "I'm curious how other e-skateboard makers respond — a lot of them banked on
  being **cheaper** than Boosted; now they have to consider this." His competitive-landscape instinct.
