# iPhone 11 Pro Review: For the Love of Cameras! (@mkbhd, 2019-09-20)

## Summary
The full iPhone 11 Pro review. The pitch is entirely the cameras — an A+ daylight camera "the closest to what the
human eye sees of any smartphone." Reiterates the "pro isn't warranted" critique, and lands the **competition
thesis in camera form: "they're all fighting to make the best camera, and we win from that."**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-09-20] **The A+ daylight camera — "closest to the human eye."** "'In broad daylight I'd go as far as
  saying this has been an **A+ camera — the sharpest, cleanest photos I've seen a smartphone take**, realistic
  (not over-processed) color, excellent dynamic range, next-level tonal mapping. The typical iPhone 11 Pro photo
  is **the closest to what the human eye sees of any smartphone camera — and I'm including the Pixel**, which
  tends to be contrastier/more dramatic.'"
- [2019-09-20] **THE COMPETITION THESIS — camera edition.** "'I'm really happy about this new focus on smartphone
  cameras — the last year of presentations spent more time on the camera than any other feature, and **they're
  all fighting each other to make the best possible camera to put in our pockets — and we win from that.**' (Pixel
  4, Huawei Mate 30 Pro, OnePlus 7T all right around the corner.)" Competition value again (cf.
  [[2019-09-05-yt-gNK_C78sBWY]], [[2019-07-02-yt-YPln3JP_gKs]]).
- [2019-09-20] **Ultrawide + night mode — great, with caveats.** "**Ultrawide:** 'finally — everyone else did it;
  it's very consistent in color/white balance but noticeably a step down in detail (smaller sensor).' **Night
  mode:** 'a great start; the iPhone is more **true-to-life** (Pixel's Night Sight does more of a "nighttime→
  daytime" transformation and lifts shadows more), and takes the most detailed low-light shots — but **it doesn't
  turn on very often and there's no manual toggle, which isn't very pro**, and no night mode on the ultrawide.'
  Idea: **a blind-camera-test bracket just for night mode.**"
- [2019-09-20] **The design/battery/display recap.** "Design 'grows on you' (the matte finish helps a lot — 'I'll
  take a matte option every time'). **Battery is excellent** (7-8 hrs SOT), largely because **ditching 3D Touch
  freed room for a bigger cell.** The brighter display is nice, but: '**what would customers notice more — slightly
  more brightness, or shrinking the notch a bunch?**'"
- [2019-09-20] **The "pro" critique + hyped-too-early-for-2020.** "'I still don't think there's enough **pro** to
  warrant the name — the features I was looking forward to (**shrink the notch, USB-C, high-refresh ProMotion,
  in-display Touch ID, reverse wireless**) just aren't here. That's got me **hyped way too early for the 2020
  iPhone.** But this package is pretty damn good — unless you have an XS, it's **worth the upgrade just for the
  love of cameras.**'"
