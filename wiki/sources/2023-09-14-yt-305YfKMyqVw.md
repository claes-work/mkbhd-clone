# iPhone 15 Event Reactions: Sneaky Updates! (@mkbhd, 2023-09-14)

## Summary
Reactions to Apple's Wonderlust event — "not much got announced, but the stuff between the lines was interesting."
A big personal beat: **Apple quoted his 15" MacBook Air review on stage** ("the 15-inch MacBook Air is going to
instantly rocket to the top of the lineup") — "they didn't tell me, didn't ask, didn't pay" — a **full-circle
moment** (from zero YouTubers at these events years ago to creators now being part of the media). The sneaky
updates: the **Apple Watch S9/Ultra 2** (visually identical, an S9 chip enabling 2,000/3,000-nit brightness, a
precision-finding UWB chip, on-device Siri — **"it'll be bad but faster"** — and the new **double-tap gesture, which
foreshadows the Vision Pro's pinch-to-select**); the **AirPods Pro 2 USB-C case is secretly new hardware** (barely
mentioned, but it enables lossless low-latency audio with the Vision Pro — "they don't want to talk about it so
people who just bought the Lightning ones don't feel bad"). And the **carbon-neutrality** push (the Apple Watch is
Apple's first fully carbon-neutral product; aiming for 2030): despite a self-congratulatory Mother Nature skit, he
lands on a **"net positive" analogy** — like a YouTuber filming a good deed, "if they really cared they'd do more"
is true, but the real improvements still happened, so **"I can't get mad at the improvements they're actually
making"** (he wants to dig in with experts later).

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2023-09-14] **Apple quoted my review on stage — full circle.** "'It was a complete surprise when one of the first
  things they did was quote my MacBook Air review on stage. They didn't tell me, didn't ask, didn't pay for it. It's
  a cool full-circle moment — I remember 6-7 years ago when there were zero YouTubers at these events, then one,
  then a dozen, and now we're all just part of the media, independent creators doing our thing.'"
- [2023-09-14] **The double-tap foreshadows the Vision Pro; Siri: bad but faster.** "'The one genuinely new watch
  feature is the double-tap gesture — and it's the same pinch-to-select gesture used in the Apple Vision Pro. Also,
  they moved all Siri requests on-device — that should be faster and more efficient. It doesn't mean Siri's going to
  be good; it just means it'll be bad but faster.'"
- [2023-09-14] **The AirPods USB-C case is secretly new hardware.** "'This might be the ultimate sneaky update — the
  AirPods Pro 2 barely got mentioned. But the USB-C version isn't just a new port: unless you have a Vision Pro,
  they're the same, but there's actually new hardware and architecture so the USB-C version can do lossless
  low-latency audio with the Vision Pro. They don't want to talk about it, so people who bought the Lightning ones
  last week don't feel too bad.'"
- [2023-09-14] **Carbon neutrality — the net-positive analogy.** "'Apple couldn't stop talking about the
  environment, including a self-congratulatory Mother Nature skit — and the Apple Watch is its first fully
  carbon-neutral product. It's like those YouTube videos where someone gives a homeless person $100 on camera:
  "if you really cared you'd do it off camera" is probably true, but that person still got $100 — some net positive
  was still done. I can't get mad at the improvements they're actually making. I'm not an expert; I want to dig in.'"
