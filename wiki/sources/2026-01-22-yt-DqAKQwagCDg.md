# Xiaomi 17 Ultra - More Camera than Phone! ★★

## Summary

Marques reviews the Xiaomi 17 Ultra Leica Edition as **"all camera that happens to have a
phone attached to it."** He was primed to be disappointed by the wall-to-wall Leica
branding, but it turned out "a much more thoughtful execution than I was expecting." His
verdict: not the *absolute best* smartphone cameras, but **the best, most *fun* camera
shooting experience he's ever had** — a real 1-inch main sensor, a 200MP variable-focal
telephoto, deep Leica look emulation baked into capture, and a haptic **camera ring** that's
"the iPhone's camera control but way more physical and flexible." And underneath it's still
a real Xiaomi flagship (silicon-carbon, IP69). ★★.

## Key claims

- **[2026-01-22]** The framing: in 2026 phones are "50% computer, 50% camera" — the 17 Ultra
  "just went all the way." Everything (unboxing, a Leica-logo case + aluminum lens cap +
  microfiber cloth + wrist strap; **"Leica" appears on the phone more than "Xiaomi"**) screams
  "I'm really trying to buy a camera." (paraphrase)
- **[2026-01-22]** Cameras: a **1-inch 50MP main (f/1.67)**, a genuinely-wide **14mm 50MP
  ultrawide**, and a **200MP telephoto that's a real variable-focal zoom** (75→100mm, f/2.39–
  2.96 — you can punch in with all 200MP before cropping). (paraphrase)
- **[2026-01-22]** The verdict: **"I wouldn't crown it the absolute best set of smartphone
  cameras… but I would still crown it the best, most *fun* smartphone camera shooting experience
  I've ever had"** — reliable quick autofocus, a big sensor for detail/shallow depth, "which is
  what you want out of a camera." It still does the classic Xiaomi skin-tone brightening. (quote)
- **[2026-01-22]** The **Leica partnership goes beyond filters**: Leica Vibrant/Authentic
  profiles, plus **Leica Essential modes emulating specific Leicas** (the M9 daylight-locked
  contrasty look; the M3; the **M11 Monochrom** with real grain) — **built into the capture
  process, not undoable** — "artsy, fun, not the highest quality, but fun." (paraphrase)
- **[2026-01-22]** The standout hardware: a **metal camera ring** with **haptics that simulate a
  notched click** — twist to launch the camera, then map it (per mode) to **zoom / exposure /
  manual focus / shutter / white-balance Kelvin.** "It's like the iPhone's camera control, but
  way more physical and clearly with way more flexibility." (quote)
- **[2026-01-22]** Underneath it's a real flagship: **Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5**, a 6.9" 120Hz
  3,500-nit display, a **6,800mAh silicon-carbon battery**, 90W wired / 50W wireless, and it
  **keeps full IP69 despite the moving camera ring.** He **shot most of his Auto Focus Tesla
  Model Y Performance review on it in 8K video** (with an external mic). (paraphrase)
- **[2026-01-22]** Flaws: the ring is **clunky in real life** (fingers get in the shot at
  ultrawide; easy to accidentally trigger); the "variable zoom" is only **3.2x–4.3x** ("you could
  just take two steps forward"); and it **forgets the 50MP setting**, reverting to 12MP binned
  every time you reopen the app — "I can't think of a real camera that would forget my settings."
  (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2026-01-22]** The close, invoking the famous line: **"the best camera is the one you have
  with you"** — "it's pretty sick that in 2026 you can have that be a full-fledged Leica experience
  that fits in your pocket. Well played, Xiaomi." (quote)
