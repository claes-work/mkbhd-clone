# Hyundai IONIQ 6 is an Awesome Design!

## Summary

Marques reviews the **Hyundai Ioniq 6** and answers his own opening question — *why buy a smaller,
less practical version of the well-liked Ioniq 5?* — with **"because it looks sick."** It's the same
platform/software as the Ioniq 5 in a sleek sedan body (he jokes it's shaped like a Magic Mouse), and
he frames it as **design-first done right**: winning awards by nailing the checkboxes (decent range,
reasonable price, a unique desirable look) without over-spending on materials. Fully Marques-narrated.

## Key claims

- **[2023-04-20]** The thesis: **"Why buy a smaller, less practical version of an established crossover?
  Because it looks sick — that's the real answer."** Same platform/software as the Ioniq 5, in a sedan
  body ("baby Porsche vibes… it looks like a Magic Mouse, maybe the worst thing about it"). ~$45–55k,
  dual-motor ~250 mi (77 kWh), 350 kW charging. (quote)
- **[2023-04-20]** ⚠️ For the money he'd want more range ("300+ for $50k"); ~250 mi is real/honest; the
  sedan back seat is a weakness vs. the Ioniq 5 (sloping roof encroaches on his 6'3" headroom); wired
  (not wireless) CarPlay; the **worst part is the "Tamagotchi-toy" key.** (paraphrase)
- **[2023-04-20]** ⭐ Design-first, done right: **"It's winning awards because it nails the basic
  checkboxes — decent range, a reasonable ~$45k start, and a unique, desirable design/feature set. It's
  a well-thought-out, minimal interior without being overly nice in materials (which would drive up the
  price)."** Praises the pixel-art lights, strong one-pedal via paddle regen, vehicle-to-load. (quote)
- **[2023-04-20]** ⭐ SELF-REVEAL: **"If you gave me a budget to develop a car in this ~$50k range, this
  is probably about where I'd end up — you can get a much nicer interior for $50k, but then it won't be
  electric. This is about as good as you can do."** (quote) Plus the running studio debate: **"sunroof
  or moonroof? We've been talking about this at the studio."** (quote)
