# Turning a Tesla into a Camera Car! (@TheStudio, 2022-02-07)

> ⚠️ ATTRIBUTION: **Marques-led** — he narrates in the first person ("on my car," "the MKBHD
> vision"), and the car being converted is his own Tesla Model S. Subject data. The MotoCrane

## Summary
Marques builds a **MotoCrane "Radical" camera crane onto his own Tesla Model S** to shoot smooth
moving car shots for the main channel. Reveals a car in his fleet (a Raven-refresh Model S) and
documents his car-video production methodology and its prior limitations.

## Key claims (Marques-attributed only)
- [2022-02-07] **BIOGRAPHY — owns a Tesla Model S (Raven refresh):** the rig is built onto "a
  brand new or a relatively new Raven Model S... we're going to build it here on my car." They
  modify an OEM Model S roof rack with industry-standard "speed rail" for vehicle rigging.
  (biography — adds a Model S to the documented fleet; not a contradiction with the later BMW /
  Rivian / Porsche entries — multiple cars over time)
- [2022-02-07] **Why previous car b-roll was limited (production methodology ★):** before this,
  moving car shots on the main channel were gotten by "stand[ing] alongside the side of the road
  and point[ing] it at a car passing by," "boosted-board behind the car," or "hang[ing] out the
  back of Andrew's [hatchback] with a gimbal." All limited: "you can't do this on public roads,
  you can't do this when it's cold outside... it's really tough and obviously not as flexible as
  we want it to be." (production-filmmaking)
- [2022-02-07] **The inspiration — Tesla's own camera car:** he got the idea visiting Tesla HQ
  for the Model Y shoot — "shout out to Tesla for showing me their camera car; all of the shots
  they've ever done on Tesla's website and in Tesla's ads... those super smooth shots as they
  drive along a public road... was shot with a Tesla with a MotoCrane on it." He wanted "the new
  and improved version for the MKBHD vision." (production-filmmaking, biography)
- [2022-02-07] **What it unlocks:** ~12-foot reach; can shoot out front of, next to, or behind a
  car "in any weather condition"; "way better, way safer, way cooler... just unlocks a whole new
  world." Notes it could be a three-person team since the arm mostly sets up in position rather
  than moving. (production-filmmaking)
