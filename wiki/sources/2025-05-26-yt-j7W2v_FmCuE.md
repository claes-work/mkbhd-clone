# Top 5 Android 16 Features: Big Visuals! (@mkbhd, 2025-05-26)

## Summary
His annual "top 5 of the new Android" on the Pixel 9 Pro (Android 16 beta), with the recurring meta-note: **Android
updates have stabilized into subtle refinements, not major overhauls — and this one is "almost purely aesthetic."**
It's the **Material 3 Expressive** coat of paint: everything a little brighter, more playful, and **more like real
physical objects.** His five: (5) **lock-screen customization** (a font-thickness slider, compact/full notification
views); (4) **recent apps** (split-screen surfaced from a dropdown; playful window physics; nice haptics); (3)
**launcher/wallpaper customization** (a slimmer At-a-Glance widget makes room for a new icon row; **wallpaper effects** —
shape cutouts, live weather (fog/rain/snow/sun), and a "cinematic" 3D-motion model); (2) **new physics** — "it feels
like they consciously went through Android to make more things feel like physical objects," most obvious in
**notifications' rubber-band feel** when you unstick one to dismiss it ("completely unnecessary, adds no functionality,
just feels nice — the best expression of Material 3 here"); and (1) — his and probably everyone's favorite — the
**Quick Settings + Notification panel**, "why I've always been a fan of Android over iOS." The update levels it up:
**a glassy transparent overlay** (before Apple's Liquid Glass — "interesting because we know Apple's gonna start doing
this classy stuff too"), a big brightness slider, resizable/rearrangeable toggles with an undo button, and smart split
targets (tap the Bluetooth logo to toggle, the rest of the button to open the menu). Bonus overhauls: a redesigned
sideways battery icon, a translucent app drawer, a reorganized Settings app, and upcoming features (glanceable ongoing
notifications à la iOS Live Activities; a **Find Hub** like Find My).

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2025-05-26] **Android has stabilized — this update is almost purely aesthetic (Material 3 Expressive).** "'The trend
  now is less major overhauls, more subtle tweaks. This latest update is almost purely aesthetic, especially in beta 1
  — it's just built different: everything's a little brighter, more playful, more expressive, like real physical
  objects. They call it Material 3 Expressive, and it actually makes sense.'"
- [2025-05-26] **The new physics make the UI feel like physical objects.** "'It feels like they consciously went through
  Android to make more things feel like physical objects — most obvious in notifications: you can feel the rubber-band
  point where you unstick one from the others to swipe it away. It's completely unnecessary, adds no functionality —
  it just feels nice. The best expression of Material 3 on this phone.'"
- [2025-05-26] **The Quick Settings are Android's edge over iOS — and now they're glassy.** "'My number one, and
  probably everyone's — the Quick Settings and Notification panel, why I've always been a fan of Android over iOS. Now
  it's a glassy transparent overlay (before Apple's Liquid Glass — interesting, since we know Apple's gonna start doing
  this classy stuff too), with a big brightness slider, resizable/rearrangeable toggles and an undo button, and smart
  split targets (tap the Bluetooth logo to toggle, the rest to open the menu).'"
- [2025-05-26] **Wallpaper effects and other refinements.** "'New wallpaper effects — shape cutouts, live weather
  (fog/rain/snow/sun), and a cinematic 3D-motion model that separates a subject from the background. Plus a redesigned
  sideways battery icon, a translucent app drawer, a reorganized Settings app, and upcoming glanceable ongoing
  notifications (à la iOS Live Activities) and a Find Hub like Find My.'"
