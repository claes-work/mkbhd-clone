# Vivo NEX S Impressions: Truly Bezelless? (@mkbhd, 2018-06-15)

## Summary
First look at the Vivo NEX S — the Apex concept made into a real, sellable phone, and "the closest thing to an
actual bezel-less phone right now." The concept-tech (in-glass fingerprint + motorized pop-up front camera)
trickles down into shipping hardware — the payoff of his earlier bezels/Apex coverage.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-06-15] **The bezel-less future arrives — 91.24% screen-to-body.** "The phone I've been wanting since we
  started getting notches. It **cracks the holy-grail 90%** screen-to-body — the highest I've seen in a real
  phone. Tiny top bezel, a ~5 mm chin (like the OnePlus 6 or smaller). The accidental-touch rejection is on
  point." The Apex concept ([[2018-03-14-yt-xIvaQ1Hv2Ug]]) becoming real.
- [2018-06-15] **The concept-tech, trickled down.** The two "where did the front stuff go?" answers: the
  **under-glass optical fingerprint reader** (the one he stress-tested, [[2018-05-15-yt-bSl9PfRX7WY]] — slower/
  finicky but cleverly hidden), and a **motorized pop-up front camera** (with selectable/silenceable sounds) that
  rises for every selfie, Snapchat, IG Live. Ties his explainer threads to a shipping device.
- [2018-06-15] **The moving-parts durability worry (his consistent caution).** "Phones are normally solid-state for
  waterproofing and durability. A motorized camera raises real questions — drops while it's out, pocket
  scratches, app freezes, someone pushing back. Vivo has presumably tested it, but **you never really know in real
  life.**" The same caution he applies to the Oppo Find X same week.
- [2018-06-15] **An honest flagship, China-only.** SD845, 8 GB, 256 GB, 4000 mAh, headphone jack, dual SIM — "a
  full honest flagship, not just a design gimmick" (recalls his tweet: "the rest could be trash and the design
  would still be cool" — but it isn't). Wishes for a US version. Vivo's software "isn't my cup of tea."
- [2018-06-15] **The framing question.** "Is this the future we asked for? Do we hate the notch enough to accept
  **mechanical moving parts** to get rid of it?" Poses the tradeoff rather than declaring it.

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "This is the closest thing we have to an actual bezel-less phone right now."
> "Do we hate the notch enough that we're willing to go to mechanical moving parts to get rid of it?"
