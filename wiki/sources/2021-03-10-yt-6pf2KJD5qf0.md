# ROG Phone 5 Ultimate Review: The Most Ridiculous Custom! (@mkbhd, 2021-03-10)

## Summary
Review of the ASUS ROG Phone 5 Ultimate — "what happens when you get to the limits of what any normal person would
want and blow right past it." The durable notes: a genuinely thoughtful **battery-health charging suite** (steady/
scheduled charging + **bypass mode**), the **headphone jack coming BACK** (the first phone he's seen ditch it one
year and re-add it the next), **18 GB of RAM as "bragging rights,"** surprisingly-decent cameras (a B-minus), and
the framing of a gaming phone as **"a sports car you can also drive on the daily"** — with the big form factor a
sensible trade-off for front-facing speakers.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-03-10] **The battery-health suite — genuinely thoughtful (+ "bypass mode").** "'The 6,000 mAh (dual 3,000
  in parallel, like the OnePlus 8T) charges 0–75% in ~30 min — but they also thought about long-term health:
  **steady charging, scheduled charging (finishes to 100% by your alarm), a manual 80/90% charge limit, and
  "bypass mode"** — when gaming plugged in, it routes power straight to the system so you're not heating/cycling
  the battery. "Who'd have thought — a great idea."'" A promotable pro-battery-longevity feature note.
- [2021-03-10] **The headphone jack came BACK — a first.** "'**Is this the first phone I've seen ditch the
  headphone jack one year and bring it back the next?** I think so — it moved to the fan accessory on the ROG 3,
  now it's back in the phone, with a DAC and a 10-band audio wizard behind it. Approaching too much customization,
  which is awesome.'"
- [2021-03-10] **18 GB of RAM = "bragging rights."** "'18 GB of RAM — "is this some kind of joke?" The performance
  difference vs. a 12 GB S21 is pretty small; **they added the extra 2 GB for bragging rights — to say they have
  the most RAM in any phone** — which is a little sad since you can't even pin apps to RAM like on Samsung/OnePlus.'"
- [2021-03-10] **Surprisingly-decent cameras — a B-minus.** "'One thing you don't expect to be good on a gaming
  phone: the cameras. The 64 MP primary makes **detailed, confident, contrasty photos — reminds me of the Zenfone
  (also ASUS, which won last year's Blind Test).** A bit aggressive on HDR, slow on close focus; the ultrawide/
  macro/selfie are weaker. **A B-minus — better than expected.**'"
- [2021-03-10] **"A sports car you can also drive on the daily" + the form-factor trade-off.** "'ZenUI is close to
  stock — **so it's a sports car you can also drive daily** (blinged-out only when you want it). The RGB, the
  ROG-Vision back screen, mappable AirTriggers + back buttons — extra, but if you go niche, go all the way. **The
  easy complaint is the clunky form factor / thick bezels — but on a gaming phone, a little extra to hold + huge
  front-facing stereo speakers is a trade-off that makes sense.**'" (The downsides: bad haptics — surprising for a
  gaming phone — a slow fingerprint reader, no wireless charging, a fiddly USB flap.)
