## Genuinely-new micro-takes (each is one short)
- ★★ **"Stop reading megapixel counts."** He submits **definitive proof that smartphone-camera megapixel counts
  don't matter**: a "super-scientific" **blind test of the best 16 smartphone cameras** of the year — viewers
  vote on photos with the phones hidden, and MP count doesn't predict the winners. *(the short-form statement of
  his signature **Blind Smartphone Camera Test** framework — image quality is processing + tuning, not spec-sheet
  numbers; a direct extension of his anti-spec-sheet / "bias is the point" doctrine.)* [2023-01-06]
  (yt-FG_KaJ6mJzg)
- ★ **Samsung S23 Ultra "moon photos" — computational-photography tell.** Photographing the moon on the S23
  Ultra is "kind of ridiculous" — the folded periscope zoom + processing produce a crisp moon. *(the
  what-is-a-photo / how-much-is-the-camera-vs-the-software adjacency that later blows up into the Samsung
  moon-photo controversy.)* [2023-02-07] (yt-6P1VRs68Cqo)
- ★ **Qi2 = "Apple giving MagSafe to Android."** The new **Qi2 wireless-charging standard is built on Apple's
  MagSafe magnets** — so magnetic wireless charging becomes an **open cross-ecosystem standard.** *(a
  standards/interop node — the walled-garden feature going universal, same thread as his RCS/tracking-standard
  takes.)* [2023-01-11] (yt-jl8bTiMiEn0)
- ★ **iPhone "slower charging" headline — actually a clean-energy W.** iOS 16.3.1 didn't nerf charging; it added
  **Clean Energy Charging** (timing top-ups to when the grid is greener). *(his debunk-the-scary-headline
  register — same instinct as the batterygate-myth PSA.)* [2023-02-27] (yt-W9_NAXd6VyE)
- ★ **"4 ways AI can save Microsoft" — the Microsoft–OpenAI thesis.** Recaps that **Microsoft invested $1B in
  OpenAI back in 2019** (DALL·E, ChatGPT) and how Bing/Office integration could reposition Microsoft in the AI
  race. *(tech-industry-commentary; his early read on the MS-OpenAI alignment.)* [2023-01-20] (yt-DUp3P12lZQM)
- ★ **AI-generated music (MusicLM).** "The same way DALL·E turns text into art, MusicLM turns text into music" —
  a text-to-music demo. *(AI-authenticity adjacency for a new medium.)* [2023-02-05] (yt-gyXjazZnJAU)
- ★ **Collab quality — the Realme Coca-Cola phone.** "Most brand collabs are half-assed — 'just print my logo on
  the back and pay me' — but this one actually committed." *(echoes his designed-not-logo-slapped ethos, same as
  the Atoms shoe.)* [2023-02-21] (yt-nmwJzPPgjpw)
- **Most-overpriced tech 2023 = the Intel Mac Pro** — Apple's still-Intel tower kept at a premium long past its
  relevance (should be replaced by Apple Silicon soon). *(a value/overpriced take.)* [2023-01-25]
  (yt-7V_XEgSjrM0)
- **The Oppo Find N2 Flip** — "the new best-designed flip phone" (a smaller crease + a bigger, more useful cover
  screen than the Z Flip). [2023-02-24] (yt-2m_y5d68xMM)
- **The Vivo X90 Pro Plus** — a "ridiculous" $1,100 flagship (faux-leather back, 12 GB RAM, 80 W charging, a
