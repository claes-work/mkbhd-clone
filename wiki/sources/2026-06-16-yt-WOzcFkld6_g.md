# The Most Interesting Displays In The World!

## Summary

A **Dope Tech** episode on the most interesting displays Marques could gather: a magnetic
**touchscreen add-on for MacBooks** (the Magic Screen), a spinning **volumetric "hologram"
display**, a **52" 6K ultrawide** he'd actually use to replace his dual monitors, and an
exclusive first look at **Project Aura** — XREAL/Google/Qualcomm Android XR glasses that sit
between smart glasses and a VR headset. The recurring note: less than a year after "smart
glasses are actually good now," the face-computer space has exploded with choice.

## Key claims

- **[2026-06-16]** The **Magic Screen (Intricuit)** — a magnetic, USB-C, touch-sensitive flexible
  glass that gives any MacBook a corner-to-corner touchscreen (it "pretends to be a trackpad"). Well-
  thought-out (a folio stand, a travel tuck, a USB-C port that doubles as a close-stopper, a stylus).
  But it has a **ticking expiration date** given the rumored **OLED touchscreen MacBook Pro** later
  this year — which will *also* make this better, because Apple will finally have to make **macOS more
  touch-friendly** (bigger touch targets — "the stoplight window controls are too small to press on
  the first try"). A ~$150 Kickstarter version is planned ("makes me nervous"). (paraphrase)
- **[2026-06-16]** The **volumetric display** ("a.k.a. a hologram," technically not) — **two displays
  back-to-back spinning at 900 RPM (~15×/sec)**, pulsing LEDs to place floating pixels in 3D space.
  A full rotation is **480 slices**, so **30fps video needs a ~7,200Hz refresh** — "an incredibly
  complex feat of processing" (plus headroom for motor-error correction). "One of the most interesting
  pieces of tech that's ever come through the studio." (paraphrase)
- **[2026-06-16]** The **Dell 52" 6K ultrawide** ($3,000; 6144×2560 ≈ 129 PPI like a 27" 4K; slightly
  curved; 120Hz; IPS, 99% P3) — **"the first single monitor I actually feel like I could use to
  replace dual monitors"** (he's been a dual-monitor person). The key is a built-in **KVM for 4
  computers** + picture-by-picture partitioning + Thunderbolt (140W power, 2.5GbE). "It might cost two
  displays, but it kind of *is* two displays." (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2026-06-16]** **Project Aura (XREAL × Google × Qualcomm)** — an exclusive first look at Android XR
  glasses **"right in between"** smart glasses and a VR headset. Its own computer (wired to a phone-
  sized **puck**), environment tracking (two corner cams + one RGB), micro-OLED **1920×1200/eye, 72fps,
  70° FoV**, **91g**, an **electrochromic tint** (clear = light sunglasses, tinted = pitch black), and
  a pin-a-video-to-your-vision feature ("perfect for doing the dishes while listening to the Waveform
  podcast"). (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2026-06-16]** Aura's pitch is **split computing / modularity** — the puck (Snapdragon Reality
  Elite, doubling as a touchpad + fingerprint reader) runs the OS/AI and can be **upgraded separately
  from the frames**; **3ms tracking latency vs ~12ms on the Vision Pro.** "Less than a year ago my
  video was titled 'Wait, smart glasses are actually good now,' and eight months later we already have
  all this choice… that's fascinating." (paraphrase + quote)
