# Antigravity A1: A New Take on Drones!

## Summary

A **sponsored** showcase of the **AntiGravity A1**, a drone built around a **360° camera**
(same lenses as the Insta360 X5) instead of a gimbal — so you fly to *position* the camera and
**frame the shot in post**, with the drone/propellers stitched invisible. Marques (who calls
himself "not a drone expert") frames it less as a tool than as **genuinely fun** — flown via
FPV goggles + a single joystick where you can freely look around. Per his usual sponsored-video
discipline, he also volunteers real critiques. *(Ledger/frontmatter flag the AntiGravity
sponsorship; treat product praise as sponsored, not an unpaid verdict.)*

## Key claims

- **[2026-06-29]** ⚠️ **Sponsored by AntiGravity** (stated up front; he negotiated to include his own
  critiques). He's "not an expert" on drones ("really good but expensive tools"), but the A1 is "a
  genuinely new, interesting take… not just a tool to get aerial footage, but also just fun." (quote)
- **[2026-06-29]** The concept: **no gimbal — two lenses (top + bottom) make it a flying 360 camera**
  (the same sensors/lenses as the **Insta360 X5**), so you **frame in post**; stitching makes **the
  drone and propellers invisible**. **8K/30 orb capture**, replaceable lenses, **249g** (the regulatory
  sweet spot), obstacle avoidance, auto-return, 20GB internal storage ("every camera should have
  internal storage"). (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2026-06-29]** Features: **Sky Genie** (auto-orbit a selected subject) and his favorite **Deep
  Track** ("drones and car shoots go hand in hand" — fly straight, match the car's speed, it frames
  without keyframing). Battery: 24-min standard / 39-min extended — but **battery anxiety feels lower**
  because you're not chasing the perfect in-air framing. (paraphrase)
- **[2026-06-29]** The fun factor: an **FPV goggle** feed with **OmniLink** — fly one direction but
  **freely move your head to look around** ("like a bird flying through the park"); 90° FoV goggles
  (the widest), an exterior screen so bystanders can see what you see, and a **single joystick** ("I
  was skeptical, but it makes sense — you're positioning the camera, not doing camera moves in the
  air"). **SkyPath** lets someone re-fly a recorded path live in 360 (pro tip: sit them down "because
  they'll look down and see themselves hundreds of feet up"). (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2026-06-29]** He **gave it to Andrew** at the studio for a day — a first morning shot → a dynamic
  sport-mode/FPV afternoon shot; "I really think anyone can pick this up and be confident within an
  hour." (paraphrase)
- **[2026-06-29]** His volunteered critiques: a **slight stitch smudge on the horizon line** (usually
  unnoticeable but can appear in horizon-heavy shots); the goggles "look kind of crazy in public — be
  ready to explain"; and he **wishes for a top strap** on the goggles for weight distribution. Notes
  they're working on **goggleless gesture/ring-remote flight** — "I appreciate they're still finding
  ways to let people get creative." (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2026-06-29]** "**This is not a review**, but… one of the most interesting hardware products I've
  seen in a while." (quote)
