# 2019 Microsoft Surface Family Impressions! (@mkbhd, 2019-10-02)

## Summary
First look at the 2019 Surface holiday lineup (Pro 7, Laptop 3, and the ARM-based Pro X). Notable for a
**biography** point — Marques says the **Surface Laptop 3 will likely be his next laptop** — and for his
appreciation of Microsoft's minimal, clean-lines industrial design.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-10-02] **BIOGRAPHY — the Surface Laptop 3 is likely his next laptop.** "'I really liked the Surface
  Laptop 2 — I was waiting for USB-C. Now the Laptop 3 has it. The **13" matte black Surface Laptop 3 will
  probably be my next [laptop], because I'm not tied to macOS / Final Cut on the go anymore** (I don't edit on
  the go).'" A concrete, datable personal-gear signal — he's moving off Mac for mobile (ties to
  [[2019-06-28-yt-dZ_Ab1XxxKg]]).
- [2019-10-02] **Praise for Microsoft's minimal industrial design.** "'Microsoft has really gotten the **clean-
  lines aesthetic** down — the layman might say it looks like a MacBook, but it's **even cleaner**: no finger-scoop
  cutout (just a perfect hinge weight and slight overhang), and the speaker fires up **through the backlit
  keyboard** to avoid grille cutouts.' A stated appreciation for cutout-free minimalism."
- [2019-10-02] **The Surface Pro X — the bigger surprise (ARM).** "'When they break out the **X** name, things are
  getting serious. **7.3 mm thin** (thinner than a lot of phones), two USB-C ports, a custom **Qualcomm-developed
  SQ1** ARM chip with a neural block, LTE, 13 hrs battery. At **$1,000 it feels very competitive with the higher-
  end iPad Pro** — each has advantages (iPad: app store + 120 Hz; Pro X: full Windows + two USB-C + a better
  keyboard).'"
- [2019-10-02] **The Laptop 3's split-silicon twist.** "'Interesting: the **13" uses Intel Ice Lake**, but the
  **15" uses AMD Ryzen** (Vega graphics) — cool to see those side by side.' Fast charging across the line
  (0-80% in an hour)."

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "The 13-inch Surface Laptop 3 in matte black will probably be my next, because I'm not tied to macOS on my
> laptop anymore now that I don't edit in Final Cut on the go."
> "The layman might look at it and say it looks a lot like a MacBook, but it's even cleaner looking than a MacBook
