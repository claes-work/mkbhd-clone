# Samsung Galaxy S9 Camera: What's New (@mkbhd, 2018-02-28)

## Summary
A dedicated deep-dive on the S9/S9+ camera — the "one feature that saved this from being called the same phone
as last year." Reiterates the variable-aperture explainer with more precision and walks the rest of the camera
(composite HDR, super slow-mo mechanics, AR Emoji, Bixby Vision).

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-02-28] **The camera is the whole story.** "It's become pretty clear this announcement was **saved from
  being the exact same phone as last year by one feature — the camera.**" Frames the deep-dive's purpose.
- [2018-02-28] **Variable aperture, precisely.** S9 = single 12 MP OIS with **dual aperture (f/1.5 ↔ f/2.4)**; S9+
  adds a fixed-f/2.4 2× telephoto. Auto shoots f/2.4 by default and **opens to f/1.5 below ~100 lux** (low light);
  Pro mode lets you force either. Context: "even the **Pixel 2, one of the best smartphone cameras ever, has a
  fixed f/1.8.**" He's already posting back-to-back f/1.5-vs-f/2.4 shots to Twitter for readers to judge.
- [2018-02-28] **The rest of the camera.** Improved **composite HDR** (stacks ~12 frames, auto, better DR/noise/
  contrast); the new sliding camera UI (iPhone-like, "encourages you to explore modes"); a genuinely deep **Pro
  mode** (shutter/ISO/manual focus/WB + the aperture toggle).
- [2018-02-28] **Super slow-mo mechanics (the honest fine print).** 960 fps @ 720p → 0.2 s becomes 6 s; it **crops
  in a lot** (back up for framing), only records 0.2 s, and offers manual or auto (motion-box) triggering. A
  clear-eyed how-it-actually-works walkthrough rather than a spec brag.
- [2018-02-28] **AR Emoji, again — worse than Animoji.** Relies only on the front camera (no depth sensor), closer
  to Bitmoji; "I didn't appreciate how smooth Apple's Animoji was until I went back to it after this — way
  better." Consistent with his impressions read.
- [2018-02-28] **Bixby Vision + the verdict-in-advance.** Bixby Vision does QR/wine/places/shopping/reverse-image/
  live-translate/makeup. His pre-review call: "just from the sample photos, this is going to be **one of the top
  smartphone cameras of the year — it's just a matter of #1, #2, or #3.**" Calibrated, evidence-first.
