# iPhone 13 Unboxing & Impressions! (@mkbhd, 2021-09-21)

## Summary
An unboxing + 5-day first impressions of the whole iPhone 13 lineup (a lighter ingest; the full review follows).
The durable notes: **credit for recycled materials** (no plastic shrink wrap this year); the **underrated battery
improvement** (his most surprising change); the **macro auto-switch with no manual override** (a control gripe);
**ProRes isn't here yet** (coming via software); and **ProMotion 120 Hz is "about damn time."** His advice: if you
were eyeing the 13, **consider a freshly-discounted 12.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-09-21] **Credit for the recycled materials (no shrink wrap).** "'The thin box (no power brick) — and this
  year **no plastic shrink wrap either, so give Apple a little credit for the recycled materials to offset their
  environmental impact** (recycled box, aluminum, phone design; just a white — not colored — Apple sticker).'"
- [2021-09-21] **The underrated battery.** "'It's ~0.25 mm thicker and ~20 g heavier (I notice, I'm calibrated to
  the 12), **mostly from a bigger battery — which may be the most underrated new change. Nearly 5 hrs screen-on
  with half battery left on the 13 Pro — crazy good.** Even without fast charging, making it a bit thicker/heavier
  for a bigger battery: thumbs up.'"
- [2021-09-21] **The cameras — bigger sensors, a 3x tele, + two quirks.** "'All sensors bigger (still 12 MP); the
  ultrawide is much sharper across the frame; the Pro's tele is now **3x** (up from 2.5x); a "super duper thick,
  damn boy he thick" camera bump. Two quirks: **(1) ProRes isn't here yet — a coming-soon software feature; (2) the
  macro auto-switch (to the ultrawide within ~14 cm) has NO manual override — it still says "1x" but the natural
  bokeh is gone and I can't switch back. On a Pro phone, at least give me the 1x button.**'" His control-gripe.
- [2021-09-21] **ProMotion — "about damn time."** "'The notch is 20% smaller (makes almost no difference — no
  battery-percentage option). But the Pros' **120 Hz ProMotion is legit — very nice, and about damn time (I'll say
  the same when it comes to the Pixel 6). Even better than the iPad Pro because it's OLED now** — animations at
  120 Hz right off the bat, even pre-launch apps.'"
- [2021-09-21] **The advice — consider a discounted 12.** "'Friends asking "should I upgrade?" — wait for the full
  review. But it's such a minor set of changes that **if you were eyeing the 13, consider a freshly-discounted 12**
  (so similar). The 13 mini fixes the 12 mini's bad battery; the Pros have 120 Hz. **For people on older phones (X,
  8), this is a good buy again.**'"

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "This may be the most underrated new change on these iPhones — I have been very impressed with the battery here."
> "It still says 1X on the right side, like it's still coming from the main camera when it isn't... on a Pro phone
> I would like at least to be able to hit the 1X button and switch back."
> "The 120 Hz ProMotion, though — it's legit, it's amazing, and it's about damn time."
