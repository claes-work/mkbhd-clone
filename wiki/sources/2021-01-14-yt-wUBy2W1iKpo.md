# Samsung Galaxy S21 Impressions: New Year, New Price! (@mkbhd, 2021-01-14)

## Summary
First impressions of the $799 Galaxy S21 and $999 S21+ — same phone, two sizes AND two materials (the S21 is
plastic, the S21+ is glass, the only thing between the $200 price gap). The durable idea: **it's technically an
update, but he won't call it an upgrade** — because Samsung quietly **removed features** (microSD gone, glass →
plastic, 1440p → 1080p) — even as the **lower $799 price is a genuinely good move.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-01-14] **Two sizes AND two materials — the plastic/glass split.** "'Usually it's two sizes; this time it's
  two sizes AND two materials — **the S21 is a matte finish over polycarbonate (plastic), the S21+ over glass,
  which is all that accounts for the $200 gap.** I found the split strange at first, but coming down to $799 I can
  let it slide.'"
- [2021-01-14] **"Plastic has its place" (reprise).** "'Plastic has a weird reputation — it's not that bad, it
  just seems cheap in expensive phones, but **it's less likely to shatter, you can do more with color, and they
  look very similar** (the S21 is just a bit lighter).'" Consistent with the "embrace the plastic" doctrine.
- [2021-01-14] **THE KEY VERDICT — "an update, but I won't call it an upgrade" (the lost features).** "'The
  displays are now adaptive 48–120 Hz — but I won't call it an upgrade, because **they technically lost another
  feature: the screens dropped from 1440p to 1080p** (after years at 1440p). Stack it up — **microSD gone, glass →
  plastic, 1440p → 1080p** — "it's not a big deal because a lot of us ran 120 Hz at 1080p anyway, but you don't
  like getting less than last year."'" A pointed, dated critique of feature regressions.
- [2021-01-14] **The lower price is the right move.** "'It's mainly a spec bump (Snapdragon 888 / Exynos 2100) in
  a new contour-cut design with tweaks that let them **lower the price to $799 — I like that lowered price; I think
  it's a good move.** It'll just depend on how you feel about what they cut.'"
- [2021-01-14] **The contour-cut design — "no copying, and I'm into that."** "'The contour-cut camera stands out
  most in the purple (gold cutout wrapping around the rails — "like a two-piece suit"). **It doesn't resemble
  anything else I've seen — no copying — and I'm into that** (wish there were more color combos, like red and
  black).'"

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "It's technically an update, but I'm not going to call that an upgrade until I test it more."
> "We had expandable storage in a microSD card slot — now it's gone. We had glass — now it's plastic. We had
> 1440p — now it's 1080p... you don't like getting less than last year."
> "I do like that lowered price — I think that is a good move."
