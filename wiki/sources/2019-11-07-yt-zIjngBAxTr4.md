# iPhone 11 Pro vs Original iPhone! (@mkbhd, 2019-11-07)

## Summary
A retro side-by-side of the 2007 original iPhone and the iPhone 11 Pro. Two durable payloads: an explicit
**"why year-over-year phones exist"** thesis, and several **biography** anchors (his first phone was the LG
Voyager; his first iPhone was the 5s; Retro Tech Season 1 arrives early December).

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-11-07] **THE THESIS — why iterative phones exist (a defense of "boring" updates).** "'For people who
  complain this year's phones are just last year's plus small bumps — **that's kind of the whole point.** Besides
  making money, the reason for yearly phones is to offer a **small year-over-year improvement so that when you
  finally upgrade 3-4 years later, it adds up to a large difference.** It's a **mature market — it's very rare we
  get a truly revolutionary device. But back in 2007, this [the original iPhone] was that.**'" A reusable frame
  for reviewing incremental phones.
- [2019-11-07] **BIOGRAPHY — his first phones.** "'Fun fact: **I've never owned a 1st/2nd/3rd-gen iPhone — the
  first iPhone I ever owned and reviewed was the iPhone 5s.** When the original launched it was AT&T/Cingular only,
  and **I was a high-school kid on Verizon, so my first smartphone was an iPhone competitor — the LG Voyager**
  (resistive, non-multitouch). I did enjoy it — I reviewed it.'" Concrete, datable origin details (cf. the iPod
  Touch as first-tech-purchase, [[2019-05-31-yt-xjFPX7EoINs]]).
- [2019-11-07] **The appreciation-for-progress lens (his signature optimism).** "'The original's 2 MP camera is
  basically a webcam — noisy, impossible to nail focus/exposure. But **that comparison is what makes it
  incredible** — this is the best Apple could make 12 years ago vs today, which has me **really pumped about the
  best we'll have another decade from now.**'" A recurring forward-looking optimism.
- [2019-11-07] **The multitouch-glass leap + design lineage.** "'When it launched, the most revolutionary part was
  the **glass display with 10-finger multitouch** — a big leap for mobile UI ("not all touchscreens are created
  alike," vs the resistive Voyager). The **dedicated mute switch** is one of the most significant Apple designs —
  they've kept it on every iPhone since.'"
- [2019-11-07] **BIOGRAPHY — Retro Tech Season 1 details.** "'A shameless plug: **Retro Tech is coming back — we
  finished shooting and editing six episodes** of iconic retro tech, arriving **right at the beginning of
  December,** with some amazing guests.'" A datable production milestone (after the Game Boy pilot).

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "The whole reason they're coming out with phones over and over again year after year... is to offer a small bump
> year-over-year, and then when you do feel like upgrading, it adds up to a larger difference three or four years
> later."
> "My first smartphone was an iPhone competitor called the LG Voyager... not all touchscreens are created alike."
