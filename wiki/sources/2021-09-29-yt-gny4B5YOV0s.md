# RED V-RAPTOR Impressions: 8K 120fps! (@mkbhd, 2021-09-29)

## Summary
First impressions of the RED V-RAPTOR — his **first big camera change in years** and a major production milestone.
It's the new **DSMC3** body (same 8K Vistavision sensor as his DSMC2, but a new computer → **8K 120 fps full frame,
600 fps at 2K**). The durable threads: his **one-person-operation need** as a creator (why RED, why the accessory
build); the **built-in-vs-modular trade-off** (this cube builds everything in — no redundant media, no HDMI, a weird
audio port); "the ecosystem doesn't exist yet"; and the plan to **shoot the entire iPhone 13 Pro review on the
V-RAPTOR.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-09-29] **His longest-single-camera run — and the one-person need.** "'It's been the longest I've ever used
  a single camera — because these RED cameras are incredible and there's no way I'd need more for YouTube. Cinema
  cameras are often designed for a crew — but **as a creator I need a one-person operation to be possible.** I got
  into RED for the world-class image quality AND because I could set it up to shoot by myself (the DSMC2 modular
  setup + the perfect accessories).'"
- [2021-09-29] **DSMC3 — the RED tick-tock (same sensor, new body/computer).** "'RED has a **tick-tock cycle:
  upgrade the sensor, then the body, not at once.** The V-RAPTOR (DSMC3) has the **same 8K Vistavision sensor as
  the DSMC2, but a new body + a new computer — so slightly better noise reduction and, crucially, higher frame
  rates: 8K 120 fps full frame, and crop in for 600 fps at 2K.** A boxy Komodo-like cube with a Canon RF mount +
  phase-detect AF.'"
- [2021-09-29] **The Stormtrooper edition — "a white camera isn't the best for shooting gadgets."** "'I like the
  white-and-black accent (the limited **Stormtrooper** edition) — but **as someone who shoots mostly dark,
  reflective surfaces (gadgets, screens), a white camera shows up in reflections a lot. That's on me.**'"
- [2021-09-29] **THE BUILT-IN-vs-MODULAR trade-off + "the ecosystem doesn't exist yet."** "'The downside of building
  everything in (vs the modular DSMC2): **you only get what RED gives you or you adapt — no redundant media (one
  card at a time), no HDMI out, one weird 5-pin audio port (no XLR).** And **how much I love it will come down to
  the accessories, and that ecosystem doesn't exist yet** (they've partnered with Core for batteries, Angelbird
  for cards, SmallHD for a monitor — hopefully more third-party stuff comes). **There's no easy way to hold this
  box; the back record button is hard to hit — it's not really meant for one-person shooting the way I do.** (A
  V-RAPTOR XL is quietly listed for 2022, with more I/O.)'"
- [2021-09-29] **The plan — shoot the iPhone 13 Pro review entirely on it.** "'We're going to **dive off the deep
  end and shoot the entire iPhone 13 Pro review on the V-RAPTOR** — all a-roll, b-roll, the intro. I'll adapt EF
  lenses (few RF), work off one 660 GB card (8K raw fills up fast), and try higher ISO (I'd avoid over ~800–1000
  on the old REDs; better computer + noise reduction here). Real-time findings on the Studio channel.'"
