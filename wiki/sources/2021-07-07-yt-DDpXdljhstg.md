# iPadOS 15 Review: Dropped Expectations! (@mkbhd, 2021-07-07)

## Summary
Review of iPadOS 15 on the M1 iPad Pro — framed by **"expectations are the thief of joy."** He'd hoped the huge
M1/16 GB hardware would finally get software that lets the iPad replace a computer (Final Cut, etc.); iPadOS 15 is
a nice update (widgets anywhere, more-visible multitasking, a great Notes app) **but "didn't change how I use the
iPad or get it closer to replacing a computer."** The durable thesis: **the iPad is destined to live between the
iPhone and the Mac, because Apple also makes computers and won't cannibalize its own products.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-07-07] **"Expectations are the thief of joy" — the dropped expectations.** "'The M1 iPad benchmarks higher
  than a 16" MacBook Pro with 16 GB — so I got my hopes up for big software (real apps like Final Cut). But
  **expectations are the thief of joy — there was almost no way it could live up.** iPadOS 15 is nice, but it
  **didn't change how I use the iPad or get it much closer to replacing a computer.**'"
- [2021-07-07] **The genuine wins — widgets anywhere, an app library, more-visible multitasking, Notes.** "'You
  can finally **put widgets anywhere (like Android tablets for a decade) + an App Library.** Multitasking is the
  same function but **more visible/memorable** (the three-dots menu → full/split/slide-over). And the **new Notes
  app (Quick Notes — drag a tweet/photo/link in, highlight a page) is so good it's tempting** — but I'll keep
  Pocket, because it syncs to my Android phone: **"I'll be damned if I have one more plug-in to the ecosystem."**'"
- [2021-07-07] **THE THESIS — the iPad has to live between the iPhone and the Mac.** "'For the "what is a computer"
  marketing — **at the end of the day, this company also makes computers. If they're going to keep selling those,
  the iPad basically has to stay firmly between the iPhone and the Mac. They didn't become a $2-trillion company
  by being bad at business — they're not going to cannibalize their own products.** That's just where the iPad
  lives, until they prove us wrong.'" A promotable read on the iPad's deliberate ceiling.
- [2021-07-07] **The iPad-only challenge — it didn't push me over.** "'I keep doing this **iPad-only challenge**
  (no laptop for a while). **iPadOS 15 didn't lengthen the time before I got frustrated and had to grab my laptop
  — but it made the time I was on the iPad that much better.**'"
- [2021-07-07] **The gripes + the perspective flip.** "'Still **no weather app** (a huge widget that just opens
  Safari to weather.com — "you bought Dark Sky, make a weather app") and **no calculator app.** But the flip side:
  this software is also on all the $329 iPads in schools/homes — **for everyone not trying to replace their whole
  computer, all those iPads just get better for this.**'"
