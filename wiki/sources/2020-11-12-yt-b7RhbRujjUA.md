# HomePod Mini Review: Big Sound, Tiny Box! (@mkbhd, 2020-11-12)

## Summary
Review of the $99 HomePod mini — which "flips the script" from the original ($350 speaker he **called "the
dumbest smart speaker"**). The durable thesis: **the more Apple things you use, the better this is** — a clean
articulation of ecosystem lock-in as a buying calculus. He deliberately **talks about sound quality last**
(it's the least of what you buy a small smart speaker for), rates **Siri still behind Google/Alexa**, and
**HomeKit a distant third in smart-home compatibility.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-11-12] **THE THESIS — "the more Apple you use, the better it is."** "'My take on the HomePod mini is:
  **the more Apple things you use, the deeper you are in Apple world, the better this thing is.**' Just an iPhone
  → Siri basics + texts/calls. Add Apple Music → it gets a lot more useful (the U1 "beam" — touch the phone to
  hand off music — is the most impressive part). Add an Apple TV, Apple Podcasts, the intercom "final boss mode"
  → it keeps compounding.'" A promotable ecosystem-lock-in framing.
- [2020-11-12] **Sound quality LAST — the deliberate reordering.** "'I'm talking about **sound quality last,
  because it's one of the last things you're actually looking for in a small smart speaker.**' When I get there:
  for 3" tall it gives a lot of audio, good bass, even sound from all directions — clearly better than a Google
  Home mini or Alexa Dot at higher volumes.'" A reviewer-priorities signal.
- [2020-11-12] **Siri is still behind — say it plainly.** "'Siri knows more facts now, does multiple timers
  without a screen — but if the **best assistant is what you're after, Google or Amazon give you a better one.
  I still think that's true.**'" A consistent, dated Siri verdict.
- [2020-11-12] **"Peek over the walls of the garden" — the honest smart-home advice.** "'If your only goal is
  starting a smart home, it's smart to **peek over the walls of that garden at the competition:** Amazon Alexa
  has a zillion more choices and, most importantly, **by far the most smart-home compatibility (~100k skills);**
  Google is second; **Apple HomeKit is a distant third.**' You could get three Google Home minis for the same
  $99.'" A rare willingness to point buyers outside Apple.
- [2020-11-12] **The buy-matrix + the Spotify wish.** "'Not the best deal, not the most compatible, not the best
  assistant — **but if you want the seamless Apple-ecosystem experience and sound quality, that's when you get
  the HomePod.** (Spotify's "potentially coming soon" and up to Spotify — fingers crossed, since I use Spotify
  for all my music.)'"

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "The more plugged in you are to Apple stuff, and the more Apple services and devices you use, the better
> HomePod is for you."
> "I'm actually going to talk about sound quality last, because I think it's like one of the last things you're
> actually looking for... when you're buying a smaller smart speaker like this."
> "It's smart to start peeking over the walls of that garden a little bit and looking at the competition."
