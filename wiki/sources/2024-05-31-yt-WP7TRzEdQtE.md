# Lucid Air Sapphire is The Best Performance Sedan Ever Made!

## Summary

Marques reviews the **Lucid Air Sapphire** ($250k flagship) and calls it **"one of the best sedans
ever made — I'm not saying electric, one of the best sedans ever made… Peak EV right now, as good
as it gets."** After a week of daily driving on New Jersey roads he finds it **top-of-class on every
axis** — driving dynamics, storage, range, build, tech — and defies-physics quick (1,234 hp in drag
mode, sub-2s 0–60) while riding compliant. His verdict crowns it his EV benchmark. Fully
Marques-narrated.

## Key claims (biography marked ⬥)

- **[2024-05-31]** ⭐ Thesis: **"This is one of the best sedans ever made — I'm not saying electric,
  one of the best sedans ever made… it's Peak EV right now, this is as good as it gets."** Everything
  is "top of its class"; "there are maybe two things that aren't top of the class." (quote)
- **[2024-05-31]** vs. the Tesla Model S Plaid: "the Plaid is a great daily driver, but it's just a
  faster Model S — they didn't do a whole lot else." The Sapphire adds bespoke everything: mixed-
  compound Pilot Sport 4S tires (regular center for rolling resistance, cup-compound shoulders for
  cornering), in-house traction control, a carbon roof, huge carbon-ceramic brakes. (quote + paraphrase)
- **[2024-05-31]** Drive: "it weighs over 5,000 lb and drives nothing like it." Three modes — Smooth /
  Swift (both 760 hp) / **Sapphire (1,100 hp, "feels like Plaid mode")**; "a rocket ship on rails…
  somehow faster than a Plaid in a straight line because of the mechanical grip, and the suspension is
  still compliant — it absorbs New Jersey potholes." **"The best-driving sedan I've ever tried, and it
  may be the best-driving sedan in the world."** (quote)
- **[2024-05-31]** Best-in-class tally: build, storage (power front AND rear trunk — "one of the only
  EV sedans with a full power front trunk"), rear-seat legroom, **400 mi of honest range**, materials,
  tech. ⚠️ The two knocks: the **key-fob battery died mid-shoot** ("maybe the one thing that's not
  great"); no NACS port yet. (paraphrase)
- **[2024-05-31]** ⭐⬥ VERDICT (a strong personal preference): **"If you made me daily-drive any EV in
  the world right now and it couldn't be a Tesla, it would be this — I'd have to matte-black wrap it (I
  don't love how it looks), but it's just way too good at driving not to pick it. I don't see any
  downsides. I think it's the best sedan in the world."** (quote)
