# Google Pixel 3a Review: A for Ace! (@mkbhd, 2019-05-24)

## Summary
The full $399 Pixel 3a review after ~a week. Resolves the "flips the script" impressions
([[2019-05-08-yt-vcaXm9dgcpI]]): flagship-class camera, a "meh" display, fine-not-fast performance, and
excellent battery. The "A for Ace" grade — one notch below the pixel 3's "A+" camera, still miles ahead of the
class.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-05-24] **The camera earns an "A."** "Same rear sensor/glass/OIS and software as the pixel 3. If the
  pixel 3 is an **A+**, this is an **A** — and still **miles ahead of any camera in this price range**, especially
  in hard light (into the sun, low light). **Night Sight is still on another level.**" Confirms the impressions.
- [2019-05-24] **No Pixel Visual Core — almost no effect.** "The 3a **lacks the dedicated Pixel Visual Core**, so
  it processes on the Snapdragon 670 instead. **Almost zero effect on final image quality** — the only tell is
  photos take **a little longer to process** after the shutter." A specific spec-vs-outcome observation.
- [2019-05-24] **The display is the real downgrade.** "A 5.6" 1080p OLED that **doesn't get very bright** (tough
  outdoors) with **early off-axis color shift** (goes cool/green). For someone who **pixel-peeps and edits
  photos on the phone**, that's the meaningful compromise — more than the specs." His honest weak-point call.
- [2019-05-24] **Performance: fine, not fast.** "Snapdragon 670 / 4 GB — it **performs like the pixel 3: fine.**
  Gets the job done, not as smooth as high-end, still struggles with RAM management (background apps closing).
  Coming from the OnePlus 7 Pro ('smoothest, fastest phone I've ever used') makes it feel like it 'chugs
  along.'" Comparative context.
- [2019-05-24] **Battery is a genuine win.** "Only pushing 1080p with low-power internals + physically larger
  battery than the flagship brothers → **consistently 5+ hrs screen-on-time**, better than the pixel 3." A place
  the budget phone *beats* its flagship sibling.
- [2019-05-24] **The nuanced verdict + the ad critique.** "It's **not just 'the pixel but cheaper'** — it cuts
  where other mid-rangers wouldn't (specs, no wireless charging, no water resistance). But for **best photo
  quality + best software for $400, you're looking at it.** (Aside: Google's ads say 'phone X' not 'iPhone X' —
  'why not just say pixel 3a vs iPhone, wouldn't that make the point more powerful?')" A marketing-craft note.

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "If pixel 3's image is an A+, then this is an A — and that's still miles ahead of any other camera of any
> phone in this price range."
> "It just kind of feels like it chugs along, doesn't care too much about smoothness, and just eventually does
> what you say."
