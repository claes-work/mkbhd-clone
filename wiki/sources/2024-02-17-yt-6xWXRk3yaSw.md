# This is the Worst Car I've Ever Reviewed (Fisker Ocean)

## Summary

**The original Fisker Ocean review** (the one he re-visits in "Ocean 2.0") — and Marques calls it
**"the worst car I've ever tested, but probably not in the ways you're expecting."** The
fundamentals (looks, space, comfort, drive) are genuinely fine; what sinks it is a **laggy, buggy
mess of software and half-baked choices** that only reveal themselves once you live with it. He
states a policy outright: **he won't wait for a promised future software update — he reviews the car
real buyers are driving now.** Fully Marques-narrated.

## Key claims

- **[2024-02-17]** ⭐ The verdict + a values statement: "This might be the single weirdest reviewing
  experience I've ever had… **it's just the worst car I've ever tested, but probably not in the ways
  you're expecting.**" (quote)
- **[2024-02-17]** ⭐ POLICY: Fisker didn't want him to review this one (**a big 2.0 update is coming**) —
  **"it's not really in my policy to wait on promised future software updates. I'm going to review the
  car that's out now that real buyers are actually living with"** (he promises to summarize the 2.0
  roadmap at the end). Got the car from a **Mitsubishi dealership** because Fisker couldn't supply one.
  (quote)
- **[2024-02-17]** The fundamentals are fine: a handsome ~$75k midsize crossover (Model Y/Ioniq 5/Mach-E
  class); good materials, comfortable, quiet, soft suspension, **"the best back-seat experience of any
  EV it competes with,"** ~320 mi real (of 360 claimed), solar roof (3–6 free mi/day claimed). "I really
  wanted this to be good — there are unique ideas here I wanted to see make it to other cars." (quote)
- **[2024-02-17]** ⚠️ The living-with litany: **buggy software (an error on almost every startup)**; a
  failing 360 reverse camera; **no hill-hold (it rolls backward on hills)**; Bluetooth that constantly
  disconnects; a **500-launch-control lifetime limit**; a screen you rotate by holding an unlabeled
  button 5 seconds; a distracting red/blue acceleration screen; no glovebox (just trays); a walk-up key
  that needs several tries; solar panels with **no readout of what they're generating.** (paraphrase)
- **[2024-02-17]** ⭐ The diagnosis: **"The theme here is it's just a young company that doesn't really
  know exactly what they're doing with a lot of these choices yet, or hasn't considered everything
  necessary to make a great daily car."** "You could give me this car and I wouldn't want to drive it."
  (quote)
- **[2024-02-17]** He still roots for it ("competition is good, the fundamentals are solid") and lists
  the promised 2.0 fixes (torque split, auto-hold, a solar energy meter, OTA-while-charging). (paraphrase)
