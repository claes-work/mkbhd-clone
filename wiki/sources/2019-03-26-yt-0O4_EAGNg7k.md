# Huawei P30 Pro Impressions: The Ultimate Camera? (@mkbhd, 2019-03-26)

## Summary
First look at the P30 Pro — a camera-focused hands-on. Explains the new quad-camera system (the RYYB sensor, the
periscope zoom) with his teach-the-mechanism instinct, ahead of the full review. Sponsored (LastPass).

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-03-26] **The quad camera, explained.** "**(1) A 40 MP 'Super Spectrum' main sensor with an RYYB array**
  (instead of RGB) — lets ~**40% more light** hit the sensor (up to 4× the ISO, ~409,600), so a great night mode.
  **(2) A 20 MP ultra-wide** — "**if you have multiple lenses and no ultra-wide, what are you doing?**" (it's gone
  from niche to mandatory). **(3) A ToF depth sensor** (portrait/AR). **(4) The 8 MP PERISCOPE zoom** — the optics
  **bend down into the phone and use a mirror** (like a periscope) for a **lossless 5× optical**, up to **50×
  digital 'super creep mode'** with impressive stabilization." His teach-the-mechanism instinct on new hardware.
- [2019-03-26] **"Super creep mode" — cool but questionable.** "50× is absurd — **who needs to zoom in on people/
  buildings blocks away? Are you a spy?** The stabilization is extreme even handheld. But I haven't shot samples yet,
  and the **autofocus seemed jumpy close up** — the concept's cool, we'll see about the execution." Skeptical-but-
  curious.
- [2019-03-26] **The rest — an S10 competitor that checks the boxes.** Gradient colors (the **Aurora** blue is his
  favorite — "reminds me of a classic Windows fish wallpaper"); a full-screen 6.47" OLED; an **optical** in-display
  fingerprint (not ultrasonic — so it has the optical cons); a smaller camera-only notch (the Face-ID sensors gone);
  Kirin 980, 4,200 mAh, IP68, the "**2.5 W troll-mode**" reverse-wireless. "**No headphone jack** is about the only
  box it misses (the cheaper non-Pro P30 gains one)." EMUI 9.1 "not my favorite."
- [2019-03-26] **The verdict-in-advance.** "**The S10+ is still the champ, especially in the US (in carriers,
  cheaper).** But for camera nerds who want to win blind tests, or if you **zoom in obscenely far**, the P30 Pro is
  worth a second look." Calibrated pre-review.

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "The periscope zoom — literally the optics go down into the phone and use a mirror to face out, like an actual
> periscope — for a lossless 5× optical zoom."
> "If you don't have that ultra-wide and you have multiple lenses, what are you doing?"
> "50× zoom is absolutely insane — I'm going to name it super creep mode."
