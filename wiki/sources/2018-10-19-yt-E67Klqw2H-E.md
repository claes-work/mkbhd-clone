# Google Pixel 3 XL Review: The Shadow of the Notch! (@mkbhd, 2018-10-19)

## Summary
The full Pixel 3 XL review — "maybe the smartest smartphone I've ever used, with caveats." The thesis, borrowed
from The Verge and endorsed: **"the best camera in a smartphone got a better phone."** Also his sharpest framing
of the notch as a **catch-22** that overshadows an otherwise great phone. (His first widescreen video.)

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-10-19] **THE THESIS — "the best camera got a better phone."** "**Pixel 3 takes the best photos of any
  smartphone — better than the iPhone XS, RED Hydrogen, any Samsung/LG, and the Pixel 2.** A masterclass in
  **computational photography** and how much software matters in 2018." He endorses The Verge's title. A+ detail/
  dynamic-range/autofocus, warmer-than-Pixel-2 colors; his only gripe: HDR sometimes too aggressive (halos).
- [2018-10-19] **One lens, because software (his thesis restated).** "In a world of 3–4 cameras, **Google is
  stubborn with one — as they should be, because their software and image processing is everything.**" Features are
  software: **Top Shot, Night Sight (coming), Super Res Zoom, Google Lens live, Now Playing** ("love this"), **Call
  Screen** ("super good — I get more spam calls now; the live transcription lets me hang up on robots"). Best selfie
  camera he's seen; best stabilization ("looks like a tripod"); but **iPhone is still the best video** (no 4K60,
  washed colors, weak mic — "not the one for vlogging").
- [2018-10-19] **THE NOTCH — a "poor use of space" + the catch-22.** "**The notch is a metaphor for the whole
  phone — you can't talk about the phone without talking about it.** It's not much bigger by volume than the
  iPhone's, but **narrower and taller**, which shifts the UI down and loses the 'ears' — **a poor use of space.**"
  The catch-22: "**if we only talk about the notch, we ignore how good the rest is; if we don't, people think we
  like it. So how good this phone is gets overshadowed by how bad the notch is — and that's everyone's fault.**"
  A sharp meta-observation.
- [2018-10-19] **Super Res Zoom underwhelms (honest).** "I was hyped, but past ~5× it goes **watercolor**; side by
  side, the **iPhone's telephoto preserves more detail.** Cool concept, still needs work to catch optical zoom."
  1×–3× "always looks great."
- [2018-10-19] **The soft-touch-glass scratch + the 4 GB memory bug (longevity watch).** The frosted glass scratches
  easily (micro-scratches buff out, but a real gash appeared while shooting). And a **memory bug**: opening the
  camera can kill a background music app — "**I wonder if 6 or 8 GB of RAM would fix it**" (ties to his 4 GB flag /
  Pixel 2 slowdown thread). Notes Google already promised to fix the off-center-video bug he flagged — "shows
  they're listening."
- [2018-10-19] **Who it's for + why Google made it.** "**Get this phone for the camera and/or the software —
  both best in class; beyond that there isn't a huge reason.** I don't think Google cares if it sells much — they
  care about **flexing what they're good at: software, ML, AI.** I'll be carrying it for the camera." A clear-eyed
  read of Google's intent.
