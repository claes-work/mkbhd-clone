# Google Pixel Buds Pro Review: Just Get These! (@mkbhd, 2022-08-30)

## Summary
A review of the $199 Google Pixel Buds Pro — and an easy recommendation ("**you could just close this video and go
pick these up**"). Fun setup: **he literally predicted these** at the end of his 2020 Pixel Buds review (a more
premium "Pixel Buds Pro" with ANC and a bigger battery) — and here they are, cheaper than expected ($199, already
on sale below the old buds). They **"nailed it from every turn — the case, the fit, the sound, the features."** The
**underrated compact case** (wireless charging, fits a coin pocket); they **dropped the wing tips** (less locked-in
but more comfortable); **really good sound with strong, punchy bass** — but **no EQ yet** (coming) and a slightly
low max volume; a B− transparency mode (**AirPods still win**); and the standout is the software — **Assistant a
hold away is "a superpower,"** plus multi-device switching, a seal-check, and 7/11-hour battery. His only ask:
**push that EQ update.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-08-30] **I predicted these — and they nailed it.** "'At the end of my 2020 Pixel Buds review I said maybe
  they'd do a premium "**Pixel Buds Pro** at $279 with ANC and a bigger battery" — **and that's exactly what this
  is, except only $199** (already on sale below the old buds). **They've nailed it from every turn — the case, the
  fit, the sound, the features.** You could just close this and pick these up.'"
- [2022-08-30] **The underrated case + dropping the wing tips.** "'The **case is underrated** — a compact, clean,
  well-made case with a satisfying magnet, USB-C + wireless charging, that fits my jeans' coin pocket (so many buds
  cases are annoyingly thick). And they **dropped the wing tips** — the upside was they locked in for running, the
  downside was a pressure point after an hour or two — **so they're less locked-in but more comfortable; I think
  it's the right move** (I'll use a wingtip like the Beats Fit Pro for actual workouts).'"
- [2022-08-30] **Really good sound, strong bass — but no EQ, low max volume.** "'They **sound really good** (new
  11 mm drivers, crisp, minimal distortion, great ANC on white noise, no perceivable Bluetooth lag). Two quirks:
  **much stronger, punchier bass than the last buds (a character highlight for hip-hop, but I wanted an EQ to even
  it out for podcasts) — and there's no EQ in the app or on the Pixel yet (a 5-band EQ is coming).** And a
  **slightly low max volume** (the bottom 40% of the slider is useless).'"
- [2022-08-30] **Assistant is a superpower; AirPods still win transparency.** "'Every ANC bud "shall have" a
  transparency mode — this one's a **B− (AirPods are still in a league of their own).** But the **Pixel Buds'
  advantage is the software: Assistant a hold away is a superpower** ("how tall is the Taj Mahal," turning on smart
  lamps). Plus multi-device switching (phone + laptop), Find My, in-ear detection, an **ear-tip seal-check.**
  The one control that didn't work well is swipe-for-volume (too small a surface — I just used the phone).'"
- [2022-08-30] **Just get these — push the EQ.** "'A slightly larger body improved battery: **7 hrs with ANC,
  11 without.** For $199, should you just get these? **Probably, yeah — easy to recommend.** (AirPods if you're in
  that world; better-sounding buds exist; a wingtip for workouts.) These are the ones going in my backpack. **Well
  done, Google — please push that EQ.**'"
