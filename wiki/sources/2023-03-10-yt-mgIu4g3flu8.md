# The Objective Top 10 Phones List (Waveform, 2023-03-10)

> ⚠️ **Ensemble podcast.** Hosts: Marques Brownlee, Andrew Manganelli, David Imel.
> No speaker labels; only **clearly-Marques** takes train the persona.

## Summary
A tongue-in-cheek "objective top 10 phones" list, but the durable content is Marques on why
**new-phone-color videos over-perform** and a camera-color observation, plus TikTok's paid
"Series" feature.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated 2023-03-10)
- ★ **[Marques, clearly — creator-business]** He'd normally never make a video about a phone
  just getting a new color — but he does, because they **over-perform**: his **purple iPhone**
  video "last year" and a fast **red iPhone unboxing** each pulled **~5 million views**.
  "If you just look at the view count, you understand why this video exists." *(a candid
  admission of following the data even on "trivial" topics — cf. the effort↔performance
  inverse.)*
- ★ **[Marques, clearly — color/camera nerd]** A saturated **red phone notoriously
  over-saturates the red channel of people's cameras** and starts to **look pink** on video
  when it isn't — so "if you watch videos on a new color you might not really know what the
  color is." His signature musing: **"I can't prove that my red is the same as your red."**
  *(production/color-science sensibility.)*
- **[group — creator economy]** **TikTok "Series"** launches paid content (collections up to
  80 videos / 20 min each, priced $1–$190, ~100% to creators minus fees) — following
  Twitter/Instagram/YouTube into creator paywalls.
