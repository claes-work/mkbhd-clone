# Dear Twitter! (@mkbhd, 2022-11-08)

## Summary
A "Dear Twitter" open letter, days into Elon Musk's chaotic takeover, from a place of love (**13+ years, 50k+
tweets**; his earlier "Dear YouTube"-style Twitter video's edit-tweets suggestion actually shipped). His thesis on
why Twitter is unique — **a small user base (fewer daily actives than Snapchat) but outsized impact, because it
makes everyone feel reachable.** He argues **"YouTube is the only true S-tier social media platform"** (Elon
replied "for now") and offers the **Virtuous Cycle of the YouTube creator ecosystem** as the model: creators →
content → monetization tools → paid/happy creators → more content. Twitter's move should be **a suite of creator
tools** (rev-share, better than YouTube's cut — Twitter's Super Follows already give creators 97%). On money: **ads
still run the internet; subscriptions cap around ~10% of revenue** (YouTube Premium: ~20M of 2.6B users), so **make
Twitter Blue genuinely good for the creator class.** His **rule #1 of the internet: you probably can't charge for
something that was previously free** (the $8 verified badge is "new money for something that's not new" — and the
benefit accrues to others, so it should be free).

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-11-08] **From a place of love — why Twitter is unique.** "'I really like Twitter (13+ years, 50k+ tweets) —
  and when you like something you want it to succeed. It's unique because **it has a small user base (fewer daily
  actives than Snapchat) but outsized impact — it makes everyone feel reachable** (you can @ an athlete/celebrity
  and they might reply). I'm rooting for it through the chaos.'"
- [2022-11-08] **YouTube is the only S-tier platform — the Virtuous Cycle.** "'I tweeted **"YouTube is the only true
  S-tier social media platform"** (Elon: "for now"). Twitter shouldn't just ape YouTube — but there's a reason
  people who blow up elsewhere convert to a YouTube channel: **the Virtuous Cycle of the creator ecosystem** —
  creators make content → YouTube builds monetization around it → creators get paid/happy → more content, round
  and round.'"
- [2022-11-08] **Twitter should build creator tools.** "'Twitter's asset is **a lot of high-profile creative
  users** — so **a suite of evolving creator tools would make a massive difference.** YouTube has the Partner
  Program (55% rev share), Super Thanks/Chats, memberships. Twitter already has some (**Super Follows give creators
  97% up to $50k** — beating YouTube's cut) but needs more, plus real video ad rev-share so a random user's
  breakout video pays them.'"
- [2022-11-08] **Ads vs subscriptions — the ~10% magic number.** "'The internet runs on ads. Companies add
  subscriptions to reduce ad reliance (Snapchat+, Instagram subs) — but **ads are still where the revenue is:
  ~10% seems to be the magic ceiling.** YouTube has 2.6B users; **only ~20M pay for Premium (~10% of its $28B).**
  Even Netflix just added ads. So to become a subscription-funded free-speech haven, **Twitter has to make Twitter
  Blue genuinely good** (people pay $30/mo for LinkedIn Premium because it delivers real value; my old "YouTube
  Blue" idea was priority processing for large uploads — worth paying for).'"
- [2022-11-08] **Rule #1 of the internet — can't charge for what was free.** "'The $8 verified badge **violates my
  rule #1 of the internet: you probably can't charge money for something that was previously free** — it's new
  money for something that's not new, and **the benefit is to everyone else (verification), not the person paying —
  so it should probably be free.** Even at 100% of 400k verified users, that's ~$77M — a drop in Twitter's ~$5B
  (92% ads). Focus on a **smaller, higher-conversion group of power users/creators**, then you still need ad
  revenue to fuel the cycle. (If posting a 10-min video costs $8/mo on Twitter but makes money on YouTube, I'm
  posting on YouTube.) The $44B won't pay itself.'"
