# iPhone 12 Pro Max Review: The Biggest Ever! (@mkbhd, 2020-11-09)

## Summary
Full review of the $1,099 iPhone 12 Pro Max — "the everything phone," with an **exclusive new camera system**
(47%-larger main sensor, sensor-shift stabilization, 2.5x telephoto). The landmark finding: **despite the first
real camera-hardware jump in a while, he couldn't see a meaningful difference** — which becomes the promotable
**"if you have to put it side by side to see it, most people won't notice it"** tell, plus a definition of **"the
Apple way"** (wait, then perfect what sticks) and a read on the **computational-photography plateau.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-11-09] **"Biggest and best" everything — but no 120 Hz.** "'The Pro Max is **the biggest and best**
  battery (6–7 hrs screen-on, ~25% left), screen (6.7", biggest canvas Apple's made), viewfinder, keyboard —
  the **no-brainer if you want the biggest iPhone.** I'd have really liked **120 Hz** (again, most likely
  battery). Reachability is tough — you can't hit all four corners one-handed.'"
- [2020-11-09] **THE CAMERA FINDING — a real hardware jump, and "not much happened."** "'The exclusive **47%-
  larger sensor + sensor-shift stabilization + 2.5x tele** screams better light-gathering, so I looked for
  better night mode, less need to trigger it, shorter hold times — **and I found none of these to be true. I
  really wanted to see a difference.** Testing it side by side with the 12 mini in low light, **I just didn't
  see it.** Apple finally made a hardware jump and **not much happened.**'" A pointed, dated verdict.
- [2020-11-09] **THE PROMOTABLE TELL — "if you need a side-by-side to see it, most people won't notice."** "'At
  some point I thought, maybe I have to put it side by side — and **that's actually a telltale sign of a feature
  regular people won't notice: if you have to put it side by side with something else to even see it in the
  first place.**'" A reusable reviewing heuristic.
- [2020-11-09] **"THE APPLE WAY" — sit back, then perfect what sticks.** "'There's a lot of crazy camera
  hardware in the rest of the world (50–100 MP, 8K, 3x–10x periscopes) — **but that's not the Apple way. The
  Apple way is to sit back, take the landscape in, see what sticks and becomes an obvious benefit, then grab it,
  make it a little better, make it the Apple thing, fit it into the ecosystem puzzle — unless it's USB-C.**'" A
  promotable characterization of Apple's product philosophy (with the recurring USB-C jab).
- [2020-11-09] **The computational-photography plateau read.** "'In low and regular light it's still really
  impressive — which speaks more to **how good iPhone computational photography has gotten over the years than
  anything about the Pro Max.** The bigger sensor didn't fix the nighttime-video haloing (glowing orbs around
  point lights).' The camera is **not the biggest reason to upgrade** — maybe the 12 Pro or just the 12 is the
  better move.'"

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "That's actually a telltale sign of a feature that probably regular people won't notice — is if you have to
> put it side by side with something else to even see it in the first place."
> "Apple finally made a jump in hardware and nothing really happened. Not much happened."
> "The Apple way is more sit back, take the landscape in, see what sticks around for a while and starts to become
> an obvious benefit, then grab that, make it a little better, make it the Apple thing... unless it's USB-C."
