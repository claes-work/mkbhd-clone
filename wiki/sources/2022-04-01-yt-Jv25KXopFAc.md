## Summary

gamed, and a Marques riff on **EV reservation culture**.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated 2022-04-01)
- ★ **[Marques, clearly — EV reservations / ship discipline]** **EV reservations "don't mean
  anything anymore"** — the market's so hot that putting down $100–$1,000 buys you a **"very
  loose reservation"**: the car probably won't ship in a year, probably not at the price you
  reserved, and probably won't be the exact product you reserved. He contrasts **Apple's
  clockwork discipline** (iPhone event → "pre-orders begin Friday" → ships like clockwork) and
  credits **Sony for getting better at unveil-then-ship-fast** (~4 months). *(the "a prototype
  is easy; shipping is the hard part" doctrine — [[../topics/ev-cars/ev-cars]].)*
- **[group]** The **Dyson Zone** (headphones + air-purifier visor) as the "insane gadget" of
  the week; a segment on gaming the Spotify algorithm.
