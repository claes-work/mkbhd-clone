# Google Pixel 7A Review: New Price! (@mkbhd, 2023-05-10)

## Summary
A review of the **$499 Pixel 7A** (up $50 from the 6A) that opens with a jab: **"Google is the worst company on Earth
at keeping things a secret"** — and he doesn't think it's an intentional-leak strategy, "they're just really bad at
it." For the extra $50 you get **wireless charging** (slow, 7.5 W — good for overnight trickle) and a **90 Hz
display** (bizarrely **off by default** — turn on "smooth display"). Same Tensor G2 + 8 GB as the Pixel 7, so
performance matches; build steps down slightly (plastic back, IP67) but nothing screams low quality. New this year:
**all-new cameras** (a 64 MP main that bins to 16, a 13 MP ultrawide) — and Google tightened the processing enough
to **match the beloved old 6A while taking advantage of the bigger sensor** — "a top-three mid-range smartphone
camera." Battery is a **C-plus** (a full day, not much more). The recommendation twist — **the "middle-child
effect"**: for the price he'd **rather get the six-month-old Pixel 7** (same chip, better cameras, faster wireless
charging, glass back, IP68, often on sale for $500–550). The bigger picture: **Google now sells a Pixel from $350
(the 6A stays) to $1,800 (the Fold)** — the 7A is right in the middle.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2023-05-10] **Google is the worst at keeping secrets.** "'Google is the worst company on Earth at keeping things a
  secret, and I don't think it's on purpose — some think it's an intentional leak for free press; no, I think
  they're just really, really bad at it. Everything about the phone is out before it even comes out. That's just the
  way it is with Pixel anything now.'"
- [2023-05-10] **The $50 buys wireless charging + a 90 Hz display that's off by default.** "'The 6A was $449; this is
  $499, so there should be a good reason for the extra $50 — wireless charging (slow, 7.5 W, fine for overnight) and
  a 90 Hz display. The 90 Hz is funny because it's off by default — out of the box it's a 60 Hz phone until you turn
  on smooth display. At 60 Hz it felt choppy for this price; turn on the 90.'"
- [2023-05-10] **The new camera matches the beloved old one.** "'The 6A rode the famous old IMX363 for four years;
  this has a brand-new 64 MP main (bins to 16, no full-res option). Google tightened the processing to actually match
  the tried-and-true 6A while taking advantage of the bigger sensor — more light, more natural depth. A top-three
  mid-range smartphone camera.'"
- [2023-05-10] **The middle-child effect — I'd rather get the Pixel 7.** "'For the price I'd actually rather get the
  Pixel 7. It's six months older, but same chip and RAM, with more capable cameras, faster 20 W wireless charging,
  glass back, IP68, thinner bezels — a bunch of little things that add up. If you can get the 7 for $500–550, take
  it over the 7A. That's the middle-child effect.'"
- [2023-05-10] **A Pixel from $350 to $1,800.** "'Google is trying this newer thing — more Pixels at more prices.
  They're keeping the 6A at $349 (a really good deal), so now you can get a Pixel anywhere from $350 to $1,800. The
  7A is right in the middle.'"
