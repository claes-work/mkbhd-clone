# YouTube Needs to Fix This (@mkbhd, 2022-04-02)

## Summary
A "Dear YouTube"-style plea to fix **comment-spam impersonation scams** (bots stealing a creator's name and photo
to lure viewers to Telegram/WhatsApp for fake giveaways). The framing quote, from Eric Schmidt: **"every problem
we have is a problem at scale"** — at 2.6B users, a 0.1% problem hits 2.6M people, so YouTube triages by threshold
(dislike-bombing got the whole dislike counter removed) — and **comment spam has crossed that threshold** (a
scanner found ~2,000 of ~6,000 comments on his last video were spam — ~30%). The stakes are personal: **the
comments are "what makes YouTube YouTube"** (his original portal into the community; he used to reply to every
comment). His ask: community tools (Theo Joe's open-source scanner) help, but **nothing beats YouTube building the
fix** — and the concrete request is to **let creators ban words from usernames** (the easiest way to stop
impersonation), invoking **the dislike-removal double standard** as precedent.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-04-02] **"Every problem is a problem at scale."** "'Eric Schmidt said something I'll never forget: **"every
  problem that we have is a problem at scale."** YouTube has 2.6B monthly users, so **a problem affecting just 0.1%
  hits 2.6M people.** They triage by a threshold (a third of users → 4K loads slowly → fix it; a fraction getting
  dislike-bombed → remove dislikes site-wide) — but tiny issues below the threshold still affect millions. **And
  comment spam has crossed that threshold.**'"
- [2022-04-02] **The impersonation scam — and it works.** "'On any recent video, spam comments with a stolen name/
  photo ("thanks for watching, send a DM, I have something for you") aim to get you onto Telegram/WhatsApp. You'd
  think it's too obviously fake — **but every day I wake up to a dozen emails from people checking whether they
  sent the money to the right place.** If that's just the people who email me, imagine how many are falling for it.'"
- [2022-04-02] **The comments are what make YouTube YouTube.** "'The comment section should be a precious priority —
  **it's one of the only places that facilitates real conversations between creators and the audience; it's what
  makes YouTube YouTube.** I used to reply to every comment on every video for years — my portal into the
  community — and now I spend most of my time in there blocking scam channels.'"
- [2022-04-02] **Community tools help, but only YouTube can fix it.** "'A scanner by YouTuber Theo Joe is much
  better than YouTube at catching spam — on my last video it flagged ~2,000 of ~6,000 comments (~30%). **No matter
  how good community tools get, nothing touches the effectiveness of YouTube building a fix themselves** — and we
  know that because the community tools are great and the problem is still here.'"
- [2022-04-02] **The ask — let us ban words from usernames.** "'The concrete fix: **let creators ban words from
  usernames**, so I can block anyone who tries to use my name in theirs — the easiest way to stop impersonation. It
  might sound brute-force (what if someone shares my name?) — but **this is the same company that saw a fraction of
  users getting dislike-brigaded and turned off dislikes for the entire site.** YouTube, get on it.'"
