# PowerBeats Pro Review: Better than AirPods! (@mkbhd, 2019-06-27)

## Summary
Review of the $250 PowerBeats Pro true-wireless earbuds — "I think I just recommended a pair of Beats on video."
Verdict: genuinely better than AirPods (fit, sound, buttons) but overpriced. Reiterates the **"pro is overused"**
gripe and a **physical-buttons-over-touch** preference.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-06-27] **"Pro is overused" — again.** "'Even though everyone in the commercial was a professional athlete,
  the word **pro is overused** in products — why are these the PowerBeats *Pro*?'" Echoes the same complaint from
  the Mac Pro / OnePlus 7 Pro ([[2019-06-04-yt-QJxbqIo5pC0]]) — a recurring MKBHD language peeve.
- [2019-06-27] **Better than AirPods where it counts — fit and sound.** "'AirPods but if Apple actually cared
  about them staying in people's ears.' The **replaceable soft ear tips + the over-ear hook** keep them in
  (AirPods never fit his ears); they **sound better** (bass-heavy but 'they're Beats, I'm not looking for a flat
  sound signature here')."
- [2019-06-27] **Physical buttons > touch controls — a stated preference.** "'Real buttons, no touch points —
  the Beats logo is play/pause, a volume rocker on top. **Better than voice control, better than swiping across
  panels — simple and easy, not over-engineered. Love it.**'" A recurring usability value.
- [2019-06-27] **The downsides — the huge case, the price.** "The **case is huge** (not pocketable — 'makes me
  appreciate the AirPods' tiny case'), Lightning-only (no USB-C, no wireless charging), and **h1 chip** = instant
  pairing + 8+ hrs. But at **$250 vs AirPods 2's $160**, 'I still don't know if I can say they're worth it' —
  points buyers to the ~$110 Jaybird X4 as a cheaper alternative."
- [2019-06-27] **Beats = Apple headphones now.** "'I'm comparing these to AirPods because Apple bought Beats, so
  Beats are Apple headphones now' — and because AirPods are the most popular earbuds everyone has." A framing note.

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "You can think of these as AirPods, but if Apple actually cared about them staying in people's ears."
> "Real buttons... better than voice control, better than the swiping across panels of the sides of headphones —
> it's just simple and easy. Regular volume rocker, not over-engineered. Love it."
