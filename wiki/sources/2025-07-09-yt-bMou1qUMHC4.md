# Samsung Z Fold/Flip 7 Impressions: Major Upgrades!

## Summary

A hands-on / first look at Samsung's Galaxy Z Fold 7 and Z Flip 7, published the day of
Samsung Unpacked. After last year's "tick" (barely-updated Fold 6 / Flip 6), this is the
"tock" — real hardware change. Marques focuses mostly on the Fold: dramatically thinner
and lighter, a flagship-grade 200MP main camera borrowed from the S25 Ultra, and better
usability both open and closed. But he catalogs three downgrades (S Pen removal, a
$100 price bump to $1,999, and a battery Samsung is "playing it too safe" on) and
benchmarks Samsung against Chinese silicon-carbon foldables it isn't matching.

## Key claims

- **[2025-07-09]** The **tick-tock upgrade cycle**: Fold 6 / Flip 6 were the "tick"
  (chipset-only); the Fold 7 / Flip 7 are the "tock" with **actual meaningful hardware
  updates**. (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-07-09]** Fold 7 is much thinner/lighter — ~8.9mm folded, **215g, lighter than
  an S25 Ultra** — and unfolds to an 8-inch tablet; **"better at both closed and open
  usage"** with the same update. (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-07-09]** It inherits the **S25 Ultra's 200MP main camera** — "essentially a
  true flagship camera" on a fold, closing the long-standing foldable-camera gap
  (ultrawide and 3x telephoto remain sub-flagship). (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-07-09]** Three downgrades, in order of significance: (1) **S Pen support
  removed** — most people didn't use it, "YouTubers" will be mad; (2) **$100 price
  bump** to **$1,999** base; (3) a **4,400mAh battery** where Samsung is "playing it
  safe" by not moving to **silicon-carbon** batteries. (paraphrase)
- **[2025-07-09]** Comparison call-out: the **Honor Magic V5** (not even a US phone) is
  thinner, similar weight, and packs a **5,820mAh silicon-carbon** battery — "a big
  difference vs 4,400." Samsung is "leaving something on the table." (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-07-09]** Flip 7: cover display now goes **full corner-to-corner (4.1")**,
  brighter and 120Hz — "more usable than ever while closed"; inner display up to 6.9".
  Another **"better closed AND better open"** win. (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-07-09]** Possible concern: the **Flip 7 moved back from Snapdragon to Exynos**
  (Exynos 2500). Not a bad chip, but not as good as the Snapdragon 8 Elite on
  peak-performance-plus-efficiency — and possibly a **preview of an all-Exynos S26**.
  (paraphrase)
- **[2025-07-09]** Also announced: Watch 8 (circular design; an "antioxidant index"
  toxin measurement via the thumb) and a Flip 7 FE for $899. (paraphrase)
