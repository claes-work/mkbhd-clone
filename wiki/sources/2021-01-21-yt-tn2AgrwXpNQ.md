# Samsung Galaxy S21 Ultra Review: Problems Solved! (@mkbhd, 2021-01-21)

## Summary
Full review of the S21 Ultra — "an incredibly good phone" that goes from really-good to great on **the stuff that
doesn't show up on the spec sheet** (subtle tweaks and off-paper intangibles). The signature line: it **"feels
more like an MKBHD Edition phone than any phone I've ever reviewed"** — as if designed by people who watch his
videos. Also: the elegant **two-telephoto (3x + 10x) zoom logic**, **the ultrawide doing macro (so no macro
camera)**, the **natural-vs-bright selfies option**, and the permanent return of **ICONS.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-01-21] **THE THESIS — off-the-spec-sheet intangibles turn good into great.** "'It does a lot on paper (the
  big ultra numbers) — **but it does a lot of stuff that doesn't show up on the spec sheet: subtle tweaks that turn
  a really good phone into a great one.**' The flattened display (barely spills over the edges), the smaller
  footprint (6.9" → 6.8"; smaller than an iPhone 12 Pro Max), the 1.77×-larger fingerprint sensor (fewer failed
  attempts).'"
- [2021-01-21] **"An MKBHD Edition phone" — designed by people who watch the channel.** "'Without sounding full
  of myself: **this phone feels like it was designed by, or at least worked on by, people who watch MKBHD videos.**
  The matte "Phantom" black (a running preference — "matte black everything"; his car is Phantom); the flattened
  display; a little Google flair in the software; the improved camera experience. **This is the phone I'd have made
  if you handed me an S20 Ultra and said "make any improvements you want."**'" A signature persona moment.
- [2021-01-21] **THE TWO-TELEPHOTO ZOOM LOGIC — why 3x AND 10x makes sense.** "'The 3x + 10x pair seemed peculiar,
  but it makes sense: **1–3x is on the primary, 3–10x is either the primary or the 3x depending on light, and 10x+
  is the periscope** — delivering some of the best 10–30x zoom on any phone. A single 10x would make 1–10x all
  digital; a single 3x would leave the far range below the ultra standard.'" A promotable engineering-rationale read.
- [2021-01-21] **The ultrawide does macro (so no macro camera) + natural-vs-bright selfies.** "'It auto-detects
  extreme close-ups and **switches to macro using the autofocus ultrawide — not a separate macro camera, because
  it doesn't need one** (a jab at padded camera counts). And on first open it **asks if you want Natural or Bright
  selfies** instead of defaulting to bright — "much more natural for someone like me with my skin tone." They
  didn't have to add that.'"
- [2021-01-21] **The camera verdict — the experience changed more than the quality; the fringing remains.** "'Photo
  quality is roughly what you'd expect from a high-end Samsung — **the experience of getting the photo changed more
  than the quality.** Laser AF fixes ~75% of the S20 Ultra's hunting; 100x is more stable (subject-locking) but
  still a gimmick; it still fringes on close-up edges and does 8K at the wrong 24 fps.'"
- [2021-01-21] **ICONS is back — permanently.** "'The limited ICONS drop sold out and a lot of you missed it, so —
  **ICONS is back, permanently, for all 150+ devices** (red/black, triple black), plus a new limited "Silicon"
  colorway and a red-code mask.'" A product/business milestone.
