# 2026 Toyota RAV4 GR - It's the Hottest Car for a Reason!

## Summary

Marques finally tests the Toyota RAV4 — specifically the sporty **GR** version — to understand
why the RAV4 is the "it car." His verdict isn't about the sport package: it's that the RAV4
**"nails every fundamental there is to nail about a car."** The GR adds aesthetics and feel
(tuned suspension, Alcantara seats, an aggressive front) without sacrificing the RAV4's chill,
efficient, practical core. Fully Marques-narrated.

## Key claims

- **[2026-01-13]** The RAV4 is the US "it car" ("dealerships can't keep them in stock… people put
  200,000 miles on them and they never die"); this is the sporty **GR** version — a hybrid (2.5L I4
  + 3 motors, ~320hp, 0–60 in 5.6s, ~50mi EV range). (paraphrase)
- **[2026-01-13]** The core verdict: after a week, **"this RAV4 nails every fundamental there is to
  nail about a car. Period."** — visibility ("maybe perfect"), space, ride, gas mileage (30–35mpg
  highway), practicality, and even good speakers for the class. (quote)
- **[2026-01-13]** ⬥ BIOGRAPHY: he checks rear legroom **"behind my own 6'3 driving position"**
  (reconfirms his height). (quote)
- **[2026-01-13]** Nitpicks: **no play/pause button** on the steering-wheel media controls, and too
  few physical HVAC controls (fan speed buried). "Those are two small complaints; the rest is
  basically perfect." (paraphrase)
- **[2026-01-13]** The GR is "more about the aesthetics and the feel" (a tuned suspension/steering,
  Alcantara-bolstered seats, a big aggressive grille, red-and-black) — he's honest he hasn't driven
  a regular RAV4 sportily, "so I won't tell you it handles dramatically better." But it stays soft/
  comfortable "unlike that BST Polestar," which was too stiffly sprung to daily. (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2026-01-13]** Price unknown at recording (~$50–55k anticipated) — "you're paying up for the
  sportiness." **If you want power over aesthetics, the Tesla Model Y Performance (~$60k) is a
  better answer** (and fully electric). (paraphrase)
- **[2026-01-13]** ⬥ Self-aware sign-off: "is this extremely sporty RAV4 up your alley, or is this
  just me getting excited about a **red and black sporty vehicle like I usually do?**" (quote)
