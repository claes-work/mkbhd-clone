# The MKBHD Setup Tour 2020! (@mkbhd, 2020-01-13)

## Summary
A full tour of his early-2020 **work editing desk** — "the last tour in this space before we move to a new space
in a couple weeks." A biography/workflow goldmine: the exact gear he's used to edit videos, much of it for
6-10+ years, and how his setup evolved.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-01-13] **The framing — a 10+ year work setup, not a home setup.** "'This is **not my personal setup at
  home — this is my work setup**, the culmination of **more than 10 years building a setup specifically to edit
  videos.** I've been sitting here editing what you've seen for **the better part of the last three years.**'"
  A clear biography/workflow anchor — and note: **they're moving to a new space in a couple weeks** (Jan 2020).
- [2020-01-13] **The desk, chair, and the oldest pieces.** "**8-ft custom NextDesk Terra Pro** motorized standing
  desk ('had it since college,' with battle scars), a **Herman Miller Embody chair** ('if you sit for hours
  editing, save your back'), **Yamaha HS8 studio monitors** on acoustic foam (6-7 years), and a **Promise Pegasus
  80 TB RAID** ('backed up all my videos since 2013')." His long-lived, wear-it-in gear philosophy.
- [2020-01-13] **The 2020 centerpiece — dual Pro Display XDR + the Mac Pro he bought.** "'Two **Pro Display XDR
  (nano-texture matte) in 6K** on the Pro Stands — matte works great with all the lights; **no glare, no regrets.**
  So glad to have **dual monitors** again. Underneath the desk is the **Mac Pro — 28-core, 768 GB RAM, Vega II Duo
  — the one I bought (not the review unit); I'm editing this on it,** dropped an 8K Q&A, it's been awesome. No
  airflow/dust problems under the desk.'" His actual daily editing machine (full review pending).
- [2020-01-13] **The control setup + small pro-tips.** "**Keychron K2** mechanical keyboard ('brown switches are
  my favorite so far'), **Logitech MX Master 3** on a worn red artisan mousepad, a **Magic Trackpad** for spaces,
  a **Logitech 4K webcam** magnetically stuck to the display, and an **Apollo Twin MkII** ('a glorified volume
  button' driving the monitors). Pro tip: **pop up a phone's selfie camera to see the ports behind a monitor.**'"
- [2020-01-13] **The recurring SD-card-reader gripe (Mac Pro).** "'The **Mac Pro has no built-in SD card reader**
  — so I keep a dongle on the desk and plug in when needed (a whole weird cabling thing with the motorized desk).'
  Reiterates his 'creative pros need card readers' point ([[2019-11-28-yt-ctWDAdQ81B8]])." Also notes acoustic
  treatment (sound panels, bass traps) for the big monitors.
