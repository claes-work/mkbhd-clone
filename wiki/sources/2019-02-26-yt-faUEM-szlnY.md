# The Galaxy S10's Incredible Display! (@mkbhd, 2019-02-26)

## Summary
A deep-dive on the Galaxy S10's display — "the best display on any phone." Three features explained (the
hole-punch, the ultrasonic fingerprint reader, the panel), with a teach-the-mechanism on ultrasonic-vs-optical
and a nice debunk of the "second front camera."

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-02-26] **"The best display on any phone."** Three things make it unique: the **hole-punch cutout**, the
  **ultrasonic in-display fingerprint reader**, and the **panel quality**. (This is a display deep-dive, not the
  full review.)
- [2019-02-26] **The hole punch + the "dual camera" debunk.** "A **laser-cut hole in the AMOLED** (the first in
  OLED — Samsung's proud of the precision). The S10 has a single cutout, the S10+ a **dual** one — but **the second
  isn't a camera, it's a depth sensor.** The camera app's 'wide' button **literally just crops one wide sensor** —
  cover the right cutout and both 'cameras' still work." A sharp, hands-on myth-bust. "It's basically a notch, but I
  find it a little less obtrusive."
- [2019-02-26] **Ultrasonic vs optical (teach-the-mechanism).** "**Ultrasonic fires high-frequency sound off your
  finger to map the ridges in 3D** — more secure and harder to duplicate than an **optical** sensor (a tiny 2D
  camera snapshot). It's faster **if you touch lightly** (an optical would ask you to hold longer), it **doesn't
  light up the display**, and it **reads through water** — I demoed it beating the OnePlus 6T's optical reader with
  wet fingers." A clear mechanism explanation + live test.
- [2019-02-26] **Screen protectors — regular yes, glass no.** "A **regular screen protector works** with the
  ultrasonic reader (they sell them); a **tempered-glass one doesn't** (it can't read through the glass — that's
  why carriers won't sell you a glass protector for the S10)." A useful, tested buyer note.
- [2019-02-26] **The panel.** 3040×1440 (3K), **1,200 nits peak ("brighter than most TVs")**, curved edges, thin
  bezels. A notable first: **Natural mode looks "a bit muted for the first time ever in a Samsung phone"** (vs the
  saturated **Vivid** mode — "a great word for it"). Ships at 1080p to save battery (bump to 3K in settings).

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "This button is literally just cropping in and out of one camera — the right-hand cutout really is just a depth
> sensor for portrait mode."
> "Ultrasonic uses high-frequency sound waves firing off your finger to map the ridges of your fingerprint in 3D —
> way more advanced than an optical sensor taking a little 2D snapshot."
> "For the first time ever in a Samsung phone, the natural mode actually looks a bit muted out of the box."
