# Apple Magic Keyboard: Floating iPad Pro? (@mkbhd, 2020-04-20)

## Summary
A look at the $300-350 Magic Keyboard for the iPad Pro (used as his main computer for days). The verdict: **"the
best version of the iPad as a computer — but I'd still rather have a laptop."** Anchored by a **"two types of iPad
people"** framework.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-04-20] **THE VERDICT — "best iPad-as-a-computer, but I'd still rather have a laptop."** "'Well-built,
  floating design, the **best keyboard I've seen in an iPad case** (scissor, clicky, no flex, backlit), a clicky
  trackpad, ~100% USB-C pass-through. It really feels like a computer — **but I still need a laptop sometimes.**'"
  Continues his iPad-alternative-not-replacement thread ([[2019-06-28-yt-dZ_Ab1XxxKg]]).
- [2020-04-20] **THE FRAMEWORK — two types of iPad people.** "'**Type 1: keep it in the case/folio forever** (it's
  basically their laptop). **Type 2: pop it out, use it mostly as an iPad, and dock it only when they need to
  type.** For **Type 2, this is the best possible version of the iPad — spring the $300.** For me (multitasking), I
  keep the laptop.'" A clean who-is-this-for split.
- [2020-04-20] **The quirks that keep it from replacing a laptop.** "'**No escape key, no function keys** (brightness/
  volume/backlight all require touching the screen or digging into Settings); it's **top-heavy** (the iPad is the
  computer); **command-Q doesn't quite work**; and **multitasking is painful** — skipping a Spotify track, or a
  Zoom call + notes, "good luck."'" Concrete limits.
- [2020-04-20] **The price math + the honest pull.** "'A **$1,000 iPad + $350 case = $1,350** vs a **$1,200 MacBook
  Air.** If I could only have one, **I want to say I'd take this** — pop off an iPad any time, 120 Hz, more apps,
  cameras, more fun — **but I still need that laptop sometimes,** so I'm not getting rid of it.'" His genuine
  ambivalence.

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "This is the best version of the iPad as a computer, for sure — but I would still rather have a laptop."
> "There are two types of people in this world: those who get a case and keep it in it all the time... or those
> who pop it out and use it mostly like an iPad, then dock it when they need to type."
