# Mercedes G-Wagen EV - Terrible Specs But That's Not the Point

## Summary

Marques does the walkaround/interior of the electric **Mercedes G580 (G-Wagen EV)**, then hands
the driving to co-host **Miles**. His frame: he usually prefers a ground-up EV, but the G-Wagen
is the rare case where "you've got to keep the old design" — an iconic boxy shape "as aerodynamic
as a tombstone" (hence weak ~240mi range), but the point is preserving the G-Wagen experience.
Miles's driving impressions are quarantined.

## Key claims — SUBJECT-ATTRIBUTED (Marques)

- **[2025-10-03]** The framing: **"there are ground-up EVs and there's recycling the old design but
  making it electric. I usually prefer the ground-up EV… but sometimes you've got to keep the old
  design."** The G580 EQ is a middle-tier G-Wagon (not replacing the gas G550/AMG G63). (quote)
- **[2025-10-03]** "The specs aren't great" — quad-motor, ~579hp / 859 lb-ft, ~7,000 lb, 0–60 ~4.1s,
  116kWh — and **"about as aerodynamic as a tombstone,"** so ~240mi range. "But if you want the
  G-Wagon experience but electric, that's what this delivers." (quote)
- **[2025-10-03]** Praise: he's **glad they didn't go all-screens "like the new Porsche Cayenne going
  electric"** — it keeps the familiar Mercedes software + physical luxury; the doors sound "so nice"
  (a classic G-Wagon experience); crazy active bolster seats that hug you in corners. Speakers
  (Burmester) "not as good as I anticipated." (paraphrase)
- **[2025-10-03]** ⬥ BIOGRAPHY: checks the back seat "this is **my 6'3 driving position**… and this
  might be a crazy cross-reference, but it reminds me of **my Rivian R1T.**" (quote)
- **[2025-10-03]** His summary before handing off: "I was pretty impressed — the battery's low so the
  weight's low, it rides really well and feels in line with the luxury of a ~$200k Jeep. But is it
  better than the gas versions? Take it away, Miles." (paraphrase)
