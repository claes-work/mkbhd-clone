# The iPad Only Challenge! (@mkbhd, 2019-06-28)

## Summary
A two-week experiment using an iPad Pro as his only portable computer (11" cellular, 512 GB, iPad OS 13 beta).
The verdict: a "computer **alternative**, not replacement." Rich **workflow/biography** source — his real mobile
computing needs, his RED-footage editing constraint, and Retro Tech production context.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-06-28] **The setup + why the 11" over the 13".** "Ran the iPad OS beta on the **11" iPad Pro** (not the
  13" as planned) because it's 'way more portable and easy to type on' — 'now this is my iPad Pro of choice.'
  Naked, **no case/keyboard folio** ('I don't think there are any good keyboards I like for the iPad... covering
  up this design with a cheap rubber case just feels wrong')." His stated aesthetic stubbornness.
- [2019-06-28] **His actual mobile computing needs (biography/workflow).** "'In between **shooting Season 1 of
  Retro Tech** and traveling, my needs are simple: email, sign attachments, social, **photo editing**, watching/
  publishing videos, music. When I'm back in the studio I work on the **iMac Pro** — a real computer.'" A concrete
  window into his 2019 production split.
- [2019-06-28] **The big win — the photo workflow via the improved Files app.** "'**Canon EOS R → SD card → SD
  reader → the new Files app → pick the exact RAW → Lightroom → edit with the Pencil.** The iPad makes a fantastic
  mobile image editor.'" iPad OS ([[2019-06-08-yt-O2iorLuSjOg]]) is what unlocked this.
- [2019-06-28] **The pain point — typing/ergonomics; and why he still won't video-edit on it.** "'The worst
  ergonomic position is **typing on glass** over long periods — nothing like a real keyboard, and **there still
  isn't a great non-wobbling keyboard case.'** And **'I stopped video editing on the go because RED footage is
  really heavy — laptops can barely keep up, so I bring it to the desktop.'**" A hard constraint of his pipeline.
- [2019-06-28] **INDUSTRY aside — the Jony Ive departure + the rumored 16" MacBook Pro.** "'Still excited for the
  rumored **16" MacBook Pro**... and **with Jony Ive not designing Apple products anymore**, maybe we'll get
  radical new laptop designs — fingers crossed for something worthy of the "pro" in the name.'" A dated
  industry-watch note (and a third "pro in the name" reference).

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "iPad Pro with iPad OS beta feels the closest to — not a computer replacement, but a computer alternative —
> more than anything else I've used lately."
> "I stopped video editing on the go because RED footage is really heavy — laptops can barely keep up, so I just
> bring it to the desktop."
