# AirPods Pro 3 Review: I Understand It Now!

## Summary

Marques — self-described as "not normally an earbuds person" and not an all-day-headphone
wearer — reviews the AirPods Pro 3 and comes away converted: "I get it. I totally get it."
The Pro 3 look nearly identical to the Pro 2 but stack up better sound, a redesigned tip
that finally stays in his ears, IP57, longer battery, a heart-rate sensor, and an ANC
claim Apple made with no asterisk ("world's best"). He scrutinizes that claim, finds it
credible via back-to-back tests, and frames 2025 as **"the year of live translation."**

## Key claims

- **[2025-09-15]** He's "not normally an earbuds person" and not a wear-them-all-day type,
  "but with all the improvements... I get it. I totally get it." (quote)
- **[2025-09-15]** Sound is a notch better than the (already-good) Pro 2 — more bass, full
  mids, crisp highs, louder — thanks to **slightly bigger vents / redesigned airflow** and a
  new tip angle. (paraphrase)
- **[2025-09-15]** The new **foam-bolstered silicone tips + new insertion angle** make these
  "the first AirPods that have ever stayed in my ears" — "a game changer for me" after years
  of AirPods wiggling out. (quote) But he still gives workouts to the **Powerbeats Pro** for
  the over-ear hook.
- **[2025-09-15]** New **IP57** dust/water resistance (up from IPX4) — can survive being
  submerged; the case is IP57 too. (paraphrase)
- **[2025-09-15]** On Apple's **"world's best ANC, no asterisk"** keynote claim: Apple "says
  a lot of things" that are exaggerated (e.g. "studio-quality microphones"), but this one is
  credible — same H2 chip, but the new tips/angle/vents/tuning combine for allegedly **2×**
  the Pro 2's cancellation. In his tests the ANC is "pretty incredible"; on a flight the
  airplane white noise "was almost completely erased." Beats the Bose QC Ultra 2, Sony
  WF-1000XM5, and Galaxy Buds 3 Pro on fit + cancellation. (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-09-15]** New **heart-rate sensor** — "a nice to have," well integrated with Apple
  Fitness (calories, rings); if you also wear an Apple Watch, Apple silently picks the better
  source. Nice for people **without** an Apple Watch. (paraphrase)
- **[2025-09-15]** No charging cable in the box (like AirPods 4) — Apple assumes AirPods
  buyers already have USB-C/MagSafe; "they're probably right." Battery up to **8h ANC** (from
  6) and **10h transparency**. (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-09-15]** **Live Translation** launched alongside (not exclusive to these; Apple
  Intelligence, offline per-language). "2025 is the year of live translation" — he wants to
  do a big comparison of all the versions (phone app vs phone+buds vs smart glasses). (quote)
- **[2025-09-15]** Verdict: better fit/sound/ANC/IP/battery for the same price — "if you're
  an iPhone user, these really don't have any weaknesses. Well played, Apple." He still
  personally prefers **over-ear (his Sonys)** for travel/music and **Powerbeats** for
  workouts. (quote)
- **[2025-09-15]** Aside: the original AirPods were announced **3,295 days ago** and the case
  still looks like "small white glossy dental floss." (quote)
