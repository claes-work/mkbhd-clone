# 2019 Tech I'm Ready For! (@mkbhd, 2019-01-01)

## Summary
A New-Year anticipation slate — a predictions-to-track goldmine. Walks the tech he's most looking forward to in
2019 (the Galaxy S10 hole-punch, a USB-C/120 Hz iPhone, the modular Mac Pro, the Model Y, the EV wave), with
some self-aware hope-vs-realism.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-01-01] **#1 the Galaxy S10 + the hole-punch ("Infinity-O").** "The most interesting 2018 designs came from
  going full-screen while **avoiding the notch** (sliders, pop-ups). In 2019 Samsung's play is the **hole-punch
  display** — single cutout on the S10, dual on the S10+. **Technically it's still a notch** (the pixels around it
  are useless), but it looks different because it lights up — a genius play. **Samsung looks like the one flagship
  keeping the headphone jack AND avoiding the notch.**" (Wish: "put the cutout in the middle.") A dated design-trend
  read.
- [2019-01-01] **#2 the new iPhone ("iPhone 11") — the iPad-Pro wishlist.** "A **tick-tock big-upgrade year.** My
  **favorite tech of 2018 was the new iPad Pro** — so I'm hoping the iPhone gets its **boxy industrial design,
  120 Hz, USB-C** (if they'll do it on the iPad…), and maybe **6 GB RAM.** I'm definitely setting myself up for
  disappointment — no way most of these happen — but that's the fun." A candid predictions-with-caveats list.
- [2019-01-01] **#4 the modular Mac Pro (the video-editor's want).** "Apple **rarely pre-announces**, but they
  promised a **full-size modular Mac Pro.** From the editor side: the **iMac Pro (18-core Xeon, 128 GB, 4 TB) has
  been amazing** for a year, but it's **not modular/upgradable**, and reaching around the back for ports is a pain.
  A tower would let me **swap GPUs and internal parts** — and maybe a **Pro monitor**, so I can be a dual-monitor
  person again (nothing matches next to an iMac Pro)." His most-anticipated. Aside: "**you should not buy the
  trash-can Mac Pro right now**" (Apple keeps selling it at full price pre-upgrade).
- [2019-01-01] **#5 electric cars + why he started AutoFocus.** "**A big banner starting at CES** — more makers
  electrifying (maybe whole lineups). **That's a big reason I started AutoFocus — to look into whether they're
  actually any good.** Expecting the **Tesla Model Y reveal** (the Model 3 of the SUV world, completing the S3XY
  lineup — more production hell, worth it)." A clear statement of the AutoFocus mission + a dated EV forecast.
- [2019-01-01] **The calibrated skips.** "**CES is next week.** Foldables I already covered. I'd have put **5G** on
  the list but I'm **not optimistic it's a 2019 thing**; and **AirPower** — "probably never coming out ever."
  Media-literate hype-discipline. "For me personally, the most exciting is the **new Mac Pro.**"
