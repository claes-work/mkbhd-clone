# Sony WH-1000XM5 Review: Two Steps Forward, One Step Back! (@mkbhd, 2022-05-12)

## Summary
A review of Sony's $400 flagship noise-cancellers, the WH-1000XM5 — "**two steps forward, one step back.**" The
forward: **the most comfortable NC headphones he's ever tried** (softer vegan leather, lighter, a cleaner minimal
design), **even-better best-in-class noise cancellation** (8 mics now, catching higher frequencies too), and faster
charging (3 min → 3 hours). The step back — and it's a big one: **they no longer fold**, so the case is much bigger.
For **travel headphones, that's the whole point** ("everything about them is more difficult to travel with"). His
theory: Sony copied Bose's 700 sliding design. Verdict: for an Android user with a big enough bag, they're amazing —
**but for most people he brings back the XM4** (fold down, ~90% of the sound/comfort, cheaper). AirPods Max still win
transparency mode. And, as always, **a terrible name.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-05-12] **The step back — they no longer fold.** "'These are a real departure from the formula. The one I
  don't like: **the new sliding-headband design (like the Bose 700 / AirPods Max) means they no longer fold down at
  all — so the case is quite a bit bigger.** For **travel headphones, that's the whole point** — everything about
  them is now bigger and harder to travel with. (Bose at least kept the folding QC45 around.)'"
- [2022-05-12] **The forward — most comfortable ever + best-in-class ANC.** "'Two steps forward: **the softer vegan
  leather + light weight make them the most comfortable NC headphones I've ever tried**; and the **noise
  cancellation, already King of the Hill, got even better** (8 mics now — it crushes the low droning and catches
  more higher-frequency stuff too). Faster charging: 3 minutes = 3 hours. (Battery stays 30 hrs, not the rumored
  40.)'"
- [2022-05-12] **Great sound, but AirPods Max still win transparency.** "'For music these are among the **best-
  sounding NC wireless headphones on an Android phone** (a new 30 mm carbon-fiber driver, LDAC). Transparency mode
  is good (better than Bose) — **but the AirPods Max are on a completely different level; theirs are the best I've
  ever heard** (it sounds like you're not wearing headphones; these are a muffled version of reality).'"
- [2022-05-12] **The recommendation — most people should get the XM4.** "'A nuanced rec: **for an Android user with
  a big enough bag who travels, these are amazing.** But **for almost anyone else, consider the XM4** — cheaper,
  they actually fold, and they're ~90% of these headphones (you're mainly missing the faster charging and the
  extra mics/processor for NC). Great, comfortable — and, of course, **a terrible name, as we'd expect from
  Sony.**'"
