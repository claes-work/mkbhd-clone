# Galaxy S24 and Vision Pro are Here! (Waveform, 2024-01-19)

> ⚠️ **Ensemble podcast.** Hosts: Marques Brownlee, Andrew Manganelli, David Imel.
> No speaker labels; only **clearly-Marques** takes train the persona. Corroborates his
> main-channel S24 coverage ([[2024-01-17-yt-n7lM36yFh2Y]], [[2024-01-26-yt-XaqOejIaFgM]]).

## Summary
Reactions to the Galaxy S24 Unpacked and the imminent Vision Pro launch. Marques leads
the S24 hands-on walkthrough — flat design, titanium, and an AI/software-first story.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated 2024-01-19)
- **[Marques, clearly]** S24 line pricing ($800 / $1,000 / $1,300); **all screens are now
  totally flat** (he's "really happy about the totally flat Ultra"), Snapdragon 8 Gen 3
  across the board, 2,600-nit displays, the Plus upped to Quad HD.
- ★ **[Marques, clearly]** **The S24's flat sides "look exactly like the iPhone" — the
  camera is the only thing that differentiates them from the side.** He's fine with it
  (he liked the squared iPhone sides too) but is careful not to say "they copied the
  iPhone" too freely on camera.
- ★ **[Marques, clearly]** **Galaxy AI is the whole story:** as predicted from the last
  Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 phone, the interesting features are all software/AI. He's "happy for
  Google getting rid of their Pixel Division and merging with Samsung" — the presentation
  **"felt very googly… it felt like Google I/O,"** hardware minimal, software heavy.
