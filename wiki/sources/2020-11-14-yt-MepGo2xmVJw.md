# PlayStation 5 Review: Next Gen Gaming! (@mkbhd, 2020-11-14)

## Summary
Full review of the $500 PS5 — a **thumbs up ("a major go").** The durable idea: **no single next-gen feature is
a huge difference by itself, but all of them together add up to feel next-gen.** Threads in the **statement-piece
design (higher ceiling AND lower floor)**, the **fidelity-vs-performance mode** choice (he prefers 60 fps), the
**console-as-a-cheap-long-lived-PC** value framing, and — extending his DualSense video — the finding that the
**adaptive triggers are great but underutilized** across most games.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-11-14] **THE THESIS — no single thing, but together it adds up.** "'Console graphics have never touched
  PC, but this time you hear ray tracing, better reflections/shadows, high resolution, high frame rates —
  **no single one of these by itself makes a huge difference, but putting them all together, this stuff adds up
  to feel different (next-gen).**'" A promotable "sum of small things" framing.
- [2020-11-14] **The statement-piece design — higher ceiling AND lower floor.** "'It's huge, an eye-catcher,
  intentional — **risky, because not every home aesthetic fits it.** This crazy design has **both a higher
  ceiling and a lower floor** than something safe like the Xbox: in the right room it looks amazing; elsewhere
  it sticks out. (Matte white sides good; glossy black middle = the cheapest-looking part + fingerprint city.)'"
- [2020-11-14] **FIDELITY vs PERFORMANCE MODE — he picks 60 fps.** "'Spider-Man lets you switch: **high-fidelity
  (ray tracing on, real reflections, but 30 fps) or high-performance (fake reflections, 60 fps).** The PS5 does
  "partial ray tracing" — big glass surfaces reflect realistically, smaller ones just do lighting. **Most of
  the time I prefer high-performance at 60 fps — the smooth motion, and it still looks damn good** even with
  ray tracing basically off.'" His fps-over-fidelity lean (cf. the Xbox fps-over-resolution take).
- [2020-11-14] **Console-as-a-cheap-long-lived-PC — the value framing.** "'It's heresy to compare to PCs, but:
  **if the PS5 were a gaming PC it'd be extremely small, pretty cheap at $500, and relevant for ~4 years,
  potentially getting better over time — hard to find a PC that small, cheap, and relevant for that long.** A
  pretty impressive value proposition for a console.'"
- [2020-11-14] **The DualSense — great, but underutilized (the honest follow-up).** "'The **haptics and adaptive
  triggers are the real next-gen feeling** (Astro's Playroom: sand feels like sand). **But most games
  underutilize it** — Spider-Man barely uses either; the standout is **2K21, where the sprint trigger gets
  harder to pull as your player tires — you feel fatigue instead of reading the screen.** Devs may be tentative
  (people turn off vibration), but I wish I saw more.'" (Notes **2K is "the iPhone of console games"** — same
  stuff, a little better each year.)
- [2020-11-14] **The other wins — quiet, faster loads, good UI.** "'The console is **quiet** (a stark difference
  from the jet-engine PS4), **load times are noticeably faster in every game** (the fast SSD — one more little
  thing that adds up), and the new card-view UI is a welcome addition. "As flossy Carter would say, the PS5 is a
  major, major go if you can get your hands on it."'"

## Notable verbatim quotes
