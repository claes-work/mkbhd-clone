# Galaxy S23 / Ultra Impressions: Cruise Control! (@mkbhd, 2023-02-01)

## Summary
A deliberately short first-look at the S23 / S23+ / S23 Ultra — framed as **"Cruise Control"** because the phones
are **"very similar to last year's phones, which is great"** (last year's were rock-solid). Practically the only
changes are **the chip and the cameras.** The **"Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 for Galaxy"** branding implies a custom chip,
but when he asked Samsung it's basically **a slightly higher max clock — "maybe it's just binning"** (don't expect a
dramatic gap vs a normal 8 Gen 2). Cameras are refreshed across the board (50 MP main on the base; **200 MP main on
the Ultra** with dual telephotos); early shots look good but **"a bit Pixy"** in bright light, and he flags
**shutter lag** to watch. The Ultra's real visible change is aesthetic: **the flatter, boxier sides** ("much more
boxy like the notes of the past — I really like it"), reshaped for the S Pen (not for a bigger battery — still
5,000 mAh). Pricing: S23 $799, S23+ $999, Ultra $1,199; no charger in any box (Anker sponsor). Full testing to come.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2023-02-01] **Cruise control — the shortest video he can make.** "'Spoiler: I've played with them for two hours and
  they're very similar to last year's phones, which is great — those were rock solid. That also means I can make
  this the shortest MKBHD video of the past year, so I'm going to try.'"
- [2023-02-01] **"Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 for Galaxy" is basically binning.** "'Samsung calls it a customized chip, but
  when I asked, it seems like a slightly higher max clock speed — maybe it's just binning for all we know. I don't
  expect a dramatic difference versus other phones with the regular 8 Gen 2. Samsung phones always feel this fast
  brand new.'"
- [2023-02-01] **New cameras — good but "a bit Pixy," and watch the shutter lag.** "'Refreshed camera systems across
  the board (a 50 MP main + 12 MP ultrawide + 3× tele; the Ultra headlined by a 200 MP main). Early shots looked
  really good but very HDR and a bit Pixy in this bright room. One thing I didn't like: shutter lag — you think you
  got the shot but it captured slightly later. If I notice it in perfect lighting, it'll strike again.'"
- [2023-02-01] **The boxier Ultra.** "'The two noticeable Ultra changes: slightly bigger camera rings, and the sides
  are much flatter, so the whole phone is more boxy — and I really like it. Samsung says it's reshaped for the S Pen;
  the battery's still 5,000 mAh and it's still huge, so it feels mostly for aesthetics and feel in the hand.'"
