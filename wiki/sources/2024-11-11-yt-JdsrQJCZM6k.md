## Summary

Marques drives a **Sacrilege Motors 964** — a classic 1989-era Porsche 911 (964) restomodded to
be fully electric (~500hp, rear-wheel drive, ~200mi range, no screens). His frame: it's the analog-
Porsche throwback experience with a modern EV powertrain, and living with it, "I actually get why
people do it." **Biography-rich** in his portion: he ties it to daily-driving a Tesla (and borrowing
the 2008 Tesla Roadster) and to now daily-driving a 911 — of which he owns a black one.
