# Apple Vision Pro is Missing Something... (@mkbhd, 2024-02-13)

## Summary
A gen-2 wishlist, framed by the observation that **the Vision Pro won't be a yearly product** (like VR headsets
generally — Quest 3 came ~3 years after the Quest 2) so "very early thoughts" matter. His **#1 missing feature:
shared experiences** — **"you are always so alone in this headset."** Every experience is solo (aside from
FaceTime); nobody else can see what you see. He wants two kinds: two people in the same room both seeing a shared
virtual object (harder — each headset maps the room independently), and two people anywhere joining the **same
virtual environment** (a no-brainer — multiplayer table tennis, watching a movie in a shared virtual theater; a
huge part of what makes VR gaming fun, per Rec Room / the Quest). **#2: memory of windows across multiple spaces** —
the VP is "already amazing" at pinning windows in *one* space (he left a window in a parking lot, walked away, came
back, it was still there — "9/10, occlusion is the only ding"), but it forgets your layout the moment you use it
somewhere else; he wishes for **beacons/QR codes** so it recognizes "I'm at home" and restores your windows. Smaller
asks: an **iPhone-notification hub** (99.99% of buyers have an iPhone, yet it's a separate device — you can't even
see an incoming call); **more color** (the displays are super sharp — 3,386 PPI — but cover **92% of DCI-P3, which
is only ~half the colors the human eye can see**, and a headset is "replacing your eyes"); a **wider field of view**
("ski goggles from the inside" — the Quest 3 is wider); weight reduction; and keyboard pass-through.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2024-02-13] **The Vision Pro won't be a yearly product.** "'I don't think the Vision Pro will be a yearly update
  like the iPhone. It'll be every couple of years — you stack up enough tech updates and it's worthy of a new
  generation, and it's a bigger deal when it comes out. That's how a lot of VR headsets have been.'"
- [2024-02-13] **#1 missing feature — shared experiences; you're always alone.** "'The biggest thing: it's missing
  shared experiences. You are always so alone in this headset — no matter how immersive the movie or game, you're in
  there by yourself and nobody else can see what you're seeing (aside from FaceTime). Two people in the same room
  should be able to share a virtual object; two people anywhere should be able to join the same virtual environment
  (watch a movie in a shared theater, play a multiplayer game). That's a huge part of what makes VR fun.'"
- [2024-02-13] **#2 — memory of windows across spaces.** "'It's already amazing at pinning windows in one space — I
  left one in a parking lot, walked all the way inside, came back, and it was still there (9/10, occlusion's the only
  ding). But the moment I use it in another space, the home layout disappears. I wish it had a memory — maybe beacons
  or a QR code so it sees "I'm at home" and restores all my windows.'"
- [2024-02-13] **More color — 92% of DCI-P3 is only ~half of what the eye sees.** "'The displays are absurdly sharp
  (3,386 PPI), but they cover 92% of DCI-P3 — pretty good for a display, but a VR headset is replacing your eyes, and
  100% of DCI-P3 is only about 50% of the colors the human eye can see. So this shows me a little less than half the
  colors of reality. I'd love more color, plus a wider field of view.'"
- [2024-02-13] **The iPhone-notification hub — a separate device.** "'99.99% of Vision Pro buyers have an iPhone, but
  it's a separate device like a Mac or iPad — it shows iMessage, but if I get a phone call I can't even see it; I'd
  have to take the headset off. It'd be nice if the two talked better — a little hub for my phone's notifications.'"
