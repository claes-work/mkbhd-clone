# Asus ROG Phone 3 Review: The Spec King Returns! (@mkbhd, 2020-07-22)

## Summary
Review of the ROG Phone 3 — the follow-up to his 2019 Smartphone-Awards triple-winner (best big phone, best
battery, most improved). Two things stand out again: the **battery** (a genuine two-day phone) and the **maxed-out
specs** — with the recurring caveat that **software is how specs become experience.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-07-22] **The maxed spec sheet — "is there a single thing that isn't bleeding edge?"** "'**SD865+ (up to 3.1
  GHz), 16 GB, a 1080p 144 Hz OLED, up to 512 GB UFS 3.1, a 6,000 mAh battery** — every number is the highest you
  can put in a phone. **But the way you transition big numbers to actual user experience is great software** — Asus's
  is okay/improving but not perfect (occasional UI hiccups).'" His recurring specs-need-software point.
- [2020-07-22] **The two-day battery test.** "'The **6,000 mAh** (biggest I've used) got me **two full light days at
  144 Hz** (ended day two at 3% / ~6 hrs SOT). And with 30 W charging, the quick 0-65% top-up **adds ~4,000 mAh** —
  fewer charge cycles, healthier battery longer. Probably a lock for the battery award again.'" A concrete, dated
  battery result.
- [2020-07-22] **Finally too big to daily — and a media machine.** "'It's **so massive it's finally too big for me
  to want to daily** (I rarely say that). The **incredible front-facing stereo speakers** (some of the best in a
  phone) make it — for most of us who aren't hardcore mobile gamers — a **glorified/really-nice media-consumption
  machine.** For the tiny group who play well-optimized games, the air triggers / 144 Hz / X-mode are worth it.'"
- [2020-07-22] **THE MACRO-CAMERA GRIPE (stated as advice).** "'Mediocre cameras (expected). The **5 MP macro is the
  perfect example of adding the cheapest extra camera just to say "triple cameras."** **Dear manufacturers: if the
  choice is between a bad macro camera or not adding it — you can just not add it.**'" A promotable, recurring
  critique.

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "The best way to transition from all those big numbers to actual user experience is great software."
> "Dear smartphone manufacturers, if you have the choice between adding a bad macro camera... or just not adding
> it, you can just not add it."
