# My Tech Hot Takes! (@mkbhd, 2022-12-04)

## Summary
An off-format video (filmed from the basement studio with a handheld Yeti mic — he **finally caught COVID two years
in**, recovered, quarantining until he tests negative) sharing **four end-of-2022 hot takes.** **(1) Crypto
investing is basically gambling** — the optimist in him watches web3/NFTs/Bitcoin, but **"if I can't explain why
something is more valuable other than lots of people being interested in it right now, it's as good as gambling"**
(the Dr. Mike clip: "as close to zero as you can be without being zero"). **(2) TikTok and YouTube coexist —
separate pies**, not overlapping slices (different use cases — sit-down chosen viewing vs a quick dopamine scroll;
the Netflix analogy). **(3) Regular people don't care about panel gaps** — it's true of Tesla, but so low on the
buying list it stays fine (the "trade a feature" thought experiment: nobody trades the Supercharger network or the
software for perfect panel gaps; Tesla fixes it only when it hits the bottom line). **(4) 5G sucks right now** —
slower, less consistent, and more expensive than 4G (low-band 5G; **"expectations are the thief of joy"**).

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-12-04] **#1 Crypto investing is basically gambling.** "'I'm the optimist, always trying to see the web3/NFT/
  blockchain future — but I'm a skeptic because every pitch **defaults to how much money you can make; I need other,
  tangible benefits.** I own a little Bitcoin, but I can't use it for anything. **If I can't explain why something
  is more valuable other than "lots of people are interested right now so it's hot," then it's as good as
  gambling.**' (The Dr. Mike clip: "as close to zero [interested] as you can be without being zero.")'"
- [2022-12-04] **#2 TikTok and YouTube coexist — separate pies.** "'The biggest question I get is "should YouTube be
  worried about TikTok?" **They're such different use cases they barely overlap** — YouTube is sit-down, chosen/
  searched viewing (a whole search engine attached); TikTok is a quick dopamine burst you don't choose. **Like
  YouTube and Netflix — different expectations in different places. TikTok isn't a slice of YouTube's pie; it's a
  new pie.** YouTube responded correctly with Shorts (and beat TikTok's monetization).'"
- [2022-12-04] **#3 Regular people don't care about panel gaps.** "'Reviewers harp on Tesla's inconsistent panel
  gaps — **and it's true** (less consistent than 50–100-year-old manufacturers). But **it's so low on the buying
  list that it stays fine** (like McLaren's gaps). The thought experiment: would you trade a perfect-panel-gap
  Model 3 for Ford's charging network? Ford's software? **No — most people take the Tesla as-is.** So Tesla's done
  the math: they focus on the reasons people buy (battery, drivetrain, Superchargers, software) and **will only
  fix panel gaps in earnest when it meaningfully hits the bottom line** (they'll improve slowly meanwhile).'"
- [2022-12-04] **#4 5G sucks right now.** "'Not "it takes time to build infrastructure" — the hot take is **5G right
  now is worse than 4G: slower, less consistent, more expensive.** Carriers (esp. AT&T) push low-band 5G that feels
  slower than 4G, with confusing status-bar labels (5Ge, 5G+, etc.). **It's mostly just a thing manufacturers point
  at to say "our phones are new" — while making phones pricier (radios), battery worse (switching), and plans more
  expensive.** And **expectations are the thief of joy** — the promises were sky-high (robot surgery, no
  buffering), so the gap makes it feel brutally bad.'"
- [2022-12-04] **Biography — caught COVID, from the basement.** "'Coming at you from the basement studio — I finally
  caught COVID two years in (recovered; quarantining until I test negative). Forgive the production — me, a camera,
  and a Yeti mic. (Also: we have the best merch in the YouTube game.)'"
