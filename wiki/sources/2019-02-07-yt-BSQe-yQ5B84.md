# What's on my Tech: 2019! (@mkbhd, 2019-02-07)

## Summary
An expanded "what's on my phone" covering his whole daily-tech ecosystem — a biography/production/gear goldmine:
the two-phone system (and why the Pixel's camera keeps him), the app suite, the iPad-over-MacBook shift, and the
studio storage backbone.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-02-07] **The two-phone system + why the Pixel wins (BIOGRAPHY).** "Android of choice = **Pixel 3 XL**;
  iPhone of choice = **iPhone XS Max** (space gray). The **OnePlus 6T is the better *performer* — faster, smoother,
  more RAM, more pleasurable — but the camera isn't as good, and every time I come back to the Pixel's camera it
  reminds me why I love it. **The Pixel's camera is what's keeping me here.**" His clearest statement of the
  camera-over-everything daily-driver logic.
- [2019-02-07] **The app suite (dated).** **TickTick** (to-do — "**if I don't write it down, I'm not going to do
  it**"); Pocket Casts ("I've gotten more into podcasts"); **Waze** (navigation — potholes/closed roads); Spotify
  (+ an **MKBHD-videos playlist** with his intro music); **Flamingo** (his favorite Twitter app, now delisted) with
  the official app for notifications; **Sky Guide** ("**the best AR app, period** — overlays constellations/planets
  on your real sky"); **Dark Sky** (hyper-local rain alerts); **StockX** (shoes); MyFitnessPal (macros); reads
  **audiobooks** (~3 books last year). A dense dated inventory.
- [2019-02-07] **The Apple Watch ring obsession (biography).** "You may notice **I wear an Apple Watch** — I've
  developed this **obsession with closing my rings**, like a lot of Watch people do." + a HealthMate digital scale
  (weight tracking), Philips Hue / LIFX. Confirms the smartwatch conversion stuck.
- [2019-02-07] **The iPad-over-MacBook shift + the typing gripe.** "I've **found every excuse to use the iPad Pro
  over a MacBook Pro** the past couple weeks (I gave it a rave review). But **I still hate typing on it** — you
  can't reach the middle of the keyboard, and **the iPad Pro is the only iPad that won't split the keyboard** — so
  email / lots of typing I do on the laptop." A dated workflow tension.
- [2019-02-07] **PRODUCTION — the desktop + the storage backbone.** "The **maxed iMac Pro (2019)** is my daily +
  editing machine (**Final Cut Pro**, Safari default, **Apollo Twin** audio). I **really miss the dual-screen setup**
  (my 2012/2013 days) — **excited for the modular Mac this year so I can go back to two monitors.**" The storage:
  an **80 TB Promise Pegasus** RAID + a **120 TB LumaForge Jellyfish** NAS — "**how multiple people work on the same
  Final Cut Pro project at the same time.**" A key studio-infrastructure record. (Also: the Apollo/Tesla app
  "hasn't been opened in a while — car's been gone ~3 months, update soon"; ordered **titanium RED Hydrogens**.)
