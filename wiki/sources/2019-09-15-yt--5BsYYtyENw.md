# Samsung Galaxy Fold Re-do: Everything New! (@mkbhd, 2019-09-15)

## Summary
A look at the fixed, re-launched Galaxy Fold after the breaking-review-units saga. Resolves the Fold arc
([[2019-04-26-yt-6C_4Bz62b0E]]). Two durable commentary beats: **"test with real people, not just robot hands,"**
and praise for Samsung **owning the failure** rather than taking a stubborn "you're holding it wrong" stance.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-09-15] **What was actually fixed.** "'**(1)** the screen-protector-looking layer now **extends under the
  bezel to the edges** so you can't peel it; **(2)** new **T-shaped caps** at the hinge top/bottom to keep dust/
  debris out (surprising in hindsight it didn't have these); **(3)** a slightly **smaller closed gap** and a
  **firmer hinge snap.** Otherwise it's the same phone — still a soft-plastic screen (a new setup warning says
  don't press hard).'"
- [2019-09-15] **THE LESSON — test with real people, not robot hands.** "'If you keep a product **completely
  secret** and **never take it out of a lab to test with real people**, the **first real people who use it will do
  things you never thought of** — folding it with things other than robot hands, lint-filled pockets, beaches with
  sand.'" A durable product-development principle.
- [2019-09-15] **THE COMMENTARY — Samsung owned it; glad they didn't "copy Apple's stubbornness."** "'I'm glad
  Samsung **saw what was going wrong, accepted it, took them all back** (never shipped one to a customer) and
  **fixed it internally.** I could easily see some other companies take the **stubborn, Apple-like approach —
  "you're holding it wrong, it's user error, just ship it anyway."** Let's be real, Samsung copies Apple on a lot
  of things — **glad they didn't copy that.**'" A pointed, dated comparison.
- [2019-09-15] **THE VALUE — glad companies take big risks.** "'My feelings are the same: **I'm just really happy
  it exists**, even if I wouldn't daily it. I'm **glad companies still take big risks like this** in a world of
  iterative phones (you just saw the new iPhone — very similar to the last one). This was clearly a risk, we saw
  what happened, and it's still awesome to see.'" His reward-experimentation stance (cf. the original Fold review).
- [2019-09-15] **Realistic on durability.** "'I don't think this is radically different — it's still a delicate
  phone, and honestly **people are probably still going to break this**, a month or a year in. But it represents
  Samsung **taking a stance** that they believe in the foldable form factor.'"

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "If you keep a product completely and totally secret like this and you never take it out of a lab to test it
> with real people, then the first real people that use it are gonna do things you may have never thought of."
> "I could easily see a world where some other companies take the more stubborn, Apple-like approach — 'you're
> holding it wrong, you guys are using it wrong'... I'm glad Samsung didn't do that."
