# The iPhone USB-C Law: Explained! (@mkbhd, 2022-11-02)

## Summary
An explainer/analysis of the EU's common-charger law (passed 602–13) mandating **USB-C on all wired-charging devices
sold in the EU by end of 2024** — and a pushback on the "Apple confirms a USB-C iPhone" headlines. His close read:
Apple's media-trained execs said they'll **"comply," NOT that they'll make a USB-C iPhone** — a big difference —
because **Apple's real next jump was never USB-C; it's portless** (MagSafe). He explains the history (Lightning was
a genuine 2012 innovation; the **MFI program takes a cut of every certified accessory** — millions for Apple), and
why the EU law reads as a short-term win (Lightning sucks now) but a **structurally flawed approach: the government
being too prescriptive about the exact tech.** His principled take (agreeing with Apple's Greg Joswiak, minus the
PR): **mandate the goal — a unified standard to reduce e-waste — not the specific technology**, because we'll move
past USB-C too. And **"I don't want to hear about e-waste from Apple of all people."** Bottom line: **"when wires
wane, wireless will win."**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-11-02] **Apple said "comply," not "USB-C iPhone."** "'The EU law (602–13) requires **USB-C on all wired-
  charging devices by end of 2024** (page 27: if it charges via wire, it "shall have a USB-C port"). But Apple's
  execs (media-trained, careful) said they'll **"comply" — not that they'll make a USB-C iPhone.** Big difference.
  I'm with Dave2D: they never said it, and I don't think they will.'"
- [2022-11-02] **Apple's real plan is portless — MagSafe.** "'Lightning was a genuine 2012 innovation (smaller,
  freed internal space) — and the **MFI program takes ~10% off every certified accessory (millions for Apple).**
  But Lightning sucks now — and **Apple's next jump was never USB-C; it's no port at all** (I have a whole video).
  They'd rather go portless than switch to USB-C. Picture the stage moment: "how much do you use the charging
  port? — we had the courage to invent MagSafe." With the EU's hard 2024 deadline, they have the iPhone 15 and 16
  to push MagSafe before the **iPhone 17 could go portless** (maybe shipping only a MagSafe puck to acclimate
  people).'"
- [2022-11-02] **Don't lecture me about e-waste, Apple.** "'They'll also have to move the Magic Keyboard/Mouse/
  AirPods off Lightning by 2024 (the Apple TV remote already went USB-C). **I don't want to hear about the
  environment and e-waste from Apple of all people — that never stopped you from changing everything else to
  USB-C.**'"
- [2022-11-02] **The real problem — the government being too prescriptive.** "'As self-serving as it is, **I agree
  with almost everything Joswiak said: fine for governments to regulate toward a positive end goal, but they
  shouldn't be so prescriptive about exactly HOW technologically** — leave that to the engineers (like mandating
  hearing-aid compatibility, then letting companies build the tech and improve it). **Mandating USB-C specifically
  feels like a win because Lightning sucks — but we're already pushing past USB-C (200 W charging), and in a couple
  years we'll move to something better. Then what?** Better to mandate the goal (a unified standard / less e-waste)
  and let the law update — but how much do you trust that to happen?'"
- [2022-11-02] **The bottom line — wireless will win.** "'I'll take a USB-C iPhone if that's what happens — but it's
  probably not. It can feel like a win short-term (USB-C beats Lightning now), but long-term Apple has plenty of
  time to think about theirs. **When wires wane, wireless will win.**'"
