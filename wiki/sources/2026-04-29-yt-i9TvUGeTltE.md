# My Take on The New Apple ★★

## Summary

Marques's read on **Tim Cook stepping down as Apple CEO, replaced by John Ternus** (starting
September). He normally skips CEO changes, but argues this one matters because it's **not one
or two people — it's a coordinated wave** of long-tenured C-suite executives retiring at once
(Johny Srouji moving to hardware chief), "a bunch of coordinated oars changing direction." His
thesis: **Ternus is a "product guy"** (ex-hardware-engineering, behind the Apple Silicon
MacBook Pros, the $599 Mac mini, the MacBook Neo), where **Cook was a supply-chain/business CEO,
not a product guy** — so he's optimistic Apple's **interesting hardware** is coming back, if
they can fix the software (Siri, Apple Intelligence, Liquid Glass). A landmark strategy take. ★★.

## Key claims

- **[2026-04-29]** The news: **Tim Cook steps down as CEO (to Chairman of the Board); John Ternus
  (ex-SVP Hardware Engineering) becomes CEO in September; Johny Srouji becomes hardware chief.** He
  credits Cook — "he stepped into the single hardest job in tech, following Steve Jobs" — and took
  Apple to $4T "through 15 years of wildly different economies. Investors love him." (paraphrase)
- **[2026-04-29]** Why it matters more than a normal shuffle: **a coordinated retirement wave** of
  long-tenured executives, with Cook (65) "the last big domino in a large, likely orchestrated
  series." "A bunch of coordinated oars all changing direction at the same time that seems to
  actually be changing the direction of the boat." (credits Snazzy Q's video.) (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2026-04-29]** The core framework: **Jobs was a "product guy"** (in the nitty-gritty of what made
  products great); **Cook is not** — "whenever he talks about Apple, it's pretty clear he's not in the
  nitty-gritty" (the **Magic Mouse meme** from Marques's own 2-years-ago Cook interview — "you can tell
  it's the first time he's thought about that product in a long time"). **Ternus is a product guy
  again** (his own 2-years-ago Ternus interview on repairability-vs-durability was "quite nuanced").
  (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2026-04-29]** The evidence for optimism: Apple's **hardware engineering has gone really well** —
  the too-thin Jony-Ive MacBook Pros are out, the thicker Apple-Silicon Pros (more battery/ports) are
  in; the **$599 Mac mini**; the **MacBook Neo** "putting the entire Windows laptop industry on notice."
  Inside baseline: **Ternus already gave the "good morning" intro at the MacBook Neo event** (before
  being announced), signaling he's being groomed and **spearheaded the Neo — and likely the iPhone
  Fold** this year. (paraphrase)
- **[2026-04-29]** The worry: **"Apple avoids the possibility of failure extra hard, which makes them
  try less."** Every new category "has to be this massive revolutionary thing," which holds them back
  from making stuff he wants (**a dedicated camera, a smart display, smart glasses, even a printer that
  doesn't suck** — "I say this on my podcast all the time"). **"If Apple were a YouTuber, they'd upload
  every six months"** — where **Samsung and Google are daily uploaders** (Google has "a literal
  graveyard of dozens of killed products"). Apple's flops **echo** (Vision Pro, iPhone 16e, Apple
  Intelligence, AirPower), which pushes them to **play it ridiculously safe → parts-bin variations.**
  (quote)
- **[2026-04-29]** Under Cook plenty of solid products shipped (Apple Watch, AirTags, the Vision Pro
  swing, and **AirPods — "literally the most popular headphones on planet Earth"**), but Apple **shifted
  toward services** (iCloud/TV/Music/Fitness — "sweet recurring revenue off the iPhone install base").
  He wants the **interesting products back** — "and they need to step up their software game to match
  (macOS Tahoe, Liquid Glass, Apple Intelligence, Siri). If they nail both, that could be special."
  (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2026-04-29]** Signs off with characteristic humility: "Bookmark this and come back in 5–10 years
  to see how it aged. Or if I turned out super wrong, send it to someone to show them how dumb I am."
  (quote)
