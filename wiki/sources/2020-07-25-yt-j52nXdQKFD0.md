# Why Tesla Model Y is Their Most Important Car! [Auto Focus] (@mkbhd, 2020-07-25)

## Summary
An Auto-Focus-style look at the Model Y Performance (~75% shared with the Model 3 — "it almost IS a Model 3"). The
durable payload: **Tesla's main challenge is manufacturing and quality control** — with a "review-unit-vs-what-you-
get" caveat modeled on his smartphone reviews.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-07-25] **"It almost IS a Model 3" (75% shared parts).** "'From the front it looks like a Model 3; from the
  side you see the Model X headroom. Inside it **shares ~75% of its parts** — the same staggeringly minimal
  interior, the same feel. Higher off the ground (utility/visibility vs. **less connected to the road**). A
  hatchback with far more storage + a power liftgate with memory height.'"
- [2020-07-25] **THE KEY POINT — manufacturing + quality control is Tesla's challenge.** "'Tesla's **two main
  concerns are manufacturing and quality control** — especially with the Model Y. Even the ~$70k unit **Tesla gave
  me** had **panel gaps** (a serious back-left one letting in wind/water), a **loose passenger sun visor**, a
  **misaligned hazard button**, an uneven frunk lip. **If the review unit has these, customers will have the same
  or more** as they ramp production.'" A pointed, dated read on Tesla's weak spot.
- [2020-07-25] **The everything-on-the-touchscreen critique (wanting manual control).** "'It's a super-cool
  futuristic UI, but **I kind of wish I had more manual control** — the **wipers, glovebox, and even the AC vents**
  are all on the screen. Sometimes I just want to point the air at my face with one button.'" A recurring
  minimalism-vs-usability tension (cf. Single Take, "I like taking the photos myself").
- [2020-07-25] **The smartphone-review analogy — fundamentals vs. what-changes.** "'Like a smartphone review: I can
  tell you about the **fundamentals** (handling, 0-60, autopilot, the minimal interior) — **and those crush it** —
  but a lot of the **small stuff (fit/finish, seats, software features) may change** by the time someone else gets
  their car (Teslas change so much over time). **I'm not picky about panel gaps; I focus on the driving
  experience** — and I'm a big fan of this car.'" His characteristic hardware-fundamentals-vs-tweakable-details
  framing, applied to a car.

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "I think the main focus for Tesla right now is manufacturing and quality control — those are the two main
> concerns, especially with Model Y."

> control."
