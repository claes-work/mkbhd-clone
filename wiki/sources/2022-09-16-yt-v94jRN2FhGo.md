# iPhone 14 Review: Repackaging 101! (@mkbhd, 2022-09-16)

## Summary
The review of the base iPhone 14 — **"almost the exact same phone as last year,"** and the launchpad for his
**"repackaging" thesis.** The 14 uses the **same A15, same design (iPhone 13 cases fit), same 60 Hz display, same
battery/charging/Lightning** — **"even less of an update than the old 'S' updates."** He walks the full list of
"everything technically new" (crash detection, satellite SOS, eSIM, a slightly bigger sensor + f/1.5, a new selfie
camera, the Photonic Engine) and finds it's mostly **"spot the differences"** — the camera is nearly identical (a
color-temperature shift; slightly faster night-mode). The durable idea: **Apple's "repackaging/re-gifting"
strategy — the iPhone SE formula (new chip, old body) generalized across the lineup (the M2 MacBook Pro too)** —
reduces cost/risk/supply-chain exposure, so **"the innovation, aka the risk, is saved for the more expensive
phones"** (the Pro's Dynamic Island, 48 MP, always-on display). Verdict: the base iPhone is **"just boring, and
that's the way it is"** — and **the iPhone 13, still on sale at $699, is a fine upgrade for most** (the only reason
for a 14 is one extra year of updates).

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-09-16] **Less than an "S" update.** "'This is almost the exact same phone as last year. Remember the "S"
  updates (5 → 5S, "S for speed")? **This is even less of an update than those** — the same A15 (a slight GPU
  bump), the same design (**iPhone 13 cases literally fit the 14**), the same 60 Hz display, storage, battery,
  charging, Lightning. "If it ain't broke" — but that also means things that could have improved (like 90 Hz)
  didn't.'"
- [2022-09-16] **"Everything new" is minor — the camera is spot-the-differences.** "'The full list of what's
  technically new: crash detection, satellite SOS, a slightly higher display resolution, eSIM, colors, better
  thermals/repairability, a new primary + selfie camera. **Crash detection and satellite SOS are nice features I
  hope I never use.** The camera (a bigger 12 MP sensor, f/1.6→f/1.5, the Photonic Engine) is **"a game of spot the
  differences"** — in daytime, no difference; mostly a slight color-temperature shift and ~5–10% faster night-mode
  (the 13 sometimes focused better). Action mode's stabilization works, but the video is noisy and constantly asks
  for "more light."'"
- [2022-09-16] **The repackaging strategy — the SE formula generalized.** "'This is Apple's **"repackaging"
  strategy, done more lately** — the **iPhone SE formula (new chip, old body)** generalized: the 1st SE was an
  iPhone 5s body, the 2nd/3rd an iPhone 8 body — and **the 13" M2 MacBook Pro is the exact M1 body.** It's no
  mystery why: **it reduces risk and cost (less new development), lets prices drop, and insulates them from supply-
  chain challenges. The Tim Cook logistics touch.**'"
- [2022-09-16] **Innovation is saved for the Pro.** "'So **the innovation — aka the risk — is saved for the more
  expensive phones**: the Pro gets the Dynamic Island (new to the iPhone), the 48 MP camera, the always-on
  display. **This is a long-winded way of saying the baseline iPhone, with this strategy, is just boring — and
  that's the way it is.**'"
- [2022-09-16] **The wrench — the iPhone 13 is still on sale.** "'One wrench: **the iPhone 13 is still on sale
  ($699 from Apple, cheaper elsewhere) — a fine upgrade for a lot of people. The one extra year of software updates
  is maybe the only reason I'd get a 14 over a 13.** Think about how much the new colors / slightly-new camera /
  satellite / crash detection actually mean to you.' (The selfie still struggles with darker skin tones vs the
  Pixel's Real Tone.)'"
