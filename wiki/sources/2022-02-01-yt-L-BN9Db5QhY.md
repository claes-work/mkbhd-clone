# An Announcement! (@mkbhd, 2022-02-01)

## Summary
A direct-to-camera "new-year update" laying out the studio's early-2022 moves — a window into the business. The
ethos: **the Jan/Feb post-CES lull is a chance to refresh, because "if you don't change things up you might find
yourself in cruise control" / "if we're not moving forward, we're standing still."** The announcements: a **new
MKBHD Shorts channel** (the fifth channel — he genuinely enjoys the short-form challenge, "30fps for life"); a
**rebuilt mkbhd.com** (with a dark-mode toggle — the old one was "embarrassing"); a **refreshed merch store
graduating beyond apparel** (a desk mat, a "studio scent" candle); new 2022 graphics/intro/banners; **hiring** (an
assistant Waveform producer, ad sales, and — notably — **an editor, "after 13 years of editing all these videos"**);
and trialing **member livestreams / Q&As.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-02-01] **The refresh ethos — don't hit cruise control.** "'The second half of the year is product-focused
  and we're in the thick of it; then Jan/Feb after CES is a chill period — **a chance to refresh, which I enjoy as
  much as the rest, because if you don't change things up and stay on your toes, you might find yourself in cruise
  control.** New intro, new channel banner, fresh graphics — **if we're not moving forward, we're standing
  still.**'"
- [2022-02-01] **The MKBHD Shorts channel — the fifth channel.** "'I've officially launched an **MKBHD Shorts
  channel.** I really like TikToks and Shorts — probably sacrilegious to say in cinematography circles (though,
  30fps for life) — but **packaging information into a great short is a different, genuinely fun challenge.** The
  point is modularity: separate channels (Waveform, The Studio, now Shorts) so you subscribe to exactly what you
  want.'"
- [2022-02-01] **A rebuilt website with a dark-mode toggle.** "'A brand-new **mkbhd.com** — the old one was
  honestly embarrassing. **We took the extra time; the new site is beautiful, functional, secure, mobile-friendly,
  with two entirely unique versions you flip between via a dark-mode toggle.**'"
- [2022-02-01] **Merch graduating beyond apparel.** "'The merch store is refreshed and the checkout is fully on
  mkbhd.com. **We're graduating from just apparel** — still the soft shirts/hoodie/windbreaker, plus hats/beanies,
  and now actual products: the **desk-mat mousepad** we've been teasing, and a **matte-black woodwick "studio
  scent" candle** (it smells like a studio full of new gadgets). The best drop and most complete store we've had —
  the best way to support the channel directly.'"
- [2022-02-01] **Hiring an editor after 13 years + member livestreams.** "'Running a channel isn't easy once you
  have ambitions to do more — **that's exactly why we're hiring: an assistant Waveform producer, ad sales, and an
  editor** (after 13 years of editing all these videos myself, maybe there's room for growth). And I'm going to
  trial some **live stuff — member livestreams, Q&As, games** — for channel members, who so far have only gotten a
  badge. I appreciate y'all.'"
