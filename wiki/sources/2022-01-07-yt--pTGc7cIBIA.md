# My Everyday Tech: 2022! (@mkbhd, 2022-01-07)

## Summary
An everyday-carry / what's-on-my-devices update (a biography/gear anchor). The durable facts: the **two-phone
habit** (an **Android phone always in the right pocket, an iPhone always in the left** — "I'm always in both
worlds/ecosystems"); he **downsized from the Pro Max to a 1TB space-gray iPhone 13 Pro** (for the ProRes video he
shoots — the Rivian video was shot on it); the current Android is a **Pixel 6 Pro** ("getting more buggy," odd);
the laptop is the **16" M1 Max** ("the first time I'm actually editing videos away from my desk"); a **Peak Design
20L MKBHD-edition bag**; **AirPods Max** for travel; **Canon R5 (15–35) as his everyday photo camera**, the **Sony
A1 for bigger shoots**; the **Opal C1 webcam (he's an investor)**; **AirTags on his keys**; and his favorite app of
the year, the **TickTick** to-do app.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-01-07] **The two-phone habit — Android right, iPhone left.** "'I pretty much always carry **an Android phone
  and an iPhone, one in each pocket — always the Android on the right, the iPhone on the left** — because I'm
  always in both worlds, both ecosystems, testing both. **The iPhone has continuity (changes once or twice a year);
  the Android changes about monthly** as I put my SIM into the next phone I'm testing.'"
- [2022-01-07] **Downsized to a 1TB iPhone 13 Pro; the Pixel 6 Pro is buggy.** "'The constant in the left pocket is
  the iPhone — **I actually downsized from the Pro Max to the 1TB space-gray iPhone 13 Pro**, because with the
  amount of ProRes video I shoot it helps (I shot the whole Rivian video on this phone). The Android at the moment
  is the **Pixel 6 Pro — it's fine, but it's definitely gotten more buggy than when it started, which is odd and
  annoying** (happened with Pixels before). It keeps dropping to 60 Hz even with 120 on.'"
- [2022-01-07] **The bag + the 16" M1 Max — editing away from the desk.** "'The bag is the **Peak Design Everyday
  20L (an MKBHD edition — black with red stitching, a patch).** The laptop is the **16" M1 Max** — I went 16" (not
  14") because **for the first time I'm actually editing videos away from my desk** (on a couch, at a table,
  random places). Plus a MagSafe charger and **AirPods Max (space gray)** for travel.'"
- [2022-01-07] **The cameras + the Opal C1 (investor disclosure).** "'For everyday photos, **the Canon R5 with the
  15–35 is my go-to** (lots of wide first-person shots); for bigger shoots I take the **Sony A1** (I took it to
  shoot the MrBeast video). And a **webcam, the Opal C1 — disclaimer, I'm an investor in the company, but I really
  believe in it** (great for all the video calls we're on). **AirTags on my keys.**'"
- [2022-01-07] **Favorite app of the year — TickTick.** "'My favorite app of the entire year is my to-do app,
  **TickTick** — good to-do apps don't get enough credit; I'm juggling a lot of simultaneous, parallel projects
  and one place for all of it is super helpful. I've tried pretty much every to-do app and this is the best (great
  UI, a focus planner, a Pomodoro timer). I hate that Apple bought Dark Sky and made the best weather app
  iPhone-only.'"
