# Samsung Z Fold 8 (Wide) Impressions: Better Than I Thought! (2026-07-22)

> **SOLO @mkbhd** (the subject directly, first-look/hands-on). Full-attribution L2.

## Summary
Marques's hands-on with **three new Samsung foldables** — the **Z Flip 8**, the **Z Fold 8 Ultra** (the
familiar candybar-shape fold, now renamed "Ultra"), and a **brand-new smaller passport-style "Z Fold 8"**. He
reads Samsung's slow-and-steady incrementalism, a big **crease/hinge improvement**, and — the headline — a
**clever product-positioning move** clearly aimed at Apple's expected first foldable iPhone. He comes away
surprised at how much he likes the smaller Fold.

## Key claims / observations (2026-07-22)
- **Samsung's foldables: "started first, but the more aggressive competition has caught up and passed them."**
  His follower-not-first critique applied to Samsung — slow, safe, incremental generation-over-generation.
- **The crease is finally close to solved.** A new **titanium-alloy film behind the OLED + a titanium backplate**
  greatly reduces the center crease. His comparative rating: if the **Oppo Find N6 is a 10/10**, this is an
  **8–8.5/10** (vs **~5/10 last year**) — with a new anti-reflective coating + **3,000-nit** brightness you
  barely see it in use. *(the [[2026-mkbhd-shorts-digest-01|Peak Foldable]] Oppo-Find-N6 reference recurs.)*
- **Specs bump:** Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 for Galaxy; new **50 MP ultrawide (8K video)** alongside the same
  200 MP primary; **first silicon in a Samsung battery** (cautiously — "given Samsung's history with batteries")
  → biggest-ever Samsung Fold battery at **5,000 mAh**; charging finally updated **25→45 W wired, 15→20 W
  wireless.**
- ★ **The clever anti-Apple positioning read.** With Apple expected to unveil its **first foldable iPhone in
  September** in a rumored **passport/squatter aspect ratio**, Samsung preemptively **drops a passport-style
  "regular Z Fold 8" positioned *under* the candybar "Z Fold 8 Ultra."** So when Apple launches, Samsung can
  say "cute, you finally made a fold — we have one just like it, *and* a better Ultra." He calls the positioning
  clever and sensible (Ultra = power-user/multitasking; smaller Fold = media-focused).
- **The smaller Fold surprised him.** 201 g (about a Galaxy S26+), very thin/light; 5.5" outer (10:16), 7.6"
  inner (**4:3**, 3,000 nits), 4,500 mAh, primary + ultrawide only (50 MP primary). Reminds him of "the good
  parts of the **Surface Duo**." Great for camera viewfinder reachability, 4:3 photo review (fills the screen),
  and an e-reader experience. "The more I use it, the more I'm liking it… I can really see myself dailying it —
  I didn't think that coming in."
- ★ **Pricing / macro read.** Flip 8 **$1,200**, smaller Z Fold 8 **$1,900**, Z Fold 8 Ultra **>$2,000**
  (~$100 more than last year). "Everything's more expensive… I would not be shocked if many more devices over
  the next couple months are also more expensive because of the **memory price increases** — and especially
  **that iPhone**. We know that's gonna be expensive." *(his 2026 rising-prices / memory-cost macro lens.)*
