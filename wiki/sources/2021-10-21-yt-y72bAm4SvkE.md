# Surface Duo 2: Can This Be Saved? (@mkbhd, 2021-10-21)

## Summary
Impressions (not a full review) of Microsoft's Surface Duo 2. The durable idea: **Microsoft fixed every
individual flaw of the original and the device got WORSE to use — which reveals the idea may be fundamentally
flawed.** Every change is objectively better on paper (top-end Snapdragon 888, bigger/faster 90 Hz displays,
real rear cameras) — yet the curved-edge display eats pixels in the middle, the new camera bump stops it folding
flat, and the software is buggy again. The **root flaw: no outside screen** — you must open+unlock it for
anything. Ties into a 2021 through-line: **companies going back on things** (like Apple with the MacBook Pro).

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-10-21] **Objectively-better-yet-worse — the fundamental-flaw thesis.** "'Microsoft valiantly improved a ton
  of stuff versus the original, found the flaws, made them all objectively better — **and yet some of that also
  made it worse to use. This idea may be fundamentally flawed in the way it's used.**'"
- [2021-10-21] **The curved display eats the middle.** "'The new display curves over both edges near the hinge (a
  waterfall look that makes the two screens feel like one) — but when you span an app across both, **important
  pixels in the middle spill over the edge, unreadable, and some are just missing.** Forget spanning games
  horizontally — imagine aiming with the crosshairs gone. Top-bottom split-screen gaming, though, is a great
  form factor (Game Pass is a lot of fun).'"
- [2021-10-21] **The rear cameras broke the fold.** "'They caved and added a real triple camera on the back
  (objectively better than the old webcam) — **but the camera bump now stops it from folding fully flat, so
  one-handed use is less comfortable and you must swivel it open every time you take a photo. Physically less
  convenient.**'"
- [2021-10-21] **THE ROOT FLAW — no outside screen.** "'The fundamental flaw: **there's no outside screen, so
  there's no usability when it's closed — you have to open AND unlock it to do anything.** The Razr and Z Flip 3
  have usable outside screens; the Fold 3 has a whole phone UI outside. It sounds petty, but in daily use it's a
  big deal — plus, folded flat, you've got bare display glass exposed to scratches. The software's buggy again
  too (touch-responsiveness issues), for a $1,499 device — MORE expensive than the first one that flopped.'"
- [2021-10-21] **Companies go back on things.** "'Here's my question — we just saw Apple release a MacBook Pro
  we're all loving because they **went back on a ton of the stuff we hated.** Should Microsoft give up on the
  no-outside-screen form factor, add one, or leave the Duo as-is for the people who love it? I couldn't come up
  with a fix myself.'"

## Notable verbatim quotes
