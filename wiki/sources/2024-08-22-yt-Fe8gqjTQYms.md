# Porsche Taycan Turbo GT - King or Contender?

> ⚠️ **ATTRIBUTION — FULLY QUARANTINED (co-host Miles, NOT the subject).** This @AutoFocus video is
> narrated end-to-end by **co-host Miles**, not Marques Brownlee — the narrator explicitly
> distinguishes himself from Marques ("**Marques and I** both got to drive the Lucid Air Sapphire")
> and it's an LA/Angeles-Crest/Carmel drive with "draggy." **Nothing here trains the persona.**

## Summary

Co-host **Miles** reviews the **Porsche Taycan Turbo GT** — the ~1,092hp flagship Taycan — as
Porsche's answer to the Lucid Air Sapphire. His verdict: the **best track-focused electric
performance sedan** (double-wishbone + active hydraulic suspension, "body roll is nonexistent"), with
the most analog steering feel of any EV, but weak ~280mi range and less space than the Sapphire —
so the **Air Sapphire remains his overall pick**, while the Taycan wins on bespoke customization + track
feel. Attributed entirely to Miles.

## Key claims (all Miles — context only, NOT persona data)

- **[2024-08-22]** *(Miles)* ~1,092hp / 914 lb-ft dual-motor AWD; a 10-piston front caliper "bigger
  than my head"; a suspension that raises on door-open for easy entry. ~280mi range ("not livable for
  a lot of people," but Porsche buyers have other cars). ~$250k after options. (paraphrase)
- **[2024-08-22]** *(Miles)* "The most analog, most satisfying steering feel of any EV I've driven,"
  but **"I wholeheartedly disagree it drives like an electric 911"** (it's ~2,000lb heavier). Notes
  **CarPlay "1.5"** (a Porsche/Aston pre-CarPlay-Ultra step) with car-control integration. Overall best
  EV sedan = still the **Lucid Air Sapphire** (no compromises); the Taycan wins on bespoke styling +
  track. (paraphrase)
