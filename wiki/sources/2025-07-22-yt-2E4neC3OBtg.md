# The Escalade IQ *actually* has 500 Miles of Range!

## Summary

Marques lives with the **Cadillac Escalade IQ** — the biggest, heaviest vehicle he's ever tested
(9,300 lb, a 205kWh battery) — and it delivers the **longest range he's ever tested in an EV
(465mi quoted, 500 on the dash)**. He uses it to restate a standing doctrine: **certain vehicles
are "perfect to go electric" — massive, luxurious, soft, quiet, where you don't care about weight
— so "the electric one will be the best version ever."** A "living room on wheels" and the future
Uber Black of NYC. Fully Marques-narrated.

## Key claims

- **[2025-07-22]** Tesla justified the Cybertruck's modest range by claiming buyers value driving
  dynamics over a big heavy battery — "then GM said hold my beer" and built a **205kWh** battery into
  the Escalade IQ. "The biggest, heaviest vehicle I've ever tested (9,300 lb) — but you get a massive
  range." (paraphrase)
- **[2025-07-22]** The standing doctrine, restated: **"certain vehicles are perfect to go electric —
  you want something massive and luxurious and soft and quiet and you don't care if it's heavy or a
  little pricey? Okay, the electric one will be the best version ever."** Like the Rolls-Royce. "I
  think it's the best Escalade ever… this might be the official Uber Black of NYC in a few years."
  (quote)
- **[2025-07-22]** Specs: 205kWh, 800V/350kW charging, dual-motor AWD, ~750hp in V-max (0–60 ~4.7s),
  ~$130k, **465mi quoted range** (he's "seen 500 on the dash"). Real-world: "I've driven it every day
  for a week and not charged it — down to 50% battery and it still has 250 miles left." (paraphrase)
- **[2025-07-22]** "A living room on wheels" — auto-opening doors (with object sensors so they don't
  hit the car next to you), heated/ventilated/massaging seats with headrest speakers, **the best AKG
  sound system he's heard in a car** with great isolation, power-folding third row, airplane-style
  trays, a huge frunk (so deep it has a pull-out tray to reach the back). **No CarPlay** (Google
  built-in — Play Store, Maps). His piano-black gripe recurs. (paraphrase)
- **[2025-07-22]** Driving: incredibly soft adaptive air suspension ("you might go over a pothole and
  not notice on 24" rims"); four-wheel steer gives a surprisingly tight turning radius; "quick — way
  quicker than it has any right to be — but really it's about cruising and dominating the lane." A
  mirror gripe (the right side-mirror lacks the convex "objects are closer" curve → a big blind spot;
  use the blinker to reveal it). (paraphrase)
- **[2025-07-22]** Verdict / preference: "me personally, I'd take the **Volvo EX90** — just as quiet,
  better speakers, three rows, an awesome interior, amazing software, and not as huge. But maybe you
  want 500 miles of range — maybe that customer Tesla doesn't believe exists actually does." (quote)
