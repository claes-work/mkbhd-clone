# Bose Headphones 700: The King is Back! (@mkbhd, 2019-07-02)

## Summary
Review of the Bose Noise Cancelling Headphones 700 ("terrible name"). The framing is the throughline of the whole
batch: **"a prime example of competition driving things forward and making better products for us the
consumers"** — Bose only updated because Sony/Microsoft ate into its sales. Ends on the explicit slogan
**"companies compete, we win."**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-07-02] **THE THESIS, stated twice.** "'These are a **prime example of competition driving things forward
  and making better products for us the consumers.**' Bose's QC line had been 'stubbornly the same for years' as
  the industry standard — until the **Sony XM3 and Microsoft Surface Headphones** ate into sales and forced a
  response. Ends: **'I'm glad the competition has brought us yet another incredible choice — that's what you get
  for having better products around. So companies compete, we win.'**" The clearest one-line version of his
  recurring value (cf. [[2019-05-22-yt-qZGpmWrVSaU]], [[2019-06-21-yt-MOJ1_FtDGNs]]).
- [2019-07-02] **The new features are 'straight from the competition.'** "USB-C (was micro-USB), **dual-device
  Bluetooth**, a touchpad, per-1 granular noise-cancellation (0-10), superb mics. 'Many of these are just what we
  got in the new competition.'" Concretely demonstrates the thesis.
- [2019-07-02] **The honest nitpicks.** "**2.5 mm** (not 3.5 mm) wired jack — need their proprietary cable; **no
  EQ** in the app (flat sound signature — 'you're rarely buying these for the thumping bass, you're buying them
  for noise cancellation'); **no airline adapter** included anymore; case not the smallest (the Sonys fold)."
- [2019-07-02] **The Bose-vs-Sony verdict.** "'The **Sonys** are lighter, fold smaller, longer battery, $50
  cheaper. But for **pure noise-cancellation quality, the Bose are back on top**, with incredible mics and
  superior build. For the money the Sony might be a slightly better buy — but I prefer the Sony's sound with a
  little more character.'" A balanced, both-can-win recommendation.
