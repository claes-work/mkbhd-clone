# This EV Pretends to be a Gas Car! (Hyundai Ioniq 5N)

## Summary

Marques tests the **Hyundai Ioniq 5 N** and is won over by a gimmick he'd normally distrust: its
**"N e-Shift" mode that fully emulates a gas car** — a 7,750-rpm tach, simulated dual-clutch shifts,
pops and burbles, a redline you bounce off, and it's even *slower* when you shift badly. Where the
Dodge Charger EV's fake noise was a one-note flop, here the **full emulation is "shockingly good…
this is a very fun car."** Weak range (~200 mi) is the trade. Fully Marques-narrated.

## Key claims

- **[2024-07-09]** The 5 N is the performance version of the Ioniq 5 (like the EV6 GT to the EV6): 640
  hp, ~2.9s 0–60, but **the performance nukes the range (~200 mi real)** — "not for road trips." "You're
  not getting the N for practicality, you're getting it for the fun, and this car has a lot of fun."
  Design praise: "whatever Hyundai is doing with the design, keep it up." (quote + paraphrase)
- **[2024-07-09]** ⭐ The party trick — **full gas-car emulation**: N mode adds a tachometer, **paddle-
  shifters that become real gear shifters**, a **redline you actually stop accelerating at until you
  shift**, built-in torque that builds with RPM, and — crucially — **it's genuinely slower if you shift
  badly** ("the software cuts torque to feel like shifting gears, slowing you down but feeling more
  exciting"). Multiple sound profiles (Ignition/Evolution/Supersonic), inside and outside speakers.
  "It's actually shocking how well it works." (quote + paraphrase)
- **[2024-07-09]** Cross-reference: he ranks it among the **top-five EVs at disguising their weight**,
  "with the Porsche Taycan and the Rimac Nevera." Also frames it as the **"the R3X exists today"** case
  a year before his EX30 version ("this has to walk for the Rivian R3/R3X to run… and it's available to
  buy"). (quote + paraphrase)
- **[2024-07-09]** Verdict: "I'm a fan of a car like this existing — it's probably not even for me,
  but it's a very fun car." For most people a **Model 3 Performance** is the more well-rounded pick
  (better range); "but if you want that speedy hatch, look at one of these — just don't road-trip it."
  (quote)
