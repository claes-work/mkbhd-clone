# Top 5 Android 17 Features: I Swear It's New!

## Summary

Marques's annual Android top-five, this year for **Android 17 "Cinnamon Bun."** His framing:
these updates aren't generational overhauls anymore — "what would've been a dot-one update" — but
a thoughtful collection of quality-of-life tweaks. He also mounts a one-man campaign to **bring
back the public dessert names** (Google kept them internally). The #1 feature is small but
telling: they **finally re-split the Wi-Fi and mobile-data toggles** — proof of "Google actually
listening to users."

## Key claims

- **[2026-06-24]** Android updates "aren't massive generational overhauls anymore" — mostly cosmetic
  tweaks and quality-of-life stuff that "back in the day would've been a dot-one update." (paraphrase)
- **[2026-06-24]** He campaigns to **bring back the public dessert names** — Google stopped them after
  Q but **kept internal codenames** (Red Velvet Cake → … → Baklava for 16, **Cinnamon Bun for 17**);
  the system-notification glyph is literally a cinnamon bun. **"Bring back the public dessert names,
  Google, it's more fun this way."** (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2026-06-24]** #5 **built-in screen reactions** (key yourself into a screen recording — "nicely
  done, Google," even if it Sherlocks apps people already use); #4 **foldable game mode** (half screen
  = game, half = on-screen gamepad — "really cool, not gonna lie"); #3 the **"bubble bar"** (up to
  four floating app bubbles as a multitasking hub — "imagine taking notes over a YouTube video; I'm
  not the only freak who writes notes instead of having Gemini summarize everything"). (paraphrase +
  quote)
- **[2026-06-24]** #2 **aesthetic tweaks** — hide home-screen app labels, less empty white space in
  settings, more transparency in the widget picker, per-app dark-mode selection, and "You're all
  caught up" + a trophy when notifications are cleared. "It feels like somebody is going through being
  very thoughtful about little things." (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2026-06-24]** #1 **finally re-separated the Wi-Fi and mobile-data toggles** in Quick Settings
  (they'd been merged into one "Internet" toggle years ago). **"It's about damn time… part of a nice
  theme of Google actually listening to users."** Another example: you can finally customize the
  un-removable home-screen search bar. (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2026-06-24]** More coming: **natural-language widget creation**, the "Rambler" speech-to-text, and
  Pause Point wellbeing (from I/O). "Stuff like this makes me want to switch to the Pixel again… before
  I come back to my senses and remember the type of pain that can turn into." He's **again hoping Tensor
  takes a leap next generation, "but I've been hurt before, so I'm not gonna cross my fingers."**
  (quote)
- **[2026-06-24]** The Android-17 **Easter egg** (five taps → a constellation you connect any-way → the
  Asteroids game). Signs off "I'll cinnamon bun you later." (paraphrase)
