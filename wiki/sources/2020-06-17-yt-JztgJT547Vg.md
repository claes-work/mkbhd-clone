# PS5 Impressions: My Thoughts! (@mkbhd, 2020-06-17)

## Summary
An early take on the PS5 reveal (before hardware in hand) — the design, the two versions, price speculation, and
what the new hardware enables. A biography note: the **PS4 was his first-ever gaming console**, and he'll get the
digital edition.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-06-17] **BIOGRAPHY — the PS4 is his first-ever gaming console.** "'**The PS4 is my first gaming console ever
  and my current console of choice** — so I've been looking forward to what the PS5 brings 7 years later. (Also
  watching the Xbox Series X — a separate video.)'" A datable personal data point.
- [2020-06-17] **The polarizing design (a CyberTruck parallel).** "'People love it or hate it — **kind of like the
  CyberTruck.** I like it; it looks like an alien-planet next-gen console. But the **Xbox Series X looks better —
  more modern and minimal** (even if more boring). A **black PS5** would win more people over (white plastic ages
  badly).'"
- [2020-06-17] **The two versions make sense — he'll go digital.** "'The digital edition (no optical drive) makes
  sense: **I download all my games, never bought a physical one, don't need the Blu-ray player** — I'll get
  digital. But some people have worse internet, physical collections, or use it as their **only 4K Blu-ray
  player** (my PS4 is my only Blu-ray player). It also looks more elegant and will be cheaper.'"
- [2020-06-17] **The console-optimization advantage over PC.** "'The biggest upgrade is the **SSD.** Some compare
  the PS5 to a desktop gaming PC — I don't see it (thermal envelope). But the win is **optimization: everyone has
  the same PS5, so devs can max every detail** — unlike PC, where a $200-$2,000 tower might run your game.'" A
  reusable console-vs-PC lens.
- [2020-06-17] **Price speculation + the games.** "'Sony didn't reveal price/date. History (PS3 $599, PS4 $399)
  suggests **~$499 / $399 digital** — they can't charge $800+ if they want 100M impulse upgrades. Of the teasers,
  only **Gran Turismo 7** (looked close-to-done, would shine at 120 fps) and NBA 2K21 (a "cringy" over-lit teaser)
  interested me. I hope the PS5 is **quieter than the jet-engine PS4.**'"

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "These games can develop and optimize for every little detail... because everyone playing these games has the
> same PS5, whereas if you're developing a PC game, optimization's a bit harder."
> "The PS4 is actually my first gaming console ever and my current gaming console of choice."
