# Samsung Z Flip/Fold 6, Watch Ultra, Buds Pro and Ring Impressions (@mkbhd, 2024-07-10)

## Summary
Hands-on first impressions from Samsung's July Unpacked: **two phones and three wearables**. The **Z Fold 6 and Z Flip
6** are "very much still minor iterations" — squared-up sides, slightly thinner bezels, Snapdragon 8 Gen 3, a bigger
vapor chamber, new colors — but he reframes the dismissiveness: **"these are still at the forefront of two of the most
advanced parts of tech — foldables and AI."** The Flip's inside screen hits 2,600 nits and gets a bigger 4,000 mAh
battery + its first vapor chamber; both phones cost **$100 more than last year** ("the direction they're trending").
The AI push is **Galaxy AI** — surfaced into a Settings section (like Apple just did with Apple Intelligence) so
regular people use it: Call Assist, live Interpreter, Note/Transcript Assist, generative fill in Gallery, and **Drawing
Assist** (sketch→art, "looks almost exactly like Apple's Image Playground"). The wearables: the **Galaxy Watch Ultra**
(titanium/sapphire/100m, an orange "Quick Button," 100-hour power-saving battery) — **openly targeting the Apple Watch
Ultra** at $650 vs $800; the **Buds3 / Buds3 Pro** ("everyone's saying they look like first-gen AirPods" — with blade
lights on the Pro stems); and finally the **Galaxy Ring** ($399, titanium, ~7-day battery, 100m water resistance) — a
24/7 tracker taking on the Oura Ring, with the key difference being **no subscription** (Oura's ~$6/mo adds up) — but
it requires a Samsung Galaxy phone. "All pretty premiumly priced."

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2024-07-10] **Minor iterations — but at the forefront of foldables + AI.** "'The Fold 6 and Flip 6 are very much
  still minor iterations on last year — squared sides, thinner bezels, Snapdragon 8 Gen 3, a bigger vapor chamber. That
  sounds dismissive, but these are still at the forefront of two of the most advanced parts of tech: foldables and
  AI.'"
- [2024-07-10] **Galaxy AI, surfaced like Apple Intelligence.** "'Similar to what Apple just did, Samsung is surfacing
  all its AI into a Settings section and branding it Galaxy AI so regular people actually use it — Call Assist, a live
  Interpreter, Note/Transcript Assist, generative fill in Gallery, and Drawing Assist (sketch→art), which looks almost
  exactly like Apple's Image Playground.'"
- [2024-07-10] **The Watch Ultra openly targets the Apple Watch Ultra.** "'The new Galaxy Watch Ultra — titanium,
  sapphire, 100m, an orange "Quick Button," a 100-hour power-saving battery — is clearly targeted at the Apple Watch
  Ultra; they're not hiding it, same name. But it's $650 vs Apple's $800.'"
- [2024-07-10] **The Galaxy Ring's edge over Oura: no subscription.** "'The Galaxy Ring is $399 (titanium, ~7-day
  battery, 100m water resistance), taking on the Oura Ring — which is the standard. Oura is $100 cheaper at base but has
  a ~$6/month subscription that adds up; the Galaxy Ring has no subscription, which I'm happy to see. The catch: it
  only works with a Samsung Galaxy phone.'"
- [2024-07-10] **Everything's $100 more.** "'The cherry on top: both foldables are $100 more than last year — the Flip 6
  is $1,099 and the Fold 6 is $1,899. That's the direction they're trending. All this stuff is pretty premiumly
  priced.'"
