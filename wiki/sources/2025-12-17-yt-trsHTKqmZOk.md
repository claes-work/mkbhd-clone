# Samsung Galaxy Z Tri Fold Impressions: NOW It Makes Sense!

## Summary

Hands-on with the **Samsung Galaxy Z Tri Fold**, a phone the comments called "ridiculous,"
"pointless," a "gimmick" — but which Marques says is **"actually starting to make foldables
make sense to me."** It folds twice into a **10-inch, ~16:1 widescreen tablet in your pocket**,
and the key insight is that **the rectangular aspect ratio — not just the size — is what makes
the difference** versus the "blank pixels around the video" problem of an 8-inch square fold.
Thick and heavy closed, first-gen durability caveats, and pricey (~$2,500–3,000), but "for the
right person who knows what they're getting into, this can be worth it."

## Key claims

- **[2025-12-17]** The comments called it ridiculous/pointless/a gimmick, "but now that I've used
  this phone, this is a device that's actually starting to **make foldables make sense to me.**"
  (quote)
- **[2025-12-17]** It opens **twice** into a **~10-inch tablet** that fits in a pocket. The key
  isn't the extra 2 inches over an 8" fold — **"it's the shape of that screen, the rectangular
  aspect ratio, that's making all the difference."** (quote)
- **[2025-12-17]** Design: it folds like a **"U"** (a cover display outside; unfold fully to a
  second interior display that folds twice; the cover sits unused on the back) — contrasted with
  the 2024 **Huawei Mate XT**, which folded like a "Z" with the big screen always exposed. (paraphrase)
- **[2025-12-17]** Specs: **Snapdragon 8 Elite for Galaxy** (last-gen, not the Gen 5 — they'd been
  developing it long enough); 16GB/512GB; the Z Fold 7's cameras (flagship 200MP main + mid 3x/
  ultrawide) and 6.5" cover display. Closed it's **12.9mm thick, over 300g** — "about 50% more phone"
  — but "doesn't feel that ridiculous... it's actually thinner than the Z Fold 1 through 5." (paraphrase)
- **[2025-12-17]** Open, it's **3.9–4.2mm thin** and "suddenly 309g is not so heavy" (most 10" tablets
  are ~500g). Battery **5,600mAh** (largest ever in a Samsung foldable, but small for a 10" screen,
  split into three columns). **"Silicon carbon battery, maybe the next gen — it just feels like the
  perfect phone for that"** (a thread he says he's "hammered home so many times this year"). Fastest-
  charging Samsung foldable at 45W (charger in box). (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-12-17]** Why the widescreen matters: a full-screen video on an 8" square fold is "not that
  much bigger [than a slab] — a bunch of blank pixels around it," but on the trifold's 16:1 canvas
  "you see how much better that is." Also great for full-screen games, three-column Files, tablet
  YouTube, web browsing, and **three side-by-side apps**. It's **the only Samsung phone that runs
  Samsung DeX standalone** (no monitor needed). (paraphrase)
- **[2025-12-17]** Praise for the engineering thoughtfulness: the **bottom leaf is slightly longer**
  so a lip sticks out to grab; strong magnets; a guided fold order (it vibrates and warns if you fold
  the wrong side first). "You can almost feel the different variations they went through." (paraphrase)
- **[2025-12-17]** Downsides: the twice-folding **middle screen is the most delicate part** (two
  creases at two different radii; a **first-gen durability asterisk**); the inner screen is only
  1,600 nits (vs 2,600 outside); thick/heavy closed; and it's a huge table-rocker (a case is included).
  (paraphrase)
- **[2025-12-17]** Price: he **imported it from Samsung Korea for ~$2,500**; expects ~$2,500–3,000 in
  the US. "It's pricey... but also maybe not insane." Verdict: **"for the right person who knows what
  they're getting into, this can be worth it"** — a legit 10" tablet in your pocket "is such a game."
  (quote)
- **[2025-12-17]** Closing observation on Samsung's split personality: "it's hilarious that this exists
  from Samsung — the same company that is **ridiculously slow-moving and conservative on their Galaxy S
  phones**... but they'll also try this completely insane trifold thing and **throw stuff at the wall
  to see if it sticks**." (quote)
