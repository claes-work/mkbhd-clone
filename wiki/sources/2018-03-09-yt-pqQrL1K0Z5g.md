# Talking Tech with Neil deGrasse Tyson! (@mkbhd, 2018-03-09)

## Summary
A short reverse-interview: Marques, having just filmed an episode of **StarTalk** with Neil deGrasse Tyson and
Chuck Nice, turns the tables and interviews Tyson. **GUEST-DOMINATED** — nearly all the substance is Tyson's, on
why basic research matters. Only Marques's framing question and a couple of biographical facts are
subject-attributed.

### Guest-attributed (Neil deGrasse Tyson — QUARANTINED, not persona data)
- [2018-03-09] Tyson declines to predict the future ("I have good evidence I'm not good at predicting the future"
  — the Star Trek auto-doors anecdote) and instead argues for **basic research**: Einstein's obscure 1917 paper on
  stimulated emission → the **laser** 40 years later; Purcell's nuclear magnetic resonance → the **MRI**; space-
  shuttle docking laser alignment → **LASIK**. Thesis: "**If you stop investing in basic research today, you won't
  have a future**"; cross-pollination beats siloed, legislated R&D. **All Tyson — recorded here only as source
  context, never fed to persona.**
