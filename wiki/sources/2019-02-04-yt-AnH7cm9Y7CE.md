# The Samsung Galaxy S10 Model! (@mkbhd, 2019-02-04)

## Summary
A pre-launch hands-on with casemaker dummy models of the Galaxy S10 / S10+ (a recurring format — "always has an
expiration date"). Reads the design/lineup from the mockups ahead of the Feb 20 event, and calls this "probably
the last year Samsung keeps the headphone jack."

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-02-04] **The lineup — "at least three phones."** "**Galaxy S10 (6.1"), S10+ (6.44"), and an S10e/S10 Lite
  (~5.8", single camera) — Samsung's answer to the iPhone XR.** Prices ~$800–$1,100, up to ~$2,000 for a 1 TB
  model. Maybe even five phones (a budget one, a folding one) at the event." Situates the stratified lineup.
- [2019-02-04] **What the models show.** "**Feels more like a mini Note 9 than a larger S9** (boxier, squarer
  corners); thin side bezels, the Infinity Edge maxed. **The back is now just cameras + flash + biometrics — the
  fingerprint reader moves under the display glass.** Triple rear cameras (standard/telephoto/super-wide, like the
  V40); **a single front camera on the S10, dual on the S10+ (regular + super-wide selfie, like the Pixel 3 — which
  I love).**" A careful pre-read.
- [2019-02-04] **"Probably the last year Samsung keeps the headphone jack."** "It keeps the jack — and **I think
  this may be the last year. If you're dead set on a jack, this or the next Note is probably your only flagship
  option.**" A dated industry call.
- [2019-02-04] **The worries + the recurring gripe.** "I hope they **don't hobble the smaller phone** (I fear dual
  cameras on the S10 vs triple on the S10+ — put the same stuff in both)." And **the un-remappable Bixby button,
  again.** What he wants most: **a camera competitive with the Pixel** (a Night Sight competitor) + One UI.

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "It feels more like a mini Note 9 than a larger S9."
> "It does keep that headphone jack — and I think this may be the last year Samsung keeps it around. If you're
> dead set on a headphone jack, this is probably going to be your only flagship option."
> "I hope they don't hobble the smaller phone — I hope they put all the same stuff in both."
