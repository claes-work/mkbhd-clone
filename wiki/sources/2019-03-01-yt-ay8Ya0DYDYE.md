# OnePlus 6T Revisited: Better than Pixel 3? (@mkbhd, 2019-03-01)

## Summary
A comparison essay on why he kept switching between his two favorite 2018 phones — the Pixel 3 XL and the OnePlus
6T. The durable insight: **there are two "best cameras" (best quality vs best experience)**, and **objectively
better ≠ a better experience.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-03-01] **The back-and-forth — camera vs performance.** "My favorite phones of 2018 to *use* were a toss-up
  between the **Pixel 3 XL (A+ camera, C+ performance)** and the **OnePlus 6T (A+ performance — the fastest Android
  phone I've used — but a C+ camera).** I kept switching: the Pixel's performance pushed me away, the camera pulled
  me back." A candid, dated dilemma.
- [2019-03-01] **THE INSIGHT — "there are two best cameras."** "There's the camera that takes the **absolute best
  quality images** (the Pixel — and it's not even close) and the **best overall camera *experience*** (quality +
  **speed** + everything else). Pixel keeps winning best *quality*; I'd give best *experience* to the iPhone (speed/
  video), Samsung (the new ultra-wide), or the Mate 20 Pro." A genuinely useful distinction.
- [2019-03-01] **"4 GB of RAM actually isn't enough."** "The Pixel's performance/lag issues stuck for a **second
  year in a row** — disappointing, especially since Google makes the phone. I'm thinking **4 GB of RAM really isn't
  enough.**" Ties his RAM-matters / Pixel-longevity thread.
- [2019-03-01] **The Gcam-port tip.** "Installing the **Google Camera APK** on the 6T brings its camera from a C+
  to a **B/B+** (better DR/exposure/white-balance + Night Sight) — still nowhere near the Pixel on detail, but
  **every 6T owner should know about it.**" A practical, dated recommendation.
- [2019-03-01] **The things he missed on the 6T.** No **wireless charging** ("I have chargers in convenient places
  — I really missed it; and the OnePlus 7 reportedly won't have it either"); no **water resistance**; the Pixel's
  **stereo front speakers** (the one upside of its thick bezels). The notch was a **net-zero** for him. OxygenOS is
  great (fast, dark mode, frequent updates) but Pixel's Android is smarter (Call Screening, Now Playing, Night
  Sight).
- [2019-03-01] **THE VERDICT — "objectively better ≠ a better experience."** "**Objectively, the Pixel 3 is the
  better phone** (wireless charging, water resistance, better camera/display/software/build). **But the *experience*
  of using the 6T was often better — even more impressive given the $200+ price difference.** There's just never a
  perfect phone: **Pixel = A+ camera / C+ performance; 6T = A+ performance / C+ camera. Which do you choose?**" A
  sharp, honest close (looking ahead to the OnePlus 7 / Pixel 4).

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "There really are two best cameras: the one that takes the absolute best-quality images, and the best overall
> camera *experience* — a combination of image quality and speed and a bunch of other things."
> "Objectively? No, the OnePlus 6T isn't better than the Pixel 3 XL. But the experience of using the 6T was often
> better than the experience of using the 3 — which is even more impressive because of the price difference."
> "I'm thinking maybe four gigs of RAM actually really isn't enough."
