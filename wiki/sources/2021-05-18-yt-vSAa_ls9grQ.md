# M1 iMac Review: Ultra Thin Design Choice! (@mkbhd, 2021-05-18)

## Summary
Review of the 24" M1 iMac — "basically an M1 in a new, colorful, really thin outfit," so it's **a design video.**
The durable critique: **"after I decide where to put it, I don't care how thin it is"** — he'd rather it were
thicker with no chin. He comes around on the *why* of the **white bezels** (they melt into white walls — the
friendly-family-computer positioning) but still prefers black; praises the **best mics ever in a Mac** + a finally-
1080p webcam; and flags the **base-model port situation** (likely fewer ports than your current computer).

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-05-18] **It's an M1 in a new outfit — so this is a design video.** "'Performance testing is barely
  necessary — **it runs circles around the baseline 21.5" it replaces, and performs like the other M1 Macs** (an
  M1 Mac mini glued to a nice display). So this is really a **design video** — what I think of the M1 inside this
  new 2021 design.'"
- [2021-05-18] **The friendly-family-computer positioning + coming around on white bezels.** "'A retro throwback
  to the '90s colorful iMacs — an **even friendlier, more accessible "family-room" computer, not a metal box**
  (the same challenge as a Google Home or HomePod). **I came around on the white bezels: against a white wall
  they melt away more than black would** — the Nest Hub logic. I still take black bezels every day, but I see it.'"
- [2021-05-18] **THE CHIN CRITIQUE — "nix the chin; I don't care how thin it is."** "'Most of the computer is in
  the chin, letting them make it **razor-thin (11.5 mm — thinner than the depth of a headphone jack, so the jack's
  on the side).** But **after I get it out of the box and lock it in place, I don't care how thin it is. ~100% of
  buyers would've been fine with 20 mm thick, the computer behind the display, no chin — and it would look way
  better** (and leave room for better speakers). That's just me.'" A promotable design-over-substance critique.
- [2021-05-18] **The best-in-class built-ins — finally a 1080p webcam + the best Mac mics.** "'As an all-in-one,
  it's the gold standard for built-ins: **finally a 1080p webcam (better colors/contrast than the M1 laptops),
  and the best mics ever in a Mac** (they focus on your voice from across the room). Plus the Touch ID Magic
  Keyboard ("literally an old iPhone's Touch ID button cut into a key") — a bit slow over Bluetooth, but works.'"
- [2021-05-18] **THE PORT WARNING — the base model.** "'The $1,299 base model has **almost no ports (two
  Thunderbolt + a headphone jack, no Ethernet-on-brick, no SD slot) — a wired mouse and keyboard and you're out.**
  The $1,499+ models add two more USB-C (four total) + Ethernet-on-brick + a gpu core + Touch ID. **Be wary of the
  base situation — it's almost guaranteed fewer ports than your current computer.**'"
- [2021-05-18] **The verdict + the anticipation for the higher-end silicon.** "'Even not being the target buyer,
  **it's a great computer — miles ahead of the outdated 21.5" it replaces** (go Mac mini + monitor only if you
  hate the design/glossy/white bezels). But **I'm excited for the next steps — the big MacBook Pro, an iMac Pro
  (maybe no chin), an Apple-silicon Mac Pro.**'"
