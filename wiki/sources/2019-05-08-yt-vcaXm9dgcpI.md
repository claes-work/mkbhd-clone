# Google Pixel 3a Impressions: Flips the Script! (@mkbhd, 2019-05-08)

## Summary
First hands-on with the $399 Pixel 3a (announced at Google I/O 2019). The thesis: it "flips the script" on
the usual budget-Android formula — instead of high-end specs with a compromised camera/screen/build, it
compromises the specs but keeps a flagship-class camera and a good screen.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-05-08] **"It flips the script" — the inverted budget formula.** "The **typical champion budget Android
  phone** (e.g. the Pocophone F1) brings **high-end specs down in price** but sacrifices in three predictable
  areas: **camera, screen, build quality.** The 3a does the **opposite** — it sacrifices in specs (Snapdragon
  670, 4 GB RAM) but keeps a **killer camera and a pretty great screen.** That's a very different set of corners
  to cut." His central framing.
- [2019-05-08] **The camera is the whole point.** "It shares the **same single sensor and essentially the same
  camera software as the pixel 3** — arguably the image-quality reigning champ — making it **instantly the best
  camera on any budget phone** and arguably right up there with the best on any phone, period. That's a first
  for a sub-$500 phone." Continues his best-budget-camera thread ([[2019-05-24-yt-XnSqlX1kCQo]]).
- [2019-05-08] **Where the money was saved.** "**No wireless charging, no water resistance** (they skipped IP
  certification), no expandable storage; **hard polycarbonate** instead of glass; a **single top speaker**
  instead of dual front-facing stereo. But it **keeps the headphone jack, an OLED display, and the pixel 3's
  excellent haptic motor** — 'literally the best in any Android phone.'"
- [2019-05-08] **The concern — Pixel software staying fast over time.** "Even the flagship pixel 3 hasn't had
  the best time **staying fast** with its silicon, so an even lower-end chip may suffer the same fate. Google is
  **trusting the software** to give a good-enough experience in spite of mid-range specs." A dated reservation.

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "This pixel 3a kind of flips that formula — it flips the script backwards on its head, where this blatantly
> sacrifices in the specs but it has a killer camera and a pretty great screen."
> "I don't think I've ever been able to say a budget Android phone actually legit has an excellent camera."
