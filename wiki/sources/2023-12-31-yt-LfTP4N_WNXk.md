# The Strangest Electrified Sports Car Yet! (Corvette E-Ray)

## Summary

Marques reviews the **Corvette E-Ray** — a **performance hybrid** (not an efficiency hybrid) — and
digs into its "weird implementation." A front-axle electric motor (160 hp) + a rear-axle V8 (500 hp)
= ~650 hp, the **first AWD Corvette and quickest ever**, on all-season tires so you can daily it
year-round. Its charm is a set of quirks: a silent **stealth mode** (electric-only startup), a
**Charge+** button that refills the tiny battery in ~15 seconds of driving. His verdict: **the most
livable Corvette ever made.** Fully Marques-narrated.

## Key claims

- **[2023-12-31]** The category: **"one of those hybrids geared for performance, not gas mileage — the
  best of both worlds"** (he ties it to the coming wave of sports-car hybrids, cf. the Ferrari 296).
  ~650 hp (500 V8 + 160 e-motor), first AWD Corvette, quickest ever (~2.5s 0–60); **all-season tires
  from the factory = "the biggest statement you can make: drive this all year round."** (quote)
- **[2023-12-31]** The unusual architecture: **the front axle is powered ONLY by the battery/e-motor;
  the rear ONLY by the V8** — technically AWD, but the axles "don't talk to each other," so you don't
  get all the traction benefits of a normal AWD/hybrid. (paraphrase)
- **[2023-12-31]** The quirks (a Doug-DeMuro "sub-quirk" nod): **stealth mode** — silent electric-only
  startup (~3–4 mi, up to 45 mph) to leave the neighborhood without waking anyone — **but only from
  ignition, not mid-drive, and not below 50°F**; **Charge+** opportunistically refills the small battery
  **"like 15 seconds"** from ~80% to 100% while driving. (paraphrase)
- **[2023-12-31]** ⭐ VERDICT: **"The most friendly, most daily-drivable, in my opinion the most livable
  Corvette ever made, thanks to going kind of electric."** The one that makes the most sense to daily in
  winter (AWD + all-seasons + still quick and great-sounding). (quote)
