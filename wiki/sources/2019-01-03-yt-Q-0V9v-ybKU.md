# Xiaomi Mi Mix 3: The Ultimate Slider? (@mkbhd, 2019-01-03)

## Summary
Not a full review — a "deeper look at a notch-avoidance technique": the Mi Mix 3's **manual slider**. Marques
catalogs the whole taxonomy of notch-avoidance, argues the manual slider beats motorized on durability, and flags
the slippery-glass design flaw via a smart BlackBerry Priv comparison.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-01-03] **The notch-avoidance taxonomy.** "The race to bezel-less has spawned: the **standard notch, the
  teardrop, the infamous bathtub, the selfie pop-up, the motorized chassis — and now the manual slider.** Honestly
  **the manual slider is one of my favorite ways to do it.**" A useful map of the design landscape. (The Mi Mix
  won his runner-up best-design 2018.)
- [2019-01-03] **Manual slider > motorized on durability.** "The spring-loaded slide **snaps into place** (that
  satisfying, addicting click + a sound/light effect); it opens the dual front cameras. The upside of a **manual**
  slider vs a motorized pop-up: **no motors = a lower chance of failure** (springs are good for hundreds of
  thousands of cycles; a tiny software-driven motor I can't be so sure about). And **if it fails, you only lose the
  front cameras.**" His engineering-minded durability read. (Still: "**it's a moving part — a single grain of sand
  could become a problem**"; you can see light passing through the inside.)
- [2019-01-03] **The design insight — the BlackBerry Priv was grippy for a reason.** "There was another vertical
  slider — the **BlackBerry Priv** (I still have mine) — and they made its back a **tacky rubberized finish: the
  grippiest phone I own.** That made sense, because **sliding a phone open needs a secure grip.** The **Mi Mix 3 is
  all slippery mirror glass**, which is more dangerous in the hand for a slider (and double-dangerous because glass
  breaks). **The slippery back is a legit con here** — maybe future sliders use better materials." A sharp
  materials-follow-function observation.
- [2019-01-03] **A clever detail + the format note.** Notes the **fake second earpiece grille** (a hollow
  pass-through so the real earpiece still works when slid up). Solid specs (6.4" OLED, SD845, 6 GB). "Let me know
  what you think of these **shorter, more regular topical videos** — I think they're fun." A dated format experiment.
