# 2026 Tesla Model Y Performance — Actually still the best?

## Summary

Marques reviews the Tesla Model Y Performance (borrowed from George at Jan S Auto Haus) and
uses it to argue that even though the competition has caught up on aesthetics, build quality,
and interiors, **testing a Tesla reminds you of the things Tesla is still the best at**: the
display, the software, regenerative braking, self-driving, and the cameras. The trade-off is
the appliance-like, button-less feel — "the emotion and fun of a lot of other cars is
virtually absent." Fully Marques-narrated (a Marques-fronted @AutoFocus video).

## Key claims

- **[2026-01-15]** He's long praised Teslas (**"I called the Model S the best daily driver in the
  world"**; Tesla's been "ahead of the curve for EVs for a long time"), and while the competition
  has "caught up in many ways," driving one "reminds you of all the ways Tesla is still ahead."
  (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2026-01-15]** The Model Y Performance: ~460hp, 0–60 in 3.3s, ~305mi on 21" wheels, in his
  "poppy ultra red" (**"I have a soft spot for red cars"**). "The best-looking back of any Tesla
  ever." Practical family stuff (power-folding second row, sub-trunk, NACS fast charging).
  (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2026-01-15]** What Tesla still does best: (1) the **16" display** — "the gold standard for
  physical displays in cars" (bright/high-res/thin-bezel, "like an iPad"), ironic since there's no
  HUD or cluster; (2) **the best software in a car that isn't CarPlay/Android Auto** (most
  responsive/smoothest; a real-time car model); (3) **regenerative braking — "A+, the best anyone's
  ever done"** (smooth lift-off regen with a "limo stop" roll-off, unlike the Taycan); (4) **the
  best self-driving** he's tried (links his tier-list video); (5) **the best cameras** (blinker/
  reverse) "maybe only Rivian comes close." (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2026-01-15]** The critique: **Tesla has the fewest buttons of any car — "it really contributes
  to the way these things end up feeling, which is basically like an appliance. The emotion and fun
  of a lot of other cars is virtually absent."** He wants more physical controls and a single
  "sport-everything" button. (quote)
- **[2026-01-15]** The refresh's real improvement: **suspension/NVH** — "way less crashy, way
  smoother, quiet" (a better passenger experience "for those of you who ride in the back of these as
  Ubers all the time, like I do"). (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2026-01-15]** **Tesla commoditized straight-line speed** long ago (0–60 in 3.3s for ~$60k; the
  Model S Plaid's ~1,000hp under $100k) — "testing these reminds you how different Teslas are that
  way." But the Performance "isn't dramatically different in brakes or suspension — it's just more
  power." (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2026-01-15]** Verdict: he now **prefers many other interiors to Tesla's**, and others lead on
  build quality — "but it's really hard to escape the stuff they still do super well: the best
  self-driving, screen, regen braking, software." (quote)
