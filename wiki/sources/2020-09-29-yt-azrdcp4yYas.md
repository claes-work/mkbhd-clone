# The Galaxy S20 Fan Edition: Hear Me Out! (@mkbhd, 2020-09-29)

## Summary
A landmark thesis video hung on the $699 Galaxy S20 FE: **flagship phones getting much better AND much more
expensive has made the whole landscape more interesting** — it birthed a **flourishing mid-range tier.** Threads
in the **etymology of "flagship,"** Samsung's **throw-everything-at-every-price** strategy, and a revealed
**sales-volume curve** (the Ultra was most popular week one, then the cheaper models blew past it). The FE itself
is "a surprisingly good phone."

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-09-29] **PRICES CREPT UP → a more interesting landscape.** "'~8 years ago phones **maxed out at ~$550**
  (Droid X, Galaxy Nexus) — there were only flagships or not-flagships. Prices crept up (GPe HTC One $599, S5
  $649, iPhone 6 Plus $749, iPhone X $1,000), and now a folding phone runs $1,500–$2,000. **Flagships getting
  much better AND much more expensive made the overall landscape much more interesting.**'"
- [2020-09-29] **THE MID-RANGE TIER IS FLOURISHING — the real point.** "'Now that the **ceiling of smartphone
  tech is so much higher,** there's **way more variety and difference between all these middle prices than ever
  before — the birth and flourishing of an awesome mid-range tier** that takes the high-end stuff and brings it
  down. The FE isn't just for fans — it's **for anybody wanting a compelling set of boxes checked at $699.**'"
- [2020-09-29] **SAMSUNG'S STRATEGY — a phone at literally every price.** "'Sort samsung.com by price and
  they'll sell you a phone at $99, $129, $199... $699, $750... $1,000, $1,200, $1,980, $3,299 — **a phone at
  basically every significant price.** We've known this is their **throw-as-many-options-as-they-can, see-what-
  sticks** strategy, but seeing it laid out is something different.'"
- [2020-09-29] **THE SALES-VOLUME CURVE — the reveal.** "'Samsung revealed that the **S20 Ultra was the most
  popular S20 in month one** (the enthusiasts, week one) — **but the volume buyers caught up and blew past it,
  and now the Ultra is the LEAST popular of the lineup.** There are **way more buyers at the low-to-mid range
  than the high end, even though we spend all our attention on the flagship.** That's why companies focus so
  hard on mid-range.'" A promotable enthusiast-vs-mass-market data point.
- [2020-09-29] **THE FLAGSHIP ETYMOLOGY — why we use the word.** "'That's why we use "flagship" — **the ship
  with the flag, the biggest, most heavily armed, most important one to pay attention to.** Only partially true.
  And it's every company: cheaper iPhones outsell the $1,000 ones, the OnePlus Nord put them back on the map,
  Xiaomi/Huawei dominance is mostly lower/mid-tier.'"
- [2020-09-29] **The FE itself — "surprisingly good," and "plastic to get the price down is a good call."** "'A
  **big flat 120 Hz OLED** (Samsung's others are curved or folding), Snapdragon 865 in the 5G version everywhere
  (no Exynos), three years of updates, good battery, fine cameras. **Plastic to get the price down turns out to
  be a good call** (less likely to shatter; most people case it anyway). A good buy for a lot of people.'"

## Notable verbatim quotes
