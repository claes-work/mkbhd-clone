# Huge Apple 2021 Rumor Reactions! (@mkbhd, 2021-01-17)

## Summary
Reactions to a wave of Mark Gurman rumors about Apple's 2021 roadmap (the rumors are Gurman's, quarantined; only
Marques's reactions train the persona). Durable threads: the **M1's GPU ceiling** (the Intel 16" MacBook Pro still
beats it on graphics), **MagSafe returning to laptops** + **"RIP Touch Bar, good riddance"** framed as **"we're
going backwards to the 2015 MacBook Pro"** (a good thing), and the most-excited item — a **smaller Apple-silicon
Mac Pro** (G4-Cube-sized) as "the fully unlocked best version" of Apple silicon.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-01-17] **Preface — "a lot of it sounds too good to be true."** "'This is a very reliable source (Gurman),
  but a lot of it sounds too good to be true — **I wouldn't bet my happiness entirely on it.**' (The rumors —
  14"/16" Apple-silicon MacBook Pros, a redesigned iMac, a smaller Mac Pro, an S-year iPhone, AirTags, a mini-LED
  iPad Pro — are Gurman's; quarantined.)'"
- [2021-01-17] **The M1's GPU ceiling — the real open question.** "'As good as the M1 Macs are, **they're great
  at replacing the baseline models that didn't need GPUs — but the Intel 16" MacBook Pro still destroys the M1 on
  GPU.** These higher-end Macs will be our first look at whether Apple builds a massive integrated GPU or keeps
  discrete GPUs.'" His grounded read on where Apple silicon still has to prove itself.
- [2021-01-17] **MagSafe back in laptops + "RIP Touch Bar, good riddance" = going backwards to 2015.** "'**MagSafe
  returning to laptops would be a pleasant surprise** (the original was just magnets and safe — kick the cable and
  it falls out). And **no Touch Bar — good riddance** ("it never lived up to its potential"; ~75% of my replies
  agreed). Funny how these rumors feel like **we're just going backwards to the 2015 MacBook Pro** — old keyboards,
  no Touch Bar, MagSafe back — which is great.'"
- [2021-01-17] **Most excited — the smaller Apple-silicon Mac Pro (the "fully unlocked" Apple silicon).** "'The
  rumored **Apple-silicon Mac Pro at less than half the size (G4-Cube-nostalgic)** is what the creative in me is
  most excited for — when I first saw Apple silicon I went straight to the top: **what does the fully unlocked best
  version look like? It looks like that Mac will be it.** The question: is it above the range, or a cheaper
  mid-desktop?'" (Extends his M1 "most-excited-for-the-desktops" take.)
- [2021-01-17] **The iPhone S-year + the recurring wishes.** "'An "S year" (minor updates); the main change is an
  **in-display fingerprint reader — I'm bracing for it to be an excuse to get rid of something else** (cf. the
  portless video). **No mention of a 120 Hz iPhone display — weird that it doesn't have one; I really hope the
  iPhone 13 gets it.** A **foldable iPhone** is a long-term background project — "the day Apple joins, it
  legitimizes the whole alternate-form-factor space."'"
- [2021-01-17] **The creator asides — HDR workflow + "matte black laptops, bring them back."** "'I've been
  considering **moving this channel to an HDR workflow** (the Pro Display XDR, YouTube, and the cameras all support
  it — it's getting the pipeline together that's the challenge) — so mini-LED/brighter high-contrast displays
  interest me. And a rumored light-absorbing black finish: **matte black laptops — Apple, you used to do it, bring
  them back.**'" Persona/gear + aesthetic signals.

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "The 16-inch MacBook Pro currently with an Intel chip still destroys the M1 Mac in the GPU department."
