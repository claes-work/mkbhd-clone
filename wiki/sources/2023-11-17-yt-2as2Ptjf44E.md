# Is Humane's AI Pin the Future or a Flop? (Waveform, 2023-11-17)

> ⚠️ **Ensemble podcast.** This episode is **Marques + David** (Andrew absent — new dad),
> which makes attribution unusually clean. No speaker labels, but the Humane analysis is
> first-person Marques. **High yield — his EARLY (pre-review) Humane Pin take.**

## Summary
The Humane AI Pin has just been unveiled and priced ($699 + $24/mo). Marques gives his
**first, pre-hands-on read** — months before his April 2024 "worst product" review — a
mix of deep skepticism about the projector UI and genuine interest in the *idea* of an
ambient AI assistant.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated 2023-11-17)
- ★ **[Marques, clearly — the early Humane take]** *"This gadget has the potential to be a
  really big or really small gap between how I think it's going to go and how it could
  actually go."* Watching the videos, "**this does not seem great**": the **hand-projector
  UI** raises a stack of doubts — hand-tracking, the pin **sagging on clothing** from its
  weight, **daylight visibility**, **different skin tones**, **accessibility if you don't
  have a hand**. *"This could turn out to be just as bad as I think, or it could totally
  blow my mind."* **★ The precursor to the April-2024 "worst product" verdict —
  documents his skepticism dating to launch.**
- ★ **[Marques, clearly — the steelman]** He genuinely **likes the idea of an ambient AI
  assistant** you "just have around you," and wonders if it's simply **ahead of its time**
  ("the tech just isn't good enough yet"). Compares to **Ray-Ban smart glasses** (camera +
  language model) — the invisible, personalized assistant is "sick if it works."
- ★ **[Marques, clearly — personal/fitness]** He's **wanted to log his macros for years**
  and finds manual apps miserable: **"MyFitnessPal is horrible — if your food doesn't have
  a barcode, good luck."** The Humane "log my macros from a photo" pitch is exactly the
  use case he wants. *(ties to his fitness/tracking thread.)*
- **[Marques, clearly — design jab]** *"Chrome-on-tech is just a terrible idea"* (re: the
  $100 chrome/black Humane color option).
