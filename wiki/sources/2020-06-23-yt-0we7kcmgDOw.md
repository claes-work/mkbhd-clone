# WWDC 2020 Impressions: Widgets and The Great Transition! (@mkbhd, 2020-06-23)

## Summary
WWDC 2020 coverage — the online keynote. The landmark: **Apple announced the Mac's transition from Intel to Apple
Silicon**, validating his 2018 prediction. Plus iOS 14 (with a heavy Apple-copies-Android theme) and macOS Big
Sur. (Sponsored by MacPaw.)

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-06-23] **THE GREAT TRANSITION — Apple Silicon (his prediction validated).** "'Apple announced the Mac is
  moving **from Intel to their own custom silicon** — not a surprise ("we saw it churning; we knew it was
  coming"). We've seen best-in-class performance from the iPhone and iPad chips; now imagine it in a **laptop/
  desktop thermal envelope** — a **Mac Pro with all the thermal headroom, or a MacBook Pro that doesn't throttle.**
  The upside: **Apple controls its own destiny** instead of waiting on Intel; the downside: a ~2-year **transition
  period** (devs choose Intel vs Apple-Silicon; Universal binaries smooth it). First Apple-Silicon Mac by **end of
  2020.**'" The payoff to his 2018 Apple-Silicon-in-Macs call ([[2018-11-29-yt-e7sw5xA066Y]]).
- [2020-06-23] **iOS 14 — "stop me if you've seen this before" (Apple-Android convergence).** "'The App Library
  (an app drawer), **home-screen widgets**, picture-in-picture, App Clips (≈ Android Instant Apps), default email/
  browser apps finally — **so many "new" iOS 14 features have been in Android since the beginning.** The theme:
  **two mature OSes overlapping more and more.**' (And a few Android could copy back — pinned/inline-reply group
  chats.)" His even-handed both-copy-each-other framing.
- [2020-06-23] **The ecosystem magic — AirPods auto-switching.** "'A great example of **why the ecosystem matters
  to Apple**: with iOS 14, **AirPods auto-switch audio** as you move from iPhone to iPad to Mac. **That's the kind
  of stuff you look forward to.**'" His walled-garden-as-a-benefit note.
- [2020-06-23] **macOS Big Sur (macOS 11) — mixed feelings.** "'The biggest change since macOS 10 began — mostly a
  **design overhaul** (more rounded corners, transparency; a notification center clearly modeled on iPad — "feels
  like it'd work better with touch than a mouse"). **I have mixed feelings — I don't like a lot of the new
  icons** (the shading), though the size uniformity is good.'"
- [2020-06-23] **The production praise + the Federighi tease.** "'As a video nerd: the **online keynote production
  was A+** — pre-recorded, dynamic locations, tasteful drone transitions, tight pacing. **Craig Federighi is coming
  on the Waveform podcast this week.**'" A craft appreciation + a biography note.

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "I'm imagining in a couple years a Mac Pro just being so sick, having all the thermal headroom in the world — or
> even just a MacBook Pro that doesn't thermally throttle, because they're so much better at that architecture."
> "Home screen customization, an app drawer, widgets on the home screen — just stop me if you've seen this before."
