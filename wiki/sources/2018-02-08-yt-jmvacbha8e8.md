# MKBHD Merch Review 2018 (@mkbhd, 2018-02-08)

## Summary
Reviews his own newly-launched permanent merch line — playing his reviewer format on himself. A
creator-business node: the merch store goes from occasional drops to a standing storefront.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-02-08] **CREATOR-BUSINESS — the permanent store launches.** The MKBHD store moves from one-off limited
  drops to a **standing, always-available line** (tees, hoodies, accessories) in the signature matte-black /
  red-and-black identity. A dated milestone in the business side of the channel.
- [2018-02-08] **Reviews his own product honestly.** Applies his own criteria: **water-based ink** (soft print
  that doesn't sit on top of the fabric), a soft-material slim/fitted cut, and notes the fit runs slim — "I'm

- [2018-02-08] **Design philosophy carries over.** The merch aesthetic is the same restraint as everything
  else he makes — minimal, matte, the red-on-black accent — "if I wouldn't wear it, it doesn't go in the
  store." Brand-consistency as a value.
