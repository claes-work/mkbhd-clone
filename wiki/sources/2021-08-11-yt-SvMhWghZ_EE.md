# Samsung Galaxy Z Flip 3 Impressions: Design Refresh! (@mkbhd, 2021-08-11)

## Summary
Impressions of the $999 Galaxy Z Flip 3 — a modest update, but **"the folding phone I'm most likely to start
recommending to regular people."** Samsung answered what people asked for: a **bigger, more functional cover
screen** (notifications manageable from the outside), a **120 Hz inside screen**, and a **lower ($999, lowest-ever)
price** — plus IPX8 water resistance. The durable idea: it's **the trajectory of getting foldables down to a
regular-phone price** — the flip, not the fold, is the one to recommend.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-08-11] **The most recommendable foldable — they made the right calls.** "'Of all the folding phones, **the
  Flip 3 is the one I'm most likely to recommend to regular people.** People asked for a bigger cover screen, a
  high-refresh main screen, and a lower price — **check, check, and kind of check.**'"
- [2021-08-11] **The lowest-ever price ($999) — and how they turned the narrative.** "'It starts at **$999 — the
  lowest price of any of their folding phones — but the base now has 128 GB (previous flips started at 256 GB), so
  they turned the narrative a bit.** Still, **$999 is clutch: it's now in line with regular flagships people are
  already considering — it just happens to fold in half.**'" A key "getting foldables to normal money" milestone.
- [2021-08-11] **The bigger cover screen — manageable notifications from the outside.** "'The cover screen grows
  from **1.1" to 1.9"** (not the Razr's 2.7", but a big jump) — now the widgets/quick actions are a usable size,
  and most importantly **you can read and swipe-to-dismiss individual notifications right from the outside screen.**
  A big usability improvement; plus customizable clocks/widgets.'"
- [2021-08-11] **120 Hz inside + IPX8 + better speakers.** "'The inside folding display is now **120 Hz** ("the
  iPhone will be the last flagship to go high-refresh"), the hinge is improved to **IPX8 water resistant** (not
  dust-resistant — the hinge grit nemesis remains), and it finally gets **Dolby Atmos stereo speakers.** A dual-
  tone design — "if you told younger me this was a folding Pixel, I'd have believed you."'"
- [2021-08-11] **THE VERDICT — the crown for the most reasonable foldable + the trajectory.** "'It doesn't go as
  high-end as the Fold 3 (no S Pen, no under-display camera — still a hole-punch). But as **the most reasonable
  folding phone yet — flagship specs, cameras, water resistance, nicely designed at $999 — this one takes the
  crown.** Even if it's not THE one people start buying, **the trajectory is good: getting the price down and
  making it as close to a regular phone as possible.**'"
