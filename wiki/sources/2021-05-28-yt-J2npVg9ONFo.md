# Ford F150 Lightning Impressions: Better Than I Thought! (@mkbhd, 2021-05-28)

## Summary
An early hands-on with the electric Ford F-150 Lightning — **"the most important EV of the year,"** because the
F-150 is the best-selling *vehicle* (not just truck) in the US for 40 years straight. The durable ideas: **Ford
took it seriously** ("make it better at everything so switching is a no-brainer"); a promotable **under-promise-
over-deliver range strategy** (the 300-mile EPA rating is WITH 1,000 lb of cargo — empty it quoted ~460 mi); the
massive **frunk** and **bi-directional power** (truck + generator + Powerwall in one); and the **instant-torque**
advantage of EVs. "Not a truck person, but they thought of pretty much everything."

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-05-28] **The most important EV of the year — the best-selling VEHICLE.** "'More important than the
  CyberTruck, Rivian, or the Plaid — **the F-150 is the best-selling vehicle in the US for 40 years straight (not
  the best-selling truck — the best-selling vehicle).** So an electric version is a big deal, especially if Ford
  takes it seriously.'"
- [2021-05-28] **Ford took it seriously — "make it better at everything."** "'It looks like any F-150 (great —
  tried and true) with nice light-bar details, and **the electric benefits are known: they understand EVs are a
  big deal and the electric F-150 has to be straight-up better than the gas one and do things it can't. Mission
  accomplished.**' Competitive pricing (~$40k base / ~$50k extended; the most affordable electric truck yet).'"
- [2021-05-28] **THE RANGE REVELATION — under-promise, over-deliver.** "'The 300-mile rating sounded just okay —
  **but I learned it's the EPA rating WITH 1,000 lb of cargo. Empty, the truck quoted me 367 mi at ~80% —
  extrapolating to ~460 mi at 100%.** Ford's rating people with cargo so they can **under-promise and over-deliver
  — give you a safe 300 miles no matter the cold, towing, or worksite power draw**, to make switching from gas a
  no-brainer.' (A promotable EV-range-communication insight.)'"
- [2021-05-28] **The two killer new features — the frunk and bi-directional power.** "'No engine = **the biggest
  front trunk I've ever seen** (weather-sealed, lit, a drain hole so you can fill it with ice, a golf-bag diagram
  — I tested my clubs). And **bi-directional power: an inverter (included on the long-range) can power your entire
  home for several days in an outage — a pickup + a portable generator + a Tesla Powerwall in one vehicle**
  (240 V + 120 V outlets in the bed run power tools; no separate generator).'"
- [2021-05-28] **The instant-torque explainer — the inherent EV advantage.** "'It's **the fastest truck they've
  made (0–60 in 4.5 s at 6,000 lb, faster than a Raptor) — a byproduct of electrical power.** The real story is
  **instant torque: a gas engine's power band is an arc (needs a 10-speed to stay near peak); an electric motor's
  is basically flat — max torque from 0 rpm across a wide band.** So it's smoother, faster, and towing should be
  more efficient.'" (A reusable teaching moment.)'"
- [2021-05-28] **The verdict — "better than I thought; they thought of everything."** "'I'm not a truck person,
  but **it seems complete from every angle — a more powerful, responsive, functional F-150 that doubles as a
  portable generator, with new covered-locked storage, that just happens to be electric.** ~70,000 reservations.
  CyberTruck or F-150 Lightning? Two different buyers.'"

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "The most popular, best-selling vehicle in the U.S. for the last 40 years straight is the Ford F-150 — I didn't
> say the most popular truck, I said the most popular vehicle."

> "Now you've got a pickup truck, a portable generator, and basically a Tesla Powerwall all in one vehicle."
