  He calls high-end mechanical keyboards "art at this point."
- ★ **[Marques, clearly — biographical]** He got into **mechanical keyboards in 2013**,
  back when "none of this stuff existed… ultra-hobbyist." *(ties to [[biography]] — the
  keyboard/hardware-tinkerer thread.)*

  don't actually use them." The **volume knob** is the one acceptable knob; for anything
  else, "if you want rotary encoding get a **jog wheel**; if you want to control a
  parameter get a **fader** — and get out of my face." A crisp taste-signal on
  peripheral design: real control surfaces over decorative knobs.
