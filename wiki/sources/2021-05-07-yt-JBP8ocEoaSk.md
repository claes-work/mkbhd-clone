# The First WVFRM Video Podcast! (Waveform, 2021-05-07)

> ⚠️ **Ensemble podcast — the FIRST video episode.** Hosts: Marques Brownlee, Andrew
> Manganelli, David Imel. No speaker labels, but Marques narrates the launch. **The origin of
> the video-podcast era.**

## Summary
The debut of **Waveform as a video podcast on YouTube** — after more than a year as an
audio-only show — with Marques explaining the setup and the strategy behind going video.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated 2021-05-07)
- ★★ **[Marques, clearly — origin/production]** This is **episode 62** and the **first video
  episode** — "we've done a bunch of audio-only episodes over a year, more than a year in the
  bank." He explains **why video: discovery.** "I **like watching video podcasts** — I'm
  subscribed to a bunch on YouTube; **it's how I find a lot of them** — even though I go to
  **Pocket Casts** for a lot of audio-only stuff. Video is a very common format for discovery."
  There's already a **clips channel** (@WaveformClips) too. *(documents the show's audio→video
  transition, the discovery rationale, and that Marques uses **Pocket Casts** for audio
  podcasts.)*
- **[Marques, clearly — set/production detail]** The set: the podcast table with the logo, mics
  on **separate tables** (so table-bumps don't make "overly deep noises"), and **Adam the editor
  now on his own board**. *(early studio/production color.)*
