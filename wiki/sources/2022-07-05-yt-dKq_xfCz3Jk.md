# ROG Phone 6 Pro Review: Daily Driver Material! (@mkbhd, 2022-07-05)

## Summary
A review of the Asus ROG Phone 6 Pro — a gaming phone that's now also **"daily driver material."** The durable
frame: modern gaming phones have **two goals now — be great for gaming AND be great to live with daily** — like a
**Porsche 911** (high performance but daily-drivable, vs the old harsh manual sports car). It's the **fastest phone
he's ever used** (not on paper but in pure responsiveness — a 165 Hz display with a 720 Hz touch-sampling rate;
23 ms touch latency). The 18 GB RAM is "for the headline" but genuinely helps multitasking. The weaknesses are the
usual: the **camera layout** (a macro-instead-of-ultrawide third camera buried in settings, no telephoto but a "2x"
crop button), no wireless charging, and the **sheer size.** His closing point: unlike a fun car you drive alongside
a daily, **you typically have just one phone — so it has to do double duty**, which is why softening a gamer phone
into a well-rounded daily is a good thing.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-07-05] **Two goals now — the Porsche 911 analogy.** "'Gaming phones used to have one goal (be great for
  gaming); the latest have **two — be incredible at gaming AND great to live with daily.** Like sports cars that
  used to be harsh manual track toys — **the ROG Phone is like a Porsche 911: high-performance but also
  daily-drivable** (at least it wants to be).'"
- [2022-07-05] **The fastest phone he's used.** "'As crazy as it sounds, **this is the fastest phone I've ever
  used — not on paper, but in pure responsiveness** (every input immediately answered). The **165 Hz display with a
  720 Hz touch-sampling rate (double last year) brings touch latency down to 23 ms** — everything follows your
  finger super tight. It benchmarks slightly ahead of the 8 Gen 1 (still behind the iPhone 13), but 9/10 it feels
  faster than anything else.'"
- [2022-07-05] **18 GB RAM "for the headline" — but it helps.** "'Do you need 18 GB of RAM for one game? No — that's
  for the headline. **But lots of RAM helps multitasking — I flip between five or six open apps easily and it keeps
  them all open.** Best display yet on a gaming phone (800 nits, HDR10+), better haptics (a new motor), a faster
  fingerprint reader — **Asus was clearly listening to the nitpicks from last year.**'"
- [2022-07-05] **The camera-layout gripe.** "'The cameras are a weakness — passable (B− to B+ in good light, a Sony
  IMX766 that's in sub-$600 phones). But I hate the **layout: a primary, a softer ultrawide, and then a macro
  instead of a proper telephoto** (and it's a bad macro, buried deep in settings) — while there's a prominent "2x"
  button that's just a crop on the main camera. **Just put a telephoto there instead.** No wireless charging (the
  ROG Vision back display took the room); IPX4 only.'"
- [2022-07-05] **A phone has to do double duty.** "'There'll always be people who want the exotic McLaren /
  stiff-suspension Lambo — fine. **But the difference is: with cars, those people have a daily AND a fun car; with
  a phone you typically just have one, so it needs to do double duty.** So softening a gamer phone into a more
  well-rounded daily is a good thing — props, Asus.'"
