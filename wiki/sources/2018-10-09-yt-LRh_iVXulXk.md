# Google Pixel 3 Impressions! (@mkbhd, 2018-10-09)

## Summary
An exclusive first-look at the Pixel 3 / 3 XL (Google flew him to Mountain View under embargo). Covers the design
(all-glass, matte-black-everything), the divisive tall XL notch, the software-driven camera (why one lens still
wins), and a couple of durable observations about small phones and non-handicapped variants.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-10-09] **Matte-black-everything appreciation.** The all-glass back has a **frosted soft-touch lower half**;
  the colors are "just black, clearly white, and not pink" (real names) with colored power buttons (mint/orange).
  "They even **painted the inside of the USB-C port black** — completing the truly matte-black-everything Pixel 3.
  Well done, Google." His aesthetic reflex.
- [2018-10-09] **The XL notch — "tall as hell" (and a chin).** "The 3 XL notch is **taller than any other notch
  we've seen, and there's still a chin.** Up there: two front cameras + a big stereo speaker — no Face ID/IR. Is it
  worth it? Hard to say — I'll test it." The smaller **Pixel 3 has no notch** — "a sneaky candidate for **small
  phone of the year. Small phones are getting big** — that's the state we're in." A quotable read on the market.
- [2018-10-09] **The camera — one lens, because software.** "The camera we love so deeply is upgraded again. **Pixel
  Visual Core** is why the Pixel has been the **undisputed camera champ** — and why they use **one lens instead of
  two or three** (software does the rest)." The features are software: **Top Shot, Night Sight, Super Res Zoom**
  (uses your hand-shake to compute a higher-res digital zoom — "so Google"), **Google Lens in the viewfinder**,
  **Shush mode**, and **Call Screening via Duplex.** Reinforces his software-first camera thesis.
- [2018-10-09] **The non-handicapped-small-variant observation.** "I'm just happy that this year, when phones have
  a small and a big version, **they're not handicapping the smaller one** — no less RAM, no removed second camera.
  The Pixel 3 and 3 XL have the same features except display and battery, same as the XS/XS Max." A pro-consumer
  trend he calls out.
- [2018-10-09] **The 4 GB RAM flag (ties to the Pixel 2 slowdown).** "SD845 and **4 GB — actually less RAM than I
  expected; we'll keep an eye on that.**" A pointed watch-item given his documented Pixel 2 performance decline.
- [2018-10-09] **The extras — fast charger + real accessories.** The box includes an **18 W fast charger** and
  USB-C earbuds (unlike Apple this year); the **Pixel Stand** (wireless charging + a docked Assistant/Google-Home
  mode); the Home Hub; and the Pixel Slate ("a laggy prototype — reserving judgment").
