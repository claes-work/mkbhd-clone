# The Cheapest 600HP EV... (Chevy Blazer EV SS)

## Summary

Marques spends a few days commuting in the **Chevy Blazer EV SS** and reframes the SS badge as
**"Sensible Sport."** His headline: it's **"basically the cheapest way to get 600 horsepower, new
EV, with 300 miles of range"** (~$62k), notable because so many performance EVs *nuke* their range
to hit their power figures (he cites the EV6 GT's ~200mi). Big power that stays on all drive
(no time-limited boost), Google-built-in software good enough that he doesn't bother plugging in
CarPlay, and genuine practicality. Fully Marques-narrated.

## Key claims

- **[2025-04-22]** Headline value: **cheapest 600hp new EV with 300mi range**, ~$62k start (~$64k as
  tested); 615hp / 650 lb-ft, dual-motor AWD, 102kWh battery, 190kW peak charge. "We've seen so many
  performance EVs nuke their range to achieve better performance figures — remember the EV6 GT with
  200 miles?" (quote)
- **[2025-04-22]** SS extras over the base Blazer EV: six-piston front brakes, a bespoke independent
  suspension, more power — "and still 300 miles of range." (paraphrase)
- **[2025-04-22]** Cross-reference: performance numbers are **"basically a Lyriq V in a straight line"**
  (same GM drivetrain; the Cadillac costs a lot more and is more luxurious, but the straight-line power
  is identical). (quote)
- **[2025-04-22]** **Wide Open Watts** mode unlocks the full 615hp and, unlike some rivals' 10-second
  boost, **"stays on for the entire duration of your drive"** (it just eats range faster). (quote)
- **[2025-04-22]** Software praise: **Google built-in** (Play Store, Waze, Spotify, watch videos while
  parked) — "I actually don't feel the need to attach Android Auto or Apple CarPlay… I have Waze built
  in." (quote)
- **[2025-04-22]** Interior: not as premium as the Lyriq (more cloth/plastic) "but I actually don't
  mind it — plenty of physical controls, spacious, comfortable"; the SS colorway (gray/orange) is fun;
  heated rear seats; he fits behind his own 6'3" driving position. (paraphrase)
- **[2025-04-22]** ⬥ Production note: shot on the **Oppo Find X8 Ultra** ("the Oppo is capturing
  [habanero red] pretty well"). (paraphrase)
- **[2025-04-22]** Verdict: **"I would challenge you to find another brand-new car, especially an
  electric one, with 600 horsepower and 300 miles of range for $62,000. I don't think there is one…
  can't really fault it. Chevy, well done on this EV."** (quote)
