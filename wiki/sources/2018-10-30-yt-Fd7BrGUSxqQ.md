# 2019 iPad Pro Impressions: Incredibly Thin! (@mkbhd, 2018-10-30)

## Summary
First impressions from Apple's NYC event — the redesigned iPad Pro (plus the new MacBook Air and Mac mini). The
headlines: a striking flat/boxy 5.9 mm redesign, USB-C on an iPad (a genuine surprise), the magnetic Apple Pencil,
and the "systematic" removal of the headphone jack.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-10-30] **The redesign — flat, boxy, "incredibly thin."** "A complete redesign — thin bezels on all four
  sides, **5.9 mm thick**, super boxy/sharp like a MacBook Pro or the iPhone 5s (stands up on its own). It feels
  like you're **holding a display.** So thin it now has a **camera bump** — which makes me question keeping the
  camera at all, but people scan documents." No home button, **Face ID that works in any orientation.**
- [2018-10-30] **USB-C — "I did not see this coming."** "The shocker: **USB-C on an iPad Pro.** Plug in USB-C
  accessories, charge by USB-C, even **charge an iPhone from the iPad.** Clearly Apple sees the port's benefits in
  their pro tablet — **way earlier than I thought.** My hope for a **USB-C iPhone just went from 0% to 1%.**" A
  hedged-but-notable optimism (revisited in the full review).
- [2018-10-30] **The new Apple Pencil — the charging fix.** "The old Lightning-charging Pencil was awful; the new
  one **snaps onto the side with magnets, pairs, and wirelessly charges there — way better** — plus a touch-
  sensitive area for double-tap (switch tools/eraser), which third-party apps can support." A clear upgrade.
- [2018-10-30] **A12X + 120 Hz + storage to 1 TB.** "The 8-core A12X pushes all those pixels; the already-fast iPad
  Pro is faster; 120 Hz stays; storage now 64 GB → **1 TB.**" Spec headline.
- [2018-10-30] **The jack removal — "not about space anymore."** "**No headphone jack — just USB-C.** With a tablet
  there's obviously space; **with Apple it's not about space anymore — it's about systematically removing the jack
  across the lineup where we don't seem to need it** (they'll keep it longer in laptops/desktops). A bummer for DJs
  and editors who relied on it — but not a dealbreaker for me; I'm already wireless." Names the pattern.
- [2018-10-30] **The verdict — "the best tablet on the planet, the gap got wider."** "The iPad is one of only ~three
  tablets worth buying; when a rival appears (the Pixel Slate), I stack it against the iPad — and **this redesign
  just made the gap even wider. I'm picking one up.**" ($799 → $1,899 specced up.)
