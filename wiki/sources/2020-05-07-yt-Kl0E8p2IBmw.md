# The 2020 13" MacBook Pro Impressions: Wait a Minute! (@mkbhd, 2020-05-07)

## Summary
Impressions of the 2020 13" MacBook Pro — "episode 5 of very very minor updates by Apple." The long-awaited
butterfly-keyboard fix arrives on his go-to laptop, but a two-config split and an awkward middle-child price make
it complicated. Frames a pro-**frequent-minor-updates** stance.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-05-07] **BIOGRAPHY — his go-to laptop finally fixed.** "'The **13" MacBook Pro has been my go-to for years**
  (the Air's too lightweight for photo editing; the 16" is bigger than I want to carry). I told people **"don't buy
  one for five months"** knowing the butterfly fix was coming — the whole 16" review was titled "now do it to the
  smaller one." **Goodbye butterfly, welcome back the scissor keyboard.**'" A datable personal-gear anchor.
- [2020-05-07] **"Wait a minute" — the two-config catch.** "'There are **two versions**: the new specs (10th-gen,
  faster RAM, doubled base storage) **only apply to the high-end configs**; the **baseline is essentially last
  year's 13" with just the new keyboard** (8th-gen, 8 GB, two ports). Per Dave2D, Apple used 8th-gen on the
  baseline for **cost** (small perf difference, $100+ cheaper).'"
- [2020-05-07] **The awkward-middle-child problem.** "'The high-end is **$3,000 — at which point the 16" MacBook Pro
  (better thermals, a dedicated GPU, an 8-core i9) is right there for ~the same price.** And the baseline shopper
  can save more with the Air. It's an **awkward middle child** again.'" His recurring middle-child framing (cf.
  the OnePlus 8).
- [2020-05-07] **THE ADVOCACY — frequent minor updates are good.** "'**Small updates are a good thing — I'm with
  Rene Ritchie: frequent minor spec-bumps that keep things up to date are good.** I'd love an iMac and a Mac mini
  update. **I'd rather not have to tell people "don't buy this for five months"** because Apple's updates are so
  infrequent that the ones we get become a bigger deal.'" A promotable pro-cadence stance.
- [2020-05-07] **The unchanged annoyances + the 14" question.** "'Still the **garbage 720p webcam** ("the company
  that makes the best smartphone cameras still makes this"), still thick-ish bezels. **A lot of us expected a 14"**
  (like the 15"→16" bump) — I want to hope, but I'm not sure. WWDC's coming; rumors point to a 14" or an ARM Mac.'"

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "Small updates are still a good thing. We need small updates... frequent minor updates and spec bumps to just
> keep things running up to date are a good thing."
> "For the past five months I've been telling people: do not buy a 13-inch MacBook Pro."
