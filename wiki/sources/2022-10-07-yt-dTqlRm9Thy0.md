# 2023 Cadillac Lyriq Might be Underrated!

## Summary

**The origin of the "Lyriq underrated" callback** that recurs across the corpus. Marques's hot take:
the **Cadillac Lyriq** "does pretty much everything a Tesla Model Y does, but in more comfort — and
it's just a better car," at the same price. It's Cadillac's first real consumer EV, and he praises the
luxury/materials (massaging seats, AKG headrest speakers), the Android Automotive software ("the best
non-Tesla EV software I've used"), and 312 mi range. And it explicitly foreshadows the **electric
Escalade** doctrine. Fully Marques-narrated.

## Key claims

- **[2022-10-07]** ⭐ THE HOT TAKE: **"An underrated EV is rare — there's so much hype on every new EV.
  But I think I found one: the Cadillac Lyriq does pretty much everything a Tesla Model Y does, but in
  more comfort — and it's just a better car. Same price (~$60–65k)."** Cadillac's first true consumer-
  facing EV. (quote)
- **[2022-10-07]** Luxury wins: leather everywhere, **massaging front seats, cooled/heated seats with
  fans + speakers in the headrests (AKG surround)**, a very quiet double-paned cabin. **"In luxury and
  comfort there's no argument — head and shoulders above the Ioniq and Model Y. Cadillac has build
  quality down — shout out to GM."** (quote)
- **[2022-10-07]** ⭐ Software praise: **"This is the best non-Tesla EV software I've ever used — Android
  Automotive: quick, responsive, intuitive; the Play Store; a good version of Google Maps in a car that
  knows your charge state and routes you through chargers."** 312 mi real range; the strongest one-pedal
  regen he'd felt + a GM regen paddle. (quote)
- **[2022-10-07]** ⚠️ Nitpicks: the flashy glowing grille ("understated luxury is being able to fly
  under the radar"); no front trunk; while-driving software lockouts + "trash" voice control ("I tried
  to enter my [frisbee] practice field and it just didn't hear me"); ⚠️ **not sporty** ("it's a
  Cadillac — I happen to be into sportiness, so this feels tame even in Sport mode"). (quote)
- **[2022-10-07]** ⭐ THE FORESHADOW (doctrine origin): **"What this leaves me thinking is Cadillac should
  make an electric Escalade — wouldn't that make a ton of sense? You want something quiet, luxurious;
  Escalades are already huge, high off the ground, not efficient, and expensive — so 300 miles on a big
  battery, quieter and more performant than any Escalade out now. That would make a lot of sense."** (quote)
