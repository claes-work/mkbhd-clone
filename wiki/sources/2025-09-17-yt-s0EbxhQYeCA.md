# iPhone 17/Pro/Air Unboxing: 1 Hot Take!

## Summary

The annual pre-review unboxing / benchmarks / FAQ for the iPhone 17 lineup, recorded a few
days into testing. Two throughlines: a "hot take" that the **base iPhone 17 is one of the
best deals in all of smartphones**, and open **skepticism about Apple's battery-life
numbers** — especially for the thin iPhone Air, whose battery he flags as "a bit of a red
flag." He compares the Air head-to-head with Samsung's S25 Edge as the two "pioneers of
thinness."

## Key claims

- **[2025-09-17]** **Hot take: the base iPhone 17 is "one of the best deals in all of
  smartphones"** and "the one most people should get" — vs the equivalent Pixel it has one
  fewer camera but an arguably better main + ultrawide, a much more powerful chip, the new
  selfie camera, 256GB base, 120Hz, and is **$100 cheaper**. (He first floated this on the
  Waveform podcast.) (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-09-17]** The **orange Pro** color is the most controversial — he likens it to "a
  bright shirt... looks good in the moment, but I might not want to wear it every day"; most
  people want black/neutral (like car colors), and **Apple isn't offering black on the Pro**.
  He'll likely go **blue** (closest to black) because he films screens/reflections and needs
  a dark phone. (quote)
- **[2025-09-17]** The **iPhone Air** (note: "iPhone Air," not "iPhone 17 Air" — hinting Apple
  may not refresh it yearly) is "ridiculously thin" — his **third-thinnest phone ever** (after
  the Moto Z and one ~4mm phone). It feels "almost like a piece of jewelry" with the Liquid
  Glass UI and glossy titanium rails. (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-09-17]** **Battery skepticism**: "I don't really know if I can believe anything
  Apple says about battery life." Apple rates the Air for **27h** video vs the 16's 22h, but
  that just reflects the efficient Pro chip on video playback; real-world use is more varied.
  The Air's battery is **3,149mAh — about the size of the iPhone 13 Pro's** — and in his early
  use it's trended to ~**4.5h screen-on time**, "which used to be good, but these days that's
  like C-minus level." (quote)
- **[2025-09-17]** Battery **capacities** across the line grew: the **Pro Max cracks 5,000mAh
  for the first time**; the smaller Pro sees a ~**20% year-over-year** jump; Pros now have
  **12GB RAM** (up from 8). The A19 / A19 Pro benchmarks step up nicely in multi-core CPU/GPU.
  (paraphrase)
- **[2025-09-17]** **Air vs S25 Edge** (the two thin pioneers): both use ~flagship chips and
  both drop the vapor chamber. But the **S25 Edge has a full flagship main camera + an
  ultrawide + stereo speakers + a 3,900mAh battery**; the **iPhone Air has one camera, no
  ultrawide, no bottom speaker at all (just mics), and a smaller battery** — but is thinner.
  "I personally lean S25 Edge just a bit" (better camera system, boxier design). (paraphrase +
  quote)
- **[2025-09-17]** The Air is "the most interesting iPhone of this lineup" and will pull
  people into stores, "but I actually think that's a distraction, because the base iPhone this
  year is more clearly than ever the one that most people should get." (quote)
