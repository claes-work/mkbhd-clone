# Apple Watch Series 6: Everything New! (Product RED) (@mkbhd, 2020-09-17)

## Summary
First look at the Apple Watch Series 6 (+ a touch on the $279 Watch SE). A minor refresh — new colors, S6 chip,
a blood-oxygen sensor. Two durable observations: **the missing wall adapter in the box is "writing on the wall"
for a no-charger iPhone 12** (framed as environmental cover for a cost/e-waste move), and the **SE is the value
play** — a repackaged older watch at a great price.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-09-17] **NO WALL ADAPTER — "writing on the wall" for the iPhone 12.** "'The box now ships **the cable
  but no USB wall adapter** — sold separately — and Apple tucked that into the **environmental section** of the
  keynote. Like I predicted, **this is basically writing on the wall for the next iPhone probably also shipping
  without a brick — "to save money, I mean to save the environment from e-waste."**'" A pointed read on
  environmental framing over a cost move.
- [2020-09-17] **The S6 chip — "Apple's silicon team flexing on us again."** "'The real improvement is the
  **S6 chip (dual-core, based on the A13)** — the **silicon team continuing to make strides, flexing on us
  again** with efficiency. It lets them **double the always-on brightness and keep the day-and-a-half battery**,
  plus tightened, shorter, snappier animations.'"
- [2020-09-17] **Blood oxygen — "cool that it works," but not a reason to buy.** "'The new blood-oxygen sensor
  uses infrared to read your blood's oxygen % — **not medical grade, just there to inform you generally.** I get
  normal readings, but **it probably shouldn't be your reason to buy this watch.**'" (Contradiction-adjacent: the
  full review sharpens this into "it's bait" — see [[2020-10-02-yt-XDamqi_3Aro]].)
- [2020-09-17] **The SE is the value play — "the new old new Apple Watch."** "'The **$279 Watch SE** is basically
  a Series 5 (no blood oxygen, no ECG, no always-on) — **a lot of smartwatch for $279,** one of the more popular
  upgrades/gifts this holiday. The Series 6 price stays the same.'"
- [2020-09-17] **The nebulous big-picture wishlist.** "'I'm still thinking about the bigger picture: **when do
  they do a different shape (a round version)? Qi wireless charging? A true third-party watch-face store?** None
  of that dramatic stuff is happening in Series 6 — it's still the same familiar customization.'" His recurring
  "where's the bold move" lens on mature Apple categories.

## Notable verbatim quotes
