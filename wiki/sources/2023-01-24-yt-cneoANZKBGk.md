# M2 Pro Mac Mini Review: Game Changer! (@mkbhd, 2023-01-24)

## Summary
The counterpoint to the MacBook Pro review he'd just posted ("I said the Mac mini is actually the more
interesting machine — and I mean it"). The refreshed Mac mini comes in **base M2 ($599, price *dropped* from
$699 — "Apple almost never lowers the price of the entry level of anything") and M2 Pro ($1,299)**. The
headline: **this tiny machine has more CPU power than a base Mac Pro right now** — "absolutely insane." The
real reason he calls it a game changer is **performance per dollar** — the M2 Pro Mac mini uses the same SoC
as the $1,999 M2 Pro MacBook Pro for $1,299, plus it gains two Thunderbolt ports (four total, three external
displays), 10 GbE option, and HDMI 2.1; its taller active-cooling chassis may even sustain CPU performance
better than a laptop. A recurring theme: **stop overlooking the desktop** — with a monitor-less desktop you
**pick whatever display you want with the $700 you save**, and the true "sustainability" win is **not being
locked to a non-upgradable iMac display/webcam forever.** Personal callback: his college **"Fastest Mac Mini
in the World"** project cost $1,759 in 2012; this one blows it away for less. Buying advice: **if you have
any M1 Mac, you're good — you don't have to buy a new Mac every year;** upgrading from something older,
**default to the base $599 M2 Mac mini** and **don't buy the iMac right now** (still M1, non-removable
screen) — get the mini plus any $600 screen.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2023-01-24] **More power than a base Mac Pro — and the price went *down*.** "'This little machine has more
  CPU power than a base Mac Pro right now, which is insane. The base M2 mini is a game changer: all the
  new Apple Silicon benefits for $599 — down from $699. Apple almost never lowers the entry price of
  anything, but we just got a more powerful, more capable, more efficient computer for less money.'"
- [2023-01-24] **The real reason it's a game changer: performance per dollar.** "'The M2 Pro mini uses the
  exact same system-on-a-chip as the M2 Pro MacBook Pro that starts at $1,999 — for $1,299. That's the real
  reason I'm calling these a game changer. You don't usually expect performance-per-dollar out of a Mac.'"
- [2023-01-24] **Stop overlooking the desktop; the iMac lock-in.** "'For so long Apple prioritized the
  laptops — makes sense, they're great and sell. But some people keep the laptop in one room because the
  desktop options weren't appealing. With a monitor-less desktop you pick whatever display you want with
  the $700 you save. Call me crazy, but a lot of people don't want to be stuck with a non-upgradable
  display, webcam, and white bezels forever.'"
- [2023-01-24] **The buying advice — default to the mini, skip the iMac.** "'If you already have an M1-
  generation Mac, you're good — you don't have to buy a new Mac every year. If you're upgrading from
  something older, I'd default to the base $599 M2 Mac mini. I would not buy the iMac right now — it's still
  M1 and $1,299 for a non-removable 24" display; get the mini and whatever $600 screen you want. Only go
  laptop if you specifically need to compute on the go.'"
- [2023-01-24] **The college callback.** "'I haven't done a Mac mini video since the "Fastest Mac Mini in
  the World" project in college — I maxed out a 2012 mini for $1,759 for a dorm room where space was at a
  premium (my priorities were video editing over homework). This M2 Pro mini has all the power I'd have
  killed for then, and it costs less.'"
