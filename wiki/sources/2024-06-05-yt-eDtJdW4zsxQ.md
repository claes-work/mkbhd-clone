# BMW i5 is Cool, but Compromised

## Summary

Marques reviews the **BMW i5 M60** (~$100k) and uses it to state clearly one of his core EV
frameworks: **ground-up EVs vs. gas cars converted to electric.** The i5 is "a 5 Series that got
turned electric" — well-equipped, genuinely fun to drive (the torquiest low-end he's felt in an EV),
but it pays the conversion tax: no frunk, a wasteful center transmission hump, weaker range
(~230–240 mi). His verdict: **"good enough"** — but he gives more credit to ground-up designs.
Fully Marques-narrated.

## Key claims

- **[2024-06-05]** ⭐ The framework, stated outright: **"There are two types of electric cars — the
  ground-up EVs made on a new platform, designed to be electric from the moment they're conceived, and
  gas cars that a manufacturer turns into an electric version. The second camp doesn't have as many
  benefits."** The i5 (and i7) are the second camp; the i7 "got away with it more because it's so big,"
  but the i5 is where "you start to feel a little less of those benefits." (quote)
- **[2024-06-05]** "Seeing a lot of them on the road for $100k is a pretty good sign it's a good car"
  (his recurring on-the-road-frequency heuristic). ~600 hp, 3.3s 0–60, but **~230–240 mi range** (a
  smaller battery than the i7). (paraphrase)
- **[2024-06-05]** The conversion tax: **no front trunk** ("a lot of room wasted"), a **huge center
  hump in the back seat** ("in a gas car that's the transmission tunnel — in an EV, what is this here
  for?"), and less center storage than a ground-up EV should have. "Comfortable but not spacious." (quote)
- **[2024-06-05]** The upside of the smaller car: **it's the sportier, more fun one to drive** — "the
  most torquey low-end I've ever felt in an EV… it legitimately hits you in the back of the head," air
  suspension that "handles New Jersey's potholes really well." But it'd "get smoked in a straight line
  by a Model S Plaid or a Lucid Air GT in the same price range." (quote)
- **[2024-06-05]** ⭐ Verdict: **"This car is in the 'good enough' section for me. It's not going to make
  headlines for being amazing… it's just a comfortable, well-equipped, basic i5, and I have no issues
  with it really — other than the fact that it could be built ground-up differently. I give a lot more
  credit to the ground-up ones."** (quote)
