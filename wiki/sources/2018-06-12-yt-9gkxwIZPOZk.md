# HTC U12+ Review: A Phone With No Buttons! (@mkbhd, 2018-06-12)

## Summary
Reviews the $849 HTC U12+ — one of the few no-notch 2018 flagships, whose headline is that it has **no real
buttons** (pressure-sensitive fake buttons). Verdict: "almost there," undone mainly by those buttons and weak
battery. Best material: his defense of good clicky buttons.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-06-12] **"No real buttons" — and why that's the problem.** The power/volume buttons are pressure-sensitive
  pads with a vibration motor simulating a click. "The best version is **Apple's haptic engine** (the iPhone 8
  home button fully convinces you). This is a **decidedly average vibration motor** — there's a slight delay that
  makes it feel **software-driven**, and **double-pressing barely works.** Not a good experience." A precise haptics
  critique.
- [2018-06-12] **The principle — "I'll keep praising good clicky buttons until haptics get way better."** A clear,
  quotable taste position (pairs with his OnePlus/LG "underrated clicky buttons" notes).
- [2018-06-12] **What it gets right.** Loves the **translucent blue** back ("I'd never put a case on it"); the
  "leveled-up" **Edge Sense** squeeze (quick vs long, plus double-tap-side for one-handed mode); no notch, thin
  bezels, IP68, back-center fingerprint ("right where it should be — excellent use of space"), micro-SD. "It has
  a lot of what people asked for."
- [2018-06-12] **"A sneaky top-5 camera."** 12 MP OIS main + 16 MP telephoto — "not the best, but a sneaky top-5:
  **Pixel 2 and iPhone X are the clear top dogs**, then this fits in around the Galaxy S9, **better than the
  OnePlus 6.**" Biggest flaw: overexposes / weak dynamic range. Camera-ranking continuity.
- [2018-06-12] **The verdict — "almost there."** "Spec'd, built, and priced like a flagship, but it falls short on
  the buttons and the **subpar battery** (barely a day). If you're staunchly anti-notch, your choices are basically
  this and the Galaxy S9." A qualified thumbs-up.

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "I personally still prefer really good clicky buttons — and I'll keep praising them until haptic engines get way
> better."
> "Double-tapping the volume buttons doesn't raise the volume twice — you have to press really slowly. This is not
> fun at all."
> "I think this is a sneaky top-5 smartphone camera right now."
