# The New Prius is... SICK?! (Solar panel roof)

## Summary

Marques reviews the 2023 **Toyota Prius Prime** and — to his own surprise — really likes it. He uses
it to state **what he's into ("high-tech cars")** and to make the case that **hybrids are a decent
stopgap on the way to EVs** in 2023 (EVs are expensive, with range/infrastructure anxiety). The
standout is the **solar roof** that adds meaningful daily range — enough that with a short commute
you might "never pay for gas or electricity." Fully Marques-narrated; contains biography.

## Key claims (biography marked ⬥)

- **[2023-06-05]** ⬥ SELF-DESCRIPTION: **"The question I usually get now that we have this @AutoFocus
  channel is 'what type of cars are you into?' — and the answer I give is: high-tech cars. A lot of
  electric cars are on the forefront of that, but anything high-tech in a car is interesting."** (quote)
- **[2023-06-05]** ⭐ Hybrids-as-stopgap: "Not everybody is ready for an EV — they're expensive, still
  reliant on charging infrastructure. The stop-gap is a hybrid, and the Prius is the most familiar one.
  **I'm on board with hybrids in 2023 being a decent stop-gap on the way to more people getting EVs.**"
  The Prius Prime: a plug-in hybrid, 44 mi EV range, 400+ mi gas, ~55 mpg, 220 hp. (quote)
- **[2023-06-05]** ⬥ BIOGRAPHY: **"I took this for my daily commute, which for me is about 29 miles"** —
  drove it 29 mi on pure electric; then **"I left it in the sun and didn't drive it for the weekend
  because I had an Ultimate Frisbee road trip — we went to Boston and Montreal and came back"** — and it
  **gained meaningful battery just sitting in the sun.** (quote — corroborates the ~29-mi commute and the
  NY→Boston→Montreal frisbee road trip.)
- **[2023-06-05]** ⭐ The solar take: **"This is maybe the only shipping production car I've seen with a
  solar panel good enough that with a short enough commute you might never have to plug in or pay for
  gas."** But on EVs generally: **"When I see a solar panel on a car it feels gimmicky — 500 Wh/day
  against a 100 kWh battery is only a mile or two. On a *hybrid* it's cool because you always have the
  gas backup."** (quote)
- **[2023-06-05]** Neat details: a transparent-car top-down parking view, a camera-or-mirror rear view,
  the fastest windows he's seen. ⬥ A social observation: **"a sports car gets people revving to race you;
  the Prius has the opposite effect — everyone tries to cut in front of you."** Verdict: "I get it. I
  like the Prius, man." (quote)
