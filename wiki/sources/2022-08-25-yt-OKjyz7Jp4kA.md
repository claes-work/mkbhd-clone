# Rivian R1T After 500 Miles! (the FIRST @AutoFocus video)

## Summary

**The first-ever @AutoFocus video** — Marques launches the channel ("a spinoff, separate, brand-new
YouTube channel just for my thoughts on cars, which I have a lot of") and reviews his own **Rivian
R1T** after ~2 weeks / 500 miles. Canonical for **why @AutoFocus exists, that he shoots it on
smartphone cameras**, and a key fleet story: he'd **ordered a Cybertruck as the studio work truck**,
but a 48-hour R1T loan convinced him to order the R1T instead. Fully Marques-narrated.

## Key claims (biography marked ⬥)

- **[2022-08-25]** ⬥ CHANNEL LAUNCH: **"Welcome to the new Auto Focus YouTube channel — a spinoff,
  separate, brand-new channel just for my thoughts on cars, which I have a lot of, and all kinds of
  things I want to talk about in the car world that aren't fit for a full MKBHD video."** (quote)
- **[2022-08-25]** ⬥ PRODUCTION: **"I'm shooting these on smartphone cameras — right now the iPhone 13
  Pro in ProRes — a lighter lift so I can focus on the car, and it's the advice I always give: if you
  want to start a YouTube channel in 2022, just use your smartphone camera."** (quote)
- **[2022-08-25]** ⬥ BIOGRAPHY (fleet): the R1T is **"the newest addition to the fleet."** "You might
  have seen my 'Why We Ordered a Cybertruck' video two years ago — **the Cybertruck was going to be our
  studio work truck** (fast, silent, utility-focused). But Rivian loaned me this for 48 hours, and
  within a week I realized this is the same thing, actually better in a lot of ways — so I put in my
  order." (Took delivery; now 500+ mi in.) (quote)
- **[2022-08-25]** ⬥ BIOGRAPHY: mentions the **camera rig they built on a Tesla** (studio channel) —
  "much harder to shoot with on regular roads; I'll use the Tesla on the track. This one you can just
  drive around and nobody bats an eye." (quote)
- **[2022-08-25]** The R1T take: loves the aesthetic (headlight/taillight bars — **"it gets the most
  looks of any vehicle I've tested"**); the Dr.-Jekyll/Mr.-Hyde sport mode ("makes a 7,000 lb, 800 hp,
  quad-motor, 3s-0–60 truck feel nimble"); excellent interior/materials. ⚠️ Nitpicks (feature
  suggestions): **it should add a dash cam via software update** ("Tesla did"); a smarter reversing
  camera; the **Bluetooth phone-key calibration** (unlocks from 15 ft — "Teslas nailed this"); sliding
  wireless chargers; a low-functionality app. **"It's one of the cars getting meaningful software
  updates — I'm a fan of that."** (quote + paraphrase)
