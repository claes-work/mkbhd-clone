# Can New EV Companies Succeed?

## Summary

A CES-week tier-list where Marques ranks EV startups that **haven't shipped (or have failed)** by
whether their idea deserved to exist. His organizing thesis: **Tesla's master plan** (high-price/
low-volume → down-market to mass production) plus **a real differentiator** is what it takes to
survive — and almost nobody has both. Fisker and the Sony-Honda Afeela rank highest of the bunch;
Dyson and Faraday Future rank lowest. Fully Marques-narrated.

## Key claims (biography marked ⬥)

- **[2025-01-15]** ⭐ Framework: **"If you're going to be a new startup in the US you need a master plan
  that's going to work AND a differentiator that's a little bit different."** The only US EV-startup
  successes are Tesla (the master plan), Rivian (the adventure/pickup differentiator), and Lucid
  (high-materials/efficiency, "barely hanging on"). "Competition is good." (quote)
- **[2025-01-15]** ⬥ BIOGRAPHY: **"You might remember on the MKBHD channel I actually made a video with
  the Faraday Future FF91 in 2017"** — they showed him a production-spec car and a driving engineering
  prototype. He tiers **Faraday Future = C** ("$339k, a super-elite luxury/performance SUV… you have to
  find people willing to buy a $300k car from a new company"). (quote)
- **[2025-01-15]** The tier list: **Lordstown = E** (hub-mounted motors — "unsprung mass is so
  important… not a good trade-off"; poor execution on a not-great idea); **Aptera = D** (the efficiency-
  maxed 3-wheel solar EV; "noble to want the most efficient vehicle possible," but "a niche of a niche"
  that fits Europe better than America — "I just do not want to be in that on the road"); **Canoo = C**
  (great design, no financials, pivoted to fleet); **Sony-Honda Afeela = B** ("this feels like it could
  actually happen — Honda + Sony have the money and resources; a Tesla alternative"); **Dyson = F**
  ("who's going to buy a $184k Dyson car with no partner?"); **Fisker = A** (the Ocean actually shipped;
  "people were buying it… it didn't even look super electric, which is the best chance of getting normal
  people to buy one" — but "letting the cookies out of the oven too early" + management killed it).
  (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-01-15]** ⚠️ Parting shot: **"Tesla's not immune from vapor either — remember the Roadster?
  That 2017 announcement still hasn't come out."** (quote)

## Notable verbatim quotes
