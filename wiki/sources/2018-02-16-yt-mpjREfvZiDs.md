# Apple HomePod Review: The Dumbest Smart Speaker? (@mkbhd, 2018-02-16)

## Summary
Reviews Apple's $349 HomePod and lands a memorable one-line thesis: **best-sounding speaker in its class,
dumbest assistant in its class.** A clean case study of his "judge the thing on all its pillars, and name the
one that's holding it back" method.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-02-16] **The verdict, compressed (MEMORABLE).** "It might be **the best-sounding smart speaker you can
  buy — and the dumbest.** The audio hardware is genuinely excellent; **Siri is the ceiling.**" The
  title-thesis is the quotable core.
- [2018-02-16] **What's great — the audio + auto-tuning.** Real praise for the sound: spatial awareness that
  senses the room and tunes itself, big clean bass, strong mid-range — "for $350 it competes with speakers that
  cost more, on sound alone." Gives credit where the hardware earns it.
- [2018-02-16] **What's limiting — Siri + the walled garden.** Siri does far less than Alexa/Google Assistant;
  it's **Apple-Music-locked** (no native Spotify), tied tightly to iPhone. And "classic Apple" — **you can't
  even use it as a plain computer/TV speaker** the obvious way you'd expect a great speaker to work. The
  ecosystem lock is the cost.
- [2018-02-16] **The nitpick + the buyer's-frame close.** Notes the white model can leave a ring on wood
  finishes; closes with his standard framing — "**who is this for?** If you're all-in on Apple Music and
  iPhone and you care most about sound, it's great. For anyone else, the smarts and the lock-in are real
  problems." Buyer-first, not scored-out-of-ten.

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "This might be the best-sounding smart speaker you can buy — and also the dumbest."
> "The hardware is fantastic. Siri is the ceiling."
> "It's classic Apple — you can't even just use it as a normal speaker for your computer. Why?"
