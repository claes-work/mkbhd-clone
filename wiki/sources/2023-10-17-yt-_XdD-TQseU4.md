# The Vision for Mixed Reality: Now vs The Future! (@mkbhd, 2023-10-17)

## Summary
A framework video around a theory: **someday we may all wear computers on our faces**, and the tech is racing there
from **two ends of a sliding scale** — **VR headsets shrinking down** (the **Quest 3**) and **smart glasses getting
more capable** (the **Ray-Ban Meta glasses**) — **"both basically racing toward each other,"** with companies (Meta
invests in both) spending huge sums to see **which strategy hits mass adoption first.** On the **Quest 3** ($500):
the big pivot is **mixed reality** — dramatically better color/stereo/low-latency **pass-through** (intuitive
room-mapping via RGB + LiDAR, no more drawing a play area), better hand-tracking, and it's still a great games
machine. It's **better than the Quest Pro but not as good as the Apple Vision Pro** (which he's tried — "still the
best pass-through I've ever experienced; I took notes on my phone *while wearing it*"), but for $500 it's "as good as
you'll see." On the **Ray-Ban Meta glasses**: the opposite end — only as much tech as fits in normal-looking glasses
(a 12 MP camera, pinhole speakers, a Llama-2 voice assistant) — surprisingly capable, though **battery is the
obvious limit** (~2 hrs heavy use; 36 hrs with the case). The takeaway: **"we are barreling toward a computers-on-
our-faces future"** — even if it won't be *this*, it'll be something smaller — and **"what a time to be alive"**
(the Vision Pro is on track for early 2024).

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2023-10-17] **The sliding scale — VR shrinks, glasses grow, racing toward each other.** "'My theory: VR headsets
  are racing to get smaller and smaller, and smart glasses are racing to get better and better tech — and they're
  both basically racing toward each other. Companies like Meta are spending tons to develop both to figure out which
  strategy will hit mass adoption first.'"
- [2023-10-17] **Quest 3 — great pass-through for $500, but the Vision Pro is still the best.** "'The Quest 3's pivot
  is mixed reality — dramatically better color/stereo/low-latency pass-through, intuitive room-mapping via RGB +
  LiDAR. It's better than the Quest Pro but not as good as the Apple Vision Pro, which is still the best pass-through
  I've ever experienced — I took notes on my phone while still wearing the headset. But for $500, this is as good as
  you'll see.'"
- [2023-10-17] **Ray-Ban Meta — as much tech as fits in normal glasses.** "'The glasses start at the other end —
  only as much as you can stuff into a normal-looking pair nobody would look twice at (a 12 MP camera, pinhole
  speakers firing into your ears, a Llama-2 voice assistant). Surprisingly capable as a little computer on your
  face; the obvious downside is battery — I can kill them in under two hours, 36 total with the case.'"
- [2023-10-17] **Which side hits mass adoption first — the question companies must answer.** "'Which side would you
  bet gets mass adoption first? That's the question tech companies have to answer before investing. Meta's in both,
  so maybe they win either way. Snapchat tried just glasses; Apple started with the Vision Pro and will shrink it
  year after year.'"
- [2023-10-17] **Barreling toward computers on our faces — what a time to be alive.** "'I'm pretty sure we're
  barreling toward a computers-on-our-faces future — it's picking up that much steam and investment. Even if you
  don't think we'll all wear these for long, it won't be this, it'll be something smaller, and slowly we'll get more
  face-computer time. The tech we'll have in 10-20 years will look insane vs today, the way today looks archaic vs 10
  years ago. What a time to be alive.'"
