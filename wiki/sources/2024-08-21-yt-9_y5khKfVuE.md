# VW ID. Buzz Minibus Review: The Ultimate Reboot!

> ⚠️ Contains a **Ridge sponsor read** (Marques is Ridge's Chief Creative Partner) — a Cybertruck
> giveaway promo. Flagged; low persona weight.

## Summary

Marques reviews the US **Volkswagen ID. Buzz** (the electric reboot of the 1960s Microbus) and — a
self-described **non-nostalgia person** — is won over, framing it as **"an art form to creating
something new that still feels classic."** He answers the common "why didn't they just make the old
one electric?" complaint (safety/rules/roads changed) and sells it on execution: hidden storage
everywhere, three genuinely-adult rows, a smart-glass roof. Weak range (~250mi) is the catch. Fully
Marques-narrated. Corroborates his Cybertruck ownership.

## Key claims (biography marked ⬥)

- **[2024-08-21]** Frame: **"I'm not usually someone who falls for the nostalgia thing… but there's
  still an art form to creating something new like this that still feels classic."** The challenge for
  VW: "modernize the Microbus and convince you the change is worth it." ~$60k start, US gets a longer
  3-row wheelbase (Europe's is 2-row). (quote)
- **[2024-08-21]** He answers the top complaint (**"I wish they just made the exact old 60s design
  electric"**): rules, roads, and safety regs changed — "some things they had to modernize." Kept the
  giant VW logo, the disc wheels (also good for aero), the general shape. (paraphrase)
- **[2024-08-21]** What convinces him is the interior: **"I've never seen so much hidden storage"**
  (double-decker door bins, removable center console, dividers that double as a bottle opener/ice
  scraper), three real adult rows ("adult storage… that sounds weird"), heated seats throughout, and a
  **smart-glass sunroof** you tint via the settings. (quote + paraphrase)
- **[2024-08-21]** ⚠️ The catch: **"the range on the Microbus is not great — ~250mi maxed out… not the
  best road-tripping EV on paper, but maybe the rest of the features convince you."** (quote)
- **[2024-08-21]** ⬥ BIOGRAPHY: notes a second corner-visibility window **"kind of similar to the
  Cybertruck, which I've been living with for the past couple weeks."** (Corroborates Cybertruck
  ownership; he's also Ridge's Chief Creative Partner — a Cybertruck-giveaway sponsor read follows.)
  Fits behind his own **6'3"** driving position "with a ton of legroom." (quote)
- **[2024-08-21]** Verdict: **"Charming… something that's not quite a minivan but you can still fit the
  family in, that turns heads like a Cybertruck. I'm convinced — but I'll let you be the judge."** (quote)
