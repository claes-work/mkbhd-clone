# LG G8 Review: Master of None! (@mkbhd, 2019-04-09)

## Summary
The full LG G8 review — the title is the verdict: **jack of all trades, master of none.** Decent at everything,
outstanding at nothing, with no reason to pay $820 over the competition. Best material: the "phones are so good
this is almost unfair" framing and the blunt "just buy an S10e instead."

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-04-09] **"Jack of all trades, master of none."** "It's **decent at pretty much everything — you probably
  wouldn't regret it — but it doesn't stand out in any way to give you a reason to get one over the competition at
  $820.**" A verdict that doubles as the title.
- [2019-04-09] **"LG needs a stronger identity."** "The design is nothing special — **if I asked you to describe an
  LG phone in a couple words, what even comes to mind?** I hate encouraging weird-for-the-sake-of-different, but LG
  needs a stronger identity." His recurring sympathetic LG diagnosis. (Nice: a **rare perfectly flat back** — but
  "the sneaky slipperiest phone of all time.") The Crystal Sound OLED earpiece "sounds a bit weird — a buzzing
  rattle"; the boombox speaker "sounds worse than I remember — flat and hollow"; a strange **scrolling stutter**
  (a bug?).
- [2019-04-09] **The cameras — good, but LG's ultra-wide lead got copied away.** "**LG was first to consistently do
  the ultra-wide — but it was so easy to copy that everyone caught up; LG didn't master it in a special way.** The
  cameras are a **confident, fast, consistent shooter** (good color match between the standard and ultra-wide,
  better than Huawei's/Samsung's), but not incredibly unique." A bittersweet read on LG's one advantage.
- [2019-04-09] **Hand ID + Air Motion = still gimmicks.** "**Usable at best, not good.** Hand ID's learning curve is
  too much (and **your hand blocks the on-screen feedback**) — I turned it off and use the fingerprint reader like
  most people will. Air Motion's twist-volume tracks well, but **waving to open apps is slower than the buttons or
  the Google Assistant button — you might as well just hit the button.**" Confirms the impressions read.
- [2019-04-09] **"Phones are so good this is almost unfair" + "just buy an S10e."** "The G8 has good build, software,
  and cameras — **that's a testament to how good phones have gotten around it.** It feels unfair to beat it up by
  pulling the Pixel's camera, the P30's battery, the S10's jack. **So I'll make it simple: if you're thinking about
  the G8, just buy a Galaxy S10e instead — there isn't a single thing the G8 does better, and the S10e is
  cheaper.** If it were $650, 'cheaper' would be the reason — but it isn't yet. **Don't buy an LG G8.**" A rare
  blunt no-buy.
