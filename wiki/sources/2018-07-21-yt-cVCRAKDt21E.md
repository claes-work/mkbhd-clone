# i9 Macbook Pro 2018: Hottest Laptop on the Planet! (@mkbhd, 2018-07-21)

## Summary
Reviews the $6,700 i9 15" MacBook Pro amid the **thermal-throttling controversy**. Rather than repeat others'
benchmarks, he runs **his own Final Cut render test**, explains Apple's "thin-and-quiet-to-a-fault" philosophy,
and concludes most people should **skip this great laptop and wait** — even floating "maybe we should ask Apple
for a *thicker* pro laptop."

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-07-21] **The verdict up front — don't upgrade.** "You probably should **not** upgrade to this if you have a
  2017 or even 2016 MacBook Pro — and not really because of the price." Leads with the honest buying advice.
- [2018-07-21] **The thermal problem, explained.** "Apple loves thin, quiet laptops **to a fault** — so much that
  they'd rather **throttle the CPU than ramp the fans.** Their most powerful laptop is a thin unibody with small
  fans and vents; ask anyone else to build a max-performance workstation and you'd picture a thick chassis with
  huge vents and fans cranking. **The thermal design could be a lot better.**" A systems-level diagnosis (people
  were literally refrigerating theirs).
- [2018-07-21] **OWN-TESTING — he runs his real workflow, not synthetic benchmarks.** "I rely on these for **one
  thing — mobile Final Cut editing.** So I exported a 3-min RED 8K (8:1) timeline: **iMac Pro 6:53, the 2017 MBP
  25:38, this 2018 i9 21:21.**" i.e., about **15% faster** than last gen. "On a big project that's maybe 3 hours
  → 2.5. Technically the faster laptop I wanted — barely." Rigor over spec-sheet hype (shout-out to
  Jonathan Morrison / TLD for aggregating other variables).
- [2018-07-21] **"Skip this great laptop and wait" + the thicker-pro-laptop provocation.** "Because of the
  throttling, someone with a 2016/2017 machine probably shouldn't spend the money. **Apple could get a bigger
  performance boost by updating the design than by dropping in new chips — maybe we should start asking Apple for
  a *thicker* pro laptop.**" His anti-thinness campaign, applied to laptops (echoes the phone "make it thicker for
  battery" thread). Reuses the **"skip this great [device]"** framing.
- [2018-07-21] **The minor-new-stuff, honestly rated.** True Tone display (+ Touch Bar matches) — "I turn it off
  immediately, as will most video editors/photographers"; the quieter **butterfly keyboard membrane** (addresses
  reliability, "not game-changing — I'd take more travel"); the **T2 chip** enabling always-on "Hey Siri" ("what
  would you even use Siri for on the desktop? — **please make Siri better**"). The 4 TB SSD is "peace of mind" but
  "you probably don't need it — the same-perf machine is $3k less" (calls the pricing "clickbait").

## Notable verbatim quotes
