# Let's Talk About Tesla Roadster 2020! (@mkbhd, 2017-11-17)

## Summary
A reaction to the Tesla Semi unveiling and its **"one more thing" surprise** — the Roadster 2.0. Clone-
relevant as an EV/enthusiast node: a rare **spec sheet that leaves a P100D owner "speechless"** (0–60 in 1.9s),
plus the Roadster's history (the original Lotus-bodied "Frankenstein" that built Tesla's early reputation).

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2017-11-17] **The Semi as the headline (with owner's honesty).** "Elon drove one out on stage — 500-mile
  range, 0–60 in 5s (empty) / 20s (80,000-lb payload), recharge to 400 miles in half an hour, a center
  driving position, autopilot. Looks really cool — but I'm not a truck driver, so I'll probably never sit in
  one." Reports it fairly while flagging it's not his lane.
- [2017-11-17] **The "one more thing" — the Roadster 2.0.** "The lights dimmed, came back on, and out of a
  trailer rolled the Roadster 2.0 — the one we all knew was eventually coming. It looks **absolutely
  incredible** — a Tesla in its curves, with cues from Porsche, Aston Martin, some McLaren lines; a really
  aggressive, good-looking sports car." His design read.
- [2017-11-17] **A spec sheet that leaves him speechless — "and I own a P100D."** "I'm a spec person, I like
  numbers — and there aren't a lot of spec sheets that can make me speechless, **especially coming from
  someone who's owned a P100D** (which has a silly spec sheet itself). The new Roadster: **0–60 in 1.9
  seconds.**" A rare register of genuine awe, credibility-anchored to his own fast car.
- [2017-11-17] **The Roadster history (source-critical context).** "Tesla didn't have much reputation when
  the original Roadster came out — a two-door that was essentially a Lotus body with Tesla's motors/batteries,
  a weird Frankenstein hybrid; not the most practical or comfortable, but zippy and fun to drive. It didn't
  Supercharge, wasn't a flagship, had problems, sold only a couple hundred/thousand — then rumors of a 2.0
  for years, but we never saw anything until last night." Grounds the reveal in Tesla's arc.

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "There's not a lot of spec sheets that can just make me kind of speechless — especially coming from someone
> who's driven and owned a P100D for a while, which has a silly spec sheet by itself."
> "0 to 60 miles per hour in 1.9 seconds."
