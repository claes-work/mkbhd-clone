# Samsung Galaxy Buds 4 Pro Review: Better than AirPods!

## Summary

An "under the radar" review of the Galaxy Buds 4 Pro, released quietly alongside the S26.
Marques cuts to the chase: **"these are incredible — I think they're better than AirPods
Pros."** He grants the comparison is somewhat meaningless (few people cross-shop them), but
as a performance benchmark the Buds clear it: they **finally stay in his ears**, sound as
good as the sound-maxed Sony XM6 but in an everyday package, have very strong ANC, and match
AirPods' ecosystem tricks while adding extras. The one thing stopping them from being the
go-to iPhone earbud: **Apple would never allow that level of integration.**

## Key claims

- **[2026-02-28]** The verdict up front: **"these are incredible. I think they're better than
  AirPods Pros"** — same $250 price, ANC + transparency, quick pairing and auto-switching, but
  "a lot of other things I think are better." Think of them as **"the AirPods Pro for the Android
  / Samsung universe."** (quote)
- **[2026-02-28]** Design: a new **cube case** (tinted clear lid, "satisfying magnetic slap,"
  wireless + USB-C — but **the case isn't IP-rated, a flaw**); redesigned buds with a **square
  metal-backed stem** that makes pinch/swipe gestures "the ideal setup," **IP57**, and — "huge
  breaking news" — **they stay in his ears** even with the default tips. (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2026-02-28]** Sound: new dual drivers (subwoofer + tweeter, an amp per driver) sound "really,
  really good"; he likes the **dynamic (V-shaped) EQ** for punch. The key framing vs the **Sony
  WF-1000XM6**: the Sonys are **sound-maxed** ("that's the slider they dragged everything toward")
  at the expense of everyday use (bigger case/buds, less comfortable, fall out) — **the Galaxy Buds
  sound every bit as good to him but in a package you can take anywhere** (work out, fly, meetings).
  (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2026-02-28]** ANC: **5 levels** ("honestly just need low and high"); at max it's **"easily as
  strong as AirPods Pro, actually a bit stronger"** — flight-grade, "I'm hearing new things in my
  music." Transparency (also 5 levels) is "just shy of AirPods transparency," which is very good.
  Nice touches: **auto-switch to ambient on a detected siren** (for cyclists/pedestrians) and **when
  you start talking** (then back to ANC ~5–10s after). (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2026-02-28]** Feature parity + extras: instant switching between Samsung devices, 360° head-
  tracking spatial audio, nod/shake-to-answer, in-ear detection, Find My, live translate — **"just
  like AirPods."** But **unlike** AirPods: a **gaming mode** (low latency), a **full 9-band EQ**, and
  **3 colors** (vs 1). Battery ~6h ANC / 7h off. (paraphrase)
- **[2026-02-28]** The ecosystem catch: he **paired them to his iPhone** — they work as basic
  Bluetooth earbuds (stem controls, ANC/ambient, volume) but **lose the Samsung-app customization**
  (the multi-band EQ, advanced features). "The only reason these couldn't become the new go-to iPhone
  earbud tomorrow is **Apple would never let that happen** — the same way they don't let Garmin
  watches integrate like the Apple Watch. **If you're mad at that, just blame Apple.**" (quote)
- **[2026-02-28]** Verdict: **"if you're on an Android phone, these are the ones to beat. My favorite
  wireless earbuds 2026, sneaky winners."** (quote)
