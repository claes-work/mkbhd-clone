# This Car Should've Been Electric! (Bentley Flying Spur S Hybrid)

## Summary

Marques lives a week with the **Bentley Flying Spur S** plug-in hybrid — coining **"athletic luxury /
luxury AF"** for its Maybach-comfort-meets-Model-S-athleticism blend — and uses it to argue his
**perfect-to-go-electric doctrine**: a giant luxury sedan is **exactly the kind of vehicle that would
most benefit from going fully electric** (quieter, smoother, more spacious). He'd pick the hybrid over
the V8, but really he wants the electric Bentley that doesn't exist yet. Fully Marques-narrated.

## Key claims (biography marked ⬥)

- **[2023-05-09]** The car: a ~$300k Flying Spur S plug-in hybrid — a 2.9L V6 + e-motors + 18 kWh
  battery, ~20 mi EV range, 500 mi total, ~4s 0–60. **"I'd personally pick the hybrid over the V8 —
  same power, but you add silent EV-only comfort and it's a little stealthier/higher-tech."** Coins
  **"athletic luxury / luxury AF."** (quote)
- **[2023-05-09]** ⬥ PRODUCTION/CONTEXT: **"our friend Miles is reviewing a Cadillac Escalade, and I
  parked this next to it — they're the same length"** (confirms **Miles as an @AutoFocus colleague
  reviewer as early as 2023**). Shot on the **iPhone 14 Pro without ProRes** (so an external mic works)
  — "nobody noticed, so I'm going to keep doing that." (quote)
- **[2023-05-09]** ⚠️ The interior: "the opposite of a Tesla interior — a ton of chrome and buttons…
  you overwhelmed yet?"; old-school-90s UI ("this is why people love CarPlay on cars like these"); not
  much storage ("if this were electric I'd expect a lot more opened up"). A pop-out rear tablet he
  "much prefers to the Maybach's Samsung-tablet-in-the-middle." (quote)
- **[2023-05-09]** ⭐ THE DOCTRINE (explicit): **"This is the type of vehicle that would most benefit
  from going completely electric. It's already super heavy — so yes, the electric version is also
  heavy, but it'll be quieter, more spacious, and smoother, and all of those things are important to
  someone who buys one of these. There should be an electric version of the Flying Spur."** (He ties it
  to the coming Cadillac EV Escalade — "how obvious is that?") (quote)

## Notable verbatim quotes
