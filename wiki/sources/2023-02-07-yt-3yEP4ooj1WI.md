# OnePlus 11 Review: They're Back?! (@mkbhd, 2023-02-07)

## Summary
The redemption-arc review: after the 10T won his **"Bust of the Year"** trophy (he keeps the trophy), the OnePlus 11
shows **"shades of old OnePlus"** — a genuinely good $699 flagship (Snapdragon 8 Gen 2, 6.7" 1440p 120 Hz LTPO OLED,
5,000 mAh with 80 W charging). His **semi-hot take: "this could be a better phone for a lot of people"** than the
default $799 S23. The good: excellent battery (two light days at 1080p/120 Hz), top-tier future-proof internals
(Wi-Fi 7, UFS 4.0, LPDDR5X), the **iconic alert slider is back**, a nice textured matte back. The corner-cutting:
**no wireless charging** and IP64 (not IP67); a "good enough not to be a dead giveaway" camera (throwback old-OnePlus
— solid, not flagship; slow focus; overexposes darker skin tones). The real reason he won't keep dailying it is
**the software** — a pile of little ColorOS notification quirks that add up (only swipe-right to clear; can't
swipe-to-expand; multiple taps to reach a playing app). His plea: **"look at what people loved about OxygenOS and
bring some of it back."** Verdict: **not full redemption yet, but an inflection point in the right direction** — "if
the character arc was like this, and then like this, it feels like a shift."

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2023-02-07] **OnePlus is kind of back — and used the trophy as motivation.** "'I was wondering how long before I
  could say this again: OnePlus is kind of back. The 10T literally won my Bust of the Year — I got a trophy — and
  apparently they used it as motivation. The next phone was already in the works by the time they got that trophy,
  but if you want to give me some credit, I'll take it.'"
- [2023-02-07] **A better phone for a lot of people.** "'This is a $699 flagship in a world where the S23 is $799 —
  the de-facto default. My semi-hot take: this could be a better phone for a lot of people. It's not perfect, and
  they still do some classic corner-cutting, but the basics are pretty damn good.'"
- [2023-02-07] **The corner-cutting — no wireless charging — and the alert slider is back.** "'The big cut is no
  wireless charging, and it's IP64 instead of IP67. But the Hasselblad branding is back, and the alert slider is
  back — an iconic OnePlus feature they somehow thought was a good idea to drop last time.'"
- [2023-02-07] **Old-OnePlus battery + a "good enough" camera.** "'Battery is where it feels the most like old
  OnePlus — two light days at 1080p/120 Hz, plus 80 W charging. The cameras are a throwback too: not as good as the
  flagships, but really solid — if the bar is a camera that isn't a dead giveaway of a cheaper phone, it clears
  that. When it misses it overexposes, more so with darker skin tones, and focus is suspiciously slow.'"
- [2023-02-07] **The software gripes — "look at what people loved about OxygenOS."** "'The reason I won't keep
  dailying it is the software — a bunch of little notification quirks that add up (only swipe-right clears; you
  can't swipe to expand a group; multiple taps to get to a playing app). If OnePlus really wants to redeem
  themselves, look back at what people loved about OxygenOS and bring some of it back. Not full redemption yet — but
  it feels like an inflection point.'"
