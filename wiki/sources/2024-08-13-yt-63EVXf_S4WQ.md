# Google Pixel 9/Pro/Fold Impressions: They've Finally Done It! (@mkbhd, 2024-08-13)

## Summary
Two hours hands-on at the Pixel 9 event, and the opening line: **"I think Google might have done it"** — the
**modernized hardware glow-up** he's wanted (with a self-aware caveat: **"I said the same thing about the Pixel 6"**).
The lineup: Pixel 9 ($799, 6.3"), Pixel 9 Pro (same size — **"a Pro that's not a Pro Max," which he loves**), Pixel 9
Pro XL, and the Pixel 9 Pro Fold. Right away the phones **"really feel and look like actual flagships"** — heavier/more
solid, much brighter screens, thinner bezels, a finally-good **ultrasonic fingerprint reader**, and the new flat-island
camera bar. Under the hood: the same **Tensor G4** across the line (more efficient, big AI/NPU gains) — but he flags
the recurring **"base and Pro share the same main + ultrawide camera"** (the Pro adds telephoto/RAM/better selfie for
$200), which makes the base look like the deal. The AI features get his **"what is a photo?"** treatment again —
**Reimagine** (type any scene into a photo), **Add Me** (genuinely impressive group-selfie compositing — "another
moment that never happened"), Pixel Studio (fast on-device generation), plus the confusing two-Gemini situation. But
the star surprise is the **Pixel 9 Pro Fold**: "Google cooked" — dramatically improved over last year's "best foldable
to use *closed*," now a bigger 6.3" cover screen that's actually reachable, a huge 8" inside display, **barely thicker
than a normal phone**, feeling more premium than ever — **"a better version of the OnePlus Open"** (the previous best),
and enough to **tempt him back to foldables** as a daily driver. His only real spec-sheet bummer: **no Qi2**. Verdict:
**"these feel like they've achieved the modernized glow-up we've been hoping for — some of their finest work yet."**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2024-08-13] **They might have finally done it (Pixel-6 déjà vu aside).** "'I think Google might have done it — the
  only reason I hesitate is I've had this exact feeling and said this same line about the Pixel 6. But right from the
  start these really feel and look like actual flagships: heavier and more solid, much brighter screens, thinner
  bezels, and finally an ultrasonic fingerprint reader.'"
- [2024-08-13] **The Pro that isn't a Pro Max is great.** "'The 9 Pro is the exact same size as the base Pixel 9 — just
  like what the iPhone's been doing — and only the XL is bigger. A lot of people want a better phone but not a gigantic
  one, so a Pro that's not a Pro Max is sick.'"
- [2024-08-13] **Base and Pro share the main camera.** "'All the new Pixels have the same Tensor G4, and the base and
  Pro share the exact same primary and ultrawide camera — the Pro just adds a telephoto, more RAM, and a better selfie
  camera for $200. Like before, that makes the lowest-priced one look like a great deal.'"
- [2024-08-13] **AI: "what is a photo?" — Reimagine and Add Me.** "'Magic Editor's new Reimagine lets you highlight a
  section and type it into something that totally didn't happen — what is a picture? But the best "what is a photo"
  feature is Add Me: it composites two shots so the photographer ends up in the group photo — "another moment that
  never happened," and genuinely impressive. Plus Pixel Studio generates images on device in ~3 seconds.'"
- [2024-08-13] **The Pixel 9 Pro Fold is the star — better than the OnePlus Open.** "'Google cooked with the Fold. Last
  year's was the best foldable to use closed (a half-compliment); this one has a bigger, reachable 6.3" cover screen, a
  huge 8" inside display, and is barely thicker than a normal phone — it feels like a better version of the OnePlus
  Open, the previous best foldable. It has a real chance to get me back to being a foldable daily-driver. The only
  spec-sheet bummer is no Qi2. These feel like the modernized glow-up we've been hoping for — some of their finest work
  yet.'"
