# Desk Tour & Daily Tech Update 2023! (@mkbhd, 2023-03-16)

## Summary
A 2023 desk-tour + everyday-carry update, framed around **"the stuff that sticks around longest"** (what's held up
says as much as what's new). The one big change at the desk: **the editing machine is now a Mac Studio *on* the
desk** (it was a Mac Pro under the desk) — "look at the cable management." Otherwise it's the durable staples:
Yamaha HS8 monitors, a ~96 TB Promise Pegasus storage block, the Herman Miller Embody ("the best chair I've ever
seen"), Pro Display XDRs + an Opal C1 webcam, a Keychron Q1, HD 650s for editing, and the **two-phone system**
(phones he's actively using are **face up and on**; phones he's signed into but not using are **face down**). The
everyday carry is the **Peak Design Everyday 20L** (custom red stitching) with **AirPods Max** ("still overpriced,
but really great"), an Anker 747 charger + a cable mess, and — critically — the **same M1 Max 16" MacBook Pro**:
**"I didn't upgrade it to the M2 because I didn't really feel like I needed it, which says a lot about how good the
M1 Max is."** The R5 is now "sometimes carry" (he captures so much on phones). Razer Edge sponsor (a dedicated
gaming handheld he packs for travel).

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2023-03-16] **The Mac Studio replaced the Mac Pro — the cable-management win.** "'The editing machine was a Mac
  Pro under the desk — a sit-stand desk, so all the cables had to make their way up. Now it's a Mac Studio on the
  desk. Look at the cable management, look how nice that is — nobody's going to roast my cable management anymore.'"
- [2023-03-16] **Still the M1 Max — didn't feel the need for the M2.** "'The laptop I carry every single day
  everywhere is the same M1 Max 16" MacBook Pro, nice and worn in. I didn't upgrade it to the M2 because I didn't
  really feel like I needed it — which says a lot about how good the M1 Max is.'"
- [2023-03-16] **The two-phone face-up/face-down system.** "'There's a bit of a system with smartphones here: the
  phones I'm using right now are face up and on, and the phones I'm also signed into but not actively using are face
  down.'"
- [2023-03-16] **The philosophy — the stuff that sticks around longest.** "'You don't always get to spend a lot of
  time with things, so it's good to know something's actually held up and lasted. I can personally vouch for the
  stuff I'm linking — there are lots of alternatives I'd be perfectly happy with, but this is what I carry every day.'"
- [2023-03-16] **AirPods Max — still overpriced, still great; the Lightning bummer.** "'My one-year-later on the
  AirPods Max: I'd still say they're overpriced, but they're really great, comfortable, and I use them a lot
  travelling. They charge by Lightning, which is a huge bummer, so I have to carry a Lightning cable everywhere.'"
