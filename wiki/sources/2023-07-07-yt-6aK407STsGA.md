# This Phone is Nearly Perfect! (Asus Zenfone 10) (@mkbhd, 2023-07-07)

## Summary
A near-love-letter to the **Asus Zenfone 10** — his highest personal-taste praise: **"if you task me with making my
own phone, and I didn't have to care how well it sells but I'd actually want to use it, I think this is what I would
make."** It keeps everything that made the Zenfone 9 his best small phone of the year (a compact body, the latest
Snapdragon 8 Gen 2, world-class battery, a headphone jack) **and directly fixes his three nitpicks: (1)** it now has
**wireless charging** (the one missing spec); **(2)** a **more durable back finish** (the 9's soft-touch got "chewed
up" from black pants — "if you make a phone with a nice finish you want to be able to touch it"); **(3)** improved
**cameras** — same 50 MP sensor but a big **six-axis hybrid gimbal stabilization** system delivering sharper
handheld/low-light shots and "some of the most impressive shake-resistant video I've ever seen." Plus a
genuinely-him detail: at setup you can **choose which parts are stock Android vs Asus-customized** ("I've never seen
this in any other phone... I love it"). All at the **same $699 price as last year** — "who does that?" His one
remaining gripe — the thing keeping it from truly perfect — is **software support** (only ~2 years, not the 3–4 a
phone like this deserves). "Clearly there's no perfect phone, but this one is pretty close."

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2023-07-07] **If I made my own phone I'd want to use, this is it.** "'If you task me with making my own phone —
  and I didn't have to care how well it sells but I'd actually want to use it — I think this is what I'd make. They
  just don't make phones like this anymore.'"
- [2023-07-07] **They fixed all three of my nitpicks.** "'They kept all the great stuff and fixed my three nitpicks
  from last year: it now has wireless charging (basically the only missing spec); a more durable back finish (my
  Zenfone 9 got absolutely chewed up from black pants — if you make a phone with a nice soft-touch finish, you want
  to be able to touch it); and improved cameras.'"
- [2023-07-07] **The gimbal stabilization does the work, not a bigger sensor.** "'Same 50 MP sensor, but an improved
  six-axis hybrid gimbal stabilization — that's why the rings are so big. Without making the sensor bigger you get
  sharper handheld and low-light shots because the whole system cancels hand movement, plus some of the most
  impressive shake-resistant video I've ever seen.'"
- [2023-07-07] **Choose stock vs Asus at setup — the enthusiast touch.** "'The most unique thing — something I'd do
  if I were making an enthusiast phone most people wouldn't care about — is that at setup you choose which parts are
  stock Android and which are Asus-customized (launcher, quick settings). I've never seen this in any other phone.
  Only Asus does it. I love it.'"
- [2023-07-07] **Same price, one gripe: software support.** "'All of it at the same $699 as last year — who does
  that? My last remaining thing, what keeps it from truly perfect, is software support: Asus promises ~2 years,
  which isn't the full life cycle; you'd ideally have it 3–4. Clearly there's no perfect phone, but this one is
  pretty close.'"
