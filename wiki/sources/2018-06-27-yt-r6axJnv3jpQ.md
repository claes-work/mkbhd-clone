# $2400 Headphones? Sennheiser HD 820! (@mkbhd, 2018-06-27)

## Summary
A Dope-Tech-style review of the $2,400 Sennheiser HD 820 — "the most ridiculous headphones I've ever owned, and I
regret nothing." Explains the open-soundstage-in-a-closed-back engineering, his honest streaming-not-audiophile
caveats, his test-track ritual, and a memorable "video person" analogy for what high-end audio reveals.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-06-27] **Not a buyer's guide — the honest price stance.** "$2,400. **Past a certain price I don't really
  care** — $2k, $3k, $4k, they're all ridiculous next to the **$100–300 headphones I spend most of my time
  listening to.**" He keeps his own everyday reference frame in view.
- [2018-06-27] **The engineering — open-back soundstage, closed-back isolation.** "The trick: a **concave Gorilla
  Glass cup behind the 56 mm drivers** cuts the bleed of a typical open-back (like the HD 800), so people around
  you hear less and — more importantly — **you hear less of the world.** Not noise-canceling, but a big reduction,
  while keeping the **wide soundstage** (the air between driver, glass, and ear is why they're so huge)." A clean
  teach-the-mechanism.
- [2018-06-27] **PRODUCTION/GEAR — his listening chain, stated honestly.** Drives them with an **Apollo Twin Mk2**
  (~$900 amp; "I've had the Mk1 for years driving my speakers"), streaming **compressed files (Tidal Masters /
  Spotify)**: "I'm clearly **not getting 100%** out of these at this setup — but it still sounds damn good." Refuses
  to overclaim.
- [2018-06-27] **The test-track ritual (a method tell).** His go-to reference tracks: **Daft Punk "Contact,"
  Deadmau5 "Strobe," Chaka Khan, Avicii, C2C** — "songs I'm familiar with, with amazing mastering, where
  **instrument separation** makes you appreciate them in a whole new way." Shows how he actually evaluates audio.
- [2018-06-27] **The "video person" analogy (signature).** "Best way I can explain it: a regular pair floods your
  senses — lots of bass, highs, mids. Switch to these and it might seem **flat at first**, then you realize the
  **highlights, shadows, and textures** you were missing get vibrant and clear. That's the best analogy I've got
  — **because I'm a video person.**" Translates audio into his native visual/grading language.
- [2018-06-27] **The verdict — certified Dope Tech.** "Amazing at high volume — turn it up and it gets **fuller,
  not harsher.** Perfect for a **studio** where I want to block high-end noise but keep a wide soundstage.
  **Certified Dope Tech** — everything from packaging to sound to comfort. Sennheiser, I don't think I want to give
  them back." (ColorWare aside for supporting the channel.)
