# Top 5 iOS 12 Features! (@mkbhd, 2018-06-07)

## Summary
A hands-on Top-5 for the iOS 12 beta — notable as the first time he does his signature "top new features" format
(long an Android staple) for **iOS**, prompted by iOS 12 being feature-rich. Companion to the WWDC recap;
overlaps it, with more hands-on detail and a couple of extra tells.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-06-07] **Doing "top features" for iOS for the first time.** "On this channel you know I usually do a top-
  new-features-of-Android video — what better time to start an **iOS version** than when there's a bunch of great
  new features." A dated format/biography note (his coverage broadening).
- [2018-06-07] **#1 Grouped notifications — "finally, as an Android user."** "Got the loudest cheer on stage.
  Notifications from one app stack; tap to expand, act one-by-one, collapse. **Immensely better** — still a little
  buggy. I'd still like to **act on notifications without leaving the tray** like I do on Android — probably a
  couple years off." Praises but keeps the Android-is-still-ahead marker.
- [2018-06-07] **#2 Memoji — build-your-own (not scan).** "Unlike Samsung, it **doesn't scan a photo** — you build
  it. The **eye-movement detection gives it life** (previous animoji eyes were beaded dots); tongue detection is
  cool but the eyes sold me." Consistent with his AR-emoji-vs-Animoji thread (still iPhone-X-only).
- [2018-06-07] **#3 Screen Time — digital wellbeing (with a wry aside).** App-usage breakdowns, pickup counts, and
  per-app limits (a wall when time's up). Notes the irony: "on the **same stage 25 minutes later they had a lady
  texting from an exercise bike while multitasking on her watch** — so the message got a bit lost." Sharp media
  read.
- [2018-06-07] **#4 Siri Shortcuts — "now just fix the rest of Siri, please."** Map any in-app action to any phrase
  (he sets "**show me the money**" → Apple stock). "I was looking forward to bigger Siri improvements — mainly
  making it **not bad** — but this is interesting." Faint-praise honesty.
- [2018-06-07] **#5 New apps + the Measure app (his favorite).** Redesigned Books/News/Stocks/Voice Memos, and the
  **AR Measure app** ("possibly my favorite addition — accurate, better on smaller/closer objects"). Plus
  smaller wins: swipe-to-kill apps on iPhone X, harder-to-take accidental screenshots ("**thank you, Apple** — I
  think they heard our feedback").

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "Grouped notifications — finally. As an Android user: finally."
> "The eyes are what really give Memoji life — the other animojis before were just beaded dots."
> "Siri Shortcuts is interesting… now just fix the rest of Siri, please."
> "It's now harder to take accidental screenshots — thank you, Apple. I think they heard our feedback."
