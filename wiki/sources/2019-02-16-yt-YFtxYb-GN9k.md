# Apollo is Back - My Tesla Repair Process! (@mkbhd, 2019-02-16)

## Summary
The follow-up to the Apollo crash — a detailed, dated timeline of his 3-month Tesla repair, framed as a
teach-people-the-process piece (why it took 3 months, not 3 days or a year). Ends with a genuine reflection: he
prefers the EV experience.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-02-16] **BIOGRAPHY — the repair timeline (Nov 16 → Feb 13).** Crash **Nov 16, 2018**; towed to the Tesla
  service center (which doesn't do bodywork); an **Acura loaner** via insurance; the **NJ state police report took
  3 weeks (Dec 8)** — the **trucker was 100% at fault**, so his insurance paid **$0.** Structural work done by Jan
  3; cosmetics after; picked up **Feb 13.** A concrete, dated ownership record.
- [2019-02-16] **THE KEY — a certified body shop with parts IN STOCK.** "Why 3 months, not 9: **JMB Body Shop
  (Aston Martin- and McLaren-certified; Lambos/McLarens/Teslas out front) had 100% of the parts in stock.**"
  Explains the **Tesla certification program** (like Apple's): "you *can* take a Tesla anywhere, but a **Tesla-
  certified shop can order official parts and has the tooling for an undetectable repair** — and **Tesla
  notoriously takes 3–9+ months to ship parts.** Having them in stock is what saved months." A genuinely useful
  ownership explainer.
- [2019-02-16] **The wrap detail + the cost.** It's a **wrapped car** (matte space-gray), so new naked parts had
  to be re-wrapped; after re-wrapping the left side, the year-old right side looked faded, so **he had the whole
  car re-wrapped to match** (plus bonus paint-chip/glass buffing the shop threw in). **$49,000 in repairs total —
  $0 to him.** Transparent about the numbers.
- [2019-02-16] **The reflection — "I like the electric car experience more."** "**I really do like the EV
  experience more than the gas experience** — I got disappointed waking up to a gas loaner that **wasn't refueled
  overnight**, and I kept **leaving it running** because EVs auto-off when you put it in park and walk away. Those
  little things — I appreciate them all over again." A dated, first-hand EV-vs-ICE preference.
- [2019-02-16] **The advice.** "**(1) Pick a certified body shop** for a better experience; **(2) call ahead to
  check if they have the parts in stock** — it made a world of difference. I'd probably still be waiting on Tesla
  otherwise." Teaches from his own experience (his signature move). Notes future updates on the complexity of
  **selling a repaired car.**

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "Had they not had all the parts in stock, they'd have to order them from Tesla — which has notoriously taken
> three, six, sometimes nine months or longer to ship parts for repairs."
> "I really do like the electric car experience more than the gas car experience. I was getting disappointed
> waking up and the car wasn't refueled overnight, and I kept getting out of the gas car and realizing it was
> still running."
> "Total: forty-nine thousand dollars of repairs. Balance due from customer, thanks to the truck driver's
> insurance: zero dollars."
