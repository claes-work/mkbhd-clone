# AirPods 3 Review: Easy Mode! (@mkbhd, 2021-11-02)

## Summary
Review of the $179 AirPods 3 (a lighter ingest; opens noting he's finishing the M1 Max review, edited on the
laptop itself). The durable idea: **AirPods are "middle of the road" / "easy mode"** — they don't do any single
thing amazingly (they'll get beaten in any one category), but they do everything you'd want well and fit the
iPhone ecosystem seamlessly, which is what makes them the easy choice. His personal caveat: **the hard plastic
tips don't fit his ears** (the right one keeps sliding out — "one of the cursed ears"), so he'd pay up for the
AirPods Pro (silicon tips + ANC) every time. Sound is now "a perfectly respectable" — a real improvement he
couldn't have said before. Ends with the **"AirPods Inc. would be worth ~$175B"** stat — the best lure into the
ecosystem.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-11-02] **Hard tips still don't fit his ears.** "'They look like AirPods Pro with the silicon tips chopped
  off — the one-size hard tip. That's simultaneously their biggest advantage and, **for me, the biggest downside:
  the right one keeps sliding out until it falls. I'm jealous of people who just pop AirPods in and run around —
  I'm one of the cursed ears; I'll take silicon tips every time.**'"
- [2021-11-02] **Respectable sound now — a real improvement.** "'And I can't believe I'm saying this — they have a
  perfectly respectable sound now. **I've trashed AirPods for how weak and tiny they sounded; these have much more
  body and bass.** Caveats: no built-in EQ, and they're tuned as all-rounders (calls, podcasts, music, movies).
  Spatial audio is a rare treat on the few well-done Dolby Atmos mixes.'"
- [2021-11-02] **Middle of the road / easy mode — the thesis.** "'These are meant to **sit right in the middle of
  the road — they don't do any one thing amazingly, so they'll get beaten in single categories, but they do
  everything you'd want headphones to do daily very well** and fit the iPhone ecosystem perfectly (instant
  pairing, seamless switching, better Find My). **That's what makes them so easy to recommend** — the easy
  choice, out right before the holidays.'"
- [2021-11-02] **I'd pay up for the Pro.** "'A lot of people compare AirPods 3 with AirPods Pro — **I'd personally
  pay the extra money for the Pro every time** (on Amazon they've been ~$190 — so ~$10 more for ANC and silicon
  tips). But if you don't care about that, this is what you'll pick.' The whole landscape is more diverse now
  (Beats Fit Pro's wingtip fit, the Sony XM4's sound).'"
- [2021-11-02] **"AirPods Inc." would be worth ~$175B — the best lure.** "'Fun fact: if you spun AirPods off as
  its own company, it'd be worth ~$175 billion (a 2019 estimate) — for context, Logitech is ~$14B and Sony (which
  makes movies, the PlayStation, phones, cameras) is ~$150B. **That's not to say these headphones are amazing —
  it's to say they're probably the best lure to bring people into the iPhone ecosystem.**'"
