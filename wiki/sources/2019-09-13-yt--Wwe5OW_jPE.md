# Asus ROG Phone 2 Review: The Spec King! (@mkbhd, 2019-09-13)

## Summary
Review of the Asus ROG Phone 2 — "the phone with every spec maxed out, the absolute dream phone on paper." The
video is explicitly a **test of the question "how well do specs on paper translate to real-world use?"** — a
callback to his Dream Phone video ([[2019-06-18-yt-M5NVwuyk2uM]]). Verdict: mostly great, but design and camera
prove specs aren't everything.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-09-13] **THE PREMISE — do paper specs translate?** "'A world-class spec sheet: **Snapdragon 855+, 12 GB,
  1 TB UFS 3.0, a 6.6" 120 Hz OLED, a 6,000 mAh battery, 48 MP dual cameras — with room for a headphone jack.**
  On paper this is the best phone you can buy in almost every metric. **So how does dominating a spec sheet
  translate to real-world use? Spoiler: pretty good.**'" The framing device.
- [2019-09-13] **Which specs translate (and which you can ignore).** "**Translate great:** the **6,000 mAh
  battery** ('the biggest I've ever used — never came close to killing it, 5 hrs SOT with half left at 10pm even
  at 120 Hz'), the 1 TB storage, and the **120 Hz display** ('the fastest mobile display I've ever seen'). **A
  spec to ignore:** '**48 MP** — you might assume it means good, but it's a **B/B-minus camera**; sharpness/color/
  white-balance are inconsistent, with real shutter lag.'" Concrete spec-by-spec skepticism.
- [2019-09-13] **HIGH-REFRESH ADVOCACY (explicit).** "'I've said it once and I'll say it again: **high refresh
  rate, all the things, please.**' 240 Hz touch response, super smooth/snappy." His recurring championing
  (cf. [[2019-09-26-yt-XRiUNPf-_-4]]).
- [2019-09-13] **FRONT-FACING SPEAKERS ADVOCACY.** "'In a world where headphone jacks are dying and being
  replaced by wireless audio, **dual front-facing stereo speakers are also an endangered species — I'd like to
  bring awareness to that.** There really isn't a great replacement; you just get worse speakers now.'" A stated
  value.
- [2019-09-13] **THE VERDICT — specs deliver, but design matters too.** "'The spec king on paper **does** deliver
  in real life — **and it also turns out design is very important.** I prefer something cleaner/more understated;
  the phone's Achilles heel is the sheer **size** (240 g, huge). Props to Asus for now offering a **near-stock Zen
  UI option** instead of forcing the heavy gamer skin.'" Specs alone don't make the dream phone.
