# NEW Mac Studio & Apple Event Reactions! (@mkbhd, 2022-03-09)

## Summary
Reactions to Apple's March event. The headline for him: **the Mac Studio + M1 Ultra** — "**this machine feels so
perfectly targeted; it's clearly built for someone like me**" (a creative pro in a studio). The **M1 Ultra is
essentially two M1 Max chips fused** (2.5 TB/s interconnect, up to a 20-core CPU / 64-core GPU / 128 GB unified).
His key question, answered live on stage: **is it a "Mac Pro mini" or a "Mac mini Pro"?** — John Ternus's "our
transition is nearly complete, with just one more product to go: Mac Pro" confirms it's a **Mac mini Pro** (a small
desktop at a tenth the price of the tower) — so **he's still waiting for the Mac Pro tower.** Also the **Studio
Display** ("everything the LG UltraFine wanted to be" — a repackaged 27" 5K iMac panel + an A13 for the webcam),
the **iPad Air (M1)** ("add Final Cut Pro, cowards"), and the iPhone SE. His posture throughout: impressed by the
graphs, but **"I'll test it myself."**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-03-09] **The M1 Ultra — two M1 Max fused.** "'The M1 Ultra is basically **two M1 Max chips fused together**
  (2.5 TB/s between the halves, 800 GB/s memory bandwidth) — a 20-core CPU (16 performance), a 48/64-core GPU, up
  to 128 GB unified memory. The new biggest, most powerful chip Apple's ever made, at the top of the lineup. (Shout
  out to Samsung for the "Ultra" name.)'"
- [2022-03-09] **The Mac Studio — "built for someone like me."** "'This machine feels so perfectly targeted — **oh,
  you're a creative professional in a studio? Okay, this is for you.** A Mac mini that ate another Mac mini (same
  7×7 footprint, 3.7" tall — half the internal volume is cooling). **It worries me a little because it reminds me
  of the trashcan Mac Pro** (air in the bottom, fans out the top) — but I trust they learned their lesson, since
  it's Apple silicon now, not a hot Intel chip.'"
- [2022-03-09] **"Mac Pro mini or Mac mini Pro?" — answered on stage.** "'My main question, which I tweeted during
  the event: **is this a "Mac Pro mini" (the end of the road) or a "Mac mini Pro"?** Ternus answered it — "our
  transition is nearly complete, with **just one more product to go: Mac Pro**" — so **it's a Mac mini Pro**, a
  small desktop at a tenth the tower's price that's just as capable. There's a reason the Mac Pro towers are still
  in their labs; the tower unlocks the very highest end and modularity. **That's the one I'm going to keep waiting
  for** — though a maxed Mac Studio should handle everything we do here.'"
- [2022-03-09] **The Studio Display — everything the LG UltraFine wanted to be.** "'A 27" 5K display like a junior
  Pro Display XDR — basically **they popped the panel out of a 27" iMac and wrapped Apple features around it** (a
  12 MP Center Stage webcam, six speakers, and a whole A13 Bionic just as the webcam's image processor). **It's
  everything the LG UltraFine 5K ever wanted to be** — pricey, but pretty unique (though the $400 height-
  adjustable stand upcharge is crazy).'"
- [2022-03-09] **The rest — and "I'll test it myself."** "'The **iPad Air got an M1** (the fastest chip in any
  tablet — but still **add Final Cut Pro, cowards**). The **iPhone SE** followed the formula (A15 in the iPhone 8
  body, $429). On the Mac Studio graphs (vs an RTX 3090 at 200 W less, etc.) — sounds sick, **but I'm going to be
  testing that for myself; we've got to see.**'"
