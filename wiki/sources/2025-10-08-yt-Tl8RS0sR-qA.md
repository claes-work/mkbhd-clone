# Google Pixel 10 Pro Fold Review: Not Compatible!

## Summary

The Pixel 10 Pro Fold review, framed by a thesis he's "been thinking about a lot lately":
**the concept of a Pixel and the concept of a folding phone are fundamentally at odds.** A
Pixel is defined as never-the-best-hardware-but-clever-software-and-a-good-deal; a foldable
is inherently niche, premium, bleeding-edge $2,000 hardware. So a ~$2,000 folding Pixel is
"opposites." He likes it (as a Pixel fan and a foldable fan) but can recommend it to very
few people. Standouts: the new gearless hinge, surprisingly strong battery life, and the
first fully IP68 foldable; weaknesses: chunky/heavy ergonomics, a let-down Tensor G5, and
half-step-down cameras.

## Key claims

- **[2025-10-08]** The framing: **"the concept of a Pixel and the concept of a folding phone
  are not actually really compatible, and that's okay."** A Pixel = clever software + a good
  deal, never the best hardware; a foldable = niche, premium, bleeding-edge $2,000 hardware.
  "They're opposites." (quote)
- **[2025-10-08]** He likes it "as someone who really likes Pixels and someone who likes
  folding phones," but "there are very few people I can recommend this to." (quote)
- **[2025-10-08]** The biggest change is the **new gearless, much-smaller hinge** — thinner,
  more even bezel, satisfying resistance. "Shout out to the hinge engineers, the hingeneers."
  It also enables the **first foldable ever to be fully IP68** dust/water resistant.
  (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-10-08]** Displays: bigger 6.4" cover screen, brighter (3,000 nits — "one of my
  favorite trends in phones this year"); 8" inner screen; still a visible crease you learn to
  live with. (paraphrase)
- **[2025-10-08]** **Pixelsnap** (Qi2 magnets) again "elite" — snaps to any MagSafe accessory
  and even holds in a car dash despite the extra weight. (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-10-08]** **Surprise strength: battery life.** The thinner hinge freed internal room
  for a **5,015mAh** battery — used mostly closed he gets ~6h screen-on easily. "Worth it.
  Shout out again to the hingeneers." (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-10-08]** **Tensor G5 is a let-down power-wise** (as in the Pixel 10 review) — "nowhere
  near the most powerful chips," about to be lapped by the Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 — but
  decently efficient; paired with 16GB RAM for multitasking. (paraphrase)
- **[2025-10-08]** Weaknesses vs modern rivals: still a **chunky, heavy foldable** — "the
  ergonomics are right up there on the list of most important things about a foldable," and
  newer ultra-thin folds "just feel more pleasant to use" (lighter, thinner bezels, smaller
  creases); not silicon-carbon and doesn't charge fast. Cameras are ~last year's — "almost
  flagship quality... just a half step down"; the 10MP selfie cameras "not that great at all."
  (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-10-08]** The verdict via his standing lens: **"this is a really good phone in a
  vacuum, but we're not in a vacuum"** — in the competitive $2,000 segment people expect the
  absolute max hardware, and this "pretty clearly" isn't giving it. "I'll keep using this one
  though." (quote)
