# My FBI Declassified Story (@mkbhd, 2024-07-26)

## Summary
A story-time video (finally cleared to tell) about **the time the FBI and IRS visited the studio** over his **Escobar
Fold** exposé. Background: months earlier he'd published a video revealing the "Escobar Fold" scam — a company selling
a **$400 "gold folding phone"** ("to destroy Apple and Samsung") that was really **Samsung Galaxy Folds covered in gold
stickers**, shipped only to YouTubers expected to praise it while **scamming regular buyers** out of their money. In
**June 2020** (peak COVID, "just me and Andrew" in the then-new studio building furniture and recording the podcast),
**three agents flashed FBI/IRS badges** at the front desk — and **"Bob"** (the security guard) **held to their standing
rule**: never let anyone in asking for Marques; he has to come out and confirm. The agents wanted **every communication
he'd had with the company**; they **took the half-peeled Escobar Fold 2 and the scratched gold iPhone 11 Pro from his
videos as evidence** (in sealed "evidence" bags — which is why he'd said at that year's Smartphone Awards the phone was
"in the possession of the FBI"). He kept quiet for years at their request; since then the dominoes fell (PayPal then
Klarna dropped the company; ringleader **Olaf Gustafsson was arrested and imprisoned in Spain for fraud and money
laundering** — "basically for everything I talked about"), and the agents cleared him to tell it. His videos may yet
appear in US court documents. He signs off wanting to **get back into more investigative deep-dive videos** — and
teases it "wasn't the only encounter we've had with the FBI running this channel."

- [2024-07-26] **The Escobar Fold was a sticker-covered Samsung Fold scam.** "'The Escobar company was selling a $400
  gold folding phone to "destroy Apple and Samsung." What they were really doing — as I uncovered — was covering

  regular buyers' orders. Those people were getting scammed out of their money.'"
