# 2020 Tech I'm Ready For! (@mkbhd, 2019-12-31)

## Summary
His year-ahead "tech I'm ready for" for 2020 — foldables, high-refresh-everywhere, the Pixel 4a, Galaxy S11, the
2020 iPhone, 5G, the RED Komodo, and EVs. Also a rich **biography** wrap-up of 2019 (110 videos, the Waveform
podcast, and an undefeated ultimate-frisbee championship season).

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-12-31] **BIOGRAPHY — the 2019 scorecard.** "'2019 goal was **100 high-quality tech videos** (99 the year
  before); **this is video 110** — and I'm proud we didn't over-crank. We did Retro Tech, interviews with **Bill
  Gates, Will Smith, Satya Nadella**, the Smartphone Awards, the Blind Camera Test, and started the **Waveform
  podcast (already a top tech podcast per Apple).** And in ultimate frisbee, **Pride of New York won Pro
  Championships**, and the pro team **New York Empire went 15-0 undefeated to win New York's first AUDL
  championship.**'" Dense, datable biography.
- [2019-12-31] **"HIGH REFRESH RATE ALL THE THINGS" — his stated 2020 wish.** "'This was a **theme of 2019** —
  some ruffled feathers when the **OnePlus 7 Pro won MVP of my Smartphone Awards.** For 2020 I hope **every
  flagship (Samsung, Motorola, Apple) has at least a 90 Hz display** (some 120), and that it trickles down to
  mid-range.'" His signature advocacy, stated as a year-ahead demand.
- [2019-12-31] **The 2020 iPhone wishlist — boxy design, 120 Hz, USB-C.** "'Take everything I said last year and
  apply it with twice the confidence: I want a **boxy, industrial iPad-Pro-style design, a 120 Hz display, and
  USB-C** (if they'll do it on the iPad, maybe the iPhone). New design + high refresh + USB-C; the rest is a
  bonus.'" A dated, mostly-prescient forecast.
- [2019-12-31] **The other picks — Pixel 4a, Galaxy S11, 5G, EVs.** "**Pixel 4a** = a potential 'budget slayer'
  (excellent camera, headphone jack, no frills). **Galaxy S11** = early front-runner for phone of the year (5
  cameras, 120 Hz, 5,000 mAh, SD865); 'the **S10/S10+ were among 2019's biggest Smartphone-Awards snubs**.' **5G**:
  keep an eye on **low/mid-band** rollouts (more than mmWave headlines). **EVs**: Model Y is ahead of schedule;
  Plaid tri-motor Model S end of 2020; the **Roadster likely slips to 2021** ('I never expect Tesla to be on
  time')."
- [2019-12-31] **PRODUCTION — the RED Komodo is his most-anticipated camera.** "'Taking a page from Dave2D: the
  **RED Komodo** (softball-sized, 6K, Canon RF mount, weather-sealed) will be **my car-video and travel camera** —
  I'll probably wait on the Blackmagic Pocket 6K I was considering.'" A datable production-gear intention.
