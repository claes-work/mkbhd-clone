# Glass is glass

## Summary

A short "teach-the-mechanism" media-literacy piece on the recurring keynote claim that a new
phone is "3–4× more scratch/shatter resistant." Marques's point: **scratch resistance and
shatter resistance are inversely related** — harder glass resists scratches but is more
brittle (shatters), and vice versa — so you can't dramatically improve both at once. That's why
Gorilla Glass and Ceramic Shield **alternate** their headline claims year over year (scratch
one year, shatter the next). The claims aren't lies, but they're "misleading at best," and the
takeaway is a refrain: **"glass is still glass."**

## Key claims

- **[2026-04-24]** Every keynote has a line like "**4× more shatter resistant**" one year, "**3×
  more scratch resistant**" the next. Not lies, but **"misleading at best, because shatter resistance
  and scratch resistance are inversely related — they work against each other."** (quote)
- **[2026-04-24]** The mechanism (a slider): **harder glass = more scratch-resistant but more brittle
  (shatters); softer glass = less brittle but scratches easier.** "It's essentially impossible to
  dramatically improve both at the same time." (paraphrase)
- **[2026-04-24]** He went back through **Corning's Gorilla Glass** claims (now on gen 9, since the
  2007 iPhone) and they've been **"essentially alternating between big improvements in shatter
  resistance and big improvements in scratch resistance the entire time."** So while headlines imply
  one ever-rising curve, there are really **two alternating curves.** "It is very clever." (paraphrase)
- **[2026-04-24]** It still **scratches at level 6 (deeper grooves at 7)** because **it's still glass**,
  and everyday **sand is quartz — harder than glass — so it still cuts.** "No glass is immune to it
  yet." (paraphrase)
- **[2026-04-24]** Same for **Apple's Ceramic Shield**: gen 1 (iPhone 12) was "4× more shatterproof";
  **Ceramic Shield 2 (iPhone 17) is "3× more scratch resistant" — "I wonder why."** Good, but not
  invincible. (paraphrase)
- **[2026-04-24]** Crucially, the shatter claim **conflates the glass with the design.** Independent
  drop tests often *do* verify Apple's "4× more shatter resistant," but **"attributing this all to
  the glass would be silly"** — the **shape/thickness/material of the bezels and flat-vs-curved edges**
  matter as much or more. The **iPhone 12's return to square sides** (from the rounded 11) "had a huge
  impact," yet Apple "gets to loop all of that into their 4× claim. No asterisks." (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2026-04-24]** The unsung glass details that don't make headlines: the **oleophobic (fingerprint-
  rejecting) coating** and the **anti-reflective coating.** The refrain: next time you see the claim,
  **"just remember that glass is still glass."** (quote)
