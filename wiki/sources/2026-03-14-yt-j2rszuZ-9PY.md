# Reviewing Everything on my Desk! (2026)

## Summary

A tour and rating of every item on Marques's daily desk — **a major biography/gear source**,
since it's the equipment he's used longest and knows best (some for over a decade). Opens with
his standing **"tools are just tools, but knowing them cold matters"** principle (the sculptor
and the chisel). Documents his exact rig — the Xdesk (Next Desk) Air Pro, a Herman Miller
Embody, the aging Mac Pro (soon to be replaced), dual Pro Display XDR, Yamaha HS8s, Sennheiser
HD 650s — with candid scores, several disclosures, and the reasons behind each choice.

## Key claims (biography/gear beats marked ⬥)

- **[2026-03-14]** The framing: **"the tools are just tools… but if that artist uses the same
  chisel for 10 years straight, they're going to know it really well."** (quote)
- **[2026-03-14]** ⬥ **Desk: the Xdesk (formerly Next Desk) Air Pro** — an ~8ft × 3ft, quarter-inch
  aluminum sit-stand top he got "nearly a decade ago," rock-solid; "my forever desk." **9/10** (one
  gripe: hold-to-set height). ⬥ **Chair: the Herman Miller Embody** — "one of the best investments
  I've ever made"; had one in college. **10/10.** (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2026-03-14]** ⬥ **Computer: the M2 Ultra Mac Pro** (bought ~6 years ago, "~$12–13k"; 24-core /
  76-core GPU / 192GB / 8TB) — kept only for **one PCI card: an OWC ~64TB SSD for all live backups**.
  Now aging (unupdated since 2023). **"This is going to be replaced pretty soon"** — hoping for an
  **M5 Ultra Mac Studio** + a Thunderbolt OWC backup SSD. **4/10.** (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2026-03-14]** ⬥ **Displays: dual Pro Display XDR** ($5k + $1k stand + $1k nano-texture = ~$7k
  each), 60Hz, no webcam/speakers — "ridiculously niche." The nano-texture is effective in his
  window-walled studio but **impossible to clean**; the whole studio uses them so color matches. He
  wishes they were higher-refresh for non-video work. **8/10.** (paraphrase)
- **[2026-03-14]** ⬥ **Webcam: the Opal C1** (plugged into the display's port) — he **won't review it
  because he invested in the company.** (a disclosure/conflict note.) ⬥ Grovemade shelf **10/10** (but
  "you could replicate it way cheaper"). (paraphrase)
- **[2026-03-14]** ⬥ **Audio: an external DAC / volume knob** (Thunderbolt) whose software is "really
  buggy" — **4/10**; **Yamaha HS8** active near-field monitors (8" woofer to 40Hz, no subwoofer needed;
  "iconic to my desk"; a quirk: imperfect shielding so a nearby phone buzzes before a call) **9/10**;
  **Sennheiser HD 650** open-back headphones (worn all day so he can hear the team while editing)
  **8/10.** (paraphrase)
- **[2026-03-14]** ⬥ **Chargers**: a Belkin 3-in-1 (Apple Watch Ultra hot-swaps) **6/10**; an old
  **Oppo AirVOOC** fast wireless charger with a OnePlus cable **8/10**. **Input**: a Magic Trackpad +
  a mouse used together "because of how I've edited in Final Cut Pro for a decade"; the **Rainey 75
  Light** keyboard (with an **artisan MKBHD keycap**) — "everything I know about keyboards is because
  of **Andrew** telling me to try things" **8/10**; the **Logitech MX Master 4** (name engraved; sent
  early to test; silent, haptics) **9/10**; the **Artisan Ninja FX mousepad** ("imported from Amazon
  Japan for ~$75" when his wore out) **9/10.** (paraphrase)
- **[2026-03-14]** ⬥ On the desk's soft mat: **"the phones I haven't wiped yet that I'm still testing,
  my table-tennis paddle, whatever car keys of a car I'm testing, my Ridge wallet."** (quote)
- **[2026-03-14]** The recurring principle: he acknowledges "there are probably technically better"
  options for the monitors/speakers/headphones, "**but I know these so well**" — familiarity over
  spec-chasing. (quote)
