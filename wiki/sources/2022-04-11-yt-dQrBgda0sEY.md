# The SteamDeck is Incomplete But Fun! (@mkbhd, 2022-04-11)

## Summary
A review of the $399 Valve Steam Deck. His verdict: **there aren't a lot of people he'd recommend it to — but those
people will have a lot of fun.** The key reframe: **it's not "a Switch on steroids" — it's a full PC shrunk down to
be portable**, meant to play your whole Steam library anywhere. The joy is real: **playing games in places you
could never play them before** (a couch, a flight, a waiting room). But it's **"incomplete"** — game compatibility
is a lottery (the "Great on Deck"/"Verified" tiers exist because many games' controls don't map, and some are
outright unsupported — Apex, PUBG, Destiny 2), the numbers are modest (7" 1280×800 60 Hz), and the **battery is
"closer to 2 hours than 8" far more often.** Great ergonomics and a huge control set. He's **rooting for it** (Valve
has a spotty hardware history), and it's already getting frequent updates + a hardcore community.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-04-11] **Not a Switch on steroids — a shrunk PC.** "'The natural reaction is "a Switch on steroids," but
  it's much more than that — **a mini PC inside, meant to play any game in your Steam library wherever you want.**
  The challenge is those games were designed for different control schemes, so the Deck adapts to as many as
  possible.'"
- [2022-04-11] **The joy — playing games in new places.** "'For me the reward is **playing games in places I never
  could before** — Dirt Rally on a couch, NBA 2K on a flight, anywhere you can catch a spare hour. There's a bit
  of latency you calibrate for (you won't be as good as on a PC), **but that's outweighed by the fun of unlocking a
  level of play you couldn't access before.**'"
- [2022-04-11] **"Incomplete" — the compatibility lottery.** "'These two games work great **because their controls
  are already mapped for a controller** — but the touchscreen/touchpads map poorly. That's why there's a **"Great
  on Deck" section, and "Verified" vs "Playable" vs unsupported tiers** — as of now **Apex, PUBG, Destiny 2, Fall
  Guys are completely incompatible.** In that sense it's pretty rough.'"
- [2022-04-11] **The modest numbers + the battery.** "'A 7" 1280×800 60 Hz screen is fine; great ergonomics and
  tons of controls (thumbsticks, touchpads, 4 grip buttons) on an impressively light 688 g body. **Get the version
  with more storage (the base 64 GB is tiny and slow — a microSD works at normal performance). The one to watch is
  the battery — rated 2–8 hours, and you'll hit closer to 2 far more often** (bring the 45 W charger everywhere).'"
- [2022-04-11] **Rooting for it — know who it's for.** "'Valve isn't known for great hardware (the Steam
  Controller, Steam Link, Steam Machines were discontinued), and it's unclear how long the Deck lasts — **but I'm
  rooting for it.** It's a full portable PC (plug in a monitor/keyboard and it runs Linux; there's even room inside
  for an AirTag). **Not a lot of people are in the group I'd recommend it to — but if you want that
  play-anywhere experience, check it out, even if half the games don't work yet.**'"

## Notable verbatim quotes
