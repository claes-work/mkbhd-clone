# AirPods 2: Literally Everything New! (@mkbhd, 2019-03-26)

## Summary
A short "everything new" on the 2nd-gen AirPods — "the safest 2nd-gen Apple product in a long time." All internal
(the H1 chip + a wireless charging case), zero external change. Includes a couple of sharp reads: the Lightning
hint about the next iPhone, and the "if it ain't broke" strategy.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-03-26] **"The most popular wireless headphones in the world" (not an exaggeration).** "They're everywhere.
  On a recent flight a flight attendant recognized me and her one tech question was **'when are AirPods 2 coming
  out?'**" A dated read on their ubiquity.
- [2019-03-26] **What's new — all internal.** The **wireless charging case** (Qi, up to 5 W — "or charge them on a
  friend's reverse-wireless Android phone"; LED moved outside; $79 alone as an upgrade); the **H1 chip** (vs W1 —
  lower latency, faster pairing/switching, better mic, **up to 50% longer talk time, 2→3 hrs, same battery**);
  **always-on "Hey Siri"** (disable to save battery); Bluetooth 5.0. "The **most un-Apple move in a long time** —
  **dimensionally identical, no way to tell if someone's wearing the new ones; same exact sound (Apple confirmed).**"
- [2019-03-26] **PREDICTION — the Lightning hint.** "It's a **kind of hint that the new iPhone this year will
  probably still be Lightning, not USB-C** — they'd be moving accessories to USB-C now if they planned to switch."
  A sharp read of the accessory tea leaves.
- [2019-03-26] **The AirPower troll.** "The box **still name-drops AirPower** on the side — come on, Apple, you
  troll." (AirPower never shipped.) A characteristic aside.
- [2019-03-26] **"The safest 2nd-gen product in a long time — if it ain't broke."** "**AirPods 1.0.0.0.1** — super
  simple. But if you suddenly found yourself with the **most popular wireless headphones in the world, it's an
  'if it ain't broke, don't fix it' strategy** — a smart, safe move." Ties his no-risk-is-the-curse / incremental-
  is-a-compliment thread. (Personal note: "**AirPods never really fit my ears — they fall out when I move** — so
  I'm curious about the leaked Beats truly-wireless.")

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "This is the most un-Apple move in a long time — there's no way to tell if someone's wearing the new AirPods.
> They're dimensionally exactly the same, and the sound is exactly the same. Apple confirmed it to me."
> "This might be a hint that the new iPhone later this year will probably also be Lightning, not USB-C — they'd
> start moving accessories to USB-C now if they were planning that."
> "It's the safest second-gen Apple product in a long time — AirPods 1.0.0.0.1. But if it ain't broke, don't fix
> it — you've suddenly got the most popular wireless headphones in the world."
