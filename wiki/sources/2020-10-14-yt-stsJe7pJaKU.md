# Google Pixel 5 Impressions: A New Strategy? (@mkbhd, 2020-10-14)

## Summary
First look at the $699 Pixel 5 — Google's highest-end phone this year, but a **"flagship but not a flagship"**
(Snapdragon 765G, 90 Hz, no XL). The durable frame: **these aren't "trade-offs," they're intentional cuts** (from
$900 last year down to $700), and the strategy reads as **Google's admission it can't compete in the $1,000
flagship space** — so it's retreating to a $700 phone it can do well. Some cuts are genuinely good.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-10-14] **"A flagship, but not a flagship."** "'This is the highest-end phone Google's putting out (no
  Pixel 5 XL — this is it), **but it has a 765G instead of the top chip, 8 GB, 128 GB non-expandable, 90 Hz
  instead of 120.** The most interesting part is the **$699 price** in a world of $1,000–$2,000 phones. Will I
  even notice the missing flagship bells and whistles?'"
- [2020-10-14] **"CUTS, not trade-offs" — the reframe.** "'I'm not going to call them trade-offs — **I'll call
  them cuts, intentional cuts.** Last year's high-end was $900; this is $700. **They've made cuts.**'" A pointed
  vocabulary choice.
- [2020-10-14] **THE STRATEGY READ — Google admits it can't compete at $1,000.** "'A viewer asked if the rest
  of the Pixel would be competitive without the cameras — **I think that's part of why Google is leaving the
  $1,000 flagship space. The $900–$1,000 phone thing the last few years didn't compete or sell well; this is
  their admission they can't compete up there and need to try a $600–$700 phone and do well there.**'" A
  promotable read on Google's mobile pivot.
- [2020-10-14] **The GOOD cuts — Soli and the squeeze are gone (and we got things back).** "'**Recycled-aluminum
  unibody** (soft-touch, feels like hard plastic, still has wireless + reverse-wireless charging via a cutout,
  plus water resistance). **The Soli radar and squeeze-for-Assistant are cut — a good cut** — and we got a
  **fast rear fingerprint reader** (the safest, best place) and **thin uniform bezels — the best-looking front
  of a Pixel in years.** Cutting Soli also made room for a **bigger 4,080 mAh battery in a smaller body.**'"
- [2020-10-14] **The camera — Google doubles back on "ultrawides are fun but telephotos are more useful."** "'Same
  trusty 12 MP sensor, now **joined by an ultrawide — they doubled back on the "ultrawides are fun but telephotos
  are more useful" stance.** The promised gains are for video (a historic Pixel weak point) — mostly heavy
  stabilization so far. The rest of the world is catching up to the Pixel formula, so I want to see if they
  stayed ahead.'"
- [2020-10-14] **The lingering wish + the neural-core cut.** "'I still wish they'd made a **beefier flagship
  version** with 120 Hz and the biggest chip. Also **no Pixel Neural Core** now — so a bit more time to snap and
  process photos (night sight stitching), which might not feel as premium.'"
