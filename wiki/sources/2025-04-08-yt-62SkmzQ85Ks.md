# Polestar 1: The Most Interesting Car in the World

## Summary

Marques tests the **Polestar 1**, the brand's original 2+2 plug-in-hybrid grand tourer, and frames
it as **"one of the most unique cars of all time"** — every engineering and design choice is
deliberately contrarian. A turbo-AND-supercharged inline-4 powers the *front* wheels while electric
motors power the *rear* (~619hp, ~50mi EV range), a matte-gold carbon-fiber body, a sequential
gear selector, a manual spoiler, and a tiny trunk beside large rear seats. His verdict: gorgeous
and fascinating, but at **$160k "you could get a lot more car."** Fully Marques-narrated; a Denvix
tire-inflator ad is read mid-video (flagged, not review content).

## Key claims

- **[2025-04-08]** Polestar's naming is a mess ("bet you don't know the difference between the
  Polestar 1/2/3/4/5 off the top of your head… they're not good at names"), but the Polestar 1 "is the
  one that started it all." (quote)
- **[2025-04-08]** **Deliberately contrarian powertrain**: turbocharged + supercharged (dual
  forced-induction) inline-4 drives the **front** wheels; electric motors + a big **34kWh** battery
  drive the **rear**; ~619hp / 738 lb-ft, 0–60 ~3.7s at ~5,000lb, ~50mi EV-only range. "Every single
  choice sounds like they were trying to do something no one else would do." He thinks the reversed
  layout (engine front, motors rear) is "just a little weird — switch it and it'd be better in every
  performance way." (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-04-08]** Design highlight: matte-gold carbon-fiber body, 21" black wheels, real vents +
  tailpipes, active spoiler. **"I really like the look of this car… it has the aesthetic highlight."**
  (quote)
- **[2025-04-08]** Quirks (Doug-DeMuro-flavored): **sequential gear selector** ("you can't go straight
  to reverse — bop-bop for neutral then reverse"); **manual spoiler** you raise with a button and can't
  park raised; a 2+2 layout with tiny trunk ("golf clubs won't fit… one-and-a-half suitcases"); he
  literally gets **trapped in the rear seat** filming ("this is where I'll spend the rest of my days").
  (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-04-08]** Sound system: Bowers & Wilkins, **"A-tier, not quite S-tier"** — the reliable Volvo/
  Polestar B&W signature. (quote)
- **[2025-04-08]** ⚠️ Software: "not great… old-school, outdated — you'll immediately want CarPlay/
  Android Auto and never use the built-in again." (quote)
- **[2025-04-08]** Verdict: efficient and fun, one of the best-looking cars he's driven, but **"$160,000
  — for all that money you could get a lot more car"** (Corvette Z06, 911, M8 Competition, a Model S
  Plaid + Model 3 Performance). "You've got to really want that uniqueness." (quote)
