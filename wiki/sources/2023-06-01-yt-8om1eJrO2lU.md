# RAZR+ Impressions: Is This What You Wanted? (@mkbhd, 2023-06-01)

## Summary
A hands-on with the **Motorola RAZR+ (2023)**, which — unlike the incremental flip-phone updates lately — **"feels
like a big jump forward... the second generation of these flipping phones,"** entirely because of its **giant 3.6"
144 Hz outside cover display** that goes nearly corner-to-corner (even wrapping pixels around the cameras). The rest
is solid upper-mid-range ($999; Snapdragon 8+ Gen 1, a subtle-crease inner 6.9" 165 Hz LTPO display, a bigger 3,800
mAh battery). The outer screen is a **"super screen"** — you can run basically any app, make calls, type, navigate,
pay, even play games or watch YouTube on it. But he raises a genuinely thoughtful counter-question: **is that
actually what flip-phone buyers want?** The big hot-dog folds are productivity monsters, but **"it's also kind of
nice to have a barrier between using your phone and not using your phone"** — a small flip lets you clear a
notification without getting **"sucked into scrolling TikTok endlessly"** — and now you *can* get sucked in on the
outside screen. For him, more functionality is better, but he wonders if it's high on the typical flip buyer's
priority list. Motorola also announced a **cheaper plain RAZR** with a tiny postage-stamp outer screen (likely
$599–799) — more room for a bigger battery/camera — which "just seems to make a lot of sense."

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2023-06-01] **This feels like the second generation of flip phones.** "'A lot of these foldables have felt like a
  small bump. This one feels like a big jump — like we're leaping into the second generation of these flipping
  phones — obviously because of the giant outside display.'"
- [2023-06-01] **The outside screen is a "super screen."** "'It's a 3.6" 144 Hz display covering nearly the whole
  outside. You can do basically anything on it — make and receive calls with the phone closed, a full keyboard, a
  calculator, Google Maps, gpay, even games Motorola built and YouTube in a weird square aspect ratio. There's
  basically no limit — it's a super screen.'"
- [2023-06-01] **But is a barrier between using and not using your phone actually nice?** "'The big hot-dog folds are
  productivity monsters; these little flips have always been smaller and more regular. It's kind of nice to have a
  barrier between using your phone and not — if there's a notification you can clear it, but I won't get sucked into
  scrolling TikTok endlessly because I'd have to open the phone. Now you can get sucked into TikToks on the outside
  screen. For me, more functionality is better — but I wonder if the flip buyer has that high on their priority
  list.'"
- [2023-06-01] **The cheaper plain RAZR makes sense.** "'They also announced the plain RAZR — a tiny postage-stamp
  outer screen like the earliest flips, slightly lower specs, likely meaningfully cheaper (I'm guessing $599–799).
  Without the huge outer screen it has room for a bigger battery (4,200 mAh) and a bigger 64 MP camera — which just
  seems to make a lot of sense.'"
