# OnePlus 7T Review: High Refresh, Low Price! (@mkbhd, 2019-09-26)

## Summary
Review of the $599 OnePlus 7T — "a Frankenstein between" the mid-range OnePlus 7 and the premium 7 Pro. The
headline is bringing the **90 Hz high-refresh display down to the cheapest phone that has one**, reinforcing his
high-refresh advocacy. Also notes OnePlus's steady price creep and the strong 30T fast charging.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-09-26] **THE HEADLINE — cheapest phone with high refresh; "the x-factor."** "'As far as I know the
  **cheapest phone with a high-refresh (90 Hz) display** — I'm glad they brought that down; it's becoming a
  signature characteristic. **The 90 Hz x-factor is so hard to switch back from.** High refresh is the most
  important thing they brought down from the Pro.' Ends: **'High refresh rate all the things, please.'**"
  (cf. [[2019-09-13-yt--Wwe5OW_jPE]]).
- [2019-09-26] **"A Frankenstein" — the positioning.** "'Right between the upper-mid-range (OnePlus 7) and the
  premium (7 Pro). Brings **down from the Pro**: the great haptic motor ('only matched on Android by the Pixel'),
  the in-glass fingerprint reader, the speakers. Keeps **mid-range** compromises: 1080p (not 1440p), a flat
  teardrop-notch screen (down with that), thicker bezels, and some off-axis tint.'"
- [2019-09-26] **The price-creep observation.** "'OnePlus has continued this **fascinating slow creep up in
  price** — but at **$599** everything they've combined here is on point.'" A recurring note on OnePlus's brand
  drift (cf. [[2019-06-21-yt-MOJ1_FtDGNs]]).
- [2019-09-26] **Two charging philosophies (no preference).** "'**Warp Charge 30T** (23% faster than before,
  70% from dead in 30 min — silly fast). Two philosophies: a **chunky big-battery phone** (never worry all day)
  vs a **trim phone with crazy fast charging** (plug in for 5-10 min, huge boost). I don't have a preference —
  it'd be great to have both.'"
- [2019-09-26] **Cameras: same as the 7 Pro + a genuinely useful macro mode.** "'Essentially the **same cameras
  as the 7 Pro** (no big improvement — and the 7 Pro's camera was the one part that held it back from being
  arguably perfect). New **super-macro mode** (2.5-8 cm) let me get close-ups **I couldn't even get with the
  iPhone 11.** And they **finally enabled 4K30 on the ultrawide** — "they've heard our feedback."'"
