# This 'Phone' is Bad... Which Makes it Better? (@mkbhd, 2024-08-30)

## Summary
A review of the **Boox Palma** — a phone-shaped **e-ink Android device** that "objectively is a bad smartphone, but
something nice is going on." It's the shape/size of a phone (octacore, 6 GB RAM, 6.1" e-paper screen) but **worse on
purpose**: no SIM tray / no cellular, no selfie camera, a rear "camera" that's just a low-res document scanner, near-
useless speakers. His framing: **smartphones are OP** (a marvel that crams computing, a great screen, fast internet,
and cameras into a pocket box) — but the downside is distraction/addiction, which has spawned a wave of **"dumb phone"
/ anti-distraction devices**. The Boox's trick vs. single-purpose e-readers (Kindle, Kobo): it **runs full Android with
the Play Store**, so you can sideload Kindle, Pocket/Readwise, Audible, **Spotify** — "if smartphones are OP up here and
single-purpose e-readers are limited down here, the Palma sits right in between: more capable than an e-reader, not as
capable as a phone, but just enough to be charming in a can't-get-sucked-into-a-TikTok-rabbit-hole way." E-ink perks:
**days-to-weeks battery** (e-ink only draws power on refresh), a front light, mappable page-turn buttons, and refresh
modes (HD → ultra-fast, which can even "legibly watch video, Harry Potter Daily Prophet style"). His honest verdict:
**he's not converting / not daily-driving it** (his colleague Ellis went to full lengths trying, "a lot of work, not
worth it — it misses the point") — it's **best as a secondary device you avoid smartphone tasks on**: an alarm clock
with no notifications, a distraction-free reader, a **"portable offline iPod you never watch YouTube on."** Why *this*
one became the movement's face over similar options (Hisense A9, Kobo Libra Color)? It's in the **Goldilocks zone of
just-enough missing features** — like the **ATH-M50s representing affordable good headphones or the GoPro representing
action cams**, "that's the Boox Palma, and that's why it exists."

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2024-08-30] **Objectively bad — and that's the point.** "'Objectively this is a bad smartphone: no SIM/cellular, no
  selfie camera, a rear camera that's just a document scanner, near-useless speakers. But if the mission is to spend
  less time on your actual smartphone, then all of those downsides are actually good things. You either get it or you
  don't.'"
- [2024-08-30] **Smartphones are OP — hence the anti-distraction wave.** "'The modern smartphone is a marvel — all that
  computing, a great screen, fast internet, and good cameras in a pocket box that lasts all day. The downside is it can
  be a bit much, distracting, addicting. That's spawned a wave of "dumb phone" / anti-distraction devices that can't get
  caught in a doom-scrolling loop because they have no apps or distractions.'"
- [2024-08-30] **It sits between an e-reader and a phone.** "'Unlike single-purpose e-readers (a Kindle only reads
  Amazon books), the Palma runs full Android with the Play Store — sideload Kindle, Pocket, Readwise, Audible, even
  Spotify as a zero-distraction music/podcast player. If phones are OP up here and e-readers are limited down here, the
  Palma is right in between: more capable than an e-reader, less than a phone, but just enough to be charming.'"
- [2024-08-30] **E-ink battery is days-to-weeks; refresh modes even do video.** "'E-ink only uses power when refreshing,
  so battery is in days — sometimes weeks for a light user. Refresh modes range from HD (best quality, most ghosting-
  free, slowest) to ultra-fast (smoothest, worst quality) — it gets quick enough to legibly watch video if you really
  wanted, a trippy Harry Potter Daily Prophet moving-newspaper vibe.'"
- [2024-08-30] **Not daily-driving it — it's a charming secondary device.** "'No, I'm not converting or daily-driving
  it — my colleague Ellis went to full lengths trying (carried a Wi-Fi hotspot everywhere) and it's a lot of work, not
  worth it; that misses the point. It's best as a secondary device you avoid smartphone tasks on: an alarm clock with no
  notifications, a distraction-free reader, a portable offline iPod you never watch YouTube on. That's charming. Why
  this one became the movement's face over the Hisense A9 or Kobo Libra Color? It's in the Goldilocks zone of just-
  enough missing features — like the ATH-M50s representing affordable good headphones, or the GoPro representing action
  cams.'"
