# RED Hydrogen One Unboxing! (Houdini Edition) (@mkbhd, 2018-08-31)

## Summary
An update/unboxing of the long-delayed **RED Hydrogen One** — the camera company's first phone, a year after the
Aug-2017 exclusive reveal. Notable for his candid read on **why a great camera company struggles to build a
phone**, and the unusual **Houdini** early-prototype program he's part of.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-08-31] **The honest read — "a camera company discovering how hard it is to build a good smartphone."** "It's
  so delayed it's silly. RED is a camera company **slowly discovering how hard it is to build a good phone in this
  really competitive landscape** — their development has been much slower than the competition." Fair, not a dunk —
  and a data point in his long RED relationship.
- [2018-08-31] **The Houdini program (an unusual model).** "A couple weeks ago they emailed pre-orders to join the
  **Houdini** program — beta/prototype testing where they send you one early and you bug-report and help build the
  phone as they go. **I haven't seen anything quite like that from a phone in a while.**" His unit is **serial 008
  of 300** prototypes. He treats it as day-one exploration, not a verdict.
- [2018-08-31] **The design — "it looks like another RED camera — a tool."** Big, unique, with the back **pins
  (for future modules)**, deep grip ridges, many front-sensor cutouts. "It doesn't have the sleek glass-sandwich
  look of other flagships — it looks like a device you'd use for a function." A distinctive, function-first read.
- [2018-08-31] **Who it's actually for.** "Last year's specs (SD835, 6 GB, but a 4000 mAh battery + expandable
  storage). **Most people who pre-ordered aren't buying it as a primary phone — they want a baller camera
  experience first, and a good software experience second.**" Frames the buyer honestly.
- [2018-08-31] **The holographic display + the caveat.** The "4-View" holographic screen "**doesn't show up on
  camera at all** — it just looks like pixelated content; in person it's really trippy, but **shooting content that
  takes advantage of it will be a challenge.**" Skeptical-but-curious. (Aside: a bandaged finger — "stitches, long
  story.")
