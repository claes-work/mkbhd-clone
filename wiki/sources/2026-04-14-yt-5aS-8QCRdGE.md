# The Unreleased Rollable Smartphone!

## Summary

Marques gets his hands (via dbrand) on the **unreleased LG rollable phone** — the "craziest
shot" LG had in the barrel right before shutting down its mobile division in 2021. He uses it
to retell **why LG died**: they made wildly creative phones (the Wing, the G8X dual-screen)
"without also having a steady moneymaker at the same time" — "it's why we love them, but
that's why they died." The rollable itself is a functional, near-shippable prototype that
three-finger-swipes from a 6.7" phone into a 7.4" mini-tablet, with genuinely thoughtful
software and over-engineered internals.

## Key claims

- **[2026-04-14]** **Why LG died: crazy phones, no steady moneymaker.** The last releases were
  the **LG Velvet and the Wing** (a swiveling T-shaped dual-screen); earlier, the **G8X** (a
  case with a built-in second display + a 180° hinge + pass-through charging). "All the
  engineering that went into making these feel intentional was insane" — but they never had a
  "real, solid, consistent moneymaker," ran out of money, and shut down mobile in 2021. **"It's
  why we love them… but that's why they died."** (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2026-04-14]** The concept: closed it's a normal-looking **6.7"** slab; a **three-finger
  swipe** rolls it open into a **7.4" squarish mini-tablet** "like a scroll or parchment." It
  plays a sound every time (to mask the motors — "about as effective as coughing to cover up a
  fart"; he finds the "engineer who had to find the perfect frequency to hide the motor" idea
  hilarious). (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2026-04-14]** The engineering: the extra screen is **hidden curving over the back** (clear
  glass, usable for a rear display / selfies with the main 64MP + 12MP ultrawide) — reminiscent
  of the **Xiaomi Mi Mix Alpha** concept; **pressure-sensitive volume "buttons"** and a rear
  power/fingerprint button (no room on the rails); polished **software tricks** (a blooming-
  flower unlock animation, a settings-app column that slides in on expand) — "it feels like
  they're really close to releasing this." (paraphrase)
- **[2026-04-14]** Hands-on findings: the **motors are strong** (it can push a 16" MacBook Pro
  across a table; squeeze it while open and it auto-closes to prevent damage + flashes an error);
  it **can pinch** small items (microfiber cloths); it's almost certainly **not water/dust
  resistant** (visible gear teeth and air gaps; the flexible display is constantly exposed);
  **less crease than a fold** but "not no crease" (a larger-radius wave); a 4,500mAh battery, 12GB
  RAM. **"None of this really matters because it's not a real phone."** (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2026-04-14]** Sourcing/credit: **dbrand got it to him** ("I don't ask questions"); **Zack
  (JerryRigEverything)** also got one and tore it down (two motors + three spring-loaded arms —
  "hilariously over-engineered"). (paraphrase)
- **[2026-04-14]** Takeaway: LG "got insanely far in the development process — functional hardware,
  working software, special features — they just didn't survive long enough to ship it." **"LG is
  the only one who would actually try to ship a phone like this"** — though **Samsung is rumored to
  still be working on a rollable.** (quote)
