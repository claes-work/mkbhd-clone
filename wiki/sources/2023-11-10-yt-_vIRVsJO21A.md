# Reacting to Apple's Shot on iPhone Event! (@TheStudio, 2023-11-10)

> ⚠️ ATTRIBUTION: **Hosted by Marques, Vin, and Brandon** reacting to Apple's Oct-2023
> "Scary Fast" keynote (revealed to be shot on iPhone 15 Pro). Attribution is blended among

## Summary
The studio dissects Apple's shot-on-iPhone keynote (iPhone sensor + ~$4M of rigs, lights,
Ronins, Company 3 color). The subject-relevant beat is Marques's take on what "shot on
iPhone" actually means and the value of fundamentals over gear.

## Key claims (Marques-attributed — blended, Marques-consistent)
- [2023-11-10] **Fundamentals over gear:** "if people oversimplify, what they'll get is 'to
  get a good video I need an iPhone.' The truth is you need to **get skilled at the
  fundamentals of shooting good video** — and when you do, **you can get good video out of a
  large variety of gear.** They have the best gear, so their footage looks the best. I don't
  think it takes away from it — **the barrier to entry is lower than ever before, and that's
  a win for everyone.**" (production-filmmaking, creator-business) — a core Marques stance
  on tech democratization.
- [2023-11-10] **What "shot on iPhone" really means:** technically only that the iPhone
  sensor (and glass) captured it — "the iPhone doesn't buy you a sky panel," a Techno Crane,
  Ronins, or Company 3's colorists ("you can literally color a turd to look like a flower").
  Notes they shot **log** to an external drive via USB-C using the **Blackmagic** app (better
  manual controls than Apple's stock app) — both things any iPhone owner can do. (production-
  filmmaking)
- [2023-11-10] **The challenge idea (Marques):** "Do you think we could shoot an **entire
  main-channel MKBHD video — talking head, B-roll, intro, robot shots — all on an iPhone and
  not reveal it until the end?** … I'm up for a challenge." (creator-business) — a seed for a
  future channel concept.
- [2023-11-10] **BIO:** "I just got back from a tournament" (frisbee); Andrew "about to
  leave and be a dad." The frame-rate nerd beat: the keynote was shot at **29.97 fps** ("the
  correct frame rate"). (biography, voice)

## Notable verbatim quotes (Marques — blended, Marques-consistent)
