# Driving The New Fastest Car Ever Made! (Rimac Nevera) (@mkbhd, 2023-08-18)

## Summary
A drive of the **$2.4M Rimac Nevera** (150 will be made) — the all-electric hypercar that is **"absolutely the
smackdown of gasoline cars that we knew was eventually coming, even if it didn't come from Tesla"** (the Roadster
2.0 is indefinitely delayed). Its numbers are incomprehensible — **23 world records** (0–60 in 1.74s, 0–100 in
3.2s, the quarter in 8.3s, plus "the record for most records broken in a day"); on the track he **gave a Porsche
911 Turbo S a two-second head start over 400 yards and walked it** ("nothing is supposed to walk a car like that").
Crucially these are **production-car** records — everything in a straight line beats a Bugatti Chiron. But the
review's real thesis is the **Jekyll-and-Hyde / multiple-personality factor**: a great EV can be **both** genuinely
soft, docile, and smooth (range mode drives "like a super-low Tesla Model 3 with incredible brakes") **and**, at
the turn of a knob, a vicious weapon (track mode: stiffer, sharper, the wing extends, an air brake deploys — "the
most direct, immediate control over exactly how fast you're going I've ever felt, more than the Plaid, more than any
gas car"). His conclusion: **"the future of cars is now proven to be electrified"** — every flagship supercar
(Corvette, Lamborghini, Ferrari, Bugatti, McLaren) is going electrified. "Speed is ruined for me forever."

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2023-08-18] **The smackdown of gas cars — even if it didn't come from Tesla.** "'The Tesla Roadster 2.0 was
  promised as the flagship smackdown to all gas cars, but it's indefinitely delayed. This Rimac Nevera is absolutely
  that smackdown — even if it didn't come from Tesla. You've been able to buy million-dollar gas supercars for years;
  now we get to see what an all-out, maximum-performance all-electric hypercar feels like.'"
- [2023-08-18] **23 production-car world records — and it walked a 911 Turbo S.** "'0–60 in 1.74s, 0–100 in 3.2s, 23
  world records including the record for most records broken in a day — and these are production-car records, faster
  in a straight line than a Bugatti Chiron. I gave a 640 hp Porsche 911 Turbo S a two-second head start over 400
  yards and it walked it. Nothing is supposed to walk a car like that.'"
- [2023-08-18] **The Jekyll-and-Hyde multiple-personality thesis.** "'The most impressive thing about this EV
  transition is the Jekyll-and-Hyde factor — these cars can have multiple personalities. In range mode it's
  shockingly calm, soft, and smooth, like a super-low Tesla Model 3 with incredible brakes. Turn the knobs to track
  mode and it becomes a weapon of speed and precision — the most direct, immediate control over exactly how fast
  you're going I've ever felt, more than the Plaid, more than any gas car. The lag is non-existent.'"
- [2023-08-18] **The future of cars is now proven electrified.** "'People will say they'd rather take the Chiron for
  the noise and soul — and a $2M exotic is about fun, so I get it. But the point is the future of cars is now proven
  to be electrified in some way: the newest Corvette, Lamborghini, Ferrari, Bugatti, and McLaren flagships are all
  electrified. That's what we've learned.'"
