# Nintendo Switch 2 Impressions: One Big Asterisk! (@mkbhd, 2025-04-03)

## Summary
A hands-on first look (a Nintendo NYC event) at the **Nintendo Switch 2** — which, almost a decade after the 2017
original, **"hit basically every single one"** of the wishlist items, with **one big asterisk overhanging it all.**
The upgrades: a bigger **7.9" 1080p VRR LCD up to 120 Hz** (HDR — but **not OLED**, so a Switch 2 OLED likely comes
later), new **magnetic Joy-Cons** (they slap on and align; **steel SR/SL buttons are what the magnets grip**; stronger
HD Rumble 2; and each Joy-Con doubles as a **mouse** via a side optical sensor for supporting games), **256 GB** faster
storage (microSD Express), and a **4K dock with a cooling fan.** The software theme is **"a much more social console"**:
**Game Chat** (the new C button — mic + live screen-sharing, shown deliberately laggy/low-res so Nintendo "didn't make
fake promises"), an optional **camera** that cuts your face out next to your gameplay, and **GameShare** (a host lets up
to three other Switches temporarily play a game they don't own). He tucks in a jab — **"you know how iPhone defenders
say you can't tell 60 from 120 Hz? high refresh and gaming go hand in hand."** **The big asterisk is pricing:** the
**$449 console feels reasonable** (inflation/tariffs/a decade later), but **$80–90 games are "pushing it"**, and — the
real miss — **the Welcome Tour tutorial-demo game is *paid*** ("Astro's Playroom and Wii Sports were free and
pre-installed — even charging for it at all seems like a huge miss; so many people won't experience it"). TBD on chip/
RAM, battery, and whether Joy-Con drift is fixed. Verdict: the signs point to another really good, well-priced,
versatile console (his "perfect airplane game console") — "I really like it."

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2025-04-03] **It hit every wishlist item — but the screen isn't OLED.** "'Almost a decade after the original, they
  hit basically every wishlist item. The biggest upgrade is a bigger 7.9" 1080p VRR LCD up to 120 Hz with HDR — but not
  OLED, so no inky blacks or OLED battery advantages; I think a Switch 2 OLED comes in a few years.'"
- [2025-04-03] **The magnetic, steel-buttoned, mouse-mode Joy-Cons.** "'The new Joy-Cons are so much better — bigger,
  magnetic (they slap on and auto-align), with steel SR/SL buttons that are what the magnets grip, and stronger HD
  Rumble 2. And each Joy-Con doubles as a mouse via a side optical sensor for supporting games — I did not see that
  coming, but it worked well. My one worry is the durability of the connector pins.'"
- [2025-04-03] **A much more social console.** "'The software theme is a much more social console: Game Chat (the new C
  button — mic + live screen-sharing, shown deliberately laggy and low-res so Nintendo didn't make fake promises), an
  optional camera that cuts your face out next to your gameplay, and GameShare (a host lets up to three other Switches
  temporarily play a game they don't own).'"
- [2025-04-03] **High refresh and gaming go hand in hand.** "'You know how iPhone defenders love to say people can't
  tell 60 from 120 Hz? High refresh and gaming go hand in hand — every game here takes advantage of the VRR up to 120,
  and a lot of people will appreciate the smoother games and UI.'"
- [2025-04-03] **The big asterisk is pricing — and the paid Welcome Tour is a huge miss.** "'The $449 console feels
  reasonable with inflation, tariffs, and a decade of updates. But $80–90 games are pushing it. And the real miss: the
  Welcome Tour tutorial-demo game is *paid* — Astro's Playroom and Wii Sports were free and pre-installed. Even
  charging for it at all seems like a huge miss; so many people won't experience it.'"
