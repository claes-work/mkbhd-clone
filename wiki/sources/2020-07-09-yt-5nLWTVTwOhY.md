# Can You Trust MKBHD? (@mkbhd, 2020-07-09)

## Summary
The definitive statement of his **review ethics and integrity policy** — prompted by the "how much did they pay
you?" comment culture. A landmark creator-values source: he lays out his hard rules, defines what a "review" is,
and explains how he works with brands.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated — his stated policies)
- [2020-07-09] **THE HARD RULES.** "'(1) **I have never and will never do paid reviews.** (2) **I have never and
  will never give a manufacturer an early look at a review before it goes live — every review, you're seeing it at
  the same time the manufacturer is.** (3) **There will never be a product review sponsored by the company that
  makes that product** — not a good fit for me (others can do it well, e.g. Peter McKinnon's Canon-sponsored R5
  review, but not me).'" The core integrity commitments.
- [2020-07-09] **What a "review" IS (a precise definition).** "'A tech **review is an evaluation of a product to
  figure out if you should buy it or not** — my experiences, opinions, recommendations. **Not every video is a
  review**: a first-impressions/hands-on isn't ("nice review" — it's not); a **sponsored video is never a review**
  (it's a product showcase). Reviews are in a **reviews playlist** and almost always say "review" in the title.'"
  A clear boundary that shapes how his catalog reads.
- [2020-07-09] **How he works with brands — sponsored segments vs. unpaid collaborations.** "'Two ways: **(a)
  sponsored segments** (a hand-selected paid message, always disclosed — I prefer these over YouTube's auto mid-
  rolls, which I mostly don't enable); **(b) brand collaborations** — any video that wouldn't be possible without a
  company's cooperation (the camera-robots studio visit, the **Kobe Bryant interview via a Nike partnership**, a
  phone sent two weeks early for a launch-day review). **Importantly, none of the collaborations are paid.** I've
  done exactly **one fully-sponsored video** ever (the Buick one) in 11 years / 1,000+ videos.'"
- [2020-07-09] **The truth-every-time stance.** "'If I say something positive, it's because I **genuinely think
  it's good**; negative, genuinely bad. **I have no concern about my relationship with a company changing** — I
  plan on saying the truth every time. **If a company gets mad and stops sending devices, that looks worse on
  them.** We're fortunate not to rely on being first — **if I don't get a product early, I buy it and review it
  when it arrives.**'" A promotable independence principle.
- [2020-07-09] **"I work for you" + don't get all your advice from one person.** "'I'm **bringing back disclosing
  provided-vs-purchased** in the description. You can accuse any reviewer of bias/preferences (calibrate to them) —
  **but you can't accuse me of being paid off.** And **you shouldn't get all your tech advice from one person** —
  it's a purchase decision; experiment with other opinions. **I work for you at the end of the day.**'" His core
  audience-first stance.
