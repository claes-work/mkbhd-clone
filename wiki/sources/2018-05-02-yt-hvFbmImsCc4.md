# LG G7 ThinQ Impressions! (@mkbhd, 2018-05-02)

## Summary
First look at the LG G7 ThinQ — "a V30 updated for 2018," a competent but reactive phone whose challenge is
standing out. Best material: his **remappable-button principle** (a fair rule he applies consistently to Bixby
AND Google), the "boombox" resonance-chamber speaker, and his growing **AI-buzzword fatigue**.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-05-02] **"A V30 updated for 2018" — reactive, not new.** "It doesn't really do anything we haven't seen,
  but it puts LG's own twist on things." Frames the whole phone. Also a small voice tell: "the name **LG G7 ThinQ**
  is terrible — I'm just going to call it **G7** for the rest of this video and probably forever."
- [2018-05-02] **The remappable-button principle (a consistency check on himself).** The G7 adds a **dedicated
  Google Assistant button** — "better than the Bixby button because it's Google, but here's the thing: **I didn't
  roast the Bixby button because Bixby is bad — I roasted it because it wasn't remappable.** This one **also isn't
  remappable.** No one wants a useless extra button. **LG, let us remap it — be the people's champ.**" A clean
  example of applying the same standard regardless of brand.
- [2018-05-02] **The "boombox" speaker — a genuinely novel bit.** The single speaker uses the phone's internal
  space as a **resonance chamber** — "**you can feel air moving** out of the bottom and the whole phone vibrating;
  ~40% louder." Real praise for an original idea (tempered: no stereo, easy to block).
- [2018-05-02] **LCD-not-OLED, for the super-bright mode.** LG went **LCD** with a boostable outdoor brightness
  toggle — "Samsung has something similar on OLED, and nobody does panels like Samsung, but **as long as what LG
  is doing is effective, I don't mind** — it worked outdoors." Pragmatic, evidence-first.
- [2018-05-02] **AI-buzzword fatigue.** "AI kind of feels like a **buzzword everyone's throwing around** now."
  The AI Cam recognizes ~18 scenes (like the P20 Pro) and suggests filters — "it seems very early; **no one really
  knows exactly what they want to do with AI and the camera** yet." A skeptical, wait-and-see read (pairs with the
  P20 Pro anti-auto-enhancement note).
- [2018-05-02] **LG's identity problem.** Keeps the **headphone jack + Quad DAC** (premium audio), the **wide-angle**
  second camera (LG sticking to their guns), micro-SD, IP68. But "**LG's biggest challenge is standing out** — none
  of these features quite break it from the pack. I think it'll fly under the radar again." A recurring, sympathetic
  LG diagnosis.
