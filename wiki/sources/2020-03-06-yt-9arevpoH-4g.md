# Oppo Find X2 Pro: One-Upping the Champ? (@mkbhd, 2020-03-06)

## Summary
A **sponsored** highlight (his word: "not a review") of the Oppo Find X2 Pro, framed as one-upping the S20 Ultra on
two fronts: the display and the cameras. Notable for proving the S20 Ultra's **120 Hz + 1440p** limitation was a
choice, and for a rare **praise-the-color** camera note.

> ⚠️ SPONSORED: Marques flags this video is sponsored by Oppo and is a "highlight video, not a review." His
> opinions are still his own, but treat the framing as promotional-adjacent.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-03-06] **120 Hz AT 1440p — proving the S20 Ultra's limit was a choice.** "'6.7" 1440p 120 Hz AMOLED — **and
  yes, all of those at the same time** (independent refresh + resolution). If you wondered whether the S20 Ultra's
  either/or was an SD865 limitation — **no; this phone has the same SD865 and enables both.**'" A pointed,
  cross-referenced correction ([[2020-03-03-yt--wOdL7HFNOs]]).
- [2020-03-06] **The camera NAILS color — a rare compliment.** "'Two things I've been happy with: **sharpness and
  color** — and **you don't hear me say "color" very often** about smartphone cameras. It **nails white balance**,
  tones, hues, skin tones — very accurate. (Dynamic range and exposure consistency are weaker.)' Give the Oppo
  image-processing team a pat on the back.'" A distinctive praise-the-color note.
- [2020-03-06] **The big-sensor problem recurs.** "'The 48 MP main is a big primary sensor again, so it'll suffer
  the **same big-sensor/big-aperture close-focus problems as the S20 Ultra** — but on further subjects the natural
  blur is solid. A high-quality, fast camera. Night mode is really good.'"
- [2020-03-06] **The gripes + the software surprise.** "'Still **curved edges** (content bleeds over — accidental
  presses; I'd prefer flat). But **ColorOS 7.1 is a surprise — much cleaner**, stock Google apps/keyboard by
  default, multi-user mode, an excellent haptic (largest x-axis motor in an Android phone). **65 W SuperVOOK** (at
  the cost of wireless charging — "OnePlus-style doubling down on fast charging").'"
- [2020-03-06] **THE TAKEAWAY — "overkill specs are the new norm."** "'A dark-horse phone making waves on the
  things people care about (camera, screen, software) — and it brought me to a realization: **overkill specs are
  like the new norm in high-end smartphones now; it's just what we've come to expect.**'" A dated state-of-the-
  market observation.
