# The Only REAL 'Pro' Smartphone?! (@mkbhd, 2021-01-27)

## Summary
A look at the $2,500 Sony Xperia PRO — "the only phone that deserves the word Pro" because it's **an actual
professional tool** (an HDMI viewfinder + 5G live-streaming rig for a mirrorless camera), not just an upscale
everyday phone with "Pro" in the name. The durable ideas: **"pro" is a marketing word on most phones**; **when you
make something no one else makes, who's to say it's overpriced?**; and the phone is **the ultimate expression of
the "why people don't buy Sony" thesis** — technically brilliant, but the narrowest target demographic ever (~30
NFL-sideline photographers).

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-01-27] **"The only real Pro phone" — an actual professional tool.** "'Hot take: **this is the only phone
  that deserves the word "Pro."** iPhone 12 Pro, Mate 40 Pro, OnePlus 8 Pro — **those just use "Pro" to sell you
  upscale everyday phones. This is an actual professional tool:** a micro-HDMI port, a ruggedized polycarbonate
  jacket, and four beamforming 5G antennas — designed to be a **primary 4K viewfinder for a Sony mirrorless (or
  any HDMI camera).**'"
- [2021-01-27] **What it does — 4K viewfinder + 5G live-streaming + power bank/FTP.** "'Plug it into the camera's
  HDMI and get a 4K feed on the phone; the 5G workflow lets you **live-stream full-frame quality to Twitch/YouTube
  without being tied to a computer** ("that awesome freeing feeling"); USB-C makes the phone a **power bank + FTP
  uploader** for live-blogging photos straight to the truck.'"
- [2021-01-27] **The inelegance — it's a first-gen pro rig.** "'It's not elegant: you need a cage, the weight
  distribution is off, the HDMI feed disables the camera's touchscreen (no touch-to-focus), there's no focus
  peaking/false color, and the 21:9 feed doesn't fill the display. A few minutes to set up.'"
- [2021-01-27] **"When you make something no one else makes, who's to say it's overpriced?"** "'As a phone with a
  few tricks, $2,500 is crazy high. But **if you've ever shopped for a thin 4K OLED viewfinder with all-day
  battery that charges your camera and has 5G — nothing else does that, so $2,500 seems like a deal.** A SmallHD
  on-camera monitor that's not even 4K or OLED is $3,000. **When you make something no one else makes, there's no
  equivalent to compare it to — who's to say it's overpriced?**'" A promotable pricing-of-the-unique framework.
- [2021-01-27] **THE THESIS PAYOFF — the narrowest demographic ever; the Sony imbalance taken to the extreme.** "'This
  takes the enthusiast-features-at-the-expense-of-the-everyday imbalance farther than any phone I've seen. **My
  theory: there are about 30 people in the target demographic — NFL-sideline photographers sending live video/photos
  to the truck.** Even as a professional, I'd take a brighter SmallHD monitor with waveforms/false color over the
  5G. **It falls perfectly into my "why people don't buy Sony phones" thesis** — technically brilliant, but the
  demographic is too small.'" (Cf. [[2020-10-05-yt-j7QwJ-M_GAI]].)
