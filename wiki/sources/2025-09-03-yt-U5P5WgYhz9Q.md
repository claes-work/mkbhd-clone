# Lucid Gravity Review - Will this save Lucid?

## Summary

Marques does the walkaround/living-with review of the **Lucid Gravity** (Lucid's first SUV,
aimed squarely at the Model X), then hands the driving-dynamics section to co-host **Miles**.
His verdict on the parts he covers: Lucid's signature strengths (engineering, efficiency,
packaging, storage, and genuinely good software) are all here — though the real-world range
falls short of the 450mi headline. Miles's driving impressions are quarantined. (⚠️ contains an
Insta360 sponsor read.)

## Key claims — SUBJECT-ATTRIBUTED (Marques)

- **[2025-09-03]** Lucid's first SUV ("SUVs are very popular in the US… this might turn over a new
  leaf for them"). "A lot of the things you expect from a Lucid are still here — the engineering, the
  efficiency, the packaging." A bit "minivan-looking" (not a bad thing for a functional three-row).
  (paraphrase)
- **[2025-09-03]** **Range reality**: the 450mi headline "feels impossible" alongside 828hp — and in
  daily driving **he never got near 450** (best ~386mi at 100% on the bigger wheels). "Expectations
  are the thief of joy… but this is still a very aerodynamic vehicle." First Lucid with a **NACS
  port** (the Airs still don't have it). (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-09-03]** **Packaging/storage praise** (Lucid's hallmark): a huge power frunk + a massive
  clamshell rear opening; folds to **the same interior storage as a Cadillac Escalade IQ but 3 feet
  shorter**; a laptop tray in the second row; decent third row ("slightly better than the R1S, worse
  than the Escalade IQ"). (paraphrase)
- **[2025-09-03]** **Software praise**: "really good and intuitive" — two separate screen environments
  (whatever you do on one never interrupts the other, "the opposite of old Lucids"), physical volume/
  HVAC buttons, a programmable glovebox button. **One gripe: no CarPlay out of the box** (supposedly
  coming via OTA). The steering-wheel touch pads are "a cool high-tech thing I appreciated in my
  hands-on and then ended up not using much." (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-09-03]** ⚠️ **Insta360 X5 sponsor read** (a 360 camera for car creators — 8K, bigger 1/1.28"
  sensor). Sponsored content, not an unpaid verdict. (paraphrase)

## Context (co-host Miles — QUARANTINED, not persona data)

- **[2025-09-03]** Marques hands off "to Miles for more in-depth thoughts on driving dynamics." Miles's

  vehicle to have both."
- "Expectations are the thief of joy. I shouldn't be all mad that this doesn't get 450."
- "[It has] the same amount of interior storage space as the Cadillac Escalade IQ, but 3 feet shorter."
