# iPad Killer or Clone? (OnePlus Pad) (@mkbhd, 2023-05-26)

## Summary
An expanded take on the **OnePlus Pad**, framed by the reality that **US tablets are "basically just iPads and
everything else"** — friends **won't switch off the iPad no matter how good the hardware**, because it's the
iMessage/FaceTime/ecosystem lock-in, not the specs. So taking users from the iPad is an uphill battle, and OnePlus
runs **three strategies. (1) Copy the iPad** — a near-identical pencil (magnetic, inductive charging, pairing
animation), a Magic-Keyboard-style folio with the iPad's exact cursor mechanic, quad speakers in the same spots, a
Center-Stage clone ("Limelight") — and his principle: **"I don't mind when products copy each other, as long as they
copy the good stuff."** **(2) Undercut the iPad** — $479/128 GB vs the iPad Air's $599/64 GB, achieved by trimming
the right things (a mid Dimensity 9000, no fingerprint reader, a dimmer 500-nit screen, a cheaper keyboard). **(3)
One-up the iPad** — objectively better in spots: a **144 Hz** display, a landscape front camera, an iPad-Pro-sized
battery with **67 W charging**, clever split-screen extras. But it's **still an Android tablet** — no ecosystem, few
optimized tablet apps (so 144 Hz rarely actually engages, cameras are meh), and it ships on Android 13 — **"never
buy a product based on potential future software updates."** Verdict: a good tablet for a good price, up against an
"impossible task" (same fate as the Galaxy Tab S8 Ultra).

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2023-05-26] **It's still not an iPad — the ecosystem lock-in.** "'US tablets are basically just iPads and
  everything else. Friends straight up won't switch — it can be amazing hardware, the best screen and battery in the
  world, and it still wouldn't be an iPad: the iMessage, the FaceTime, the ecosystem. Taking users from the iPad is
  clearly an uphill battle.'"
- [2023-05-26] **Strategy 1 — copy the good stuff.** "'I actually don't mind when products copy each other as long
  as they copy the good stuff. They ripped the pencil (magnetic, inductive charging, the pairing animation), the
  iPad's exact cursor mechanic, quad speakers in the same spots, and a Center-Stage clone called Limelight. If
  you're taking people from the iPad, you at least have to make them feel at home.'"
- [2023-05-26] **Strategy 2 — undercut, by trimming the right things.** "'$479 for 128 GB vs the iPad Air's $599 for
  64 GB. To get there you trim just the right set of things people hopefully don't notice much — a mid Dimensity
  9000 (~50% below the flagship chip), no fingerprint reader, a dimmer 500-nit display, a cheaper non-backlit
  keyboard.'"
- [2023-05-26] **Strategy 3 — one-up the iPad in spots.** "'Some things are objectively better than any iPad: a 144
  Hz refresh rate (I'm a sucker for it), a landscape front camera so you're centered on calls, an iPad-Pro-sized
  battery with 67 W charging (vs the iPad's slow 30 W), and clever split-screen extras — no Stage Manager disaster.'"
- [2023-05-26] **Still Android — and never buy for future updates.** "'But it's still an Android tablet: few
  well-optimized tablet apps (so the 144 Hz rarely engages, cameras are meh), and it launches on Android 13. Android
  14's on the horizon, but never buy a product based on potential future software updates. It's a good tablet for a
  good price, up against an impossible task — same as the Galaxy Tab S8 Ultra.'"
