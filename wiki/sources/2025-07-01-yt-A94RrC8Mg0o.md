# Nothing Headphone 1 Review: As Good as They Look? (@mkbhd, 2025-07-01)

## Summary
A review of the **$300 Nothing Headphone 1** — Nothing's first over-ear ANC headphones, now "playing with the big dogs"
(Sony, Bose, Sennheiser) in a category people usually buy for audio and ANC, not looks. His framing: **Nothing is a
design company** (design as the distinguishing feature), and this **cassette-tapes-glued-to-your-head** look is **"the
Cybertruck effect"** — some will love it *because* it's different, some will hate it *because* it's different (though
"more elegant than a Cybertruck"). Crucially, the weird design was easier to accept **because the rest of the product is
actually good.** He investigates the **"Sound by KEF"** partnership (KEF sells $225k Hi-Fi speakers and exactly one pair
of headphones, the Mu7) — **so he bought the Mu7 to check if this is a white-label rebadge**, and reports **"thankfully
they're almost nothing alike"; a genuinely different product.** The standout is the **all-physical control system** (a
scroll-wheel-slider for volume, a paddle for tracks, real switches — **no touch controls, "thumbs up"**), plus solid
comfort/build (folds *down* but doesn't *fold up*, so a bigger case with an **annoying zipper** — "I just came from the
perfect Sony XM6 magnetic-buckle case"). **ANC is "a notch and a half behind the Sony" (an A+ vs a B+)**; transparency
falls in with the pack (a booming own-voice); and the KEF 40 mm drivers sound **"like $300 headphones — clear,
balanced, plenty of range,"** helped by thick pads' passive isolation and a deep app EQ. His value verdict: **the
category leaders moved up to $350/$450/$550, so a rock-solid $300 pair is well-positioned.** Can you get better? Yes —
the **Sony XM6 is "better at everything, objectively"** — but **"is it $150 better? That's up to you"** (some will
prefer the physical buttons, the design, or the money saved). (Insta360 X5 sponsor.)
