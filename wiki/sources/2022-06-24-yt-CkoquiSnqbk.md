# Ford F150 Lightning is the iPhone of Pickup Trucks! (@mkbhd, 2022-06-24)

## Summary
A week living with the electric F-150 Lightning, framed as **"the iPhone of pickup trucks."** The core thesis: the
F-150 has been **the best-selling vehicle in America for 30+ years**, so its EV isn't about impressing him — it's
about **converting the huge world of loyal F-150 buyers** (like iPhone owners who'll only ever get a newer iPhone).
The winning strategy is **familiarity: make an EV of a car that's already accepted** — it looks and drives just
like an F-150 (only quieter, smoother, with one-pedal driving), so **"if the future is electric, this is one of the
most important cars in the world."** The signature differentiator is the **Mega Power Frunk** (14 cu ft / 400 lbs,
outlets, a drain plug), and the truck **can power your house** (a giant battery bank, several Power Walls). His
verdict: **"the best F-150 ever made"** — more powerful, more capable, cheapest-per-mile — delivering ~280 real
miles. "Now it's up to the competition to catch up."

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-06-24] **The iPhone of pickup trucks — converting the loyalists.** "'The F-150 is **the best-selling vehicle
  in America for 30+ years** — so the Lightning is a really big deal. **It's like people with older iPhones who'll
  only ever get a newer iPhone** — a huge world of F-150 loyalists waiting for the next convincing upgrade. It's
  not me they have to impress; it's them. **If the future of cars is electric, this is one of the most important
  cars in the world.**'"
- [2022-06-24] **Familiarity is the strategy — an EV of an already-accepted car.** "'In a lot of ways it doesn't
  matter how it drives — but it's actually a better F-150 (quieter, smoother, **one-pedal driving — you should
  always have the option**, the first F-150 with independent rear suspension). Crucially **it looks and feels
  exactly like the truck you're used to** — I drive past other F-150s and it looks the same. **You just need an EV
  version of a car that's already well accepted** (Ford did it with the Mustang name — but really did it with the
  whole truck).'"
- [2022-06-24] **The Mega Power Frunk — the visible differentiator.** "'The one obvious tell: **the Mega Power
  Frunk — 14 cu ft / 400 liters of enclosed storage (bigger than a Corolla's trunk), 400 lbs, four outlets, a USB,
  a drain plug** (fill it with ice for tailgating). It'd pull up next to other trucks and no one would bat an eye
  — until the front trunk opens and other truck owners' heads turn.'"
- [2022-06-24] **It can power your house.** "'It's essentially a giant battery bank — 120V and 240V outlets in the
  frunk and cab — and **with Ford's inverter it can back up your house for days (several times the size of a Tesla
  Power Wall).**'"
- [2022-06-24] **"The best F-150 ever made" — and up to the competition.** "'Even if nothing else were different,
  **it's the best F-150 ever made — more powerful (more hp and torque than the best gas F-150), the most cargo
  space, most high-tech, least maintenance, cheapest per mile, and the fastest/quietest/smoothest.** The
  extended-range version promises 300 mi; real-world I got ~280 (there's even a built-in scale that adjusts range
  for what you're hauling/towing). It delivers. **Now it's up to the electric Ram, the Tundra, even the Cybertruck
  to catch up to this one.**'"
