# Do Bad Reviews Kill Companies? (@mkbhd, 2024-04-16)

## Summary
His definitive statement on the **ethics and impact of honest reviews**, prompted by Twitter blaming his **Fisker
Ocean** and **Humane AI Pin** reviews for those companies' troubles. The reframe: **"Do bad reviews kill companies —
or do bad *products* kill companies?"** He rebuilds first principles: **what a review is** — someone uses the product
and honestly says whether it's any good; **"if they're not honest, they're basically useless."** Everything in the
ecosystem of reviews depends on truthfulness (the **Razer Phone story**: he trashed the "straight garbage" vibration
motor, and a year later Razer walked him to the Phone 2 saying "try the new vibration motor" — **honest feedback
turned into action**). Reviews serve **two audiences**: the small slice about to buy (his channel literally started
by flagging a Media Center remote no other laptop review mentioned) and — crucially — the **majority who just want an
interesting, informative video** and will never buy the thing. On the accusation: **zoom out.** He wasn't the only
Fisker reviewer, the stock was in **free-fall for months before his video** and had just gotten a sub-$1 delisting
notice; on Humane, reviewers were **texting each other** to confirm the overheating/2-hour battery was real, not a
unit defect — **"a bunch of honest reviews will all say the same stuff; that's the easiest way to tell a review is
honest."** His ethics: **"I don't care about any company's stock price, I'm not invested in anything I cover; I have
no duty to the companies — only to the people watching."** The thesis: you don't get bad reviews without a bad
product, and **"all that any honest review actually does is accelerate whatever was already going on."**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2024-04-16] **Do bad reviews kill companies, or do bad products?** "'Twitter pointed at my Fisker Ocean and Humane
  AI Pin reviews. The simple logic: do you still get a bunch of negative reviews and die as a company if the product
  is actually really good? You don't get those bad reviews without the product being bad to begin with.'"
- [2024-04-16] **What a review is — and honesty is everything.** "'A review is just someone using a product and
  honestly delivering whether it's any good. If reviews aren't honest, they're basically useless — everything in the
  ecosystem of reviews depends on it being truthful. I trashed the first Razer Phone's vibration motor; a year later
  they walked me to the Phone 2 saying "try the new vibration motor." Honest feedback turned into action.'"
- [2024-04-16] **Reviews are for the viewer, not the company.** "'Most people watching a review were never going to buy
  the thing — they're here for an interesting, informative, entertaining video. My first tech video reviewed a laptop
  I bought with allowance money, to tell the next buyer about a remote hidden in the PCI slot. The video is for the
  people watching it.'"
- [2024-04-16] **Zoom out — one review doesn't kill a company.** "'The Fisker stock was in free-fall for months before
  my video, and had just gotten a sub-$1 delisting notice right before it dropped. I was far from the only reviewer
  saying these things. Zoom out.'"
- [2024-04-16] **The easiest tell that a review is honest — we all find the same things.** "'We're all reviewing the
  same product, so we all find a lot of the same upsides and downsides. On the Humane Pin, when mine overheated and
  died in two hours I texted others — same thing was happening to them. A bunch of honest reviews will all say a lot of
  the same stuff. That might be the easiest way to tell if a review is honest.'"
- [2024-04-16] **I have no duty to the companies — only the viewers.** "'I literally don't care what any company's
  stock price is, and I'm not invested in anything I cover. I won't sugarcoat or leave things out to protect a $700
  device with a subscription. I have no duty to the companies whose products I cover — only to the people watching. All
  an honest review does is accelerate whatever was already going on.'"
