# Pixel 6/6 Pro Review: Almost Incredible! (@mkbhd, 2021-10-27)

## Summary
The full review after ~3 weeks. His verdict: **"almost incredible" and the most important Google phone yet** — the
first real Google flagship (own Tensor chip, new design, much better hardware), his favorite Pixels ever, but it
comes up short in a few places. Big frames: **Tensor is about features, not benchmarks** (game-changing
speech-to-text, Magic Eraser); the **camera-consistency framing** — "would you rather a camera that's 10/10
sometimes and 7/10 other times, or 9/10 every time?" (the iPhone is the 9-every-time); **over-processed HDR**;
and **battery is the flagship's real weakness**. Bottom line: **the $599 Pixel 6 is the real deal / the better
deal**; the $300 jump to the Pro doesn't vault it over the other flagships.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-10-27] **The most important Google phone yet — the pivot.** "'This is the first year I'm expecting a real
  Google flagship — a new camera array, a new design language, their own chip. **My favorite Pixels ever and the
  most "Google" phones ever made.** This is the most important Google phone yet — the pivot from enthusiast phones
  to actually competing on hardware while staying the smartest smartphone. I'm super into it.'"
- [2021-10-27] **Tensor is about features, not benchmarks — speech-to-text.** "'Benchmarking Tensor is the wrong
  way to think about it (~8–10% slower CPU on paper than the 888). **The biggest gap I've ever seen between a
  chip's benchmarks and what the phone can actually do.** Speech-to-text is unreal — it changed how I use the
  phone; you can rap at it 200 wpm like Eminem and it keeps up, adds punctuation, and is smart (spells contact
  names, "delete this"/"send" as commands). Magic Eraser reads like magic (content-aware fill made simple).'"
- [2021-10-27] **The camera — 10/10 sometimes vs 9/10 every time.** "'A brand-new 50 MP sensor (always binned to
  12.5 MP — no way to keep all 50) — but **Google is still running its old algorithms for a weak sensor, so photos
  now look a bit over-processed / over-HDR** (shadows never hit black; that "clarity slider too high" look). The
  question: **would you rather a camera that's 10/10 sometimes and 7/10 other times (the Pixel), or 9/10 every
  single time (the iPhone)?** The iPhone system is just more consistent right now. The pixel fan in me hopes a
  software update makes it 10/10 more often.'"
- [2021-10-27] **Battery is the flagship's weakness.** "'I expected Tensor to mean amazing battery (like Apple
  silicon) — **it's not; 3.5–4.5 hrs screen-on, average at best, dead by the end of a long day. On a $600 phone
  you forgive it; on a flagship it's a weakness** — and no charging brick in the box.'"
- [2021-10-27] **The 6 is the deal — the $300 delta.** "'$599 for the 6 / $899 for the 6 Pro. **The Pixel 6 is a
  really, really great deal — one of the best phones you can get for $600. The $300 to the Pro makes it a better
  phone but doesn't vault it over the other flagships** — get the Pro only if you must have the telephoto, the
  ultrawide selfie, or the bigger/faster screen. (I prefer the 6's matte sides.)'"

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "Would you rather have a camera that's a 10 out of 10 sometimes but also a 7 out of 10 some other times, or just
> a camera that gives you 9 out of 10 every single time? Because that's the iPhone."
> "This is the most important Google phone yet."
> "It's not about the performance as much as it's about the features."
