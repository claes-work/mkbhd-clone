# Samsung Galaxy Z Flip Impressions: Mixed Feelings! (@mkbhd, 2020-02-12)

## Summary
Hands-on with the Galaxy Z Flip at Unpacked — "very mixed feelings." The most polished/mature folding phone yet
(flagship specs, folding *glass*, an excellent hinge), but the crease is still a real problem. A clear statement of
his **hinge-engineering-is-what-matters** view for foldables.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-02-12] **"The most polished folding phone yet — but a long way to go."** "'Flagship internals (SD855+, 8 GB,
  256 GB) that **wipe the floor with the Moto Razr's mid-range specs.** But it **still has a long way to go to be
  something I'd actually buy** at $1,380.'" A balanced mature-but-flawed read.
- [2020-02-12] **Ultra-thin folding GLASS — the real advancement.** "'The screen is now **ultra-thin folding glass,
  not soft plastic** — you can feel it immediately (you can't dig a fingernail into it). **That's a great
  advancement.**' It makes it feel more like a real Samsung flagship (hole-punch, thin bezels)." A concrete
  materials-progress note.
- [2020-02-12] **The crease is still major — and worse on a vertical fold.** "'The crease is **still major** — better
  than the original Fold, but because it's a **vertical fold, the crease runs across the center where you're
  constantly scrolling, so you feel it under your finger over and over** (unlike a horizontal fold). I still prefer
  it to the Razr's plastic, but I won't tell you the crease is gone.'" His honest, tactile assessment.
- [2020-02-12] **THE HINGE — "hinges are extremely important; learn from your past ones."** "'The **best hinge I've
  seen yet** — nearly gapless, with tiny brush-like fibers inside for debris protection (Samsung clearly **learned
  from the first Fold's failures**). Great hinge weight (one-handed openable) and a **three-step hinge** — fold it
  halfway like a little laptop and set it down (the camera/gallery/Duo apps use this).'" A key foldable-design
  principle.
- [2020-02-12] **The optimism.** "'I'm just **glad folding phones are evolving as fast as they can** — maybe 3-4
  generations will give us the sleek, bezel-less, 120 Hz, iPad-size-in-your-pocket folding devices I hope we're
  working toward. A big maybe, but that's the hope.'" His steady reward-experimentation stance.

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "Hinges are extremely important for folding phones, and learning from your past hinges to make better ones is
> going to be something we start to see."
> "I'm glad folding phones are actually evolving as fast as they can."
