# 18-core iMac Pro Review: Not a Trap! (@mkbhd, 2018-03-02)

## Summary
The full review of the **maxed-out 18-core iMac Pro** — and the **resolution of the "is it a trap?" arc**: the
answer is **no**. He orders the highest-end config ($13k), makes it his full-time daily editing machine
(replacing the trash-can Mac Pro), and highly recommends it (though not this specific 18-core) — while still
planning to move to the modular Mac Pro when it ships.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-03-02] **TRAP ARC RESOLVED — "not a trap."** "A couple months ago I wondered if the iMac Pro was a
  trap — an interim machine until the modular Mac Pro. After using it I can highly recommend it (maybe not this
  18-core one); it's **one of my favorite new Apple products in a long time.**" Closes the loop on his own Dec
  2017 "give it a chance" promise ([[2017-12-12-yt-h-h5Mhlt6O0]]).
- [2018-03-02] **BIOGRAPHY/PRODUCTION — the maxed config + it becomes his daily driver.** The review unit: **18-core
  Xeon W, 128 GB ECC RAM, Vega 64 (16 GB), 4 TB SSD — ~$13,000.** "This is my new full-time daily-use and
  editing machine for everything, for now." A dated production-rig milestone (supersedes the 10-core he'd been
  trialing).
- [2018-03-02] **The "Rocket League" render benchmark (a signature bit).** His studio measures Final Cut export
  time in **"how many games of Rocket League we can play before it's done."** The trash-can Mac Pro = 2–3 games
  for an 8K→4K transcode; the 10-core iMac Pro = ~1 game; the 18-core = "we booted the PS4, a Rocket League
  update was installing, and it finished exporting before we even started playing." A memorable, self-aware
  unscientific-metric.
- [2018-03-02] **8K RED performance is the headline.** Cutting/playing back **8K REDCODE at 12:1 or 8:1** on the
  timeline — with effects and color layered on — plays back "almost like ProRes" after a split-second buffer,
  where any old Mac Pro would chug and drop frames. The GPU-heavy Final Cut workflow is where it shines.
- [2018-03-02] **Buyer-first advice by app suite (his standard close).** "**Final Cut editor → 100% no-brainer.**
  Adobe (Premiere/After Effects), Maya/Max → you benefit but consider whether a high-end regular iMac does the
  job. Motion → maybe not." And the config advice: "**go 10-core** — the cores-vs-clock sweet spot — and if you
  can upgrade one more thing, the **Vega 64 GPU**." Prescriptive, tailored to the viewer.
- [2018-03-02] **The honest tradeoffs + the aesthetic tell.** Concedes you can build a more-customizable PC for
  less, and that he'd still rather have dual monitors / a matte finish / reachable ports (he still wants the
  modular Mac Pro). But loves the **space gray** ("I think it's hot — I'd love an even more matte-black
  undertone"), the T2 chip, the dead-silent cooling. "It's the iMac I always wanted."
