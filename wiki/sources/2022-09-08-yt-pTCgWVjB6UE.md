# iPhone 14/Pro Impressions: Welcome to Dynamic Island! (@mkbhd, 2022-09-08)

## Summary
Hands-on first impressions of the iPhone 14 lineup from Cupertino (RIP the mini). The base 14/14 Plus are "an
iPhone 13 Pro a year later minus the telephoto and ProMotion, dressed in aluminum" — same A15/design/notch — with
a new camera, a brighter display, and two connectivity firsts: **eSIM-only in the US** (which he flags as **"an
extra wall in the walled garden"** — harder to switch off an iPhone) and **satellite SOS.** The Pro's three real
upgrades: the **A16**, a **48 MP camera (the first megapixel bump in ~a decade)** with a **2x optical-quality crop**,
a class-leading always-on display, and **Dynamic Island** — which the theater **audibly laughed at when named, but
which he came to really like**: the shrunk cutout becomes **a friendly, "next-level moving version of the hole-punch
wallpaper" — and a little multitasking UI** for background activities. His reads: **"the Plus will be a hit"** (a
big screen for +$100), and **"Apple is late to things, then does them the Apple way."**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-09-08] **The base 14 = a 13 Pro minus tele/ProMotion; the Plus will be a hit.** "'The 14/14 Plus look like
  last year (same A15, design, notch — I bet a 13 case fits). **Essentially an iPhone 13 Pro a year later, minus
  the telephoto and ProMotion, dressed in aluminum.** But a big 1080p 60 Hz phone means the best battery ever in an
  iPhone — **I'm pretty sure the Plus will be a hit; +$100 for a way bigger screen is a no-brainer for regular
  people.**'"
- [2022-09-08] **eSIM-only — a wall in the walled garden; and satellite SOS.** "'The corest change: **US iPhones
  drop the SIM tray for eSIM-only.** It's fine — but I wonder if it's **also a bit of an extra wall in the walled
  garden (how hard will it be to switch from an iPhone to a non-iPhone?).** And **satellite SOS** is genuinely
  impressive — the antennas + a UI that helps you point at a satellite to send an emergency message with no
  cellular (free for 2 years, US/Canada).'"
- [2022-09-08] **The Pro camera — the first megapixel bump in a decade + the 2x crop.** "'The Pro gets the A16 and a
  bigger, all-new camera — **the first time in ~a decade we get a megapixel bump (a 48 MP main).** It bins to 12 MP
  normally (shoot ProRAW for 48), and **the new 2x button is an exact crop of the middle 12 MP — optical-quality 2x**
  (plus the 3x tele). A class-leading 2,000-nit display, and finally an always-on display. I've been shooting the
  13 Pro on the new AutoFocus channel — curious to compare with the 14 Pro.'"
- [2022-09-08] **Dynamic Island — laughed at, then loved.** "'When they named it "Dynamic Island" the **entire
  theater audibly laughed.** But it makes sense: the cutout (a shrunk selfie/Face-ID system) **extends with UI for
  ongoing notifications/live activities** (music, timers, an Uber), with a great animation — tap to open, hold for
  more info. It's **a next-level, moving version of the friendly hole-punch wallpaper — thoughtful, playful,
  intuitive** — and with multiple background activities it **low-key becomes a little multitasking UI.** It's
  generous about taps around the cutout. Even if it looks worse in full-screen video, being so useful makes up for
  it. I hope I never have to say "Dynamic Island" out loud again.'"
- [2022-09-08] **Apple is late, then does it the Apple way.** "'No folding, no super-fast charging — just
  refinements. **At this point we know what the iPhone is; Apple tends to be late on the train to picking things
  up, then does it the Apple way — that's exactly the cutout and the 14s.** Same prices as last year; they sneakily
  left the 13 mini / 13 in the lineup.'"
