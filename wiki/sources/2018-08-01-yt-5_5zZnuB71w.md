# Why I Don't Use the Pixel! (@mkbhd, 2018-08-01)

## Summary
A personal/methodology video: he reveals his daily driver has quietly switched **from the Pixel 2 XL to the
OnePlus 6** — because the Pixel 2 has visibly **slowed down** in under a year. It becomes a meditation on **brand
non-allegiance**, the real-world value of RAM, and the hardest part of reviewing: **you can't know how a device
will age.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-08-01] **BIOGRAPHY — the daily-driver switch (Pixel 2 → OnePlus 6).** "People assume MKBHD uses a Pixel 2.
  That's usually true — but lately I've ping-ponged between the Pixel 2 XL and the OnePlus 6, and **my SIM is in
  the OnePlus 6 right now.**" A dated update to his two-phone setup (iPhone X + Android-of-choice).
- [2018-08-01] **"I have no allegiance."** "As soon as I like a phone better, that's the one I stick with — I'm
  fortunate to be in that position." He recaps resisting the Razer Phone's 120 Hz and the Galaxy S9's screen
  (and Lew from Unbox Therapy's S9+ pitch), always returning to the Pixel **for the camera** — until now. A clear
  statement of his no-brand-loyalty principle.
- [2018-08-01] **The tilt is PERFORMANCE — the Pixel 2 slowed down.** "My Pixel 2 has felt like it's slowed down
  more than other phones in the same time — it's **not holding animations at 60 fps** like it did, and it's less
  than a year old. Artem from Android Police said his is 'devastatingly, embarrassingly laggy' and only stays for
  the camera. **I even bought a second Pixel 2 XL to keep betas off it — both slowed down the same way.**" A
  documented, cross-checked observation.
- [2018-08-01] **RAM matters now — a reversal.** "A couple years ago I'd have said **OnePlus was just putting extra
  RAM in to impress spec-sheet readers.** Now I'm actually **observing 4 GB vs 8 GB make a real-world difference**
  — the OnePlus 6 holds apps in memory and keeps animations smooth where the Pixel drops frames and reloads." A
  dated evolution of his view.
- [2018-08-01] **The deep reviewing insight — "you're guessing at longevity."** "This highlights the tough part of
  reviewing a phone: **you don't fully know how it'll bear out over time — you're guessing.** You can tell if
  build quality will physically hold up, but whether the **flash storage or RAM degrades or slows down** — you
  just find out when it happens." A genuinely useful meta-point about the limits of a launch review.
- [2018-08-01] **Still the best camera — and a good sign about the market.** "The Pixel 2 is **still by far the
  best camera in any smartphone** — that's what I miss most. But it also says something good that I'm happily
  using a **$500-something phone surrounded by $900–1100 phones.** **Preference and your own priorities are
  everything** with something as personal as your daily phone." Nuanced, non-dogmatic close. Eyeing the Pixel 3 /
  Note 9 / next iPhone.
