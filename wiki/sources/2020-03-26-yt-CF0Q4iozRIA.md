# Huawei P40 Pro Impressions: What We Should Copy! (@mkbhd, 2020-03-26)

## Summary
Impressions of the Huawei P40 Pro through a deliberate lens: **since the Google-ban means few will buy it, "what
from this phone should other manufacturers copy so those features reach more hands?"** A constructive way to cover
a phone the market can't recommend.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-03-26] **THE LENS — "what should other manufacturers copy?"** "'No matter how good it is, the **Google-
  services situation means I won't recommend importing it** — so I'm looking at it through: **what should others
  copy so these features spread?** That's a win for all of us.'" A generous, competition-minded framing (given the
  Huawei ban, [[2020-01-03-yt-sr2fBCzXo4g]]).
- [2020-03-26] **COPY THIS — the camera philosophy is the OPPOSITE of the S20 Ultra's.** "'If the S20 Ultra didn't
  exist you'd think Huawei was playing the numbers game — but **their angle is the opposite: "we don't need to go
  all out on numbers; strike a balance between high numbers and actually good performance." Please copy that.**'"
  A direct rebuke of the numbers-race (cf. [[2020-03-03-yt--wOdL7HFNOs]]).
- [2020-03-26] **COPY THIS — the back finish, the fingerprint, and 40 W wireless charging.** "'One of my **favorite
  phone-back finishes ever** — silky soft-touch glass, hides fingerprints, doesn't mirror; **don't skin this one,
  go naked.** A bigger/faster in-screen fingerprint (keep working toward whole-bottom-half sensors). And **40 W
  wireless charging** — for context the $1,000 iPhone 11 Pro Max ships an 18 W *wired* charger. **Copy that.**'"
- [2020-03-26] **DON'T necessarily copy — the overflow display + the notch-shrink hope.** "'The "surface-tension"
  overflow display curves on all four edges — unique but **not necessarily better** (like wrapping a sphere, you
  get creases). The **pill cutout is about as small as you can make three sensors** — I'm **hoping this pushes
  Apple to finally shrink the iPhone notch this year.**'"
- [2020-03-26] **The camera in practice (look past the numbers).** "'Big 1/1.2" sensor → **same big-sensor razor-
  thin-focus/fringing** as the S20 Ultra on close subjects, **but autofocus is rock-solid** (unlike the Ultra). The
  standout was the **detailed selfie camera with beauty modes off.** Solid — the industry can pick a lot up here.'"

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "Huawei's angle is actually almost the exact opposite of that — we don't think we need to go all out with the
> numbers... we want to strike a balance between high numbers and actually good performance. Please copy that."
> "The thousand-dollar iPhone 11 Pro Max ships with an 18-watt wired fast charger [vs 40 W wireless here]... other
> manufacturers, yes, please copy that."
