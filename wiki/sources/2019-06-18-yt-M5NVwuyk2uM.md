# The Dream Smartphone! (2019) (@mkbhd, 2019-06-18)

## Summary
His 2019 "dream phone" concept — a Frankenstein of the best available parts. A landmark **personal-preferences**
source: it lays out, explicitly, what Marques personally values in a phone (not a review of any product). Collab
with renders by Concept Creator; a callback to his 2014 dream-phone video.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-06-18] **His stated priorities (the whole point).** "The **simplified** version: 'a OnePlus 7 Pro (specs,
  90 Hz, OS) with the **P30 Pro's battery** and the **Pixel's camera** — that would already be amazing.' The full
  dream: **A12 Bionic** (the power champ), **OnePlus 7 Pro internals** (12 GB, UFS 3.0), a **6" 1440p 120 Hz
  OLED** with Samsung's brightness, a **4,500 mAh** battery (RED Hydrogen's) with OnePlus warp charging."
- [2019-06-18] **Design values — matte black, front-facing speakers, headphone jack, no gimmicks.** "'The
  overarching theme would be **matte black** with a little red accent and carbon fiber.' Keeps **front-facing
  stereo speakers** (piezoelectric under-glass ones 'just aren't cutting it'), a **headphone jack** (moved on but
  'every once in a while it's convenient'), and — notably — **polycarbonate over glass** ('glass backs are a bit
  overrated... won't shatter when you drop it')." A concrete map of his taste.
- [2019-06-18] **Camera philosophy — Pixel photos + iPhone video, and ultrawide over telephoto.** "'**Pixel 3's
  photo quality + the iPhone's video quality — best of both worlds.**' Wants a **second lens of the same quality
  for ultrawide** (not telephoto) — reinforcing his stated preference elsewhere that **ultrawide is more useful
  and fun than a telephoto.**"
- [2019-06-18] **No notch/hole-punch/pop-up needed.** "The top speaker slot leaves room for **real front-facing
  cameras** — 'no notch, no hole punch, no crazy mechanisms, no pop-ups that don't make it water resistant.'"
  His consistent anti-workaround stance (cf. [[2019-05-29-yt-ySMGudnpdXQ]]).
- [2019-06-18] **BIOGRAPHY/FORMAT — the 2014 callback + why he makes these.** "'Last time I made a dream-phone
  video was 2014, five years ago — **baby Marques** asking for a Snapdragon 805, 3 GB RAM, a 3,500 mAh battery.'
  Making the video is partly to 'push the dream a little bit closer to reality' by making his voice heard." A
  datable creator-arc anchor.

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "Glass backs are kind of a bit overrated... the matte black on this phone can be polycarbonate, so when you drop
> it, it won't shatter."
> "I would take the Pixel 3's photo quality from the cameras and the iPhone's video quality — it's the best of
> both worlds."
