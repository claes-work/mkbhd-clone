# WWDC 2022 Impressions: M2 Macs and iOS 16?! (@mkbhd, 2022-06-07)

## Summary
Reactions to WWDC 2022. Software: **iOS 16's big new personalized lock screen** (custom clocks/fonts, widgets,
multiple lock screens tied to Focus modes; notifications now roll in from the bottom — better for big phones),
**editable/unsend iMessages** ("salty that you can edit an iMessage before a tweet"), **Live Text for video** and
**lift-a-subject-out-of-a-photo** ("Next Level"). **Next-gen CarPlay** can take over a car's entire screen suite —
prompting his two jabs: **"why would anyone use the built-in infotainment"** (no motivation for makers to get good
at it), and **"hey Apple, ever considered making the rest of the car... like an electric one?"** Hardware: **the M2
chip** (2nd-gen baseline: ~25% more transistors, ~50% more bandwidth, ~18% CPU / ~35% GPU) and the **redesigned
MacBook Air** (flat, notch, MagSafe) — while the **M2 MacBook Pro "didn't get the same love"** (get a 14" M1 Pro
instead). Plus **Continuity Camera + Desk View** (the surprise-me feature) and **Stage Manager** bringing overlapping
windows to the iPad ("the iPad evolves toward a computer, frustratingly slow"). Launches his **Masterclass.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-06-07] **iOS 16 — the personalized lock screen + edit iMessage.** "'The biggest iOS 16 update is a **way more
  personalized lock screen** (custom clocks/fonts, wallpaper depth, **widgets**, and **multiple saved lock screens
  tied to Focus modes** — like swapping watch faces); **notifications now roll in from the bottom** (the opposite
  of Android — better for seeing your wallpaper and reaching, if Apple's selling big phones). Plus **Live
  Activities** (one updating notification), **editable and unsend iMessages** ("salty you can edit an iMessage
  before a tweet"), **Live Text for video**, and lifting a subject out of a photo — "that's Next Level."'"
- [2022-06-07] **Next-gen CarPlay — and two jabs.** "'Next-gen CarPlay can take over a car's entire screen suite
  (gauges, side screens) — pretty sick. Two thoughts: **(1) why would anyone use the built-in infotainment if
  CarPlay does everything? There's no motivation now for makers to get good at that software.** (2) **"Hey Apple,
  ever considered making the rest of the car... maybe an electric one?" — probably never mind.**'"
- [2022-06-07] **The M2 chip + the redesigned MacBook Air.** "'The **M2** is the 2nd-gen baseline (M1 Pro/Ultra are
  still higher-end): 5 nm, 20B transistors (~25% more), 100 GB/s bandwidth (~50% more), ~18% CPU / ~35% GPU. The
  machine that matters is the **redesigned MacBook Air — flat (no wedge), four colors, a MagSafe port, a bigger
  13.6" 500-nit display with a notch and a 1080p webcam** — a nice set of updates for the world's most popular
  laptop (start $1,199; the M1 Air stays at $999).'"
- [2022-06-07] **The M2 MacBook Pro "didn't get the same love."** "'The **M2 MacBook Pro is basically the same
  machine as the M1 one with an M2 inside** — not bad, but it didn't get the same love; **giving up MagSafe, the
  new display/speakers/webcam/fast-charging just for slightly better sustained performance — you should probably
  get a 14" M1 Pro instead.**'"
- [2022-06-07] **Continuity Camera + Desk View — and Stage Manager on iPad.** "'macOS Ventura's **Continuity Camera**
  uses the iPhone's rear cameras as a webcam (basically admitting webcams suck) — and **Desk View** uses the
  ultrawide to give a top-down view of your desk ("the one feature that blew me away on stage"). **Stage Manager**
  brings overlapping, resizable windows + external-display extend to the M1 iPad — **I like how the iPad slowly
  evolves toward a more capable computer; it's frustratingly slow, but I'll dive into the betas.** And finally, the
  iPad gets a Weather app — Bravo. (Also: my Masterclass just launched.)'"
