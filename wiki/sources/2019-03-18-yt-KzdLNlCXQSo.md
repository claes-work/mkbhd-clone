# Top 5 Android Q Features! (@mkbhd, 2019-03-18)

## Summary
A walkthrough of Android Q Beta 1 (on a Pixel 2) — "not a big visual overhaul (I missed that), but full of smaller
updates Android power-users will appreciate." A dated snapshot; the best bits are the share-sheet fix and the
(buggy) native screen recorder.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-03-18] **The refinement-era framing (again).** "**Not a big visual overhaul** like the letter updates used
  to be — a lot of smaller updates." His standard mature-platform read. Beta, unstable — "don't put it on your main
  device."
- [2019-03-18] **#1 light theming + the dark-mode tease.** Accent color / font / **icon shape** (circle / teardrop /
  squircle / rounded rect — "I don't hate the teardrop or squircle"). "**We still don't know if native dark mode is
  coming — but battery-saver mode now auto-applies a system-wide dark theme** (helps OLED battery). Worth watching."
- [2019-03-18] **#2 granular permissions (iOS-like, overdue).** "Location can now be **allow-always / never / only-
  while-the-app-is-running — basically like iOS. Glad it's here; overdue.**"
- [2019-03-18] **#4 THE SHARE SHEET FIX (the one he's most excited about).** "**A huge deal** if you've used Android
  more than a week: the old share sheet's quick-share options **load after a painful delay and move what you were
  about to press** (I do this every day). **Now they all load at once — I cannot wait to never deal with the old
  one again.**" A visceral, relatable improvement.
- [2019-03-18] **#5 the little things + the buggy screen recorder.** A better Files app, a battery-time-remaining
  estimate, **undo home-screen removal**, blurred lock-screen album art, foldable support, and a **native screen
  recorder** ("one of my favorite iOS features — long-press power → screenshot → record, with screen-touches + audio
  — but **it's super buggy: the stop button crashes; I could only stop it by restarting**"). Screenshots now include
  the notch cutout. Guesses the "Q" dessert ("quiche? quesadilla? q-tip? — I don't know any"); credits Android
  Police.

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "The new share sheet — this may seem tiny, but if you've used Android for more than a week, this is a huge deal.
> The share shortcuts now all load at once. I cannot wait to never have to deal with the old share sheet again."
> "It's not a big visual overhaul, which is kind of what the big letter updates used to be, and I missed that a
> bit — but it's full of smaller updates power-users will appreciate."
> "A native Android screen recorder would be pretty sweet — but the stop button crashes the UI, so the only way I
> could stop it was to restart the whole phone."
