# iPad Pro M1 Review: The Ultimate Spec Bump! (@mkbhd, 2021-05-19)

## Summary
Full review of the M1 iPad Pro — "the ultimate spec bump." The durable ideas: a **massive, almost absurd
performance leap** (Geekbench higher than a 16" MacBook Pro) that a regular user can barely tap — so it's a
**Ferrari in city traffic**; it's **"the GOAT of tablets,"** but the recurring **"what is a computer?"** question
returns; and the promotable theory that **the iPad might be "too good" — the only thing holding it back is Apple
protecting the Macs** (the software, not the hardware, is the limit).

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-05-19] **A massive performance leap — "a completely new stratosphere."** "'Tempting to think the M1 isn't
  a big jump over the A12Z (both Apple silicon) — **that'll be wrong. Geekbench ~6,900 (higher than the 16"
  MacBook Pro); AnTuTu ~1,084,000 — 30–45% faster than the last iPad Pro, which already beat every other
  tablet.** With 16 GB RAM (Apple's finally telling us), **nothing ever drops from memory — Lightroom from hours
  ago picks right up.**'"
- [2021-05-19] **The Ferrari-in-traffic problem — "impatient for a better use of all this power."** "'It's a
  struggle to find where a regular user taps this — that's the point (they'll get an iPad Air). The pro benefit
  is real (a 1-hour iMovie export: 17 min → 12 min; heavy Lightroom/LumaFusion/Photoshop). But **I'm impatient
  for a better use of the power — like upgrading to a Ferrari but sitting in city traffic, or strapping rockets
  to your car when you wanted a pickup truck.**'" A promotable framing of the iPad's hardware-software gap.
- [2021-05-19] **The genuine wins — Thunderbolt, mini-LED (12.9 only), Center Stage.** "'M1 makes the USB-C a
  **Thunderbolt/USB4 port (40 Gbps — real for fast camera imports).** The **12.9" gets a mini-LED Liquid Retina
  XDR (2,500 dimming zones, 1,600 nits) — "about as good as OLED,"** great for HDR + the letterbox black bars.
  The new ultrawide front camera enables **Center Stage** (auto-follows your face on video calls) — "a neat touch
  that makes sense on the iPad." (The 11" keeps last year's screen — so I'm not upgrading my 2018 11".)'"
- [2021-05-19] **"Apple making a product thicker for a bigger battery — nice."** "'It's 0.5 mm thicker and 50 g
  heavier to fit the M1 + mini-LED + a bigger battery. **Would you look at that — Apple making a product slightly
  thicker for a slightly bigger battery. Nice.**'" A recurring pro-thickness-for-battery jab (cf. the M1 iMac chin
  critique).
- [2021-05-19] **THE GOAT of tablets — but "what is a computer?"** "'If it's a computer you're after, **the M1
  MacBook Air is the same computer — cheaper, thinner, better balanced.** But if it's a tablet — touchscreen, 5G,
  the apps, the Pencil, the XDR — **this is the GOAT of tablets, I'm saying it.** It can partly convert into a
  computer, but it isn't ready to be a full-time one for most people — yet.'"
- [2021-05-19] **THE THEORY — "is this iPad too good? Apple protecting the Macs."** "'Here's a theory: **is this
  iPad too good? The only thing holding it back is Apple protecting the Macs.** If it got the iPadOS-15 makeover I
  want, Final Cut, and a proper keyboard dock — **I wouldn't have a MacBook; I'd just use this. Would anyone need
  a laptop if the software made the difference?**'" A promotable software-is-the-real-limit thesis.
