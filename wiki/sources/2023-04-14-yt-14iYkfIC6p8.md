A review chat on the **Nothing Ear (2)** ($150 earbuds), plus a Windows quality-of-life
tangent about the **Print Screen / Snipping Tool**.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated 2023-04-14)
- **[Marques, clearly — his framing]** He categorizes the **Nothing Ear (2)** as a **"minor
  update, but with one important new thing — custom drivers,"** which could meaningfully
  improve sound; aesthetically they look the same, similar battery, slightly smaller case.
  He hadn't used them as much as the co-hosts, so he defers to their listening. *(his
  "what actually changed" framing of an iterative product.)*
- **[co-hosts]** The co-hosts report the **sound quality is significantly better than gen 1**
  — "these are $150 earbuds; do they sound like $150 earbuds? Yes." (co-host verdict.)
- **[group — Windows tip]** The **Print Screen key now lets you select a region immediately**
  (rather than dumping the whole multi-monitor desktop to the clipboard to paste into Paint)
  — the enhanced Snipping Tool. A shared quality-of-life aside.
