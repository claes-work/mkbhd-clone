# MKBHD Studio Tour 2021! (@TheStudio, 2021-07-27)

> ⚠️ ATTRIBUTION: **Pure Marques** — he hosts a first-person tour of the entire studio he built.
> Subject data throughout (team members' desk cameos are context). ★★ production/biography goldmine.

## Summary
The flagship studio tour: Marques walks every room of the space, explaining its design. Rich on
his production philosophy, the physical/technical setup, and a couple of durable personality notes
(tidiness ethos; the battery-station rule).

## Key claims (Marques-attributed)
- [2021-07-27] **Studio-design principle — kill the echo:** every room where video is shot gets
  **carpet (1-ft interlocking tiles) + white serrated foam in the ceiling** ("it was just a
  concrete echo box" before). Recurs room to room. (production-filmmaking)
- [2021-07-27] **The top-down room (permanent set):** because they do so many top-down shots, it
  "made sense" to build a permanent top-down set (camera lives on the jib, never leaves) rather
  than rebuild lights/flags each time — plus the **anti-reflection blocker** to keep the camera off
  glass/screens (the recurring reflection obsession). Surfaces (carbon fiber, wood, "robot skin")
  + prop shelves live there. (production-filmmaking)
- [2021-07-27] **The camera robot ("Mia"):** does "extremely precise, often very fast, very
  repeatable moves that a human literally could never," with a FIZ (focus/iris/zoom) on an Xbox
  controller + iMac. "A 6-second video from the robot takes a lot longer than 6 seconds to make" —
  used sparingly. (production-filmmaking)
- [2021-07-27] **The gray-wall room lighting grid:** nine **Aperture 300D MkII** in a top-down
  grid, **inspired by a room in Apple Park** that was "just a top-down softbox light." Motorized
  blackout shades (in channels, no light leaks) in every window room. (production-filmmaking)
- [2021-07-27] **The phone drawer:** a gear closet holds "the last 3 years of phones from every
  manufacturer" — the famed/infamous phone drawer. (biography, tech-reviews)
- [2021-07-27] **The camera room (his favorite) + the battery-station rule:** cinema + mirrorless
  cameras and glass live here; the "only organization that matters" is the **battery station** —
  batteries charge at the bottom, and "once they're 100% they live up top. If it's up top, it's
  100% and you can go. That's the only thing I care about." (production-filmmaking — a clean
  operating rule)
- [2021-07-27] **Personality — clean-surfaces guy:** "I'm a clean-surfaces type of guy — I really
  like when surfaces are clear," so storage hides paper towels/screwdrivers/batteries off the
  surfaces. Corroborates the "get rid of it all" backup ethos in [[2021-08-06-yt-cxp-FCDUkNc]].
  Also: "I'm bad at drawing but I really try." (biography)
- [2021-07-27] **Gear corroboration:** Herman Miller Embody + Yamaha HS8s "both for 10 years now";
  Keychron K2; Pro Displays; Mac Pro; XDesk. Waveform studio = "best-sounding room" (bass traps,
  acoustic wall, Canon C300s). Private bathroom ("good boss, Marques"). (production-filmmaking)
