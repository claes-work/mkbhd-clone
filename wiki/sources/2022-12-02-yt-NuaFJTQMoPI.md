# The 2023 Chevy Bolt EUV is the Best Deal in Electric Cars!

## Summary

Marques reviews the **Chevy Bolt EUV** (~$32k) and argues the long-awaited affordable EV has been
"right under our noses" — **the most affordable EV he can reasonably recommend.** The theme: it's a
**very well-equipped economy car** that's "not missing anything" (heated/ventilated seats, one-pedal,
Super Cruise, an efficiency "leaf" his own Model S only just got). Contains two nice biography beats
(**"I daily-drive a Model S still today"** and his **Top Gear column's household-electronics analogy**).
Fully Marques-narrated (Insta360 sponsor).

## Key claims (biography marked ⬥)

- **[2022-12-02]** The frame: "We've all wanted a $25,000 high-quality EV — that might not happen for a
  long time. But right under our noses is **the most affordable EV you can reasonably recommend: the
  Chevy Bolt.**" ~$32k start, ~$36k as-specced, 200 hp FWD, ~220–250 mi. ⚠️ Weak spot: **~50 kW charging
  ("not the fastest")**; no frunk. Shot on the **Pixel 7 Pro** (first @AutoFocus video on it). (quote)
- **[2022-12-02]** ⭐ The theme: **"This is a very well-equipped economy car — at no point did I feel I
  was missing anything. It has equivalent experiences to a Tesla Model 3 or Model S: heated/ventilated
  seats, one-pedal driving, wireless CarPlay/Android Auto, Super Cruise, and an efficiency 'leaf' — which
  my Tesla Model S Plaid only just got via a software update."** (quote)
- **[2022-12-02]** ⬥ BIOGRAPHY: **"I daily-drive a Model S still today"** (dated Dec 2022 — corroborates
  the Plaid ownership). (quote)
- **[2022-12-02]** ⬥ BIOGRAPHY: **"When I write the column on Top Gear summarizing my thoughts on these
  vehicles, one thing I get to say is: if I compared it to a reasonable household-electronics object,
  what would it be? For the VW ID.4 I said a toaster oven — pure utility."** (Confirms his **Top Gear
  column** and a signature analogy device.) He puts the Bolt in that same utility class but "a level
  more attainable." (quote)
- **[2022-12-02]** ⭐ Verdict: **"I think we're sleeping on the $30,000 Bolt as the most affordable, best
  version of a cheap EV you can get — we should use it as a baseline more often. On the way to the
  eventual dream (a ~$25–29k, 300-mi, fast-charging, sporty EV, probably a decade out), we need more
  good ones like this."** (quote) Contains an **Insta360 sponsor** segment (flagged). (paraphrase)
