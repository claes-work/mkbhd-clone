# Tech Rewind 2021! (@mkbhd, 2021-12-26)

## Summary
His 2021 year-in-review — and, coming a few days after Smartphone Awards 2021, effectively the **close of the 2021
era.** Two durable business notes: he made **93 MKBHD videos (short of his ~100 goal, down from 121 in 2020) — but
launched 3 new channels (Waveform, Waveform Clips, The Studio) for 240 videos total across the team**; and his
guiding mantra **"more better"** (make both more AND better, fighting the usual trade-off). Then his **five biggest
tech movements of 2021** (counted up): #5 **short-form content is here to stay** (teasing a possible shorts
channel); #4 **crypto/web3/NFTs/Dogecoin**; #3 **custom silicon is underrated** (Apple M1 Pro/Max, Google Tensor —
"if I were Qualcomm or Intel I'd be paying close attention"); #2 **alternate form factors** gaining momentum; and
**#1 EVs, especially trucks** ("every year for the next decade is going to be the most interesting year yet for
electric cars"). Plus the top-5 things that DIDN'T ship (the Note, a Pixel Watch/fold, Cybertruck, an Apple-silicon
desktop, and a good sub-$30K EV).

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-12-26] **"More better" — the team mantra + 240 videos.** "'We made 93 MKBHD videos — short of my ~100 goal
  and way down from 121 last year, easy to get down on. **But we started three other channels this year — Waveform
  (now in video), Waveform Clips, and The Studio — so the total is 240 videos across the team, and I'm very proud
  of them.** The saying always in the back of my head is **"more better"** — usually more means lower quality and
  better means fewer, so our goal is to make both more AND better.'"
- [2021-12-26] **#3 — custom silicon is underrated.** "'I really feel **custom silicon is underrated for how much
  it's changing the landscape.** Apple's M1 → M1 Pro/Max, and Google's Tensor gave the Pixel 6 the best-value phone
  of the year. I fully expect more manufacturers — phones, tablets, laptops, even headphones — doing more with
  proprietary in-house silicon. **If I were Qualcomm or Intel right now, I'd be paying very close attention.**'"
- [2021-12-26] **#2 — alternate form factors gain momentum every year.** "'Foldables gained a little more usability
  and a little more momentum — the Z Flip 3 at a normal flagship price, the Fold 3, the mid-size Oppo Find N, the
  Razr, Xiaomi, Huawei, Surface Duo 2. **It's all getting more usable and slowly coming down in price — clearly
  working its way toward being arrived.**'"
- [2021-12-26] **#1 — EVs, especially trucks; every year the most interesting yet.** "'Number one is EVs, and
  specifically the trucks. **I've said it before: every year for the next decade is going to be the most
  interesting year yet for electric cars.** For years we got promises (electrify by 2030) — now it's time to
  ship: 2021 gave us the Rivian R1T, Hummer EV, F-150 Lightning, Lucid Air, Model S Plaid, plus lower-priced
  volume EVs (ID.4, Leaf, Mini). I plan to check out as many as I can (and keep writing for Top Gear).'"
- [2021-12-26] **What didn't ship — the sub-$30K-EV wish.** "'The top-5 no-shows: the Galaxy Note (a full year with
  no built-in-S-Pen phone), a Pixel Watch / foldable Pixel, Tesla's Cybertruck/Roadster (delayed; the Plaid Plus
  was announced then canceled — I've never seen a car announced and canceled like that), an Apple-silicon true
  desktop (the Mac Pro is the one I'm really waiting for), and — #1 — **a good sub-$30,000 EV with 220+ miles of
  range** (the rumored Tesla Model 2 or whoever beats them to it). Maybe 2022.'"
