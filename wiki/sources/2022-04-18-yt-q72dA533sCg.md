# Ford's Coolest Electric Truck is from 1978! (@mkbhd, 2022-04-18)

## Summary
A one-day drive of Ford's **F-100 Eluminator concept** — a **1978 Ford F-100 restomod with the guts of a 2022
Mustang Mach-E GT** (480 hp, 600+ lb-ft, 0–60 in 3.5s, ~200 mi range). His **new favorite two-door EV.** It's a
delightful old/new mashup (manual roll-up windows next to a huge vertical touchscreen; new parts disguised as old).
The durable through-line: **it's "easy to build one" — the hard part is shipping in mass** — so this is a **proof
of concept** (not for sale), but Ford sells the **Eluminator crate motor (~$3,900)** to convert old cars to
electric. Fun asides: a **bigger frunk than most EVs on the road**, and it drives smooth at low speed but sways at
speed with "the aerodynamics of a block of cheese." He wants Ford to reconsider making it.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-04-18] **A 1978 body with Mach-E GT guts — his new favorite two-door EV.** "'A **1978 Ford F-100 restomod
  over the guts of a 2022 Mustang Mach-E GT** (performance edition) — **my new favorite two-door EV**, and not the
  one you'd picture. It drives basically the same as the Mach-E GT (480 hp, 600+ lb-ft, 0–60 in 3.5s), and it
  looks/sounds/feels ridiculous doing it.'"
- [2022-04-18] **The old/new mashup — the charm.** "'A healthy combo of old and new, and new disguised as old: **the
  Mach-E's huge vertical touchscreen right next to manual roll-up windows and door locks**; new seats/steering
  wheel in period tan/brown ("F-100"); old-school speakers hooked up. Only tells you it's a restomod up close (the
  camera cutouts, the fake grille, the CCS charge flap).'"
- [2022-04-18] **Easy to build one — the hard part is shipping.** "'I've been saying EV prototypes are easy to build
  — you make one with a custom chassis, motors, a battery, and parade it around; **the hard part is building and
  shipping them in mass.** This is a **concept car with no plans to ship** — but one of the coolest EV builds I've
  seen. **A bigger frunk than most EVs on the road**, ~200 mi range ("not amazing, not terrible, considering it has
  the aerodynamics of a block of cheese").'"
- [2022-04-18] **A proof of concept — and the crate motor.** "'People asked "can I get one?" — no, it's a concept.
  **But Ford sells the Eluminator crate motor (~$3,900 — lower than I expected) to convert older cars to
  electric.** So this cool concept is really a **proof of concept** — the ultimate retro-modern truck. Shout-out to
  Ford; I'd love it if they reconsidered making it.'"
