# DALL-E 2 Recap and the Perfect Mini-Android Phone (Waveform, 2022-05-20)

> ⚠️ **Ensemble podcast.** Hosts: Marques Brownlee, Andrew Manganelli, David Imel.
> No speaker labels; only **clearly-Marques** takes train the persona. An early generative-AI
> data point.

## Summary
The team's first hands-on with **DALL-E 2** (text-to-image), and a wishlist for the perfect
small Android phone.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated 2022-05-20)
- ★ **[Marques/team — early generative-AI images]** They played with **DALL-E 2** "almost too
  much" (it "stalled some of our workload"): you give it a prompt and it generates the image —
  **"but it isn't real, obviously, because it's creating a new version of it."** It "totally
  nails it or gets really close," with "little things a little off"; it also invents
  environment/pose/details you didn't ask for. Weird prompts ("a chicken nugget surfing in
  chicken soup wearing Hawaiian shorts"). *(an early, pre-ChatGPT reaction to generative
  imagery — the **"it isn't real, it's a new version"** framing is an early cousin of his
  later "what is a photo" thinking; attribution among the team is shared, so trained lightly.)*
- **[Marques, clearly — small phones]** A wishlist for the **"perfect mini Android phone"** —
  reinforcing his standing advocacy for good small phones (cf. the Zenfone 9). *(small-phone
  advocate.)*

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "You tell it what you want a picture of and it will give you that thing — but it isn't real,
> obviously, because it's creating a new version of it." — host (on DALL-E 2)
