# Apple iPhone 12 Lineup Reactions! (@mkbhd, 2020-10-14)

## Summary
Reactions to Apple's October event (a pre-recorded virtual keynote) — the four iPhone 12s + HomePod mini. Durable
threads: **5G is a two-sided thing** (not ready now, but good it's in all four since people keep iPhones longest);
**MagSafe is the coolest new thing** (and the seed of an accessory ecosystem); the **no-charger-in-the-box call
came true**; the **missing 120 Hz ProMotion** is a real disappointment; and **the iPhone 12 is so good it makes
the 12 Pro a less convincing buy.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-10-14] **5G is a two-sided thing — but good that all four have it.** "'**Right now 5G isn't ready — most
  people don't have coverage; you're not buying a phone for 5G today.** But **people keep iPhones the longest of
  any phone,** and in 3–5 years 5G will be more built out, so you might regret not having it. **It's a good thing
  all the new iPhones have 5G, so you don't have to pick.**'" His keep-it-for-years buying lens applied to 5G.
- [2020-10-14] **MAGSAFE is the coolest new thing (and an ecosystem seed).** "'**MagSafe being back is the
  coolest new thing** — magnets that align a wireless charger (or a wallet, cases) perfectly on the back. $39,
  15 W. I'm into it **not just for charging but for the whole ecosystem of MagSafe accessories** it'll spawn.
  Hot (bad) take: everyone gets so used to snapping things on that a future iPhone ships portless.'"
- [2020-10-14] **The no-charger call came true — "we called it."** "'**Officially no charging brick in any iPhone
  12 box** — just a USB-C-to-Lightning cable. **We called it** — they put it in the environmental section
  (e-waste, smaller box, shipping efficiency), exactly as I predicted in my dedicated video.'" (Cf. the Apple
  Watch S6 "writing on the wall," [[2020-09-17-yt-Mm0NvlXdz4A]].)
- [2020-10-14] **NO 120 Hz PROMOTION — the pointed disappointment.** "'The one thing missing I hoped for: **120 Hz
  ProMotion — it's not in the 12 Pro or Pro Max.** The defend-Apple-no-matter-what line is "the average person
  won't notice" — okay, technically true — **but Apple is notorious for the extra next-level stuff the average
  person won't notice (10-bit Dolby Vision, display calibration).** For the $1,000 customer I want convinced over
  Android, I'd appreciate high refresh. Fingers crossed for next year.'" A structured critique of the
  defend-Apple reflex.
- [2020-10-14] **The 12-makes-the-Pro-less-convincing thesis.** "'**A lot of the iPhone 12 is so good it makes
  the 12 Pro a less convincing buy for me** — the Pros aren't that different this year (size, colors, cameras).
  The **real reason to buy the Pro is the camera module** (new stack, LiDAR — more useful on a phone than the
  iPad — Dolby Vision HDR 4K60, "the only camera in the world that'll shoot it").'"
- [2020-10-14] **HomePod mini — "still Siri."** "'The **$99 HomePod mini** focuses on audio (S5 computational
  audio) + an intercom feature, but **it's still Siri** — in a world of Google/Alexa speakers with screens and
  clocks, **you've got to really want Siri or the ecosystem plug-in to pick it.**' (I called the original the
  "dumbest smart speaker.")'"

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "You're not really buying a phone right now because it has 5G... but people keep iPhones for probably the
> longest amount of time of any new phone."
> "Apple is notorious for doing that extra next level of stuff that the average person probably won't notice...
> so I'm also a little bummed that they don't have ProMotion here."
> "A lot of iPhone 12 is so good that it makes the 12 Pro a little bit less of a convincing buy for me."
