# Escobar Responds! A PSA (@mkbhd, 2020-05-29)

## Summary
A follow-up PSA after the Escobar company sent him yet another product (a gold-plated iPhone 11 Pro, the "Escobar
Gold 11 Pro") in response to his exposé. He lays out the **anatomy of the scam as a repeatable 4-step formula** —
media-literacy content — and warns viewers off it.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-05-29] **THE 4-STEP SCAM FORMULA.** "'**(1)** List a **desirable tech product remarkably cheap** (a $2,000
  fold, a $1,000 iPhone). **(2)** Hype it with a made-up story (wild videos, the Escobar name, "limited edition,"
  buying ripapple.com to redirect). **(3)** Marketing — **ship a few to YouTubers/influencers for press** ("those
  videos generate sales you never have to fulfill"). **(4)** Take money via **non-refundable methods only — bank
  transfer, crypto, Western Union — the biggest red flag ever.**'" A promotable media-literacy framework.
- [2020-05-29] **The $2.6B Apple lawsuit as a press stunt — all press is good press.** "'The box included a **14-page
  "suing Apple for $2.6 billion"** filing (over a FaceTime bug). **They don't expect to win — they expect press
  coverage**, and every article mentions ripapple.com / the new campaign. **All press is good press** — maybe the
  smartest thing they've done.'" Ties the all-press-is-good-press thread ([[2020-05-09-yt-mBHY_Qaw5AI]]).
- [2020-05-29] **Why they keep sending him product.** "'Why send me another after I tore them down? **They've done
  the math — it's still good press for them.** When my last video went up, ~5-6 people still tried to give them
  money.'" A self-aware note on the paradox of covering a scam.
- [2020-05-29] **THE ETHIC — don't give them money; give it to charity.** "'**Do not give them your money unless
  you never want to see it again.** There are much better uses of giving away money — **give it to charity, to a
  better cause right now.** As long as they keep rinsing and repeating, it's not worth paying attention to.'" A
  clear values statement.
- [2020-05-29] **Keeping the focus on the pattern, not the product.** "'I don't want to spend more time on the gold
  phone — **the more time we spend on it, the more we lose sight of the bigger picture:** they're not going to ship
  you this, just like the Fold 1 and 2.'" A deliberate reframe from product to pattern.
