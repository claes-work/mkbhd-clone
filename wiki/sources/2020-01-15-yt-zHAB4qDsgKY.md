# Why Everyone is Copying AirPods: Explained! (@mkbhd, 2020-01-15)

## Summary
An explainer on why the whole industry cloned AirPods. The thesis: Apple **created a problem (killed the headphone
jack) and sold the solution 27 minutes later** — and it worked so well ($8B business) that everyone followed.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-01-15] **THE THESIS — "solve a problem you created a half hour earlier."** "'On the same 2016 stage, Apple
  killed the headphone jack ("courage") and **27 minutes later unveiled AirPods** — selling you the solution to a
  problem they'd just created is a **brilliant way to make a ton of money on top of already making a ton.**' It fits
  the ecosystem playbook (iCloud, Apple Pay, AppleCare all capitalize on the iPhone base)." A sharp
  create-the-problem/sell-the-cure read (ties the walled-garden thread).
- [2020-01-15] **The scale — AirPods ≈ a Fortune 500 company.** "'~$8 billion from AirPods — **spun off as its own
  company, AirPods Inc. would be a Fortune 500** (mid-pack). AirPods are **Apple's most impactful and successful
  product since the original iPhone** — who wouldn't copy that?'"
- [2020-01-15] **Everyone followed the exact same move.** "'Google mocked the jack, then dropped it and shipped
  Pixel Buds; OnePlus, Huawei (FreeBuds), Razer (Hammerheads — "I feel like I've seen that shape"), Amazon, Microsoft
  (Surface Buds), and finally **Samsung** (jack gone on the Note 10 / S20, pushing Galaxy Buds that charge on the
  phone's back). The **headphone jack is becoming an enthusiast feature** — headlines when one appears.'"
- [2020-01-15] **The pros/cons of the wireless push — competition upside.** "'Downsides: dongles, and **wireless
  audio just isn't there yet** (latency makes editing video/music with wireless painful). But the upside is the same
  as the cause: **everyone is now racing to make the best wireless earbuds** — reverse wireless charging, Razer
  minimizing latency, the Sony XM3's class-leading ANC, assistants in all of them. Everyone working toward a better
  wireless future.'" His competition-makes-it-better lens again.
- [2020-01-15] **The shameless clones (a creator-integrity note).** "'Beyond the legit competitors, there's a world
  of **blatant AirPods ripoffs (TWS i500s), and companies using STOLEN footage from my AirPods Pro review in
  Instagram ads** to sell fake drop-shipped clones.'" A dated note on content theft.
