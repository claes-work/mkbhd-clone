# Samsung Z Flip 5 Review: I Was Wrong! (@mkbhd, 2023-08-09)

## Summary
A **course-correction** review: in his impressions he'd said Samsung "settled," but after using the **Z Flip 5** for
days — **"I was wrong about this phone... this is actually one of the biggest updates yet."** Three new things, in
order of importance: **(1)** the new overclocked Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 (great, but same battery, no efficiency win);
**(2)** the **hinge** — "you can't ace a folding phone without acing the hinge" — now **fewer parts (durability), a
gentler fold radius, and, most important, it folds completely flat** (no gap for dust — every foldable is racing to
"gapless close"); **(3)** the big one, the **3.4" Flex Window** cover screen. He admits a second wrong prediction:
he thought he'd barely use the bigger outer screen and would enjoy "the barrier against endless Doom scrolling" —
but he found himself doing **notification triage on it** far more (better than opening the phone and accidentally
Doom-scrolling TikTok). His framework for outside-vs-open: **it's about time** — how long the action takes (quick =
green light for the outside; a two-hour Netflix doc or writing an email = red light; lots in the middle is up to
you). Feedback: make the outer screen LTPO (it's 60 Hz) and higher-res; add DeX. Verdict: **"the best folding phone
on the planet for the most people right now — the iPhone of Android foldables,"** the safest, easiest-to-recommend.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2023-08-09] **I was wrong — one of the biggest updates yet.** "'I was wrong about the Z Flip 5. In my impressions
  I said they'd settled — but after using it, this is actually one of the biggest updates yet. Three main things: the
  chip, the hinge, and the new outside display, in that order of importance.'"
- [2023-08-09] **The hinge — you can't ace a foldable without acing the hinge.** "'The hinge is the most important
  physical part of a folding phone — you can't ace a foldable without acing the hinge. Samsung simplified it (fewer
  moving parts = durability), gave the OLED a gentler fold radius, and, most importantly, it now folds completely
  flat — no gap for dust. Every foldable is working toward that gapless close.'"
- [2023-08-09] **I was wrong again — I use the outer screen for notification triage.** "'I thought I'd barely use the
  bigger outer screen — I'd keep using the phone open and enjoy the barrier against endless Doom scrolling. But I was
  wrong: what I do much more, especially early in the day, is notification triage on the outside — way better than
  opening the phone and accidentally Doom-scrolling TikTok.'"
- [2023-08-09] **The framework — it's about time.** "'The point of a flipping phone remains the barrier between open
  and shut. Where's the line? My theory: it's about time — how long the action takes. Checking a notification or
  pausing music (two seconds) is a green light for the outside; a two-hour Netflix doc or writing an email is a red
  light; a bunch of stuff in the middle (a Spotify playlist, your calendar) is up to you.'"
- [2023-08-09] **The iPhone of Android foldables.** "'Should you buy it? It's the best folding phone on the planet
  for the most people right now — a great chip, solid cameras, decent battery, fast fingerprint reader, wireless
  charging, IPX8, 256 GB base for $1,000, five years of updates. Until they make a big form-factor jump, this is the
  safest, easiest-to-recommend foldable — the iPhone of Android foldables.'"
