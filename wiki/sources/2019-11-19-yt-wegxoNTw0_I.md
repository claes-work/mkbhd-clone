# The 108-Megapixel Smartphone Camera?! (@mkbhd, 2019-11-19)

## Summary
A deep-dive on the Xiaomi Mi Note 10's 108 MP camera. The core lesson revisits a favorite thread: **megapixels
don't mean everything, and software is a huge part of a camera** — a real 108 MP sensor is "really good…
sometimes."

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-11-19] **THE PRINCIPLE — megapixels ≠ quality (a recurring thread).** "'We've seen the megapixel push
  before (the **41 MP Nokia Lumia** was king for a while). But **just because something has more megapixels
  doesn't mean it's better — often it's the other way around.** Everyone settled around 12 MP for a reason.'"
  Reinforces the specs-skepticism thread ([[2019-07-26-yt-WpPw7lUXyI0]]).
- [2019-11-19] **The real 108 MP: center-sharp, edges fall apart.** "'The 108 MP photos are **significantly
  sharper/more detailed than any phone I've used — but mostly center sharpness.** The edges and corners **quickly
  distort and fall out of focus**, even on flat landscapes. Zooming into an iPhone-11-Pro-vs-Mi-Note-10 pair, you
  can **read a tiny sign on the Xiaomi that's just blurred lines on the iPhone** — that's the payoff of the
  resolution.'"
- [2019-11-19] **SOFTWARE IS THE CAMERA — the decisive point.** "'It **falls apart in low light** — the iPhone
  has far less noise and less smoothing, so **12 MP with excellent image processing beats 108 MP drowned in noise
  reduction.** Software is clearly a big part of a camera's experience.'" His consistent computational-photography
  belief.
- [2019-11-19] **The experience tax of a huge sensor on mid-range silicon.** "'108 MP mode has **>1s shutter lag**
  and slow processing/writing (20-30 MB files) on a **Snapdragon 730G** — it's an "extra mode," not the default
  (which shoots pixel-binned ~27 MP). **Not the highest-end phone**, so not equipped to spit out these files in
  real time.'"
- [2019-11-19] **Why he really tested it — the Galaxy S11 preview.** "'I got interested because of the rumor that
  **the Galaxy S11 will have a 100+ MP sensor** — exciting because a **higher-end phone can put the resources
  (faster storage, better CPU/RAM, a tried-and-true Samsung pipeline) toward making it feel normal**, not a bolt-
  on mode. Fingers crossed **we don't lose sight of** the actual camera-tech improvement (not just the number).'"

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "Just because something has a 16-megapixel camera doesn't mean it's necessarily better than the 12-megapixel
> camera. In fact, a lot of times it's the other way around."
> "108 megapixels with all this noise reduction is less detailed than 12 megapixels with excellent image
> processing — software is clearly a big part of a camera's experience."
