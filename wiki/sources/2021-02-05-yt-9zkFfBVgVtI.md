# Huawei Mate Xs Impressions: The Hottest Foldable! (@mkbhd, 2021-02-05)

## Summary
Impressions of the Huawei Mate Xs — the outer-folding foldable he'd never seen in person, finally secured by
dbrand (a viewer first offered to ship his). The durable read: it's **the most advanced, "sexiest" folding phone
he's ever seen — but the outward-fold still isn't technically ready** (the exposed back plastic scratches; the
screen protector's uneven edges). He expects the next Mate X to **switch to an inside fold** (like the Galaxy
Fold), which would make sense given the tech.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-02-05] **How he got it — dbrand secures the un-gettable.** "'A viewer offered to ship me his (too risky to
  insure/return), so we tried to buy one — **impossible to get in the US** — until **dbrand secured one. Never
  doubt dbrand's ability to secure hard-to-get things** (the same company that secured 12 PS5s + 12 Xbox Series X
  at peak demand).'"
- [2021-02-05] **The most advanced folding hardware — and the flip-around-for-selfies benefit.** "'Strikingly
  thin (5.5 mm at the thin part; 11 mm folded) — the "wings" are just screen and battery, all the components
  (Kirin 990, cameras) packed in the grip column. **I like that a folding phone lets you use the main cameras for
  everything, including selfies — flip it around and the back screen shows the subject the framing.**'"
- [2021-02-05] **The crease + the screen-protector problem.** "'The crease is **as good as it gets with current
  tech** — you don't notice it looking at content, only running a finger over it or catching the light. But the
  **built-in screen protector's uneven edges caught my eye all the time** (dust collecting) — I know better than
  to pull it off.'"
- [2021-02-05] **The exposed-back problem — "when you put it down, it touches the ground."** "'I've set it down
  like a normal phone too many times and gotten scratches on the back plastic — **when you put an outer-fold down,
  the plastic touches the surface** ("scratches at a level one, deeper grooves at a level breathing too hard").
  The included bumper case is the best solution — less elegant, but no scratches.'"
- [2021-02-05] **THE VERDICT — most advanced, but the outer-fold "isn't technically ready" → inside-fold next.** "'On
  one hand, **the most advanced, sexiest folding phone I've ever seen** — stunning hardware. On the other, **like
  I said with the FlexPai, the outside-fold isn't quite technically ready.** Word is the **next Mate X switches to
  an inside fold like the Galaxy Fold — which makes sense given the tech.** Do we go inside-fold now and come back
  to the outside-fold when the tech is better? We'll see.'" (Cf. [[2020-12-15-yt-4oco9pLw13E]].)

## Notable verbatim quotes
