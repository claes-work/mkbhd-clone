# What's on my Phone 2026!

## Summary

A "What's on my Phone" tour (his first in ~5 years) — a **biography / personal-preferences
source** documenting his exact daily setup. Confirms he's a **two-phone person** (a primary
Android + a secondary iPhone), currently the **OnePlus 15 + iPhone 17 Pro (Orange)**, and
walks his home screens and go-to apps. Notable: he keeps **Gemini on both lock screens
because "Siri is bad,"** regrets the orange iPhone, and reveals personal details (a home
solar/battery setup, EV widgets, fantasy leagues).

## Key claims (biography / preferences marked ⬥)

- **[2025-11-26]** ⬥ He's been a **two-phone person for a long time** — two SIMs, two numbers,
  **always one Android + one iPhone**; the **Android is his main phone** (the number people have),
  the iPhone is secondary. (paraphrase)
- **[2025-11-26]** ⬥ Between review units, the phones he's **gone back to most this year**: Galaxy
  S25 Ultra → Pixel 10 Pro → now the **OnePlus 15**. He almost settled on **OnePlus 15 + iPhone
  Air**, but **both having subpar cameras was the deal-breaker**, so he's on the **OnePlus 15 +
  iPhone 17 Pro (Orange)**. (paraphrase)
- **[2025-11-26]** ⬥ He **regrets the orange iPhone** ("I wish there was a black one"; the orange
  bezels bug him — he made an orange wallpaper "to own the Creamsicle thing"). (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-11-26]** ⬥ **"I have Gemini on the lock screen because Siri is bad and I never wanna use
  it"** — on both phones. He keeps **Liquid Glass on** to know "what people's default iPhone
  experience is." (quote)
- **[2025-11-26]** ⬥ Core apps: **TickTick** (tasks — "I basically live in the TickTick app... it's
  how I remember to do anything in life"; mapped to the iPhone Action button), **Spotify** (strictly
  music — "I don't like having [podcasts and music] all in one place"), **Google Messages**,
  **Relay for Reddit** (paid), **Notion** (studio), **CARROT Weather** (a Dark Sky clone + more —
  "Dark Sky was one of the best weather apps of all time" and died), **Pocket Casts / Neuecast**
  (podcasts), **Flighty** (flights — "one of the most useful apps of all time"), **Blip** (cross-
  platform AirDrop), **Raindrop.io** (replacing the dead Pocket), **Athlytic** (turns Apple Watch/
  Health data into Whoop-style recovery metrics — "I wear an Apple Watch and don't wanna also wear
  another thing"), **Waze**, **Superhuman** (email), **Authy**, **Yahoo Fantasy** ("get crushed in
  two leagues"). (paraphrase)
- **[2025-11-26]** ⬥ Personal-life details: the **Tesla app on his phone is for the solar + battery
  setup at his house, not a Tesla vehicle**; a home screen of **Porsche / Rivian / Tesla real-time
  widgets** (EV/energy setups he tracks); he keeps **long-term stuff (car key, authenticator) on the
  iPhone** because he swaps Android review units so often. (paraphrase)
- **[2025-11-26]** ⬥ He notes app-ecosystem churn over 5 years: Dark Sky, Pocket, and (his desktop
  use of) Arc all died; praises **OxygenOS for being cleanly set up** so he barely changes settings
  — "not like the Samsung phone days where I was changing a whole bunch of default settings."
  (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-11-26]** ⬥ A minor recurring gripe: **iPhone widgets still don't scroll** ("they've had
  widgets for a couple years... let us scroll"). (quote)
- **[2025-11-26]** Plugs the **new mkbhd.com merch refresh**. (paraphrase — creator-business)
