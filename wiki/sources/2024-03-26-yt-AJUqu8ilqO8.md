# The Vinfast VF8 is.... trying

## Summary

Responding to viewers who asked whether it's worse than the Fisker, Marques reviews the **VinFast
VF8** — a Model-Y-sized crossover ($40–50k) from a huge but EV-new Vietnamese company. His verdict:
**not the worst car he's reviewed** (less buggy than the Fisker), but **laggy and behind the times** —
a programmed-in throttle delay that kills EV instant torque, vague steering, a "Kindle Fire 2013" UI.
It crystallizes his recurring buying question: **new-cheap-tech vs. a depreciated older flagship**,
and he'd take the flagship. Fully Marques-narrated.

## Key claims

- **[2024-03-26]** ⭐ The framing (his smartphone analogy applied to cars): **"It brings back the age-old
  smartphone question — would you rather buy a brand-new cheap piece of tech, or a depreciated older
  flagship? This one makes a good argument for the depreciated older flagship."** (quote)
- **[2024-03-26]** The good fundamentals: power lift-gate, real HVAC controls ("a lot of these Tesla-
  copiers try to do everything on the screen; because this software is so bad, I'm happy there are real
  buttons"), **an HUD with no gauge screen — "maybe Tesla should take notes from VinFast"**, wireless
  CarPlay/Android Auto, heated+ventilated front AND rear seats, a physical volume knob. (quote)
- **[2024-03-26]** ⚠️ The bad: vegan-leather + cheap plastics; a **"Kindle Fire 2013" UI** (Asphalt 9 /
  Nancy Drew preloaded); **weirdly low-frame-rate "1990s bad" cameras**; a **programmed-in throttle
  delay** ("the nice thing about an EV is instant torque — this builds it up; I don't see the point");
  vague, floaty steering (it weighs ~1,000 lb more than a Model Y); rolls back on hills. ~200–230 mi
  range. Several **shared quirks with the Fisker** (walk-up key dead, seatbelt-gates-parking-brake). (quote)
- **[2024-03-26]** ⭐ Verdict + the real question: "Would I take this over the Fisker? Yes. But the more
  important question — **would you spend $50k on this when you could get an older Model S, or a 3–4-year-
  old more-refined car, or even a Model Y for $50–55k today?** VinFast is a gigantic company but at the
  mercy of being new to EVs; they'll get better. I just wouldn't recommend this one right now." (quote)
