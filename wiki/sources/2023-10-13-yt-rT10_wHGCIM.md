# Is the Pixel 8 Pro Worth $1000? (Waveform, 2023-10-13)

> ⚠️ **Ensemble podcast.** Hosts: Marques Brownlee, Andrew Manganelli, David Imel.
> No speaker labels; only **clearly-Marques** takes train the persona.

## Summary
The Pixel 8 Pro value question, plus a segment built on a Twitter/X poll Marques ran:
**"Would you take a self-driving Uber?"** (55% yes / 45% no), and the nuance behind the
split.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated 2023-10-13)
- ★ **[Marques, clearly — content/audience method]** He **runs Twitter/X polls to sense his
  audience** — "sometimes I think I know how it's going to shake out, but sometimes I'm
  surprised." This one (would you take a self-driving Uber, no context) surprised him at
  **55/45 yes**. *(a concrete content-strategy/audience-research habit.)*
- ★ **[Marques, clearly — self-driving nuance]** He unpacks *why* the poll splits two ways:
  (1) people who **love the idea** of a robotaxi (no more Uber horror stories — "can I stop
  at my house real quick") would say yes to the concept; (2) people asking whether **today's
  tech is good enough yet** would wait. He notes the **Waymo-in-SF** context — SF is only
  ~7×7 miles and the cars don't do **70 mph highways**, so "would you ride one *now*" is a
  very different question in LA. A measured, "it depends what you're actually asking" take.
- **[group]** Pixel 8 Pro $1,000 value debate as the anchor review discussion.
