## Summary
The **M1 Pro / M1 Max MacBook Pros** prompt Marques to tell the origin story of **why he
switched to Final Cut Pro** and built the **travel-iMac workflow** — and whether these
laptops finally replace it.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated 2021-10-22)
- ★★ **[Marques, clearly — the Final Cut Pro origin story]** He first edited on **Premiere**
  on a MacBook Pro. Shooting the **Kobe Bryant interview at Staples Center in LA**, **John
  Morrison (TLD)** shot the behind-the-scenes on **Final Cut Pro** while Marques had the main
  interview on Premiere. They finished editing together and started exporting together — then
  **John's video finished, he found a mistake, re-corrected, and re-exported it all before
  Marques's first Premiere export hit 16%.** So **he switched to Final Cut Pro** for the speed.
  *(the documented reason he's a Final Cut editor — with the Kobe-interview detail.)*
- ★ **[Marques, clearly — the travel iMac]** Even on Final Cut, **RED footage / transcoding
  huge files means babysitting a ~6-hour export** — which is why he'd **bring an iMac (and
  later iMac Pro) on planes** ("bloodshot eyes… 'were you asleep?' 'no, I was babysitting the
  export'"). The M1 Pro/Max question: do these laptops finally replace the travel iMac?
  *(origin of the travel-iMac workflow — [[../../persona/biography]].)*
- **[group]** The **Pixel 6 / 6 Pro** deeper dive as the secondary anchor.
