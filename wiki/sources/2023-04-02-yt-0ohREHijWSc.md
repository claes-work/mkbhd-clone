# The World Record Most Efficient Electric Car! (Mercedes Vision EQXX)

## Summary

Marques walks around the **Mercedes Vision EQXX** prototype — **the most efficient EV ever (1,000 km /
745 mi on one charge)** — as an explainer for **why EVs are shaped the way they are.** Every square
inch is optimized for the two resistances (aero + rolling), and many of those hacks (aero wheels,
sticker logos, small mirrors, teardrop shape) trace directly to production EVs making trade-offs. The
software **gamifies efficiency** so you hypermile every drive. Fully Marques-narrated.

## Key claims

- **[2023-04-02]** The frame: "A lot of what I'll show you traces to why electric cars are shaped the
  way they are." Two resistances: **rolling (tires) + air.** The EQXX has **the lowest drag coefficient
  of any car ever (0.17)** — 0.18 with the active rear diffuser retracted. 745 mi on one charge, a
  100 kWh battery, RWD, electronically limited to 140 km/h, all for efficiency. (paraphrase)
- **[2023-04-02]** The aero hacks (→ production EVs): active front flaps, relief outlets, **aero wheels**
  ("a huge reason electric cars have uglier wheels — they're optimizing airflow"), narrow low-rolling-
  resistance tires, a **sticker logo** (saves weight + doesn't disturb airflow), small mirrors instead
  of cameras ("the display's power draw roughly canceled the aero savings from cameras"), a long teardrop
  tail. **"Certain production cars use some of these and then have to make sacrifices elsewhere."** (quote)
- **[2023-04-02]** ⚠️ Solar skepticism (consistent): the roof is solar, but **"I don't buy going by
  solar alone — it's just a math equation; against a 100 kWh battery, 500 Wh/day is a couple of miles.
  Solar's most useful when it offsets a bit, or on a hybrid with a gas backup."** (quote)
- **[2023-04-02]** ⭐ The software: **"the gamification of efficient driving"** — a game-engine-rendered
  micro-LED display shows real-time energy use, the ideal route/speed for max range, and even real-time
  wind speed (from air-pressure sensors); accelerate too hard and it turns red. **"Everything in this
  car is designed to let you hypermile every time — most cars balance sporty/comfortable/luxurious;
  this one turns the efficiency knob all the way up."** (quote)

## Notable verbatim quotes

- "I don't really buy the idea of only going by solar — it's just a math equation."
