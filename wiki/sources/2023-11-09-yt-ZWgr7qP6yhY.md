# Space Black M3 Max MacBook Pro Review: We Can Game Now?! (@mkbhd, 2023-11-09)

## Summary
The full review — "two new things: the color and the chip." The **Space Black is "just a darker gray"** (a new
anodization chemistry, not a coating — better on fingerprints than most black laptops, but "if you want a black
laptop, this isn't it"; partial credit — black stickers and cable, still a white brick). The **M3 Max** is a solid
bump (~20–30% CPU, a bigger single-core jump than expected; the GPU up to M1 Ultra / ~RTX 3070 level with
first-ever **hardware ray tracing** on a Mac). That raises the "we can game now?" question — and his real answer:
**the Mac's problem was never power, it's that the games aren't there.** "Lies of P" plays beautifully at 4K, "but I
just don't care about that game" — **"if you're going to buy a computer this expensive to game, you're not buying a
Mac"** (the games you're enthusiastic about aren't available). The personal arc is the point: at the event he was
excited enough that he **ordered a maxed M3 Max in space black — then, after testing the review unit, canceled it**,
because "the color isn't as impressive as I hoped and the chip won't make a meaningful difference to my workflow."
**He'll keep dailying the M1 Max (three years now) — "a testament to how good that thing is."** Verdict: like any
spec bump, it's **for people with older/Intel machines or a different tier**, where it unlocks something new — not
for M1/M2 Max owners.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2023-11-09] **Space black is just a darker gray.** "'The color is literally "Space Black" — a new anodization
  chemistry, not a coating, extra fingerprint-resistant. But in reality it's just a little darker than the old Space
  Gray. If you want a black laptop, this isn't it — they make black laptops already. I'll give partial credit; they
  didn't fully commit (still a white brick).'"
- [2023-11-09] **"We can game now?" — the games aren't there.** "'The M3 Max GPU is up at M1 Ultra / ~RTX 3070 level
  with hardware ray tracing. So you think gaming — but the reason people don't associate gaming with Macs isn't power,
  it's that the games they're enthusiastic about aren't available (Witcher, Fallout, Apex). Lies of P plays
  beautifully at 4K, but I just don't care about that game. If you're going to buy a computer this expensive to game,
  you're not buying a Mac.'"
- [2023-11-09] **I ordered a maxed M3 Max — then canceled it.** "'At the event I was excited enough that I clicked
  order and bought a maxed M3 Max in space black. But after actually testing the review unit — the color isn't as
  impressive as I hoped, and the chip won't make a meaningful difference to my workflow (a couple of videos I edit
  on the go). So I logged in and canceled. Turns out it's not for me.'"
- [2023-11-09] **Still the M1 Max, three years — a testament.** "'I'll keep dailying the M1 Max 16" I've had for
  three years now. That's a testament to how good that thing is. Maybe I'll do a two-or-three-years-later review.'"
- [2023-11-09] **It's a spec bump — for older machines.** "'If you already have an M-chip MacBook in the tier you
  want — an M1/M2 Pro or Max — the M3 version isn't a huge difference. Like any spec bump, it's far more for people
  with older/Intel machines or a different tier, where it unlocks something new. That's what's great about them —
  you get a better machine than ever. For me, I'm keeping the M1 Max.'"
