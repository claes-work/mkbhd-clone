# iPhone X Revisited: Still Worth $1000?! (@mkbhd, 2018-04-28)

## Summary
A **6-month "Revisited"** long-term review of the iPhone X — an example of his revisit format (how does the hype
hold up once you've actually lived with it?). Verdict: it's aged gracefully and is still one of the best phones,
with the same handful of stubborn iPhone caveats — Face ID on a table, the huge notch, and its extreme
slipperiness (which cost him one unit).

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-04-28] **The revisit format + verdict.** "The hype died down; we've actually used it and had our share of
  issues or non-issues — so is it still worth $1000? **It's held up really well, which is not easy to do.**" The
  measured, lived-with reassessment (vs the launch review).
- [2018-04-28] **The durability story — "the slipperiest phone of all time."** "This is my **second** iPhone X: the
  first sat on a counter, **buzzed itself off the edge with notifications, fell ~5 feet, and shattered.** You're
  not supposed to drop any phone from five feet — but **a glass phone is still a glass phone**, and this one is
  super slippery. I wouldn't be surprised if it's the **#1 most-cased phone ever.**" Honest, self-deprecating.
- [2018-04-28] **Ages gracefully — Apple's long-support edge.** Glass + stainless steel wears predictably (micro-
  scratches give the steel a "texture"); "Apple products age gracefully — I have no concerns iPhone X keeps
  getting iOS updates for years." Notes this is the first iPhone since Apple let users choose **performance vs
  battery** (the throttling controversy) — a watch-item.
- [2018-04-28] **Still a top camera + power.** "As fast as tech moves, iPhone X is still the **second or third best
  smartphone camera on Earth**; the A11 Bionic is a beast and will be for a while." Consistent with his
  Pixel-2-#1 ranking.
- [2018-04-28] **The red-iPhone-X wish (ties to the same-batch RED iPhone 8).** Wishes for a red iPhone X; the best
  guess for why there isn't one is that the **stainless steel band couldn't be dyed red** the way the RED iPhone
  8's aluminum could — "actually, a **black side band with a red back** would've looked great." Prefers the
  **silver** X to carry (shows fewer fingerprints). Links to [[2018-04-11-yt-4O9dNuiOWDg]].
- [2018-04-28] **The persistent flaws — "stubborn little iPhone things."** **Face ID** remains "my most
  frustrating part" — no faster, and annoying **off-axis on a table** (needs a wider/downward-facing dot array);
  the **notch is huge** ("might be the biggest of any phone that has one" — he shrinks full-screen video to avoid
  it); lock-screen controls still fixed. "These are the stubborn little things about the iPhone — you get used to
  it." Ties to [[2018-03-14-yt-xIvaQ1Hv2Ug]].
- [2018-04-28] **Forward look — iPhone 11 wishlist.** "People won't spend $1000 two years in a row on the same
  form factor, so what does Apple do next? I'm hoping for two things: **a bigger-screen version** (an X the size
  of the 8 Plus) and **a smaller notch.**" Calibrated anticipation.
