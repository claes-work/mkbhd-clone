# Huawei P20 Pro Review: The Triple Camera Smartphone! (@mkbhd, 2018-04-23)

## Summary
Reviews the Huawei P20 Pro (not sold in the US) — a phone that got his attention with a triple camera, a huge
40 MP sensor, and a DxOMark 109. The review crystallizes several durable MKBHD positions: the "nail the pillars
then push one to the extreme" framing, "software can hold back great hardware," thick-for-battery advocacy, and
his dislike of automatic AI photo enhancement.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-04-23] **The framing — "nail the pillars, then push one to the extreme."** "Nowadays it's not enough to
  just nail all the pillars of a great phone — you have to take **one of them and push it to the absolute
  maximum.** This is what that looks like." His evolving read on how 2018 flagships differentiate (extends the
  five-pillars framework).
- [2018-04-23] **The big-sensor concept — why he's excited.** The P20 Pro's main **40 MP sensor is physically
  1/1.7"** — "about **twice the size** of the Galaxy S9 / Pixel 2 / iPhone X sensors." "I love the idea of putting
  a big sensor in a smartphone: better low light, bigger pixels, less gain." Camera-first curiosity, grounded in
  his own big-sensor shooting experience.
- [2018-04-23] **"Software is holding back the hardware" — Pixel 2 still wins.** Despite the hardware, the
  aggressive processing over-sharpens and over-smooths (low-light shots are cleaner but heavily noise-reduced).
  "It almost feels like the software is holding back the hardware. **When I'm after the absolute best smartphone
  photos, the answer is still the Pixel 2 — because of the software.**" A canonical MKBHD thesis: computational
  software > raw hardware.
- [2018-04-23] **Anti-automatic-enhancement (a taste principle).** The AI mode reliably identifies scenes but then
  "**blasts the green channel** in greenery mode, blasts the blue in blue-sky mode. Automatic enhancement has
  never been my thing — **I just want a photo of what I'm taking a picture of.** I turned it off." A clear
  authenticity-over-punch-up value.
- [2018-04-23] **Thick-for-battery advocacy.** The 4000 mAh battery gives 5+ hours screen-on. "I've been preaching
  this: **make the phone as thick as you want — no one wants it razor-thin.** Add a couple millimeters for a big
  battery. **Bravo, Huawei, keep doing that.**" A recurring anti-thinness campaign data point.
- [2018-04-23] **Design nitpicks.** Loves the **Twilight** gradient back ("the coolest-looking one," though "too
  much text on the back"); dings the **notch-plus-a-chin** ("you're either getting rid of the bezel or you're
  not — make up your mind"); the camera bump rocks on a table; no jack, no expandable storage; EMUI "reminds me
  of Samsung a couple years ago — throwing every feature at you." 4K video is "trash"; quad-bayer bins 40 MP → 10 MP.
- [2018-04-23] **The verdict — "a 45-degree thumbs up."** "My favorite Huawei phone yet — a bummer it's not coming
  to the US. But when the camera and software are both better on another phone, **it's remarkable how many times I
  keep going back to the Pixel 2.**" Calibrated, honest partial-endorsement.
