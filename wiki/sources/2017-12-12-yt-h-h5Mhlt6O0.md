# iMac Pro: 1 Week Impressions! (@mkbhd, 2017-12-12)

## Summary
One week with the iMac Pro — the follow-through on his June "I'd give the iMac Pro a chance" promise from the
"is it a trap?" video. Clone-relevant as a production-workflow milestone: **it replaced his 2013 trash-can Mac
Pro** as his editing rig, and he's editing this very video on it — while the modular Mac Pro still looms in 2018.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2017-12-12] **Follows through on the "give it a chance" promise (production milestone).** "We (creative
  pros on Final Cut/Motion) were waiting for a new Apple Pro desktop, and we finally got it. I said in the
  'is it a trap?' video I'd give the iMac Pro a chance before the modular Mac Pro comes in 2018 — so shout out
  to Apple for getting one to the studio. **It's replaced the 2013 trash-can Mac Pro that's been on my desk
  since 2013 — we needed that upgrade** — and this video is edited on it." A concrete rig-replacement node
  (the trash-can Mac Pro he'd griped about for years, finally retired).
- [2017-12-12] **"On the outside it's just an iMac; what makes it a Pro is all on the inside."** "Almost
  identical to any 27" 5K iMac if you paint it space gray, plus better mics/camera/speakers — the design,
  stand, display, bezels are all the same. What matters is inside." Reads the product honestly (it's not a
  visual redesign).
- [2017-12-12] **His config + the sweet-spot logic.** "A **10-core iMac Pro, 128 GB RAM, the 16 GB Vega GPU**
  — somewhere in the sweet spot between core count and clock speed. The 8/10-core ship early; the 14/18-core
  wait till early next year. All workstation-grade Intel Xeon-W + AMD Vega, as expected." The buy-the-sweet-
  spot reasoning.
- [2017-12-12] **The unresolved tension (the modular Mac Pro still looms).** Restates the context from the
  trap video: the modular full Mac Pro is coming in 2018 — so the iMac Pro is the interim answer for pros
  whose 2013 machines are on their last legs. (Impressions, not a full review.)
