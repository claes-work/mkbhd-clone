# What's in my Tech Bag! [2019] (@mkbhd, 2019-07-16)

## Summary
An updated everyday-carry / tech-bag tour. Primarily a **biography/personal-setup** source: his actual 2019 daily
drivers, camera, and gear, and a stated preference for a **minimal** carry over the "super high-end, very
expensive tech bags out there."

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-07-16] **His 2019 daily-driver phones.** "'Still the **OnePlus 7 Pro** as the daily and the **iPhone XS
  Max** (space gray) — those are the smartphone daily drivers of choice when I'm not testing other stuff.'" A
  datable personal-use data point (matches his 7 Pro raving).
- [2019-07-16] **The everyday camera — Canon EOS R (no longer the Hasselblad).** "'The camera of choice is **no
  longer the Hasselblad** — I've been really enjoying the **Canon EOS R** with the 28-70 f/2 lens.' Not a medium-
  format 50 MP, but 'much faster,' 30 MP, great Canon color science and dual-pixel autofocus. 'I don't shoot much
  video on it right now — I have another camera in mind I'm testing for that.'" His gear evolution.
- [2019-07-16] **The bag + the minimal philosophy.** "The **Peak Design Everyday Backpack (20L)** — 'easily the
  most expensive backpack I've ever bought,' charcoal (for the **red accents**, not matte black this time). 'I got
  so picky about backpacks.' The point of the video: 'there's so many super high-end, very expensive tech bags out
  there — **this is a bit more of a minimal setup.**'" Stated taste toward minimal/practical.
- [2019-07-16] **The rest of the carry.** "Sony noise-cancelling headphones ('I did really like the Bose, but
  these are still my go-tos' — cf. [[2019-07-02-yt-YPln3JP_gKs]]), an **11" iPad Pro on iPad OS 13** ('still doing
  the job for everything I need' — cf. [[2019-06-28-yt-dZ_Ab1XxxKg]]), an Anker battery, the McLaren OnePlus 30 W
  charger, AirPods, an Apple Pencil, and an **MKBHD-edition microfiber.** Spoiler: **no laptop.**" Confirms the
  iPad-only workflow is holding.
