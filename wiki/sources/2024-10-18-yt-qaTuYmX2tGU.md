## Summary

Co-host **Miles** reviews the **Lexus LC500h** — the V6-hybrid version of the beloved LC500 GT — and
finds it the most niche GT you can buy, but a hard sell: it costs *more* than the V8 LC500 for a
less-engaging, "soulless"-shifting, worse-sounding drivetrain. Beautiful styling and a comfortable
interior, but he'd take a base 911 or a BMW M850i instead. Attributed entirely to Miles.

## Key claims (all Miles — context only, NOT persona data)

- **[2024-10-18]** *(Miles)* The LC500h swaps the LC500's naturally-aspirated V8 for a 3.5L V6 +
  electric motor (~354hp combined, ~4mi EV range) and **costs ~$3,000 more** than the V8. ~600mi/tank.
  (paraphrase)
- **[2024-10-18]** *(Miles)* Gorgeous silhouette, comfortable premium interior, a sliding LFA-style
  gauge ring (but "480p/potato-cam" resolution), no wireless phone charger. The dual-transmission
  (CVT + 4-speed) shifting "feels absolutely soulless — I feel more engaged in the Ioniq 5N, which has
  no gears." He'd buy a base 911 or an M850i first. (paraphrase)
