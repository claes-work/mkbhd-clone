# AirPods 4 Review: Which Ones To Get? (@mkbhd, 2024-09-16)

## Summary
A review of the **two new AirPods 4** — **$129 base** and **$179 with active noise cancellation** — sorting out the
differences and whether the ANC is worth it. The buds are the same tweaked hard-tip shape (slightly better driver angle,
IP54, shorter stem) — and his personal verdict on fit is unchanged: **"if you had problems with previous AirPods,
you'll still have problems; if they fit, these fit"** (they still slowly work out of his ears). The real differences are
in the **case**: the ANC version adds a speaker (chime/find-my), **wireless charging**, but — a downside — **loses
MagSafe alignment** (the case is now slightly too small for the magnet ring). On the headline question, ANC: **"less
noise cancellation and more noise reduction"** — because the hard tip leaves air gaps, it's ~a 4–5/10 vs. AirPods Pro's
8/10, most useful in loud environments (trains, planes), barely noticeable in a quiet room. But the **transparency mode
"might be the best I've ever heard on any AirPods"** (natural, because some outside sound already leaks in). He takes a
sharp shot at the simultaneous **AirPods Max "refresh"**: "**all they did was add USB-C and new colors — no H2 chip, no
driver/material/mesh updates, no on-off switch — absolutely ridiculous** for a $550 flagship." (He explains the H2 chip
does the ANC/transparency/adaptive-audio processing + the nod/shake-to-answer-Siri trick, which works well.) His own
pick: **he's still a silicone-tip AirPods Pro person** (better seal → dramatically better ANC — and the Pros are only
~$20 more on Amazon) — but grants that many will happily save the $20 for the hard-tipped ones, whose real strength is
**the Apple-ecosystem seamless switching** and a generic-useful frequency response for everything.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2024-09-16] **Two versions; fit is unchanged.** "'There are two AirPods 4: $129 base and $179 with ANC — same
  identical-looking buds. The shape is tweaked, but if you had problems with previous hard-tip AirPods, you'll still
  have problems; if they fit your ears, these fit. They still slowly work their way out of mine.'"
- [2024-09-16] **The case is where the difference is — but MagSafe is gone.** "'The ANC case adds a speaker (chime/find-
  my) and wireless charging, but it lost MagSafe — the case is now slightly too small for the magnet ring, so it'll
  charge on a Qi pad but won't stick and align. USB-C is the upside; MagSafe gone is the downside.'"
- [2024-09-16] **The ANC is really noise *reduction*, not cancellation.** "'Because the hard tip leaves little air
  gaps, some sound gets in around the bud. If AirPods Pro are an 8/10 for ANC, these hard-tipped ones are about a 4 or
  5 — less noise cancellation, more noise reduction. Useful in loud places (trains, planes), barely noticeable in a
  quiet room. But the transparency mode might be the best I've heard on any AirPods.'"
- [2024-09-16] **The AirPods Max USB-C "refresh" is ridiculous.** "'They also refreshed the AirPods Max — and I use
  "refresh" lightly: all they did was make it USB-C and add colors. No H2 chip, no driver, material, mesh, or case
  updates, no on-off switch, no new features. That's absolutely ridiculous for a $550 flagship. The H2 chip does the
  ANC/transparency/adaptive-audio processing and the nod/shake-to-answer-Siri trick, which works great — and the Max
  didn't even get it.'"
- [2024-09-16] **I'm still a silicone-tip AirPods Pro person.** "'Personally I'm still a silicone-ear-tip person — they
  seal much better, which makes the ANC dramatically better, so I lean AirPods Pro (only ~$20 more on Amazon right now).
  But people will happily save the $20 for the hard-tipped ones. Others beat AirPods in individual categories (fit,
  battery, sound), but AirPods' strength is the ecosystem — seamless switching across your Apple devices — and a
  generic-useful frequency response for everything.'"
