# So This is Peak Smartphone ★★

## Summary

The companion to "Peak Foldable": using the maxed-out **Oppo Find X9 Ultra** (which "makes
Samsung's S26 Ultra feel not so ultra"), Marques asks whether we've hit **peak slab phone**.
Walking his **five pillars** (display, battery, build, performance, cameras), he argues four
are essentially "watermark achieved" — but **cameras are the one stuck pillar**, because the
watermark people now want ("as good as a real camera") is **a battle against physics that can
never be won**. His reframe: a truly great smartphone camera isn't a DSLR replacement — it's
the one that **"minimizes the misses and maximizes the makes"** (the self-correcting basketball
hoop). A landmark camera-philosophy + market-maturity essay. ★★.

## Key claims

- **[2026-04-21]** The **Oppo Find X9 Ultra** (Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5, 7,050mAh, 6.8" OLED, a
  huge 200MP-heavy camera system) is "the most ridiculous camera system I've ever [seen]" — the
  occasion to ask about **peak slab phone** ("a moving goalpost"). (paraphrase)
- **[2026-04-21]** Via the **five pillars**: **display** — "everyone just wanted a screen that
  looks good all the time," now achieved (big, flat, bright OLED, high-refresh, 1-nit-to-blinding,
  hole-punch — curved edges "came and went"); **battery** — "I want it to last all day no matter
  what," achieved (silicon-carbon took flagships 5,000→6,000→7,050mAh; his battery-award phone is
  "actually a two-day phone"); **performance** — high-end chip + smooth + long updates = box
  checked; **build** — metal, IP69, "built like a tank." **Four watermarks reached.** (paraphrase)
- **[2026-04-21]** **Cameras are the stuck pillar.** Presentations went "from one or two slides to
  20 slides," and the watermark people now want is **"a smartphone camera as good as a regular
  camera"** — but **"this is a battle against physics that can never fully be won."** (quote)
- **[2026-04-21]** The X9 Ultra has five sensors, each "the best or among the best numbers I've ever
  seen" (a **200MP main**, a **200MP 3x telephoto** — the biggest telephoto sensor ever in a phone —
  a 50MP 10x, a 50MP ultrawide, and a dedicated **True Color** sensor). "A shocker to no one, it's a
  great camera system" (Oppo's X9 Pro won his camera of the year). But **"super versatile ≠ as good
  quality as a regular camera — those are two different things."** (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2026-04-21]** The irony: Oppo markets it as a **Hasselblad** (licensing, accent colors, based
  on the **Hasselblad X2D Mark II**, which he owns) — **"these are opposite cameras, they could not
  be further apart."** The smartphone is a computer trying its hardest **"to not let you take a bad
  picture"** (computational photography); the X2D is a deliberate pro camera with **no computational
  photography and no video mode.** "It's a mistake to compare these two." (quote)
- **[2026-04-21]** The reframe: **"a truly great smartphone camera minimizes the misses and maximizes
  the makes"** — like **Stuff Made Here's self-moving basketball hoop** where "no matter how bad your
  shot is, you can't miss," and you might mistake that for your own skill. Enthusiasts don't love the
  over-processed look, so "**if you can do that without the over-processed look, that would be
  great.**" The updated watermark: **"a great smartphone camera can take a really good photo or video
  anytime."** (quote)
- **[2026-04-21]** The **lens-attachment era**: the X9 Ultra (Hasselblad) and the **Vivo X300 Pro**
  (Zeiss) ship real lens/battery-grip accessories (400mm zoom) — "hilarious that you can carry all
  this and technically say you shot it on your smartphone," but it's still **mimicking the real
  thing**; the strength stays in **versatility.** (paraphrase)
- **[2026-04-21]** The governing analogy: **it's like turning an iPad into a laptop** — "you can kind
  of do it," good enough for most people's real use, but "nobody sane would say it's a one-to-one
  replacement for a pro-level laptop." Pro cameras keep their place ("what I keep shooting these
  videos with"), but **he'll keep shooting Auto Focus exclusively on smartphones** (the latest on the
  X9 Ultra) to show how good they've gotten. (paraphrase + quote)
