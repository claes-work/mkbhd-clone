# We Drove the Tesla Cybertruck! (Waveform, 2023-12-01)

> ⚠️ **Ensemble podcast.** Hosts: Marques Brownlee, Andrew Manganelli, David Imel (+ Ellis
> on the shoot). No speaker labels, but the Cybertruck segment is **unmistakably Marques**
> (first-person: "I'm one of three people outside Tesla to have driven it"). **High yield.**

## Summary
Two big segments: Marques's hands-on drive of the **Tesla Cybertruck** (he was one of ~3
people outside Tesla to have driven it pre-delivery), and the **dbrand vs. Casetify**
teardown-skin ripping controversy.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated 2023-12-01)
- ★ **[Marques, clearly — the Cybertruck drive]** He drove the triple-motor Cybertruck.
  Verdict: **every spec except 0–60 came in worse than promised — a first for a Tesla**
  (they usually deliver, just late). Numbers: **340 mi range** (promised 500), **11,000 lb
  tow** (promised 14,000), **2.7 s 0–60** (better than the promised 2.9), triple-motor
  **$100,000** (promised $69,900), ~850 hp; ~5% smaller in every dimension than the
  concept.
- ★ **[Marques, clearly — his favorite thing]** *"The number-one most unique, most amazing
  thing about the Cybertruck is not the way it looks — it's the way it drives."* Tesla's
  **first steer-by-wire** + a flattened "squircle" wheel, **up to 10° rear-axle steering**,
  a **170° full-lock turn (never hand-over-hand)** — **"the most nimble truck I've ever
  driven,"** feels like steering with a joystick; much easier to place than a Rivian or
  F-150. He explains **torque vectoring** (independent rear-wheel motors, "like F1 cars").
- **[Marques, clearly — car-guy aside]** Joke: he drives at "11 and 4" hand position.
- **[group — creator-business]** **dbrand vs. Casetify:** Casetify (via JerryRigEverything/
  Zack's teardown video) allegedly **ripped dbrand's teardown-skin designs** — badly
  Photoshopped-out Easter eggs gave it away; the earlier "Inside Parts" line even showed
  the *wrong phone's* internals regardless of model. *(dbrand is a frequent MKBHD sponsor;
  a design-integrity story he'd care about.)*
