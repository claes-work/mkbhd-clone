# The ROG Phone 7 Ultimate is Just Ridiculous! (@mkbhd, 2023-04-13)

## Summary
A review of the **Asus ROG Phone 7 Ultimate** as the purest expression of purpose-built excess. The spec sheet has
"everything" (Snapdragon 8 Gen 2, 16 GB, 512 GB UFS 4.0, a flat **165 Hz** OLED with 720 Hz touch sampling, a
**6,000 mAh split battery** with 65 W parallel charging, two USB-C ports + a headphone jack) — everything except a
world-class camera, **and that's fine: the camera is a "B/B-plus," and it's not why you buy this phone** (just good
enough not to make you mad). His two framing analogies. First, the phone is **"perfectly balanced"** (a split
battery with the motherboard in the middle so it's centered in landscape). Second — the thesis — it's like a **sports
car**: loaded with specialized tech most people don't need (the clip-on **AeroActive Cooler** that literally
**motorizes open a vent in the phone's back** to blow cool air through it, dropping surface temps ~20–25°C; air
triggers; a subwoofer) — **"gaming phones of today walk so flagships of tomorrow can run"** (trickle-down). And a
recurring MKBHD principle: **it uses basic off-the-shelf parts for the things that don't matter** (a normal optical
fingerprint reader, a B-plus camera) and **throws the kitchen sink only at what a gamer cares about** (frames,
latency, cooling).

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2023-04-13] **The camera is a B-plus — and that's fine, it's not the point.** "'It has everything except a
  world-class camera — but that's probably fine, it's a gaming phone. The objective is a camera good enough that
  you're not mad, but not one you're bragging about. I'll confidently give the whole suite a B. It's not why you buy
  this phone.'"
- [2023-04-13] **"Perfectly balanced."** "'You can't have a giant camera bump on a gaming phone — it has to be
  balanced for holding in landscape. That's why the split battery (3,000 + 3,000 mAh) with the motherboard in the
  middle — perfectly balanced, as all things should be. And the split lets them fast-charge in parallel (65 W, 0–100
  in ~45 min).'"
- [2023-04-13] **The AeroActive Cooler literally opens the phone.** "'The AeroActive Cooler 7 clips on, and a
  motorized lift-gate on the phone's back opens to allow active airflow through the side — it can bring the back
  surface down 20–25°C. Plus trigger buttons, RGB, a subwoofer that genuinely improves the sound. Their dedication
  to shaving a few degrees for a couple extra frames is impressive.'"
- [2023-04-13] **Off-the-shelf parts for what doesn't matter; the kitchen sink for what does.** "'I respect their
  willingness to use basic off-the-shelf parts for things that don't matter much — a normal optical fingerprint
  reader, a B-plus camera. But for anything a gamer wants — that last one or two fps, that last bit of latency —
  they'll throw the kitchen sink at it.'"
- [2023-04-13] **Gaming phones walk so flagships can run.** "'It feels like a sports car — a lot of ridiculous
  specialized tech unnecessary to most people, but it eventually trickles down into everyday cars/phones. In a few
  years regular flagships will have all this power and cooling; they'll just be normal phones. Gaming phones of
  today walk so flagships of tomorrow can run.'"
