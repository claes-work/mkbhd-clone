# Living with a Supercar! [Auto Focus Ep. 3] (@mkbhd, 2018-09-06)

## Summary
**Auto Focus Episode 3** — a week living with the Acura NSX as an "everyday supercar," explicitly framed by his
own **2020 Tesla Roadster deposit** ("what's it like to live with a supercar every day?"). Includes his supercar
definition, a raft of daily-driving observations, and his **first time ever on a track**.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-09-06] **The framing — tied to his Roadster deposit (BIOGRAPHY).** "**As someone who has a deposit in for a
  2020 Tesla Roadster**, it got me thinking — what's it like to live with a supercar every day?" The episode is
  research for a car he's actually buying. (Confirms the Roadster deposit; ⚠️ the "2020" slips, per the earlier
  contradiction-watch.)
- [2018-09-06] **His supercar definition.** "A supercar is **anything that makes big trade-offs for maximum driving
  performance.**" (The NSX qualifies: twin-turbo V6 + 3 electric motors, 0–60 in 2.9 s, 191 mph.) A clean,
  reusable definition.
- [2018-09-06] **"Living with it" observations.** How **low** you sit (you feel every bump; scraping the bumper on
  driveways/speed bumps is a constant hazard); **visibility** (others can't see you; but thin A-pillars help your
  own view); **storage is an afterthought** (the surprisingly usable rear trunk gets engine-hot); the hybrid uses
  its motors **for performance, not efficiency** (filling torque gap / turbo lag); the four modes (Eco/Sport/Sport+/
  Track); "the worst part is the infotainment — a backup cam that looks like 1994 — but at least it has **CarPlay +
  Android Auto** so you never touch the garbage built-in maps."
- [2018-09-06] **First time on a track (biography/craft).** At **Monticello Motor Club** with an owner-instructor
  (Ari) doing tutorial laps: "for a tech guy you're not afraid to throw a car around." The NSX is "much easier to
  drive than the Aventador" (which was harsh). A production-ambitious car episode.
- [2018-09-06] **The verdict — "I'm one of those people."** "**It is possible to live with a supercar.** People who
  don't love the trade-offs won't — but people who really love driving, who want a race car with a license plate,
  will make them. **And I feel like I'm one of those people.**" Self-locating.
