# Our Favorite Cars of 2025! Auto Focus Awards

## Summary

The second annual **Auto Focus Awards**, co-hosted by **Marques and Miles**, handing out
category awards for the cars they reviewed in 2025. A dialogue, so **only Marques-attributed
opinions train the persona** (Miles's takes — including the "Miles Award" — are context). Rich
in biography: **the Camry was his first car (2015)**, he's a **Rivian R1T owner**, he keeps a
**personal 0–60 leaderboard**, and he considered buying a **Volvo EX90**.

## Key claims — SUBJECT-ATTRIBUTED (Marques)

- **[2025-12-18]** ⬥ BIOGRAPHY: the **Hybrid of the Year / Most Efficient = the Toyota Camry Hybrid**
  — **"my first car. I first got my Camry back in 2015. No regrets, I put a ton of miles on it,
  averaged ~45mpg for its lifetime."** The 2025 Camry "is the only car we had all year I did not once
  think about the gas station — it went a whole week without one." (quote)
- **[2025-12-18]** ⬥ BIOGRAPHY: the **Best Performance honorable mention = his own Rivian R1T Quad**
  ("~1,000hp on a street tire, ran mid-10s at the drag strip… 2.7s 0–60, still top-three on **my
  little leaderboard where I log all my 0–60s in cars**, in a 7,000lb truck"). "I'm so happy with my
  first-gen." (quote)
- **[2025-12-18]** ⬥ BIOGRAPHY: the **Best Luxury = Volvo EX90** — "I'll die on this hill." Top-three
  car speakers he's ever heard, Rolls-Royce-Spectre-level isolation; **"I thought about getting one —
  do we need the Rivian anymore?"** (quote)
- **[2025-12-18]** The joint awards (co-hosted; Marques concurs): **EV of the Year = Lucid Gravity**
  (best EV you can actually buy despite no CarPlay/software bugs — "the packaging is second to none");
  **runner-up the Xiaomi SU7** (impressive, but "the value is theoretical" — you can't buy one here);
  **Performance = the 992.2 911 Turbo S**; **Bust of the Year = the Dodge Charger Daytona EV** (a bad
  EV *and* a bad muscle-car successor — "all we wanted was tire-spin"; Jeep Wagoneer S an honorable
  mention); **Most Improved = the McLaren Artura**; **Vehicle of the Year = the Hyundai Ioniq 9**
  ("does ~80% of the Gravity for tens of thousands less — such a complete package for the money").
  (paraphrase)
- **[2025-12-18]** ⬥ The **"Marques Award"** (his personal pick, "an irrational favorite I'm not
  necessarily recommending, I just really enjoyed it") = the **Volvo EX30** — "so much fun… **it feels
  like the iPhone Air — I wish it had a better battery and a little more capability, but it was too
  fun not to shout out.**" (quote)
