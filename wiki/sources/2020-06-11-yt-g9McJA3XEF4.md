# CyberTruck Phone Impressions... Ridiculous (@mkbhd, 2020-06-11)

## Summary
A look at the $7,000 Caviar "CyberPhone" — an iPhone 11 Pro in an angular metal case riding the CyberTruck press
wave. "Ridiculous, and nobody should buy one." Reinforces the **luxury/price-signaling** thesis and adds a
worse-to-actually-use angle.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-06-11] **The price is about the logo, not the materials (a wheels callback).** "'Why would a $1,000 phone
  wrapped in ~$300 of metal cost **$7,000?** Same as the **$700 Mac Pro wheels** — it's about the **Caviar logo**
  (they made the $100k gold iPhone too). Otterbox couldn't sell a metal case for more than ~$249 — but Caviar goes
  over-the-top (fancy packaging, certificate) so customers see **a tech-themed collectible riding the CyberTruck
  press wave.**'" Ties [[2020-05-09-yt-mBHY_Qaw5AI]].
- [2020-06-11] **The case makes the iPhone worse (the use-it angle).** "'People buy nice cars and garage them, but
  **using it (that's my job) it's bad**: a **sharp metal lip** for the swipe-up gesture, a **recessed Lightning
  port** that quality cables can't reach, **wireless charging nuked**, and an **ugly hole for the SIM tray** (with
  a special hook tool).'"
- [2020-06-11] **The CyberTruck-window moment.** "'Maybe it's a durability play? I **dropped it once, and — just
  like the CyberTruck's glass demo — the screen cracked.** Not a bulletproof strategy.'" A wry callback.
- [2020-06-11] **The tiers of absurdity.** "'This is the **CyberPhone Lite**; step up to the full CyberPhone ($7k
  with an ergonomic plate) or the **$20,000 "billionaire edition"** (silver + gold — a soft metal that won't
  survive drops).'" His "products you should never buy" register.

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "Where you and I see a six-month-old iPhone wrapped in a metal case for seven thousand dollars, Caviar and its
> customers potentially see another tech-themed accessory collectible that happens to be riding the press wave of
> the actual CyberTruck."
> "The more you actually hold this and try to use it, the more you realize this case actually makes the iPhone
> experience worse."
