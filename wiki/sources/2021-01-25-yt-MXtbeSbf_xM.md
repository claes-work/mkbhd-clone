# The Invisible Selfie Camera! (@mkbhd, 2021-01-25)

## Summary
A look at the ZTE Axon 20 5G — the **world's first under-display selfie camera.** The durable ideas: **we need
companies like ZTE to take the tech risk** (Samsung/Apple "would never ship this") to get the ball rolling; the
**under-display-tech improvement curve** (speakers and fingerprint readers started bad and got good fast — so this
will too); the verdict that **it's not worth the image-quality trade-off yet**; and a forward-looking **privacy
concern** about cameras you can't see.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-01-25] **How it works — and the "Charmin soft" result.** "'A 32 MP camera under a patch of display at half
  pixel density (720p-equivalent, gaps for the camera to see through); when active, a driver makes those pixels
  semi-transparent and a defogging algorithm reconstructs the image. **The selfies are soft — Charmin soft — like
  the lens had fingerprints on it;** the worst artifacts are in the highlights (you can even see the pixels light
  up when you pull the white notification shade over it).'"
- [2021-01-25] **The last, hardest step toward the all-screen phone.** "'We've watched the fingerprint reader go
  under the display and speakers move behind the glass — **the camera is the last thing left, and the hardest.**
  Pop-up cameras achieved the seamless front (and I liked them for the privacy of a physically blocked camera) but
  were a workaround — a moving part, worse for waterproofing, more cost/space.'"
- [2021-01-25] **THE THESIS — we need companies like ZTE to take the tech risk.** "'For as much as we talk about
  Samsung and Apple, **they would never ship this — so we need companies like ZTE to take that tech risk and put
  it out there to get the ball rolling, because now we actually have something to work with and judge.**'" His
  recurring reward-the-risk-taker value, applied to first-gen tech.
- [2021-01-25] **The improvement-curve optimism — how fast does it get good?** "'Every under-display tech started
  bad and got good quickly: **under-display speakers are now only slightly muffled; under-display fingerprint
  readers are only slightly slower/less reliable than a Touch ID button.** So it's no surprise the first under-
  screen camera is bad — the question is how fast it gets to "only a tiny bit softer." The people working on it are
  confident about better pixel-matching in future generations.'"
- [2021-01-25] **The verdict — not worth the trade-off yet.** "'Is hiding the camera worth the worse image
  quality? **In this first one, it's pretty clear: no.** But the real question for the future is — if it gets
  really good, **do you even want a hidden camera** at the expense of not knowing where it is?'"
- [2021-01-25] **THE PRIVACY CONCERN — cameras you can't see.** "'If this gets really good, what does it mean for
  privacy? Laptops could put the camera anywhere, further from the edge — and **could it be on all the time with no
  indicator? Imagine a hotel-room TV with a camera behind it — that's terrifying.** The next logical step is a
  software indicator light (like iOS did).'" A forward-looking ethics flag.
