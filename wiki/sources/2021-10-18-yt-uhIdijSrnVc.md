# NEW M1 Max MacBook Pro Reaction: The Ports are Back! (@mkbhd, 2021-10-18)

## Summary
Reactions to Apple's October event — "the MacBook Pro event." The landmark payoff: **Apple reversed three years of
bad hardware decisions because they listened** — MagSafe is back, the Touch Bar is gone (real function keys),
and **real ports are back** (HDMI, SD card slot, Thunderbolt, a higher-impedance headphone jack). Powered by the
new **M1 Pro / M1 Max** (Apple silicon "Unleashed," now with the GPU built in). For him **"the laptop I was
waiting for the most"** as a Final Cut editor — the **120 Hz mini-LED XDR display is the HDR-workflow unlock** he'd
wanted. The one weird call: the big notch.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-10-18] **"The laptop I was waiting for the most."** "'I'll stay neutral — it's very expensive, not for
  everybody. But **as a Final Cut editor, this is the laptop that applies to me the most and my weekly workflow.**
  Ever since I started editing 8K RED footage, I haven't edited on my laptop — the workflow was too constrained, so
  we go to the desktop. **This could be a game-changer.**'"
- [2021-10-18] **M1 Pro / M1 Max — Apple silicon "Unleashed" (the GPU built in).** "'After the M1 covered the
  baseline, we wondered when we'd get pro-level silicon for the higher-end MacBook Pros (still on Intel). Now: **the
  M1 Pro (10-core CPU, 16-core GPU, 70% faster CPU / 100% faster GPU than the M1) and the M1 Max (up to 32-core GPU,
  4× the M1's graphics, up to 64 GB).** They answered my question — **in a laptop, it makes sense to build the GPU
  in for efficiency's sake.** (This time they name-dropped the exact PC they compared to.)'"
- [2021-10-18] **"THE PORTS ARE BACK" — Apple listened.** "'They **went backwards on three years of questionable-
  to-bad hardware decisions and just said "we've been listening, you didn't like that, so we fixed it":** MagSafe
  back (67–140 W), **the Touch Bar gone (real function keys + Touch ID)**, and **real ports back — HDMI, an SD card
  slot ("why they ever got rid of it I'll never know"), three Thunderbolt 4, and a headphone jack that now drives
  higher-impedance headphones.** They got preachy patting themselves on the back for bringing back ports they
  removed — but I'm just glad they listened.'" A promotable Apple-course-correction moment (the "going backwards
  to 2015" prediction landing).
- [2021-10-18] **The display is the most Pro part — the HDR-workflow unlock.** "'Both sizes get a **120 Hz
  ProMotion mini-LED Liquid Retina XDR display (up to 1,600 nits) — the most Pro part, especially for video
  creators.** Real HDR high-res screens are **a massive unlock for the HDR workflow I've been thinking about — no
  separate HDR display needed.** And it's the **first-ever high-refresh Mac.**'"
- [2021-10-18] **The notch critique (no Face ID).** "'I love the reclaimed screen real estate (the menu bar lives
  there without overlapping content, on a 16:10 display). But **I don't know why the notch has to give us Pixel 3
  XL vibes — it's massive,** and there's **no Face ID (just the finally-1080p webcam + the Touch ID power button).**
  Other laptops have thinner bezels without a notch; I'd bet a future version adds Face ID.'"
- [2021-10-18] **The little Pro details + the verdict.** "'The little things: **every pro I know uses an SD card
  slot; the higher-impedance headphone jack (a DAC) is for pros' own wired headphones, not just AirPods Max;** you
  can max out the 14" or 16" to the same best config (just pick a size). **$1,999 / $2,499 — expensive, no surprise
  (the base 14 is a cut-down 8-core, so +$500 for the full chip).** These finally feel like great Pro laptops. Shout
  out to Apple for listening. (Still patient for the Apple-silicon Mac Pro — the one I'm most excited for.)'"
