# The $700 Mac Pro Wheels: Explained! (@mkbhd, 2020-05-09)

## Summary
Instead of dunking on the $700 Mac Pro wheels, an explainer on **why they exist** — a sharp analysis of **Apple as
the luxury brand of tech**, how **price signals quality**, and how absurd halo products maintain a premium image
(and generate "all press is good press").

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-05-09] **THE THESIS — Apple is the luxury brand of tech, and luxury needs absurd products.** "'Apple loves
  its **premium luxury image** — it's what lets them charge more. **Every luxury brand has products so expensive
  people aren't mad they can't get them but that are easy to make fun of** — Supreme, Gucci, Rolex, Bugatti. In
  tech, **Apple is the luxury one**, and keeping that status isn't effortless in a competitive world.'" A promotable
  framework for reading Apple's high-end SKUs.
- [2020-05-09] **Price signals quality (the studies).** "'There are studies: put **two identical items side by
  side, price one higher, and people pick the pricier as "better"** with no other context. Price is one of the
  things — alongside materials, name, packaging — that changes *perceived* quality.'" A media-literacy point.
- [2020-05-09] **Competitive where it matters, luxury on the other end.** "'A brutal, competitive tech world
  crafted the **$329 iPad and the $400 iPhone SE** (incredible deals) — Apple's **not allergic to competitive
  pricing** when it benefits them. But to **keep the luxury image they also need high-priced products** — and
  when they make just one desktop tower, they make the **expensive** one.'" Ties to his "products Apple should
  make but doesn't" thread.
- [2020-05-09] **"Sacrifice some products for the memes" + all-press-is-good-press.** "'Apple can **sacrifice
  certain products for the memes** to keep luxury status (per Linus). And **all press is good press** — virtually
  every article about the crazy wheels **mentions other Apple products / Apple's history / what you could buy for
  the same price.** So **the more you talk about the $700 wheels, the more Apple gets what it wants: attention**
  (unless it's this video).'"
- [2020-05-09] **The honest object appraisal.** "'Objectively these might be the **nicest computer wheels I've ever
  seen** (all metal + rubber, one weighs 2× an iPhone). **Are they worth $700? Of course not** — but it makes
  perfect sense they exist. Same as the $1,000 monitor stand, the $300 coffee-table book, the $17k gold Apple
  Watch.'"

## Notable verbatim quotes
