# Galaxy Note 20 Ultra Review: It Better Be Good! (@mkbhd, 2020-08-18)

## Summary
Full review of the $1,300 Note 20 Ultra. The framing verdict: at $1,300 it's competing with first-gen
folds and bleeding-edge flagships, so it has to be **top of the line at basically everything** — and it
mostly is (best-built Samsung phone, "plus-plus" display, the near-instant S Pen). The durable idea: with
Samsung, **"Ultra" is now the word that signals the best in the lineup — "Note" no longer does.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-08-18] **THE DISTILLED VERDICT — "it's really nice, but it's $1,300, so it better be."** "'A friend
  asked how I like it, and what came out was: **"it's really nice, but it's also $1,300, so it better be."**
  That thought kept coming back — at this price you only see experimental folds and bleeding-edge flagships,
  so it's got to be top of the line at **basically everything** to even consider.'" His organizing frame for
  ultra-premium reviews.
- [2020-08-18] **Best-built Samsung phone ever + the little things that justify the price.** "'The matte
  soft-touch back, boxy flat top/bottom, razor-thin bezels — **the best-looking, best-built phone Samsung
  has ever made.** And the little things add up: high-quality vibration motor, ultrasonic fingerprint, wireless
  + reverse-wireless charging, IP68 — **stuff we'd nitpick if it were missing, but it's all here.**'"
- [2020-08-18] **High refresh rate + 1080p again — "everything I want."** "'The 6.9" 120 Hz screen is
  outstanding — a "plus-plus." **120 Hz combined with the display is really everything I want in a phone now.**
  Cons: it's massive with curved edges, so I got accidental touches — **I'm still on the side of going back to
  flat displays.**'" Reaffirms the high-refresh and flat-display doctrines.
- [2020-08-18] **The camera — patient-but-outstanding; laser AF fixed the S20 Ultra hunting.** "'The **laser
  autofocus fixed the hunting** the S20 Ultra had, but the **shutter lag is still there** — be patient, hold
  still, and this turns out outstanding photos in all conditions. **A case for it being above the Pixel on
  versatility** thanks to a great ultrawide + the massive zoom you never have to use (50x, not the mistaken
  100x).'"
- [2020-08-18] **S Pen at 9 ms = Apple Pencil realism (but I'm not an S Pen person).** "'Latency down to
  **9 ms — Apple Pencil levels of responsiveness** — plus a new writing sound. A big technical improvement,
  but **the S Pen is the same fun-optional-accessory-vs-vital-tool split; I'm not the biggest S Pen person,**
  so for me a Note probably isn't the best use of money — but for those who wait for it every year, this is it.'"
- [2020-08-18] **"The opposite of the Apple strategy."** "'Samsung packs the phone to the gills — Edge lighting,
  secure folder, wireless DeX, Bixby routines. They **err on the side of shoving in too many features instead
  of keeping it super simple and leaving out anything imperfect — the opposite of the Apple strategy** — and if
  there's an upside, it makes you feel you're getting your money's worth.'" A recurring Samsung-vs-Apple axis.
- [2020-08-18] **Are you getting $1,300 of phone? Probably not — but Samsung prices are "guaranteed to fall."**
  "'Honestly, probably not — **but Samsung launch prices are guaranteed to fall;** in three or four months this
  will cost less. If any phone makes it feel like the complete package, it's this one — **but for $1,300, it
  better be.**'" A durable Samsung-pricing tell.
- [2020-08-18] **"Ultra," not "Note," is now the word to look for (crediting Becca @ The Verge).** "'A new
  thought for 2020: **just attaching the Note name doesn't mean flagship quality anymore.** Becca from The
  Verge made the point — the word that used to signal Samsung's best was "Note"; **now it's "Ultra."** PSA:
  none of us reviewers were sent the $1,000 plastic 1080p/60 Hz Note 20 — probably on purpose.'"
