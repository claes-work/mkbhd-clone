# 6K RED Komodo Impressions: The Mini Cine Camera! (@mkbhd, 2020-08-21)

## Summary
Impressions of the ~$6,000 RED Komodo — the small 6K Super-35 cine cube he was **most looking forward to at
the start of 2020**. It's a hybrid of RED image quality and mirrorless-world conveniences (RF mount, off-the-shelf
batteries + CFast), but it's **still in beta** (rudimentary autofocus, basic menus). The two durable rules:
**don't buy tech on the promise of future software updates**, and **cheaper good cameras make everyone happier.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-08-21] **Why it excites him — RED image without the RED ecosystem tax.** "'99% of you have no intention
  of buying one, but for creator friends like me this is the most exciting of 2020's cameras because **its
  potential is through the roof.** You get 6K R3D raw / 4K ProRes, RED color science, **15 stops of dynamic
  range** (more than the R5/A7S), and global shutter — cinema-camera traits — in a **two-pound 4"×4" cube.**'"
- [2020-08-21] **The hybrid conveniences — the real story.** "'Unlike other REDs: **Canon RF mount,
  off-the-shelf dual Canon BP batteries (hot-swappable, 3+ hours), and CFast 2.0 cards you buy at B&H** — no
  proprietary SSDs or mini-mags. It flies on a Ronin-S — I could never fly a Monstro on a gimbal. (Still wish
  it had dual card slots.)'"
- [2020-08-21] **"It's in beta" — and autofocus is the tell.** "'The whole camera is in beta — **RF lens
  support is a work in progress (no electronic comms yet, ships with an RF-to-EF adapter), menus are basic,
  and autofocus is rudimentary, contrast-based tap-to-focus with hunting** — unacceptable in the mirrorless
  world. **Autofocus is the biggest indicator of the overall state of this camera.**'"
- [2020-08-21] **MY #1 RULE — don't buy tech on the promise of future software updates.** "'Should you buy one
  now? **Probably no** — not for the dislikes (SDI-only, no HDMI; fixed top display; not-modular box) but
  because **my number-one rule about buying tech is: don't buy a piece of tech based on the promise of future
  software updates — buy it based on whether you like what it is right now.** I say this in smartphone videos
  too.'" A promotable buying doctrine.
- [2020-08-21] **The ecosystem thesis — a possible new entry point to RED.** "'I've never been able to
  recommend a RED to anyone — **you don't just buy a RED, you buy the SSDs, monitors, modules; the cost adds
  up and it's been a barrier to entry for so long.** If Komodo gets traction, a **third-party ecosystem** (cages,
  batteries, media) grows around it — maybe **this is the new entry point.**'"
- [2020-08-21] **"The cheaper good cameras get, the happier we can all be."** "'People say I focus on cameras
  too much in smartphone reviews — I can't help it. **I want people to record and make the highest-quality
  stuff, and the better cheap cameras get and the cheaper good cameras get, the happier we can all be.**'" His
  core camera-democratization value.
- [2020-08-21] **The whole video was shot on Komodo (and the AutoFocus tie-in).** "'This entire video is
  **shot on Komodo** — I have two. We're even considering shooting our next **Auto Focus car episode** on it,
  though those rely on autofocus, so it's a toss-up.'"
