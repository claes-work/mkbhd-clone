# Google Pixel 7/Pro Review: Hard Problems? Software Answers! (@mkbhd, 2022-10-12)

## Summary
The full review after ~a week. The thesis: **"the Pixel special is software solutions to hardware problems"** — in
contrast to the Samsung/Xiaomi special ("throw as much raw hardware horsepower as possible"). Neither is right or
wrong, but "there's a little magic to the software way when it works." Big notes: **great battery this year** (a
combo of software optimization, better modems, and the Tensor G2); the **Tensor G2 benchmarks like a Snapdragon 865
(fine), but its ML is ~60% faster/20% more efficient — "not about raw power; the Pixel is the smartest smartphone
in the room."** The software wins: Direct My Call, call screening, the world-best recorder, and **photo-unblur**
(AI that fixes long-exposure blur on any Google Photos shot — "the definition of solving hardware problems with
software"). The cameras are top-of-the-heap for point-and-shoot Android, with a clever **software-zoom** (2x/10x
crops + super-res composition) — but **the iPhone is still the king of imagery (noise, autofocus).** His steer:
**the $599 Pixel 7 is a way better deal than the $899 7 Pro — a value-phone-of-the-year contender.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-10-12] **The Pixel special — software solutions to hardware problems.** "'More than ever, **the Pixel
  special is software solutions to hardware problems** — in contrast to the Samsung/Xiaomi special (throw as much
  raw hardware as possible). Neither is right or wrong — but **there's a little magic to the software way when it
  works.** The metal camera bar (one seamless piece), brighter displays (you can't fake that with software), and a
  less-curved 7 Pro screen are the real hardware changes.'"
- [2022-10-12] **Tensor G2 — the ML, not the raw power; and great battery.** "'The **Tensor G2 benchmarks a bit
  higher (like a Snapdragon 865) — fine — but as in "The Problem with Benchmarks," the real improvements are its ML
  (~60% faster, ~20% more efficient).** So the things that need AI smarts are dramatically better. And **I've had
  great battery** (an hour commute on Waze + Spotify, still 85% left; ending days at 30–40%) — software +
  modems + the chip. (Set the 7 Pro to 1440p — no battery issues.)'"
- [2022-10-12] **The smartest smartphone — the software wins.** "'The Pixel isn't setting out to make the best
  hardware/display/raw performance — **it's the smartest smartphone in the room, and even more so this year.**
  Call screening + **Direct My Call** (surfaces the phone-menu buttons before the robot finishes), the cleaner
  home-screen search, AI Face Unlock (single camera — struggles in the dark/masked, so keep the fingerprint
  reader), the world-best recorder (transcribes with speaker labels), and **photo-unblur** — AI that fixes
  long-exposure blur on any Google Photos shot (**"the definition of solving hardware problems with software"**; I
  give it a year before it comes to more phones).'"
- [2022-10-12] **The cameras + the software-zoom — but the iPhone is still the imagery king.** "'Top-of-the-heap for
  point-and-shoot Android — the distinct Pixel HDR/contrast look, natural depth of field, a super-fast shutter, and
  the new Pro ultrawide does great macro. The **software-zoom is another "solve hardware with software"**: 1x
  (binned), 2x (a 12 MP crop), 2–5x (composited detail from the tele), 5x (the full tele), 10x (a crop of the
  tele) — passable for occasional zoomers (not as sharp as a dedicated 10x). **But it's a step behind the iPhone on
  noise and autofocus — that's why I still call the iPhone the overall king of smartphone imagery.**'"
- [2022-10-12] **The 7 is the better deal — value phone of the year.** "'Same prices ($599 / $899). **The 7 is a way
  better deal than the 7 Pro** (the $300 gap buys a screen, battery, and a whole Pro camera system — but for most
  people the 7 is a no-brainer). Both undercut most of their competition — **a value-phone-of-the-year contender
  again.** "For patient Pixel People who prefer pristine programming and pretty pictures, the Pixel Pro is pretty
  perfect." (Some early bugs — a media-player glitch, an Instagram-story render bug — but nothing like the Pixel 6.
  The 7 Pro is my daily driver now.)'"
