# Samsung Galaxy Note 9 Review: The Total Package! (@mkbhd, 2018-08-23)

## Summary
The full Note 9 review, built on his **five-pillars** framework: the Note 8 nailed everything but battery; the
Note 9 upgrades everything AND fixes the battery, so it "checks every box and then some." A clean articulation
of "**incremental is one of the best compliments you can give a phone.**"

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-08-23] **The five pillars, restated and checked.** "**Great display, build, performance, camera, and
  battery** — check every box and you've got a great phone. The Note 8 had four; the Note 9 **upgrades everything
  and makes the biggest jump on the one it lacked — battery.**" The canonical five-pillars framework, applied.
- [2018-08-23] **"Incremental is one of the best compliments you can give a phone."** Pushes back on the
  "just-an-incremental-upgrade" criticism: when the base was already phone-of-the-year, incremental = it now does
  everything. A reframing worth capturing.
- [2018-08-23] **Display + build — best-in-class, underrated hardware.** "Some, including me, would say it's
  **literally the best display in any phone** — again, no notch." The only downside: it's **big** ("one of the
  few phones that makes me work to use one-handed"). The **industrial design is underrated** (chiseled, boxy,
  IP68, headphone jack, **excellent clicky buttons**, much-improved speakers). The glossy back is a fingerprint
  magnet → a **black dbrand skin** for "a clean black-and-yellow look with the S Pen."
- [2018-08-23] **Camera — "firmly third place, which is awesome."** "**Pixel 2 at the top, then this and the iPhone
  X around #2/#3 — watch the Blind Test if you don't believe me.**" Essentially the S9+ camera setup. The new AI
  **Scene Optimizer** "was more inconsistent and annoying than useful — I turned it off" (consistent with his
  anti-auto-enhancement stance); but **flaw detection** (blink/blur/red-eye alerts) is genuinely useful. Cites his
  own [[youtube-index|Blind Camera Test]] as the authority.
- [2018-08-23] **Battery — the fix.** The 4000 mAh (a **+20%** jump from the Note 8's conservative post-recall 3300)
  now "comfortably lasts all day with room to spare — ~5 hours screen-on with heavy use." Grades it **A-minus**:
  "4000 mAh is the appropriate size for the Note." The pillar is finally filled.
- [2018-08-23] **The one real flaw — the un-remappable Bixby button (again).** "A perfectly placed Bixby button
  that **can't be remapped** — a damn shame. **I just want to remap it to Google or Twitter. Do what you can't,
  Samsung — I thought you were about that.**" His consistent remappable-button principle, restated.
- [2018-08-23] **The buyer's verdict.** "A thousand bucks is a lot, but **if you're going to spend $1000, this is
  the one to get** — you're getting your money's worth. If you think $1000 is too much, the S9/S9+ are several
  hundred less — Samsung's offering the whole spread. Good job, Samsung." Buyer-first close.
