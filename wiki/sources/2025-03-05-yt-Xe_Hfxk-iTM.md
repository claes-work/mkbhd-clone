# Reviewing my Daily Driver | 911 Turbo S

## Summary

Marques reviews **his own daily driver** — a **2024 992-generation Porsche 911 Turbo S, jet-black
metallic** — the car he returns to between all the review vehicles. **A canonical biography source:**
he calls it **"the best car in the world"** (rates it **9.82/10**), has driven it **~15,000 miles**
over about a year, came to it **from a Tesla Model S Plaid**, confirms **he writes for Top Gear**,
and describes his annual **New York → Boston → Montreal → home ultimate-frisbee road trip**. He
frames it around the things he only learned after living with it. Fully Marques-narrated.

## Key claims (biography marked ⬥)

- **[2025-03-05]** ⬥ BIOGRAPHY (canonical): the daily driver is a **992-gen 911 Turbo S, 2024 model,
  jet-black metallic**, with the aero kit, powder-coated black wheels, red brakes, carbon-fiber trim;
  **"in my belief this is the best car in the world — I literally believe it's the best car in the
  world, and I'm super lucky to be able to drive it."** He got it "about a year ago" and has done
  **15,000 miles + one (free) oil change** and one non-affecting center-lock-wheel recall. (quote)
- **[2025-03-05]** ⬥ BIOGRAPHY: **"I essentially get the exact same range as the Tesla Model S Plaid
  that I came from"** — ~300–330 mi (teens-to-mid-20s mpg on a 16.5-gal tank). Confirms his prior daily
  was a **Model S Plaid**. (quote)
- **[2025-03-05]** ⬥ BIOGRAPHY: **"I do write for Top Gear"** (declines to give "the Top Gear breakdown
  of driving dynamics on a track"). (quote)
- **[2025-03-05]** ⬥ BIOGRAPHY: **the annual ultimate-frisbee road trip** — "New York to Boston (4–5
  hrs), play a game; next day Boston to Montreal, play a game; Sunday night Montreal back to New York.
  I would not rather do that in any other car." (quote)
- **[2025-03-05]** ⬥ On his review lifestyle: **"The thing about doing reviews is I have to get
  comfortable switching my life around… one day a folding phone, the next a flip phone… same with cars.
  In between those review devices is the daily driver."** (quote) — the daily driver as the constant.
- **[2025-03-05]** ⚠️ Hot take: **the 911 is "not very digital… closer to the analog side"** — no
  built-in dashcam (he wired up a Thinkware), no wireless charger (wired CarPlay only), no full
  self-driving, slow to warm up (no remote-preheat app like his EVs). **"But that's not why you buy
  this car — you buy it to drive it."** (quote)
- **[2025-03-05]** Driving: praises the PDK, the drive modes (rarely uses Normal; Sport is "how normal
  people will drive it"; Sport Plus), the **wet mode with microphones in the wheel wells**, the
  **roomiest footwell in its class** (matters at 6'3"), and **Burmester speakers "some of the best I've
  ever heard in a sports car."** ⚠️ "Don't let people tell you there's no lag — it's a twin-turbo car,
  there definitely is lag." (quote)
- **[2025-03-05]** ⬥ Foreshadow: **"I'm curious about the next-gen Turbo S, the 992.2, because it's
  going to be a hybrid most likely, and that might solve the tiny bit of turbo lag."** (He later reviews
  exactly that — the 911 GTS T-Hybrid.) Rating: **9.82/10**, "I think I'm going to have this car for a
  long time." (quote)
