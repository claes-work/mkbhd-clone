# My Next Daily Driver? - 911 GTS T-Hybrid

## Summary

Marques tests the **first hybrid 911** — the 992.2 Carrera 4 GTS "T-Hybrid" — and finds it an
engineering masterpiece: a single big turbo kept spun-up by an electric motor gives **"the most
responsive single-turbo setup ever in a production car,"** with essentially no lag, so you can
"drive it and not really ever know it's a hybrid." But despite the title, his verdict is a
biography beat: **he still prefers his own 992.1** — "I'm becoming that old person who prefers
the older Porsches." Fully Marques-narrated.

## Key claims (biography marked ⬥)

- **[2025-06-30]** The electric *motor* (if not the electric *car*) is "basically everywhere — even
  the Porsche 911 can't resist." The 992.2 Carrera 4 GTS wears a **"T-Hybrid"** badge; "Porsche
  doesn't take changes to the 911 lightly," so a hybrid 911 "is a huge deal — they must have a really
  good reason. And I agree, really good reason." (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-06-30]** It's an unusual hybrid: a 3.6L flat-6 + **a 1.9kWh battery + electric motors that
  add only ~50kg and never actually drive the car** (no EV mode). The clever bit: instead of twin
  turbos, **one big turbo kept spun-up by the e-motor, so it's always on boost → essentially no turbo
  lag.** "The most responsive single-turbo setup ever in a production car… you can drive it and not
  know it's a hybrid." The packaging is untouched (same-size frunk). (paraphrase + quote)
- **[2025-06-30]** Small touches: **the e-motor starts the engine with no crank/ignition sound**; the
  reworked engine "sounds a bit like the Cayenne GTS V8"; it's happy to cruise under 2,000 RPM and
  still feel responsive (unlike twin-turbo cars you flip to sport mode). (paraphrase)
- **[2025-06-30]** Verdict: "I get why Porsche did it — it's that engineering masterpiece the 911
  always is… basically no downside; the slightly heavier weight is undetectable. **The only real
  downside is that it's so freaking expensive** (~$200k)." (quote)
- **[2025-06-30]** ⬥ BIOGRAPHY: **"I still prefer the older 992.1. This is me becoming that old person
  who I always thought it was weird that they prefer the older Porsches, but now I'm immediately that
  guy — I like the turnkey ignition start, the analog tachometer."** (The 992.2 has a generic
  start-button + a fully digital tach.) (quote)

## Notable verbatim quotes

- "It's the most responsive single-turbo setup ever in a production car. It's so good of a hybrid
  that you can drive it and not really ever know it's a hybrid."
- "I still prefer the older 992.1… this is me becoming that old person who prefers the older Porsches,
  but now I'm immediately that guy — the turnkey ignition, the analog tachometer."
