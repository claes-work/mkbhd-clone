# Fisker Ocean 2.0 - 2 Little Too Late?

## Summary

Marques does something he'd **never done — review a car twice** — revisiting the **Fisker Ocean**
(which he'd called **"the worst car I've ever reviewed"**) now running the new **2.0 software**, after
comment-section pushback and accusations that his review was **bankrupting the company**. His stance
is a clean values statement: **"I'm a product person — I care about the product,"** not the stock.
Some things are genuinely fixed; the fundamentals (Fisker's own laggy software, no CarPlay) and the
company's collapse mean he **still can't recommend it.** Fully Marques-narrated.

## Key claims

- **[2024-04-03]** ⭐ VALUES: **"Besides all the craziness of the accusations of me bankrupting the
  company — or caring at all about the stock price, which I don't and never have — I'm a product
  person. I care about the product."** He took the re-review because "the car I reviewed was running
  older software," and Fisker offered one with 2.0. (quote)
- **[2024-04-03]** ⚠️ The bind: the older Oceans **can't update over-the-air** — Fisker must send a
  technician to each car — and **"Fisker the company may not exist very soon,"** so 2.0 "is really tough
  to say it changes anything about my recommendation." (quote)
- **[2024-04-03]** What 2.0 fixed: Bluetooth pairing; hill-hold/brake-hold; California mode; reduced
  ADAS beeping; **OTA updates work again.** What's still broken: the walk-up key ("still have to unlock
  a few times"), a ~60-second boot animation (a *new* issue), the seatbelt-gates-the-parking-brake
  quirk, the distracting red/blue acceleration screen, uneven liftoff regen ("feels like brake failure
  over a bump"), and the delayed Earth/Fun/Hyper throttle. (paraphrase)
- **[2024-04-03]** ⭐ The worst-cars ranking: **"Is it still the worst car I've ever reviewed? Yes — but
  not by much. I've reviewed an original Tesla Roadster and a VinFast VF8, and I'd still take those over
  this."** The hardware itself (seats, recycled materials, looks) is "a really nice interior"; it's the
  **software + company** that sink it. (quote)
- **[2024-04-03]** Verdict: "I'm glad I gave it a second chance and saw real improvements — but I still
  can't recommend this version given the state of the company." (quote)
