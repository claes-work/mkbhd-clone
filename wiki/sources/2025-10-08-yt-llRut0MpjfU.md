# The Best Alternatives to Every Tesla

## Summary

Marques answers his most-frequently-asked question — "what are the best non-Tesla EVs?" — with
a top-three for every Tesla (Model 3, Y, S, X, Cybertruck). His framing: 10 years ago the
answer was "just buy the Tesla," but in 2025 the competition has "gotten way better," and a
recurring theme is that **most alternatives have CarPlay/Android Auto, which Teslas don't.** A
biography-rich EV-worldview source (fully Marques-narrated).

## Key claims (biography marked ⬥)

- **[2025-10-08]** 10 years ago this would've been a short video ("just buy the Tesla"), but now
  Tesla opening its Supercharger network + much better competition means "way more alternatives
  worthy of considering." He's **reviewed every car in the video.** (paraphrase)
- **[2025-10-08]** **Model 3 →** Hyundai Ioniq 6 (top pick; ~$20k used, NACS in 2026, has CarPlay),
  Polestar 2, Volvo EX30. **Model Y →** BMW iX, Cadillac Lyriq ("I called it underrated"), Genesis
  GV60. **Model S →** Lucid Air GT (top), Porsche Taycan, BMW i5 M60. **Model X →** Lucid Gravity
  (top), Rivian R1S, Volvo EX90 ("Rolls-Royce levels of premium… the speakers are ridiculous").
  **Cybertruck →** Rivian R1T (top), Chevy Silverado EV (~450mi), Ford F-150 Lightning ("the safe
  bet"). (paraphrase)
- **[2025-10-08]** ⬥ BIOGRAPHY: **"I daily drove a Model S for many years — I said they were
  basically the best daily driver you can get. But then I found one that one-upped the Model S in
  every useful way: the Lucid Air."** (quote)
- **[2025-10-08]** ⬥ BIOGRAPHY: **"I had a Cybertruck for a while and ended up selling it, which
  made headlines for some reason — because I had two electric trucks and I actually like the other
  one better. That other one is my top pick: the Rivian R1T."** (quote)
- **[2025-10-08]** The recurring theme: **most alternatives have CarPlay/Android Auto** ("that's
  going to be a theme here for a lot of the alternatives to Teslas, which don't have that"); and
  most **lack Tesla-level self-driving** ("if you're really into that, you're probably not looking
  at these alternatives"). (quote)
- **[2025-10-08]** "I have nothing against Tesla — their products are really good and sell a ton for
  good reasons. This is just my video answer to the most frequent question I get." (quote)
