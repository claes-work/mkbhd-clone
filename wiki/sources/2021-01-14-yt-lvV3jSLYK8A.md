# Samsung Galaxy S21 Ultra Impressions: Redemption Time! (@mkbhd, 2021-01-14)

## Summary
First impressions of the $1,200 Galaxy S21 Ultra — framed as **redemption for the S20 Ultra ("kind of a whiff"
because of the camera focus issues).** Durable notes: the striking new **contour-cut camera design**, the
**no-charger-in-the-box confirmed** ("the iPhone 12 was a huge domino"), the new **optional S Pen** (which does
NOT mean the Note is dead — they're "in very different worlds"), and **microSD going extinct.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-01-14] **The framing — the S20 Ultra "was a whiff," this is the redemption.** "'The S20 Ultra had the
  high-end numbers **but the camera focus issues meant you never quite recommended it — it got passed on a lot for
  the money.** The S21 Ultra looks to get Samsung back to the best-of-everything world with a lot of next-gen
  parts (2nd-gen 108 MP, laser AF, a 3x + 10x periscope pair, QHD@120 Hz LTPO 10–120 Hz).'"
- [2021-01-14] **The contour-cut design — "futuristic, like it melted down from a thicker phone."** "'The new
  **contour-cut camera design** grows out of the corner of the frame — "like someone made a thicker phone with
  huge cameras and melted it down to a thinner body." The more I look at it, the more futuristic it looks. Matte
  across the whole back (which I love), and the display is **nearly flat** (which makes me happy).'"
- [2021-01-14] **NO CHARGER (OR HEADPHONES) CONFIRMED — "like I warned you."** "'No headphones or charger in the
  box — **like I warned you, this is going to start happening very quickly across the industry; the iPhone 12 was
  a huge domino to fall. Don't expect a power brick in a flagship from here on out.**'" A confirmed 2020 prediction.
- [2021-01-14] **The S Pen is optional — and the Note is NOT dead.** "'S Pen support arrives, but on the S21
  Ultra it's a **very optional $40 accessory** (passive — no Bluetooth/gestures) with **no built-in silo** (a $70
  stylus+case). On the Note the S Pen is integral. So **if you'll go through all that, the obvious choice is to
  buy a Note — I predict a Note 21 Ultra; I don't think the Note's going anywhere.**'" (A prediction to track.)
- [2021-01-14] **microSD is going extinct — the RAM trade.** "'**No expandable storage anymore — the microSD card
  is basically going extinct** (Samsung was among the last). Only 128/256/512 GB, and on the Ultra you trade
  expandable storage for a lot of RAM (12–16 GB).'"

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "This phone last year was kind of a whiff... because of the focus issues with the camera, it was just never
> something you quite recommended."
> "No headphones or charger in the box, confirmed — like I warned you, this is gonna start happening very quickly
> across the industry. iPhone 12 was a huge domino to fall."
