# via Twitter for iPhone (@mkbhd, 2019-02-14)

## Summary
A media-literacy essay on "the Twitter police" — the running gag (that he helped popularize) of brand ambassadors
tweeting ads for one phone **from a competing phone** (the "via Twitter for iPhone" tag). Lands a real point:
**celebrity endorsements shouldn't mean much.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-02-14] **The origin — Gal Gadot's Huawei ads from an iPhone.** "Twitter shows what device you tweeted from.
  I noticed **Gal Gadot (a Huawei brand ambassador) posting her sponsored Huawei ads from an iPhone.** I
  screenshotted it; it blew up, made headlines, Huawei emailed me asking how I found it, and she blocked me — then
  days later re-posted the same ads 'via Twitter for Android.'" A dated, concrete case.
- [2019-02-14] **The pattern (the "Twitter police").** A parade of examples: **Anushka Sharma** (Google/Pixel
  ambassador) posting a "shot on Pixel" ad from an iPhone; **official Samsung, Razer, and Huawei accounts** tweeting
  product announcements from iPhones; **OnePlus CEO Carl Pei** from an iPad; **Linus** posting his "Android > iPhone"
  video from an iPhone; even **Apple Music** replying from an Android. "**How hard is it to just avoid an iPhone for
  your Android ad?**" (Viewers send them to him — "no, I don't have a team hunting these.")
- [2019-02-14] **THE POINT — "celebrity endorsements shouldn't mean much."** "The mechanic of an endorsement is
  'people want to be like this celebrity, so we pay them to say they use X.' But **celebrities don't really care —
  their teams take the money to *say* they use it, but they don't. And in tech it's just too easy to get caught.**"
  A clear authenticity/media-literacy read.
- [2019-02-14] **The self-implicating close (the authenticity value).** "**If you ever catch ME tweeting an Android
  ad from an iPhone, you have full rein to slap me through the internet — but it'll never happen, because it's just
  too easy to not do.**" Holds himself to the standard he's mocking.
- [2019-02-14] **The receipts + the classics.** A tool to scrub any account's tweet-clients (Gal Gadot had 100+
  iPhone tweets; the Samsung Nigeria account **deleted 300+ iPhone tweets** after he called it out). The all-time
  favorite: "**Oprah once tweeted an ad for a Microsoft Surface — from an iPad.**" Sharp, funny media literacy.
