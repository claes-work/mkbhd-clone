# Lamborghini Revuelto Proves that Some Things Never Change

## Summary

Co-host **Miles** reviews the **Lamborghini Revuelto** — the V12 plug-in-hybrid successor to the
Aventador. His verdict: dynamically a huge leap (dual-clutch, ~1,000hp, "glued to the road"), and
the naturally-aspirated 6.5L V12 "is why you buy it," but the **infotainment/software is genuinely
bad** (a convoluted Bluetooth process, no CarPlay yet) for an ~$800k Volkswagen-group car.
Attributed entirely to Miles.

## Key claims (all Miles — context only, NOT persona data)

- **[2024-08-26]** *(Miles)* 6.5L NA V12 (revs to 9,250rpm) + 3 e-motors + 8-speed dual-clutch;
  ~1,000hp, 0–60 ~2.5s, 3,900lb; ~4–5mi EV range. "Absolutely glued to the road" mechanical grip.
  (paraphrase)
- **[2024-08-26]** *(Miles)* ⚠️ Software: **"the overall software/infotainment usability is not
  great"** — a convoluted Bluetooth pairing you must repeat every drive, no CarPlay until end of year,
  a UI "very similar to their cars from a decade-plus ago." Interior livability "5/10, mostly because
  of the software." "If you don't give a crap about the software, this gets a glowing recommendation."
  (paraphrase)
