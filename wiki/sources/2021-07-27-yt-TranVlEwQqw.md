# The iPhone 13 Models! (@mkbhd, 2021-07-27)

## Summary
A pre-launch preview using the leaked dummy models of the four iPhone 13s (a lighter ingest). The durable reads:
**the ports are still there — "MagSafe isn't ready to pick up where Lightning left off"** (15 W vs. 20–100 W
elsewhere), so it's **too early to go portless**; the camera bumps hint at **bigger sensors trickling down** (to
the 13/mini) and **better stabilization** (spread-out Pro lenses); and **ProMotion hope** ("the iPhone will be the
last flagship to go high-refresh"). Also launches the **Studio Channel** (a biography milestone — his second
channel).

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-07-27] **The ports are still there — "MagSafe isn't ready."** "'Despite the portless-future rumors, **all
  the dummy models have ports — which tells me the MagSafe ecosystem isn't quite ready to pick up where Lightning
  left off.** MagSafe only supports 15 W; we're in a world of 50–100 W wireless charging elsewhere. **It feels too
  early to go portless** — maybe they're waiting for better wireless charging.'" (Cf. the Portless-iPhone framework,
  [[2020-12-07-yt-Qfmeb2e_kb4]].)
- [2021-07-27] **The cameras — bigger sensors trickle down + better stabilization.** "'The camera modules are all
  bigger. On the 13/mini, the diagonal dual layout with a larger lower lens hints at a **bigger sensor trickling
  down** (last year only the 12 Pro Max got the big sensor — the best-of-last-year trickling down in price). On
  the Pro/Pro Max, the lenses are more spread out — which reads as **better sensor-shift stabilization across all
  sensors** (less noise via longer shutter without the huge-sensor drawbacks — shallow DoF, fringing).'" A
  read-the-hardware inference.
- [2021-07-27] **ProMotion hope + a smaller notch.** "'The models can't show it, but the biggest rumor is
  **ProMotion (120 Hz) — the iPhone being last, they've got to do it (maybe just the Pros).** And the **earpiece
  moved up**, which you'd only do if you're compressing the Face ID cluster to make **a smaller notch** — not
  removing it, but giving some screen real estate back.'"
- [2021-07-27] **The upgrade question.** "'Is 120 Hz + maybe better cameras enough to upgrade? A weird question —
  **most people with last year's phone shouldn't upgrade anyway;** and the smaller models look like almost the
  same phone with a slight camera bump.'"
- [2021-07-27] **BIOGRAPHY — the Studio Channel launches.** "'We finally launched **the secret second channel —
  the Studio Channel** — the first video is a **studio tour** (the finished space, meet the team) plus the
  behind-the-scenes stuff that doesn't fit the main channel. A goal: 100k subscribers in the first week.'" A
  datable channel milestone.
