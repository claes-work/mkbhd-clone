# M2 MacBook Air Review: More Than a Refresh! (@mkbhd, 2022-07-18)

## Summary
The full review of the redesigned M2 MacBook Air — **"more than a refresh"**: the first look at what Apple can do
in a thin-and-light form factor now that it designs the whole thing around its own silicon. The flat redesign is a
win (thinner overall, a bigger 500-nit display, a 1080p webcam, **MagSafe + a color-matched cable + fast
charging**, surprisingly great hinge-tucked speakers, all-day battery). Two persistent negatives: **the Midnight
color is an absolute fingerprint magnet with a port-scratch durability concern** ("the most fingerprint laptop
we've seen, aside from all the other matte-black laptops"), and **the price is deceiving** — the base 256 GB model
has half-speed SSD + 8 GB shared memory that swaps, so **"consider it a $1,500 laptop."** The **fanless-vs-fan
throttle point is ~5 minutes of sustained load** (video/renders/gaming); below that, the Air keeps up with the M2
Pro. His steer: the Air's sweet spot is ~$1,500; for creative/sustained work **the $2,000 14" M1 Pro "runs circles
around it"** (a better screen, ports, an SD slot). Biography: **he now regularly edits video on a laptop** (carries
the 16").

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-07-18] **More than a refresh — the flat redesign.** "'Two years ago they swapped the Intel Air's chip for
  an M1; now they've **redesigned the whole computer around Apple silicon** — the first look at what's possible in
  a thin-and-light. The wedge is gone (flat, 11 mm, thinner overall) — "one of those shapes you can take
  everywhere." A bigger 500-nit display with a notch (fades away) + a 1080p webcam, **MagSafe + a color-matched
  braided cable + 67 W fast charging**, surprisingly great hinge-tucked speakers, and all-day battery.'"
- [2022-07-18] **The most-fingerprint laptop + a durability concern.** "'Midnight is a very dark blue that can look
  matte-black (sick) — **but it's an absolute fingerprint magnet, impossible to keep clean**, and the review unit
  showed **paint scratching around the MagSafe/USB-C ports if you miss plugging in (silver underneath shows on the
  dark laptop).** Solutions: don't get this color, skin it (dbrand), or take really good care. "The most
  fingerprint laptop we've seen, aside from all the other matte-black laptops."'"
- [2022-07-18] **The fanless-vs-fan throttle point is ~5 minutes.** "'The most-asked question: the difference
  between the M2 in a fan-cooled Pro vs the fanless Air? **In short bursty stuff (up to a couple minutes) they're
  the same; the Air throttles under sustained load — the point is ~5 minutes** (not photo editing or browsing, but
  video editing / long renders / gaming lighting up all cores). It heats up in the middle. Amazing that we're even
  asking if a MacBook AIR can handle four 4K streams.'"
- [2022-07-18] **The deceiving price — "consider it a $1,500 laptop."** "'The one thing against it is price:
  **$1,199 (up $200 from the M1 Air, which stays at $999).** The base 256 GB model has the **half-speed single-SSD
  issue plus 8 GB shared memory that swaps to the slow SSD** — so **you should upgrade to 512 GB ($1,400) — that's
  what it should have started at. Consider it a $1,500 laptop; the sweet spot.**'"
- [2022-07-18] **For creative work, the 14" M1 Pro runs circles around it.** "'If you spec the Air up for creative
  work (10-core GPU, 24 GB, 2 TB) you're past $2,000 — and **the $2,000 14" M1 Pro runs circles around it** (a
  nicer screen, ports, an SD card slot that creative work needs). Biography aside: **I regularly edit video on a
  laptop now, so I carry the huge 16" — I'm jealous of this Air.**'"
