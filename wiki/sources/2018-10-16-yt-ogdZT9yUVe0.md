# Huawei Mate 20 Pro Impressions: Underrated? (@mkbhd, 2018-10-16)

## Summary
"Techtober" first-look at the Mate 20 Pro — "the most Samsung-feeling phone Huawei's ever made." Notable for the
arrival of the **in-glass fingerprint reader** on a major phone, **reverse wireless charging**, and his
"commit to one" critique of stacking Face-ID + in-glass. Opens with a 7-million-subscriber merch drop.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-10-16] **BIOGRAPHY — 7 million subscribers.** "A quick 7-day merch drop (the optical-flow / Joy-Division-
  inspired wavy-line shirt) **in honor of hitting 7 million subscribers very recently.**" A dated milestone (up
  from 5M in Jan 2018).
- [2018-10-16] **The in-glass fingerprint reader arrives — his explainer pays off.** "The Mate 20 Pro has an
  **in-glass fingerprint reader — beating OnePlus to the punch, becoming the first major big-name phone to do
  this.** Being first by a few days/weeks isn't a huge deal, but it's awesome to see it happening this fast. This
  is the early generation — as it develops we end up with bigger, cheaper, faster under-glass sensors." The payoff
  of his Feb/May in-glass-fingerprint videos ([[2018-02-21-yt-dRtqFjYYUY0]], [[2018-05-15-yt-bSl9PfRX7WY]]).
- [2018-10-16] **"Commit to one" — the Face-ID-AND-fingerprint critique.** "It has a big **face-unlock notch AND**
  an in-glass fingerprint — but **everyone will pick one or the other, not both.** Spending on both feels like
  throwing stuff at the wall; **commit to one.** I'm pretty sure OnePlus will just commit to the fingerprint and
  not make a big deal of the RGB face unlock." A pointed design-discipline critique.
- [2018-10-16] **The triple camera done right (like the V40).** "Unlike the P20 Pro's regular/telephoto/**monochrome**
  setup, the Mate 20 Pro is **three genuinely different focal lengths** (40 MP normal / 8 MP tele / 20 MP ultra-
  wide) — the useful end, like the V40." Consistent multi-camera principle. (Wary of **Master AI 2.0**
  auto-switching to wide: "I like more manual control.")
- [2018-10-16] **REVERSE WIRELESS CHARGING — "the coolest feature."** "It can become a **mobile battery bank —
  wirelessly charge up other phones.** I rested my iPhone on the back and it charged (works through a case/skin,
  even hovering a centimeter apart). You can be that guy who offers to wirelessly charge someone else's phone."
  Genuine enthusiasm for a novel feature.
- [2018-10-16] **The verdict — wait for the P30 Pro.** "The most **Samsung-feeling** Huawei ever (the S9 + P20 Pro
  had a baby); EMUI 9 won't change your mind if you disliked it. **Not the critical phone everyone's watching — it
  feels like they're throwing a lot at the wall. I'll wait for the P30 Pro.**" Measured.
