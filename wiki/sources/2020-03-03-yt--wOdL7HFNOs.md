# Samsung Galaxy S20 Ultra Review: Attack of the Numbers! (@mkbhd, 2020-03-03)

## Summary
The full S20 Ultra review — "**Attack of the Numbers.**" Samsung justifies a $1,400 phone by throwing every big
spec at it (108 MP, 100x zoom, 8K, 120 Hz, 16 GB, 5,000 mAh). The verdict extends his signature thesis: **the
numbers don't always translate** — the camera still lands in third place.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-03-03] **"Attack of the numbers" — and numbers aren't everything.** "'Samsung's answer to "how do you
  justify a phone that expensive?" is **throw every big number at you.** But **as we've hopefully learned, camera
  specs don't translate — I've seen a million 64 MP cameras worse than the 12 MP Pixel/iPhone.**'" His core
  spec-skepticism thesis at full volume.
- [2020-03-03] **The display — "the highest A+ I've ever given."** "'6.9" 120 Hz 1440p — **the best display in any
  smartphone: brightness, color, contrast, viewing angles.** And they **flattened the edges** (I appreciate it —
  hope they keep it up).' ⚠️ Still **120 Hz OR 1440p, not both** (I pick 120 Hz every time); it's not an SD865
  limit — they just didn't enable it (battery)." His flat-over-curved + high-refresh preferences met.
- [2020-03-03] **THE CAMERA — still third place (the big-sensor physics problem).** "'Better in some ways, worse
  in others — **still solidly third behind the Pixel and iPhone.** The binned 12 MP is the **sharpest 12 MP out
  there**, but the **huge sensor + no dual-aperture (wide-open f/1.8 always) = a razor-thin plane of focus**, so
  close subjects get **fringy, bad bokeh and jumpy autofocus** — physics you can't fully fix in software.'" A
  precise, physics-grounded critique.
- [2020-03-03] **8K video — "I recommend against using it."** "'A laundry list of trade-offs: **locked at 24 fps**
  (not 30), **punches in** (8K window in the sensor), **no focus tracking**, the **worst rolling shutter I've ever
  seen in smartphone video ("turns into jelly")**, and huge files. Great for still/slow subjects; **I'll take
  another year or two of "measly" 4K.**'" Look-past-the-headline-spec at its sharpest.
- [2020-03-03] **THE SPEAKER ANALOGY (100x Space Zoom).** "'The **sharpest 30x zoom I've seen** (with Huawei); 100x
  is a cool demo but looks bad. **Trashing the 100x because it's bad at 100x is like trashing a speaker that goes
  to 100 dB because it sounds bad at 100 dB — it'll sound better at 50 dB than the ones maxing out at 50.**
  Appreciate the good tech when it's there.'" A memorable, reusable rebuttal to headroom-bashing.
- [2020-03-03] **The verdict — the strategy "mostly worked."** "'You don't need it; most people do great with a
  phone half the price. But **as a tech fan I'm happy when the high end pushes things forward** — and **this is the
  phone I'll be pocketing until further notice** (fingers crossed for software updates).'"
