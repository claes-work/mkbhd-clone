# Apple HomePod 2 Review: I'm Confused (@mkbhd, 2023-01-31)

## Summary
A review (opening with a skit — Marques voicing an "old HomePod / new HomePod" bit riffing on the
Craig/Tim/"Steve still loves me" meme) of the **second-gen HomePod**, which he calls **"one of the most
confusing new Apple products I've seen in a long time."** History recap: the original ($350, 2018) flopped,
got cut to $300, then discontinued in 2021 after the $99 HomePod mini sold far better — **and now the big
one is back at $299.** What's new: a slightly more vibrant top display, a **now-removable power cable**, new
temperature/humidity sensors and sound recognition (which the mini secretly already had, now enabled by
software), and **Matter/Thread support.** The biggest change is what they **removed** — two tweeters and two
mics (7→5, 6→4) and $50 — yet it "still sounds every bit as good" (crisp, balanced, satisfying thump; great
as a stereo/Atmos pair). His central verdict is a **ratings-philosophy point** (referencing the Waveform
podcast): it's **"a really good product and a really bad product at the same time" — hard to give a number
rating.** As a *speaker* for an iPhone/Apple Music, it's the best. As a *smart speaker* it's still bad —
won't default to Spotify, won't pair to anything non-Apple, no aux, no useful screen, and **"at the end of
the day it's still a Siri speaker,"** and Siri is limited. He suspects Apple is **ignoring the sunk-cost
fallacy** — "we spent the money developing it, might as well ship it again and see if people want it this
time." PSA: **the white one still stains wooden tables.** Sign-off: **"Siri sucks seamlessly."**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2023-01-31] **The most confusing Apple product in a while — didn't you try this already?** "'The
  second-gen HomePod is one of the most confusing new Apple products I've seen in a long time. The original
  flopped, got a price cut, then was discontinued in 2021 — and now it's back at $299. It's basically the
  same proposition that flopped on its face before: an expensive premium audio product with Siri.'"
- [2023-01-31] **A really good and a really bad product at the same time.** "'We were just talking about
  ratings on the Waveform podcast — the thing about products like this is it's hard to give a number rating,
  because it's a really good product and a really bad product at the same time. Both are true.'"
- [2023-01-31] **Great speaker, bad smart speaker — still a Siri speaker.** "'Just as a speaker for your
  iPhone and Apple Music, it's really good — clearly the best, incredible sound, versatile placement, quick
  pairing. But it's still a bad smart speaker: it won't set Spotify as default, won't pair to anything
  non-Apple/AirPlay, can't be a pure Bluetooth speaker, no audio jack, no useful screen — and at the end of
  the day it's still a Siri speaker, and Siri is still very limited and pretty bad.'"
- [2023-01-31] **They removed hardware and $50 — and ignored sunk cost.** "'They removed two tweeters, two
  mics, and $50 from the price, and it still sounds as good as the original to me. People don't really like
  paying a huge premium just for sound quality — that's why the mini sold way more. Maybe Apple just doesn't
  care anymore; they're ignoring the sunk-cost fallacy — "we spent the money developing it, might as well
  ship it again and see if people want it this time."'"
- [2023-01-31] **The PSA — it still stains tables.** "'Remember the original's white base staining wooden
  tables? The redesigned base takes longer, but it does in fact still stain wooden tables. This is your PSA:
  if you get the white HomePod, do not expect it to not stain your table.'"
