# The Closest Thing to AirPower! (@mkbhd, 2019-06-12)

## Summary
A quick look at "Air Unleashed," a $99 three-coil wireless charger that shamelessly mimics the cancelled Apple
AirPower. The real payload is a **manufacturing-reality argument**: Apple *could* have shipped this simpler
three-coil version but wouldn't "come up short" of its stage promise, so it killed the product entirely.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2019-06-12] **THE ARGUMENT — Apple could have shipped a "good enough" version but chose not to.** "AirPower was
  a **magic overlapping-coil mat** (drop any device anywhere) — 'really tough to make,' with heat issues, so
  cancelled ([[wiki/sources]] AirPower death video). This knockoff is just **three coils** (Qi left, Qi right,
  proprietary Apple Watch coil in the middle). **Could Apple have made this three-coil version? 'Yeah, I think
  they could have — but they were so set on that magic and didn't want to come up short from what they promised
  on stage, so they cancelled it completely.'**" A durable read on Apple's all-or-nothing standard.
- [2019-06-12] **The honest-recommendation move.** "'There's a ton of other wireless chargers I'd recommend first
  — cheaper or more functional. But if you're just trying to get **as close to the AirPower dream** as possible,
  this is the one.' It's not fast (~5.5 hrs phone 0-100 across all three coils) — 'totally average.'" He steers
  buyers away from the very product he's covering.
- [2019-06-12] **The "is it a knockoff if the original never existed?" riff.** "'Is it really a knockoff if the
  original product never existed in the first place? I don't know — still feels like they're copying Apple.'"
  A voice/humor beat.
