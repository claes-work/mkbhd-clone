# Tesla Opens its Superchargers and Weird MWC Gadgets? (Waveform, 2023-03-03)

> ⚠️ **Ensemble podcast.** Hosts: Marques Brownlee, Andrew Manganelli, David Imel.
> No speaker labels; only **clearly-Marques** takes train the persona. Strong EV segment.

## Summary
Tesla begins opening its **Superchargers to non-Tesla (CCS) cars** via the new **Magic
Dock** — and Marques drove out to test one himself. Plus weird MWC gadgets.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated 2023-03-03)
- ★ **[Marques, clearly — EV charging thesis]** Why he **"largely recommends Teslas in the
  US"**: Teslas charge on the reliable Supercharger network *and* public chargers for
  backup, while everyone else depends on unreliable public charging (Electrify America) —
  "if you're buying a **$170,000 Lucid Air**, your best bet is crossing your fingers the EA
  charger works." That calculus **just changed**: ~**10 US Superchargers were retrofitted
  with the Magic Dock** so a CCS car with a built-in adapter can officially charge. *(his
  core "buy a Tesla for the charging network" advice, with the moment it starts to shift.)*
- ★ **[Marques, clearly — biography]** He **drove his Rivian R1T ~1.5 hours upstate into
  New York** to the nearest Magic Dock Supercharger to test it firsthand. *(BIO: owns a
  **Rivian R1T**; does hands-on charging testing.)*
