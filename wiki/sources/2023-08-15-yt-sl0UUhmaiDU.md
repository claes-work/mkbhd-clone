# Samsung Tab S9 Ultra: Is the iPad Killer Real? (@mkbhd, 2023-08-15)

## Summary
A review of the **14.6" Galaxy Tab S9 Ultra** ("it feels like I'm holding a small TV") — razor-thin, an 11,200 mAh
battery (bigger than any iPad's), a new 8 Gen 2 for Galaxy + vapor chamber, 20 quad speakers, first-time IP68, an
included S Pen, DeX. "All huge numbers," and it's genuinely fun to hold. But the recurring question: **is an "iPad
killer" a real thing?** His answer weaves two ideas. First, **the Sony parallel**: like Sony's enthusiast phones,
this piles on features and expandable memory that the competition doesn't offer — but **the number of people who
actually take advantage of those features is tiny; they're everywhere on YouTube, but people vote with their wallets
for the standard option** (the ecosystem effect — iMessage/FaceTime — is far stronger than YouTube comments suggest).
Second, a cleaner analogy he "likes even better": **"people like bigger screens as long as it doesn't sacrifice too
much in other areas."** Evidence: the 13" MacBook Air was Apple's best-seller; people who wanted a bigger screen had
to jump to the overkill 16" Pro and drifted to other brands — so Apple finally made the **15" MacBook Air, exactly
what people wanted**; conversely the **mini iPhone** (a "brilliant idea if you only read YouTube comments") didn't
sell and got discontinued — most people want the *bigger* screen, hence the Plus. So the Tab S9 Ultra is a bigger
screen than the iPad Pro (amazing to hold) but **a very different Android tablet with too much sacrifice for most —
yet "a hell of a tablet" for those not locked into the ecosystem.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2023-08-15] **The Sony parallel — enthusiast features nobody actually buys.** "'This reminds me of the Sony phone
  conversation: incredibly well-equipped, super high-end, with enthusiast features the competition doesn't offer —
  at the expense of price and a bit of everyday usability. It's easy to point out all the extra features, but the
  number of people who actually take advantage of them is tiny. They're everywhere on YouTube, but when it's time to
  vote with their wallets, most go with the standard one.'"
- [2023-08-15] **The ecosystem effect beats YouTube comments.** "'Videos about these tablets do numbers — people
  love seeing them — but when it comes to actually buying, the ecosystem effect is way stronger than YouTube comments
  suggest. People flock to the iPad for iMessage, FaceTime, all of it.'"
- [2023-08-15] **People want a bigger screen — as long as it doesn't sacrifice too much.** "'My favorite analogy:
  people like bigger screens as long as it doesn't sacrifice too much elsewhere. The 13" MacBook Air was Apple's
  best-seller; wanting a bigger screen meant jumping to the overkill 16" Pro, so people drifted to other brands —
  until Apple made the 15" Air, exactly what people wanted. On the flip side, Apple tried a mini iPhone for two years
  (a brilliant idea if you only read YouTube comments), but most people want the bigger screen, so it got
  discontinued and we got a Plus.'"
- [2023-08-15] **A hell of a tablet — for the not-locked-in.** "'This is a bigger screen than the iPad Pro (which is
  why it's so amazing to hold) but a very different Android tablet, $100 more, with a whole different feature set —
  too much sacrifice for a lot of people, who'll keep getting iPads. But for those not looped into the ecosystem and
  who don't depend on those features, this is a hell of a tablet.'"
- [2023-08-15] **It out-features the iPad Pro — and DeX out-computes it.** "'It one-ups the iPad Pro on numbers — a
  bigger screen, bigger speakers, expandable storage, a fingerprint reader — for $100 more. And DeX gives you a real
  desktop experience with independent windows, more powerful than anything Apple has even tried on the iPad.'"
