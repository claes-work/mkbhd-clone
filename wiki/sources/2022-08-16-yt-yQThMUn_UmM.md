# Android 13 Hands-On: Top 5 Features! (@mkbhd, 2022-08-16)

## Summary
A top-5 walkthrough of Android 13 — with two framing observations. **(1) iOS updates saturate fast (iOS 15 hit
~90% of compatible iPhones within a year), while Android is deeply fragmented** (only ~12% on the latest, more on
2-gen-old versions), so this trickles out slowly. **(2) Both platforms' updates are more minor than ever** (they're
mature; Android 12 already did the big Material You overhaul) — but **"a lot of the small stuff adds up to quality-
of-life improvements."** His top 5: #5 a much-improved **clipboard** (edit-on-copy, auto-clear of sensitive data,
cross-device copy/paste coming); #4 **security/privacy** (partial photo access, opt-in notifications); #3 **audio**
(native spatial audio + Bluetooth LE); #2 **app-specific language**; and #1 **aesthetics** (Material You theming
tweaks, a color-changing media player). One gripe: **they lowkey made some haptic patterns worse.**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2022-08-16] **iOS saturates fast; Android fragments.** "'Two things struck me: **the iOS videos always get nuts
  views — because iOS updates saturate so fast (iOS 15 hit ~90% of compatible iPhones within a year).** Android is
  the opposite: **only ~12% are on the latest, ~27% two gens old, and the fragmentation continues** — Pixel owners
  get 13 first, then it trickles out phone by phone.'"
- [2022-08-16] **Updates are more minor than ever — but the small stuff adds up.** "'In both camps, **updates are
  more minor than ever** (they're mature; iOS's lock screen is the exception; Android 12 already did Material You,
  so 13 is tweaks). Look at the Easter eggs — nearly the same. **But a lot of the small stuff adds up to quality-of-
  life improvements.**'"
- [2022-08-16] **#5 the clipboard, #4 security.** "'**#5 clipboard**: a preview box you can edit on copy; images go
  straight to markup; **sensitive info auto-clears after ~1 hour** (so apps peeking at your clipboard find it
  empty); and universal cross-device copy/paste is coming (the Pixel tablet). **#4 security**: **partial photo
  access** (catching up to iOS), **opt-in notifications** (apps must ask), and an active-app indicator.'"
- [2022-08-16] **#3 audio, #2 app-specific language.** "'**#3**: native **spatial audio + Bluetooth LE** (lower
  latency; the Pixel Buds Pro will get spatial audio via a firmware update). **#2** (one of the most-requested):
  **app-specific language** — if you use one app in a second language (a family group chat), Android now supports
  that switch natively (developers opt in).'"
- [2022-08-16] **#1 aesthetics — and the haptics got worse.** "'**#1 aesthetics**: Material You theming is more
  precise (16 multicolor + 16 single-color options vs 4), a bigger **media player that changes color to the album
  art with a squiggly playing line**, a slightly new lock clock/unlock animation, the settings shortcut moved to be
  more reachable. Lots of bug/performance fixes (should run better than 12 on a Pixel 6). One thing that got worse:
  **I think they lowkey made some of the haptic patterns worse — I hope they reconsider.**'"

## Notable verbatim quotes
