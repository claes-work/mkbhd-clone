# The C8 Corvette Z06 Has Multiple Personalities!

## Summary

Marques tests the gas **C8 Corvette Z06** through his tech-reviewer lens — **"but is there any tech in
it?"** — and finds the answer is yes: it takes real tech to give a hardcore track car **multiple
personalities** (touring / sport / track modes that transform steering, suspension, exhaust valves,
and shift aggression) so it can be both a track weapon and a daily driver. Along the way he nerds out
on **the appreciation of building torque** (vs. an EV's flat torque shelf). Fully Marques-narrated.

## Key claims

- **[2023-10-11]** The frame: coming off the electrified E-Ray, "is there any tech in the *gas* Z06?"
  The Z06 "is the most popular Corvette I've ever seen — a revelation for Corvette the way EVs were for
  Kia/Hyundai." A 670 hp naturally-aspirated V8 — "the most powerful NA V8 ever in a production car." (quote)
- **[2023-10-11]** ⭐ The insight: **"It takes a lot of tech for a car like this to have multiple
  personalities"** — the everyday-supercar cliché (a track car is the *opposite* of what you want at the
  grocery store: hard/loud/firm vs. soft/relaxed). **Touring / Sport / Track** modes vary steering,
  suspension, **variable exhaust valves** (a "stealth" setting), and shift feel, so it's "genuinely
  daily-drivable." (quote)
- **[2023-10-11]** ⭐ Torque appreciation: **"An EV has a flat torque shelf — stab it anywhere and you're
  jolted forward. This NA engine builds torque steadily through every gear; there's no magic boost
  point. Torque is fun — people talk about horsepower because the numbers compare in straight lines, but
  the way it *builds* is really neat."** He's glad it keeps the "come on, drive faster" edge McLarens
  have. (quote)
- **[2023-10-11]** Creature-comfort tech: wireless CarPlay/Android Auto, a 360 camera, a HUD, a Bose
  system he was "very impressed" with, a front/rear trunk, remote start ("I was talking at normal volume
  — you can't appreciate through YouTube how *loud* it is"). (paraphrase)

## Notable verbatim quotes

- "It takes a lot of tech for a car like this to have multiple personalities."
- "An EV has a flat torque shelf. This NA engine builds torque steadily — torque is fun, and the way it builds is really neat."
- "You can't appreciate how loud it is through a video — you feel it in your chest."
