# The TRUTH About OnePlus Nord! (@mkbhd, 2020-07-27)

## Summary
The full OnePlus Nord review + the strategy behind it. Verdict: "**every part is second-best for the price**" — no
standout, but all right up there. Two durable points: the **"cut the useless cameras"** argument, and the analysis
of **why the Nord was inevitable** for OnePlus.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-07-27] **"Every part is second-best for the price."** "'No super-standout flagship spec anywhere, but all
  of them are right up there with the best (the 90 Hz flat OLED is the best example). **Self-correction: I said
  "metal and glass" in the impressions — it's actually glass front/back with painted-to-look-like-metal PLASTIC
  rails** (nothing wrong with plastic — often more durable).'" A candid correction of his own earlier claim.
- [2020-07-27] **THE CAMERA TRUTH — cut the useless cameras.** "'They've **fallen victim to their own marketing
  hype.** The **2 MP macro is unnecessary — the tell: the OnePlus 8 Pro auto-switches to macro because it's good;
  the Nord never does, because the macro is worse than cropping the main camera.** The **depth sensor does nothing**
  (I covered it with my finger — no change). **Companies add triple/quad cameras just to LOOK like a flagship on
  the spec sheet — just get rid of the two useless ones and put the money into wireless charging or a better main
  camera.**'" A promotable critique of camera-count marketing (cf. [[2020-07-22-yt-IEwEDTmqCEs]]).
- [2020-07-27] **THE STRATEGY — "this was inevitable."** "'OnePlus **lacks the brand clout to compete at $1,000+**
  (ask 5 friends — some haven't heard of OnePlus; hard to sell a $1k phone from an unknown brand vs Apple/Samsung).
  So as their flagships crept up, **they needed a mid-range Nord to keep the good-cheap-phone reputation** that
  built them. It can't be a "flagship killer" anymore (they make a flagship now) — so it's a **distinct mid-range
  brand** that won't cannibalize sales. They added wireless charging + IP to the 8-series to compete high, opening
  the Nord slot below.'" A sharp brand-strategy read.
- [2020-07-27] **The verdict — easy to recommend under ~$500.** "'With 2 years of software + 3 years of security
  updates, **I could easily daily this for a couple years.** Under ~$500/€500, **it's easy to recommend** — they
  made the right trade-offs. Looking forward to a US Nord.'" (Battery is quite good; the mono speaker is bad but
  "better than no speaker.")

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "Just get rid of those last two cameras that are so bad they're useless, and put that money you save into
> something else — the camera really is one of those places where you can just cut the fat."
> "They can't make this a flagship killer, because they make a flagship now and they're not trying to cannibalize
> their own sales."
