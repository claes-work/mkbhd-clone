# The AI Assistant Battle! (2023) — Bing vs Bard (@mkbhd, 2023-04-08)

## Summary
A head-to-head of the two new AI chat assistants — **ChatGPT-powered Bing vs Google's Bard** — explicitly the
**"level two"** successor to his old Google-Assistant-vs-Siri test, with the caveat that **"this is just a snapshot
in early April 2023"** (both improve fast). Categories: **Simple questions** — a draw; both get the high-level right
but flub specifics, so **you still have to fact-check** (his refrain). **Complex questions** (a jump-higher workout
plan, fixing a golf slice, brightening a too-dark video shoot) — **edge to Bing** in "creative" mode: more nuanced,
cites sources, has a slider to turn depth up. **Performing tasks** (write working HTML for a "generate a cat"
button; overly-friendly/flirty emails) — **Bing wins** (Bard just says "I'm a language model, I can't"; Bing writes
code that works, and even **deletes its own too-spicy replies mid-generation** — "almost too creative for its own
good"). **Information summary** — **edge to Bard** (it knew his latest video, though it got the products wrong).
**Creative** (alliteration, an MKBHD-style script on "why the iPhone is the worst camera," an Edgar-Allan-Poe tweet)
— **Bing** goes wilder. His overall pick right now: **Bing** — but Google **limits Bard because it has more to
lose.** The real conclusion: **"the winner is actually us."**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2023-04-08] **A snapshot — and you still fact-check.** "'This is the level-two version of the assistant battle,
  but just a snapshot in early April 2023 — both will keep improving; the gap could shrink or grow. On simple
  fact-based questions, what we said last video still applies: you kind of want to fact-check this stuff, because it
  will lie to you if you don't know the subject.'"
- [2023-04-08] **Bing wins complex questions and tasks.** "'On complex questions Bing in creative mode is more
  nuanced, cites its sources, and has a slider to turn the depth up. On performing tasks it wins clearly — Bard just
  says "I'm a language model, I can't do that," while Bing writes HTML that actually works. The fact that it works at
  all is pretty sick.'"
- [2023-04-08] **Bing is almost too creative for its own good.** "'Ask for an overly-flirty email and Bing starts
  writing, realizes halfway through it's too much, and deletes it — "we shouldn't do that." That's why it has a
  20-question limit; it's almost too creative for its own good.'"
- [2023-04-08] **Bard edges information summary; Google limits it because it has more to lose.** "'Bard actually knew
  my latest video (though it got all the products wrong), so I give it the edge on summary. And the more wild you get
  with prompts, the more Bing will get wildly creative — part of why Google is limiting what Bard can do: they have
  a lot more to lose and can't have it go completely crazy.'"
- [2023-04-08] **The winner is us.** "'Tallied up, it's crazy to say, but I prefer Bing right now for these
  capabilities (and GPT-4 under the hood is about to be multimodal). Is this the future of search? I don't know —
  it's just one of many places we'll see these chatbots. But the winner, as of right now, is actually us.'"
