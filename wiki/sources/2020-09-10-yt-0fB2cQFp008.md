# Surface Duo Review: The Good, The Bad & The Ugly! (@mkbhd, 2020-09-10)

## Summary
Full review of the $1,400 Surface Duo — reframed as **"the good, the meh, and the bad."** Verdict up top:
**"I don't think most people should buy this,"** and that's fine — some cool devices just aren't right for
most people. The durable thesis: **practicality suffering at the expense of folding it in half is what's
holding back ALL first-gen foldables** — but the dual-screen productivity idea is real, so **"do not buy, but
keep an eye on it."**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-09-10] **The upfront honesty — "most people shouldn't buy this," and that's okay.** "'I'll come right
  off the top: **I don't think most people should buy this — and that's fine.** There are a lot of cool devices
  that just aren't right for most people to buy and use. **It's the second generation of most things that's the
  most telling** — I'm literally already looking forward to Surface Duo 2.'" His willing-to-say-don't-buy stance.
- [2020-09-10] **THE GOOD — the hinge and genuine dual-screen productivity.** "'Sticking by the hardware rave:
  the **crazy thinness and the 360° hinge that stops at any position — props to the hinge engineers.** The core
  win is **real multitasking: an app on each screen, app-pairs/groups (my most-common is calendar + email).**
  It's the same reason I sit in front of dual monitors instead of an ultrawide — **the compartmentalization,
  staying in your flow, it works, it's real.**'"
- [2020-09-10] **THE MEH — bezels, pocketability, typing, performance, battery.** "'Thick top/bottom bezels;
  **shaped like a passport, sticks out of a front pocket;** typing is a mixed bag (SwiftKey adapts to posture,
  Gboard looks silly spanned); **last-year Snapdragon 855 + 6 GB feels laggy at 60 Hz** — a multitasker device
  should've had 12 GB; middle-of-the-road 3,600 mAh battery.'"
- [2020-09-10] **THE BAD — trash speaker and a terrible camera (with an ergonomics problem).** "'**The speaker
  is absolute trash** — no room for a good one. **The camera is terrible** — you swivel the selfie camera around
  to become "possibly the worst main camera in a $1,400 phone," and the ergonomics of flipping-unlocking-swiveling
  mean **it actively discouraged me from taking photos.** Why is there even a flash?'"
- [2020-09-10] **THE UNIFYING THESIS — practicality sacrificed for the fold holds back all gen-1 foldables.**
  "'**Overall practicality suffering at the expense of folding it in half is the thing holding back all
  first-generation folding phones** — no 5G, no headphone jack, no high refresh, no wireless charging, little
  app support. **In future generations, when the fold is just another feature and you're not sacrificing to get
  it — that'll be the sweet future** I'm looking forward to.'" A promotable foldables framework.
- [2020-09-10] **The verdict — "do not buy, but keep an eye on it."** "'**File this first one in do-not-buy,
  but keep an eye on it because I really think they're onto something.** I like the dual-screen thing.'"
