# Let's Talk About Panels (@mkbhd, 2024-10-11)

## Summary
A candid post-launch accountability video after **Panels** (his wallpaper app) drew heavy internet backlash — mostly
over a **$50 price tier** and badly-communicated launch. His framing is pure ownership: **"that's 100% on me... this is
the two-sided coin of building in public: if we're going to dish it, we've got to be able to take it too"** (as a
reviewer, "I would not have been very nice to this app"). He explains **why the app exists** (for years the #1 question
under his videos is "where's that wallpaper?" — a place to point to, on iOS + Android, that also **drives eyeballs to
the artists** whose art he uses), then addresses each blunder: **Pricing** — "**this should be a great *free* app** for
the vast majority" — so he killed the in-feed ads, made all non-collection wallpapers free in 1080p (full-res 4K/8K via
a single 30-sec ad or **$2/mo ad-free**), with **Panels+ at $12** unlocking all paid collections (~$100 of art) and
**$50/yr for power users** ("most will find $50 crazy — because it's for power users, and you're most people"); the
**50/50 profit split with artists** (modeled on YouTube's ~45/55) with **no exclusivity** (artists can sell the same
art on their own sites — "still a win for everyone"); **Privacy** — the scary App Store tracking sheet was over-broad
checkboxes from the ad service, "a dumb choice — I do not want your data; you can use the app without even an email";
and **"it's a bad app"** — owning bugs and the identical-iOS/Android-UI design choice, debunking the "bought an old app
+ AI slop" rumors (AI-*enhanced* pieces are labeled; every piece is **curated** so it'll "never be AI slop"). He even
thanks reviewers (juxtaposed's "constructive facelift" — "one of my favorite videos of the week, I subscribed"). The
kicker is self-aware: **"never buy a product based on the promise of future software updates — buy it for what it is
today"** — "the irony is not lost on me that I've made a lot of promises about this app's future; grab it for what it
is today (a wallpaper app)." Commits to **weekly Friday art drops**.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2024-10-11] **The mistakes are 100% on me — and building in public cuts both ways.** "'I launched Panels and made a
  lot of mistakes — I didn't explain the UI, the point, or why there's a $50 tier, and the internet was rightly
  disappointed. That's 100% on me. This is the two-sided coin of building in public: if we're going to dish it, we've
  got to be able to take it. If I were reviewing this app, I would not have been very nice.'"
- [2024-10-11] **The point is discovering and paying artists.** "'For years the #1 question under my videos is "where'd
  you get that wallpaper?" I wanted a place to point to, on iOS and Android, and to leverage this platform to give way
  more eyeballs to the artists whose art I use. That's the main point — a platform for discovering artists.'"
- [2024-10-11] **The free experience should be great; here's the pricing.** "'This should be a great free app for the
  vast majority — I dropped the ball there. So: no more in-feed ads; all non-collection wallpapers free in 1080p (full-
  res via one 30-sec ad, or $2/mo ad-free). Panels+ is $12 to unlock every paid collection (~$100 of art), and $50/yr
  is for power users — most will find $50 crazy, because it's for power users, and you're most people.'"
- [2024-10-11] **50/50 with artists, no exclusivity, always curated.** "'We split profits 50/50 with artists (modeled
  on YouTube's ~45/55), and the agreements have no exclusivity — artists can host the same art on their own sites and
  sell it without the split; I still consider that a win. And every piece is curated by us — AI-*enhanced* pieces are
  clearly labeled — so it'll never just be AI slop.'"
- [2024-10-11] **Never buy for the promise of future updates — the irony noted.** "'I keep preaching: never buy a
  product based on the promise of future software updates — buy it for what it is today. The irony isn't lost on me
  that I've made a lot of promises about this app's future in this video. So grab Panels for what it is today — a
  wallpaper app. We're committing to weekly Friday drops, and hopefully with enough transparency we earn your trust
  back.'"
