# M1 Max MacBook Pro Review: Truly Next Level! (@mkbhd, 2021-11-05)

## Summary
The full review of the 14"/16" 2021 MacBook Pros (M1 Pro / M1 Max) — the counterpart to the Oct event reaction
[[2021-10-18-yt-uhIdijSrnVc]]. The landmark line: **"the first time in a really long time I can truly say
something is actually next level."** His frame throughout: **a laptop is a tool**, and this one happens to be next
level at the specific things it was designed for — his own being video editing. The proof: he **edited the entire
AirPods 3 review in 8K RED raw on the 16" M1 Max**, and on export it **beat a $50,000 Mac Pro (4:06 vs 5:42) — on
battery, dropping 2%.** Design wins: **the ports are back** ("in a pinch, one less dongle"), the Touch Bar is gone
for real keys, incredible speakers, a mini-LED 120 Hz XDR display — and the notch (which "disappears" in use, but
if the next one has this notch and no Face ID he'll "wear socks and sandals for a day"). One of his favorite
products of the year; the travel iMac is retired.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2021-11-05] **"Truly next level" — and it's a tool.** "'This Apple-silicon-from-Max transition is impressive,
  and **this is the first time in a really long time I can truly say something is actually next level.** At the
  end of the day these are just tools to get a job done — everyone has a slightly different thing they need done;
  for me it's making these videos.'"
- [2021-11-05] **The ports are back — "in a pinch, one less dongle."** "'HDMI, a Thunderbolt 4, and a half-depth
  SD slot (I use an SD card for audio every video) on the right; two more TB4, a headphone jack, and MagSafe on
  the left. **It feels silly to celebrate ports, but I'm happy they're back — Apple walking back the last few
  years is not something they do all the time.** The catch: the HDMI is only 2.0 (not 2.1) and the SD is only
  UHS-II — cut-rate on a computer this expensive — but the theme of all these ports is **"in a pinch, one less
  dongle."** The high-impedance headphone jack is the same idea (one less DAC to carry).'"
- [2021-11-05] **Real keys, killer speakers, a great display — and the notch.** "'The Touch Bar is gone for a full
  row of physical function keys — a shame it never reached its potential, but these are better. **The 16"
  speakers are still the best I've ever heard in a laptop.** The mini-LED 10-bit HDR 120 Hz display hits 1600
  nits. The notch is way bigger than the hardware needs — but it **disappears when you're actually using the
  laptop** (the menu bar lives in extra display you didn't have before; the mouse goes under it). **But if the
  next version still has this notch and doesn't add Face ID, I'll wear socks and sandals for a day like Linus.**'"
- [2021-11-05] **The proof — beating a $50K Mac Pro on battery.** "'I edited the whole last video in 8K RED raw
  on the 16" M1 Max, plugged in and on battery — shockingly well, playback in high quality, <¼-second play delay,
  just like the desktop Mac Pro. **On export I copied the project across machines: M1 Pro 14" did it in 6:40, the
  $50,000 28-core Mac Pro in 5:42, and the M1 Max in 4:06 — beating the Mac Pro handily, on battery, dropping just
  2% (96→94). High Power mode got it to 3:49. I never once heard the fans.** The vertical integration worked;
  the ProRes media engine is next level.'"
- [2021-11-05] **The recommendation + retiring the travel iMac.** "'If you're a professional who works on your
  laptop and you're reaching the limits of an older one — yeah, you'll be very happy. **The real choice is M1 Pro
  vs M1 Max and 14" vs 16"** (M1 Max nicks a bit of battery for extra GPU cores; go Pro for the longest life). I'm
  going 16" M1 Max for the GPU and the bigger editing screen. **The travel iMac I used to lug through airports in
  a Pelican case is now retired — I can safely edit everything on a laptop.** One of my favorite products of the
  year.'"
