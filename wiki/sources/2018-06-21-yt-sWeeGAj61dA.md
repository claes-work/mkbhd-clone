# On IGTV and YouTube Competition! (@mkbhd, 2018-06-21)

## Summary
A creator-business essay tied to Instagram's IGTV launch: why he stays on YouTube, why no competitor has
dethroned it (a graveyard of failed challengers), why IGTV is the most credible #2 yet, and why **competition
is good for users.** Also a notable reversal on vertical video.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2018-06-21] **CREATOR-BUSINESS — "don't put all your eggs in one basket; you don't own your audience on any one
  platform."** "Part of being a **creator** — not just a YouTuber — is creating for multiple platforms. I'm on
  Twitter, Instagram, Facebook — for creative reasons but also because **you don't own your audience on any one
  platform, so you diversify.**" His core platform-risk principle.
- [2018-06-21] **"What if YouTube disappeared tomorrow?" — his standing answer.** "I get asked this in every
  interview. YouTube is **too big to instantly disappear** — if it started failing there'd be a trajectory, I'd
  see it, and I could do something about it. As long as **there's no better place for a video creator than
  YouTube** — all the tools, stats, eyeballs, monetization, search in one place — I'll keep uploading there."
- [2018-06-21] **The graveyard of YouTube challengers (his platform history).** Periscope (niche, no archive/
  search), **Snapchat** (ignored creators too long, no real monetization), **Vine** ("we saw the whole life
  cycle — 100% creators, but the monetization was never secure, and it crumbled — RIP"), **Vimeo** (most complete
  competitor but ~10,000× fewer eyeballs), **Facebook video** ("90% stolen from YouTube; search is a dumpster
  fire; copyright is a mess"), and **Google+** ("do I even need to say more?"). A sharp survey of why YouTube wins.
- [2018-06-21] **IGTV — the most credible #2 yet.** Instagram showed him IGTV privately at WWDC. "It has the most
  complete set of factors: **eyeballs** (1B+ MAU), the beginnings of **discovery** (Explore, algorithms), and the
  **secret sauce — a genuine creator focus** (the App Store listing is creators, not A-list TV hosts). The only
  real question is: **will they pay creators?** I'm pretty sure they'll do some creator-benefiting monetization."
- [2018-06-21] **"Competition is good for us users."** "I'm excited about competition to YouTube stepping up. Just
  like the iPhone — **as much as you love it, it would never get better if there were no phone chomping at the bit
  to steal its customers.** Competition benefits the people using the services." A recurring belief, applied to
  platforms.
- [2018-06-21] **Reversal — from anti-vertical-video to willing.** "A couple years ago I was **staunchly
  anti-vertical video** because I make YouTube videos. But IG's pitch is mobile-first, and **we all hold our phones
  vertically** — if vertical video is the biggest complaint about IGTV, that's actually a good sign. **I'm willing
  to experiment within 9:16.**" A documented on-record evolution.
