# Poco F2 Pro: The Full Screen Deal! (@mkbhd, 2020-05-26)

## Summary
A look at the ~$500 Poco F2 Pro — a budget-flagship that "checks a lot of the boxes." The standout is a **pop-up-
camera full-screen display** (his favorite way to kill the hole-punch), plus a note that the real action in phones
in 2020 is at the budget end, not the incremental high end.

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2020-05-26] **The pop-up full-screen — "my favorite method."** "'A **full-screen, corner-to-corner, uninterrupted
  6.67" 1080p flat AMOLED** — no hole-punch, no notch, using **my favorite method of getting rid of the selfie
  camera: the pop-up.** And a flat display, which I'm totally fine with.'" Reinforces the pop-up-camera preference
  ([[2020-04-14-yt-LTDuGU38-70]]).
- [2020-05-26] **"If I didn't know it was $500…" — the tells.** "'The only things that tip me off it's not a $900
  phone: **a bit of off-axis rainbow banding** and the **bigger corner radius** (lower-end phones swoop the corners
  more; higher-end tighten them, more boxy). No wireless charging, no IP rating (those cost money).'"
- [2020-05-26] **The best single mono speaker + the vibration-motor petition.** "'A single mono speaker is bad on
  the spec sheet, but this is **maybe the best single mono speaker I've ever heard** (loud, crisp). The **rattly
  vibration motor** reminds you — "petition for everyone to just make those better" (expensive phones have bad ones
  too).'" A recurring haptics gripe.
- [2020-05-26] **The 5 MP macro is actually decent (a reversal).** "'I've been **trashing 2 MP macro cameras all
  year — but this 5 MP one is actually decent; macro shooting is fun.** The depth camera does something for
  portrait mode too. The main 64 MP is capable but not high-end (don't buy this for image quality — get a Pixel
  3a / iPhone SE).'"
- [2020-05-26] **The verdict + the budget-action observation.** "'A **real deal for $500.** MIUI is the only thing
  keeping me from dailying it (occasional ads in the UI; not as smooth as it should be). The point: **the very high
  end is incremental this year, but there's a lot of action in the budget space** — exciting.'"

## Notable verbatim quotes
> "It's doing my favorite method of getting rid of the hole-punch camera and the selfie camera... a full-screen
> corner-to-corner display."
> "A lot of the improvement at the very high end now is pretty incremental — lots of minor updates this year — but
> there's a lot of action down here."
