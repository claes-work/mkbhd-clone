## Summary
Marques unboxes and first-shoots the **RED V-Raptor** (DSMC3 body, same 8K Monstro sensor, 8K120)
for the iPhone 13 Pro review intro. His camera-nerd assessment + the recurring anti-reflection
preference are the persona-relevant parts.

## Key claims (Marques-attributed only)
- [2021-10-02] **Committed RED shooter — how RED works:** RED updates sensor and body separately in
  a staggered way; the V-Raptor is a **body update on the same 8K Monstro sensor** they'd been
  shooting, in the more-modular DSMC3 form ("you get the brain, then configure it exactly the way
  you want" — vs the all-in-one Komodo). (production-filmmaking, tech-reviews)
- [2021-10-02] **Anti-reflection preference (consistent theme):** he got the white "Stormtrooper"
  first-run body but "I'll probably end up getting a black one later... because reflections."
  (mirrors the Pro Display XDR nano-texture choice in [[2021-08-06-yt-cxp-FCDUkNc]]) (production-filmmaking)
- [2021-10-02] **Verdict:** image quality "to my eyes so far a little better than Monstro," cleaner
  in low light (he "would never shoot Monstro over 800 if I didn't have to"); but the DSMC2
  monitoring software is more mature. **"Whoever makes the best handle for it, that's what's going
  to get me to love shooting with this camera"** — he does a lot of handheld, first-person,
  pointing-at-things work, so the split view/settings ergonomics matter most. "I can't wait to
  shoot a car with this." (production-filmmaking, tech-reviews)
