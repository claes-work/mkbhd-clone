# I Said Yes to Every Email for a Month! (@mkbhd, 2023-03-03)

## Summary
A challenge/format video: for one month Marques **said yes to everything in his inbox** (he normally says no to
**"literally 99% of the things I get asked"**). The parade of stuff, each rated: a **$1,299 direct-to-consumer Titan
casket** (the one Taylor Swift popped out of in the "Anti-Hero" video — its real story is cutting out the
funeral-home middleman); a VR-headset audio clip-on (7/10); a $250 neck AC/heater ("you could get a scarf," 5/10);
custom memoji cases (3/10); a $250 aftermarket car touchscreen adding CarPlay to old cars (8/10); a **Coca-Cola
Realme 10 Pro** he rates **"11 out of 10 — the best brand collab I've seen in a really long time, even if I'd never
buy it"**; a screwdriver-swappable earbud case (7/10); a walkie-talkie (useful for car shoots); and a
multi-screen-laptop contraption (9/10). The real payoff is a **creator-business lesson**: even saying yes, he
couldn't get *everything*, because **smaller companies with a limited review allotment want to guarantee a video —
sometimes making you sign a contract that you'll post — and "creators don't sign those."** Closing reflection:
saying no to 99% is **"a really good problem to have."**

## Key claims (paraphrase, dated)
- [2023-03-03] **The premise — I say no to 99%.** "'I get all kinds of crazy stuff in my inbox all the time — some
  tech, some not even tech. I say no to literally 99% of the things I get asked. But what if, just for fun, I said
  yes to everything? So I did, for a whole month.'"
- [2023-03-03] **The Coca-Cola Realme — an 11/10 collab.** "'You didn't have to go this hard — the wallpaper, icon
  packs, and touch sounds are all Coca-Cola. I don't even drink it, but I've got to respect that they went to 11
  with this collab. It's an 11 out of 10 — the best brand collab I've seen in a really long time, even if I'd never
  buy it.'"
- [2023-03-03] **The casket's real innovation is the business model.** "'The innovation isn't the casket — it's a
  normal high-quality steel one — it's that Titan sells direct-to-consumer, cutting out the funeral-home middleman
  and the huge markups, so you get a $1,299 casket instead of five or ten thousand.'"
- [2023-03-03] **The creator-business lesson — the guaranteed-video contract.** "'Even saying yes, I couldn't end up
  with everything. Smaller companies have a small allotment of review units, so they want to guarantee a video —
  some even make you sign a contract that you'll make a video if you receive it. Creators don't sign those.'"
- [2023-03-03] **A good problem to have.** "'I'm in a fortunate place where I can say yes to a bunch of stuff and
  cool tech shows up. But at the end of the day, saying no to 99% of things is a really good problem to have. I'm
  glad this challenge is over.'"
